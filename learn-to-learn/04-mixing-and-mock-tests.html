Mixing and mock tests混合练习与模拟测验

Mix topics in one session, use a mock test as a map, and score yourself honestly · ~10 min · reading and practice一次学习里混合多个主题，把模拟测验当作地图，并诚实地给自己打分 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Mix the topics第 1 步 · 把主题混起来

Learn先学

Practising one topic in a block feels smooth because the answer is the same kind each time. Mixing topics in one session feels harder, but it makes you first work out which idea a question needs, as in the real exam.

一个主题连着练，会很顺手，因为每次答案都是同一类。一次学习里混合多个主题会觉得更难，但它逼你先判断一道题要用哪个想法，这正是真实考试的情形。

Blocked: A A A A B B B B C C C C Mixed: A C B A C B B C A B C A

Step 2 · A mock test is a map第 2 步 · 模拟测验是一张地图

Learn先学

Take a mock test under real timing, then do not just look at the score. Sort every miss by topic. The pattern of misses is a map of where to study next.

在真实计时下做一次模拟测验，之后别只看分数。把每个错题按主题分类。错题的分布，就是接下来该往哪里学的地图。

Which result gives you the most useful next step?

哪个结果能给你最有用的下一步？

Step 3 · Score yourself honestly第 3 步 · 诚实地给自己打分

Learn先学

Mark each answer “sure” or “guess”. A guess that turns out right still counts as a gap. Your honest score is the right answers you were sure about.

给每个答案标上“有把握”或“猜的”。猜对了也算空缺。你的诚实得分，是有把握且答对的题数。

Right: 7 of which guessed: 2 Honest score: 7 - 2 = 5 out of 10 The 2 guesses and the 3 wrong ones are the gaps.

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Mixing topics trains you to pick the right idea for each question.
• A mock test is a map: sort the misses by topic.
• A right answer you guessed is still a gap.
• Your honest score counts only the answers you were sure about.

• 混合主题，训练你为每道题选对想法。
• 模拟测验是一张地图：把错题按主题分类。
• 猜对的答案仍是空缺。
• 诚实得分只算有把握的答案。
