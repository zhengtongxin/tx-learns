Spacing your reviews间隔复习

Spread reviews over growing gaps and fit them into a normal week · ~10 min · reading and practice把复习分散在越来越长的间隔里，并安排进寻常的一周 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Space beats cramming第 1 步 · 间隔胜过突击

Learn先学

Ten hours spread over three weeks beat ten hours in one night, because each review arrives just as the memory starts to fade. A common pattern is to review after 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.

分三周学的十个小时，胜过一晚上的十个小时，因为每次复习都恰好赶在记忆开始淡忘的时候。常见的间隔是：1、3、7、14、30 天之后复习。

Review 1 +1 day Wed 4 Nov Review 2 +3 days Fri 6 Nov Review 3 +7 days Tue 10 Nov Review 4 +14 days Tue 17 Nov Review 5 +30 days Thu 3 Dec

Step 2 · Let the result set the gap第 2 步 · 让表现决定间隔

Learn先学

The schedule is not fixed. Right and easy: move to the next, longer gap. Wrong or shaky: go back to a short gap. A lucky guess counts as shaky, because you could not have produced it on purpose.

日程不是固定的。答对且轻松：进入下一个更长的间隔。答错或不稳：退回较短的间隔。侥幸猜对也算不稳，因为你并不能有把握地答出来。

Decide the next review for each case.

为每种情况决定下一次复习。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Plan backwards from a date第 3 步 · 从日期倒推

Learn先学

For an exam, start from the date and count back. Ten minutes a day with the old topics mixed in is easier to keep than a weekend marathon. Leave the last two days for light review of your marked gaps, not new material.

备考时从考试日期往回数。每天十分钟、把旧主题混在里面，比周末突击容易坚持。最后两天留给标出的薄弱点做轻量复习，不要再学新内容。

Exam: Sat 12 Dec First pass of all topics by Sat 21 Nov (3 weeks out) Second pass, gaps first by Sat 5 Dec Last week: 10 minutes a day on marked gaps Last 2 days: light review only

Takeaways要点回顾

• Spread reviews beat one long session.
• A common gap pattern is 1, 3, 7, 14 and 30 days.
• Right and easy lengthens the gap; wrong, shaky or guessed shortens it.
• Plan backwards from the exam date and keep the last days light.

• 分散的复习胜过一次长学习。
• 常见的间隔是 1、3、7、14、30 天。
• 答对且轻松就拉长间隔；答错、不稳或猜的就缩短。
• 从考试日期倒推，最后几天保持轻量。
