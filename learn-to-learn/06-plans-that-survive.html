Plans that survive坚持得下去的计划

Set a small daily goal, link it to a cue and recover from a missed day · ~10 min · reading and practice定一个小小的每日目标，把它绑在一个触发点上，并在漏掉一天后重新接上 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Small enough for a bad day第 1 步 · 小到糟糕的日子也做得到

Learn先学

A plan that needs a perfect day fails on the first bad one. Set a goal you can still do when you are tired, and a smaller minimum for the worst days. Doing five minutes keeps the habit alive; doing nothing breaks it.

一个需要完美日子才能执行的计划，遇到第一个糟糕的日子就失败了。定一个你累的时候也做得到的目标，再定一个更小的“最低限度”给最糟的日子。做五分钟能让习惯活着，什么都不做则会断掉。

Goal: 20 minutes, 10 questions Floor: 5 minutes, 3 questions (bad days) Never: zero

Step 2 · Tie it to a cue第 2 步 · 绑在一个触发点上

Learn先学

“I will study more” depends on remembering. “When I finish dinner, I open my notes” depends on something that already happens. Name the cue, the place and the first action.

“我要多学点”靠的是记得。“吃完晚饭，我就打开笔记”靠的是每天本来就会发生的事。说清触发点、地点和第一个动作。

Step 3 · Never miss twice第 3 步 · 不连续漏两次

Learn先学

You will miss days. One missed day changes almost nothing. Two in a row starts a new habit of not doing it. So the rule is simple: never miss twice, and on the day after a miss, do only the floor.

你一定会漏掉几天。漏一天几乎没有影响。连着漏两天，就开始养成“不做”的新习惯。所以规则很简单：不连续漏两次，漏掉的第二天，只做最低限度。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Set a goal for a bad day, with a floor smaller still.
• Tie the habit to a cue that already happens.
• One missed day is nothing; two in a row start a new habit.
• Raise the goal slowly and keep the floor.

• 按糟糕的日子定目标，再定一个更小的最低限度。
• 把习惯绑在每天本来就会发生的事上。
• 漏一天没什么；连漏两天，就开始养成“不做”的习惯。
• 目标慢慢提，并保留最低限度。
