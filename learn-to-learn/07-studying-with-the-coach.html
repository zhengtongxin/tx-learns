Keep going · Lesson 2坚持下去 · 第 2 课

Studying with the coach和学习教练一起学

Put the lessons into practice with the study coach on your own material · ~10 min · reading and practice用学习教练，把这些方法用到你自己的资料上 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Bring your material and pick a goal第 1 步 · 带上资料，选一个目标

Learn先学

The study coach works on your own files in your browser: PDF, slides or text. They are read on your device and are not uploaded. You choose a goal, either understanding the material or preparing for an exam with its areas and weights, and the coach writes practice questions from your passages.

学习教练在你的浏览器里处理你自己的文件：PDF、幻灯片或文本。它们只在你的设备上读取，不会上传。你选一个目标，要么是理解资料，要么是按考试的各领域和占比备考，教练会根据你资料里的段落出练习题。

Material -> Goal -> Model -> Practise -> Progress files on your device a small AI model runs in your browser questions come from your passages, in the page language

Step 2 · Practise, mark, and read the gaps第 2 步 · 练习、标记、读空缺

Learn先学

This is the same loop as the lessons. Answering is retrieval. Mark “I guessed” when you were not sure, so a lucky answer still counts as a gap. The coach then names the weak passages and asks again after growing gaps.

这和前面的课是同一个循环。作答就是提取练习。没把握时标上“我猜的”，侥幸答对也算空缺。之后教练会指出薄弱的段落，并在越来越长的间隔后再问。

Match each coach feature to the idea behind it.

把教练的每个功能和它背后的方法配起来。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Keep going with a small goal第 3 步 · 用小目标坚持

Learn先学

Set a daily goal you can meet on a bad day. The coach shows today’s questions against the goal and the days in a row you have studied. A missed day keeps yesterday’s streak, but two in a row end it: never miss twice.

设一个糟糕的日子也做得到的每日目标。教练会显示今天做了多少题、和目标比较，以及连续学习的天数。漏一天会保留昨天的连续天数，连漏两天就断了：不要连续漏两次。

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• The coach turns your own material into practice questions on your device.
• Practising is retrieval; marking guesses keeps the score honest.
• Gaps come back after growing gaps, as in spacing.
• A small daily goal and never missing twice keep the streak.

• 教练在你的设备上，把你自己的资料变成练习题。
• 练习就是提取；标出猜的，让分数诚实。
• 空缺会在越来越长的间隔后回来，就像间隔复习。
• 小的每日目标，加上不连续漏两次，保住连续天数。
