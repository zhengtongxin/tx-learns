Recall beats rereading回想胜过重读

Turn reading into practice by pulling ideas out of your head and checking the gaps · ~10 min · reading and practice把阅读变成练习：把想法从脑子里取出来，再核对空缺 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Read, close, write, check第 1 步 · 读、合上、写、核对

Learn先学

Retrieval practice means trying to recall an idea instead of looking at it again. The simplest routine has four moves: read a short section, close it, write what you remember, then open it and mark what you missed.

提取练习就是尝试回想，而不是再看一遍。最简单的流程有四步：读一小段，合上，写下记得的，再打开对照，标出漏掉的。

The gaps you mark are the most useful part: they tell you exactly what to study next.

你标出的空缺是最有用的部分：它们准确告诉你接下来该学什么。

Read one section 5 min Close it, write what you recall 3 min Open it, mark what you missed 2 min Next round starts with the marked gaps.

Step 2 · Questions that make you recall第 2 步 · 逼你回想的问题

Learn先学

A good study question cannot be answered by recognising a word on the page. It asks you to list, explain, compare or apply. “Read chapter 3” is a task. “List the five steps without looking” is a question.

好的学习问题，不能靠认出页面上的某个词来答。它要求你列出、解释、比较或应用。“读第 3 章”是任务；“不看书列出五个步骤”才是问题。

Sort each prompt.

给每个提示分类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Check right after, then fix第 3 步 · 做完马上核对，再补漏

Learn先学

After an attempt, check the answer straight away. A wrong answer you never check can settle in as a wrong memory. When you find a miss, write the correct version in your own words and test it again later.

尝试之后要马上核对答案。没核对过的错答案，可能会变成一个错误的记忆。发现漏洞时，用自己的话写出正确版本，之后再测一次。

Wrong answers are not wasted: they are where the learning happens.

答错不是浪费：学习恰恰发生在那里。

• 流程是：读、合上、写、核对。
