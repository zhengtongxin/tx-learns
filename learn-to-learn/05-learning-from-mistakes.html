Learning from mistakes从错题里学

Find why you missed a question and keep a short log that closes each gap · ~10 min · reading and practice找出自己为什么答错，并用一份简短的错题记录把每个空缺补上 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Sort the miss by its cause第 1 步 · 按原因给错题分类

Learn先学

Not every miss needs the same fix. Ask why it happened. Did not know: study the idea again. Misread: you knew it but answered a different question, so slow down and underline what is asked. Rushed: you knew it and slipped, so build a checking habit.

并非每个错题都用同一种补法。先问为什么会错。不知道：重新学这个想法。看错题：你会，但答了另一道题，所以要放慢，把题目问的划出来。太急：你会，只是失误，所以要养成检查的习惯。

Name the cause for each case.

为每个情况判断原因。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · A short mistake log第 2 步 · 一份简短的错题记录

Learn先学

A log only works if it is quick. Four columns are enough: the question in a few words, the cause, the correct idea in your own words, and the date to retest.

记录只有简单才坚持得下去。四列就够：用几个词写下题目、原因、用自己的话写出正确的想法、重测日期。

Step 3 · Close the loop第 3 步 · 把环闭上

Learn先学

A miss is not fixed when you read the answer. It is fixed when you get it right on two different days without looking. Until then it stays in the log.

看过答案并不算补上。隔了两天，各自不看资料答对，才算补上。在那之前，它一直留在记录里。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask why you missed it: did not know, misread or rushed.
• Each cause has its own fix.
• Keep a four-column log in your own words.
• A gap is closed after two correct days without looking.

• 先问为什么错：不知道、看错题，还是太急。
• 每种原因有各自的补法。
• 用自己的话记一份四列的记录。
• 隔天不看资料答对两次，才算补上。
