Why we forget为什么会忘

See why most of what you read fades, and what makes a memory last · ~10 min · reading and practice看清为什么读过的东西大多会淡忘，以及什么能让记忆留得久 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Forgetting is the default第 1 步 · 遗忘是默认状态

Learn先学

Read something once and most of it fades within days. The loss is steepest at the start, then slows. This is normal, not a sign that you are bad at learning.

读一遍的内容，几天内大半会淡忘。一开始掉得最快，之后才变慢。这很正常，不代表你不擅长学习。

Every time you pull the idea back out of your head, the memory lasts longer before it fades again. Learning is not one act of reading, but a series of pulls.

每一次你把这个想法从脑子里重新取出来，它再次淡忘之前能撑得更久。学习不是读一次，而是一连串的“取出”。

Read once, never again -> mostly gone in a week Read once, tested next day -> still there at one week Tested after 1, 3, 7 and 14 days -> still there months later

Step 2 · Familiar is not known第 2 步 · 眼熟不等于会

Learn先学

Rereading feels productive because the page is right in front of you, so everything looks familiar. Familiar is a feeling. Knowing means you can produce the idea with the page closed.

重读让人觉得有收获，因为内容就摆在眼前，所以一切都眼熟。“眼熟”只是一种感觉。“会”是指合上书之后你自己也能说出来。

Step 3 · A little struggle is the signal第 3 步 · 一点吃力就是信号

Learn先学

If recalling something took effort and you nearly missed it, that effort is what strengthens the memory. If it came instantly and felt easy, it may already be solid, or you may only be recognising it.

如果回想某件事时费了劲、差点没想起来，这份吃力恰恰在加固记忆。如果它瞬间冒出来、毫不费力，说明要么已经很牢，要么你只是在认出它。

So do not pick study methods only because they feel smooth. Pick the ones that make you retrieve.

所以别因为“顺手”才选学习方法，要选那些逼你把内容取出来的。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Most of what you read once fades within days.
• Each time you retrieve an idea, it lasts longer.
• Familiar is a feeling; knowing means producing it with the page closed.
• A little struggle in recall is a good sign.

• 读一遍的内容，几天内大半会淡忘。
• 每取出一次，记忆就留得更久。
• 眼熟是感觉；会，是合上页面也说得出。
• 回想时有点吃力，是好信号。
