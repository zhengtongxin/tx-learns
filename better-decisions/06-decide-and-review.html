Decide and review决定与复盘

Write the decision and why, set a time to look back, and learn from the result · ~10 min · reading and practice写下决定和理由，定好回头看的时间，并从结果中学习 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Write it down第 1 步 · 写下来

Learn先学

When you decide, write three things: what you chose, why, and what you assumed. Memory rewrites itself to make us look sensible, so a note made at the time is the only fair record. It also lets the team act on the decision without asking you again, and lets a newcomer see the reasoning, not just the result.

决定的时候写下三样：选了什么、为什么、假设了什么。记忆会自己改写，让我们显得合理，所以当时写下的笔记是唯一公平的记录。它还让团队不用再问你就能执行，也让新来的人看到推理，而不只是结果。

Decision: rebuild only the three worst pages. Why: fastest, fits the budget, keeps the rest stable. Assumed: the other pages are good enough. Look back on: end of next month.

Step 2 · Set a time to look back第 2 步 · 定好回头看的时间

Learn先学

A decision you never revisit teaches you nothing. Pick a date and a signal: “if sign-ups have not risen by then, we reconsider”. This also saves you from clinging to a choice out of pride, because you agreed in advance what would make you change direction.

从不回头看的决定，什么也教不了你。选一个日期和一个信号：“到那时注册数还没涨，我们就重新考虑”。这也让你不至于出于面子死守一个选择，因为你事先就约定好了什么情况下转向。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Judge the process第 3 步 · 评判过程

Learn先学

A good decision can have a bad result, and a bad decision can get lucky. If you judge only by results, you learn the wrong lessons. When you review, ask: did we frame the question, see real options, separate facts from guesses and check the risks? If yes and it still went badly, you may have been unlucky. If not, that is what to fix next time.

好决定可能碰上坏结果，坏决定也可能运气好。如果只看结果，你学到的会是错的教训。复盘时问：我们有没有说清问题、看过真正的选项、分清事实与猜测、检查过风险？如果都做了，结果仍然不好，也许只是运气差；如果没做，那就是下次要改的地方。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write the decision, the reasons and the assumptions.
• Set a date and a signal to look back.
• Judge the process, not just the result.

• 写下决定、理由和假设。
• 定好回头看的日期和信号。
• 评判过程，而不只是结果。
