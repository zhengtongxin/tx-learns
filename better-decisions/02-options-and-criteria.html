Options and criteria选项与标准

List real options, include “do nothing”, and score each against your criteria · ~10 min · reading and practice列出真正的选项（包括“什么都不做”），再按标准逐项打分 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · More than two options第 1 步 · 不止两个选项

Learn先学

A choice between exactly two options is often a sign that you stopped looking too early. Try to find at least three: a bigger version, a smaller version, a different approach. Ask “what would we do if this option were not allowed?” and a new one often appears. You do not need to like them all; you need to have seen them.

只有两个选项的选择，往往说明你过早停止了寻找。试着找出至少三个：大一点的做法、小一点的做法、完全不同的做法。问一句“如果这个选项不允许，我们会怎么办？”，新的选项常常就出现了。你不必喜欢每一个，但要都看过。

Step 2 · Do nothing is an option第 2 步 · “不做”也是选项

Learn先学

Always include “do nothing” or “keep things as they are”. It is the baseline every other option must beat, and it has costs too: problems that continue, chances that pass. Putting it on the list also stops a change from winning only because it feels like progress.

一定要把“什么都不做”或“维持现状”列进去。它是其他所有选项必须胜过的基准，而且它也有代价：问题持续、机会流走。把它列出来，还能避免某个改变只是因为“看起来在进步”而胜出。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Score against criteria第 3 步 · 按标准打分

Learn先学

Draw a small table: options down the side, criteria across the top. Give each cell a simple mark such as good, fair or poor, then look at the pattern rather than adding up numbers. If a criterion is a must-have, an option that fails it is out, however well it scores elsewhere. Do not hide a doubtful judgement inside a precise-looking number.

画一张小表：选项排在左边，标准排在上面。每格给一个简单的评价，如好、一般、差，然后看整体的样子，而不是硬把数字相加。如果某条标准是“必须满足”，不满足它的选项就出局，哪怕别处得分再高。别把不确定的判断藏进一个看起来很精确的数字里。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Look for at least three options.
• Keep “do nothing” as the baseline.
• Drop options that fail a must-have.

• 至少找三个选项。
• 把“不做”留作基准。
• 淘汰不满足必要条件的选项。
