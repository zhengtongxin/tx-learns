Deciding with doubt · Lesson 4带着疑问做决定 · 第 4 课

Deciding together一起做决定

Name one owner, hear views and reasons before you count votes, and close the decision in writing · ~9 min · reading and practice指定一位负责人，先听意见和理由再数票，并以书面收尾 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Decide who decides第 1 步 · 先定谁来拍板

Learn先学

A group can discuss a decision for an hour and leave without one. Before the talk, name one owner: the person who makes the final call after hearing the others. The rest advise. This is not about rank. It is about making sure that when the talk ends, someone is clearly responsible for the answer. Say it at the start: “We will hear everyone, then Mei decides by Friday.”

一群人可以讨论一个小时，却没有得出结论。谈之前，先指定一位负责人：听完大家的意见后，由这个人做最后的决定，其他人提供建议。这与职位高低无关，而是为了在讨论结束时，有人明确对答案负责。开头就说清楚：“我们先听大家的，然后由 Mei 在周五前决定。”

Step 2 · Hear views before you share yours第 2 步 · 先听，再说自己的

Learn先学

People copy the first voice they hear. Ask everyone to write their view down, then share them together. Ask for the reason as well as the choice. Suppose 5 people look at two options and 3 pick A and 2 pick B: that is 3 ÷ 5 = 60% for A. But the 2 may hold a fact the 3 missed, so listen to why before you count. A vote counts opinions, and it does not check them.

人们会跟随听到的第一个声音。请每个人先把自己的看法写下来，再一起分享。除了选择，也要问理由。假设 5 个人看两个方案，3 人选 A，2 人选 B：支持 A 的占 3 ÷ 5 = 60%。但那 2 个人可能掌握了另外 3 个人没看到的事实，所以先听理由，再数票数。投票只是统计意见，并不检验意见。

Step 3 · Close it clearly第 3 步 · 把它收尾

Learn先学

Every talk costs people’s time. A meeting of 8 people for 30 minutes is 8 × 30 = 240 minutes, which is 4 hours of work, so make it count. End by saying out loud what was decided, who does what and by when, then send those three lines in writing the same day. If someone disagrees, record it and say why the decision still stands. A decision nobody can find later is only a conversation.

每次讨论都要花大家的时间。8 个人开 30 分钟的会，就是 8 × 30 = 240 分钟，也就是 4 个小时的工作，所以要开得有价值。结束时大声说出决定了什么、谁做什么、什么时候完成，并在当天把这三行写下来发出去。如果有人不同意，记录下来，并说明决定为何仍然成立。事后找不到的决定，只能算一次谈话。

Sort each habit as a good way to decide in a group or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“集体决策的好方式”或“弱方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Name one owner before the talk: the others advise, one person decides.
• Hear written views and reasons before you share yours.
• A vote counts opinions and does not check them: 3 of 5 is 60%, but ask the other 2 why.
• A meeting of 8 people for 30 minutes costs 8 × 30 = 240 minutes.
• Close with what, who and by when, in writing, the same day.

• 谈之前指定一位负责人：其他人提建议，一个人拍板。
• 先听大家写下的看法和理由，再说自己的。
• 投票只是统计意见，并不检验意见：5 人里 3 人是 60%，但要问问另外 2 人为什么。
• 8 个人开 30 分钟的会，代价是 8 × 30 = 240 分钟。
• 用“什么、谁、何时”收尾，当天书面发出。
