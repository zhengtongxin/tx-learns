Common biases常见的思维偏差

Spot four habits of thought that bend decisions, and a simple check for each · ~10 min · reading and practice认出四种会扭曲决定的思维习惯，并为每种配一个简单的检查办法 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Four habits第 1 步 · 四种习惯

Learn先学

Everyone has habits that bend their judgement. Four common ones: confirmation, where you notice facts that agree with you and skip those that do not; anchoring, where the first number you hear sticks and colours everything after; sunk cost, where you keep going because of what you already spent; and overconfidence, where your estimate range is far too narrow. Knowing the names helps you catch yourself.

每个人都有会扭曲判断的习惯。四种常见的：确认偏差，只注意到与你一致的事实，漏掉不一致的；锚定，先听到的数字一直留在脑中，影响之后的一切；沉没成本，因为已经花了很多就继续下去；过度自信，估计的范围远远太窄。知道这些名字，有助于你抓住自己。

Step 2 · A check for each第 2 步 · 各有一个检查办法

Learn先学

For confirmation, ask “what would convince me I am wrong?” and go look for it. For anchoring, make your own estimate before you see anyone else’s number. For sunk cost, ask “if I were starting today, with what I know, would I choose this?” For overconfidence, widen your range until you would really be surprised to be outside it.

对确认偏差，问“什么会让我相信自己错了？”然后去找。对锚定，在看到别人的数字之前先做自己的估计。对沉没成本，问“如果今天重新开始，凭现在知道的，我还会选这个吗？”对过度自信，把范围放宽，直到落在范围之外真会让你吃惊。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Invite disagreement第 3 步 · 邀请反对意见

Learn先学

Others can see what you cannot, but only if they feel free to say it. Ask one person to argue against your plan, or ask “what is the strongest reason not to do this?” before you commit. Treat their answer as information, not as an attack. If nobody disagrees with you, that is not a sign you are right; it may be a sign nobody felt safe saying so.

别人能看到你看不到的东西，但前提是他们敢说。请一个人专门反驳你的计划，或者在投入之前问一句“不该这样做的最强理由是什么？”把对方的回答当作信息，而不是攻击。没有人反对你，并不说明你对；也许只是没人觉得安全、敢说出口。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Name the four habits so you can catch them.
• Use one quick check against each.
• Invite disagreement on purpose.

• 记住四种习惯的名字，才能抓住它们。
• 每种用一个快速检查。
• 主动邀请反对意见。
