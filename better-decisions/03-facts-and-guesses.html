Thinking it through · Lesson 3想清楚 · 第 3 课

Facts and guesses事实与猜测

Separate what you know from what you assume, and test the assumptions that matter most · ~10 min · reading and practice把已知和假设分开，再去验证最关键的假设 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Fact or assumption第 1 步 · 事实还是假设

Learn先学

A fact is something you can point to: a number from a report, something you saw, a quote from a named person. An assumption is something you believe without having checked: “customers will like it”, “it will take two weeks”. Both are useful, but they deserve different trust. Label each statement in your notes, because a pile of assumptions can look like knowledge once it has been repeated enough.

事实是你能指出来源的东西：报告里的数字、亲眼所见、具名的人说过的话。假设是你相信但还没核实的东西，比如“客户会喜欢”“两周能做完”。两者都有用，但可信程度不同。在笔记里给每条陈述做个标记，因为一堆假设被反复提起之后，看起来会像是知识。

Step 2 · Which assumption matters第 2 步 · 哪条假设最关键

Learn先学

You cannot check everything, so ask: if this assumption were wrong, would my choice change? If yes, it is a key assumption and worth testing. If the choice would stay the same, leave it. Often one or two assumptions carry the whole decision, such as “people will pay for this” or “the supplier can deliver on time”.

你不可能什么都核实，所以问：如果这条假设错了，我的选择会变吗？会变，它就是关键假设，值得验证；不会变，就先放着。往往一两条假设就撑起了整个决定，比如“用户愿意为此付钱”或“供应商能按时交付”。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Check it cheaply第 3 步 · 低成本验证

Learn先学

Look for the smallest test that could prove the key assumption wrong: ask three customers, run a one-page trial, read the supplier’s past record. A quick check that says “no” saves far more than it costs. Decide in advance what result would change your mind, otherwise you will explain away any answer.

找一个最小的、可能证明关键假设是错的测试：问三位客户，做一次单页试验，查看供应商过往的记录。一次说“不”的快速检查，省下的远比花掉的多。事先定好什么结果会改变你的想法，否则无论得到什么答案，你都会找理由解释过去。

Assumption: customers will pay for the new feature. Test: show the price to five customers. Changes my mind if: fewer than two say yes.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Label facts and assumptions.
• Test the assumption the choice depends on.
• Set the bar before you test.

• 给事实和假设做标记。
• 验证选择所依赖的那条假设。
• 测试之前先定门槛。
