Risk and reversibility风险与可逆性

Ask what could go wrong, how bad it would be, and whether you could undo the choice · ~10 min · reading and practice问清楚可能出什么问题、有多严重、做错了能不能撤回 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Likely and costly第 1 步 · 可能性和代价

Learn先学

A risk has two sides: how likely it is, and how much damage it would do. A small mishap that happens often can cost more in total than a rare, mild one, and a very unlikely disaster can still deserve attention if it cannot be repaired. List what could go wrong, give each a rough “likely, possible or unlikely” and “small, medium or large”, and look first at the large ones.

风险有两面：发生的可能性，以及造成的损害。常发生的小意外，总损失可能比罕见的轻微事故还大；而一个极不可能的灾难，如果无法修复，也值得认真对待。列出可能出错的事，给每件粗略标上“可能、有可能、不太可能”和“小、中、大”，先看那些大的。

Step 2 · Two-way or one-way第 2 步 · 可撤回还是不可撤回

Learn先学

Some choices are two-way doors: if they do not work, you step back through and little is lost, such as trying a new tool for a month. Others are one-way doors: signing a long contract, announcing a date publicly, deleting data. Two-way choices should be made quickly, with limited information. One-way choices deserve more time and a second pair of eyes.

有些选择像一扇可以双向通行的门：行不通就退回来，损失很小，比如试用一个新工具一个月。另一些是单向门：签长期合同、公开宣布日期、删除数据。可撤回的选择应当快速决定，信息够用就行；不可撤回的选择值得多花时间，再找个人帮你看一遍。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Care that matches the stakes第 3 步 · 谨慎程度与风险匹配

Learn先学

Slow decisions have a cost too. Spending a week choosing a lunch place is waste, and spending an hour on a contract is a gamble. A simple rule: the harder it is to undo and the larger the damage, the more checking you do. When you cannot undo it, ask what you would do if it went wrong, and whether a small first step would give you the answer more safely.

慢本身也有代价。花一周挑午餐餐厅是浪费，花一小时看合同则是赌博。一个简单的规则：越难撤回、损害越大，就越要多检查。无法撤回时，问问自己出了问题怎么办，以及能不能先走一小步，更安全地得到答案。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Judge risk by likelihood and damage.
• Decide two-way choices quickly.
• Take more care over one-way choices.

• 用可能性和损害判断风险。
• 可撤回的选择快速决定。
• 不可撤回的选择多花心思。
