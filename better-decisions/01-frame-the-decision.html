Frame the decision说清楚要决定什么

Say what is being decided, by when, by whom and what “good” would look like · ~10 min · reading and practice先说清楚：要决定什么、何时决定、谁来决定、怎样才算好 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Write it as a question第 1 步 · 写成一个问题

Learn先学

Many decisions go badly because nobody agreed what the decision was. “We need to sort out the website” is a topic, not a decision. “Should we rebuild the website this year or fix the three worst pages?” is a decision: it has a question mark, and two answers you can compare. Write the question in one sentence before you collect any facts.

很多决定出问题，是因为没人说清楚到底要决定什么。“我们得把网站弄一弄”只是个话题，不是决定。“今年是重做网站，还是只修最差的三个页面？”才是决定：它有问号，还有两个可以比较的答案。收集任何事实之前，先用一句话写下这个问题。

Step 2 · Who, by when第 2 步 · 谁来定、何时定

Learn先学

Every decision needs one owner and one deadline. Without an owner, everyone waits for someone else. Without a deadline, the decision drifts until events make it for you. Also note who must be asked and who must only be told, so the right people are involved without turning it into a vote of ten.

每个决定都需要一个负责人和一个期限。没有负责人，大家都在等别人；没有期限，决定会一直拖，直到事态替你做了决定。还要写明谁必须被征求意见、谁只需被告知，这样该参与的人都在，又不至于变成十个人投票。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · What does good look like第 3 步 · 怎样才算好

Learn先学

Before you compare options, write two or three things a good outcome must have, for example “live before the busy season” or “costs no more than the current system”. These are your criteria, and fixing them early stops you from bending them later to suit the option you already like.

比较选项之前，先写两三条好结果必须具备的东西，例如“在旺季前上线”或“成本不超过现有系统”。这些就是你的标准。提前定下，就不会事后为了迎合自己已经偏爱的选项而改标准。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write the decision as a question.
• Give it one owner and one deadline.
• Fix your criteria before you compare.

• 把决定写成一个问题。
• 给它一个负责人和一个期限。
• 比较之前先定标准。
