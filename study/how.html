Learn by being tested被考，才学得会

Reading feels like learning. Being quizzed shows what you actually know. This is the loop TX uses, and the study coach runs it for you.读完会觉得自己学会了，被考一下才知道真正会什么。这是 TX 自己用的循环，学习教练替你跑完它。

  1. Hand in your material交出资料Slides and PDFs, in English or 中文. They stay on your device.课件和 PDF，中英文都行。资料只留在你的设备上。
  2. Get quizzed to diagnose出题诊断The AI writes original questions from your material. A first round shows where you stand.AI 根据你的资料出原创题。第一轮告诉你现在在哪。
  3. Find the weak spots找出薄弱点Every answer updates a strength map by knowledge area, down to the passages you missed.每次作答都会更新按知识领域划分的强弱图，细到你答错的段落。
  4. Drill by the exam weight按考纲比例刷题If the goal is a certificate, questions follow the exam outline's content weighting, tilted toward your weak areas.目标是考证时，出题比例跟随考纲的内容占比，并向你的弱项倾斜。
  5. Re-analyse and repeat重新分析，再来一轮The weak spots are recomputed after each round and a short daily plan says what to practise today.每轮之后重新计算弱项，并用一份简短的每日计划告诉你今天练什么。
  • Free, private. The AI is an open model that runs in your browser. No login and no server: your records live in this browser, with export and import to back up or move.免费、私密。AI 是在你浏览器里运行的开源模型。不用登录，也没有服务器：记录只在这个浏览器里，可以导出、导入来备份或换设备。
  • Plain limits. Desktop Chrome or Edge, a one-time download of about 1 to 2 GB, text only, and a smaller model than the big chat apps, so check answers you doubt.说清限制。电脑版 Chrome 或 Edge，首次下载约 1 到 2 GB，只读文字，模型比大型聊天应用小，有疑问的答案请自己核对。

Under the hood背后的原理

  • Read. Your PDF, PPTX or text is read on your device and cut into short passages.读取。你的 PDF、PPTX 或文本在你的设备上读取，切成短段落。
  • Match. For an exam goal, each passage is matched to an exam area by topic words, so questions follow the outline’s shares.匹配。考证目标下，按主题词把每个段落对应到考纲的某个领域，让题目比例跟随考纲。
  • Ask. An open model in your browser writes one original multiple-choice question from a passage, while you answer the one before.出题。浏览器里的开源模型根据一个段落写一道原创选择题，在你答上一题时就已经在写下一题。
  • Weigh. Each answer updates that area’s strength, newest answers counting most, and remembers the passage you missed.计分。每次作答都会更新该领域的强度，越新的答案权重越大，并记住你答错的段落。
  • Plan. Weak, untested and overdue areas get more questions in today’s plan; a mini exam follows the outline’s shares exactly.计划。弱项、没测过和该复习的领域在今天的计划里题更多；小测验则严格按考纲比例出题。
