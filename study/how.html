How a study round works一轮学习是怎么跑的

Six steps, then the full system, left to right. Hover or tap an agent for details.先是六步，再是完整系统，从左往右读。移上去或点一下 agent 看详情。

  1. 1

    Hand in material交出资料

    Your slides and PDFs are read on your device and cut into short passages.你的课件和 PDF 在你的设备上读取，切成短段落。

    you你
  2. 2

    Get quizzed出题诊断

    An open model writes one original question per passage, while you answer the one before.开源模型为每个段落写一道原创题，在你答上一题时就已经在写下一题。

    aiAI
  3. 3

    Find weak spots找出薄弱点

    Each answer is marked and updates a strength map, down to the passage you missed.每个答案都会批改，并更新强弱图，细到你答错的段落。

    aiAI
  4. 4

    Drill by weight按比例刷题

    With an exam goal you enter the exam’s areas and weights (or pick a ready-made outline), and questions follow those shares, tilted toward your weak areas.选考试目标时，你填写该考试的领域和比例（或选现成考纲），题目按这些比例出，并向你的弱项倾斜。

    aiAI
  5. 5

    Re-analyse重新分析

    After each round the plan is rebuilt: today’s questions, weakest first.每轮之后重建计划：今天的题目，最弱的排在前面。

    aiAI
  6. 6

    Turn it into a track变成一条路线

    Optional. After you read it, one click sends an outline, key terms and your own notes to GitHub, and the site’s content agent writes a track from them. You read it all first.可选。你看过之后，点一下就把提纲、关键词和你自己的笔记发到 GitHub，站点的内容 agent 据此写成一条路线。

    you你

    Everything above runs in your browser. Nothing is sent to a server.以上一切都在你的浏览器里运行，不会发给任何服务器。
