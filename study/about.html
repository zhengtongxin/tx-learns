About this site关于本站

The study coach turns one person’s way of learning for exams into a free tool anyone can use.学习教练把一个人备考的学习方法做成了人人可用的免费工具。

01

Purpose宗旨

TX learns by being tested, not by rereading. The study coach runs that habit for you on your own slides and PDFs: it quizzes you, finds what you do not know yet and plans what to practise next.TX 靠被考来学，而不是反复重读。学习教练把这个习惯用在你自己的课件和 PDF 上：出题考你，找出你还不会的，并安排接下来练什么。

02

Privacy隐私

Everything happens in your browser. Nothing about you, your files or your answers is sent to us or anyone else unless you choose to share notes.一切都在你的浏览器里完成。关于你、你的文件和你的答案，除非你选择分享笔记，否则什么都不会发给我们或任何人。

  • Your files你的文件Read on your device. Their text is kept only in this browser.在你的设备上读取，文字只保存在这个浏览器里。
  • The AIAI 模型An open model your own browser runs. Only the model files download, once, when you ask.由你自己的浏览器运行的开源模型。只在你点击时下载一次模型文件。
  • Sharing分享Only if you press the share button: your own written notes open on GitHub for you to submit. Your files are never included, and what you submit becomes public.只有你点分享按钮才会发生：你自己写的笔记会在 GitHub 上打开，由你提交。你的文件不会被带上，提交的内容会公开。
  • Your records你的记录Answers and weak spots live in this browser. Export them to back up or move to another device.答案和弱项保存在这个浏览器里。可导出来备份或换设备。
  • Not collected不收集No login, no cookies, no tracking.不用登录，不使用 Cookie，不追踪。
03

Limits限制

  • Browser浏览器Desktop Chrome or Edge, because the model needs WebGPU.电脑版 Chrome 或 Edge，因为模型需要 WebGPU。
  • Download下载About 1 to 2 GB the first time.首次约 1 到 2 GB。
  • Quality质量A small model: some questions will be wrong. Mark them and check what you doubt.模型较小，有些题会出错。请标记它们，并核对你存疑的地方。
  • Content内容Text only. Scanned PDFs and pictures are not read.只读文字，扫描版 PDF 和图片读不了。
