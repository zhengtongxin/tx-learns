Vectors and similarity向量与相似度

Treat a list of numbers as a point, and measure how alike two of them are · ~10 min · reading and practice把一列数字当作空间里的一个点，并衡量两个点有多相似 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A list of numbers as a point第 1 步 · 一列数字就是一个点

Learn先学

A vector is just a list of numbers, read as a point, or an arrow from the start, in space. [3, 4] is a point 3 across and 4 up. Models turn words, images and sentences into long vectors with hundreds of numbers, so that “alike” things end up close together. You cannot picture 300 directions, but the same arithmetic works on any length.

向量就是一列数字，被看作空间里的一个点，或者从原点出发的一支箭头。[3, 4] 是横向 3、纵向 4 的点。模型把词、图片和句子变成有几百个数字的长向量，让“相像”的东西彼此靠近。你无法想象 300 个方向，但同样的算术对任意长度都成立。

import math v = [3, 4] print(math.sqrt(v[0] ** 2 + v[1] ** 2)) # distance from the start

Step 2 · The dot product第 2 步 · 点积

Learn先学

The dot product of two vectors multiplies them position by position and adds the results: for [1, 2] and [3, 4] that is 1×3 + 2×4 = 11. It is large when the two point the same way, zero when they are at right angles, and negative when they point apart. It is the building block of most similarity scores.

两个向量的点积，是把它们按位置对应相乘，再把结果相加：[1, 2] 和 [3, 4] 的点积是 1×3 + 2×4 = 11。两者方向一致时它很大，成直角时为零，方向相反时为负。它是大多数相似度分数的基本构件。

a = [1, 2] b = [3, 4] print(sum(x * y for x, y in zip(a, b))) # zip pairs them up by position

Step 3 · Cosine similarity第 3 步 · 余弦相似度

Learn先学

Dividing the dot product by both lengths gives the cosine similarity: 1 means the same direction, 0 means unrelated, and −1 means opposite. It ignores how long the vectors are and only compares direction, which is why it works for text, where a long document and a short one on the same topic should still count as alike. Search by meaning compares your question’s vector with stored ones and returns the highest scores.

把点积除以两个向量各自的长度，就得到余弦相似度：1 表示方向相同，0 表示不相关，−1 表示相反。它忽略向量有多长，只比较方向，所以适用于文本：同一话题的一篇长文和一篇短文，仍应被视为相像。按含义搜索，就是拿你问题的向量和库里存的向量比较，返回得分最高的。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A vector is a list of numbers read as a point.
• The dot product is large when two point the same way.
• Cosine similarity compares direction: 1 is alike, 0 is unrelated.

• 向量是一列被当作点的数字。
• 两者方向一致时，点积很大。
• 余弦相似度比较方向：1 是相像，0 是不相关。
