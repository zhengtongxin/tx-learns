Percentages and rates百分比与比率

Read percentages, changes and rates correctly, and know what each one is a percentage of · ~10 min · reading and practice正确理解百分比、变化率和比率，并知道每个百分比是相对于谁的 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A part of a whole第 1 步 · 整体的一部分

Learn先学

A percentage is a part of a whole, written out of 100. To find it, divide the part by the whole and multiply by 100: 120 correct answers out of 400 is 120 ÷ 400 × 100 = 30%. Everything depends on the whole you chose. Fifty out of a hundred and fifty out of three hundred are both “half” in different ways, and mixing up the whole is the most common mistake.

百分比是整体中的一部分，以 100 为基数来表示。算法是用部分除以整体，再乘以 100：400 题答对 120 题，就是 120 ÷ 400 × 100 = 30%。一切都取决于你选的整体。一百里的五十和三百里的一百五十都是“一半”，但含义不同，搞混整体是最常见的错误。

correct = 120 total = 400 print(correct / total * 100) # part divided by whole, times 100

Step 2 · Percentage change第 2 步 · 百分比变化

Learn先学

A change is measured against the starting value: (new − old) ÷ old × 100. Percent changes do not undo each other. A price that rises 10% and then falls 10% does not return to where it began, because the second change is measured against a larger number. The same trap hides in “accuracy went up by 5 points” versus “by 5%”: points are a plain difference, percent is relative.

变化是相对起点来衡量的：(新值 − 旧值) ÷ 旧值 × 100。百分比变化不能互相抵消。价格先涨 10% 再跌 10%，并不会回到原点，因为第二次变化是相对更大的数计算的。“准确率提高了 5 个百分点”和“提高了 5%”也藏着同样的陷阱：百分点是直接的差，百分比是相对的变化。

price = 100 price = price * 1.10 # up 10% price = price * 0.90 # down 10% print(round(price, 1))

Step 3 · A percentage of what第 3 步 · 是什么的百分之几

Learn先学

Always ask what the percentage is of. “A test that is 99% accurate” can still be wrong about most of its positives if the thing it looks for is rare: when only 1 person in 1,000 has a condition, even a small false-alarm rate produces more false alarms than true cases. A rate (such as 3 per 1,000) names both the count and the group, so it is harder to misread than a bare percentage. Sort each statement.

永远要问这个百分比是相对什么的。“准确率 99% 的检测”，如果要找的东西很罕见，仍然可能大部分阳性结果是错的：当一千人里只有一人有某种情况，哪怕误报率很小，产生的误报也会比真实病例多。比率（比如每千人 3 例）同时说明了数量和群体，比单独一个百分比更不容易被误读。给每条说法分类。
