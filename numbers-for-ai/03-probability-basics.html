Probability basics概率入门

Count outcomes to find how likely something is, and combine chances without fooling yourself · ~10 min · reading and practice通过数结果来判断可能性有多大，并在组合概率时避免被自己骗 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Chance is a fraction第 1 步 · 概率是一个分数

Learn先学

The probability of something is a number from 0 (never) to 1 (always). When all outcomes are equally likely, it is the number of outcomes you want divided by the number of all outcomes. One roll of a fair die has six outcomes, so the chance of rolling a 4 is 1 ÷ 6, about 0.167. Probabilities also work as percentages: 0.167 is 16.7%.

某件事的概率是一个从 0（绝不发生）到 1（必然发生）的数。当所有结果等可能时，概率就是你想要的结果数除以全部结果数。一枚公平骰子掷一次有六种结果，所以掷出 4 的概率是 1 ÷ 6，约 0.167。概率也可以写成百分比：0.167 就是 16.7%。

Step 2 · Independent chances multiply第 2 步 · 独立事件的概率相乘

Learn先学

Two events are independent if one does not affect the other, such as two separate dice rolls. The chance that both happen is the product of their chances: two sixes in a row is 1/6 × 1/6, about 0.028. This is why a long chain of steps, each fairly reliable, can be unreliable overall: ten steps that each work 90% of the time work together only about 35% of the time.

如果一件事不影响另一件事，两者就是独立的，比如两次分开的掷骰子。两件事都发生的概率等于各自概率的乘积：连续两次掷出 6 的概率是 1/6 × 1/6，约 0.028。这也是为什么一长串步骤，每一步都相当可靠，整体却可能不可靠：十个每步成功率 90% 的步骤，一起成功的概率只有约 35%。

Step 3 · At least one第 3 步 · 至少一次

Learn先学

“At least one” is easiest by turning it around: the chance of at least one success is 1 minus the chance of no success at all. The chance of no six in two rolls is 5/6 × 5/6; so at least one six is 1 − (5/6)², about 0.306, not 2/6. Adding chances like that double-counts the case where both happen. Use this whenever the question says “at least once” or “any”.

“至少一次”最容易的算法是反过来想：至少成功一次的概率，等于 1 减去一次都不成功的概率。两次掷骰子都没有 6 的概率是 5/6 × 5/6；所以至少一个 6 的概率是 1 − (5/6)²，约 0.306，而不是 2/6。那样直接相加，会把两次都发生的情况重复算了。凡是问题里说“至少一次”或“任何一个”，都用这个办法。

Takeaways要点回顾

• “At least one” is 1 minus the chance of none.
