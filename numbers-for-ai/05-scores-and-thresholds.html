Scores and thresholds分数与阈值

Turn a model score into a yes or no, and judge it with precision and recall · ~10 min · reading and practice把模型分数变成“是”或“否”，并用精确率和召回率来评价 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A score is not a decision第 1 步 · 分数不是决定

Learn先学

Many systems output a score, such as 0.83 for “this email is spam”. To act, you choose a threshold: everything at or above it counts as a yes. A low threshold catches more of what you want but also lets in more false alarms; a high one is stricter but misses more. Where to draw the line is a choice about which mistake costs more, not something the model decides.

许多系统输出分数，比如“这封邮件是垃圾邮件”得 0.83。要行动，就得选一个阈值：达到或高于它的都算“是”。阈值低，能抓到更多想要的，但也放进更多误报；阈值高，更严格，但漏掉更多。线划在哪里，是在选择哪种错误代价更大，不是模型能替你决定的。

scores = [0.9, 0.7, 0.4, 0.2] threshold = 0.5 flagged = [s for s in scores if s >= threshold] # keep scores at or above it print(len(flagged))

Step 2 · Hits, misses and false alarms第 2 步 · 命中、漏掉和误报

Learn先学

Compare the flagged items with the truth and count three things: true positives (flagged and really positive), false positives (flagged, but not), and false negatives (missed real ones). For a spam filter, a false positive is a good email in the spam folder, and a false negative is spam in your inbox.

把被标记的项目和真实情况对照，数三样东西：真阳性（被标记且确实是阳性）、假阳性（被标记，但其实不是）、假阴性（漏掉的真实阳性）。对垃圾邮件过滤器来说，假阳性是一封正常邮件被放进垃圾箱，假阴性是垃圾邮件出现在收件箱。

Step 3 · Precision and recall第 3 步 · 精确率与召回率

Learn先学

Precision asks: of everything flagged, how much was right? It is true positives ÷ (true positives + false positives). Recall asks: of everything real, how much did we catch? It is true positives ÷ (true positives + false negatives). Raising the threshold usually lifts precision and lowers recall, and lowering it does the opposite. Quoting one without the other can make a poor system look good. Sort each need.

精确率问：被标记的里面，有多少是对的？等于真阳性 ÷（真阳性 + 假阳性）。召回率问：真正存在的里面，我们抓到了多少？等于真阳性 ÷（真阳性 + 假阴性）。提高阈值通常会提高精确率、降低召回率，降低阈值则相反。只报其中一个，会让一个不好的系统看起来很好。给每种需求分类。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A threshold turns a score into a yes or no.
• Precision: of the flagged, how many are right. Recall: of the real, how many were caught.
• Raising the threshold usually trades recall for precision.

• 阈值把分数变成“是”或“否”。
• 精确率：被标记的里有多少是对的。召回率：真实的里抓到多少。
• 提高阈值通常是用召回率换精确率。
