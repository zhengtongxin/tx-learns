Averages and spread平均数与离散程度

Summarise a list of numbers with a typical value and how far values stray from it · ~10 min · reading and practice用一个典型值和数值偏离它的程度来概括一列数字 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The mean and the median第 1 步 · 平均数与中位数

Learn先学

One number can stand for a whole list. The mean adds everything up and divides by how many there are. The median is the middle value once the list is sorted from smallest to largest; with an even count, it is halfway between the two middle values. Both answer “what is typical?”, but they answer it differently.

一个数字可以代表一整列数据。平均数是把所有数加起来，再除以个数。中位数是把数据从小到大排好后，最中间的那个值；个数是偶数时，取中间两个数的中点。两者都在回答“什么算典型”，但回答的方式不同。

pay = [30, 32, 31, 300] # four salaries, in thousands mean = sum(pay) / len(pay) # sum() adds them, len() counts them middle = sorted(pay) # smallest to largest median = (middle[1] + middle[2]) / 2 # the two middle values print(round(mean, 2), median) # round(x, 2) keeps two decimals

Step 2 · When one value misleads第 2 步 · 当一个值误导了你

Learn先学

One very large or very small value, an outlier, drags the mean toward it while the median barely moves. In the salaries above, three people earn about 31 and one earns 300. The mean says “about 98”, which describes nobody. When a few extreme values exist, report the median too, or look at the list itself before you trust the mean.

一个特别大或特别小的值，也就是离群值，会把平均数拉向它，而中位数几乎不动。上面的工资里，三个人拿 31 左右，一个人拿 300。平均数说“约 98”，这个数字描述不了任何人。有极端值时，要同时报告中位数，或者先看看数据本身，再相信平均数。

Step 3 · How spread out is it第 3 步 · 数据有多分散

Learn先学

Two lists can share a mean and still differ a lot. Spread says how far values stray. The simplest measure is the range: the largest value minus the smallest. A small range means values cluster; a large range means they scatter. Always give a typical value together with its spread, because the typical value alone hides how much trust it deserves.

两列数据可以平均数相同，却大不一样。离散程度说的是数值偏离得有多远。最简单的度量是极差：最大值减去最小值。极差小，数值聚在一起；极差大，数值很分散。给出典型值时总要同时给出离散程度，因为只有典型值，会掩盖它值得多少信任。

Takeaways要点回顾

• The mean adds and divides; the median is the middle.
• An outlier drags the mean but barely moves the median.
• Give a typical value together with its spread.

• 平均数是加起来再除；中位数是中间那个。
• 离群值会拉动平均数，却几乎不动中位数。
• 给出典型值时，同时给出离散程度。
