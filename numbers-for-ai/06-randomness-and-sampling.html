Randomness and sampling随机与抽样

Use random choices on purpose, make them repeatable, and judge when a sample can stand for everything · ~10 min · reading and practice有意识地使用随机选择，让它们可重复，并判断样本何时能代表整体 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Random, but repeatable第 1 步 · 随机，但可重复

Learn先学

Models use randomness on purpose: to pick among likely next words, to shuffle training data, to split examples into practice and test sets. Computers produce “random” numbers from a starting number called a seed. Give the same seed and you get the same sequence every time, which is how an experiment can be re-run exactly. No seed means a different result on each run, which is fine for a game and bad for a test you need to repeat.

模型有意使用随机性：在可能的下一个词中挑选、打乱训练数据、把样例分成练习集和测试集。计算机用一个叫种子的起始数字产生“随机”数。给同样的种子，每次得到同样的序列，实验才能被精确重跑。不设种子，每次结果不同——玩游戏没问题，但对需要重复的测试就糟了。

import random random.seed(1) first = [random.randint(1, 6) for _ in range(3)] # three dice rolls random.seed(1) second = [random.randint(1, 6) for _ in range(3)] print(first == second)

Step 2 · Take a sample第 2 步 · 抽取样本

Learn先学

You rarely check everything, so you check a sample and hope it stands for the whole. A fair way is to choose by chance, so each item has an equal shot. Choosing by position, such as “the first 100 rows”, can be biased if the data is ordered, with all the easy cases at the top. In Python, items[::3] takes every third item: the slice form is [start:stop:step].

你很少会检查全部，所以检查一个样本，并希望它能代表整体。公平的办法是靠机会来选，让每一项被选中的机会均等。按位置选，比如“前 100 行”，如果数据是有序的、所有简单的案例都排在最前面，就会有偏差。在 Python 里，items[::3] 取每隔两个的一项：切片的写法是 [起点:终点:步长]。

items = list(range(10)) # 0 to 9 print(items[::3]) # every third item, starting at the first

Step 3 · When a sample misleads第 3 步 · 样本何时会误导

Learn先学

A sample can mislead in two ways. If it is too small, chance dominates: three coin flips can easily give all heads. If it is biased, it leaves out part of what you care about: a survey sent only to people who answer email misses everyone else. More data fixes the first problem, but it does not fix the second, so ask who or what could not end up in the sample.

样本可能在两方面误导你。太小，则机会占主导：抛三次硬币完全可能全是正面。有偏，则漏掉了你关心的一部分：只发给会回邮件的人的调查，会漏掉其他所有人。更多数据能解决第一个问题，却解决不了第二个，所以要问：谁或什么不可能进入样本。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A seed makes random choices repeatable.
• Choose a sample by chance, not by position.
• More data fixes a small sample, not a biased one.

• 种子让随机选择可重复。
• 靠机会选样本，不要按位置选。
• 更多数据能解决样本小，解决不了样本有偏。
