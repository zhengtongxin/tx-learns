Checking AI answers核对 AI 的回答

Treat an AI answer as a draft to verify: look for made-up details, ask for sources and check what matters · ~10 min · reading and practice把 AI 的回答当作需要核对的草稿：找编造的细节、要来源，并核对重要的部分 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Sure and wrong第 1 步 · 很确定，却是错的

Learn先学

An AI model writes the answer that sounds most likely, not the one it has checked. So it can state a wrong fact in the same calm voice as a right one, and it may invent a name, a number or a source. A confident tone tells you nothing about accuracy. Treat the answer as a draft from a fast helper who sometimes makes things up.

AI 模型写出的是听起来最像样的回答，而不是它核对过的回答。所以它可以用同样平静的语气说出错的事实，也可能编出一个名字、一个数字或一个来源。笃定的语气对准确性说明不了什么。把回答当作一个很快、但偶尔会瞎编的助手交来的草稿。

Step 2 · Check the checkable第 2 步 · 核对能核对的部分

Learn先学

Pick out what can be checked: names, dates, numbers, quotes and links. Look each up in a reliable source. Ask the AI where a claim comes from, then open that source yourself, because a listed source may not exist or may not say that. Ask it to say what it is unsure about, and be wary of any answer that shows no doubt on a hard question.

挑出能核对的部分：名字、日期、数字、引文和链接。逐个到可靠的来源去查。问 AI 一个说法出自哪里，然后自己打开那个来源，因为列出的来源可能不存在，或并没有这么说。让它说出哪些地方不确定，对难题完全不带疑问的回答要多留个心眼。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · How much checking第 3 步 · 要核对多少

Learn先学

Match the checking to the stakes. A draft birthday message needs a glance. A health claim, a legal point or a number going into a report needs a real source. Use the AI to help you check as well: ask for the opposite view, or for what would prove the answer wrong. Then confirm with something that is not the AI.

核对的力度要与风险匹配。生日祝福草稿看一眼就够。健康说法、法律问题或要写进报告的数字，就需要真正的来源。也可以让 AI 帮你核对：让它给出相反的观点，或说明什么会证明这个回答是错的。最后用不是 AI 的东西来确认。

AI says: the law changed in 2019, see article 12. Check: does the law exist? Is article 12 about this? Check: open the official text yourself. Stakes: used in a client email, so verify.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Treat AI answers as drafts.
• Check names, numbers and sources yourself.
• Match the checking to the stakes.

• 把 AI 的回答当草稿。
• 自己核对名字、数字和来源。
• 核对力度与风险匹配。
