Numbers and causes · Lesson 2数字与因果 · 第 2 课

Numbers that mislead会误导的数字

Catch common ways a true number can still leave a false impression: missing base, cherry-picking and stretched charts · ~10 min · reading and practice识别真实的数字也能造成错误印象的常见办法：缺了基数、挑着说、拉伸的图表 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Out of how many第 1 步 · 在多少里面

Learn先学

“The risk doubled!” sounds alarming, but doubling from one in a million to two in a million is still tiny. A percentage without the base hides the size of the change. Always ask for the number it is a percentage of, and for the actual counts. The same fact can feel huge or trivial depending on which way it is said.

“风险翻了一倍！”听起来很吓人，但从百万分之一变成百万分之二，依然微乎其微。只给百分比而不给基数，就藏起了变化的大小。要问清它是多少的百分之几，还要问实际的数目。同一件事，换种说法，可以显得巨大，也可以显得微不足道。

Step 2 · What was left out第 2 步 · 被略去的部分

Learn先学

A number can be true and still selective. “Sales grew 20%” may be measured from a bad month. “Nine in ten users are happy” may count only people who stayed. Ask which period, which group and which cases were chosen, and what the full picture would show. Cherry-picking means showing only the cases that fit the story.

数字可以是真的，却有选择性。“销售增长 20%”可能是从一个糟糕的月份算起的。“十个用户里九个满意”可能只算了留下来的人。问问选了哪段时间、哪些人、哪些案例，完整的画面会是什么样。挑着说，就是只展示符合故事的那些案例。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Read the scale first第 3 步 · 先看刻度

Learn先学

A chart can exaggerate with its axis. If a bar chart starts at 90 instead of 0, a small gap looks huge. A line stretched tall makes a mild rise look steep. Read the labels and the numbers on the axis before the shape. Then ask what is being compared, over what period, and whether the numbers are counts or averages.

图表可以靠坐标轴夸大。条形图如果不是从 0 而是从 90 开始，小小的差距就显得巨大。把折线拉得很高，缓和的上升也会显得陡峭。先读标签和轴上的数字，再看形状。然后问：在比什么、比多长时间、数字是总数还是平均数。

Claim: scores jumped! Chart A: axis from 0 to 100: 91 vs 94. Chart B: axis from 90 to 95: 91 vs 94. Same numbers; B looks like a big jump. Check: the labels on the axis.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask what was left out.
