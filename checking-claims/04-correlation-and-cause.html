Numbers and causes · Lesson 1数字与因果 · 第 1 课

Correlation and cause相关与因果

See why two things moving together does not prove that one causes the other, and ask what else could explain it · ~10 min · reading and practice看清两件事一起变化，并不证明其中一件导致另一件，并问还有什么能解释它 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Together is not because第 1 步 · 同时出现不等于因为

Learn先学

Two things can rise and fall together without one causing the other. Ice cream sales and sunburn both go up in summer, but ice cream does not cause sunburn. Both follow the hot weather. When you read “people who do X have more Y”, that is a pattern. It is not yet a reason to think X makes Y happen.

两件事可以一起涨落，却并非一件导致另一件。冰淇淋销量和晒伤人数都在夏天上升，但冰淇淋不会导致晒伤，它们都跟着炎热的天气走。读到“做 X 的人有更多的 Y”时，这只是一种规律，还不能说明 X 会让 Y 发生。

Step 2 · Other explanations第 2 步 · 其他解释

Learn先学

When a claim says one thing causes another, try three alternatives. A third thing may cause both, as with the weather. The direction may be reversed: perhaps people who feel well exercise, rather than exercise making them well. Or it may be chance, especially with a few cases. Naming these does not prove the claim wrong, but it shows what the evidence would have to rule out.

当一个说法称甲导致乙时，试想三种别的可能。可能有第三个因素同时导致两者，比如天气。也可能方向反了：也许是感觉好的人才去运动，而不是运动让人感觉好。还可能只是巧合，尤其是案例很少的时候。说出这些并不证明原说法是错的，但能看出证据必须排除哪些可能。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A fair test第 3 步 · 公平的检验

Learn先学

The best way to test a cause is to change one thing and keep the rest similar: two similar groups, one gets the change, and you compare. Where that is not possible, look for many cases, a plausible way it could work, and results that hold in different places. Be careful with words: “linked to”, “associated with” and “tends to” describe a pattern, while “causes” is a bigger claim.

检验因果最好的办法，是只改变一件事，其他保持相近：两组相似的人，一组做改变，然后比较。做不到的时候，就看是否有大量案例、是否说得通它怎么起作用，以及结果在不同地方是否都成立。留意用词：“与……有关”“与……相关”“往往”描述的是规律，而“导致”是更大的说法。

Pattern: people who drink coffee sleep less. Causes? Maybe coffee cuts sleep. Or: people who sleep less drink more coffee. Or: a busy job causes both. Fair test: change coffee only, compare similar people.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Together is not because.
• Look for a third cause, a reversed direction and chance.
• Prefer fair tests for causes.

• 同时出现不等于因为。
• 看有没有第三因素、方向反了或巧合。
• 检验因果优先用公平的对比。
