Sources and evidence · Lesson 1来源与证据 · 第 1 课

Who is saying it谁在说

Ask who made a claim, how they would know and what they gain, before you decide how far to trust it · ~10 min · reading and practice在相信一个说法之前，先问：谁说的、他怎么会知道、他能得到什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Name the source第 1 步 · 找出来源

Learn先学

Every claim comes from somewhere: a person, a company, a report, a post. “Studies show” and “experts say” name nobody, so you cannot check them. Before you weigh a claim, find the actual source. If you cannot find one, treat the claim as a rumour: it may be true, but nothing yet supports it.

每个说法都有出处：一个人、一家公司、一份报告、一条帖子。“研究表明”“专家说”没有指名任何人，你没法核对。衡量一个说法之前，先找到真正的来源。找不到的话，就当作传闻：可能是真的，但目前没有任何东西支持它。

Step 2 · How could they know第 2 步 · 他怎么会知道

Learn先学

Ask whether the source was in a position to know. A nurse describing her own ward knows more about it than a stranger guessing from outside. A person repeating something they read knows only what the first writer knew. Closeness to the facts, and a record of being right about similar things, are the best signs of a reliable source.

问问来源是否真的处在能知道的位置。护士讲自己病房的情况，比外人隔着墙猜要清楚得多。转述自己读到的东西的人，只知道第一个写的人知道的。离事实近，以及过去在类似的事上说得对，是可靠来源最好的标志。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · What do they gain第 3 步 · 他有什么好处

Learn先学

Notice who benefits if you believe the claim. A seller praising their own product gains from your belief, so their claim needs more checking, not none. This does not make it false: honest sellers exist. It only tells you to look for a second source with nothing to gain. Also look for a date, because old facts can quietly become wrong.

留意如果你相信这个说法，谁会受益。卖家夸自己的产品，你一相信他就有好处，所以他的说法更需要核对，而不是不用核对。这并不说明说法是假的：诚实的卖家也有。它只是提醒你去找一个没有利益关系的第二来源。还要看日期，因为旧的事实可能悄悄变成错的。

Claim: this app doubles your reading speed. Source: the app’s own web page. Could they know: they tested it on their own users. Gain: they earn from every download. Next: look for a test by someone who sells nothing.
