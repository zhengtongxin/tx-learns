Second sources第二来源

Check an important claim against a separate source, and trace it back to where it began · ~10 min · reading and practice用另一个独立的来源核对重要的说法，并一路追到它的源头 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A second source第 1 步 · 第二来源

Learn先学

For anything that matters, look for a second source before you act or share. Search for the claim in plain words and see who else reports it. A second source helps only if it is independent: ten websites copying one article are one source, not ten. Look for a source with a different way of knowing, such as the original data or an official record.

凡是重要的事，在行动或转发之前，先找第二个来源。用平白的话搜一下这个说法，看还有谁报道。第二来源只有独立才有用：十个网站抄同一篇文章，只算一个来源，不是十个。要找知道的方式不同的来源，比如原始数据或官方记录。

Step 2 · Follow it to the start第 2 步 · 追到源头

Learn先学

Most claims travel: a post quotes a news story that quotes a report. Each step can drop a word like “may”, or a number, or the limits. Follow the links back until you reach the first source, then read what it actually says. Often the original is more careful than the headline, and sometimes it says something different.

大多数说法都会一路传下去：一条帖子引用了新闻，新闻又引用了一份报告。每一步都可能丢掉“可能”这样的词、某个数字或适用的限制。顺着链接往回找，直到最初的来源，再读读它真正说了什么。原文往往比标题谨慎，有时说的甚至是另一回事。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · How sure to be第 3 步 · 有几分把握

Learn先学

You rarely get certainty, so aim for a sensible level of confidence. If two independent sources agree and the original says the same, you can be fairly sure. If the original cannot be found or sources disagree, say “not confirmed” and hold back. “I do not know yet” is a useful answer, and it is better than a confident mistake.

你很少能得到百分之百的确定，所以目标是一个合理的把握程度。两个独立来源一致，原文也这么说，就可以相当有把握。找不到原文，或来源互相矛盾，就说“未经证实”，先不下结论。“我还不知道”是个有用的回答，比自信满满的错误强得多。

Claim: a new rule starts next month. Post -> news story -> agency notice. Original says: the rule is proposed, not final. Conclusion: not confirmed; wait for the final notice.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Look for an independent second source.
• Follow a claim back to its origin.
• Say “not confirmed” when you cannot confirm.

• 找独立的第二来源。
• 把说法追到源头。
• 无法证实时就说“未经证实”。
