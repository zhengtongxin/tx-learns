Claims and evidence说法与证据

Tell a claim from the evidence behind it, and judge how strong that evidence really is · ~10 min · reading and practice分清说法和支持它的证据，并判断这些证据到底有多强 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Claim and evidence第 1 步 · 说法与证据

Learn先学

A claim is what someone wants you to believe. Evidence is what they offer to show it is true. Many texts are all claim: a confident tone, a few adjectives, no support. When you read, ask “what is the claim?” and then “what is the evidence for it?” If you cannot name the evidence, the text has not given you a reason yet.

说法是别人想让你相信的东西，证据是他拿出来证明它为真的东西。许多文字全是说法：语气笃定、几个形容词，却没有支撑。读的时候先问“说法是什么？”再问“证据是什么？”如果说不出证据，这段文字还没给你任何理由。

Step 2 · Stronger and weaker第 2 步 · 强一些、弱一些

Learn先学

Not all evidence weighs the same. A measurement anyone can repeat beats a single story. Many cases beat one case. A careful comparison, where a group with the change is set against a similar group without it, beats someone’s memory of what happened. A story can still be true, but it is a starting point, not proof.

证据的分量并不相同。别人能重复的测量，胜过一个故事。许多个案例，胜过一个案例。仔细的对比，即把做了改变的一组和相似但没改变的一组放在一起比，胜过某个人对经过的回忆。故事也可能是真的，但它只是起点，不是证明。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · What would change your mind第 3 步 · 什么会让你改变看法

Learn先学

Ask yourself what would make you doubt the claim, and ask the same of the person making it. If nothing could ever count against it, it is not much of a claim. Honest people can say what would change their mind. Also check whether the evidence is about the same thing as the claim, because a result about one group does not automatically fit another.

问问自己：什么会让我怀疑这个说法？也这样问提出说法的人。如果任何事都不可能反驳它，它就算不上什么有分量的说法。诚实的人说得出什么会让自己改变看法。还要看证据说的是不是和说法同一件事，因为关于一类人的结果，不一定适用于另一类人。

Claim: our new routine cuts late deliveries. Evidence offered: one customer said it felt faster. Stronger evidence: late deliveries per month, before and after. Would change my mind: no drop across three months.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Separate claim from evidence.
• Prefer repeatable, counted evidence.
• Say what would change your mind.

• 把说法和证据分开。
• 偏重可重复、可计数的证据。
• 说出什么会让你改变看法。
