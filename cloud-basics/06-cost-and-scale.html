Cost and scale成本与扩展

Understand what grows when users do, and keep the bill from surprising you · ~10 min · reading and practice弄清用户变多时什么会增长，并避免账单吓你一跳 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · More users, more machine第 1 步 · 用户多了，机器也要多

Learn先学

When load grows you can scale up: one bigger machine, simple but with a ceiling and a single point of failure. Or you can scale out: more copies behind a load balancer that shares the traffic, which survives one copy failing but needs the app to keep no private state in memory. Many platforms add and remove copies for you as traffic changes.

负载增加时，可以纵向扩展：换一台更大的机器，简单，但有上限，也是单点故障。也可以横向扩展：在负载均衡器后面放更多副本，由它分摊流量，某个副本挂了也不怕，但要求应用不在内存里保存私有状态。很多平台会随流量自动增减副本。

Step 2 · Estimate before you launch第 2 步 · 上线前先估算

Learn先学

Cloud bills are usage times price. Estimate with a back-of-envelope sum: requests per day, cost per request, plus the machines that are on whether anyone visits or not. For an AI feature the big line is usually tokens: calls per day times tokens per call times the price per token.

云账单就是用量乘以单价。用一个粗略的算式先估：每天的请求数、每个请求的成本，再加上不管有没有人访问都开着的机器。对 AI 功能来说，大头通常是 token：每天调用次数乘以每次的 token 数，再乘以每个 token 的价格。

calls_per_day = 2000 tokens_per_call = 1500 price_per_million = 3.0 # example price, in dollars tokens = calls_per_day * tokens_per_call * 30 print(round(tokens / 1_000_000 * price_per_million, 2))

Step 3 · Guard the bill第 3 步 · 守住账单

Learn先学

Surprises come from forgotten machines, a loop that calls a paid service forever, or traffic you did not expect. Set a budget with an alert at 50% and 80%, give each key a spending limit, tag resources so you know what each is for, and switch off what you no longer use. Sort each habit.

惊喜（吓人的那种）来自被遗忘的机器、无限循环调用付费服务，或没预料到的流量。设一个预算，在 50% 和 80% 时告警；给每个密钥设支出上限；给资源打标签，知道每个是干什么的；不用的就关掉。给每个习惯分类。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Scale up is one bigger machine; scale out is more copies behind a load balancer.
• A bill is usage times price: estimate it before you launch.
• Budgets, caps and tags stop a surprise bill.

• 纵向是一台更大的机器；横向是负载均衡器后面更多的副本。
• 账单是用量乘以单价：上线前先估算。
• 预算、上限和标签能防止账单惊喜。
