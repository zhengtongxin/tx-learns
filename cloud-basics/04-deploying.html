Deploying部署

Get a change from your laptop to real users in small, checked, reversible steps · ~10 min · reading and practice把改动从你的笔记本，用小步、有检查、可撤回的方式送到真实用户手里 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Stages, not a leap第 1 步 · 一站一站，不是一步跳

Learn先学

A safe change moves through environments of rising stakes: your laptop, a shared staging copy that mimics the real thing, then production where users are. Each stage catches what the last could not: staging has real settings and data shapes, production has real traffic.

一个安全的改动会依次经过风险越来越高的环境：你的笔记本，一个模仿真实环境的共享预发副本，最后是用户所在的生产环境。每一站抓住上一站抓不到的问题：预发有真实的设置和数据形态，生产有真实的流量。

Step 2 · The pipeline第 2 步 · 流水线

Learn先学

Continuous integration (CI) runs the tests on every push, so a broken change is caught within minutes. Continuous delivery (CD) goes further and ships every change that passes, automatically or with one click. The result is a pipeline: push, test, build, deploy. A pull request is a proposal to merge your branch into the main one, and merging means combining it in. Small frequent changes are safer than rare giant ones because each is easy to understand and undo.

持续集成（CI）在每次推送时运行测试，坏改动几分钟内就被发现。持续交付（CD）更进一步，把每个通过的改动自动发布，或点一下就发布。结果就是一条流水线：推送、测试、构建、部署。拉取请求是请求把你的分支合并进主分支的提议，合并就是把它并进去。小而频繁的改动比稀少的巨大改动更安全，因为每一个都容易理解、容易撤回。

Step 3 · Plan the way back第 3 步 · 先想好退路

Learn先学

Before you ship, know how you will undo it. A rollback puts the last good version back; it works best when each release is a labelled image or tag (a name such as v1.4.2 that marks one exact version) you can redeploy. Roll back first and investigate afterwards: users should not wait while you debug. Sort each item into CI or CD.

发布之前，先想好怎么撤回。回滚是把上一个好的版本放回去；如果每次发布都是有标签的镜像或标签（如 v1.4.2，标出某一个确切版本的名字），可以重新部署，就最顺畅。先回滚、再调查：用户不该等你调试。把每一项分到 CI 或 CD。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Changes move through environments of rising stakes.
• CI checks every change; CD ships the ones that pass.
• Know your rollback before you ship; roll back first, debug later.

• 改动依次经过风险递增的环境。
• CI 检查每个改动；CD 发布通过的那些。
• 发布前先想好回滚；先回滚，后调试。
