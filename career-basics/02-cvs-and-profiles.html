CVs and profiles简历与个人主页

Write a short CV that shows results, fits the job and is easy to scan, and keep your online profile in step · ~10 min · reading and practice写一份简短的简历：展示结果、贴合岗位、容易扫读，并让你的线上个人主页与它保持一致 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Results, not duties第 1 步 · 结果，而不是职责

Learn先学

A reader may spend under a minute on a CV, so each line should say what you did and what changed. “Responsible for reports” tells little. A line with a number or a clear outcome tells a lot. To work out a percentage saved, divide the time saved by the old time: 2 hours saved out of 5 is 2 ÷ 5 = 40%. Keep it to one or two pages, newest experience first, in plain formatting.

读者看一份简历可能不到一分钟，所以每一行都应写明你做了什么、带来了什么变化。“负责报表”几乎没有信息，带数字或明确结果的一行信息量就大得多。要算节省的百分比，用节省的时间除以原来的时间：5 小时里节省 2 小时，就是 2 ÷ 5 = 40%。控制在一到两页，最新的经历放最前，格式朴素。

Step 2 · Fit the job第 2 步 · 贴合岗位

Learn先学

Read the job description and pick the experience that matches what it asks for. Use the plain words of the job description where they are true for you. Do not stretch the truth: a claim you cannot back up in an interview costs you more than it gains.

读岗位描述，挑出与它要求相符的经历，真实适用的地方就用岗位描述里的朴素用词。不要夸大：面试里答不上来的说法，得不偿失。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Check and align第 3 步 · 检查并保持一致

Learn先学

Proofread for spelling, dates and contact details, then ask someone to read it cold. If you keep an online profile (a page on a professional networking site that lists your work, skills and story), make sure its dates, titles and story match the CV. Mismatches make a reader wonder which one is true.

检查拼写、日期和联系方式，再请别人不带背景地读一遍。如果你有线上个人主页（在职业社交网站上列出你的工作、技能和故事的一页），要确保它的日期、职称和故事与简历一致。对不上会让读者怀疑哪个才是真的。

Weak: Responsible for weekly reports. Strong: Cut weekly report time from 5 hours to 3 hours (40% less) by building a template.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Show results, not duties.
• Fit the CV to the job.
• Keep CV and profile consistent.

• 展示结果，而不是职责。
• 让简历贴合岗位。
• 让简历和个人主页一致。
