Offers and pay录用与薪酬

Compare a whole offer, not just the monthly number, ask politely with reasons and keep what is agreed in writing · ~10 min · reading and practice比较完整的录用条件，而不只是月薪；礼貌而有理由地提出请求；把谈妥的内容留成书面 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Look at the whole offer第 1 步 · 看整份条件

Learn先学

Pay is more than the monthly number. Add the bonus, then look at leave (paid time off), hours, the chance to learn and how long the commute is. Put the yearly money side by side with a little arithmetic so the comparison is fair: a year is the monthly pay times 12, plus any bonus. This is general education, not tax or legal advice.

薪酬不只是月薪数字。加上奖金，再看休假（带薪假期）、工时、学习机会和通勤时间。把全年的钱用一点算术并排摆开，比较才公平：全年等于月薪乘 12，再加上奖金。这是一般性教育，不构成税务或法律意见。

Step 2 · Ask politely, with reasons第 2 步 · 礼貌地提出，并说明理由

Learn先学

If you want something changed, ask calmly and give reasons, such as the usual pay range for the role (job sites and people in the field can tell you) or the results you bring. Be ready for a no and decide in advance what you would accept. A polite request rarely costs you the offer; pressure from either side is a reason to slow down.

如果想改变某项条件，要平静地提出并给出理由，比如该岗位通常的薪酬范围（招聘网站和同行可以告诉你）或你能带来的成果。要做好被拒绝的准备，事先想好你能接受什么。礼貌的请求很少让你丢掉机会；任何一方施压，都是放慢脚步的理由。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Take time, then get it in writing第 3 步 · 先留出时间，再落到书面

Learn先学

You can thank them and ask for a short time to consider. When you agree, make sure the terms are written down in the offer: pay, start date, role and anything promised. A spoken promise that is not written is easy to remember differently.

你可以先表示感谢，再请对方给一点时间考虑。决定后，要确认条款写在录用文件里：薪酬、入职日期、岗位和承诺的事项。口头承诺不落到书面，各人记忆容易不同。

Offer A: 4,000 a month x 12 = 48,000, plus a bonus of one month (4,000) = 52,000 a year Offer B: 4,300 a month x 12 = 51,600, no bonus

Takeaways要点回顾

• Add up the whole offer.
• Ask politely, with reasons.
• Get it in writing.

• 把整份条件加总。
• 礼貌地提出，并说明理由。
• 落到书面。
