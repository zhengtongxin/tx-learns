Owning a mistake承担错误

Tell the right people early, say what happened and what you will do, then fix it and change one thing · ~9 min · reading and practice及早告诉该知道的人，说清发生了什么和你打算怎么做，然后改正并改变一件事 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Say it early第 1 步 · 尽早说出来

Learn先学

Everyone makes mistakes at work. What matters is how fast the right people hear about it. A mistake told early is usually small to fix; the same mistake found later by someone else is bigger, and so is the loss of trust. As soon as you notice one, tell the person who is affected or who owns the work.

每个人在工作中都会犯错。关键是该知道的人多快知道。早说出来的错误通常很容易补救，同样的错误若被别人晚些发现，问题更大，信任的损失也更大。一发现错误，就告诉受影响的人或这项工作的负责人。

Step 2 · Say it plainly第 2 步 · 直截了当地说

Learn先学

Use three short parts: what happened, what it affects, what you will do. For example: a report of 40 rows has 2 wrong rows (2 ÷ 40 = 5%); fixing them takes about 3 minutes each, so 2 × 3 = 6 minutes, and you will send the corrected report today. Do not hide it, blame others or write a long apology. Facts and a plan are what people need.

用三个简短的部分：发生了什么、影响了什么、你打算怎么做。例如：一份 40 行的报告有 2 行错了（2 ÷ 40 = 5%）；每行改正约需 3 分钟，所以 2 × 3 = 6 分钟，你今天会发出改正后的报告。不要隐瞒、不要怪别人，也不要写长长的道歉。人们需要的是事实和计划。

Step 3 · Fix and learn第 3 步 · 改正并吸取教训

Learn先学

After the fix, ask what let the mistake through and change one thing, such as a second look before sending. Note it for yourself so you do not repeat it. A mistake handled this way often builds trust rather than losing it. This is general education, not career or legal advice; if a mistake could cause real harm, follow your workplace's own process.

改正之后，问问是什么让错误漏了过去，并改变一件事，比如发出前再看一遍。为自己记一笔，免得重犯。用这种方式处理错误，往往会增加信任，而不是失去信任。本课是一般性教育，不是职业或法律建议；如果错误可能造成真正的伤害，请按所在单位的正式流程处理。

To finish Step 3, sort each habit as good or weak: tap a habit, then tap its group.

完成第 3 步：把每个做法分为好或弱。先点一个做法，再点它所属的组。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Everyone makes mistakes at work; what matters is how fast the right people hear.
• Tell it early: it is usually small to fix and protects trust.
• Say what happened, what it affects and what you will do.
• In the example, 2 wrong rows of 40 is 5%, and fixing takes 2 × 3 = 6 minutes.
• Fix it, change one thing and note it so it does not repeat.

• 每个人在工作中都会犯错；关键是该知道的人多快知道。
• 尽早说：通常容易补救，也保住信任。
• 说清发生了什么、影响了什么、你打算怎么做。
• 例子里 40 行中错了 2 行是 5%，改正需要 2 × 3 = 6 分钟。
• 改正、改变一件事，并记下来，免得重犯。
