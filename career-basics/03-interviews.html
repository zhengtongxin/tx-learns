Getting the job · Lesson 3找到工作 · 第 3 课

Interviews面试

Prepare a few real stories, answer the question asked and finish with your own questions and a thank-you · ~10 min · reading and practice准备几个真实的故事，回答被问到的问题，并以自己的提问和一句感谢收尾 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Prepare第 1 步 · 准备

Learn先学

Learn what the organisation does and what the role needs, then match your experience to it. Pick three stories from your list in Strengths and story that show different strengths. Practise them out loud, because saying a story is different from knowing it. Do not memorise a script word for word; know the points and tell them naturally.

了解这家机构做什么、岗位需要什么，再把你的经历与之对应。从“优势与个人故事”那一课的清单里挑出三个能体现不同优势的故事，并大声练习，因为“说出来”和“知道”是两回事。不要逐字背稿，记住要点，自然地讲出来。

Step 2 · Tell it with a shape第 2 步 · 讲出结构

Learn先学

A simple shape, often called STAR, keeps a story clear: the situation, your task, the action you took and the result. Answer the question you were asked, then stop. If you do not know something, say so honestly and explain how you would find out.

一个简单的结构（常被称为 STAR）能让故事清晰：情境、任务、你的行动和结果。回答被问到的问题，然后停下。不知道的就老实说，并讲讲你会怎样去查清楚。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Ask and follow up第 3 步 · 提问与跟进

Learn先学

Prepare two or three questions of your own, such as what a good first few months look like. After the interview, send a short thank-you that mentions one thing you discussed. It is polite and keeps you in mind.

准备两三个自己的问题，比如前几个月怎样算做得好。面试之后发一条简短的感谢，提到你们聊过的一件事。这既礼貌，也让对方记住你。

Situation: the weekly report took 5 hours. Task: shorten it without losing detail. Action: built a template and filled it from one source. Result: down to 3 hours.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Prepare and practise.
• Tell stories with a shape.
• Ask questions and say thanks.

• 准备并练习。
• 讲有结构的故事。
• 提问并致谢。
