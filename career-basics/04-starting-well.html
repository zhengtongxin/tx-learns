Growing at work · Lesson 1在工作中成长 · 第 1 课

Starting well好的开始

Use the first few months to learn what success means, listen first and finish small things well · ~10 min · reading and practice用最初的几个月弄清什么算成功，先倾听，把小事做好 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Agree what good looks like第 1 步 · 约定“做好”的样子

Learn先学

In your first conversation with your manager, ask what a good first few months look like and how they like to get updates. Expectations that stay unspoken become surprises later. Write the answers down and check them back in your own words.

与上级的第一次谈话里，问问前几个月怎样算做得好，以及他们喜欢怎样收到进展汇报。不说出口的期望以后会变成意外。把答案写下来，并用自己的话复述确认。

Step 2 · Listen, ask, note第 2 步 · 先听、多问、记下

Learn先学

Learn how work really gets done before you change it. Ask questions early, because a question in the first month is normal and the same question in the sixth month is awkward. Keep a notebook of names, answers and how things are done so you ask each thing once.

先弄清工作到底怎么完成，再去改变它。问题要早问：第一个月问是正常的，到第六个月再问就尴尬了。用笔记本记下人名、答案和做事的方法，这样每件事只问一次。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Small early wins第 3 步 · 小而早的成果

Learn先学

Choose a small task you can finish well and visibly, such as fixing a recurring annoyance. An early win builds trust and teaches you how the place works. Build relationships by being useful and reliable, not by being loud.

挑一件你能做好、也看得见的小任务，比如解决一个反复出现的小麻烦。早期的成果能建立信任，也让你了解这里怎么运转。建立关系靠的是有用和可靠，而不是声音大。

Questions for my manager: 1. What does a good first few months look like? 2. How do you like to get updates? 3. Who do I ask for what?
