Growing at work · Lesson 3在工作中成长 · 第 3 课

Growing a career职业成长

Ask for feedback, keep a record of your wins, learn on purpose and review where you are going once a year · ~10 min · reading and practice主动要反馈，记录自己的成果，有目的地学习，并每年回顾一次方向 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Feedback and a record第 1 步 · 反馈与记录

Learn先学

Ask people you trust what you do well and what to improve, with specific questions such as “what is one thing I could do better in meetings?”. Keep a simple record of what you finished and what it changed. When your yearly performance review comes you will not have to remember a year from scratch.

向你信任的人问清楚你哪里做得好、哪里要改进，问题要具体，比如“开会时我有哪一件事可以做得更好？”。简单记录你完成了什么、带来了什么改变。到年度绩效评估的时候，你就不必凭空回忆一整年。

Step 2 · Learn and connect第 2 步 · 学习与人脉

Learn先学

Pick one skill at a time to build, and use it on real work soon after you learn it. Your network (the people who know your work) grows from being useful: share what you know, say thanks and keep in touch before you need anything. Promotions rarely come just from waiting.

一次选一项技能来培养，学完尽快用到真实工作中。你的人脉（了解你工作的人）来自对别人有用：分享你知道的，表达感谢，在需要之前就保持联系。晋升很少只靠等待而来。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Review once a year第 3 步 · 每年回顾一次

Learn先学

Once a year, sit down and ask four questions about the past year and the next. Write the answers, and pick one change to make. A short honest review beats a vague plan you never look at.

每年坐下来，围绕过去一年和下一年问自己四个问题。把答案写下来，挑出一件要做的改变。一份简短而诚实的回顾，胜过一份从不再看的模糊计划。

1. What did I finish that mattered? 2. What did I learn? 3. What do I want next? 4. Who can help me get there?

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask for feedback and keep a record.
• Learn on purpose and give first.
• Review once a year.

• 主动要反馈并做记录。
• 有目的地学习，先给予。
• 每年回顾一次。
