Getting the job · Lesson 1找到工作 · 第 1 课

Strengths and story优势与个人故事

Turn what you have done into a short, believable story about what you are good at · ~10 min · reading and practice把你做过的事变成一个简短可信的故事，说明你擅长什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Start from what you did第 1 步 · 从你做过的事开始

Learn先学

List what you have done, not what you want to be called. Study projects, volunteering, part-time work, a club you helped run and everyday tasks you took care of all count. Most people leave things out, so write down anything where you took responsibility for a result.

先列出你做过的事，而不是你想被称作什么。学习项目、志愿活动、兼职、你帮忙运营的社团、你负责过的日常事务，都算数。大多数人会漏掉很多，所以把任何你为结果负过责的事都写下来。

Step 2 · Evidence beats adjectives第 2 步 · 证据胜过形容词

Learn先学

Anyone can say “hard-working”. A strength becomes believable when it has an example: what you did and what happened. For each strength you want to claim, find one thing you did that shows it. If you cannot find one, it is a hope, not yet a strength.

谁都能说“勤奋”。一项优势有了例子才可信：你做了什么，结果如何。对每一项想主张的优势，找出一件能证明它的事。如果找不到，它还只是愿望，还不是优势。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · One sentence第 3 步 · 一句话

Learn先学

Pull it together in one sentence: who you help, what you do and the result. You will reuse it in a CV, a message to a stranger and the first minute of an interview. Keep it plain and true; you can refine it as you do more.

把它们浓缩成一句话：你帮助谁、做什么、结果如何。你会在简历、给陌生人的消息和面试的第一分钟里反复用到它。要朴素而真实，随着经历增加可以再打磨。

Strength: organising Evidence: ran the sign-up list for a 20-person class trip and nobody was missed One sentence: I keep group plans on track, so people turn up knowing what to do.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• List what you did.
• Evidence beats adjectives.
• Say it in one sentence.

• 列出你做过的事。
• 证据胜过形容词。
• 用一句话说清。
