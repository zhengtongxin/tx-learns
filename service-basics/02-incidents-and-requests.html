Incidents and requests事件与请求

Tell a broken service from a normal ask, and set the order of work by impact and urgency · ~10 min · reading and practice分清服务出了故障和一次正常的请求，并按影响和紧急程度排工作的先后 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Incident or request第 1 步 · 事件还是请求

Learn先学

An incident is an unplanned interruption to a service, or a drop in its quality: the email stops working, or the video call keeps freezing. A service request is a user asking for something that is a normal, agreed part of the service: a new account, a software install, a password reset. The first is broken; the second is simply asked for.

事件是服务的一次计划外中断，或质量下降：邮件用不了，视频通话总是卡住。服务请求是使用者要求服务里本来就有、已经约定好的事：开新账号、装软件、重置密码。前者是坏了，后者只是在提需求。

Step 2 · Why the difference matters第 2 步 · 为什么区分很重要

Learn先学

An incident is fixed as fast as possible to restore the service, because people are blocked right now. A request follows a set routine with an agreed time, often with an approval step. Mixing them up means a broken service waits in a queue of routine asks, or routine asks get rushed as if they were emergencies.

事件要尽快修复、恢复服务，因为大家现在就被卡住了。请求则按一套固定流程、在约定的时间内完成，往往还有批准的步骤。把两者混在一起，就会让坏掉的服务排在一堆例行请求后面，或者把例行请求当成紧急情况来赶。

Step 3 · Impact and urgency第 3 步 · 影响和紧急程度

Learn先学

Impact is how many people or how much of the business is hit. Urgency is how soon the harm grows if nothing is done. Together they set the priority: a failure that stops the whole sales team on the last day of the month comes first; a flickering light in one meeting room can wait. Write the rule down so everyone sets priority the same way.

影响是有多少人、多大范围的业务受到冲击。紧急程度是不处理的话，伤害多快会扩大。两者合起来决定优先级：月底最后一天让整个销售团队停摆的故障排在最前，一间会议室里闪烁的灯可以等一等。把规则写下来，大家定优先级才一致。

High impact + urgent -> top priority, fix now High impact + not urgent -> plan it soon Low impact + urgent -> handle quickly Low impact + not urgent -> in the normal queue

Takeaways要点回顾

• An incident is something broken; a request is a normal ask.
• Incidents are restored fast; requests follow a routine.
• Priority comes from impact and urgency.
• Write the priority rule down so it is shared.

• 事件是东西坏了，请求是正常的要求。
• 事件要快速恢复，请求走固定流程。
• 优先级来自影响和紧急程度。
• 把优先级规则写下来，大家才一致。
