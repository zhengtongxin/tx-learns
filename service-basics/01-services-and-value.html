Services and value服务与价值

See what an IT service is, why its value comes from outcomes, and how costs and risks are part of the picture · ~10 min · reading and practice看懂什么是 IT 服务、为什么它的价值来自成果，以及成本和风险怎样也在其中 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A service gives an outcome第 1 步 · 服务带来成果

Learn先学

A service is a way of helping someone get an outcome they want without them having to own the specific costs and risks. A staff member who wants to join a video meeting does not want to run mail servers or buy network gear. They want the meeting to work. The IT team provides that through a service.

服务是帮助别人得到他们想要的成果的一种方式，而对方不必自己承担其中的具体成本和风险。想参加视频会议的员工，并不想去运营邮件服务器或购买网络设备，他们想要的是会议能开起来。IT 团队通过一项服务把这件事办到。

Step 2 · Value comes from the outcome第 2 步 · 价值来自成果

Learn先学

Value is the usefulness and importance of something to the person using it. A fast server has no value by itself; a staff member who can finish a task on time because the system worked does. Provider and user create value together: the provider runs the service well, and the user uses it for their own goal.

价值是某样东西对使用它的人有多有用、有多重要。一台速度很快的服务器本身没有价值；员工因为系统可用而按时完成了任务，才有价值。提供方和使用方共同创造价值：提供方把服务运营好，使用方用它去达成自己的目标。

Step 3 · Costs and risks count too第 3 步 · 成本和风险也要算

Learn先学

Every service has costs for the user (money, effort, waiting) and risks (it might fail, leak data or be slow). A service is worth having when the good it does is bigger than its costs and risks. A short “service card” makes this visible and is easy to check with the people who use the service.

每项服务对使用者都有成本（金钱、精力、等待）和风险（可能出故障、泄露数据或变慢）。当它带来的好处大于成本和风险时，这项服务才值得拥有。一张简短的“服务卡”能把这些摆出来，也方便和使用者一起核对。

Service : shared printing on the second floor Outcome : staff print and collect a document in minutes Costs : paper and toner, a few minutes of queue time Risks : jams, private papers left on the tray Verdict : worth it while the outcome outweighs the rest

Takeaways要点回顾

• A service helps someone get an outcome without owning its costs and risks.
• Value is what users achieve, not a technical feature.
• Provider and user create value together.
• A service card shows outcome, costs and risks in one place.

• 服务帮人得到成果，而不必自己承担其中的成本和风险。
• 价值是使用者达成的结果，而不是技术特性。
• 提供方和使用方共同创造价值。
• 服务卡把成果、成本和风险放在一处。
