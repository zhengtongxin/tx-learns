Keeping services running · Lesson 3让服务持续运转 · 第 3 课

Getting better持续改进

Keep what you learn as knowledge and make small, measured improvements to the service over time · ~10 min · reading and practice把学到的东西留成知识，并随时间做小而可衡量的改进 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Keep what you learn第 1 步 · 把学到的留下来

Learn先学

Every solved issue holds something the next person could use. A short knowledge article turns it into a reusable answer: the symptom in the user’s words, the steps that fixed it, and when to escalate instead. A good article lets a new first-line colleague, or the user, solve the issue alone. An article nobody keeps current is worse than none, so give each one an owner.

每个解决了的问题里，都有下一个人可以用到的东西。一篇简短的知识文章把它变成可重复使用的答案：用使用者自己的话写症状、写修好的步骤、写什么时候该升级。好文章能让新来的第一线同事、甚至用户自己，就把问题解决。没人维护的文章比没有还糟，所以每篇都要有负责人。

Step 2 · Small, measured improvements第 2 步 · 小而可衡量的改进

Learn先学

Continual improvement means making the service a little better, again and again, and checking that it worked. Pick one thing, record where you are now, change it, and measure again. Suppose 20 password-reset incidents a month fall to 8 after a self-service guide: the drop is 12, and 12 ÷ 20 = 60%. A number like that shows the change was real, and where to look next.

持续改进是指一次又一次地把服务做得好一点，并检验确实有效。挑一件事，记下现在的水平，做出改变，再量一次。假设每月 20 起重置密码的事件，在有了自助指引之后降到 8 起：减少了 12 起，12 ÷ 20 = 60%。这样的数字说明变化是真的，也指出下一步该看哪里。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Feedback points the way第 3 步 · 反馈指明方向

Learn先学

Users tell you what matters if you ask: after a ticket closes, one or two short questions are enough. Combine their answers with the numbers: where do people wait longest, where do the same faults repeat, which articles get used? Then improve the thing with the biggest effect for the least effort, and tell users what changed, so they see their feedback was used.

只要你去问，使用者就会告诉你什么重要：工单关闭后，一两个简短的问题就够了。把他们的回答和数字结合起来看：大家在哪里等得最久、哪些故障反复出现、哪些文章被用得最多？然后改进“花力气最少、效果最大”的那一件事，并告诉用户改了什么，让他们看到自己的反馈被用上了。

1. Where are we now? 20 reset incidents a month 2. Where do we want to be? fewer, through self-service 3. Make one change publish a short guide 4. Measure again 8 a month: (20 - 8) / 20 = 60% fewer 5. Share it and pick the next thing

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Turn solved issues into short, owned knowledge articles.
• Improve one thing at a time and measure it.
• 20 down to 8 is (20 − 8) ÷ 20 = 60% fewer.
• Use feedback, and tell users what changed.

• 把解决的问题变成简短、有人负责的知识文章。
• 一次改进一件事，并去衡量。
• 20 降到 8，是 (20 − 8) ÷ 20 = 减少了 60%。
• 用好反馈，并告诉用户改了什么。
