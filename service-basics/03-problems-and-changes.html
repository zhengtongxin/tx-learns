Problems and changes问题与变更

Find the cause behind repeat incidents, and make changes in a way that keeps the service safe · ~10 min · reading and practice找出反复出现的事件背后的原因，并以保证服务安全的方式做变更 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A problem is the cause第 1 步 · 问题是原因

Learn先学

A problem is the underlying cause of one or more incidents. If the second-floor printer queue jams every Monday morning, each jam is an incident (something that was working has stopped). The reason it keeps happening is the problem. Fixing each incident gets people printing again; finding the problem stops the jams coming back.

问题是一个或多个事件背后的根本原因。如果二楼的打印队列每周一早上都卡住，每一次卡住是一个事件（原本正常的东西停了），而它为什么总是发生，就是问题。逐个修复事件，能让大家重新打印；找到问题，才能让卡住不再回来。

Step 2 · Workaround and known error第 2 步 · 临时办法与已知错误

Learn先学

Finding a cause can take time. Meanwhile a workaround reduces the harm: for example, clearing the queue with a short script every Monday. Once a cause is understood but not yet fixed, it is recorded as a known error with its workaround, so the service desk (the team users contact when something is wrong) can help the next user at once instead of starting from zero.

找到原因可能要一段时间。在此期间，临时办法可以减少伤害，比如每周一用一个小脚本清空队列。原因已经弄清、但还没修好时，就把它连同临时办法记成已知错误，这样服务台（出问题时用户联系的团队）遇到下一位用户时能马上帮忙，而不是从零开始。

Step 3 · Changes and their risk第 3 步 · 变更和它的风险

Learn先学

A change is adding, modifying or removing anything that could affect a service. Handle it by risk, and write each one in the change record: what changed, who approved it and when. A low-risk, well-understood change, such as adding a user to a group, can follow a pre-approved routine (a way of working that was agreed in advance). A bigger one is assessed and approved before it happens. An emergency fix still gets a quick check, then a review afterwards. Assessing a bigger change first catches risks before they cause an outage (a time when a service is down). Changes that were not checked are a common cause of outages.

变更是添加、修改或移除任何可能影响服务的东西。要按风险来处理，并把每一项记入变更记录：改了什么、谁批准、什么时候。低风险、已被充分了解的变更（比如把用户加入某个组）可以走预先批准的固定流程（事先约定好的做法）；较大的变更要先评估、批准再做；紧急修复也要快速核对，事后再复盘。先评估较大的变更，能在故障（服务停摆的时段）发生前发现风险。没有核对过的变更，是故障的常见原因。

Low risk, well known -> pre-approved routine, note it in the change record Bigger or less known -> assess and approve first Emergency fix -> quick check now, review afterwards

Takeaways要点回顾

• A problem is the cause behind one or more incidents.
• A workaround reduces harm while the cause is open.
• A change is adding, modifying or removing something that could affect a service.
• Match the care to the risk.

• 问题是一个或多个事件背后的原因。
• 临时办法在原因未解决时减少伤害。
• 变更是添加、修改或移除可能影响服务的东西。
• 谨慎程度要和风险相称。
