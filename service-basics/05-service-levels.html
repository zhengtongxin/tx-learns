Service levels服务水平

Agree clear targets for a service and measure them, including what 99% availability really allows · ~10 min · reading and practice为服务约定清晰的目标并去衡量，包括 99% 的可用性实际上允许多少停机 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Targets in plain words第 1 步 · 用平实的话定目标

Learn先学

A service level agreement is a written promise between provider and user about what to expect: when the service is available, how fast a request is answered, how soon a fault is fixed. Vague promises like “we will be quick” cannot be checked. A good target is a number and a time that both sides understand. The agreement also says what counts, for example whether planned maintenance is counted as downtime.

服务水平协议是提供方和使用方之间的一份书面约定，说明用户可以期待什么：服务什么时候可用、请求多快得到回应、故障多快修好。“我们会很快”这种含糊的承诺没法检验。好的目标是双方都懂的数字和时间。协议还要写明算什么，比如计划内的维护算不算停机。

Step 2 · What 99% allows第 2 步 · 99% 究竟允许多少停机

Learn先学

Availability is the share of agreed time that a service works. In a 30-day month there are 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200 minutes. At 99%, 1% may be down: 0.01 × 43,200 = 432 minutes, which is 7.2 hours. At 99.9%, only 0.1% may be down: 43.2 minutes. One extra nine (99% becomes 99.9%) cuts the allowed downtime to a tenth, and usually costs far more to deliver.

可用性是约定时间内服务可用的比例。30 天的一个月有 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200 分钟。按 99%，允许 1% 不可用：0.01 × 43,200 = 432 分钟，也就是 7.2 小时。按 99.9%，只允许 0.1% 不可用：43.2 分钟。多一个 9（99% 变成 99.9%），允许的停机缩到十分之一，而做到它通常要贵得多。

Step 3 · Measure it honestly第 3 步 · 如实地衡量

Learn先学

Targets only help if they are measured and shared. Say a desk promises to answer within 15 minutes and, of 40 requests this week, 34 were answered in time. That is 34 ÷ 40 = 0.85, or 85%. Report the number as it is, and look at the six (40 − 34) that missed: the misses show what to fix. Judge a service by what users feel, not only by what the figures say.

目标只有被衡量并公开，才有帮助。比如服务台承诺 15 分钟内回应，这周 40 个请求里有 34 个按时回应。34 ÷ 40 = 0.85，也就是 85%。按实际数字如实汇报，再去看没达标的那六个（40 − 34）：没达标的地方，正是要改进的地方。评价一项服务，要看用户的感受，而不只看数字。

30-day month = 30 x 24 x 60 = 43,200 minutes 99% available -> up to 432 min down (7.2 hours) 99.9% available -> up to 43.2 min down Response target met: 34 of 40 = 85%

Takeaways要点回顾

• A service level agreement is a written, checkable promise.
• A 30-day month is 43,200 minutes; 99% allows 432 of them down.
• 34 of 40 on time is 85%.
• Report the real figure and study the misses.

• 服务水平协议是一份可检验的书面约定。
• 30 天的一个月有 43,200 分钟；99% 允许其中 432 分钟不可用。
• 40 个里 34 个按时，是 85%。
• 如实报告数字，并研究没达标的部分。
