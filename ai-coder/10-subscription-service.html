Subscription service订阅制服务

Build the plan limits and billing-event rules of a small subscription service, with tests · ~45 min · project做出一个小型订阅服务的套餐额度和账单事件规则，并配上测试 · 约 45 分钟 · 项目

Step 1 · Plan the platform in slices第 1 步 · 把平台切成小块

Learn先学

A software-as-a-service (SaaS) platform is many parts: sign-in, a database, pages, billing. Do not give an agent all of it. Write a short spec, cut it into slices that each work end to end, and build one slice per session.

软件即服务（SaaS）平台有很多部分：登录、数据库、页面、计费。不要一口气交给智能体。先写一份简短的规格，切成每块都能从头到尾跑通的小块，每个会话做一块。

Buy what is solved: use a hosted sign-in service and a payment provider. Your own code should hold what only you decide, such as what each plan allows.

已经被解决的事就买现成的：用托管的登录服务和支付服务。你自己的代码只放只有你能决定的事，比如每个套餐允许什么。

Product: a photo-captioning service for small shops. Plans: free = 3 captions a month, pro = 100 a month. Slice 1: plan limits in code, tested (this lesson). Slice 2: sign-in with a hosted provider. Slice 3: a page that calls the caption model. Slice 4: payment webhooks upgrade and downgrade the plan. Not yet: teams, invoices, admin screen.

Step 2 · Rules in code: limits and repeats第 2 步 · 写进代码的规则：额度与重复

Learn先学

Two rules decide whether billing is trustworthy. First, limits: a free account cannot use more than its plan, and the month reset starts a new count.

有两条规则决定计费是否可信。第一是额度：免费账号不能超过套餐上限，月初重置后重新计数。

Second, repeats. Payment providers send a webhook event and may send the same event twice. Remember event ids you have seen, and ignore the repeats, so a plan is not changed twice and nobody is charged twice.

第二是重复。支付服务会发 webhook 事件，而且可能把同一个事件发两次。记住见过的事件编号，忽略重复的，套餐就不会改两次，也不会有人被扣两次钱。

Step 3 · Review what the agent built第 3 步 · 审阅智能体做出来的东西

Learn先学

SaaS code has sharp edges. Check each one yourself: no secrets in the code or the browser; every route checks who is asking; the price and plan are decided on the server, never trusted from the browser; the webhook checks the provider's signature.

SaaS 的代码有不少尖角，每一个都要你自己检查：代码里和浏览器里没有密钥；每一个路由都验证请求者是谁；价格和套餐在服务器上决定，绝不信任浏览器传来的；webhook 要验证支付服务的签名。

Run the tests, then try to break it: use a free account 4 times, send an event twice, send a plan that does not exist.

运行测试，然后试着把它弄坏：用免费账号用 4 次、同一个事件发两次、发一个不存在的套餐。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write a spec, cut it into end-to-end slices, one slice per session.
• Buy solved parts (sign-in, payments); own the rules only you decide.
• Limits and repeat-safe webhooks keep billing honest.
• Never trust the browser for price or plan; check who is asking on every route.

• 先写规格，切成端到端的小块，每个会话一块。
• 已解决的部分去买（登录、支付）；只有你能决定的规则自己做。
• 额度和重复安全的 webhook 让计费靠得住。
• 价格和套餐绝不信任浏览器；每个路由都要验证请求者。
