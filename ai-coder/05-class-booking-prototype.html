Class booking prototype课位预订原型

Shape a small paid product: one user, one pain, one price, and a tested booking rule · ~40 min · project做一个可收费的小产品：一个用户、一个痛点、一个价格，外加一条经过测试的预订规则 · 约 40 分钟 · 项目

Step 1 · Cut the idea down to one sheet第 1 步 · 把点子削成一页纸

Learn先学

A minimum viable product (MVP) answers one question: will someone pay for this? Write it on one sheet: one user, one pain, one thing they pay for, and a not-now list of everything you refuse to build yet.

最小可行产品（MVP）只回答一个问题：有人愿意为它付钱吗？把它写在一页纸上：一个用户、一个痛点、他们为哪一件事付钱，再加一张暂不做清单，列出你拒绝现在去做的一切。

Ours: Auntie Lim runs a small yoga studio in Bukit Timah. Classes hold 8 people, and she tracks bookings on WhatsApp. The paid thing: a booking page that never double-books and moves the next person on the waitlist in when someone cancels.

我们的例子：林阿姨在武吉知马开一家小瑜伽馆。每节课 8 个位子，她用 WhatsApp 记预订。要付钱的那件事：一个永不重复订位、有人取消时自动让候补第一位补上的预订页。

User: Auntie Lim, owner of a 1-room yoga studio. Pain: double bookings and a messy WhatsApp waitlist. Paid for: a booking page, S$19 a month. Not now: accounts, reminders, reports, a mobile app, a second studio. Proof: 3 studios say yes to the price before we build the rest.

Step 2 · Test the one rule that matters第 2 步 · 测试最关键的那条规则

Learn先学

Every product has one rule that, if wrong, kills trust. Here it is: a class never has more than its seats, and a cancel moves the first waitlisted person in. Write it as a test first, then let the agent make it pass.

每个产品都有一条一旦出错就毁掉信任的规则。这里就是：一节课的人数永不超过座位数；有人取消，候补第一位自动补上。先把它写成测试，再让智能体把它做到通过。

Keep the rule in plain functions with no screen attached. That way the agent can build the page around it, and the rule stays testable.

把规则写成不带界面的普通函数。这样智能体可以围着它搭页面，而规则始终能被测试。

Step 3 · Charge early, then ship第 3 步 · 尽早收费，然后上线

Learn先学

Before building billing, put a hosted payment link on the page (card providers such as Stripe offer them). Real money from three studios teaches more than a perfect checkout.

在做计费系统之前，先在页面上放一个托管的付款链接（Stripe 这类支付服务提供）。三家瑜伽馆付来的真钱，比一个完美的结账流程教给你更多。

Deploy to a host that gives you a public address in minutes, such as Vercel. Then watch what people actually do, and let that, not your guesses, decide the next item on the not-now list.

部署到几分钟就能给你公开网址的平台，比如 Vercel。然后看人们实际怎么用，让这个而不是你的猜测来决定暂不做清单里的下一项。

Takeaways要点回顾

• An MVP tests one thing: will someone pay?
• One user, one pain, one paid thing, a not-now list.
• Test the single rule that would kill trust.
• Keep rules in plain functions, apart from the screen.
• Take real money early with a payment link.

• MVP 只检验一件事：有人愿意付钱吗？
• 一个用户、一个痛点、一个付费点、一张暂不做清单。
• 测试那条会毁掉信任的核心规则。
• 把规则写成纯函数，和界面分开。
• 用付款链接尽早收到真钱。
