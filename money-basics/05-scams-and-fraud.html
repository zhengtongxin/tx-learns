Scams and fraud骗局与诈骗

Spot the signs of a scam, slow down when pushed and know what to do if you have been caught · ~10 min · reading and practice识别骗局的迹象，被催促时慢下来，并知道被骗后该怎么办 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The signs第 1 步 · 迹象

Learn先学

Most scams share a few signs: urgency (“act now or lose it”), secrecy (“do not tell anyone”), a promise too good to be true, and a request to pay in a way that is hard to undo, such as gift cards or transfers to a stranger. A message pretending to be your bank or a government office, asking for a password or code, is another common one. The pressure is the real clue.

大多数骗局都有几个共同的迹象：催促（“马上行动，不然就没了”）、保密（“别告诉任何人”）、好得不真实的承诺，以及要求用难以撤回的方式付款，比如礼品卡或转账给陌生人。假冒银行或政府部门、索要密码或验证码的信息，也很常见。真正的线索是那股压力。

Step 2 · Slow down and verify第 2 步 · 慢下来，核实

Learn先学

A real organisation can wait while you check. Do not click the link or call the number in the message; find the official contact yourself, in the app you already use or on a card, and ask there. Never share a password or a one-time code with anyone who contacts you first. Ask someone you trust before sending a large sum, because a second opinion breaks the secrecy that scams rely on.

真正的机构能等你去核实。不要点信息里的链接或打里面的电话；自己去找官方联系方式，比如你一直在用的应用里、或卡片背面，再去询问。对于主动联系你的人，绝不要透露密码或一次性验证码。转一大笔钱之前，先问问你信任的人，因为第二个意见能打破骗局所依赖的保密。

Step 3 · If it happens第 3 步 · 如果被骗了

Learn先学

Scams can catch careful people, so act fast rather than feel ashamed. Contact your bank at once, using the official number, and ask whether the payment can be stopped. Change passwords that may be exposed, report the scam to the police or the official reporting service, and keep the messages as evidence. Telling others helps them avoid the same trap.

骗局也会骗到细心的人，所以要赶紧行动，而不是觉得羞耻。立刻用官方电话联系银行，问问这笔付款能不能拦下。更换可能泄露的密码，向警方或官方举报渠道报案，并保存好聊天记录作为证据。把经过告诉别人，也能帮他们避开同样的陷阱。

1. Urgent + secret + pay now = stop. 2. Never click the link in the message; open the official app instead. 3. Never share a password or one-time code. 4. Ask someone you trust before a large payment. 5. If caught: call the bank, change passwords, report.
