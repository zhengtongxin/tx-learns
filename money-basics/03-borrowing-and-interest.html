Borrowing and interest借钱与利息

Understand what borrowing really costs, why interest on interest grows fast and how to keep debt small · ~10 min · reading and practice弄清借钱的真实代价、利息生利息为何增长很快，以及怎样让债务保持在小范围 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The price of borrowing第 1 步 · 借钱的价格

Learn先学

Borrowing means getting money now and paying back more later. The extra is interest, quoted as a percentage per year. Before you borrow, ask three things: how much will I pay back in total, how long will it take, and what happens if I pay late? A low monthly payment can hide a high total cost if it runs for many years.

借钱就是现在拿到钱，以后还得更多。多出来的部分是利息，通常以每年的百分比表示。借钱之前先问三件事：我一共要还多少？要还多久？晚还会怎样？每月还款额低，如果拖很多年，总成本可能很高。

Step 2 · Simple and compound第 2 步 · 单利与复利

Learn先学

With simple interest, you pay interest only on the original amount: 1,000 at 5 percent costs 50 each year. With compound interest, interest is added to the balance, so you then pay interest on interest: after one year 1,050, after two years 1,102.50. The gap looks tiny at first and grows with time. Compound interest works against you on debt and for you on savings.

单利只对本金计息：1,000 按 5% 计算，每年利息 50。复利会把利息加进余额，于是你要为利息再付利息：一年后是 1,050，两年后是 1,102.50。差距一开始看着很小，时间越长越大。复利对债务不利，对储蓄有利。

Step 3 · Costly debt first第 3 步 · 先还最贵的债

Learn先学

If you have several debts, list them by interest rate. Pay the minimum on all, and put any extra on the one with the highest rate, because it grows fastest. When it is gone, move that payment to the next. Avoid new debt for wants, and never borrow to pay another debt without checking the total cost first.

如果有几笔债，按利率从高到低排好。每一笔都付最低还款，多出来的钱全部还给利率最高的那笔，因为它涨得最快。还清后，把这笔还款挪到下一笔。别为了“想要”的东西借新债，也别在没算清总成本之前，借新债去还旧债。

Card A: 1,000 at 20% a year Loan B: 3,000 at 6% a year Extra 100 a month goes to Card A first (highest rate). After A is cleared, send A's payment to Loan B.
