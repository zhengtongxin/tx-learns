Handling money · Lesson 1管好日常的钱 · 第 1 课

Income and spending收入与支出

See where your money comes from and where it goes, so every month you can make a plan instead of a guess · ~10 min · reading and practice看清钱从哪里来、花到哪里去，让每个月都能做计划，而不是靠猜 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · What comes in and goes out第 1 步 · 进与出

Learn先学

Start with two numbers: how much money comes in each month (income) and how much goes out (spending). Look back at the last month of bank records or receipts and add up each side. Many people are surprised by small costs that repeat, such as subscriptions and snacks, because a small amount paid often adds up to a large one.

先弄清两个数字：每个月进来多少钱（收入），出去多少钱（支出）。翻看上个月的银行记录或收据，把两边分别加起来。很多人会被反复出现的小开销吓一跳，比如订阅和零食，因为小额而频繁的支出，加起来就不小。

Step 2 · Needs and wants第 2 步 · 必需与想要

Learn先学

Sort each cost into a need or a want. Needs are things you must pay to live and work: rent, food, transport, basic bills. Wants are things you enjoy but could pause: takeaway coffee, extra apps, new gadgets. Some costs sit in between, so decide honestly. The point is not to ban wants, but to choose them on purpose.

把每项开销归入“必需”或“想要”。必需是为了生活和工作必须付的：房租、食物、交通、基本账单。想要是你喜欢、但可以暂停的：外带咖啡、多余的应用、新玩意儿。有些开销介于两者之间，要诚实地判断。目的不是禁止想要，而是有意识地去选。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A simple plan第 3 步 · 简单的计划

Learn先学

Give every month a plan before it starts: write income at the top, then needs, then savings, then wants with what is left. A simple starting split is about half for needs, a fifth for saving and the rest for wants, adjusted to your life. Check it at the end of the month, and change the plan, not your mood, when the numbers do not fit.

每个月开始之前先做计划：顶部写下收入，接着是必需开销，然后是储蓄，剩下的才是想要。一个简单的起点是大约一半用于必需、五分之一用于储蓄、其余用于想要，再按你的生活调整。月底核对，如果数字对不上，改的是计划，而不是靠心情硬撑。

Income: 3,000 Needs: 1,500 (rent, food, transport, bills) Saving: 600 Wants: 900 Total: 3,000 (nothing left over by accident)

Takeaways要点回顾

• Know what comes in and goes out.
• Separate needs from wants.
