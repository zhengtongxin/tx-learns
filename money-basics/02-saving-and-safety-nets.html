Saving and safety nets储蓄与应急金

Build a cushion for surprises first, save in a place that fits the goal and make saving automatic · ~10 min · reading and practice先为意外攒一笔缓冲金，把钱存在适合目标的地方，并让储蓄自动发生 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The emergency fund第 1 步 · 应急金

Learn先学

An emergency fund is money set aside for surprises: a medical bill, a broken phone, a gap between jobs. Without it, a surprise becomes debt. A common first target is enough to cover three months of needs, but start smaller: even one month of needs makes a real difference. Keep it somewhere you can reach quickly and will not spend on everyday things.

应急金是为意外预留的钱：医药费、手机坏了、两份工作之间的空窗期。没有它，意外就会变成债务。常见的第一个目标是够付三个月的必需开销，但可以从小处开始：哪怕只有一个月的必需开销，也有很大的不同。把它放在能快速取到、又不会被日常花掉的地方。

Step 2 · Goals with numbers第 2 步 · 带数字的目标

Learn先学

Give each saving a goal, an amount and a date: “1,200 for a laptop in 12 months” is easier to keep than “save more”. Divide the amount by the months to find the monthly figure: 1,200 ÷ 12 = 100 a month. Short goals belong in safe, easy-to-reach places; money you will not need for many years can be handled differently, which a later lesson covers.

给每一笔储蓄定下目标、金额和日期：“12 个月内攒 1,200 买笔记本电脑”，比“多存点钱”更容易坚持。用金额除以月数，就得到每月要存的数：1,200 ÷ 12 = 每月 100。短期目标适合放在安全、容易取用的地方；许多年用不到的钱，可以另作安排，后面的一课会讲。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Make it automatic第 3 步 · 让它自动发生

Learn先学

People who save what is left usually find nothing left. Instead, set up an automatic transfer to savings on payday, so the money moves before you can spend it. Start with a small amount you will not miss, and raise it when you get a pay rise. Treat the transfer like a bill you owe to your future self.

“剩多少存多少”的人，通常发现什么也没剩。相反，设一个发薪日自动转入储蓄的转账，让钱在你能花掉之前就已经挪走。从你不会感到心疼的小数目开始，加薪时再提高。把这笔转账当作你欠“未来的自己”的一笔账单。

Goal: laptop, 1,200, in 12 months. Per month: 1,200 / 12 = 100. Automatic transfer: 100 on payday, to a separate savings account. Emergency fund first target: one month of needs (1,500).
