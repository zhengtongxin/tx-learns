Big purchases大额消费

Decide on a large purchase by the total cost, the alternatives and a cooling-off wait, not by the sales pitch · ~10 min · reading and practice按总成本、其他选择和冷静期来决定大额消费，而不是听销售的话术 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Total cost, not the sticker第 1 步 · 算总成本，不看标价

Learn先学

The price on the tag is only the start. Add what comes after: running costs, repairs, insurance, interest if you borrow and what it loses in value over time. A cheap phone that breaks in a year may cost more than a better one that lasts three. Dividing the total by the years you will use it gives a cost per year you can compare.

标价只是开始。再加上之后的花费：使用成本、维修、保险、借钱的利息，以及它随时间的贬值。一部便宜、一年就坏的手机，可能比一部能用三年的好手机更贵。把总成本除以你会使用的年数，得到“每年成本”，就可以拿来比较。

Step 2 · Compare and ask第 2 步 · 比较并追问

Learn先学

Never judge one option alone. Look at two or three, including the choice to do without, to repair, to buy used or to wait. Ask what you really need the item to do, because the best version for that job is often not the most expensive one. Be wary of offers that must be accepted today: a good deal is still good tomorrow.

别孤立地评判一个选项。至少看两三个，包括“不买”、修理、买二手或再等等。问清楚你到底需要它做什么，因为适合这份工作的最佳版本，往往不是最贵的。要警惕“今天必须答应”的优惠：真正的好价格，明天依然是好价格。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Wait, then decide第 3 步 · 等一等，再决定

Learn先学

Add a cooling-off wait: for a non-urgent purchase, sleep on it for a day or more, longer for larger amounts. If you still want it and it fits the plan, buy it with a clear head. Check that you can pay without touching your emergency fund and without borrowing at a high rate. Often the wish fades, and the money stays for something that matters more.

加一段冷静期：对不急的消费，睡一觉再说，金额越大等得越久。如果过后你仍然想要，而且符合计划，就清醒地买下。确认你付款时不动用应急金，也不必以高利率借钱。很多时候，那股想买的冲动会退去，钱就留给了更重要的事。

Laptop A: 900, lasts 4 years = 225 a year. Laptop B: 500, lasts 2 years = 250 a year. Cooling-off: wait 3 days. Pay from savings goal, not the emergency fund.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Total cost, not sticker price.
• Compare real alternatives.
• Wait before you buy.

• 看总成本，不看标价。
• 比较真实的替代选择。
• 买之前等一等。
