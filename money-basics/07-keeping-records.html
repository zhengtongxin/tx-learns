Handling money · Lesson 4管好日常的钱 · 第 4 课

Keeping records记账

Write down what comes in and goes out, group it into a few categories and review it once a month · ~9 min · reading and practice记下收入和支出，归入几个类别，并每月回顾一次 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Write everything down第 1 步 · 把每一笔都记下来

Learn先学

You cannot manage money you cannot see. For one month, write down every amount that comes in and goes out, even small ones. A note on your phone or a page in a notebook is enough. Do not judge yourself yet. The first goal is only a true picture. Small purchases are the ones people forget, and they add up faster than they feel.

看不见的钱，就管不了。在一个月里，把每一笔收入和支出都写下来，小额的也不例外。手机上的一条备忘或笔记本上的一页就够了。先别急着评判自己，第一个目标只是得到一幅真实的图景。人们最容易忘记的是小额消费，而它们累积得比感觉的要快。

Step 2 · Group and total第 2 步 · 分类并合计

Learn先学

At month end, group the entries into a few categories and add each one up. Say income was 2,400, and spending was rent 900, food 500, transport 200 and other 200: 900 + 500 + 200 + 200 = 1,800. Money left is 2,400 − 1,800 = 600, which is 600 ÷ 2,400 = 25% of income. Rent is 900 ÷ 1,800 = 50% of spending. Keep the categories few, or you will stop doing it.

月底，把记录归入几个类别，每类加总。假设收入是 2,400，支出为房租 900、餐饮 500、交通 200、其他 200：900 + 500 + 200 + 200 = 1,800。剩下的钱是 2,400 − 1,800 = 600，占收入的 600 ÷ 2,400 = 25%。房租占支出的 900 ÷ 1,800 = 50%。类别要少，否则你会坚持不下去。

Step 3 · Review and adjust第 3 步 · 回顾并调整

Learn先学

Records matter only if you look at them. Ask three questions: Did anything surprise me? Is a category bigger than I wanted? What is one small change for next month? If “other” is the largest and vaguest line, split it so you can see what is in it. Then change one thing, not five. This is general education, not personal financial advice.

记录只有被翻看才有意义。问自己三个问题：有什么让我意外吗？有没有哪一类比我希望的更大？下个月有哪一个小改变？如果“其他”是最大也最含糊的一行，就把它拆开，看清里面有什么。然后只改一件事，不要同时改五件。本课是一般性教育，不是个人理财建议。

Sort each habit as a good way to keep records or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“记账的好方式”或“弱方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write down every amount for a month, even small ones: you cannot manage what you cannot see.
• Group entries into a few categories and total each one.
• 2,400 income less 1,800 spending leaves 600, which is 25% of income.
• Review with three questions and change one thing, not five.
• This is general education, not personal financial advice.

• 一个月内把每一笔都记下来，小额也不漏：看不见的就管不了。
• 把记录归入几个类别，每类加总。
• 收入 2,400 减去支出 1,800 剩 600，占收入的 25%。
• 用三个问题回顾，只改一件事，不要改五件。
• 本课是一般性教育，不是个人理财建议。
