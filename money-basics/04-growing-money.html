Step 1 · Risk and return第 1 步 · 风险与回报

Learn先学

Money you put to work can grow, but growth has a price: risk. As a rule, things that can grow more can also fall more. A savings account is steady but grows slowly; shares in companies can grow more but can lose value for a while. Match risk to your goal and time: money you need next year should not sit in something that can fall by then. This lesson is general education, not personal financial advice.

投放出去的钱可以增长，但增长有代价：风险。一般来说，可能增长更多的东西，也可能跌得更多。储蓄账户稳定但增长慢；公司的股份可能增长更多，但也可能在一段时间内贬值。让风险和你的目标、时间相匹配：明年就要用的钱，不该放在到那时可能下跌的东西里。本课是一般性的教育，不构成个人理财建议。

Step 2 · Do not put it all in one place第 2 步 · 不要把钱放在一处

Learn先学

Spreading money across many different things is called diversifying. If one thing falls, the others may hold up, so one bad result does not wreck everything. Putting all your money in a single company, or a tip from a friend, concentrates the risk. Funds that hold many things at once are one way people spread risk, though they still carry risk of their own.

把钱分散到许多不同的东西上，叫做分散投资。一样东西下跌，其他的可能撑住，所以一次糟糕的结果不会毁掉一切。把所有的钱押在一家公司、或朋友给的“内部消息”上，风险就集中了。同时持有许多资产的基金，是人们分散风险的一种办法，但它们自己也有风险。

Step 3 · Time and costs第 3 步 · 时间与成本

Learn先学

Time is the strongest helper, because compound growth works on growth. Starting early with small amounts often beats starting late with large ones. Costs matter too: a fee of 1 percent a year takes a bit every year and compounds against you. Be wary of promises of fast, guaranteed high returns; if it sounds too good to be true, it usually is.

时间是最强的帮手，因为复利是在增长之上再增长。早早开始、哪怕金额小，常常胜过晚开始、金额大。成本也重要：每年 1% 的费用，每年都会拿走一点，还会对你不断复利。对“快速、保证、高回报”的承诺要警惕；听上去好得不真实，通常就不真实。

Money needed in 1 year: keep in a safe, easy-to-reach place. Money not needed for 10+ years: can accept some ups and downs. Spread across many things, not one. Red flag: "guaranteed 20% a month".
