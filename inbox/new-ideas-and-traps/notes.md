## Goal
Generate good business ideas with AI and recognise the common traps early.

## Points
1. Where ideas come from: watch the work. Where do people copy, re-type, search, wait or write the same text again? Ask staff for their most boring task. Look at customer questions that repeat.
2. Turn a pain into an idea with one line: for whom, what changes, how we will measure it.
3. Traps: technology looking for a problem; a demo that wows but fails on real data; the last 10 percent of accuracy that costs most; ignoring data you do not have or cannot use; no owner; building what a vendor already sells; a pilot that never ends.
4. Test early: two weeks, real examples, a number, a go or no-go.
5. Keep a short list and kill ideas openly; a clear no is cheap.

## What I got wrong (common slip-ups; tell them through Mdm Rohani or Mr Tan)
- Falling for the demo and skipping the test on real data.
- Starting with the most exciting idea, not the most useful.
- Letting pilots drag on with no end date.

## Try it yourself
Spend one hour observing a team. Write five pains, turn two into one-line ideas, and name the trap each is most likely to hit.

## Takeaways
Ideas come from watching work. Test two weeks on real data, and say a clean no to the rest.
