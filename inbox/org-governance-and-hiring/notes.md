## Goal
Set up the people, the rules and the hiring plan to run AI without chaos.

## Points
1. Three org shapes: central (one AI team serves everyone), embedded (each business unit has its own people), and hub and spoke (a small central group sets standards, units do the work). Small firms: start central, with a named lead.
2. Governance in one page: an owner for each AI system, an approved list of tools, rules for what data may go in, a human check for risky decisions, a way to report problems, and a yearly review. Short, written, enforced.
3. Roles to cover: a sponsor, a product owner who knows the work, someone technical who builds or buys, someone who guards data and risk, and a champion in each team.
4. Hiring: first grow people you have; hire for judgement and curiosity; rent expertise for the first project; hire a specialist only when there is steady work.
5. Training for everybody: what AI is, what to never paste in, how to check an answer.

## What I got wrong (common slip-ups; tell them through Mdm Rohani or Mr Tan)
- A grand policy nobody reads.
- Hiring an expensive expert before there is a project.
- No named owner, so every problem goes to nobody.

## Try it yourself
Draw your org shape on a page, name an owner for one live or planned AI use, and write five rules for staff.

## Takeaways
One named owner per system, a short written rulebook, and a plan to grow people before hiring specialists.
