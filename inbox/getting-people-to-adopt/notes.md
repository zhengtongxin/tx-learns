## Goal
Help people use the new tools, so the project does not die after launch.

## Points
1. Adoption fails for human reasons: fear of replacement, extra work, distrust after one bad answer, and no time to learn.
2. Tell the truth early about what changes in jobs. Say what stays human. Show how the tool removes work they dislike.
3. Start with willing champions in each team; let their results spread. Pick the task people hate most.
4. Make it easy: put the tool where they already work, give a ten-minute guide, and a place to ask.
5. Collect feedback and fix quickly. Celebrate saved hours. Track use: how many people, how often, and whether results are accepted.
6. Leaders go first: use the tool in front of the team.

## What I got wrong (common slip-ups; tell them through Mdm Rohani or Mr Tan)
- Announcing a tool by email and hoping.
- Skipping the people who do the work when choosing the tool.
- Measuring only licences bought, not real use.

## Try it yourself
Pick a tool and write: who are the champions, what is the first task, how people ask for help, and which usage number you will watch.

## Takeaways
People adopt what saves them work and feels safe. Start with champions, remove a pain, and track real use.
