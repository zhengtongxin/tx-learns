Checking and oversight检查与监督

Keep a person in charge: check high-stakes work in full and low-stakes work by sampling · ~10 min · reading and practice让人继续负责：高风险的工作全部检查，低风险的工作抽样检查 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A person stays responsible第 1 步 · 人要继续负责

Learn先学

An AI cannot be held to account, so a person must be. Whoever sends the work is the one who answers for it. This is human oversight: someone with the right knowledge reads the result, can change it and can stop it. If nobody can say who checked it, nobody did.

AI 无法被追责，所以必须有一个人来负责。谁发出这份工作，谁就要为它负责。这就是人的监督：有相关知识的人读结果、能改它、也能叫停它。如果没人说得出谁检查过，那就是没人检查过。

Step 2 · Check by the stakes第 2 步 · 按风险高低来检查

Learn先学

Match the checking to the stakes. Where a mistake could hurt someone or cost real money, check every item. Where mistakes are cheap, check a sample: a small, random part of the whole. Pick the sample without looking, so you do not choose only the easy ones.

检查的力度要和风险匹配。出错可能伤到人或造成真金白银损失的，每一项都查。出错代价低的，查一个样本：整体里随机抽出的一小部分。抽样时不要挑，免得只选了容易的。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Estimate from a sample第 3 步 · 用样本来估算

Learn先学

A sample tells you roughly how common mistakes are. Check 20 out of 200 items, which is 10% of them. If 2 of the 20 are wrong, that is 10% wrong, so you might expect about 20 wrong in all 200 (200 x 10 / 100). It is an estimate, not an exact count. If the rate looks too high, check more.

样本能让你大致知道错误有多常见。200 项里查 20 项，也就是 10%。如果这 20 项里错了 2 项，就是 10% 错，所以全部 200 项里大约有 20 项错（200 × 10 ÷ 100）。这是估算，不是准确数字。如果错误率太高，就多查一些。

200 items, check 20 = 10% of them 2 of the 20 are wrong = 10% wrong Estimate for all 200: 200 x 10 / 100 = about 20 wrong Too many? Check more, or check them all

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A person always answers for the work.
• Check everything where a mistake could hurt; sample the rest.
• Pick the sample without looking.
• Estimate from the sample, and check more if the rate is high.

• 总要有一个人对工作负责。
• 出错可能伤人的全查，其余的抽查。
• 不看内容地抽样。
• 用样本来估算，错误率高就多查。
