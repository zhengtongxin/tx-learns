Using it well · Lesson 3用得好 · 第 3 课

A simple team policy一份简单的团队规则

Write a one-page rule for using AI that a small team can remember and follow · ~10 min · reading and practice写一页小团队记得住、做得到的 AI 使用规则 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three lists第 1 步 · 三张清单

Learn先学

A short rule beats a long one nobody reads. Sort uses of AI into three lists: allowed (drafting, brainstorming, tidying text), check first (anything sent to a customer, anything with figures) and not allowed (pasting private data, deciding about a person with no human check). Keep each list to a few lines.

简短的规则胜过没人看的长规则。把 AI 的用途分成三张清单：允许（起草、头脑风暴、整理文字）、先检查（发给客户的东西、带数字的东西）和不允许（贴隐私数据、不经人工核对就决定一个人的事）。每张清单只写几行。

Step 2 · An owner and a place to report第 2 步 · 负责人和报告的地方

Learn先学

Name one person who owns the rule and can answer questions, and give people an easy place to report a mistake or a near miss. If reporting is blamed, nobody will do it. A report that is thanked tells you where the rule needs to change.

指定一个人负责这条规则、能回答问题，并给大家一个容易的地方报告错误或险些出错的事。如果报告会被责怪，就没人会报。被感谢的报告，会告诉你规则哪里需要改。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Review it on a date第 3 步 · 定一个复查的日期

Learn先学

Tools and risks change, so a rule needs a review date. Reviewing every 3 months means 12 / 3 = 4 reviews a year. At each review, read the reports, drop rules that no longer help and add any that are missing. Write the next review date on the rule itself.

工具和风险都在变，所以规则需要复查日期。每 3 个月复查一次，一年就是 12 ÷ 3 = 4 次。每次复查时，读一读报告，去掉不再有用的规则，补上缺的。把下次复查的日期写在规则上。

Allowed : drafting, brainstorming, tidying text Check first : anything sent to a customer, any figures Not allowed : private data in prompts, decisions about a person with no human check Owner: Mei Report to: #ai-notes Review: every 3 months = 12 / 3 = 4 times a year

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Keep the rule short: allowed, check first, not allowed.
• Name an owner and make reporting safe.
• Set a review date, and write it on the rule.
• Reports from real use are how a rule gets better.

• 规则要短：允许、先检查、不允许。
• 指定负责人，让报告变得安全。
• 定下复查日期，并写在规则上。
• 来自真实使用的报告，让规则越来越好。
