Seeing the risks · Lesson 1看清风险 · 第 1 课

What can go wrong哪里会出错

Name the four ways AI use can cause harm, and judge how much a mistake would cost · ~10 min · reading and practice说出用 AI 造成伤害的四种方式，并判断一个错误会让你付出多少代价 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A confident answer can be wrong第 1 步 · 答得自信也可能是错的

Learn先学

An AI assistant writes text that sounds right. It does not check facts the way a person looks them up, so it can state a wrong date, a made-up quote or a source that does not exist, in the same calm tone as a correct answer. Tone is not evidence. This is the first reason to keep a person in charge.

AI 助手写出的文字听起来对。它不会像人那样去查证事实，所以可能用同样平静的口气说出一个错误的日期、一句编出来的引文，或一个并不存在的来源。语气不是证据。这是让人继续负责的第一个理由。

Step 2 · Four kinds of harm第 2 步 · 四种伤害

Learn先学

Think of four things that can go wrong. Wrong: the answer is false. Unfair: it treats some people worse than others. Leaked: private details end up where they should not. Misused: the tool is used for something it was never meant for, such as a decision about a person with no check. Most cases are one of these four.

想想会出错的四件事。错：答案是假的。不公平：对某些人比对别人差。泄露：隐私信息跑到了不该去的地方。误用：把工具用在它本不适合的地方，比如不加核对就用它决定一个人的事。多数情况都属于这四类之一。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Size the risk by its cost第 3 步 · 按代价衡量风险

Learn先学

Not every mistake matters equally. A wrong word in a party invitation costs almost nothing. A wrong figure in a quote to a customer costs money and trust. Estimate it: how often could it be wrong, and what would each mistake cost to fix? Then decide how much checking the task deserves.

不是每个错误都同样要紧。生日邀请里错一个字几乎没代价；给客户的报价里错一个数字，要花钱，还会损失信任。估一估：多久可能错一次？每个错误要花多少去补救？然后决定这项任务值得多少检查。

200 summaries, about 3 in 100 are wrong 200 x 3 / 100 = 6 wrong summaries 6 x S$50 to fix each = S$300 a month A check that catches most of them costs less than S$300

Takeaways要点回顾

• Confident does not mean correct.
• The four harms are wrong, unfair, leaked and misused.
• Size a risk by how often it could be wrong times what each mistake costs.
• Give more checking to tasks where a mistake costs more.

• 自信不等于正确。
• 四种伤害：错、不公平、泄露、误用。
• 风险按“多久可能错一次”乘以“每个错误的代价”来衡量。
• 错误代价越高，越要多检查。
