Reviewing AI use回顾 AI 的使用

Keep a simple log of AI use, read problem rates over time, and change the team rule where problems cluster · ~9 min · reading and practice记一份简单的 AI 使用记录，读懂问题率的变化，并在问题集中的地方修改团队规则 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Why look back第 1 步 · 为什么要回头看

Learn先学

A team rule for AI is a first guess. To learn whether it works, keep a simple log: for each piece of work that used AI, one line with what it was, who checked it and whether a problem turned up. A log takes seconds to fill in and gives you facts to look at later, instead of memories.

团队的 AI 规则只是第一次的猜测。要知道它管不管用，就记一份简单的记录：每一项用了 AI 的工作写一行，写明是什么、谁检查过、有没有发现问题。记录几秒钟就能填完，日后你看到的是事实，而不是印象。

Step 2 · Reading the numbers第 2 步 · 读懂数字

Learn先学

At the end of the period, count. Say the log has 40 items and 6 had a problem: 6 ÷ 40 = 15%. A problem rate of 15% is high enough to act on. Next period you log 50 items and 3 had a problem: 3 ÷ 50 = 6%. The rate fell by 9 percentage points (15% minus 6%). Use rates, not raw counts, because the number of items changes.

期末时数一数。假设记录里有 40 项，其中 6 项出了问题：6 ÷ 40 = 15%。15% 的问题率高到值得行动。下一期记录了 50 项，有 3 项出了问题：3 ÷ 50 = 6%。比例下降了 9 个百分点（15% 减 6%）。要看比例，而不是单看个数，因为项目的总数会变。

Step 3 · Acting on what you find第 3 步 · 根据发现采取行动

Learn先学

Look for where the problems cluster: one kind of task, one tool, or one missing check. Change the rule for that, for example “customer emails need a second reader”, and date the change. Share what you found without blaming anyone, so people keep logging honestly. Review again after the same period, for example every 3 months, which is 4 reviews a year.

看看问题集中在哪里：某一类任务、某一个工具，或者缺少的某一道检查。针对它修改规则，例如“给客户的邮件需要第二个人看”，并标注修改日期。分享发现时不要责怪任何人，这样大家才会继续如实记录。隔同样的时间再回顾一次，例如每 3 个月一次，一年就是 4 次。

Sort each habit as a good review habit or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“好的回顾习惯”或“弱的回顾习惯”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A log of AI use is one line per piece of work: what, who checked, any problem.
• Problem rate is problems divided by items: 6 of 40 is 15%.
• Compare rates, not raw counts: 15% to 6% is a fall of 9 points.
• Change the rule where problems cluster, and date the change.
• Review on a schedule, such as every 3 months, without blame.

• AI 使用记录每项工作一行：什么、谁检查、有无问题。
• 问题率是问题个数除以总项数：40 项中 6 项是 15%。
• 比较比例而不是个数：从 15% 到 6% 下降了 9 个百分点。
• 在问题集中的地方修改规则，并标注日期。
• 按计划回顾，例如每 3 个月一次，不责怪任何人。
