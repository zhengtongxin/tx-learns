Being open about AI如实说明用了 AI

Decide when to say that AI helped, and how to say it in one clear line · ~10 min · reading and practice决定什么时候要说明 AI 帮了忙，以及怎样用一句清楚的话说出来 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · People deserve to know when it matters第 1 步 · 在要紧的时候，人们应该知道

Learn先学

If someone would decide differently knowing that AI helped, they should be told. A reader of a report, a customer, a teacher marking an essay: each may care. Rules vary, and a school or employer may require a note, so check them. When in doubt, a short honest line costs little.

如果别人知道 AI 帮了忙会做出不同的决定，就应该告诉他们。报告的读者、客户、批改作文的老师：每一位都可能在意。规定各不相同，学校或雇主也可能要求写一条说明，所以先查规定。拿不准时，写一句诚实的话成本很低。

Step 2 · Own what you send第 2 步 · 对发出去的东西负责

Learn先学

Using AI is fine in many places. What is not fine is passing off work as entirely your own thinking when it is not, or sending text you have not read. Read it, correct it, and make sure you can explain every part. If you cannot explain a sentence, change it or take it out.

在很多场合用 AI 没有问题。不对的是：把不全是你自己的思考说成全是你的，或者发出你自己没读过的文字。读一遍、改一改，并确保每个部分你都能解释。某一句你解释不了，就改掉或删掉。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A short disclosure line第 3 步 · 一句简短的说明

Learn先学

A good note is short and specific: what AI did, and what you did. For example, “A draft of the summary was written with an AI assistant; I checked every figure and edited the text.” Vague notes help nobody. Put it where the reader will see it, such as the end of the document.

好的说明简短而具体：AI 做了什么，你做了什么。比如：“摘要初稿由 AI 助手起草；每个数字我都核对过，文字也由我修改。”含糊的说明没有用。放在读者能看到的地方，比如文档末尾。

Weak : "Made with help." Better: "A draft of section 2 was written with an AI assistant. I checked every figure and edited the text." Where : the end of the document, or the cover note

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Say so when knowing it would change someone’s decision.
• Read, correct and own everything you send.
• Say what the AI did and what you did.
• Put the note where the reader will see it.

• 别人知道后会改变决定时，要说明。
• 发出的一切都要读过、改过、能负责。
• 说明 AI 做了什么、你做了什么。
• 把说明放在读者看得到的地方。
