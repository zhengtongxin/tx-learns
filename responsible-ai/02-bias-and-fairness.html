Bias and fairness偏见与公平

See how an AI can treat groups differently, and learn to check results by group · ~10 min · reading and practice看出 AI 怎样对不同群体区别对待，并学会按群体检查结果 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · It learns from the past第 1 步 · 它从过去学习

Learn先学

An AI learns patterns from examples written or chosen by people. If the past examples were unfair, for instance if one group was rarely hired, the pattern can be copied and then repeated at speed. Nobody has to intend this for it to happen. The data simply carries the habit forward.

AI 从人写下或挑选的例子里学习模式。如果过去的例子本身不公平，比如某个群体很少被录用，这种模式就可能被照搬，然后快速地重复。不需要谁有这个意图，数据本身就把习惯带了下去。

Step 2 · Compare results by group第 2 步 · 按群体比较结果

Learn先学

To find out whether a tool treats groups differently, split its results by group and compare the same measure. Do not stop at an overall average, which can hide a gap. Also ask who is missing from the examples. A tool tested only on adults may do badly with children.

想知道一个工具是否区别对待不同群体，就把结果按群体分开，用同一个指标比较。不要只看总体平均数，它可能把差距盖住。还要问：例子里缺了谁？只拿成年人测试过的工具，面对孩子时可能很差。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · A gap is a reason to look, not proof第 3 步 · 差距是要去看的理由，不是证明

Learn先学

A gap can come from bias, but also from small numbers or real differences in the cases. Treat a gap as a signal to look closer: read some cases, ask the people affected, and check whether the rule makes sense. Keep a person in charge of any decision that matters to someone.

差距可能来自偏见，也可能来自样本太小或案例确有不同。把差距当成要仔细看的信号：读一些具体案例，问问受影响的人，检查规则是否说得通。凡是对某人重要的决定，都要让一个人负责。

Group A: 10 of 20 shortlisted = 50% Group B: 4 of 20 shortlisted = 20% Gap: 50% - 20% = 30 percentage points Look closer: read the cases, do not conclude yet

Takeaways要点回顾

• An AI copies the patterns in its examples, fair or not.
• Compare results by group, not only the average.
• Ask who is missing from the examples.
• A gap is a reason to look closer, not proof.

• AI 会照搬例子里的模式，不论公平与否。
• 按群体比较结果，不要只看平均数。
• 问问例子里缺了谁。
• 差距是要仔细看的理由，不是证明。
