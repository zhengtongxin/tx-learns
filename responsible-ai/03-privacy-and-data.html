Privacy and data隐私与数据

Know what not to paste into an AI tool, and how to remove or replace private details first · ~10 min · reading and practice知道哪些内容不要贴进 AI 工具，以及怎样先去掉或替换隐私细节 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · What you paste may be kept第 1 步 · 你贴的内容可能被保存

Learn先学

When you paste text into an AI tool, it is sent to the company that runs it. It may be stored, read by staff or used in ways set out in the terms. So treat a prompt like a message to someone outside your team. Never paste passwords, keys or anything you would not email to a stranger.

把文字贴进 AI 工具，就是发给了运营它的公司。它可能被保存，被工作人员读到，或按条款用于其他用途。所以把提示词当作发给团队以外的人的消息。绝不要贴密码、密钥，或任何你不会用邮件发给陌生人的东西。

Step 2 · Replace details before you paste第 2 步 · 贴之前先替换细节

Learn先学

Often you only need the shape of the text, not the details. Swap names, numbers and addresses for placeholders such as [Customer], [Amount] and [Date]. You get the same help, and the private details stay with you. Add the real details back yourself afterwards.

你常常只需要文字的结构，不需要细节。把姓名、数字、地址换成占位符，比如 [客户]、[金额]、[日期]。你照样得到帮助，隐私细节还留在你手里。事后你自己把真实内容补回去。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Other people’s data, and the rules第 3 步 · 别人的数据，以及规定

Learn先学

Data about other people, such as customers, students or colleagues, is not yours to share freely. Ask first, or use a version with details removed. Your school or employer may also have rules on which tools are allowed. If you are not sure, ask before you paste, not after.

关于别人的数据，比如客户、学生或同事的信息，不是你可以随便分享的。先问一声，或用去掉细节的版本。你的学校或雇主也可能规定了允许使用哪些工具。拿不准，就在贴之前问，而不是之后。

Before: Mei Tan (mei@example.com) owes S$420 since 3 May. After : [Customer] owes [Amount] since [Date]. Ask : "Write a polite reminder for [Customer]." Then fill in the real details yourself.

Takeaways要点回顾

• What you paste may be kept and read by others.
• Replace names and numbers with placeholders first.
• Ask before using other people’s data.
• Check the rules of your school or workplace before you paste.

• 你贴的内容可能被保存，也可能被别人读到。
• 先把姓名和数字换成占位符。
• 用别人的数据之前先问。
• 贴之前先查学校或工作场所的规定。
