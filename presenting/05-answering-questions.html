Answering questions回答提问

Listen to the whole question, answer it briefly and honestly, and say so when you do not know · ~10 min · reading and practice听完整个问题，简短而诚实地回答，不知道时就直说 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Listen first第 1 步 · 先听完

Learn先学

Let the person finish. Answering before the end often answers the wrong question. If the question is long or unclear, say it back in your own words: “So you are asking whether the delay changes the budget?” This shows you are listening, buys a moment to think and checks that you and the room heard the same thing.

让对方把话说完。没听完就回答，常常答非所问。如果问题很长或不清楚，用你自己的话复述一遍：“您是想问，延期会不会改变预算，对吗？”这表明你在听，也给你争取一点思考时间，还能确认你和在场的人听到的是同一个问题。

Step 2 · Brief and straight第 2 步 · 简短而直接

Learn先学

Answer the question that was asked, in a few sentences: the answer first, then one reason or example. Resist the urge to deliver a second talk. If someone asks a hostile question, stay calm, answer the useful part and do not argue. When a question is off-topic, offer to talk after the session instead.

回答被问到的问题，用几句话：先给答案，再给一个理由或例子。别忍不住又发表第二场讲话。遇到带敌意的提问，保持平静，回答其中有用的部分，不争辩。问题离题时，提出会后再聊。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Say “I do not know”第 3 步 · 说“我不知道”

Learn先学

No one knows everything, and guessing is worse than admitting a gap. Say “I do not know yet, but I will find out and send it to you by Friday,” and then do it. Listeners trust a speaker who is honest about the limits of what they know. The box below is an invented example. Write down the questions you receive; they tell you what to explain better next time.

没有人什么都知道，而猜测比承认空缺更糟。说“这个我暂时不清楚，我会去查，周五前发给您”，然后真的去做。听众信任对自己的知识边界诚实的讲话人。下面的方框是一个虚构的示例。把收到的问题记下来；它们告诉你，下次哪里要讲得更清楚。

Q: Does the delay change the budget? A: No. The three weeks are covered by the current team. Q: What is the second bug exactly? A: I do not know the details yet. I will send you the report by Friday.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Listen to the whole question.
• Answer briefly.
• Admit what you do not know.
