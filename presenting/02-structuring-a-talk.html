Structuring a talk讲话的结构

Give the talk a simple shape: an opening that sets up the point, a few clear parts and an ending that says what to do · ~10 min · reading and practice给讲话一个简单的形状：先开场点明要点，再分成几个清楚的部分，最后说明该做什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Open with the point第 1 步 · 开场点明要点

Learn先学

The first minute decides whether people lean in. Skip the long thanks and the history of the project, and say what the talk is about and why it matters to them: “In ten minutes I will ask you to move the launch to March, and show you why.” A listener who knows where you are going can follow you there.

第一分钟决定听众会不会往前倾身。省掉冗长的致谢和项目历史，直接说这场讲话讲什么、为什么和他们有关：“十分钟后，我会请大家同意把上线推迟到三月，并告诉你们原因。”知道你要去哪里的听众，才跟得上你。

Step 2 · Three parts第 2 步 · 三个部分

Learn先学

Split the body into about three parts, each one supporting the message. Three is easy to say, easy to remember and enough to make a case. Give each part a short label, and say them out loud as you go: “First, what we found. Second, what it costs. Third, what I suggest.” If you have six parts, group them into three.

把主体分成大约三个部分，每一部分都支撑核心信息。三个好说、好记，也足够把道理讲清。给每个部分起一个简短的名字，讲的时候说出来：“第一，我们发现了什么；第二，要付出什么代价；第三，我的建议是什么。”如果你有六个部分，就把它们归并成三组。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Close with an ask第 3 步 · 以行动号召收尾

Learn先学

End by repeating your message and saying what you want the listeners to do: approve the plan, try the tool, reply by Friday. Do not end with “that is all” or a trailing “any questions?” alone. A clear last sentence is the one people take away, so write it out in full and practise it.

结尾重复你的核心信息，并说清你希望听众做什么：批准计划、试用工具、周五前回复。不要用“就这些了”收尾，也不要只丢下一句“有问题吗？”。清楚的最后一句，是人们带走的那一句，所以要把它完整写出来，并练习。

Open: In ten minutes I will ask you to move the launch to March. 1. What testing found (two serious bugs). 2. What the delay costs (three weeks, no extra budget). 3. What I suggest (March, with a weekly check). Close: Please approve March by Friday so the team can plan.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Open with the point.
• Use about three parts.
• Close with an ask.

• 开场点明要点。
• 分成大约三个部分。
• 以请求收尾。
