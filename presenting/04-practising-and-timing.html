Practising and timing练习与掌握时间

Rehearse out loud, time yourself and cut to fit, so the talk is smooth and ends when you promised · ~10 min · reading and practice出声排练、掐表计时、按时间删减，让讲话流畅，并在承诺的时间结束 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Out loud, not in your head第 1 步 · 出声，而不是在脑子里

Learn先学

Reading your notes silently feels like practice but is not. Words that look fine on paper are often awkward to say. Stand up and say the talk aloud, as you will give it, even to an empty room. You will find the clumsy sentences, the missing links and the parts that run too long, while it is still cheap to fix them.

默默读笔记感觉像练习，其实不是。纸面上看着不错的话，说出来往往拗口。站起来，像真正讲话那样把全文说出声，哪怕面对的是一间空屋。你会发现拗口的句子、缺失的衔接和过长的部分，而此时修改还很便宜。

Step 2 · Time it and cut第 2 步 · 计时并删减

Learn先学

Time the full run with a clock. Talks nearly always run longer in front of people, so aim to finish a little under your slot (the time you are given to speak), for example 8 minutes for a 10-minute slot. If you are over, cut whole points rather than speaking faster. A calm talk that ends early is welcome; one that runs over time spends the listeners’ patience and your final point.

用钟表给完整的一遍计时。真正面对听众时，讲话几乎总会比排练更长，所以目标是比分配给你的时间（你被安排讲话的时长）略短一点，比如 10 分钟的时段讲 8 分钟。超时了就整点整点地删，而不是加快语速。从容而提早结束的讲话受欢迎；超过时间的讲话会耗光听众的耐心，也牺牲你最后的要点。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Know the edges第 3 步 · 熟记头尾

Learn先学

You do not need to memorise everything, but know your first sentence and your last sentence by heart. A strong start settles nerves, and a sure finish leaves a clear final impression. For the middle, keep only a few cue words on a card, so you speak to people rather than read to them. Rehearse the whole run at least twice before the day.

你不必背下一切，但要把第一句和最后一句熟记于心。有力的开头能稳住紧张，笃定的结尾留下清楚的最后印象。中间部分，只在卡片上留几个提示词，这样你是在对人说话，而不是对着稿子念。正式讲话之前，至少完整排练两遍。

Run 1: 12 minutes (slot is 10). Cut the second example. Run 2: 8 minutes. Good. By heart: first sentence, last sentence. Card: three cue words, one per part.
