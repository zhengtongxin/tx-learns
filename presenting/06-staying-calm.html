Staying calm保持从容

Work with nerves instead of fighting them: prepare well, slow your breathing and your pace, and recover from slips · ~10 min · reading and practice与紧张共处而不是对抗它：准备充分、放慢呼吸和语速，并从失误中恢复过来 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Nerves are normal第 1 步 · 紧张很正常

Learn先学

Almost everyone feels nervous before speaking, including people who do it often. The racing heart is your body getting ready, not a sign you will fail. Listeners usually notice far less of it than you feel. Try to think “I am excited and ready” rather than “I must calm down”, because it is easier to redirect the energy than to switch it off.

几乎每个人讲话前都会紧张，包括经常讲话的人。心跳加快，是身体在做准备，并不是你会失败的信号。听众注意到的，通常比你感觉到的少得多。试着想“我很兴奋，也准备好了”，而不是“我必须冷静下来”，因为把这股能量引导到别处，比把它关掉更容易。

Step 2 · Calming habits第 2 步 · 让自己平静的做法

Learn先学

Preparation is the best calmer: the more you have rehearsed, the less there is to fear. On the day, arrive early, check the room and the equipment, and take a few slow breaths before you start, breathing out longer than in, which often helps the body settle. Plant your feet, and begin slowly. Looking at friendly faces one at a time makes the room feel like conversations.

充分准备是最好的镇静剂：排练得越多，要害怕的就越少。当天提早到场，检查场地和设备，开口前做几次慢呼吸，呼气比吸气更长，这常常能帮身体安定下来。双脚站稳，开头慢一点。一次看一张友善的面孔，整个房间就像是一场场对话。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · When something goes wrong第 3 步 · 出了差错

Learn先学

Slips happen: a lost word, a slide that will not load. Pause, take a breath and carry on; most listeners will not notice or will forgive it. If you lose your place, look at your cue card, or repeat your last sentence to find the thread. If the equipment fails, keep talking without it, since your message was never on the slide alone.

失误难免：忘了词，幻灯片打不开。停一下，深呼吸，继续讲；大多数听众不会注意到，或者会原谅。如果丢了思路，看看提示卡，或者重复上一句找回线头。设备出问题，就不依赖它继续讲，因为你的核心信息从来不只在幻灯片上。

Day before: rehearse twice, pack your cue card (a small card with a few reminder words) and a spare. Day of: arrive 20 minutes early, test the screen. Before starting: three slow breaths, feet planted. If lost: pause, look at the card, repeat the last sentence.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Nerves are normal.
• Prepare, breathe, start slowly.
• Pause and carry on after a slip.

• 紧张很正常。
• 准备、呼吸、慢慢开始。
• 出错后停一下再继续。
