Preparing a talk · Lesson 4准备一场讲话 · 第 4 课

Telling a story讲一个故事

Make a point memorable with one short, real story that proves it, timed and told plainly · ~9 min · reading and practice用一个简短、真实、能证明观点的故事让要点好记，并把它计好时间、平实地讲出来 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Pick one story第 1 步 · 选一个故事

Learn先学

Facts are easier to remember when they come inside a short story: a person, a problem and what changed. Pick one real example that proves your main point, and leave out the others. A story that does not prove the point is only decoration.

事实放进一个简短的故事里更容易记住：一个人、一个问题，以及后来发生的改变。选一个能证明你主要观点的真实例子，其他的先放一边。不能证明观点的故事，只是装饰。

Step 2 · Keep it short第 2 步 · 把它讲短

Learn先学

Give the story its own small place in the talk, and time it. Say you have a 10-minute talk with 3 main points of 3 minutes each, plus a 1-minute opening: 3 × 3 + 1 = 10 minutes. A 2-minute story inside one point leaves 3 − 2 = 1 minute for the rest of that point, so keep a story to about a minute and let it carry only one point.

给故事在讲话里留一小块位置，并计好时间。假设一场 10 分钟的讲话有 3 个各 3 分钟的要点，外加 1 分钟的开场：3 × 3 + 1 = 10 分钟。若在某个要点里放一个 2 分钟的故事，那个要点只剩 3 − 2 = 1 分钟讲其余内容，所以故事控制在大约一分钟，并且只负责一个要点。

Step 3 · Tell it plainly第 3 步 · 平实地讲

Learn先学

Use concrete details (a name, a place, a number) and plain words. Say what happened, then say in one sentence why it matters to your listeners. Do not use a private story or someone else's secret. If you are not sure it is true or fair to the people in it, pick another one.

用具体的细节（名字、地点、数字）和平实的词。先讲发生了什么，再用一句话说它对听众意味着什么。不要用私人的故事或别人的秘密。如果不确定它是否属实、对故事里的人是否公平，就换一个。

To finish Step 3, sort each habit as good or weak: tap a habit, then tap its group.

完成第 3 步：把每个做法分为好或弱。先点一个做法，再点它所属的组。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A short story makes a point easier to remember.
• Pick one real example that proves your main point.
• In a 10-minute talk, a 2-minute story in a 3-minute point leaves only 1 minute for the rest.
• Use concrete details and plain words.
• Do not use private stories or someone else's secret.

• 简短的故事让要点更好记。
• 选一个能证明主要观点的真实例子。
• 10 分钟的讲话里，3 分钟的要点中放 2 分钟的故事，其余内容只剩 1 分钟。
• 用具体的细节和平实的词。
• 不要用私人的故事或别人的秘密。
