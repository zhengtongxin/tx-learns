Preparing a talk · Lesson 1准备一场讲话 · 第 1 课

Know your audience了解听众

Start from who is listening and what they need, then boil the talk down to one message they can repeat · ~10 min · reading and practice从“谁在听、他们需要什么”出发，再把整场讲话浓缩成一句他们能复述的话 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Who is listening第 1 步 · 谁在听

Learn先学

A talk is for the listeners, not for you. Before writing anything, ask three questions: who will be in the room, what do they already know, and what do they want from this talk? A team that knows the project needs the decision and the next step; a visitor who is new to it needs the background first. The same facts need a different talk for each group.

讲话是为听众而做，不是为你自己。动笔之前先问三件事：房间里会有谁？他们已经知道什么？他们想从这场讲话里得到什么？熟悉项目的团队需要的是决定和下一步；刚接触的访客首先需要背景。同样的事实，对不同的人要讲成不同的样子。

Step 2 · One message第 2 步 · 一句核心信息

Learn先学

Write the point of your talk as one sentence a listener could repeat to a friend tomorrow: “We should move the launch to March because testing needs three more weeks.” If you cannot say it in a sentence, you are not ready to say it in twenty minutes. Everything else in the talk exists to explain or support that sentence.

把讲话的要点写成一句话，一句听众明天能转述给朋友的话：“我们应该把上线推迟到三月，因为测试还需要三周。”如果一句话说不清，二十分钟也说不清。讲话里的其他一切，都是为了解释或支撑这句话。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Cut to fit第 3 步 · 按需删减

Learn先学

Test each point against the message: does it help the listeners understand or accept it? If not, cut it, even if it took hours to find. Listeners remember a few things well and many things badly. Keep the extra material in your notes for questions, where it earns its place without crowding the talk.

把每一点和核心信息对照：它帮听众理解或接受这句话吗？如果没有，就删掉，哪怕你花了几个小时才找到它。听众能把少数几件事记牢，却记不住很多件事。多余的材料留在笔记里备问答用，在那里它有用武之地，又不会挤占讲话。

Audience: the project team (know the project, short on time). They want: the decision and what to do next. Message: Move the launch to March; testing needs three more weeks. Cut: the history of the tool choice (keep in notes for questions).

Takeaways要点回顾

• Know who is listening.
• Write one message.
• Cut what does not serve it.
