Slides that help有用的幻灯片

Use slides to support what you say, not to replace it: one idea per slide, few words and a picture or number worth seeing · ~10 min · reading and practice让幻灯片辅助你讲，而不是替你讲：每张一个想法，字少，配一张值得看的图或一个数字 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One idea per slide第 1 步 · 一张一个想法

Learn先学

Listeners cannot read and listen at the same time, so a slide full of text splits their attention and loses both. Give each slide one idea, and make its title say it as a sentence: “Testing found two serious bugs” instead of “Test results”. If a slide needs a long explanation, it is probably two slides.

听众没法同时阅读和聆听，所以满是文字的幻灯片会分散注意力，两头都落空。每张幻灯片只放一个想法，并让标题把它说成一句话：用“测试发现了两个严重的缺陷”，而不是“测试结果”。如果一张幻灯片需要很长的解释，它多半应该拆成两张。

Step 2 · Show, do not list第 2 步 · 展示，而不是罗列

Learn先学

Replace paragraphs with something the eye can take in at once: one large number, a simple chart, a photo, a short diagram. Keep text large enough to read from the back of the room, and aim for a handful of words, not sentences. The details belong in your voice or a handout, not on the screen.

用一眼就能看懂的东西代替大段文字：一个大数字、一张简单的图表、一张照片、一幅简短的图示。文字大到后排也看得清，字数控制在几个词，而不是整句话。细节放在你的讲述或讲义里，而不是屏幕上。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Sometimes no slide第 3 步 · 有时不用幻灯片

Learn先学

A slide is a tool, not a requirement. For a short update or a personal story, talking without slides can be stronger, because everyone looks at you. A blank slide or a pause helps when you want attention back on the speaker. If you use a handout, give it out after the talk, so people are not reading while you speak.

幻灯片是工具，不是必须。简短的汇报或个人故事，不用幻灯片反而更有力，因为大家都在看你。想把注意力拉回到讲话人身上时，可以用一张空白幻灯片或停顿一下。如果有讲义，讲完再发，免得大家一边读一边听。
