Solving it · Lesson 3动手解决 · 第 3 课

Review and learn复盘

Look back after solving a problem to record what worked, what did not and what to do differently next time · ~10 min · reading and practice解决问题之后回头看，记下什么有效、什么无效，以及下次怎么改 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Look back第 1 步 · 回头看

Learn先学

When the problem is solved, or you stop, look back while it is fresh. Compare the result with the goal you wrote at the start. A gap between the two is not a failure; it tells you what to learn.

问题解决了，或者你决定停下时，趁记忆还新鲜回头看。把结果与开头写下的目标比较。两者之间的差距不是失败，它告诉你该学什么。

Step 2 · What worked, what did not第 2 步 · 什么有效，什么无效

Learn先学

Write what worked, so you can do it again, and what did not, so you can avoid it. Look at the process as well as the result: a good result can come from luck, and a bad one from bad luck. Be fair to yourself in both directions.

写下什么有效，好让你再用；写下什么无效，好让你避开。既看结果也看过程：好结果可能来自运气，坏结果也可能来自倒霉。对自己两头都要公平。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · One thing to change第 3 步 · 改一件事

Learn先学

End with one concrete change for next time, not a list of ten. Put it where you will see it when you start the next problem. Small, specific lessons get used; big vague ones do not.

最后写下一件下次要做的具体改变，而不是十条清单。把它放在下次开始新问题时你会看到的地方。小而具体的教训会被用上，大而模糊的不会。

Goal was: 2 hours. Result: 2.5 hours. Missed by: 0.5 hours. Learned: the data step (collecting the numbers) is slow. Next time: get the data ready the day before.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Compare with the goal.
• Note what worked and what did not.
• Change one thing.

• 记下有效与无效。
