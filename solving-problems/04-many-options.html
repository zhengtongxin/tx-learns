Many options多想几个办法

List at least three ways to close the gap and compare them on cost and effect before you pick one · ~10 min · reading and practice至少列出三种缩小差距的办法，按代价和效果比较之后再选 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The first idea is rarely the best第 1 步 · 第一个想法很少是最好的

Learn先学

Your first idea is the easiest to think of, not the best. Make yourself write at least three ways to close the gap, including a very small one and a do-nothing one. Do not judge while you list; judge afterwards.

第一个想法只是最容易想到的，不一定最好。强迫自己至少写出三种缩小差距的办法，其中包括一个很小的、一个“什么都不做”的。列的时候不要评判，列完再评。

Step 2 · Cost and effect第 2 步 · 代价与效果

Learn先学

For each option, write what it costs (time, money, effort) and how much of the gap it closes. Compare on the same scale. The cheapest option is not always best, and the biggest effect is not always worth its cost.

给每个办法写下它的代价（时间、金钱、精力）和能缩小多少差距，用同一把尺子来比较。最便宜的不一定最好，效果最大的也不一定值得它的代价。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Choose and say why第 3 步 · 选定并说明理由

Learn先学

Pick one option and write one sentence on why, naming what you give up. Say what you will check to know it worked. If two options are close, pick the one that is easier to undo and try that first.

选一个办法，写一句理由，并点明你放弃了什么。说明你要看什么来判断它有没有用。如果两个办法差不多，选更容易撤回的先试。

Option A: do it by hand, 2 hours each time; the report stays the same. Option B: make a template (1 hour), then 15 minutes each time; the report stays the same. Option C: stop the report; 0 hours, but there is no report at all. Over 8 weeks, Option A = 8 x 2 = 16 hours.

Takeaways要点回顾

• List three options.
• Compare cost and effect.
• Choose and say why.
