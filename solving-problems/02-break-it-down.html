Understanding the problem · Lesson 2看清问题 · 第 2 课

Break it down把它拆开

Split a big problem into small parts you can start on, and order them so the first one is easy · ~10 min · reading and practice把大问题拆成能着手的小块，并排好顺序，让第一块容易开始 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Big things stall第 1 步 · 大事容易卡住

Learn先学

A big problem feels heavy because you cannot see where to begin. Split it into parts, and split a part again if it is still vague. When every part is something you could start on today, you are done splitting.

大问题让人觉得沉重，是因为看不出从哪里开始。把它拆成几块，某一块还模糊就再拆。当每一块都是今天就能着手的事，就不用再拆了。

Step 2 · Small and clear第 2 步 · 小而清楚

Learn先学

A good part says what to do and when it is finished, such as “write the invitation text”. A weak part is a topic, such as “invitations”. If you cannot tell when a part is done, it is not yet small enough.

好的一块说清要做什么、做到什么算完成，比如“写好邀请文字”。差的一块只是个话题，比如“邀请”。如果说不出这一块什么时候算完成，就还不够小。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Order the parts第 3 步 · 排好顺序

Learn先学

Put the parts in the order you will do them, and start with an easy one: a quick win builds momentum. Note anything that must wait for something else. Check that the parts together cover the whole problem.

把各块按动手的顺序排好，从容易的开始：一次小小的完成会带来动力。标出必须等别的事完成才能做的部分。再检查各块合起来是否覆盖了整个问题。

Goal: send 40 invitations by Friday. Part 1: list the guests (1 hour). Part 2: write the message (1 hour). Part 3: send them in 4 batches of 10 (waits for Part 2). 4 x 10 = 40.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Split it into parts.
• Make each part small and clear.
• Start with an easy one.

• 拆成几块。
• 让每块小而清楚。
• 从容易的开始。
