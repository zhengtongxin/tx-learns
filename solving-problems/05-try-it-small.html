Try it small小步试验

Test your chosen fix on a small scale first and measure it against the gap before you commit to it · ~10 min · reading and practice先在小范围试验你选定的办法，并对照差距衡量，再决定是否全面采用 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Small before big第 1 步 · 先小后大

Learn先学

Before you roll a fix out everywhere, try it once on a small piece: one report, one week, one person. A small test costs little, and if it fails you lose little. Decide in advance what a good result looks like.

把办法全面铺开之前，先在一小块上试一次：一份报告、一周、一个人。小试验成本低，失败了损失也小。事先定好什么样的结果算好。

Step 2 · Measure it第 2 步 · 衡量结果

Learn先学

Use the same measure you wrote in the gap. If the gap was hours per week, measure hours per week with the fix. Compare with before, not with how you feel. Feelings say “it seemed better”; numbers say by how much.

用你在差距里写下的同一个量来衡量。如果差距是每周小时数，就测用了办法之后的每周小时数。和之前比，而不是和感觉比。感觉只会说“好像好了”，数字才说好了多少。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Keep, change or drop第 3 步 · 保留、调整或放弃

Learn先学

After the test, decide one of three things: keep it, change it and test again, or drop it and try the next option. Dropping an idea that did not work is not failure; it is the test doing its job.

试验之后在三件事里选一件：保留；调整后再试；或放弃它，去试下一个办法。放弃一个没用的想法不是失败，而是试验在发挥作用。

Test: try the template once this week. Time before: 2 hours. Time with the template: 1.5 hours (first use, so it needed fixing). Saved: 0.5 hours = 30 minutes. Decision: keep it, and improve it; later uses should be near 15 minutes.
