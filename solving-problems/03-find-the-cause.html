Understanding the problem · Lesson 3看清问题 · 第 3 课

Find the cause找出原因

Look past the symptom by asking why a few times, so you fix the cause and not just what shows · ~10 min · reading and practice透过表面现象，连问几次为什么，修的是原因，而不只是表象 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Symptom or cause第 1 步 · 表象还是原因

Learn先学

A symptom is what you see; a cause is why it happens. Wiping up a puddle treats the symptom, and fixing the leak treats the cause. If you only treat symptoms, the problem keeps coming back.

表象是你看到的，原因是它为什么发生。擦掉地上的水是处理表象，修好漏水点才是处理原因。只处理表象，问题会一再回来。

Step 2 · Ask why again第 2 步 · 再问一次为什么

Learn先学

Ask “why did that happen?” and then ask it again of the answer. Three or four rounds usually move you from what happened to what made it possible. Stop when the answer is something you can change. Blaming a person usually ends the questions too early; ask what let it happen.

问“为什么会这样？”，然后对答案再问一次。通常三四轮就能从“发生了什么”问到“是什么让它成为可能”。当答案是你能改变的东西时就停下。怪罪某个人通常会让追问过早结束；要问是什么让它发生的。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Check before you fix第 3 步 · 先验证再动手

Learn先学

A cause you reached by asking is still a guess. Test it: if you removed the cause, would the symptom go? Look for a case where the cause was absent and the problem too. Then fix it, and watch whether the symptom stops.

问出来的原因仍然只是猜测。验证一下：如果去掉这个原因，表象会消失吗？找一个没有这个原因、也没有这个问题的例子。然后再去修，并观察表象是否停止。

Problem: the plant died. Why? It had no water for two weeks. Why? Nobody was in charge of watering it. Why? There was no rota. Fix: a weekly rota with one named person. Check: once the rota started, was the plant watered every week? Yes, so the cause fits.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Tell symptom from cause.
• Ask why a few times.
• Check before you fix.

• 分清表象和原因。
• 连问几次为什么。
• 先验证再动手。
