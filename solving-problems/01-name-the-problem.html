Understanding the problem · Lesson 1看清问题 · 第 1 课

Name the problem说清问题

Write a problem as the gap between how things are and how they should be, so you know what you are solving · ~10 min · reading and practice把问题写成“现状”与“期望”之间的差距，这样你才知道自己要解决什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A gap, not a complaint第 1 步 · 是差距，不是抱怨

Learn先学

“Reports are awful” is a complaint; you cannot start on it. A problem you can solve says how things are now and how they should be, and the gap between the two is what you work on. Write both sides before you think about answers.

“报告太糟了”是抱怨，没法着手。一个能解决的问题，要说明现在是什么样、应该是什么样，两者之间的差距才是你要处理的。想办法之前，先把两边都写下来。

Step 2 · Whose problem, how big第 2 步 · 谁的问题，有多大

Learn先学

Name who feels the problem, and how much it matters to them. A problem nobody feels is not worth your hours, and a problem with a clear owner is far easier to fix. If you can, give the gap a number so you can tell later whether it shrank.

写明谁感受到了这个问题、对他们有多重要。没人在意的问题不值得花时间，有明确主人的问题则容易解决得多。可以的话给差距一个数字，之后才能判断它有没有变小。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Write it down第 3 步 · 写下来

Learn先学

Put the problem on paper in three short lines: now, wanted, gap. Writing it forces you to be exact, and it gives you something to check any later idea against. If the lines will not come, the problem is not clear yet: ask who feels it and what they want instead.

用三行短句把问题写在纸上：现状、期望、差距。写下来会逼你说得精确，也给你一把尺子，用来衡量之后的每个想法。如果写不出来，说明问题还不清楚：问问谁感受到了它、想要什么。

Now: the weekly report takes 5 hours. Wanted: it takes 2 hours. Gap: 3 hours per week. 3 hours x 4 weeks = 12 hours a month.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write now and wanted.
• Say whose problem it is.
• Add a number.

• 写下现状和期望。
• 说明这是谁的问题。
• 加上数字。
