Working with the tech team on AI choices和技术团队一起做 AI 选择

Ask better questions, get options in one shape, and see real results · ~7 min · decisions问更好的问题，让选项有统一的格式，再看真实结果 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani agreed to be the sponsor, the senior person who backs an AI project. Today it fails in the meeting room, because she nods at words she does not understand. We fix it by learning five questions, one option shape, five words and one small test.

上回，Rohani 女士答应当 AI 项目的发起人，也就是出面撑腰、最后拍板的高层。今天它卡在会议室：她对听不懂的词只会点头。我们的办法是学会五个问题、一种选项格式、五个词，再加一个小测试。

The story故事

On Monday, Devi and her team say: "We could use a large language model with retrieval, tuned for latency." (In plain words: an AI chatbot engine that looks up our own files first, and is set up to reply fast.) Mdm Rohani nods. Mr Tan asks what it costs. Nobody has an answer. Auntie Lim, the senior buyer, has known every supplier for thirty years, and she is not nodding. Mdm Rohani decides she will not decide that way again. Her goal: leave the next meeting knowing what the choices are, what each costs, and what she is betting on.

星期一，Devi 和她的团队说：“我们可以用带检索的大语言模型，并针对延迟做调优。”（大白话：一个先查阅我们自己文件、再回答，而且回得快的 AI 聊天引擎。）Rohani 女士点点头。Tan 先生问要花多少钱，没人答得上来。Lim 阿姨是资深采购，跟供应商打了三十年交道，她没有点头。Rohani 女士决定不再这样做决定。她的目标：下次开完会，她要清楚有哪些选择、各要多少钱、自己在赌什么。

Step 1 · Five questions that open the real trade-offs第 1 步 · 五个问题，打开真正的取舍

Learn先学

You do not need to know how it works. You need to know what you are choosing between. Five questions do most of that. What is the simplest thing that could work? How will we know it is right? What is the worst it can do? What does it cost per month at today's use, and at ten times? What would make us stop?

Think of a doctor who suggests surgery. You would ask: is there a simpler treatment, how do we know it worked, what can go wrong, what does it cost, and when do we stop? Same habit.

你不必懂它怎么运作，只需要知道自己在几个选项之间选什么。五个问题基本够用。最简单、能行得通的做法是什么？我们怎么知道它对不对？最坏会出什么事？按今天的用量，每月花多少？十倍用量呢？出现什么情况我们就停？

就像医生建议动手术，你会问：有没有更简单的疗法、怎么知道有效、会有什么风险、要花多少钱、什么时候收手？是同一个习惯。

Step 2 · Ask for options in one fixed shape第 2 步 · 要求选项用同一种格式

Learn先学

When the team brings one idea, you can only say yes or no. Ask for two or three options, each in the same shape: option, cost, risk, time to learn, and what we give up. End with the team's own recommendation. Same shape means you can compare them side by side, like three quotes from three contractors.

团队只带来一个主意，你就只能说好或不好。请他们给两三个选项，每个都用同一种格式：选项、成本、风险、学会要多久、我们要放弃什么。最后附上团队自己的推荐。格式一样，才能并排比较，就像三家承包商的三份报价。

In the table below, A buys a ready-made tool from a vendor, B has our own staff build a small helper, and C waits. "Data leaves us" means our records sit on someone else's computers.

下表中，A 是向供应商买现成的工具，B 是让我们自己的员工做一个小助手，C 是先等等。“数据离开我们”是指我们的记录存在别人的电脑上。

Option Cost / month Risk Time to learn We give up A Buy a ready tool S$400 data leaves us 2 weeks control B Build a small helper S$150 it may be wrong 3 months staff time C Do nothing yet S$0 we fall behind none the chance to learn Team recommends: A for 3 months, then compare with B.

Step 3 · Five words, then a small test on real examples第 3 步 · 五个词，再做一次真实样例的小测试

Learn先学

Five words save hours. Latency is how long an answer takes. Accuracy is how often it is right. A hallucination is a confident answer that is made up. An evaluation is a test set of questions with known answers. A guardrail is a rule that stops bad output before anyone sees it.

Then, before any big decision, ask for a small test on real examples from your own business, and read some of the results yourself. A demo on clean sample data tells you little.

五个词能省下大量时间。延迟是出答案要多久。准确率是答对的频率。幻觉是自信但编造的答案。评测是一套有标准答案的测试题。护栏是在别人看到之前拦住坏输出的规则。

然后，在任何大决定之前，请团队用你们自己业务里的真实样例做个小测试，并亲自看其中一部分结果。在干净的样例数据上做的演示，说明不了多少。

Step 4 · Trust, but verify第 4 步 · 信任，但要核实

Learn先学

Respect the team's judgement: they know things you do not. But make them show numbers, not adjectives. "Pretty good" and "fast enough" are adjectives. "18 of 20 right, 3 seconds each" is a number. It is like a supplier who says the durians are fresh: Auntie Lim opens one.

尊重团队的判断：他们懂你不懂的东西。但要让他们拿出数字，而不是形容词。“挺不错”“够快了”是形容词；“20 个对了 18 个，每个 3 秒”才是数字。就像供应商说榴莲很新鲜，Lim 阿姨总要亲手打开一个看看。

What I got wrong我犯过的错

Common slip-ups常见失误Nodding along. Mdm Rohani nodded at "retrieval" for ten minutes. Now she says: "Please say that again, with one example from our shop." Asking "can AI do this?" The answer is always "yes, probably". Ask "how good is it on our data?" instead. Letting the team pick both the question and the test. Then they mark their own homework. Mr Tan and she now choose the test examples.只会点头。Rohani 女士曾对“检索”这个词点了十分钟头。现在她会说：“请再说一遍，举一个我们店里的例子。”问“AI 能不能做这个？”答案永远是“大概可以”。要问：“在我们自己的数据上效果有多好？”让团队同时出题又出考卷。那等于自己批改自己的作业。现在测试样例由 Tan 先生和她来挑。

Now make it yours轮到你了

Back at the mart回到超市At the next Monday meeting, Mdm Rohani asks Devi for three options in one shape and a test on 20 real supplier invoices. Mr Tan gets a monthly cost at today's use and at ten times. Auntie Lim reads five of the results herself, and finds two the team missed. Nobody nods without understanding.下一个星期一的会议上，Rohani 女士请 Devi 用统一格式给出三个选项，并用 20 张真实的供应商发票做测试。Tan 先生拿到了今天用量和十倍用量下的月成本。Lim 阿姨亲自读了五条结果，还发现了团队漏掉的两处。没有人再不懂装懂地点头。
What changed这一课之后Now Mdm Rohani can run a meeting with the tech team and decide on trade-offs, not on adjectives. The next problem is that one option, an AI agent (a program that takes steps on its own, not just answers), has costs and failure modes (ways it can go wrong) that are easy to miss.现在 Rohani 女士能和技术团队开会，并凭取舍而不是形容词做决定。下一个问题是：其中一个选项，也就是 AI 智能体（一种能自己一步步行动、不只是回答的程序），它的成本和出错方式（会怎样出错）很容易被忽略。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask five questions: simplest thing, how we know it is right, the worst case, cost now and at ten times, and what makes us stop.
• Ask for two or three options in one shape: option, cost, risk, time to learn, what we give up. Add a recommendation.
• Five words save hours: latency, accuracy, hallucination, evaluation, guardrail.
• Before a big decision, ask for a small test on real examples, and look at some results yourself.
• Trust the team, and make them show numbers, not adjectives.
• Do not nod along, do not ask "can AI do this?", and do not let the team set both question and test.

• 问五个问题：最简单的做法、怎么知道对不对、最坏情况、现在和十倍用量下的成本、什么情况下停。
• 要求两三个格式统一的选项：选项、成本、风险、学会要多久、要放弃什么，再加一个推荐。
• 五个词能省下大量时间：延迟、准确率、幻觉、评测、护栏。
• 大决定之前，请团队用真实样例做个小测试，并亲自看一部分结果。
• 信任团队，但要让他们拿出数字，而不是形容词。
• 不要不懂装懂地点头，不要问“AI 能不能做这个”，也不要让团队同时出题和出考卷。
