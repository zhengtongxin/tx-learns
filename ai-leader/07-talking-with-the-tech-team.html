Working with tech teams与技术团队协作

Ask better questions, get options in one shape, and see real results · ~7 min · decisions问更好的问题，让选项有统一的格式，再看真实结果 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定

Step 1 · Five questions, options in one shape第 1 步 · 五个问题，格式统一的选项

Learn先学

You do not need to know how it works, only what you are choosing between. Ask:

• What is the simplest thing that could work?
• How will we know it is right?
• What is the worst it can do?
• What does it cost per month now, and at ten times?
• What would make us stop?

Then ask for two or three options in one shape (option, cost, risk, time to learn, what we give up) plus the team's recommendation, so you can compare them like three quotes.

你不必懂它怎么运作，只需知道自己在选什么。要问：

• 最简单、能行得通的做法是什么？
• 怎么知道它对不对？
• 最坏会出什么事？
• 每月花多少？十倍用量呢？
• 出现什么情况我们就停？

然后请团队给两三个格式统一的选项（选项、成本、风险、学会要多久、要放弃什么），再附团队的推荐，这样就能像比三份报价一样比较。

Step 2 · Five words, then a test on real examples第 2 步 · 五个词，再用真实样例测试

Learn先学

Latency is how long an answer takes. Accuracy is how often it is right. A hallucination is a confident, made-up answer. An evaluation is a test set with known answers. A guardrail blocks bad output before anyone sees it. Before a big decision, ask for a small test on real examples from your own business, read some results yourself, and want numbers ("18 of 20 right, 3 seconds each"), not adjectives ("pretty good").

延迟是出答案要多久。准确率是答对的频率。幻觉是自信但编造的答案。评测是一套有标准答案的测试题。护栏在别人看到之前拦住坏输出。大决定之前，请团队用你们自己业务的真实样例做个小测试，亲自看一部分结果，并要数字（“20 个对了 18 个，每个 3 秒”），不要形容词（“挺不错”）。

Now make it yours轮到你了

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask: simplest thing, how we know it is right, worst case, cost now and at ten times, what makes us stop.
• Ask for two or three options in one shape, plus a recommendation.
• Know five words: latency, accuracy, hallucination, evaluation, guardrail.
• Test on real examples, read some results yourself, and want numbers, not adjectives.
• Do not nod along, and do not let the team set both question and test.

• 要问：最简单的做法、怎么知道对不对、最坏情况、现在和十倍用量下的成本、什么情况下停。
• 要求两三个格式统一的选项，再加一个推荐。
• 认识五个词：延迟、准确率、幻觉、评测、护栏。
• 用真实样例测试，亲自看一部分结果，要数字，不要形容词。
• 不要不懂装懂地点头，也不要让团队同时出题和出考卷。
