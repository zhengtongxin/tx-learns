Who sells what in AI谁在卖什么

Tell the kinds of AI supplier apart, so you buy from the right one · ~7 min · concept分清 AI 供应商的类型，才能向对的人买 · 约 7 分钟 · 概念课

Step 1 · Six kinds of supplier第 1 步 · 六种供应商

Learn先学

Brochures that look alike often sell different things, like a flour mill, a bakery and a caterer. Sort them first; compare prices second.

看起来相似的宣传册，卖的常常是不同的东西，就像面粉厂、面包店和宴会承办商。先分类，再比价格。

Each brochure line is one supplier. What type is it?

每条宣传册的内容对应一家供应商，它是哪种类型？

Step 2 · Four shapes of an offer第 2 步 · 报价的四种形式

Learn先学

• Subscription seat: fixed fee per person per month. Easy to predict, grows with headcount.
• Usage-based API: pay for what you send and receive. Tiny at first, can surprise later.
• Managed platform: the vendor runs it, billed by size or capacity.
• Custom build: a project, then upkeep. Hardest to leave.

• 订阅席位：每人每月固定收费。好预测，随人数增加。
• 按用量的 API：按收发的量付费。一开始很少，后面可能让人吃惊。
• 托管平台：供应商替你运行，按规模或容量计费。
• 定制开发：先是项目，再是维护。最难离开。

Step 3 · Five questions for any vendor第 3 步 · 向任何供应商都要问的五个问题

Learn先学

Before you sign, ask: Who owns the data we send? Where is it stored? Can we leave and take our work with us? What happens when the model underneath changes? What is the price at ten times our volume? Ask about exits on day one, not year two.

签字之前，先问：我们发过去的数据归谁？存在哪里？我们能离开并带走自己的成果吗？底层模型换了会怎样？用量涨到十倍时价格是多少？退出的办法，第一天就要问，不要等到第二年。

Now make it yours现在轮到你

Takeaways要点回顾

• Model makers sell the brain, cloud platforms the computers and many models, application companies a finished tool.
• Integrators build custom; data and tooling vendors sell the tools behind the scenes.
• An offer is a seat, usage-based API, managed platform or custom build, each with its own price curve and lock-in.
• Ask every vendor: data owner, storage, exit, model changes, price at ten times the volume.
• Test on your own data for a short trial before signing for a year.

• 模型公司卖大脑，云平台卖电脑和许多模型，应用公司卖成品工具。
• 集成商做定制；数据和工具供应商卖配套设备。
• 报价是席位、按用量的 API、托管平台或定制开发之一，各有价格曲线和锁定程度。
• 向每家供应商都问：数据归谁、存在哪、怎么退出、模型变了怎么办、十倍用量的价格。
• 签一年之前，先用自己的数据做一个短期试用。
