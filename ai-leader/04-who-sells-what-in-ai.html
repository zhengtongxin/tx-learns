Step 1 · The layers: brains, platforms, chips第 1 步 · 底层：大脑、平台、芯片

Learn先学

Model makers build the brain, the model itself, and usually sell it through an API: a way for software to send a question and get an answer, priced by how much you use. Cloud platforms rent out computers and host many models in one place, with security settings and a monthly bill, much like the storage and computers many firms already rent. Chip and hardware makers sit under everything and make the processors the models run on.

Think of flour. The mill makes the flour (model maker), the wholesaler stores and delivers many brands (cloud platform), and the oven maker sits beneath the whole trade (chips).

模型公司造出“大脑”，也就是模型本身，通常通过 API 来卖：软件把问题发过去、拿回答案，按用量计价。云平台出租电脑，并把许多模型放在一处，附带安全设置和月账单，和许多公司早就在租的存储、电脑一样。芯片和硬件公司在最底下，造出模型运行所需的处理器。

想想面粉。磨坊做面粉（模型公司），批发商存放并配送多个品牌（云平台），烤炉制造商则在整个行业的最底层（芯片）。

Step 2 · The top: tools, builders, plumbing第 2 步 · 上层：成品工具、定制开发、配套设备

Learn先学

Application companies sell a finished tool for one job, such as a support desk, legal drafting or coding help. You start fast, but you control little. Many of these tools run on a model bought from a model maker, so when that model changes, the tool can change too. Integrators and consultancies build a custom system around your own processes. Data and tooling vendors sell the plumbing, meaning the parts behind a tool: a place to store your documents so the AI can search them, a way to watch a system, and a way to test its answers.

Back to the bakery: a bakery sells ready bread, a caterer designs the whole wedding for you, and the shop that sells you fridges and thermometers is the plumbing.

应用公司卖针对单一工作的成品工具，例如客服台、法律文书起草或编程助手。上手快，但你能控制的不多。许多这类工具底下跑的是从模型公司买来的模型，所以那个模型一变，工具也可能跟着变。集成商和咨询公司围绕你自己的流程定制一套系统。数据和工具供应商卖配套设备，也就是工具背后的部件：一个存放你的文件、让 AI 能搜索它们的地方；一个监控系统的办法；一个检验答案的办法。

回到面包店：面包店卖现成的面包，宴会承办商替你设计整场婚宴，卖冰箱和温度计的店就是配套设备。

Step 3 · Four shapes of an offer第 3 步 · 报价的四种形式

Learn先学

The same supplier can offer more than one shape. A subscription seat is a fixed fee per person per month. Usage-based API charges for what you actually send and receive. A managed platform is a service the vendor runs for you, billed by size or capacity. A custom build is a project: you pay people to make it, then to look after it.

Each shape has a different price curve and a different lock-in. A seat is easy to predict but grows with headcount. Usage can be tiny at first and surprising later. A custom build is hardest to leave, because your processes are now baked into it.

同一家供应商可以提供不止一种形式。订阅席位是每人每月固定收费。按用量的 API 按你实际发送和收到的量收费。托管平台是供应商替你运行的服务，按规模或容量计费。定制开发是一个项目：你付钱请人做出来，再付钱请人维护。

每种形式的价格曲线不同，锁定程度也不同。席位好预测，但会随人数增加。用量一开始可能很少，后面却让人吃惊。定制开发最难离开，因为你的流程已经嵌在里面了。

Step 4 · Five questions for any vendor第 4 步 · 向任何供应商都要问的五个问题

Learn先学

Before you sign, ask: Who owns the data we send? Where is it stored? Can we leave and take our work with us? What happens when the model underneath changes? What is the price at ten times our volume?

It is the same as asking a new produce supplier about delivery days, returns and what happens when the price of durian jumps. Auntie Lim already knows how to do this. The questions are just new words on old habits.

签字之前，先问：我们发过去的数据归谁？存在哪里？我们能离开并带走自己的成果吗？底层模型换了会怎样？用量涨到十倍时价格是多少？

这和你向新的蔬果供应商问送货日、退货规则，以及榴莲涨价怎么办是一回事。Lim 阿姨早就会这一套，只是换了新词。

What I got wrong我当初想错的地方Mdm Rohani nearly made three slips, and Mr Tan caught them. First, she compared a model maker with an application company as if they sold the same thing; one is a brain, the other is a finished tool built on a brain. Second, she wanted to sign for a year before testing on the mart's own data, where a one-month trial would have told her more. Third, she ignored lock-in until the first price rise, when leaving had already become expensive. Ask about exits on day one, not year two.Rohani 女士差点犯了三个错，都是 Tan 先生拦下来的。第一，她把模型公司和应用公司当成卖同一种东西来比；一个卖大脑，另一个卖建在大脑上的成品工具。第二，她想先签一年，再用超市自己的数据测试，其实先试一个月就能知道得更多。第三，她没想过锁定问题，直到第一次涨价才发现要离开已经很贵。退出的办法，第一天就要问，不要等到第二年。

• Model makers sell the brain, usually by API, priced by usage.
• Cloud platforms rent computers and host many models, with security and billing you know. Chip makers sit underneath.
• Application companies sell a finished tool for one job: fast start, less control.
• Integrators build custom systems; data and tooling vendors sell the plumbing.
• An offer is a seat, usage-based API, managed platform or custom build, each with its own price curve and lock-in.
• Ask every vendor: data owner, storage place, exit, model changes, price at ten times the volume.
• Most companies end up with a mix: bought tools for common jobs, a platform for custom ones.
