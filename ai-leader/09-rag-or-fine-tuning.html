RAG or fine-tuningRAG 还是微调

Choose between handing the model your documents and training it further, with one simple test · ~7 min · decision用一个简单的测试，在“把文档交给模型”和“继续训练模型”之间做选择 · 约 7 分钟 · 决策

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani learned what an agent really costs and what can go wrong. Today a new question lands on her desk: Devi's team says "we can use RAG or fine-tuning" and wants her to pick. We fix it by learning one test that tells the two apart.

上回 Rohani 女士了解了智能体的真实成本。今天桌上多了一个新问题：Devi 的团队说“可以用 RAG，也可以微调”，请她选一个。办法是学会一个能分清两者的测试。

Story故事

Heng Huat Mart wants an assistant for store staff. At the Monday meeting Jason says it must know this week's promotions. Auntie Lim says it must sound like the mart, short and warm, not like a robot. Devi, who leads the tech team, writes two words on the whiteboard: RAG and fine-tuning. Mdm Rohani does not need to build either. She needs to know which one fits which problem, so she can ask the right questions and say yes or no.

Heng Huat Mart 想给门店员工做一个助手。周一例会上，Jason 说它必须知道本周的促销；Lim 阿姨说它说话要像超市自己人，简短、亲切，不能像机器人。技术团队负责人 Devi 在白板上写了两个词：RAG 和微调。Rohani 女士不用自己去做，但她要知道哪个问题配哪个办法，这样才能问对问题、说好或不好。

Step 1 · RAG: look it up, then answer第 1 步 · RAG：先查资料，再回答

Learn先学

RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) means the system finds the right passages in your own documents and hands them to the model together with the question. Think of a shop assistant who checks the price-change binder before answering, instead of guessing from memory.

It suits three cases: the facts change often, you need to show the source, or the answer simply lives in your files. It is also easy to update: change the document, and the next answer changes.

RAG（检索增强生成）的意思是：系统先在你自己的文档里找出相关段落，连同问题一起交给模型。就像店员回答之前先翻一下调价记录本，而不是凭记忆猜。

它适合三种情况：事实经常变、需要给出出处、或者答案本来就在你的文件里。它也很好更新：改一下文档，下一次的回答就变了。

Step 2 · Fine-tuning: train a habit第 2 步 · 微调：训练出一种习惯

Learn先学

Fine-tuning is extra training on many examples, so the model behaves a certain way: a tone, a fixed format, or a narrow skill. Think of training a new cashier for weeks until the greeting and the way of handling a refund become habit.

It suits a style or task that stays stable, when you have hundreds or thousands of good examples. The catch: it is slower to change and costs more to redo. If the habit changes next quarter, you train again.

微调是用大量例子给模型做额外训练，让它形成某种行为：一种语气、一个固定格式，或一项很窄的技能。就像培训新收银员好几周，直到问候语和处理退款的方式变成习惯。

它适合风格或任务长期稳定、而且你有几百上千条好例子的情况。代价是：改起来慢，重做更贵。如果下个季度习惯要变，就得重新训练。

Step 3 · The quick test第 3 步 · 快速测试

Learn先学

Ask one question: what exactly is going wrong? If the complaint is “it does not know our facts”, use RAG. If it is “it does not behave the way we want”, consider fine-tuning. But before either, try better instructions (the prompt is the words you send the model with each question) and a few good examples. They are free and take an afternoon.

只问一个问题：到底是哪里出了错？如果抱怨是“它不知道我们的事实”，用 RAG。如果是“它的表现不是我们想要的”，才考虑微调。但在这两者之前，先试试写得更清楚的指令（提示词就是你每次和问题一起发给模型的文字），再放几个好例子。它们不花钱，一个下午就能试完。

Step 4 · Combine them, and ask for proof第 4 步 · 两者可以合用，也要要求看证据

Learn先学

The two are not rivals. Many good systems use retrieval for the facts and a tuned model for the voice, like a cashier trained in the mart's manner who still checks the price binder.

How do you, who do not build it, check the team's choice? Ask them to show the same twenty questions answered each way, side by side. Then you can see the difference yourself, and so can Mr Tan, who will ask what it costs.

两者不是对手。很多好系统用检索负责事实，用微调过的模型负责语气，就像一个受过超市作风培训的收银员，照样会去翻价格记录本。

你自己不做，怎么检验团队的选择？请他们把同样的二十个问题，用每种办法各答一遍，并排给你看。这样你自己就能看出差别，Tan 先生也能，他会问要花多少钱。

Try it yourself: sort six needs. Which approach does each need?

自己试试：给六个需求分类。每个需求该用哪种办法？

What I got wrong我曾经想错的

Mdm Rohani and Mr Tan, in their first week, made three slips that Devi has seen many times:
• Fine-tuning to teach facts. Facts go stale, and once they are trained in they are hard to remove. Facts belong in documents.
• Skipping the cheap options. Clearer instructions come first. They cost nothing.
• Building RAG on messy, outdated documents. The assistant can only be as right as the files it reads. Tidy the files first.

Rohani 女士和 Tan 先生在第一周犯了三个错，Devi 见过很多次：
• 用微调去教事实。事实会过期，训练进去之后又很难删掉。事实应该放在文档里。
• 跳过便宜的办法。先把指令写清楚。这不花钱。
• 在杂乱、过时的文档上搭 RAG。助手的答案只会和它读的文件一样准。先把文件整理好。

Now make it yours现在轮到你

  • Write down one real complaint about an AI tool at your work. Is it a missing fact or a wrong behaviour?写下你工作中对某个 AI 工具的一个真实抱怨。它是缺事实，还是表现不对？
  • Ask your tech team for the same twenty questions answered with a better prompt, with RAG, and (if they suggest it) with fine-tuning.请你的技术团队把同样的二十个问题，分别用更好的提示词、RAG，以及（如果他们建议的话）微调各答一遍。
  • Ask who owns the documents, and when they were last cleaned up.问一问文档归谁管，上一次整理是什么时候。
What changed现在有什么不同

Now Mdm Rohani can tell which of Devi's two options fits a problem, and she can ask for the twenty-question test before she says yes. The next problem is scale: will the choice still work when all the stores use it? Mr Tan will want to know what that costs.

现在 Rohani 女士能分清 Devi 的两个选项各适合什么问题，在点头之前还能要求看二十个问题的对比测试。下一个问题是规模：所有门店都用起来之后，这个选择还行得通吗？Tan 先生会想知道要花多少钱。

Back at the mart回到超市

Jason's question about this week's promotions is a facts problem, so it goes to RAG: change the promo sheet on Monday and the assistant is right by lunch. Auntie Lim's short, warm voice is a behaviour problem. Devi tries better instructions first, and only talks about fine-tuning if they fall short.

Jason 问的本周促销是事实问题，所以交给 RAG：周一改一下促销表，中午前助手就答对了。Lim 阿姨要的简短亲切的语气是表现问题。Devi 先试更好的指令，只有不够用才谈微调。

Takeaways要点回顾

• RAG looks up passages from your documents and hands them to the model with the question.
• RAG fits facts that change, answers that need sources, and answers that live in your files. To update it, change the document.
• Fine-tuning trains the model on many examples so it behaves a certain way: tone, format, a narrow skill.
• Fine-tuning fits a stable style with hundreds or thousands of good examples. It is slower to change and costs more to redo.
• Quick test: "does not know our facts" means RAG; "does not behave right" means consider fine-tuning.
• Try better instructions and examples first. They are free.
• Ask the team to show the same twenty questions answered each way.

• RAG 从你的文档里查出相关段落，连同问题一起交给模型。
• RAG 适合会变的事实、需要出处的回答，以及答案就在你文件里的情况。要更新，改文档就行。
• 微调用大量例子训练模型，让它形成某种行为：语气、格式、一项很窄的技能。
• 微调适合风格稳定、又有几百上千条好例子的情况。改起来慢，重做更贵。
• 快速测试：“不知道我们的事实”用 RAG；“表现不对”才考虑微调。
• 先试更好的指令和例子，它们不花钱。
• 请团队把同样的二十个问题，用每种办法各答一遍给你看。
