RAG or fine-tuningRAG 还是微调

Choose between handing the model your documents and training it further, with one simple test · ~7 min · decision用一个简单的测试，在“把文档交给模型”和“继续训练模型”之间做选择 · 约 7 分钟 · 决策

Step 1 · RAG for facts, fine-tuning for habits第 1 步 · 事实用 RAG，习惯用微调

Learn先学

Two ways to make a model fit your business, and they fix different problems:

• RAG (retrieval-augmented generation): the system finds the right passages in your documents and hands them to the model with the question. Like a shop assistant checking the price binder. Best when facts change, or you must show the source. To update it, change the document.
• Fine-tuning: extra training on hundreds or thousands of examples, so the model behaves a certain way: a tone, a fixed format, a narrow skill. Best for a style that stays stable. Slow to change and costly to redo.

两种让模型适应你业务的办法，解决的是不同的问题：

• RAG（检索增强生成）：系统先在你的文档里找出相关段落，连同问题一起交给模型。就像店员先翻价格记录本再回答。适合事实会变、或需要给出出处的情况。要更新，改文档就行。
• 微调：用几百上千条例子做额外训练，让模型形成某种行为：语气、固定格式、一项很窄的技能。适合长期稳定的风格。改起来慢，重做更贵。

Step 2 · The quick test第 2 步 · 快速测试

Learn先学

Ask what exactly is going wrong. “It does not know our facts” means RAG. “It does not behave as we want” means fine-tuning, but only after you try clearer instructions (the prompt) and a few good examples. Those are free and take an afternoon.

问一句：到底是哪里出了错？“它不知道我们的事实”用 RAG。“它的表现不是我们想要的”才考虑微调，而且要先试过更清楚的指令（提示词）和几个好例子。这些不花钱，一个下午就能试完。

Step 3 · Combine them, and ask for proof第 3 步 · 可以合用，也要看证据

Learn先学

They are not rivals: many systems use retrieval for facts and a tuned model for the voice. To check the team's choice, ask them to show the same twenty questions answered each way, side by side.

两者不是对手：很多系统用检索管事实，用微调过的模型管语气。要检验团队的选择，请他们把同样的二十个问题，用每种办法各答一遍，并排给你看。

Sort six needs: which approach does each one need?

给六个需求分类：每个该用哪种办法？

What I got wrong我曾经想错的

I used fine-tuning to teach facts. Facts go stale and are hard to remove once trained in, so facts belong in documents, and tidy ones.

我曾用微调去教事实。事实会过期，训练进去后又很难删掉，所以事实应该放在文档里，而且是整理好的文档。

Now make it yours现在轮到你

  • Write down one real complaint about an AI tool at work. Is it a missing fact or a wrong behaviour?写下你工作中对某个 AI 工具的一个真实抱怨。它是缺事实，还是表现不对？
  • Ask your tech team for the same twenty questions answered with a better prompt, with RAG, and with fine-tuning.请技术团队把同样的二十个问题，分别用更好的提示词、RAG 和微调各答一遍。

Takeaways要点回顾

• RAG looks up passages from your documents and hands them to the model with the question. It fits facts that change or need sources.
• Fine-tuning trains a stable behaviour (tone, format) from many examples. Slower and costlier to change.
• Quick test: "does not know our facts" means RAG; "does not behave right" means better instructions first, then maybe fine-tuning.
• Ask the team to show the same twenty questions answered each way.

• RAG 从你的文档里查出段落，连同问题交给模型。适合会变的事实或需要出处的回答。
• 微调用大量例子训练出稳定的行为（语气、格式）。改起来更慢更贵。
• 快速测试：“不知道我们的事实”用 RAG；“表现不对”先改指令，再考虑微调。
• 请团队把同样的二十个问题，用每种办法各答一遍给你看。
