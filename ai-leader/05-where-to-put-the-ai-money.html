Where to invest in AIAI 投资去向

Choose which AI bets to fund, how big, and in what order · ~7 min · decisions, no code决定该投哪些 AI、投多少、按什么顺序 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定，不写代码

Step 1 · Three kinds of bet第 1 步 · 三种投注

Learn先学

• Cost bet: the same work for less.
• Revenue bet: sell more, or sell something new.
• Defence bet: rivals will do it, so standing still costs you.

• 降本投注：同样的事，花更少。
• 增收投注：多卖，或卖新东西。
• 防守投注：对手都会做，你不动就吃亏。

Step 2 · Score, then fund in stages第 2 步 · 打分，再分阶段出钱

Learn先学

Mark each idea 1 to 5 on value, effort, risk and how soon you learn if it works (for effort and risk, higher is worse). Favour small, quick, reversible bets: they are cheap to get wrong. Then fund one month, name a number to hit and one person who can say stop. A missed number ends the bet. Across all your bets, put most of the money on proven improvements and only a little on wild experiments.

每个点子按价值、投入、风险和多快能知道行不行打 1 到 5 分（投入和风险越高越糟）。偏向小而快、能撤回的投注：押错了也不贵。然后先出一个月的钱，定下要达到的数字和一个能喊停的人。数字没达到，投注就结束。所有投注合起来看，大部分钱放在已经验证的改进上，只留一小部分给大胆的试验。

Step 3 · Build, buy or partner第 3 步 · 自建、购买还是合作

Learn先学

• Buy what everyone needs.
• Build what makes you different.
• Partner when you lack the people.

• 大家都需要的就买。
• 让你与众不同的就自建。
• 缺人的时候就合作。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Three kinds of bet: cost, revenue and defence.
• Score value, effort, risk and time to learn; favour small, quick, reversible bets.
• Most money on proven improvements, a little on wild experiments.
• Buy what everyone needs, build what makes you different, partner when you lack people.
• Fund a month, a number to hit, a person who can say stop.

• 三种投注：降本、增收、防守。
• 按价值、投入、风险和见分晓的时间打分；偏向小而快、能撤回的投注。
• 大部分钱给成熟的改进，一小部分给大胆实验。
• 大家都要的买，让你不同的自建，缺人就合作。
• 出一个月的钱，定一个数字，指定一个能喊停的人。
