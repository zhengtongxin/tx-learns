Where to put the AI moneyAI 的钱该投在哪

Choose which AI bets to fund, how big, and in what order · ~7 min · decisions, no code决定该投哪些 AI、投多少、按什么顺序 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定，不写代码

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani could tell who sells what in AI. Today it fails when every department arrives with an idea and there is one budget. We fix it by sorting the ideas into three kinds of bet, scoring them, and funding them in stages.

上回，Mdm Rohani 已经能分清 AI 领域谁在卖什么。今天的难题是：每个部门都带着点子来，预算却只有一份。我们把点子分成三种投注，打分，再分阶段出钱。

Story故事

At the Monday meeting, over kopi o and kaya toast, six AI ideas land on the table. Mdm Rohani wants to say yes to the right ones. Mr Tan wants a number, and to know when the money comes back. Auntie Lim asks one question: "Which of these will still be running in six months?" Mdm Rohani has to decide what to fund first, and how much.

周一例会上，大家喝着 kopi o、吃着咖椰吐司，桌上摆出六个 AI 点子。Mdm Rohani 想把钱投给对的那几个。Mr Tan 要一个数字，还要知道钱什么时候收得回来。Auntie Lim 只问一句：“这几个，半年后还有哪个在用？”Mdm Rohani 得决定先投什么、投多少。

Step 1 · Three kinds of bet第 1 步 · 三种投注

Learn先学

Not every AI idea is there for the same reason. A cost bet does the same work for less, like a helper that drafts supplier replies. A revenue bet sells more or sells something new. A defence bet is something you do because rivals will, and standing still costs you. Think of a shop with three tills: each one takes money from a different place, so you judge them differently.

不是每个 AI 点子的目的都一样。降本投注是用更少的钱做同样的事，比如帮忙起草供应商回信。增收投注是多卖，或卖新东西。防守投注是因为对手都会做，你不动就吃亏。想象一家店有三个收银台：钱从不同的地方进来，所以评判的方式也不同。

Cost bet: same work, less money降本：同样的事，花更少·Revenue bet: sell more or new增收：多卖或卖新东西·Defence bet: rivals will, so you must防守：对手会做，你也得做

Step 2 · Score every idea the same way第 2 步 · 用同一把尺给每个点子打分

Learn先学

Give each idea four marks from 1 to 5: value (how much it saves or earns), effort (how much work it takes), risk (how likely it goes wrong), and how soon you learn if it works. For effort and risk, a higher mark is worse. Then lean towards small, quick, reversible bets, because a bet you can undo is cheap to get wrong. That is Auntie Lim's test in practice: a quick bet tells you within weeks whether it is still worth running. A simple split many leaders use: most of the budget on proven, boring improvements, some on promising bets, a little on wild experiments.

每个点子打四个分，从 1 到 5：价值（能省或能赚多少）、投入（要花多少力气）、风险（出错的可能有多大），以及多快能知道行不行。投入和风险的分越高，就越糟。然后偏向小而快、能撤回的投注，因为能撤回的投注，押错了也不贵。这就是 Auntie Lim 那个问题的做法：快的投注，几周内就能知道它还值不值得留下。很多领导者用一个简单的分法：大部分预算投给成熟、无聊的改进，一部分投给有希望的投注，一小部分留给大胆的实验。

Step 3 · Build, buy or partner第 3 步 · 自建、购买还是合作

Learn先学

Once an idea is chosen, ask who does it. Buy what everyone needs, like a standard writing helper. Build what makes you different, like what only Auntie Lim knows about suppliers. Partner when you lack the people. It is like the stockroom: you buy rice, you pack your own gift hampers, and for the Hari Raya rush you team up with a wholesaler who brings its own lorries and drivers.

选好点子后，问问谁来做。大家都需要的就买，比如通用的写作助手。让你与众不同的就自建，比如只有 Auntie Lim 才懂的供应商门道。缺人的时候就合作。就像仓库：米是买的，礼篮自己装，Hari Raya 旺季则和批发商合作，由对方出车、出人。

Step 4 · Fund in stages, with a stop rule第 4 步 · 分阶段出钱，并设停止规则

Learn先学

Do not hand over a year of money at once. Fund one month, name a number to hit, and name one person who can say stop. If the number is missed, the bet ends and the money goes back in the pot. It is the shop-floor rule: you try a new shelf layout in one store before all thirty.

别一次给出一整年的钱。先出一个月的，定下要达到的数字，再指定一个能喊停的人。数字没达到，这个投注就结束，钱回到池子里。这就像门店的做法：新货架布局先在一家店试，而不是三十家一起换。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方Three slips Mdm Rohani nearly made. She wanted a big flashy project because a rival announced one. Mr Tan pointed out she was about to spread money thinly across several pilots that nobody owned. And her first plan had no stop rule, so a failing bet would have lived on for a year.Mdm Rohani 差点犯的三个错。对手宣布了一个大项目，她就想跟着做一个大而闪的。Mr Tan 提醒她，她正要把钱薄薄地撒在好几个没人负责的试点上。而且她的第一版计划没有停止规则，一个失败的投注会白白活一年。

Takeaways要点回顾

• There are three kinds of bet: cost, revenue and defence.
• Score every idea on value, effort, risk and how soon you learn.
• Prefer small, quick, reversible bets first; keep a few bigger ones.
• Most money on proven improvements, some on promising bets, a little on wild experiments.
• Buy what everyone needs, build what makes you different, partner when you lack people.
• Fund in stages: a month, a number to hit, a person who can say stop.
• A rival's announcement is not a reason to fund.

• 投注有三种：降本、增收、防守。
• 每个点子都按价值、投入、风险和多快见分晓来打分。
• 先投小而快、能撤回的，再留几个更大的。
• 大部分钱给成熟的改进，一部分给有希望的，一小部分给大胆实验。
• 大家都要的买，让你不同的自建，缺人就合作。
• 分阶段出钱：一个月、一个数字、一个能喊停的人。
• 对手宣布了什么，并不是出钱的理由。
