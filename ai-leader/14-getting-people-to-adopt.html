Leading the change · Lesson 4带领变革 · 第 4 课

Driving AI adoption推动 AI 落地

A tool nobody uses saves nobody any money · ~7 min · decisions, no code没人用的工具省不下一分钱 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定，不写代码

Step 1 · Why people do not use it第 1 步 · 为什么大家不用

Learn先学

Adoption means people really use the tool in daily work. It fails for human reasons:

• fear of being replaced
• expecting extra work
• one bad answer, and trust is gone
• no time to learn on a busy day

采用，意思是大家真的在日常工作里用这个工具。它失败，原因是人：

• 怕被取代
• 预期要多干活
• 一次错误回答，信任就没了
• 忙的时候没空学

Step 2 · Tell the truth, start with a pain, use champions第 2 步 · 说实话，从痛点开始，靠带头人

Learn先学

• Say early what changes and what stays human.
• Make the first task the one people hate most: a tool that removes a pain sells itself.
• A champion is a willing person in each team who tries it first, so colleagues hear it from a friend.
• Make it easy: put it where people already work, add a ten-minute guide and one place to ask.

• 尽早说清什么会变、什么仍由人来做。
• 第一件事选大家最讨厌的任务：能去掉痛点的工具，自己就会被推荐开来。
• 带头人是每个团队里愿意先试用的人，同事是从朋友嘴里听到。
• 让它变简单：放在大家本来就在用的地方，配一份十分钟指南和一个提问的地方。

Sort each store task: a good first task for an AI helper, or not yet?

逐个判断门店工作：适合让 AI 助手先做，还是暂时不要？

Step 3 · Measure real use, and go first第 3 步 · 量真实使用，并且带头用

Learn先学

Fix feedback quickly and celebrate saved hours out loud. Track real use: how many people, how often, whether they accept the results. Licences bought tell you nothing. Leaders go first, using the tool in front of everyone.

快速修复反馈，并大声庆祝省下的时间。追踪真实使用：多少人、多久一次、结果被接受的比例。买了多少授权说明不了什么。领导先用，当着所有人的面使用这个工具。

What I got wrong我犯过的错I announced the tool by email and hoped. Nobody opened it. Ask the people who do the work first, show it live, and count real use.我发了封邮件通知，然后就等着。没人打开。先问干活的人，当面演示，再统计真实使用。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Adoption fails for human reasons: fear, extra work, one bad answer, no time.
• Tell the truth early about what changes and what stays human.
• Start with willing champions and the task people hate most.
• Put the tool where people work, with a short guide and a place to ask.
• Track real use, not licences, and let leaders go first.

• 采用失败，原因是人：担心、多干活、一次错误回答、没时间。
• 尽早说实话：什么会变，什么仍由人来做。
• 从愿意试的带头人和大家最讨厌的任务开始。
• 把工具放在大家工作的地方，配一份简短指南和一个提问的地方。
• 追踪真实使用而不是授权数，并让领导先用。
