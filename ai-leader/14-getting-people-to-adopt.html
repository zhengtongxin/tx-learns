Leading the change · Lesson 4带领变革 · 第 4 课

Getting people to adopt AI and agents让大家真正用起 AI 和智能体

A tool nobody uses saves nobody any money · ~7 min · decisions, no code没人用的工具省不下一分钱 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定，不写代码

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani had a roadmap with room for experiments. Today it fails when the first tool goes live and the stores do not touch it. We fix it by planning how people will adopt it, before launch day.

上回 Rohani 女士有了一份给实验留空间的路线图。今天它的问题是：第一个工具上线了，门店却没人碰它。办法是在上线之前，先规划大家怎么接受并用起来。

The story故事

Heng Huat Mart has an AI helper that drafts supplier orders. It is an agent: software that reads, decides and does a task for you. Devi's team built it, and Mr Tan approved the cost. On Monday, Mdm Rohani sends an email to all 30 stores: "The new tool is ready. Please use it." By Friday, almost nobody has opened it. Mdm Rohani's goal: make the tool part of the week, not another email people ignore.

Heng Huat Mart 有了一个帮忙起草供应商订单的 AI 助手。它就是一个智能体：替你读资料、做判断、完成任务的软件。Devi 的团队做的，Tan 先生批了费用。周一，Rohani 女士给 30 家门店发了邮件：“新工具好了，请使用。”到周五，几乎没人打开过。她的目标：让这个工具成为一周工作的一部分，而不是又一封被忽略的邮件。

Step 1 · Why people do not use it第 1 步 · 为什么大家不用

Learn先学

Adoption means people really use the tool in their daily work. It fails for human reasons, not technical ones. People fear being replaced. They expect extra work. One bad answer makes them distrust it. And nobody has time to learn something new on a busy Saturday.

采用，意思是大家真的在日常工作里用这个工具。它失败，原因是人，不是技术。有人怕被取代，有人预期要多干活，一次错误的回答就让人不信任，而且忙碌的周六谁也没空学新东西。

Think of a new price-label gun in the chilled aisle. If it is slower than the old one, the staff keep the old one in the drawer.

就像冷藏区新换的标价枪：如果比旧的慢，员工就会把旧的留在抽屉里继续用。

Auntie Lim, the senior buyer, says: "If the helper gets one supplier wrong, I will check everything myself forever." Which worry is that?

资深采购 Lim 阿姨说：“它要是弄错一个供应商，我以后什么都得自己再查一遍。”这是哪种担心？

Step 2 · Tell the truth, then pick a pain第 2 步 · 说实话，再挑一个痛点

Learn先学

Tell people early what will change in their jobs, and what stays human. Auntie Lim still decides which supplier to trust. The tool only removes the copy-and-paste she dislikes. Then pick the task people hate most as the first one. A tool that removes a pain sells itself.

要尽早告诉大家：工作里什么会变，什么仍然由人来做。该信哪家供应商，还是 Lim 阿姨说了算；工具只是帮她省掉最讨厌的复制粘贴。然后把大家最讨厌的任务选作第一件事。能去掉痛点的工具，自己就会被推荐开来。

Jason lists four store tasks. Sort each one: a good first task for the helper, or not yet?

Jason 列出四项门店工作。逐个判断：适合做第一件事，还是暂时不适合？

Step 3 · Champions, and making it easy第 3 步 · 带头人，和让它变简单

Learn先学

A champion is a willing person in each team who tries the tool first. When a champion saves two hours, colleagues hear it from a friend, not from head office. Then make it easy: put the tool where people already work, give a ten-minute guide, and name one place to ask for help.

带头人是每个团队里愿意先试用的人。带头人省下两小时，同事是从朋友嘴里听到，而不是从总部。然后让它变简单：把工具放在大家本来就在用的地方，给一份十分钟的指南，再指定一个可以提问的地方。

Mdm Rohani wants to start in one store. Who should she ask first?

Rohani 女士想先在一家门店试行。她应该先找谁？

Step 4 · Measure real use, and go first第 4 步 · 量真实使用，并且带头用

Learn先学

Listen to feedback and fix things quickly, so people see their words matter. Celebrate saved hours out loud. And track use: how many people, how often, and whether they accept the results. Licences bought tell you nothing. Last, leaders go first: Mdm Rohani uses the tool in the Monday meeting, in front of everyone.

认真听反馈，并且快速修复，让大家看到自己的话有用。大声庆祝省下的时间。还要追踪使用情况：多少人用、多久用一次、结果被接受的比例。买了多少授权说明不了什么。最后，领导先用：Rohani 女士在周一例会上，当着所有人的面使用这个工具。

Mr Tan asks for one number. Which one tells him the tool is really used?

Tan 先生要一个数字。哪一个能说明工具真的在被使用？

What I got wrong我犯过的错Mdm Rohani announced it by email and hoped. Nobody opened it. She also picked the tool without asking the people who do the work, so it did not fit how the stores order. And Mr Tan first counted only the licences bought, which looked fine while real use was close to zero. Ask the doers first, show it live, and count real use.Rohani 女士发了封邮件，然后就等着。没人打开。她选工具时也没问过真正干活的人，所以它不适合门店的订货方式。Tan 先生一开始只数买了多少授权，数字好看，真实使用却接近零。先问干活的人，当面演示，再统计真实使用。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Back at the mart回到超市Now the mart has a plan for people, not only for the tool. Jason's most hated task goes first, one store starts with a willing champion, and Mdm Rohani opens the helper on screen at the Monday meeting. The next problem is proving it was worth it: turning saved hours into Singapore dollars Mr Tan accepts.现在超市有了面向人的计划，而不只是工具。Jason 最讨厌的任务排第一，一家门店从愿意试的带头人开始，Rohani 女士在周一例会上当众打开这个助手。下一个问题是证明它值得：把省下的时间换算成 Tan 先生认可的新元。

Takeaways要点回顾

• People adopt what saves them work and feels safe.
• Adoption fails for human reasons: fear, extra work, one bad answer, no time.
• Tell the truth early about what changes and what stays human.
• Start with willing champions and the task people hate most.
• Put the tool where people work, add a ten-minute guide and a place to ask.
• Track real use, not licences, and let leaders go first.

• 人们会采用能省事、又让人觉得安全的东西。
• 采用失败，原因是人：担心、多干活、一次错误回答、没时间。
• 尽早说实话：什么会变，什么仍然由人来做。
• 从愿意试的带头人和大家最讨厌的任务开始。
• 把工具放在大家工作的地方，配一份十分钟指南和一个提问的地方。
• 追踪真实使用而不是授权数，并且让领导先用。
