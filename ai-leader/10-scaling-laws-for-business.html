Scaling laws for business商业视角的规模定律

Bigger models get better in a steady curve, but each step costs more for less · ~7 min · decisions模型越大越好，进步呈稳定曲线，但每一步花得更多、换来的更少 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定

Step 1 · A steady curve with shrinking returns第 1 步 · 稳定的曲线，回报递减

Learn先学

Train a model with more computing power, data and parameters (its adjustable settings) and its mistakes fall along a predictable curve: a scaling law. The catch: each step costs far more than the last, for a smaller gain. Like cashiers at a Chinese New Year rush: the first extra one clears the queue, the tenth barely helps.

用更多算力、数据和参数（可调节的设置）训练模型，错误会沿着一条可预测的曲线下降，这就是规模定律。代价是：每一步都比上一步贵得多，进步却更小。就像农历新年人潮时多开收银台：第一个让队伍立刻变短，第十个几乎没用。

Step 2 · Re-test, do not patch heavily第 2 步 · 定期重测，别重型补救

Learn先学

Because the curve keeps going, a weakness today is often fixed by the next model for free.

• Something too weak or too dear last year: re-run the same small test every few months.
• If you must patch, keep it small and easy to remove.
• You buy the benefit, not the race: the same ability keeps getting cheaper. Scale does not remove made-up answers or fix a bad process.

因为曲线还在往前走，今天的弱点常常被下一个模型免费解决。

• 去年太弱或太贵的东西：每隔几个月用同样的小测试重测一次。
• 非补不可，就补得小、好拆。
• 你买的是好处，不是参加竞赛：同样的能力会越来越便宜。规模消除不了瞎编，也修不好坏流程。

Step 3 · Match the size to the job第 3 步 · 让模型大小匹配任务

Learn先学

A bigger model (more parameters) costs more per answer. Use a small, cheap one for easy repeated jobs, and keep the larger one for hard, high-stakes work. Sort each job.

更大的模型（参数更多）每次回答都更贵。简单、重复的活用小而便宜的模型，难的、风险高的活才用大模型。给每个任务分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• More computing power, data and parameters make errors fall along a steady curve.
• Each step costs much more than the last for a smaller gain.
• Re-test every few months: too weak or too dear today may work next year.
• Use the smallest model that does the job.
• Scale does not remove made-up answers or fix a bad process.

• 更多算力、数据和参数，让错误沿着稳定的曲线下降。
• 每一步都比上一步贵得多，进步却更小。
• 每隔几个月重测：今天太弱或太贵的，明年可能就行。
• 用能做好这件事的最小模型。
• 规模消除不了瞎编，也修不好坏流程。
