Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani could choose between RAG and fine-tuning for a use case. Today she cannot tell whether to build now or wait, because Devi keeps saying "the next model may fix that". We fix it by learning the one pattern behind how models improve.

上回 Rohani 女士已经能为一个场景在 RAG 和微调之间做选择。今天她不知道该现在就做还是再等等，因为 Devi 总说“下一个模型可能就解决了”。办法是弄懂模型进步背后的那个规律。

The story故事

Last year Devi's team tried an AI to read supplier invoices for Heng Huat Mart. It misread too many totals and cost too much per invoice, so Mdm Rohani said no. This morning Devi says a new model is out. Mr Tan groans: "Every few months something new. Do we wait, or do we build a clever fix today?" Today you learn how to answer that without being a technician.

去年 Devi 的团队试过用 AI 读供应商发票，总额读错太多，每张成本也太高，Rohani 女士说不做。今天早上 Devi 说有新模型了。Tan 先生叹气：“每隔几个月就有新东西。我们是等，还是今天就做个巧妙的补救？”今天你要学会不靠技术背景也能回答这个问题。

Step 1 · The pattern: a steady curve with shrinking returns第 1 步 · 规律：稳定的曲线，回报越来越小

Learn先学

Researchers found that when a model is trained with more computing power, more data and more parameters (its adjustable settings), its mistakes fall along a steady, predictable curve. That is a scaling law. The catch: each step costs far more than the last, for a smaller gain.

Think of the Chinese New Year rush. The first extra cashier clears a long queue. The tenth barely shortens it, and you still pay a wage.

研究者发现：训练模型时投入更多算力、更多数据、更多参数（可调节的设置），错误会沿着一条稳定、可预测的曲线下降。这就是规模定律。但有个代价：每一步都比上一步贵得多，换来的进步却更小。

想想农历新年的人潮。多开第一个收银台，长队立刻变短；开到第十个，队伍几乎没变，你却还要付工资。

Step 2 · Do not build heavy workarounds for a weakness that may vanish第 2 步 · 别为可能消失的弱点搭重型绕行方案

Learn先学

Because the curve keeps going, a weakness today is often fixed by the next model, with no work from you. A workaround is like patching a leaking stockroom roof the week before the landlord replaces it. If you must patch, keep the patch small and easy to peel off.

因为曲线还在往前走，今天的弱点常常被下一个模型直接解决，你什么都不用做。绕行方案就像房东马上要换屋顶了，你还花大钱去补仓库漏水的地方。非补不可，就补得小一点、好撕掉。

Step 3 · Match the size to the job第 3 步 · 让模型大小匹配任务

Learn先学

The biggest model is not always the right one. A model's size is mostly its number of parameters. More parameters means more work for each answer, so each call costs more. Easy, repeated jobs need a smaller, cheaper model, just as you do not send a lorry to deliver one bag of rice. Save the larger model for hard, high-stakes work. Sort each job.

最大的模型不一定是对的那个。模型的大小主要看它的参数个数。参数越多，每次回答要做的计算就越多，每次调用也就越贵。简单、重复的活用更小更便宜的模型就行，就像不会派一辆卡车去送一袋米。把大模型留给难的、风险高的工作。给每个任务分类。

Step 4 · You buy the benefit, not the race第 4 步 · 你买的是好处，不是参加竞赛

Learn先学

Only a few companies can afford to train the frontier models (the newest, biggest ones). Heng Huat Mart does not need to. You rent the benefit, and the race keeps making the same ability cheaper over time. But scaling is not magic: it does not remove made-up answers, and it does not fix a bad process.

只有少数公司付得起训练前沿模型（最新、最大的那一批）的成本。Heng Huat Mart 不需要。你租用的是好处，这场竞赛还会不断压低同等能力的价格。但规模不是魔法：它消除不了瞎编的答案，也修不好一个坏流程。

What I got wrong我曾经想错的

• "Bigger is always better for our task." Mr Tan caught this one: the larger model cost many times more per call and the shelf-label job did not get better.
• Locking in a design around today's limits. Mdm Rohani warned Devi's team: "If we build the whole plan around what the model cannot do now, we throw it away when it can."
• Expecting scale alone to fix wrong answers. A bigger model still made things up. Checks and a clear process stayed our job.

• “越大对我们的任务越好。”Tan 先生抓到了这一点：大模型每次调用贵好几倍，货架标签这件事并没有变得更好。
• 围着今天的局限去锁定设计。Rohani 女士提醒 Devi 的团队：“如果整个方案都围着模型现在做不到的事来建，等它做得到的时候就得扔掉。”
• 指望光靠规模就修好错误答案。更大的模型照样会瞎编。检查和清楚的流程仍然是我们的事。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Back at the mart回到商店Mdm Rohani put the invoice idea back on the list with a re-test date in a few months, and told Devi to keep any fix small. Mr Tan got his answer: wait cheaply, test regularly, and pay for the smallest model that works.Rohani 女士把发票的想法重新放回清单，定下几个月后重测的日期，并要 Devi 的补救方案保持小巧。Tan 先生得到了答案：低成本地等，定期测试，只为够用的最小模型付钱。
What changed这一课之后Now you can plan around models that keep improving, with returns that shrink: re-test, keep workarounds light and match size to the job. The next problem is leading the change itself: finding new ideas and dodging the traps.现在你能围绕“持续进步但回报递减”的模型做计划：定期重测、绕行方案从简、让大小匹配任务。下一个问题是带领变革本身：找到新点子并避开陷阱。

Takeaways要点回顾

• More computing power, data and parameters make errors fall along a steady curve.
• Each step costs much more than the last for a smaller gain.
• Something too weak or too dear today may work next year: re-test every few months.
• Do not build heavy workarounds for a weakness a newer model may fix.
• Use the smallest model that does the job.
• You buy the benefit, not the race. Scale does not remove made-up answers or fix a bad process.

• 更多算力、数据和参数，会让错误沿着稳定的曲线下降。
• 每一步都比上一步贵得多，进步却更小。
• 今天太弱或太贵的东西，明年可能就行：每隔几个月重测。
• 别为新模型可能解决的弱点搭重型绕行方案。
• 用能做好这件事的最小模型。
• 你买的是好处，不是参加竞赛。规模消除不了瞎编，也修不好坏流程。
