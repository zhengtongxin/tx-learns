# Story for the AI Leader track

Setting: Sri Murni Mart, a family-run chain of 30 neighbourhood supermarkets around the Klang Valley in Malaysia. It has never had an AI plan. Everyone keeps hearing about AI; nobody owns it. Puan Rohani, the managing director, has decided to lead it herself, without learning to code. (AI Agentic uses the Penang kopitiam, AI Engineer the Block 12 void deck, AI Production the Pasir Panjang wharf and AI FDE the Tanjong Pagar shophouse; do not mix them.)

Cast:
- Puan Rohani: managing director. Smart, practical, not technical. Asks "what do I decide, and what happens if we are wrong?"
- Mr Tan: finance head. Wants a number and a payback date before he says yes.
- Devi: head of IT and data, a team of four. The honest translator between the tech and the boardroom.
- Jason: store operations manager. Sees the shop floor; worries about staff and about things that break on a Saturday.
- Auntie Lim: senior buyer, thirty years of knowing which supplier is late and which durian is good. Sceptical, and often right.

Props: the chilled-aisle price-label gun, the Monday management meeting with kopi o and kaya toast, the stockroom whiteboard, the supplier invoice pile, the Hari Raya rush every year.

Rule: this track has no code and no tools to install. Every lesson ends with one short "Back at the mart" callout (.callout tip, EN and 中文) just before the takeaways, tying its idea to Sri Murni Mart. Widgets are decisions: sort, predict, choose and see what it costs.

## One thread: Puan Rohani's first AI year

Across the track the mart goes from "we should do something with AI" to a funded roadmap and a measured result. Constraint: no big-bang spending; each step must be small enough to stop. Success: one real improvement, measured in ringgit and hours, that Mr Tan accepts.

- Arc by topic: (1) understand what AI can and cannot do and who sells what; (2) make the first decisions with Devi and the tech team: sponsor, cost an agent, choose RAG or fine-tuning, understand scale; (3) lead it: ideas and traps, who does what and the rules, a roadmap with room for experiments, getting the stores to use it, proving the return.
