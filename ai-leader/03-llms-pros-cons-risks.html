LLM strengths and risks大模型优势与风险

What a large language model is good at, where it fails, and what you must own · ~8 min · decisions大模型擅长什么、哪里会出错、哪些责任要你来扛 · 约 8 分钟 · 做决定

Step 1 · Good at drafts, weak on facts第 1 步 · 擅长初稿，不擅长事实

Learn先学

A large language model (LLM) has read a huge amount of text and writes the next words well. That covers drafts, summaries, translation, sorting messy text and questions over a document. Weak spots:

• It can be wrong in a sure voice (hallucination).
• It does not know your private data or yesterday's news unless you hand it over.
• Ask twice and you may get two answers.
• Very long input gets patchy in the middle.

大语言模型（LLM）读过海量文字，擅长把下一句话写好。这覆盖初稿、摘要、翻译、给杂乱文字分类和针对文件回答问题。弱点：

• 可能用很肯定的口气说错（幻觉）。
• 除非你交给它，否则不知道你的私有数据和昨天的新闻。
• 同一个问题问两次，可能得到两个答案。
• 输入很长时，中间部分会变得马虎。

Step 2 · The risks you own第 2 步 · 你要扛的风险

Learn先学

These land on the company, not the vendor: a wrong answer reaching a customer, private data leaving, copyright questions, biased decisions about people, supplier price or model changes, and staff pasting secrets into a public tool. Controls: a person checks what matters, ask the model to quote the line it used, a written data rule, a log and a plan B.

这些风险落在公司头上，不在供应商：错误答案传到顾客手里、私有数据外泄、版权问题、对人的决定带偏见、供应商涨价或换模型、员工把机密贴进公开工具。控制办法：重要的由人检查、让模型引用所用原文、书面的数据规定、记录和备用方案。

Step 3 · A mistake's cost sets the checking第 3 步 · 错的代价决定检查多少

Learn先学

The cost of a wrong answer decides how much checking you need. Glance at a shift-swap note; read a legal notice twice.

答错的代价，决定你需要多少检查。换班便条扫一眼，法律通知要读两遍。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• LLMs are fast, cheap and fluent: good for drafts, summaries and sorting text.
• They can be confidently wrong and do not know your private data.
• The company owns the risks: wrong answers, leaks, copyright, bias, supplier change.
• Controls: human check, quoted sources, a written data rule, a log, a plan B.
• The cost of a wrong answer decides how much checking you need.

• 大模型又快、又便宜、又流利：适合初稿、摘要和给文字分类。
• 它可能很有把握地说错，也不知道你的私有数据。
• 风险由公司自己扛：错误答案、外泄、版权、偏见、供应商变化。
• 控制办法：人工检查、引用来源、书面数据规定、记录、备用方案。
• 答错的代价，决定你需要多少检查。
