LLMs for the business: strengths, limits and risks大模型对企业：优势、局限与风险

What a large language model is good at, where it fails, and what you must own · ~8 min · decisions大模型擅长什么、哪里会出错、哪些责任要你来扛 · 约 8 分钟 · 做决定

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani had a map of the AI tools and where each one fits. Today she has to say yes or no to a first use, and she cannot judge it. We fix it by learning what a large language model is good at, where it fails, and how much checking each task needs.

上回，Rohani 女士拿到了一张 AI 工具地图，知道每样东西放在哪儿。今天她要对第一个用法说行或不行，却没法判断。解决办法是弄清大模型擅长什么、哪里会出错，以及每项任务需要多少检查。

The story故事

Mdm Rohani runs Heng Huat Mart. At the Monday meeting, Jason says a chatbot drafted a supplier notice in ten seconds. Mr Tan asks what it costs. Auntie Lim asks what happens if it gets the durian price wrong. Mdm Rohani wants one honest answer to both, in business words.

Rohani 女士经营 Heng Huat Mart。周一例会上，Jason 说聊天机器人十秒钟就起草了一份供应商通知。Tan 先生问要花多少钱。Lim 阿姨问，榴莲价格写错了怎么办。Rohani 女士想用商业语言，给这两个问题一个诚实的答案。

Step 1 · What it is good at第 1 步 · 它擅长什么

Learn先学

A large language model (LLM) is a program that has read a huge amount of text and learned to write the next words well. That one skill covers a lot of office work: a fast first draft, a summary of a long email, a translation, sorting messy comments into piles, answering questions over a document, and turning a rough idea into a tidy list.

Think of a keen junior clerk who never gets tired and costs a few cents per task. Great for first drafts. You would still read the draft before it goes out.

大语言模型（LLM）是一种读过海量文字、学会把下一句话写好的程序。这一项本事能覆盖很多办公室的活：快速写初稿、总结一封长邮件、翻译、把杂乱的留言分类、根据一份文件回答问题，以及把粗略的想法整理成清单。

把它想成一个勤快的初级文员：从不喊累，每件活只花几分钱。写初稿很合适，但稿子发出去之前，你还是要自己看一遍。

Step 2 · Where it fails第 2 步 · 它哪里会出错

Learn先学

The same skill has five weak spots. It can say a wrong thing in a sure voice; this is called hallucination. It does not know your private data (your stock list, your contracts) unless you hand it over. It does not know yesterday's news. Ask twice and you may get two different answers, because it picks each word with some chance. And with a very long input, it gets patchy in the middle.

It is like a well-read visitor who has never been inside your stockroom. Fluent, polite and sometimes simply guessing.

同一项本事也有五个弱点。它可能用很肯定的口气说错话，这叫幻觉（hallucination）。它不知道你的私有资料（库存表、合同），除非你交给它。它不知道昨天的新闻。同一个问题问两次，可能得到两个不同的答案，因为它选每个字时都带着一点机会。输入很长时，中间的部分会变得马虎。

就像一位读书很多、却从没进过你库房的访客：说得流利、有礼貌，有时只是在猜。

Step 3 · The risks you own第 3 步 · 你要扛的风险

Learn先学

A risk is a thing that can go wrong and land on the company, not on the software. Six matter most. A wrong answer reaches a customer. Private data leaves the company. Copyright or licence questions come up. A decision about people turns out biased, meaning unfair to some groups. The supplier raises prices, or swaps in a new model that answers differently. Staff paste secrets into a public tool.

You cannot hand these to the vendor. If a wrong price goes on a label, it is Heng Huat Mart's label.

风险，是会落到公司头上的麻烦，而不是软件自己的麻烦。最要紧的有六种：错误答案传到了顾客手里；私有数据流出了公司；出现版权或授权问题；对人的决定出现偏见，也就是对某些群体不公平；供应商涨价，或换成回答方式不同的新模型；员工把机密贴进公开工具。

这些你没法推给供应商。价格写错贴在标签上，那就是 Heng Huat Mart 的标签。

Step 4 · Controls that work, and one rule of thumb第 4 步 · 管用的控制办法，和一条经验法则

Learn先学

Five controls cover most of it. A person checks anything that matters. Give the model your own documents, pasted into the chat, and ask it to quote the line it used, so you can see where an answer came from. Keep a written rule on what data may go in. Keep a log of what the AI did. Keep a plan B in case the supplier changes.

Then one rule of thumb: the cost of a wrong answer decides how much checking you need. It is the same as proofreading. You glance at a shift swap note, but you read a legal notice twice.

五项控制就能管住大部分：重要的东西由人来检查；把你自己的文件贴进对话交给模型，并要求它引用用到的那一行原文，这样你能看出答案出自哪里；用文字写明哪些数据可以放进去；记录 AI 做过什么；留一个备用方案，以防供应商有变。

再加一条经验法则：答错的代价，决定你需要多少检查。这和校对一样：换班便条扫一眼就行，法律通知要读两遍。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方Three slips, told as Mdm Rohani and Mr Tan met them. First, Mdm Rohani trusted a smooth answer because it sounded sure; a confident voice is not proof. Second, she nearly banned AI outright, but staff then used it secretly on personal accounts, which is worse than a rule you can see. Third, Mr Tan counted only the cheap price per task and forgot the cost of the person who checks it.三个失误，借 Rohani 女士和 Tan 先生的经历来讲。第一，Rohani 女士因为答案听起来很肯定就信了；语气肯定不等于有证据。第二，她差点一口气禁用 AI，结果员工会偷偷用个人账号，比一条看得见的规定更糟。第三，Tan 先生只算了每件任务那点便宜的价钱，忘了算检查它的那个人的成本。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Back at the mart回到 Heng Huat MartMdm Rohani now has her answer for the Monday meeting. Yes to first drafts and summaries, with Auntie Lim reading anything that carries a price before it goes out. Not yet for anything that decides about people. Mr Tan adds the checker's time to his sums. Now she can judge any AI idea by asking what a mistake would cost. The next problem is knowing who actually sells these tools.Rohani 女士现在有了周一例会的答案：初稿和摘要可以用，凡是带价格的内容，发出去之前由 Lim 阿姨过目；凡是要对人做决定的，暂时不行。Tan 先生把检查人员的时间也算进账里。现在她遇到任何 AI 点子，都可以先问一句：出错要付什么代价。下一个问题是弄清楚到底是谁在卖这些工具。

Takeaways要点回顾

• LLMs are fast, cheap and fluent.
• They are good at drafts, summaries, translation, sorting text and questions over documents.
• They can be confidently wrong, and do not know your private data or yesterday's news.
• The company owns the risks: wrong answers, leaked data, copyright, bias, supplier change and staff habits.
• Controls: a human check, quoting your documents, a written data rule, a log and a plan B.
• The cost of a wrong answer decides how much checking you need.

• 大模型又快、又便宜、又流利。
• 它擅长初稿、摘要、翻译、给文字分类和针对文件回答问题。
• 它可能很有把握地说错，也不知道你的私有数据和昨天的新闻。
• 风险由公司自己扛：错误答案、数据外泄、版权、偏见、供应商变化和员工习惯。
• 控制办法：人工检查、引用你的文件、书面的数据规定、记录和备用方案。
• 答错的代价，决定你需要多少检查。
