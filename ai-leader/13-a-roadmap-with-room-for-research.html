An AI roadmap with room for research给研发留空间的 AI 路线图

You will learn to write a roadmap that mixes sure wins with experiments, and to defend it · ~8 min · concept你会学会写一份把稳赢项目和实验放在一起的路线图，并为它辩护 · 约 8 分钟 · 概念课

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time we settled who does what, and the rules for AI. Today the board asks for a plan, and a list of tools is not a plan. We fix it by writing a roadmap in three horizons, with research kept safe inside it.

上回我们定下了谁做什么，以及 AI 的规则。今天董事会要一份计划，而一串工具名单不叫计划。我们的办法是写一份分成三个阶段的路线图，并把研究安全地放在里面。

Story故事

Mdm Rohani has to show the Heng Huat Mart board an AI plan for the year. Mr Tan wants a payback date for everything. Devi wants room to try things that might fail. Auntie Lim says, "Show me what already works first." Mdm Rohani needs one page that keeps all three of them happy.

Mdm Rohani 要向 Heng Huat Mart 的董事会展示今年的 AI 计划。Mr Tan 要求每一项都有回本日期。Devi 想留出空间试一些可能失败的东西。Auntie Lim 说：“先让我看看哪些已经行得通。”Mdm Rohani 需要一页纸，让三个人都点头。

Step 1 · Three horizons第 1 步 · 三个阶段

Learn先学

A horizon is how far ahead you are looking. Now (0 to 3 months) holds proven improvements with a clear return. Next (3 to 12 months) holds bigger builds that depend on what you learn first. Later holds bets and research that may not work. It is like planning Chinese New Year stock: you order the sure sellers now, and test a new snack in a few stores.

阶段就是你往前看多远。现在（0 到 3 个月）放已验证、回报清楚的改进。下一阶段（3 到 12 个月）放更大的项目，要看先学到什么。更远放可能行不通的押注和研究。就像备农历新年的货：确定好卖的现在就订，新零食先在几家店试卖。

Sort four ideas into the right horizon.

把四个点子放进对的阶段。

Step 2 · Protect the research第 2 步 · 保护研究

Learn先学

Give research its own slice of time and money, kept apart from the rest. Judge it by different rules: by what you learned, not by return. If an experiment must pay back its cost like the chilled-aisle price-label pilot from Step 1, nobody will try anything risky, and the mart only ever copies what others already did.

给研究单独留一份时间和钱，和其他项目分开。用不同的规则评判：看学到了什么，不看回报。如果实验也得像第 1 步里的冷柜价签试点那样回本，就没人敢冒险，超市只会照抄别人已经做过的事。

Step 3 · One line for every item第 3 步 · 每一项一行说明

Learn先学

Each item on the roadmap needs six things: an owner, a goal with a number, a cost, a review date, a stop rule (when we quit), and what it depends on (data, people or a vendor). Dependencies matter most: the plan slips when the data is not ready, and nobody saw it coming. Think of the stockroom whiteboard: no name and no date means nobody does it.

路线图上的每一项都要有六样东西：负责人、带数字的目标、成本、复盘日期、止损规则（什么时候放弃），以及它依赖什么（数据、人或供应商）。依赖最重要：数据没准备好，计划就会延误，而没人事先看到。就像仓库白板：没有名字、没有日期，就没人去做。

Step 4 · Defend it, then review it第 4 步 · 为它辩护，再定期复盘

Learn先学

Lead with the business result. Then show the cost and the stop rule, what is already working, and, honestly, what is still uncertain. Boards trust a plan that names its own doubts. Then review it every quarter: a roadmap that never changes is a wish list.

先讲业务结果。再讲成本和止损规则、已经在起作用的东西，并老实说出仍不确定的地方。董事会更信任敢说出自己疑虑的计划。之后每个季度复盘一次：从不改动的路线图，只是一张愿望清单。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方Mdm Rohani's first draft was only a list of tools. Mr Tan asked, "What do we get, and by when?" She also judged the research by the same return rules as everything else, so Devi stopped suggesting experiments. And she left out the dependencies, so the plan slipped when the sales data was not ready.Mdm Rohani 的初稿只是一串工具名单。Mr Tan 问：“我们得到什么，什么时候得到？”她还用同样的回报规则去评判研究，结果 Devi 不再提实验。她又漏写了依赖，销售数据没准备好时，计划就延误了。

Now make it yours现在做成你的

What changed这一课之后Now Mdm Rohani can show a one-page plan: sure wins first, a protected slice for experiments, and every item with an owner and a stop rule. The next problem is that a plan on paper does nothing until the stores actually use it.现在 Mdm Rohani 能拿出一页计划：稳赢的先做，实验有受保护的一份，每一项都有负责人和止损规则。下一个问题是：计划写在纸上没有用，要门店真的用起来才算数。
Back at the mart回到超市Mdm Rohani put the chilled-aisle price labels and invoice filling under Now, forecasting under Next, and the supplier-negotiating agent under Later as a small, protected experiment. Mr Tan got his payback dates for the first two. Devi got her experiment, judged by what it teaches. Auntie Lim saw her own proven work at the front.Mdm Rohani 把冷柜价签和发票录入放进“现在”，需求预测放进“下一阶段”，自动和供应商谈判的智能体作为一个小而受保护的实验放进“更远”。Mr Tan 拿到了前两项的回本日期。Devi 得到了她的实验，按学到的东西来评判。Auntie Lim 看到自己验证过的做法排在最前面。

• A roadmap has three horizons: Now, Next and Later.
• Mix sure wins with experiments.
• Give research its own protected slice, judged by what you learn.
• Every item has an owner, a number, a cost, a review date and a stop rule.
• Show what each item depends on: data, people or a vendor.
• Defend it with the result first, then the cost, the stop rule and the doubts.
• Review it every quarter, or it is only a wish list.

• 路线图分三个阶段：现在、下一阶段、更远。
• 把稳赢项目和实验放在一起。
• 给研究一份受保护的份额，按学到的东西来评判。
• 每一项都有负责人、数字、成本、复盘日期和止损规则。
• 标出每一项依赖什么：数据、人或供应商。
• 辩护时先讲结果，再讲成本、止损规则和疑虑。
• 每季度复盘，否则它只是愿望清单。
