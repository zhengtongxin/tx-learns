AI roadmap with research room留出研发空间的路线图

You will learn to write a roadmap that mixes sure wins with experiments, and to defend it · ~8 min · concept你会学会写一份把稳赢项目和实验放在一起的路线图，并为它辩护 · 约 8 分钟 · 概念课

Step 1 · Three horizons第 1 步 · 三个阶段

Learn先学

A horizon is how far ahead you look.

• Now (0 to 3 months): proven improvements with a clear return.
• Next (3 to 12 months): bigger builds that depend on what you learn first.
• Later: bets and research that may not work.

阶段就是你往前看多远。

• 现在（0 到 3 个月）：已验证、回报清楚的改进。
• 下一阶段（3 到 12 个月）：更大的项目，要看先学到什么。
• 更远：可能行不通的押注和研究。

Sort four ideas into the right horizon.

把四个点子放进对的阶段。

Step 2 · Protect the research第 2 步 · 保护研究

Learn先学

Give research its own slice of time and money, judged by what you learned, not by return. If every experiment must pay back, nobody tries anything risky.

给研究单独留一份时间和钱，按学到了什么来评判，不看回报。如果每个实验都得回本，就没人敢冒险。

Step 3 · One line per item, then defend it第 3 步 · 每项一行，再为它辩护

Learn先学

Each item needs six things: an owner, a goal with a number, a cost, a review date, a stop rule (when we quit), and what it depends on (data, people, a vendor). Hidden dependencies are why plans slip.

To defend the plan, lead with the business result, then cost, stop rule, and honestly what is still uncertain. Review it every quarter, or it is only a wish list.

每一项要有六样东西：负责人、带数字的目标、成本、复盘日期、止损规则（什么时候放弃），以及它依赖什么（数据、人、供应商）。藏起来的依赖就是计划延误的原因。

为计划辩护时，先讲业务结果，再讲成本、止损规则，并老实说出仍不确定的地方。每季度复盘一次，否则它只是愿望清单。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方My first draft was only a list of tools. The board asked, "What do we get, and by when?"我的初稿只是一串工具名单。董事会问：“我们得到什么，什么时候得到？”

Now make it yours现在做成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A roadmap has three horizons: Now, Next and Later. Mix sure wins with experiments.
• Research gets its own protected slice, judged by what you learn.
• Every item has an owner, a number, a cost, a review date, a stop rule and its dependencies.
• Defend it with the result first. Review it every quarter.

• 路线图分三个阶段：现在、下一阶段、更远。把稳赢项目和实验放在一起。
• 研究有自己受保护的一份，按学到的东西评判。
• 每一项都有负责人、数字、成本、复盘日期、止损规则和依赖。
• 辩护时先讲结果。每季度复盘。
