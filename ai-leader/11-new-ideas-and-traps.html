New ideas and traps新点子与陷阱

Where good ideas come from, how to write one in a line, and which traps to spot early · ~7 min · decisions好点子从哪里来、怎样用一句话写下来、哪些陷阱要早点看出来 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani could make the key decisions on an AI project with her tech team. Today it fails when the Monday meeting fills with exciting ideas and nobody knows which to try. We fix it by finding ideas in the real work, writing each as one line, and testing fast.

上回 Rohani 女士已经能和技术团队一起为 AI 项目做关键决定。今天的毛病是周一会议上点子一大堆，却没人知道先试哪个。解决办法是从真实的工作里找点子，每个写成一句话，再快速测试。

The story故事

Mdm Rohani, managing director of Heng Huat Mart, has a full whiteboard of AI ideas from the Monday meeting. A chatbot. A shelf robot. An AI that writes promotions. Mr Tan asks which one pays back. Nobody knows. Her goal: pick one or two ideas worth a small test, and say a clean no to the rest.

Heng Huat Mart 的董事总经理 Rohani 女士在周一会议后，白板上写满了 AI 点子：聊天机器人、货架机器人、会写促销文案的 AI。财务主管 Tan 先生问哪一个能回本，没人答得上来。她的目标是：挑一两个值得小测试的点子，对其余的干脆地说不。

Step 1 · Ideas come from watching the work第 1 步 · 点子来自观察工作

Learn先学

Do not start from the technology. Start from the work. Where do people copy, re-type, search, wait, or write the same text again? Ask a staff member for their most boring task. Read the customer questions that repeat every week. Think of a doctor: she does not pick a medicine and then look for a patient. She finds the pain first.

不要从技术出发，要从工作出发。哪里有人在复制、重新输入、查找、等待，或者一遍遍写同样的文字？请员工说说他们最无聊的一项工作，再看看每周都在重复的顾客问题。就像医生：她不会先挑好一种药再去找病人，而是先找到病痛。

Devi walks the stores and finds the pains: clerks re-typing supplier invoices, and the same customer questions at every counter. The picture shows the whole path, from the pain to a decision.

Devi 走访各家门店，找到了这些痛点：文员重复录入供应商发票，每个柜台都有同样的顾客问题。下图是从痛点到决定的完整路径。

Step 2 · Turn a pain into one line第 2 步 · 把痛点写成一句话

Learn先学

A pain is not an idea yet. Write each idea in one line with three parts: for whom, what changes, and how we will measure it. If you cannot fill all three, the idea is not ready. It is like a shopping list: “something for dinner” is a wish, but “two chickens and a bag of rice” can be bought and checked.

痛点还不是点子。把每个点子写成一句话，包含三部分：为谁、改变什么、怎么衡量。三样填不全，就说明点子还没准备好。就像购物清单：“晚饭的东西”只是愿望，“两只鸡加一袋米”才买得到、也查得出。

Step 3 · Know the traps第 3 步 · 认识这些陷阱

Learn先学

Most AI ideas that fail fall into a few traps. Technology looking for a problem. A demo that wows but fails on real data. The last 10 percent of accuracy, which costs the most. Data you do not have or cannot use. No owner. Building what a vendor already sells. A pilot that never ends. A pilot is a small trial run. A vendor is a company that sells the tool. Accuracy is how often the tool is right. The last 10 percent is the hardest part, because the odd, messy cases are rare and each one needs its own fix. Think of a road with seven potholes: once you know where they are, you steer around them.

大多数失败的 AI 点子都掉进少数几个陷阱。拿着技术找问题。演示惊艳，碰到真实数据就失灵。最后 10% 的准确率，花费最大。没有的、或不能用的数据。没有负责人。自己做供应商已经在卖的东西。永远不结束的试点。试点是一次小规模的试运行。供应商是卖这个工具的公司。准确率是工具答对的比例。最后那 10% 最难，因为古怪、杂乱的个案很少见，而每一个都要单独修。就像一条有七个坑的路：知道坑在哪里，就能绕开。

Step 4 · Test early, then say a clean no第 4 步 · 早测试，再干脆地说不

Learn先学

Give each idea a two-week test. Use real examples, pick one number (for example, hours saved each week, or the share of answers the tool gets right), and end with a go or a no-go. Keep a short list of ideas on one page, and kill the weak ones in the open. A clear no is cheap. A vague “let us see” is the expensive one, like leaving a tap running in the stockroom.

给每个点子一次两周的测试：用真实例子、选定一个数字（例如每周省下的小时数，或工具答对的比例），最后得出做或不做。把点子的短清单放在一页纸上，淘汰弱的点子也要公开。一个清楚的“不”很便宜，含糊的“再看看”才贵，就像仓库里一直开着的水龙头。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方Mdm Rohani fell for the demo and skipped the test on real data. She also started with the most exciting idea, not the most useful one: the shelf robot, not the invoice typing. And Mr Tan reminded her that a pilot with no end date does not end by itself, so now every test gets a date.Rohani 女士被演示迷住，跳过了真实数据的测试。她还从最令人兴奋的点子开始，而不是最有用的：先想到的是货架机器人，而不是发票录入。Tan 先生提醒她，没有结束日期的试点自己不会结束，所以现在每个测试都有日期。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Back at the mart回到店里Now Mdm Rohani can turn the Monday whiteboard into a short list: Auntie Lim's invoice typing and Jason's repeat customer questions get two-week tests on real files, while the shelf robot gets a clear no. The next problem is who does what: who owns each test, and what rules the team follows.现在 Rohani 女士能把周一的白板变成一张短清单：Lim 阿姨的发票录入和 Jason 的重复顾客问题，各做一次用真实文件的两周测试，货架机器人则得到一个清楚的“不”。下一个问题是谁来做什么：每个测试谁负责，团队遵守哪些规则。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ideas come from watching work: copying, re-typing, searching, waiting, repeat questions.
• Write each idea in one line: for whom, what changes, how we measure it.
• Traps: tech looking for a problem, a demo that fails on real data, the costly last 10 percent, missing data, no owner, building what a vendor sells, a pilot with no end.
• Test for two weeks on real examples and judge by one number.
• Keep a short list and say no openly. A clear no is cheap.

• 点子来自观察工作：复制、重新输入、查找、等待、重复的问题。
• 每个点子写成一句话：为谁、改变什么、怎么衡量。
• 陷阱：拿着技术找问题、演示在真实数据上失灵、昂贵的最后 10%、缺数据、没有负责人、自己做供应商已有的、没有终点的试点。
• 用真实例子测试两周，以一个数字来判断。
• 保持一张短清单，并公开地说不。一个清楚的“不”很便宜。
