New ideas and traps新点子与陷阱

Where good ideas come from, how to write one in a line, and which traps to spot early · ~7 min · decisions好点子从哪里来、怎样用一句话写下来、哪些陷阱要早点看出来 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定

Step 1 · Start from the work, not the technology第 1 步 · 从工作出发，不从技术出发

Learn先学

Look for pains: where do people copy, re-type, search, wait, or answer the same question every week? Ask a staff member for their most boring task. Then follow the path below, from pain to decision.

去找痛点：哪里有人在复制、重新输入、查找、等待，或每周回答同样的问题？请员工说说他们最无聊的一项工作。然后沿着下图，从痛点走到决定。

Step 2 · Write each idea in one line第 2 步 · 每个点子写成一句话

Learn先学

A pain is not an idea yet. One line, three parts: for whom, what changes, how we will measure it. If you cannot fill all three, it is not ready.

痛点还不是点子。一句话，三部分：为谁、改变什么、怎么衡量。填不全三样，就说明还没准备好。

Step 3 · Know the traps, test fast, say a clean no第 3 步 · 认识陷阱，快速测试，干脆说不

Learn先学

Most failed AI ideas fall into a few traps:

• Tech looking for a problem
• A demo that wows but fails on real data
• The last 10 percent of accuracy (how often it is right): going from 90 right in 100 to 99 can cost more than everything before it
• No data, or no owner
• Building what a vendor (a company that sells such tools) already sells
• A pilot (small trial) that never ends

Give each idea a two-week test on real examples, judged by one number, ending in a go or no-go with a date. A clear no is cheap; a vague “let us see” is the expensive one.

大多数失败的 AI 点子都掉进少数几个陷阱：

• 拿着技术找问题
• 演示惊艳，碰到真实数据就失灵
• 最后 10% 的准确率（答对的比例）：从 100 次对 90 次提到对 99 次，可能比之前所有投入都贵
• 没有数据，或没有负责人
• 自己做供应商（卖这类工具的公司）已经在卖的东西
• 永远不结束的试点（小规模试运行）

给每个点子一次两周的测试：用真实例子、以一个数字判断，最后得出做或不做，并定下日期。一个清楚的“不”很便宜，含糊的“再看看”才贵。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ideas come from watching work, not from technology.
• Write each idea in one line: for whom, what changes, how we measure it.
• Common traps: tech looking for a problem, a demo that fails on real data, the costly last 10 percent, no data or owner, building what a vendor sells, a pilot with no end.
• Test two weeks on real examples, judge by one number, and say a clear no.

• 点子来自观察工作，不是来自技术。
• 每个点子写成一句话：为谁、改变什么、怎么衡量。
• 常见陷阱：拿着技术找问题、演示在真实数据上失灵、昂贵的最后 10%、没数据或没负责人、自己做供应商已有的、没有终点的试点。
• 用真实例子测试两周，以一个数字判断，并干脆地说不。
