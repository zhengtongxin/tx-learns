Step 1 · Four layers第 1 步 · 四层

Learn先学

Think of a hawker centre. Someone owns the building and the gas, someone cooks, someone runs the stall, and you eat the plate. The AI world stacks the same way: the building and gas are layer 1, the cook is layer 2, the stall is layer 3, and the plate is layer 4. Layer 1 is chips and cloud: the machines that train and run models. Layer 2 is models for text, images, speech and code. Layer 3 is tools and platforms that stitch a model to your data. Layer 4 is applications: finished products people use.

想想小贩中心：有人拥有大楼和煤气，有人煮菜，有人经营摊位，而你吃到的是那盘饭。AI 世界也是这样叠起来的：大楼和煤气是第 1 层，厨师是第 2 层，摊位是第 3 层，那盘饭是第 4 层。第 1 层是芯片与云：训练和运行模型的机器。第 2 层是模型：文字、图像、语音、代码。第 3 层是工具与平台，把模型缝到你的数据上。第 4 层是应用：人们直接用的成品。

Now place six things Mdm Rohani heard this month.

现在把 Rohani 女士这个月听到的六样东西放上去。

Step 2 · Six words, in plain words第 2 步 · 六个词，大白话

Learn先学

You will hear these six at every vendor meeting.

每次供应商会议上你都会听到这六个词。

• Large language model (LLM): predicts the next piece of text, like a very well-read autocomplete.
• Multimodal: a model that also reads images or audio, such as a photo of a damaged carton.
• Embeddings: text turned into numbers so that similar things sit close together.
• Retrieval: look up the facts first, then answer. It is the "RAG" (retrieval-augmented generation) in vendor slides.
• Fine-tuning: extra training on your own examples.
• Agent: a model that uses a tool, checks the result, and repeats until the job is done.

• 大语言模型（LLM）：预测下一段文字，像一个读书极多的自动补全。
• 多模态：还能看图或听音频的模型，比如看一张破损纸箱的照片。
• 嵌入（embeddings）：把文字变成数字，让相似的东西靠得近。
• 检索：先查资料再回答。就是供应商幻灯片里的“RAG”（检索增强生成）。
• 微调：用你自己的例子再训练一遍。
• 智能体：调用一个工具、看清结果，然后重复，直到任务完成的模型。

Step 3 · A buying question points to a layer第 3 步 · 采购问题会指向某一层

Learn先学

Before you ask a vendor anything, ask which layer your question lives on. "Which model?" is layer 2. "How do we connect it to our files?" is layer 3. "Can we just buy something?" is layer 4. Layer 1 you usually rent and never see.

问供应商之前，先问自己的问题在哪一层。“用哪个模型？”是第 2 层。“怎么接上我们的文件？”是第 3 层。“能不能直接买个现成的？”是第 4 层。第 1 层通常是租的，你根本看不到。

Step 4 · Names change monthly, layers do not第 4 步 · 名字每月换，层不换

Learn先学

Layers 2 and 3 move fast: a new model or tool arrives almost every month. The four layers themselves hardly move. Like the stalls at a hawker centre, the dishes change but the building stays. So learn the layers, and sort each new name.

第 2、3 层变化很快：几乎每个月都有新模型或新工具。四层本身几乎不动。就像小贩中心，菜式会换，大楼不变。所以先学会这几层，再给每个新名字归位。

Devi asked "which files?" first, and the choice got easier. 3. Auntie Lim believed "open" means free. Running it still costs people and machines.1. Tan 先生把每个新工具都当成新类别，其实它只是同一层里多了一样东西。2. Jason 没问模型要读哪些数据就先选模型。Devi 先问“是哪些文件？”，选择就容易多了。3. Lim 阿姨以为“开放”就是免费。运行它仍然要人和机器。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Four layers: chips and cloud, models, tools and platforms, applications.
• Models are closed (sold by an API) or open (you can run them yourself).
• Retrieval, agents and workflow builders all live at layer 3.
• "Which model?" is layer 2, "connect to our files?" is layer 3, "can we buy one?" is layer 4.
• Open does not mean free: people and machines still cost money.
• Names change monthly. The layers change slowly.
• Place a new name on a layer and it stops being scary.

• 四层：芯片与云、模型、工具与平台、应用。
• 模型分闭源（通过 API 出售）和开放（可以自己运行）。
• 检索、智能体和流程搭建器都在第 3 层。
• “用哪个模型？”是第 2 层，“接上我们的文件？”是第 3 层，“能买现成的吗？”是第 4 层。
• 开放不等于免费：人和机器仍然要花钱。
• 名字每个月换，层变得很慢。
• 把新名字放到某一层，它就不吓人了。
