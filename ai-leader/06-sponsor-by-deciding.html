Sponsoring an AI project by making the key decisions当 AI 项目的发起人：做好关键决定

Five decisions only you can make, and what to leave to the team · ~7 min · decisions只有你能拿的五个决定，以及哪些留给团队 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time you saw the map of AI models and tools. Today an AI project fails when Mdm Rohani says yes to "use AI" and then nobody knows what she wanted. We fix it by making five decisions, and leaving the rest to Devi's team.

上回你看到了 AI 模型和工具的全景图。今天讲 AI 项目为什么会失败：Rohani 夫人说了“好，用 AI”，然后没人知道她到底想要什么。办法是拿下五个决定，其余交给 Devi 的团队。

The story故事

Heng Huat Mart has 30 stores. At the Monday meeting, over kopi o and kaya toast, Mdm Rohani, the managing director, says: "Let's use AI to improve service." Devi, head of IT and data, nods. Mr Tan, the finance head, sits at the end of the table with a calculator. Devi then asks, "Which service, and how will we know it improved?" Mdm Rohani realises she has just signed off on a mood, not a project. Today she learns what a sponsor (the person who backs the project and answers for it) must decide.

恒发超市有 30 家分店。星期一开会，大家喝着咖啡乌、吃着咖椰吐司，总经理 Rohani 夫人说：“我们用 AI 来改善服务吧。”IT 与数据主管 Devi 点点头。财务主管 Tan 先生坐在桌尾，拿着计算器。Devi 然后问：“改善哪项服务？怎么知道改善了？”Rohani 夫人这才发现，自己批准的只是一种心情，不是一个项目。今天她要学的是：发起人（出面支持项目、并对它负责的人）必须拿哪些决定。

Step 1 · What a sponsor owns第 1 步 · 发起人要负责什么

Learn先学

A sponsor owns three things, which become the five decisions in Step 2: why we are doing this, the limits it must stay inside, and the answer to "is it working?". Not the code. Think of building a new store. The owner picks the location, the budget and the opening date. The builder picks the bricks.

发起人负责三件事，它们会变成第 2 步的五个决定：为什么做、必须守住的界限，以及“有没有用？”这个问题的答案。代码不归你管。想想开新分店：老板定地点、预算和开业日，砖头用哪种，由施工队决定。

Step 2 · The five decisions only you can make第 2 步 · 只有你能拿的五个决定

Learn先学

First, a goal in one sentence with a number, such as "cut checkout queue time at Woodlands from 6 minutes to 4 on payday Fridays". Second, a budget and a date to look again. Third, which data may be used: supplier invoices yes, staff records no. Fourth, what the system may never do alone, such as changing a price label or paying a supplier. Fifth, one named person who answers if it goes wrong.

第一，目标要一句话加一个数字，比如“把兀兰分店发薪日星期五的结账排队时间从 6 分钟降到 4 分钟”。第二，预算和再看一次的日期。第三，哪些数据能用：供应商发票可以，员工档案不行。第四，系统绝不能自己做什么，比如改价签或付供应商的钱。第五，出了事由一位点名的人来负责。

Sort what Mdm Rohani hears this week: is it hers to decide, or the team's?

把 Rohani 夫人这周听到的事分一分：是她来决定，还是团队来决定？

Step 3 · Ask for reasoning, not a menu第 3 步 · 要理由，不要菜单

Learn先学

When the team has to choose a model or a tool, do not ask for a list of options. A menu hands the decision back to you, and you cannot judge it. Ask for a recommendation and the reasoning in plain words: "Which one would you pick, why, and what would change your mind?" It is like asking a good waiter what to order, not for the whole kitchen.

团队要选模型或工具时，别要一份选项清单。清单等于把决定又推回给你，而你没法判断。要一个建议，加上用大白话讲的理由：“你会选哪个？为什么？什么情况会让你改主意？”就像问好服务员点什么菜，而不是要整间厨房的菜单。

Step 4 · Say yes with conditions, then show up第 4 步 · 带条件地说“好”，然后露面

Learn先学

A good yes has edges: "Yes for four weeks, on one store, and we review against this number." It is small enough to stop, which is Mr Tan's favourite kind of yes. Then show up. A sponsor who disappears leaves the team guessing. A short weekly look at the real numbers is enough, ten minutes before the Monday meeting.

好的“同意”有边界：“先做四周，只在一家分店，到时对着这个数字复盘。”它小到随时能停，这正是 Tan 先生最喜欢的“同意”。然后要露面。发起人一消失，团队就只能猜。每周花十分钟看一眼真实数字就够了，星期一开会前看最合适。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方I thought a sponsor should either dig into the details or stay out of the way. Both are wrong. Mdm Rohani approved "use AI to improve service", which is too vague to ever fail or finish. Mr Tan then decided a technical detail he did not understand, and Devi's team built it to please him. Worst, nobody wrote down what counts as success, so the project could never end. Decide the five, ask for reasons on the rest, and write the success number down.我以为发起人要么钻进细节，要么干脆别管。两样都错。Rohani 夫人批准了“用 AI 改善服务”，太含糊，既没法失败也没法完成。Tan 先生又替团队定了一个自己并不懂的技术细节，Devi 的团队为了让他满意照做了。最糟的是，没人写下什么算成功，所以项目永远结束不了。五个决定自己拿，其余要理由，成功的数字写下来。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

What changed这一课之后Now a sponsor can say what she wants, set the limits and name who answers, while Devi's team picks the model and the tools. The next problem is how to talk with the tech team so those choices make sense to you.现在发起人能说清想要什么、划好界限、指定负责人，而模型和工具由 Devi 的团队来选。下一个问题是：怎样和技术团队沟通，才能听懂他们的选择。
Back at the mart回到超市Mdm Rohani rewrites her sentence: "For four weeks, at the Woodlands store, cut payday-Friday queue time from 6 minutes to 4. Budget capped by Mr Tan, review on the last Monday, no price changes without sign-off from Jason, the store operations manager, Devi accountable." Devi's team smiles. Now they know what to build.Rohani 夫人把那句话重写了：“四周内，在兀兰分店，把发薪日星期五的排队时间从 6 分钟降到 4 分钟。预算由 Tan 先生封顶，最后一个星期一复盘，没有店铺运营经理 Jason 签字不得改价，由 Devi 负责。”Devi 的团队笑了。现在他们知道要做什么了。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A sponsor owns the why, the limits and the answer to "is it working?". Not the code.
• Five decisions are yours: goal with a number, budget and review date, data, what it may never do alone, who is accountable.
• Leave the model, tools, build and logging to the team.
• Ask for a recommendation and plain reasons, not a menu.
• Say yes with conditions: small, short and measured against a number.
• Show up: a short weekly look at real numbers is enough.
• If any decision is "we will see", that is the gap.

• 发起人负责“为什么”、界限，以及“有没有用？”的答案，不负责代码。
• 五个决定归你：带数字的目标、预算和复盘日期、数据、它绝不能自己做什么、谁来负责。
• 模型、工具、搭建方式和记录，交给团队。
• 要建议和大白话的理由，不要菜单。
• 带条件地说“好”：小、短，并用一个数字衡量。
• 要露面：每周花很短时间看真实数字就够。
• 只要有一个决定是“到时再看”，那就是缺口。
