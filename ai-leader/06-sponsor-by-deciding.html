Sponsoring an AI project发起 AI 项目

Five decisions only you can make, and what to leave to the team · ~7 min · decisions只有你能拿的五个决定，以及哪些留给团队 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定

Step 1 · The five decisions only you can make第 1 步 · 只有你能拿的五个决定

Learn先学

A sponsor backs the project and answers for it. Say "use AI to improve service" and nobody knows what you want. A sponsor owns the why and the limits, not the code. Five decisions are yours:

1. A goal in one sentence with a number, such as "cut payday-Friday checkout queues from 6 minutes to 4".
2. A budget and a date to review.
3. Which data may be used.
4. What the system may never do alone, such as changing a price label.
5. One named person who answers if it goes wrong.

发起人出面支持项目并对它负责。只说“用 AI 改善服务”，没人知道你想要什么。发起人负责“为什么”和界限，不负责代码。五个决定归你：

1. 一句话加一个数字的目标，比如“把发薪日星期五的结账排队时间从 6 分钟降到 4 分钟”。
2. 预算和复盘日期。
3. 哪些数据能用。
4. 系统绝不能自己做什么，比如改价签。
5. 出了事由一位点名的负责人。

Is it the sponsor's call, or the team's?

是发起人来决定，还是团队来决定？

Step 2 · Ask for reasons, say yes with conditions第 2 步 · 要理由，带条件地说“好”

Learn先学

When the team must choose a model or tool, do not ask for a menu: it hands the decision back to you. Ask: "Which would you pick, why, and what would change your mind?" Then give a yes with edges: "Four weeks, one store, reviewed against this number." Show up for ten minutes a week to look at the real numbers.

团队要选模型或工具时，别要一份清单：它会把决定推回给你。要问：“你会选哪个？为什么？什么情况会让你改主意？”然后给一个有边界的“好”：“四周，一家分店，对着这个数字复盘。”每周花十分钟看真实数字。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A sponsor owns the why, the limits and "is it working?", not the code.
• Five decisions are yours: goal with a number, budget and review date, data, what it may never do alone, who is accountable.
• Leave the model, tools and build to the team; ask for a recommendation and plain reasons.
• Say yes with conditions: small, short, measured against a number.

• 发起人负责“为什么”、界限和“有没有用？”，不负责代码。
• 五个决定归你：带数字的目标、预算和复盘日期、数据、它绝不能自己做什么、谁来负责。
• 模型、工具和搭建交给团队；要建议和大白话的理由。
• 带条件地说“好”：小、短，并用一个数字衡量。
