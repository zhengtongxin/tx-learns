Proving AI ROI证明 AI 回报

You will learn to show, in numbers the finance head accepts, whether an AI project paid off · ~8 min · concept你会学会用财务主管认可的数字，证明一个 AI 项目有没有回本 · 约 8 分钟 · 概念课

Step 1 · Write down "before"第 1 步 · 先写下“之前”

Learn先学

A baseline is how the work looks today, before the AI touches it: time per task, cost per task, error rate and volume. Example for invoices: 8 minutes each, 3,000 a month, 3 in every 100 wrong. Without a first weight, “I feel lighter” proves nothing.

基线就是 AI 动手之前，这项工作今天的样子：每件事的时间、每件事的成本、错误率和数量。发票的例子：每张 8 分钟，每月 3,000 张，每 100 张错 3 张。没有第一次的体重，“我觉得轻了”什么也证明不了。

Step 2 · Count every benefit and every cost第 2 步 · 算清每项收益和每项成本

Learn先学

• A hard saving is money you can see: overtime that disappears, a hire you no longer need, fewer paid fixes.
• A soft benefit is real but not yet money, such as freed hours nobody uses. Do not count it as a saving.
• Costs are everything: licences, usage, the build, people's time, checking the AI's work and training.

• 硬节省是看得见的钱：不用再付的加班费、不用再招的人、少付的更正费。
• 软收益是真实但还不是钱的好处，比如没人用的空闲时间。不要把它算成节省。
• 成本是全部：授权费、使用费、搭建、人的时间、检查 AI 的工作和培训。

Sort each finding into a hard saving or a soft benefit.

把每个发现分成硬节省或软收益。

Step 3 · ROI, payback and an honest range第 3 步 · 回报率、回本期和如实的范围

Learn先学

ROI (return on investment) = (benefit − cost) ÷ cost. Example over a year: hard benefit $5,400 a month ($3,000 less overtime, plus $2,400 from 60 fewer disputes at $40 each), so $64,800. Cost $12,000 once plus $1,400 a month ($28,800). ROI = ($64,800 − $28,800) ÷ $28,800 = 125%.

Payback is the months until money back equals money in: $12,000 ÷ ($5,400 − $1,400) = 3 months. Finance likes a date more than a percentage.

Report a range, not one proud number. The cautious case counts only the $3,000 overtime: $1,600 net a month, payback 7.5 months, ROI 25%. Say both, and say which part is hard and which is soft. Then put it on a one-page scorecard on a fixed rhythm, and stop what does not pay.

ROI（投资回报率）= (收益 − 成本) ÷ 成本。一年的例子：硬收益每月 5,400 新元（加班费少 3,000，加上少了 60 起纠纷、每起 40，共 2,400），一年共 64,800。成本一次性 12,000，加每月 1,400（共 28,800）。ROI = (64,800 − 28,800) ÷ 28,800 = 125%。

回本期是收回的钱等于投入的钱所需的月数：12,000 ÷ (5,400 − 1,400) = 3 个月。财务喜欢日期，多过喜欢百分比。

汇报一个范围，不要只报一个骄傲的数字。保守算法只算 3,000 的加班：每月净 1,600，回本期 7.5 个月，ROI 25%。两个都说，并说明哪部分是硬的、哪部分是软的。然后做成一页记分卡，按固定节奏汇报，不回本的就停掉。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方My first draft claimed all 250 saved hours, but only 100 had become money. I also left out checking time and training.我的第一稿把省下的 250 小时全算上，但只有 100 小时变成了钱。我还漏了检查时间和培训成本。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Measure the baseline first: time, cost, error rate and volume.
• Count saved hours only when they became money or new work.
• Count every cost: licences, usage, build, people, checking and training.
• ROI = (benefit − cost) ÷ cost; always give the payback in months.
• Report a range, split hard from soft, and stop what does not pay.

• 先量基线：时间、成本、错误率和数量。
• 省下的时间，变成了钱或新工作才算数。
• 算清每项成本：授权、使用、搭建、人力、检查和培训。
• ROI = (收益 − 成本) ÷ 成本；一定要给出以月计的回本期。
• 汇报一个范围，分清硬软，不回本的就停掉。
