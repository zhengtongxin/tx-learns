Proving the return: measurable ROI from AI证明回报：看得见的 AI 投资回报

You will learn to show, in numbers the finance head accepts, whether an AI project paid off · ~8 min · concept你会学会用财务主管认可的数字，证明一个 AI 项目有没有回本 · 约 8 分钟 · 概念课

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time the stores started using AI in their daily work. Today Mr Tan asks the question every leader gets: "Did it pay?" and nobody has a number. We fix it by measuring before, counting every cost, and reporting an honest range.

上回，各家分店开始在日常工作里用 AI。今天 Tan 先生问出每位领导都会被问的问题：“到底有没有回本？”可是没人拿得出数字。我们的办法是：先量“之前”，算清每一项成本，再如实报告一个范围。

Story故事

Mdm Rohani let Devi's team try an AI helper that reads supplier invoices and matches them to deliveries. The stores like it. But Mr Tan will not sign off the next budget on "it feels faster". Mdm Rohani's goal: walk into the Monday meeting with a one-page answer he can check.

Rohani 女士让 Devi 的团队试用了一个 AI 助手：读供应商发票，再和到货单对上。各店都说好用。但 Tan 先生不会凭一句“感觉快了”就批下一笔预算。Rohani 女士的目标：周一例会上拿出一页他能核对的答案。

Step 1 · Write down "before"第 1 步 · 先写下“之前”

Learn先学

A baseline is how the work looks today, before the AI touches it. Write down four numbers: time per task, cost per task, error rate and volume. Devi counts them for invoices: 8 minutes each, 3,000 invoices a month, 3 in every 100 wrong. Cost per task is what those 8 minutes cost in pay.

It is like weighing yourself before a diet. Without the first weight, "I feel lighter" proves nothing.

基线就是 AI 动手之前，这项工作今天的样子。写下四个数字：每件事要多久、每件事多少钱、错误率和数量。Devi 数出发票的情况：每张 8 分钟，每月 3,000 张，每 100 张错 3 张。“每件事多少钱”就是这 8 分钟花掉的人工钱。

就像减肥前先称体重。没有第一次的体重，“我觉得轻了”什么也证明不了。

Step 2 · Count every benefit and every cost第 2 步 · 算清每项收益和每项成本

Learn先学

Benefits are anything that turns into money or a measurable value: hours saved, faster cycles, fewer errors, extra sales. Money you can see is a hard saving; a real gain that is not yet money is a soft benefit. Costs are everything you paid: licences, usage, the build, people's time, checking the AI's work, and training.

Here a saved hour only counts when it becomes something else: overtime that disappears, a hire you no longer need, or new work that gets done. Free time nobody uses is a soft benefit. It is like a shorter queue that never turns into more sales: nice, but the till does not ring.

收益是一切能变成钱、或能被量出来的价值：省下的时间、更快的周期、更少的错误、多出来的销售。看得见的钱叫硬节省；真实存在但还不是钱的好处叫软收益。成本是你付出的全部：授权费、使用费、搭建、人的时间、检查 AI 的工作，还有培训。

省下的时间，只有变成别的东西才算数：不用再付的加班费、不用再招的人，或是多做成的事。没人用的空闲时间只是软收益。就像队伍变短了，却没多卖出东西：好看，但收银机没响。

Sort what Devi found into a hard saving (money you can see) or a soft benefit.

把 Devi 找到的项目分成硬节省（看得见的钱）或软收益。

Step 3 · One formula and a payback date第 3 步 · 一个公式和一个回本日期

Learn先学

The simple formula is return on investment (ROI) = (benefit − cost) ÷ cost. Over the first year, Devi counts hard benefits of $5,400 a month: $3,000 less overtime (100 hours at $30 an hour) and $2,400 fewer invoice fixes (60 fewer of the 90 wrong invoices each month, at $40 a fix). That is $64,800. Costs are $12,000 to build and train, plus $1,400 a month for usage and checking. That is $28,800. So ROI is ($64,800 − $28,800) ÷ $28,800 = 125%.

Also say the payback: how many months until the money back equals the money in. Mr Tan likes a date more than a percentage. Here the one-off $12,000 is covered by $4,000 net a month, so payback is 3 months.

简单公式：投资回报率 ROI = (收益 − 成本) ÷ 成本。第一年里，Devi 算出每月硬收益 5,400 新元：加班少了 3,000（100 小时，每小时 30 新元），发票更正少了 2,400（每月 90 张出错的发票里少 60 张，每张更正 40 新元），合计 64,800。成本是搭建和培训的一次性 12,000，加上每月使用和检查的 1,400，合计 28,800。所以 ROI 是 (64,800 − 28,800) ÷ 28,800 = 125%。

同时要说回本期：多少个月后，收回的钱等于投进去的钱。Tan 先生喜欢日期，多过喜欢百分比。这里一次性的 12,000 靠每月净赚 4,000 来收回，所以回本期是 3 个月。

Step 4 · Be honest, then report and decide第 4 步 · 老实说，再汇报、再决定

Learn先学

Report a range, not one proud number. Devi's cautious case counts only the overtime: $3,000 − $1,400 = $1,600 a month, so payback is 7.5 months and the first-year ROI is 25%. The full case says 125% and 3 months. Say both, and say which is hard and which is soft.

Where you can, compare with a group that did not get the helper, or with the same months last year. Then put it on a one-page scorecard on a fixed rhythm, say what you will do next, and stop what does not pay.

汇报一个范围，不要只报一个骄傲的数字。Devi 的保守算法只算加班：3,000 − 1,400 = 每月 1,600，回本期 7.5 个月，第一年 ROI 是 25%。完整算法是 125% 和 3 个月。两个都说，并说明哪些是硬的、哪些是软的。

可能的话，拿没用助手的一组来对比，或对比去年同期。然后做成一页记分卡，按固定节奏汇报，说明下一步要做什么，不回本的就停掉。

Invoice helper scorecard
• Before: 8 min per invoice, 3,000 a month, 3% wrong.
• Hard benefit: $3,000 to $5,400 a month. Soft: 150 freed hours, not counted.
• Cost: $12,000 once, $1,400 a month.
• Result: payback 3 to 7.5 months, first-year ROI 25% to 125%.
• Next: keep it, and ask Jason, the store operations manager, to use the freed hours in the stores.

发票助手记分卡
• 之前：每张 8 分钟，每月 3,000 张，错 3%。
• 硬收益：每月 3,000 到 5,400 新元。软收益：省下 150 小时，不计入。
• 成本：一次性 12,000，每月 1,400。
• 结果：回本期 3 到 7.5 个月，第一年 ROI 25% 到 125%。
• 下一步：继续用，并请店务经理 Jason 把省下的时间用在店里。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方Mdm Rohani's first draft claimed all 250 saved hours, but Mr Tan asked where those hours went, and nobody could say. She also left out the checking time and the training, and she had no baseline for the error rate, so that number was a guess. Now she counts only hours that became money, puts the checking and training in the cost, and writes the baseline first.Rohani 女士的第一稿把省下的 250 小时全算上，但 Tan 先生问这些时间去了哪里，没人答得出。她还漏了检查时间和培训成本，错误率也没有基线，所以那个数字只是猜的。现在她只算变成了钱的时间，把检查和培训放进成本，并且先写好基线。
What changed这一课之后Now Mdm Rohani can show Mr Tan a one-page scorecard with a baseline, an honest range and a payback date. The next problem is what to do with the answer: keep growing what pays, and stop what does not.现在 Rohani 女士能给 Tan 先生一页记分卡：有基线、有如实的范围、有回本日期。下一个问题是怎么处理这个答案：把回本的做大，不回本的停掉。
Back at the mart回到超市Mr Tan read the scorecard twice, then asked for the same page from every store project, once a month. Heng Huat Mart now measures before it spends, and stops what does not pay.Tan 先生把记分卡看了两遍，然后要求每个分店项目每月都交同样的一页。Heng Huat Mart 现在是先量再花钱，不回本的就停。

• Measure the baseline first: time, cost, error rate and volume.
• Count hours saved only when they became money or new work.
• Count every cost: licences, usage, build, people, checking and training.
• ROI = (benefit − cost) ÷ cost, and always say the payback in months.
• Separate hard from soft, and report a range.
• Report on a fixed rhythm, say what is next, and stop what does not pay.

• 先量基线：时间、成本、错误率和数量。
• 省下的时间，变成了钱或新工作才算数。
• 算清每项成本：授权、使用、搭建、人力、检查和培训。
• ROI = (收益 − 成本) ÷ 成本，并且一定要说回本要几个月。
• 分清硬收益和软收益，汇报一个范围。
• 按固定节奏汇报，说明下一步，不回本的就停掉。
