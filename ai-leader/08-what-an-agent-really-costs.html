What an agent really costs, and what can go wrong一个智能体真正的成本，以及会出什么错

Cost an agent per task and name its risks before you say yes · ~7 min · decisions, no code在点头之前，按每个任务算出智能体的成本，并说清它的风险 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定，不写代码

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani learned to work with Devi and the tech team on AI choices. Today she is asked to approve an agent, and the only number on the page is a small monthly model fee. We fix it by costing the agent per task and naming what can go wrong.

上回，Rohani 女士学会了和 Devi 与技术团队一起做 AI 选择。今天她要批准一个智能体，而纸上唯一的数字只是一笔不大的每月模型费用。我们的办法是按每个任务来算成本，并说清会出什么错。

The story故事

Devi proposes an agent: an AI that takes many steps by itself to finish a job. This one would read supplier invoices, match them to deliveries, and email the supplier when something is off. Her slide says “model fee: S$180 a month”. Mr Tan says that is easy to approve. Mdm Rohani pauses. Her question is the one a good leader asks: “What does one task cost, and what is the worst thing it could do?”

Devi 提议做一个智能体：一个能自己走很多步去完成一件事的 AI。这个会读供应商发票，对照收货记录，发现不对就给供应商发邮件。她的幻灯片写着“模型费用：每月 S$180”。Tan 先生说这很容易批。Rohani 女士停了一下。她问了一个好领导会问的问题：“一个任务要花多少钱？它做得最糟的一件事是什么？”

Step 1 · The five parts of the cost第 1 步 · 成本的五个部分

Learn先学

The model fee is only one slice. An agent has five costs. Model usage: the model is the AI you pay to use, and every step is one paid call to it. Agents take many steps. Tools and data access: the systems it reads and the services it calls. Build time: Devi’s team writing and testing it. Checking time: people who review its work. Running and monitoring: someone keeps it alive and watches it. Like the mart’s rent: the shelf price is not the whole cost of selling a tin of sardines.

模型费用只是其中一块。智能体有五种成本。模型使用：模型就是你付费调用的 AI，每一步都是一次付费调用，智能体会走很多步。工具和数据接入：它读取的系统和调用的服务。搭建时间：Devi 的团队编写和测试。检查时间：审阅它工作的人。运行和监控：有人让它一直在跑，并盯着它。就像超市的租金：货架标价并不是卖一罐沙丁鱼的全部成本。

Step 2 · Ask for cost per task第 2 步 · 要一个“每个任务”的数字

Learn先学

A monthly total hides what matters. Ask for the cost per task, as an average and as a worst case. A task here is one invoice handled from start to finish. On a good day it is 8 steps and about S$0.30. But an agent that retries a failing step can keep paying for each try. A few cents can turn into several dollars on one stuck invoice, like a taxi meter running while the driver is lost.

月度总数会藏住关键。要问每个任务的成本，既要平均值，也要最坏情况。这里的一个任务，就是一张发票从头处理到尾。顺利的时候是 8 步、约 S$0.30。但智能体对失败的步骤反复重试，每试一次都在付钱。一张卡住的发票，几分钱就可能变成几新元，就像司机迷路时计价器还在跳。

Step 3 · What can go wrong, and what stops it第 3 步 · 会出什么错，什么能拦住

Learn先学

Five risks to name. It loops or wanders. It takes a wrong action, like emailing the wrong supplier or changing the wrong record. It is tricked by hostile text hidden in a document it reads. It leaks data. Or it quietly gets worse and nobody notices. Five controls answer them: a step limit and a spending limit per task, read-only access first, and only the records the job needs, a person’s approval before anything that cannot be undone, logs you can read, and a regular test set of questions with known answers.

要点名五种风险。它会循环或乱走；会做错动作，比如给错供应商发邮件、改错记录；会被文件里藏的恶意文字骗到；会泄露数据；或者悄悄变差而没人发现。对应五种控制：每个任务的步数上限和花费上限、先只给只读权限，并且只开放这份工作需要的记录、任何无法撤销的事先由人批准、看得懂的日志，以及一套有标准答案的定期测试集。

Step 4 · Compare against the person第 4 步 · 和人工做对比

Learn先学

An agent is only “expensive” or “cheap” next to something. So cost the same task done by hand, mistakes included. Jason’s team takes about 6 minutes per invoice, a few get mistyped, and each one costs time to fix. Put both side by side: cost per task, with the cost of mistakes on both sides. That is a number Mr Tan can work with.

智能体只有放在对比里才谈得上“贵”或“便宜”。所以要把同一个任务用人工做的成本也算出来，包括错误。Jason 的团队每张发票大约花 6 分钟，总有几张录错，每个错误还要花时间改。把两边并排列在一起：每个任务的成本，两边都把错误的代价算进去。这才是 Tan 先生用得上的数字。

What I got wrong我当时想错的

Three slips happen all the time. Costing only the model price. Mr Tan nearly approved on S$180 and forgot the people who check the work. Giving the agent write access on day one. Mdm Rohani first said, “let it fix the records itself”; start with read-only. No cap per task. Without a limit, one stuck invoice can bring a surprise bill.

有三个错误总在发生。只算模型价格。Tan 先生差点就凭 S$180 批准，忘了检查工作的人力。第一天就给智能体写入权限。Rohani 女士一开始说“让它自己改记录吧”；应该先只读。没有每个任务的上限。没有上限，一张卡住的发票就可能带来一张意外的账单。

Now make it yours现在轮到你

Pick one AI idea at your own work and write three lines:

选一个你自己工作里的 AI 点子，写三行：

  • Cost per task: best, typical and worst case, including checking time.每个任务的成本：最好、一般、最坏，包括检查时间。
  • The single worst action it could take.它可能做出的最糟的一个动作。
  • The control that stops that action.能拦住那个动作的控制。
What changed现在不同了

Now you can ask for a cost per task and a list of risks with their controls, instead of a monthly fee. The next problem is deciding how the agent should know about the mart’s own documents.

现在你可以要求拿到每个任务的成本，以及风险和对应控制的清单，而不是一个月费。下一个问题是：智能体该怎样了解超市自己的文件。

Back at the mart回到超市

Mdm Rohani told Devi she would approve the invoice agent for a trial: read-only, a cap per invoice, and Jason’s team approving every supplier email. Mr Tan got a cost per invoice next to the hand cost. He said yes, with a review in a month.

Rohani 女士告诉 Devi，她同意让发票智能体试用：只读、每张发票设上限，每封给供应商的邮件都由 Jason 的团队批准。Tan 先生拿到了每张发票的成本，旁边是人工成本。他点头了，一个月后再复查。

Takeaways要点回顾

• An agent’s cost has five parts: model usage, tools and data, build time, checking time and running.
• Every step is a paid call, and agents take many steps.
• Ask for cost per task, average and worst case, not only a monthly fee.
• A retry loop can turn a few cents into several dollars.
• Name the risks: looping, a wrong action, hostile text, a data leak, quiet decline.
• Cap the steps and spend, start read-only, and keep a person on anything that cannot be undone.
• Compare with the same task by hand, mistakes included.

• 智能体的成本有五部分：模型使用、工具和数据、搭建时间、检查时间和运行。
• 每一步都是一次付费调用，而智能体会走很多步。
• 要每个任务的成本，要平均和最坏情况，不能只看月费。
• 重试循环能把几分钱变成几新元。
• 点名风险：循环、做错动作、恶意文字、数据泄露、悄悄变差。
• 限制步数和花费，先只读，无法撤销的事一律由人把关。
• 和同一个任务的人工做法对比，错误也算进去。
