Who does what: organisation, rules and hiring for AI谁来做什么：组织、规则与 AI 招聘

Set up the people, the rules and the hiring plan to run AI without chaos · ~7 min · decisions把人、规则和招聘计划理好，让 AI 不乱套 · 约 7 分钟 · 做决定

Where we left off上回讲到

Last time Mdm Rohani had a list of AI ideas and a list of traps. Today it fails when three stores each start their own AI trial and nobody knows who is in charge. We fix it by choosing an organisation shape, writing one page of rules and planning who to grow and who to hire.

上回，Rohani 女士有了一张 AI 点子清单，也有了一张陷阱清单。今天的问题是：三家分店各自试起了 AI，没人知道谁说了算。办法是选定组织形式、写一页规则，再规划谁靠培养、谁靠招聘。

Devi says she has heard of neither. Mdm Rohani puts down her cup: "Who is responsible for AI in this company?" Nobody answers. That silence is today's problem.

Step 1 · Pick an organisation shape第 1 步 · 选一种组织形式

Learn先学

There are three common shapes. Central: one AI team serves everyone. Embedded: each business unit has its own AI people. Hub and spoke: a small central group (the hub) sets the standards, and the units (the spokes) do the work. Think of a hawker centre: one landlord sets the hygiene rules, and each stall cooks its own food. A small firm should start central, with one named lead.

常见有三种形式。集中式：一个 AI 团队服务所有人。嵌入式：每个业务部门有自己的 AI 人员。中心加分支：一个小的中心小组（中心）定标准，各部门（分支）去做事。就像小贩中心：房东定卫生规则，每个摊位自己煮。小公司应该先用集中式，并指定一位负责人。

Step 2 · One page of rules第 2 步 · 一页规则

Learn先学

Governance just means: who decides, and what the rules are. Keep it to one page with six parts: an owner for each AI system (any tool or process that uses AI), an approved tool list, a data rule for what may be pasted in, a human check for risky decisions, a way to report problems, and a yearly review. Short, written and enforced. Like the fire exits in a store: few rules, but everyone knows them.

治理说白了就是：谁拍板，规矩是什么。把它压在一页纸上，共六部分：每个 AI 系统（任何使用 AI 的工具或流程）的负责人、批准的工具清单、哪些数据可以贴进去的数据规则、高风险决定的人工把关、报告问题的渠道，以及每年一次的回顾。要短、要成文、要执行。就像店里的消防出口：规则不多，但人人都知道。

Step 3 · Cover five roles第 3 步 · 补齐五个角色

Learn先学

Five jobs must have a name beside them. A sponsor who backs the work and finds the money. A product owner who knows the real work. Someone technical who builds or buys. Someone who guards data and risk. And a champion in each team, a friendly colleague others ask first. One person may wear two hats in a small firm, but none of the five may be empty.

五项工作旁边都得写上名字。发起人：撑腰并找预算。产品负责人：真正懂业务。技术负责人：负责自建或采购。数据与风险守门人。还有每个团队的推广员，也就是大家第一个会去问的友好同事。小公司里一个人可以兼两个角色，但五个都不能空着。

At the mart在超市里Mdm Rohani is the sponsor. Auntie Lim, who knows suppliers, is the product owner for buying. Devi builds and buys. Mr Tan guards data and risk. Jason picks one champion per store.Rohani 女士是发起人。懂供应商的 Lim 阿姨是采购这一块的产品负责人。Devi 负责自建和采购。Tan 先生守住数据和风险。Jason 在每家店选一位推广员。

Step 4 · Grow first, hire later, train everyone第 4 步 · 先培养，后招聘，人人培训

Learn先学

First grow the people you already have. Hire for judgement and curiosity, because tools change every few months. Rent expertise (a consultant or partner) for the first project. Hire a full-time specialist only when there is steady work for them. Last, train everybody in three things: what AI is, what to never paste in, and how to check an answer.

先培养现有的人。招人看判断力和好奇心，因为工具几个月就变一轮。第一个项目靠租用专家（顾问或合作伙伴）。只有当长期有稳定的工作量，才聘全职专家。最后，人人都要培训三件事：AI 是什么、哪些东西绝不能贴进去、怎样核对答案。

What I got wrong我当初搞错的• A grand policy nobody reads. Mdm Rohani's first draft ran to twenty pages; the stores never opened it. One page works.
• Hiring an expensive expert before there is a project. Mr Tan asked what the expert would do on day one, and nobody could say.
• No named owner, so every problem goes to nobody.• 写了一份没人读的宏大政策。Rohani 女士的第一稿有二十页，门店根本没打开过。一页才管用。
• 还没有项目就先请贵的专家。Tan 先生问这位专家第一天做什么，没人答得上来。
• 没有指定负责人，结果每个问题都落到没人手里。

Now make it yours现在轮到你

• Name one owner for a live or planned AI use.
• Write five rules for staff.

• 为一个已在用或计划中的 AI 用途指定一位负责人。
• 为员工写下五条规则。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Three shapes: central, embedded, hub and spoke. A small firm starts central with a named lead.
• Governance is one page: owner, tool list, data rule, human check, reporting, yearly review.
• Cover five roles: sponsor, product owner, technical, data and risk, champions.
• Grow your people first; rent expertise for project one; hire a specialist for steady work.
• Train everybody: what AI is, what never to paste in, how to check an answer.
• One named owner per system, and a short written rulebook.

• 三种形式：集中式、嵌入式、中心加分支。小公司先用集中式，并指定负责人。
• 治理就一页：负责人、工具清单、数据规则、人工把关、报告渠道、年度回顾。
• 补齐五个角色：发起人、产品负责人、技术、数据与风险、推广员。
• 先培养自己人；第一个项目租用专家；有稳定工作量再聘专家。
• 人人培训：AI 是什么、什么绝不能贴、怎样核对答案。
• 每个系统一位指定负责人，外加一本简短的成文规则。
