What AI agents can do智能体能做什么

Know what an agent is, what jobs it suits and what it cannot do · ~7 min · decisions搞清智能体是什么、适合做什么、不能做什么 · 约 7 分钟 · 做判断

Step 1 · An agent is a model plus a loop第 1 步 · 智能体 = 模型 + 循环

Learn先学

A model reads and writes text. An agent wraps it in a loop: you give it a goal, it picks a tool (search, spreadsheet, email), looks at the result, and decides the next step until done or stuck.

模型读写文字。智能体在它外面套一个循环：你给目标，它挑一个工具（搜索、表格、邮件），看结果，再决定下一步，直到做完或卡住。

Step 2 · Three levels to tell apart第 2 步 · 要分清的三个级别

Learn先学

A chatbot answers once and stops. A workflow follows steps you fixed in advance. An agent chooses its own steps. More freedom means more value and more risk.

聊天机器人答一次就结束。工作流照你事先定好的步骤走。智能体自己选步骤。自由度越大，价值越高，风险也越大。

Step 3 · Which jobs fit第 3 步 · 哪些工作适合

Learn先学

Ask four questions of any idea: what is the input, what is the output, who checks it, and what does a mistake cost?

• Good fit: many small steps, a clear end, text or data in, a cheap check.
• Poor fit: costly mistakes with no checker, no written rules, or a need for empathy.

对任何点子问四件事：输入是什么、输出是什么、谁检查、错了代价多大？

• 适合：步骤多而小、终点清楚、输入是文字或数据、检查便宜。
• 不适合：错了代价高又没人检查、没有成文规则、需要同理心。

Sort each job.

给每项工作分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• An agent is a model plus a loop: pick a tool, look at the result, decide the next step.
• A chatbot answers, a workflow follows fixed steps, an agent chooses its own.
• Good fits: many small steps, a clear end, a cheap check.
• Ask: input, output, who checks, what a mistake costs.
• Start with one boring step, not a whole job.

• 智能体是模型加循环：选工具、看结果、定下一步。
• 聊天机器人回答，工作流照固定步骤走，智能体自己选步骤。
• 适合：步骤多而小、终点清楚、检查便宜。
• 要问：输入、输出、谁检查、错了代价多大。
• 从一个枯燥的步骤开始，别一上来就接整份工作。
