What AI agents can do for a businessAI 智能体能为企业做什么

Know what an agent is, what jobs it suits and what it cannot do · ~7 min · decisions搞清智能体是什么、适合做什么、不能做什么 · 约 7 分钟 · 做判断

The story故事

Mdm Rohani runs Heng Huat Mart, thirty neighbourhood supermarkets in public-housing (HDB) estates. Around her are Devi (head of IT), Mr Tan (finance), Jason (store operations) and Auntie Lim (senior buyer). Everyone says "agents" at the Monday meeting, over kopi o and kaya toast. She does not want to write code. She wants to know one thing: what can an agent do for the mart, and where should it stay away? By the end she can sort any idea into good fit or poor fit with four plain questions.

Rohani 女士经营恒发超市，在各个组屋区有三十家邻里店。身边的人有：Devi（IT 主管）、Tan 先生（财务主管）、Jason（店面运营经理）和 Auntie Lim（资深采购）。周一例会上，大家喝着咖啡乌、吃着咖椰吐司，嘴里全是“智能体”。她不想写代码，只想弄懂一件事：智能体能为超市做什么，又该离什么远一点？学完后，她能用四个简单问题，把任何点子分成“适合”或“不适合”。

Where we left off上回讲到

This is the first lesson of the track. Mdm Rohani has heard the word "AI" for a year, but the mart has no plan. Today she cannot tell a real use from a buzzword. We fix it by learning what an agent is and how to test an idea.

这是本路线的第一课。Rohani 女士听了一年“AI”，超市却没有计划。今天她分不清真正的用法和流行词。我们要先搞懂智能体是什么，再学会检验一个点子。

Step 1 · An agent is a model plus a loop第 1 步 · 智能体 = 模型 + 循环

Learn先学

A model is the AI that reads and writes text. An agent wraps it in a loop. You give it a goal. It picks a tool (a search, a spreadsheet, an email, a database), looks at what came back, and decides the next step. It repeats until the job is done or it is stuck. Think of a new clerk who checks the stockroom whiteboard, then the supplier invoice pile, and asks you only when stuck.

模型是读写文字的 AI。智能体是在它外面套了一个循环。你给它一个目标，它挑一个工具（搜索、表格、邮件、数据库），看结果，再决定下一步，一直重复到做完或卡住。就像新来的店员：先看库房白板，再翻供应商发票堆，实在不行才来问你。

Step 2 · Three levels to tell apart第 2 步 · 要分清的三个级别

Learn先学

A chatbot answers a question and stops. A workflow follows steps that you fixed in advance, like a recipe. An agent chooses its own steps. More freedom means more value, and also more risk. So do not call a chatbot an agent in a board paper: Mr Tan will ask what it actually does.

聊天机器人回答一个问题就结束。工作流照你事先定好的步骤走，像一份食谱。智能体自己选步骤。自由度越大，价值越高，风险也越大。所以别在董事会文件里把聊天机器人叫智能体：Tan 先生一定会问它到底做了什么。

Step 3 · Which jobs fit第 3 步 · 哪些工作适合

Learn先学

Good fits have many small steps, a clear end, text or data as input, and a cheap way to check the result. Poor fits are jobs where one wrong move is costly and nobody checks, jobs with no written rules, and jobs that need real-world judgement or empathy. Jason can see this on the shop floor: a task a new part-timer can do from a checklist is usually a good fit.

适合的工作：步骤多而小、终点清楚、输入是文字或数据，而且有便宜的办法检查结果。不适合的：错一步代价很高又没人检查的、没有成文规则的、需要真实世界判断或同理心的。Jason 在店里一眼就能看出来：兼职新人照着清单就能做的活，通常就适合。

Sort each job.

给每项工作分类。

Step 4 · Four questions for any idea第 4 步 · 评估任何点子的四个问题

Learn先学

Ask four things of every idea. What is the input? What is the output? Who checks it? And what does a mistake cost? If the check is cheap and a mistake costs little, try it. If nobody can check and a mistake is expensive, wait.

对每个点子问四件事：输入是什么？输出是什么？谁来检查？错了代价多大？检查便宜、错了代价小，就试试；没人能检查、错了代价大，就先等等。

What I got wrong我搞错的地方Mdm Rohani first thought "agent" meant a robot that replaces a person. Devi corrected her: most useful agents take over one boring step of a job. Mr Tan then wanted to start with the flashiest job, but Auntie Lim pointed at the one with an easy check. And the board paper that called a plain chatbot an agent? That was Rohani's own draft.Rohani 女士起初以为“智能体”就是取代人的机器人。Devi 纠正她：大多数有用的智能体只接手工作中一个枯燥的步骤。Tan 先生又想从最炫的工作开始，Auntie Lim 却指向那个容易检查的。至于把普通聊天机器人写成智能体的董事会文件？那是 Rohani 自己的草稿。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Back at the mart回到超市Mdm Rohani lists five jobs at Heng Huat Mart. Drafting supplier replies scores best: clear input, a draft out, Auntie Lim checks it, a mistake costs a short email. That is where the mart starts. Now she can tell a real use from a buzzword. The next problem is knowing which tools and models are out there to build it with.Rohani 女士列出恒发超市的五项工作。起草供应商回信得分最高：输入清楚、输出是草稿、由 Auntie Lim 检查、错了顶多是一封短邮件。超市就从这里开始。现在她能分辨真正的用法和流行词了。下一个问题是：有哪些工具和模型可以用来搭建它。

Takeaways要点回顾

• An agent is a model plus a loop: pick a tool, look at the result, decide the next step.
• A chatbot answers, a workflow follows fixed steps, an agent chooses its own steps.
• More freedom means more value and more risk.
• Good fits: many small steps, a clear end, text or data in, a cheap check.
• Poor fits: costly mistakes with no checker, no written rules, or a need for empathy.
• Ask: what is the input, the output, who checks it, what does a mistake cost?
• Start with one boring step, not a whole job.

• 智能体是模型加循环：选工具、看结果、定下一步。
• 聊天机器人回答，工作流照固定步骤走，智能体自己选步骤。
• 自由度越大，价值越高，风险也越大。
• 适合：步骤多而小、终点清楚、输入是文字或数据、检查便宜。
• 不适合：错了代价高又没人检查、没有成文规则、需要同理心。
• 要问：输入是什么、输出是什么、谁检查、错了代价多大？
• 从一个枯燥的步骤开始，别一上来就接整份工作。
