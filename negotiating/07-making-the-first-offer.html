Before you negotiate · Lesson 4谈判之前 · 第 4 课

Making the first offer报第一个价码

Set your first offer from your target, open a little above it with a reason and keep your limit private · ~9 min · reading and practice根据目标定第一个报价，开价略高于目标并给出理由，同时对底线保密 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Know your numbers first第 1 步 · 先弄清你的数字

Learn先学

Before you name a first offer, settle three numbers: what you hope to get (your target), what you can accept at the least (your limit) and your best alternative if there is no deal. The first offer is not a guess. It is set from these numbers, and your limit stays private.

在报出第一个价码之前，先定三个数字：你希望得到什么（目标）、最低能接受什么（底线），以及谈不成时你最好的替代方案。第一个报价不是乱猜，而是根据这些数字定出来的，你的底线则保密。

Step 2 · Open a little above target第 2 步 · 开价略高于目标

Learn先学

Most deals end somewhere between the two first offers, so the first offer shapes where you land. Say your target is 100. If you open at 100 and they open at 90, the middle is (100 + 90) ÷ 2 = 95. If you open at 110, which is 10% above target, the middle is (110 + 90) ÷ 2 = 100. Open a little high, but keep the number believable, and be ready to explain it.

多数交易最后落在两个首次报价之间，所以第一个报价会影响最终落点。假设你的目标是 100。若你开价 100、对方开价 90，中间点是 (100 + 90) ÷ 2 = 95。若你开价 110，也就是比目标高 10%，中间点是 (110 + 90) ÷ 2 = 100。开价可以略高，但数字要可信，并准备好说明理由。

Step 3 · Give a reason第 3 步 · 给出理由

Learn先学

Back your offer with one reason the other side can check, such as a comparison, a cost or a need. An offer with a reason sounds fair and is harder to dismiss. If the other side speaks first, do not rush to answer: listen, ask how they got their number, then reply from your own target, not from theirs.

用一个对方能核实的理由来支撑你的报价，比如对比、成本或需求。有理由的报价听起来更公平，也更难被一口回绝。如果对方先开口，不要急着回应：先听，问问他们的数字是怎么来的，再从你自己的目标出发回应，而不是以他们的数字为起点。

To finish Step 3, sort each habit as good or weak: tap a habit, then tap its group.

完成第 3 步：把每个做法分为好或弱。先点一个做法，再点它所属的组。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Settle your target, limit and best alternative before naming a number.
• Keep your limit private.
• Deals often land between the two first offers: with a target of 100, opening at 110 against 90 gives a middle of 100.
• Open a little high, but stay believable.
• Give one reason the other side can check.

• 报价之前先定目标、底线和最好的替代方案。
• 底线保密。
• 交易常落在两个首次报价之间：目标 100，你开 110、对方开 90，中间点是 100。
• 开价略高，但要可信。
• 给出一个对方能核实的理由。
