Know what you want想清楚要什么

Decide your goals and rank them before you talk, with a best case, a good enough case and a point where you walk away · ~10 min · reading and practice开口之前先定好目标并排出轻重，想好理想结果、能接受的结果，以及该走人的底线 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Goals, ranked第 1 步 · 目标要排序

Learn先学

Most negotiations involve several things at once: price, timing, who does what. If you walk in with a vague wish to “get a good deal”, you will give away the wrong things. Write down every goal, then rank them from most to least important.

大多数谈判同时涉及好几件事：价格、时间、谁来做什么。如果只抱着“谈个好条件”的模糊愿望进去，你会让出不该让的东西。把所有目标都写下来，再按重要程度排序。

Step 2 · Three numbers第 2 步 · 三个标准

Learn先学

For the goal that matters most, set three levels: the best you could hope for, a result that is good enough, and a limit beyond which you would rather not agree (a ceiling when you pay, such as rent; a floor when you are paid, such as salary). Know the limit before you start, because it is hard to work out under pressure.

对最重要的那个目标，定三个层次：最理想的结果、足够好的结果，以及一个底线，越过它你宁可不答成协议（付钱时是上限，如租金；收钱时是下限，如薪水）。开始前就想好底线，因为在压力下很难现想。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Write it on one page第 3 步 · 写在一页纸上

Learn先学

Put the goals, the three levels and your top priority on a single page and keep it in front of you. It stops you from being talked into a bad trade, and it lets you notice when you are getting more than you planned.

把目标、三个层次和最优先事项写在一页纸上，放在面前。它能防止你被说动去接受坏交易，也能让你注意到自己拿到的比计划的更多。

Goal 1 (most important): monthly rent. Best: 1,000. Good enough: 1,100. Limit: 1,200. Goal 2: lease length. Best: 12 months. Goal 3: move-in date. 1,200 - 1,000 = 200 between best and limit.

Takeaways要点回顾

• List and rank your goals.
• Set best, good enough and limit.
