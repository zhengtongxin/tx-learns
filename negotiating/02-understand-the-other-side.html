Before you negotiate · Lesson 2谈判之前 · 第 2 课

Understand the other side看懂对方

Look past what the other side says they want to why they want it, so you can find deals that work for both · ~10 min · reading and practice不只看对方说要什么，更看他们为什么要，才能找到双方都能接受的方案 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Position and interest第 1 步 · 立场与需求

Learn先学

A position is what someone asks for: “I want the project done by Friday”. An interest is the reason behind it: “my manager reviews it on Monday”. Two positions can clash while the interests fit together, so look for the reason, not just the demand.

立场是对方提出的要求：“我要周五前完成”。需求是背后的原因：“经理周一要审”。两个立场可能冲突，而背后的需求却能兼容，所以要找原因，而不只是看要求。

Step 2 · Make a guess before you meet第 2 步 · 见面前先猜一猜

Learn先学

Ask yourself what the other side cares about, what pressure they are under, and what they would be happy to get easily. These are guesses, not facts, so mark them as guesses. They tell you what to ask and where there may be room.

问问自己：对方在乎什么，承受着什么压力，什么东西他们很乐意轻松得到。这些是猜测而不是事实，要标明是猜测。它们告诉你该问什么、哪里可能有空间。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Turn guesses into questions第 3 步 · 把猜测变成问题

Learn先学

For each guess, write a question that checks it without sounding like an interrogation, such as “What would make Friday important for you?”. The answers often reveal an interest you can meet cheaply, such as a draft on Thursday and the final on Monday.

为每个猜测写一个能验证它、又不像审问的问题，比如“周五对你们为什么重要？”。答案常常会露出一个你能低成本满足的需求，比如周四给初稿、周一给终稿。

Your manager asks: finish by Friday. Guess (interest): the manager reviews on Monday. Question: What would make Friday important? Possible deal: draft on Thursday, final on Monday.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Separate position from interest.
• Guess, then check.
• Ask why it matters.

• 分清立场和需求。
• 先猜，再验证。
• 问它为什么重要。
