At the table · Lesson 2谈判桌上 · 第 2 课

Trade, don’t give in交换而不是让步

Give something only in return for something, and look for items that cost you little but matter a lot to the other side · ~10 min · reading and practice要让就拿东西来换，并找出对你代价小、对对方却很重要的东西 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · If this, then that第 1 步 · 如果这样，那就那样

Learn先学

A concession given for free teaches the other side to ask again. Tie every move to a return: “If I can move the date to May, can you agree to the price?”. The “if… then…” shape makes clear that you are trading, not giving way.

白白让出的东西，会让对方学会再来要。把每次让步和一个回报绑在一起：“如果我把日期改到五月，你们能接受这个价格吗？”“如果……那么……”的句式说明你是在交换，而不是退让。

Step 2 · Cheap for you, valuable to them第 2 步 · 你付出少，他们很看重

Learn先学

Different things are worth different amounts to each side. A faster payment may cost you almost nothing and matter a lot to them, while a longer deadline may cost them little and help you a great deal. Your notes on their interests show where these trades hide.

同一样东西对双方的价值不同。更快付款对你可能几乎没成本，对他们却很重要；而更宽松的期限对他们代价很小，对你却帮助很大。你记下的对方需求，正是这些交换藏身的地方。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Trade the package第 3 步 · 整体来换

Learn先学

Talking about one item at a time makes every item a fight. Put several on the table together and trade across them: give ground on the points ranked low for you, hold on the ones ranked high. Check the whole package against your limit before you agree.

一次只谈一项，每一项都会变成争执。把几项一起摆上桌，在它们之间交换：在你排得靠后的项目上让一步，在排得靠前的项目上坚持。同意之前，用你的底线检验整个方案。

They ask: lower the price from 1,000 to 900. I say: if you pay in 7 days, not 30, I can do 950. They say: 7 days is fine, but 950. Deal: 950 paid in 7 days. 1,000 - 950 = 50 given, for 23 days earlier.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Never give for free.
• Find cheap gifts.
• Trade the package.

• 不白白让步。
• 找出便宜的礼物。
• 整体来换。
