Ask and listen先问再听

Open with questions, listen for the reason behind the answer, and say it back before you reply · ~10 min · reading and practice先用问题开场，听出答案背后的原因，回应之前先复述一遍 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Questions before offers第 1 步 · 先问，后报价

Learn先学

Open with questions such as “What matters most to you here?” and “What would a good result look like?”. Open questions start with what, how or why and cannot be answered with yes or no. The more you learn first, the better your first offer will be.

用问题开场，比如“这件事你们最看重什么？”“理想的结果是什么样？”。开放式问题以什么、怎么、为什么开头，不能用是或否回答。先了解得越多，你的第一个报价就越准。

Step 2 · Listen for the reason第 2 步 · 听出原因

Learn先学

When the other side says “that is too expensive”, ask what they are comparing it with. The answer might be a budget limit, a rival quote or a worry about quality, and each calls for a different reply. Resist the urge to prepare your answer while they are still talking.

对方说“太贵了”的时候，问问他们在拿什么比。答案可能是预算上限、竞争对手的报价或对质量的担心，每一种要的回应都不同。别在对方还在说话时就忙着准备自己的回答。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Say it back第 3 步 · 先复述

Learn先学

Before you reply, say what you heard in your own words: “So the budget is fixed at 800 and delivery matters most, is that right?”. It shows you listened, it catches misunderstandings early, and it often softens the mood so they listen to you in turn.

回应之前，先用自己的话说一遍听到的：“所以预算定在 800，而且交付时间最要紧，对吗？”这表明你认真听了，能及早发现误解，也常让气氛缓和，对方也会反过来听你说。

They say: This is too expensive. Ask: What are you comparing it with? They say: Another quote at 800. Say back: So 800 is the number to beat, is that right? Now reply.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask open questions first.
• Listen for the reason.
• Say it back before you answer.

• 先问开放式问题。
• 听出原因。
• 先复述，再回答。
