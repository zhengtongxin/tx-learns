At the table · Lesson 3谈判桌上 · 第 3 课

Close and follow up收尾落实

Check that everyone agrees on the same thing, write it down with who does what by when, and keep the relationship in good shape · ~10 min · reading and practice确认各方说的是同一件事，写下谁在什么时候做什么，并把关系维护好 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Sum it up第 1 步 · 当面总结

Learn先学

When a deal seems done, say it out loud: “So the price is 950, paid in 7 days, delivery in May. Did I get that right?”. People often leave a meeting believing in slightly different deals, and a spoken summary is the cheapest way to catch it.

看起来谈成了，就当面说一遍：“所以价格 950，7 天内付款，五月交付，我理解对吗？”人们离开会议时，常常各自以为是略有不同的方案，当面总结是发现它的最便宜办法。

Step 2 · Write it down第 2 步 · 写下来

Learn先学

Send a short message the same day with each point, who does it and by when. Writing it down is not a sign of distrust, it protects both sides when memories fade. If anything in it is wrong, it is much easier to correct now than in a month.

当天发一条简短的消息，写明每一项、谁来做、什么时候完成。写下来不代表不信任，而是在记忆变淡时保护双方。其中有哪里不对，现在改比一个月后改容易得多。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Look after the relationship第 3 步 · 维护好关系

Learn先学

Most negotiations are not the last one. Thank the other side, do what you promised on time and raise problems early. A deal that both sides are glad they made is far more likely to last, and leaves the next negotiation easier.

大多数谈判不是最后一次。感谢对方，按时兑现承诺，有问题尽早说。双方都乐意的协议更可能长久，也让下一次谈判更轻松。

Agreed: price 950, paid within 7 days, delivery in May. Who: I send the invoice by Monday; they pay by the following Monday. 7 days after Monday is the next Monday. Sent the summary the same day.
