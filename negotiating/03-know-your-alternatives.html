Know your alternatives备好退路

Work out what you would do if there were no deal, because your best alternative decides how firmly you can hold your ground · ~10 min · reading and practice想好谈不成时你会怎么做，因为最好的退路决定你能站得多稳 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · What if there is no deal第 1 步 · 谈不成怎么办

Learn先学

Your best alternative is what you would really do if the talks failed: take another offer, keep what you have, or look elsewhere. It is not a hope, it is a real option. The better it is, the less you need this particular deal.

最好的退路，是谈不成时你真会做的事：接另一个报价、维持现状或去别处找。它不是期望，而是真实存在的选项。它越好，你对眼前这笔交易就越没那么迫切。

Step 2 · Compare, do not guess第 2 步 · 要比较，不要凭感觉

Learn先学

Put a value on your alternative and compare it with the offer. If the offer is better, say yes; if it is worse, say no or ask for more. Your limit usually sits at the point where the offer and the alternative are equal.

给退路估个价，拿它和报价比。报价更好就答应，更差就拒绝或要求更多。你的底线通常就在报价和退路打平的那个点。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Improve it early第 3 步 · 提前把它变好

Learn先学

You can often make your alternative stronger before the meeting: collect a second quote, finish a side project, or save a bit more. Do it early, because an alternative you only mention, without having one, is easy to see through.

会前你常常能让退路变强：多拿一份报价、做完手头的事、多存一点钱。要趁早，因为只是嘴上说说、实际没有的退路，很容易被看穿。

Offer: salary 5,000. My alternative: stay in my job at 4,600. Second quote in hand: 4,900. Best alternative: 4,900. Any offer under 4,900 is worse than walking away.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Name the alternative.
• Compare it with the offer.
• Strengthen it early.

• 说出退路。
• 拿它和报价比较。
• 提前让它变强。
