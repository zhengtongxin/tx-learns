Agents and agentic patterns智能体与智能体模式

Learn by doing: play the agent, step through the patterns, make the call yourself · ~15 min边做边学：亲自扮演智能体、逐步拆解模式、自己做判断 · 约 15 分钟

Step 1 · Be the agent第 1 步 · 亲自当智能体

Role-play before any definition: you are the brain of an AI agent (the LLM). In a real agent, a model makes the choice below every turn. Today that model is you.

先角色扮演，再看定义：你就是 AI 智能体的“大脑”（大模型）。在真实的智能体里，下面每一轮的选择都由模型来做，今天由你来做。

🧠 You = the LLM你 = 大模型read the log, pick a tool读日志，选工具
→
🔧 Tools工具do the work, return a result执行并返回结果
→
📜 Log日志the only thing you can see你唯一能看到的信息

Repeat until the goal is done, in as few turns as you can.

重复这个过程，直到目标完成，轮数越少越好。

In the game, what made it an agent and not a fixed script?在刚才的游戏里，是什么让它成为智能体而不是固定脚本？

Tools alone don’t make an agent. A script can call tools too. The LLM choosing the path does.光有工具不算智能体，脚本也能调用工具。关键在于由大模型来选路径。

Step 3 · Workflow vs agent第 3 步 · 工作流 vs 智能体

Your task could also be built as a workflow: you write the route in code (search → rate → calculate → email → answer), and the LLM only fills in each step. Both are agentic systems. The difference is who decides.

同一个任务也可以做成工作流：路线由你用代码写死（搜索→汇率→计算→发邮件→回答），大模型只负责填每一步的内容。两者都叫智能体系统，区别在于由谁决定。

Step 5 · The price of freedom第 5 步 · 自由的代价

An agent’s path, output and cost are unpredictable. Two tools keep that under control:

智能体的路径、输出和成本都难以预测。有两样工具可以把它管住：

Monitor监控Observability shows what happened: a trace is the step-by-step record of one run, like a flight's black box. Evals measure how good it is: a fixed set of test questions with known good answers, run again after every change, like a driving test.可观测性让你看清发生了什么：trace（追踪记录）是一次运行的逐步记录，就像飞机的黑匣子。评测衡量效果好坏：一组固定的测试问题和公认的好答案，每次改动后都重跑一遍，就像驾照考试。
Guardrails护栏Keep behaviour safe, consistent and inside intended boundaries.让行为安全、一致，并在设定的边界之内。

Same input: last week the agent took 3 steps, today 14. Which tool tells you what happened?同样的输入：上周智能体用了 3 步，今天用了 14 步。哪个工具能告诉你发生了什么？

You can’t fix what you can’t see. Traces show the path it took.看不见就没法修。轨迹能显示它走了哪条路。

Your agent starts emailing customers off-topic replies. What stops that?你的智能体开始给客户发跑题的回复。用什么来阻止？

Guardrails check output and limit what the agent may do. Remember the step limit in the game?护栏会检查输出并限制智能体能做的事。还记得游戏里的步数上限吗？

Step 6 · Two traps第 6 步 · 两个陷阱

🎯 No business problem没有业务问题

LLMs generate content; that doesn’t make it good. Name the business outcome you are improving and a metric for it. Without a metric you can’t tell if the agent helps.

大模型擅长生成，但不代表好。先明确要改善的业务结果和对应的指标。没有指标，就无法判断智能体是否有用。

🧑‍💼 Anthropomorphizing拟人化

A “CEO agent” and an “intern agent” feel natural, but an LLM is not a person. It predicts plausible text. Role prompts are a useful technique (the multi-agent topic uses them), but design the system around the task, not an org chart.

“CEO 智能体”“实习生智能体”听起来很自然，但大模型不是人，它只是预测合理的文字。给模型设定角色是有用的技巧（多智能体主题会用到），但系统要围绕任务来设计，而不是组织架构图。

Step 7 · Decide: workflow or agent?第 7 步 · 判断：工作流还是智能体？

The right approach: start simple, measure, and only climb when it pays off. Think of a real task of yours and answer honestly.

正确做法：从简单开始、衡量，只有值得时才往上走。想一个你自己的真实任务，如实回答。

Takeaways: why this lesson matters要点回顾：这一课为什么重要

The one idea核心一句Agents are powerful and unpredictable. Decide with a metric, not with hype. Skip this and you pay more, get more variation, and cannot prove it helped.智能体强大，但难以预测。用指标做决定，而不是靠热度。忽略这一点，你会花更多钱、得到更大的波动，还无法证明它有用。
01

Measure what matters衡量真正重要的东西

Means含义Start from a business problem and a number you can track.从业务问题和一个可追踪的数字出发。
Impact影响No metric, no proof. You can spend weeks building and never know if it helps.没有指标就没有证据。你可能花数周搭建，却始终不知道有没有用。
Do this怎么做Write one metric before you build, such as minutes saved per ticket.动手前先写下一个指标，例如每张工单节省的分钟数。
02

Let performance pick the design让实际表现决定方案

Means含义Single call, then workflow, then agent. Climb only when the metric improves.先单次调用，再工作流，最后才是智能体。只有指标改善才往上走。
Impact影响Agents add cost, variation and risk. Jumping straight to one burns budget.智能体会增加成本、波动和风险。一上来就用它会白白烧预算。
Do this怎么做Baseline with the simplest option, then compare the next one up.先用最简单的方案做基线，再和上一级方案对比。
03

Plausible is not accurate合理不等于准确

Means含义An LLM writes likely text. It can sound right and be wrong.大模型生成的是“很可能”的文字，听起来对，也可能是错的。
Impact影响Confident mistakes reach customers and decisions.自信的错误会流向客户和决策。
Do this怎么做Add evals, guardrails and a human check for high-stakes output.加入评测、护栏，高风险输出再加人工复核。
04

Tie output to business results让输出对齐业务结果

Means含义A great demo is not value. Value is a number that moved.漂亮的演示不等于价值，价值是某个数字真的变了。
Impact影响Teams ship impressive agents that change nothing the business cares about.团队做出了很惊艳的智能体，却没改变业务关心的任何东西。
Do this怎么做Report in business units: hours saved, cost per case, error rate.用业务单位汇报：节省工时、单案成本、错误率。
