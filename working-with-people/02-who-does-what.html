Who does what谁做什么

Give every task one owner, a clear finish line and a date, so nothing falls between people · ~10 min · reading and practice让每项任务有一个负责人、明确的完成标准和日期，别让事情掉在人与人之间 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One owner第 1 步 · 一个负责人

Learn先学

Tasks given to “the team” are often done by no one, because each person assumes another will do it. Give every task one named owner. Others can help, but one person is accountable, meaning the one who answers for the result. If a task is large, split it until each piece fits one person, rather than sharing one piece among three.

交给“团队”的任务，常常没有人做，因为每个人都以为别人会做。给每项任务指定一个负责人。别人可以帮忙，但只有一个人负责到底，也就是对结果负责的那个人。任务太大，就拆小到每块都能交给一个人，而不是把一块分给三个人。

Step 2 · What done looks like第 2 步 · 做完是什么样子

Learn先学

Say how you will both know the task is finished: “a one-page summary, sent to the manager” is clear; “look into it” is not. Include the date and any limits, such as budget or length. A shared picture of done prevents the quiet surprise of getting something different from what you had in mind.

说明你们怎么才算都知道任务完成：“一页总结，发给经理”很清楚；“研究一下”就不清楚。再加上日期和限制，比如预算或篇幅。对“做完”有共同的画面，就不会等到交付时才发现，拿到的和想要的不一样。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Check in lightly第 3 步 · 轻轻跟进

Learn先学

Agree up front when you will check in, so asking is not nagging. For a long task, ask for an early look at a rough version, because mistakes found early are cheap. When someone is stuck, ask what would help rather than what went wrong. Close the loop by saying thanks and confirming it is done.

一开始就约好什么时候跟进，这样询问就不算唠叨。任务周期长，就请对方早点给一个粗稿看看，因为早发现的错误代价低。有人卡住时，问“怎么帮你”，而不是“哪里出了错”。最后说声谢谢，并确认完成，把事情收住。

Task: one-page summary of the survey. Owner: Mei. Done when: sent to Alex, under 300 words. Date: Friday noon. Check-in: rough draft on Wednesday.

Takeaways要点回顾

• One owner per task.
• Define done and the date.
• Plan a light check-in.

• 每项任务一个负责人。
• 定义完成和日期。
• 安排一次轻轻的跟进。
