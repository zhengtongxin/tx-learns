Handling disagreement处理分歧

Disagree without a fight: find the real issue, restate the other side and look for what you can both accept · ~10 min · reading and practice不吵架地处理分歧：找出真正的问题、复述对方的观点、寻找双方都能接受的办法 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The real issue第 1 步 · 真正的问题

Learn先学

Many arguments are about the wrong thing. Two people may agree on the goal and disagree on the method. Or they may think they disagree about what matters, when they really see the facts differently. Ask: “what exactly do we disagree about?” and write it as one sentence. Often the real gap is smaller than the shouting suggests.

很多争论吵的不是真正的问题。两个人可能目标一致、方法不同；或者以为是价值观不同，其实只是对事实的看法不同。问一句：“我们到底在哪里有分歧？”并写成一句话。真正的差距往往比吵得很凶时看起来要小。

Step 2 · Say their view first第 2 步 · 先说对方的观点

Learn先学

Before you answer, restate the other person’s view well enough that they say “yes, that is it”. This is not agreement; it shows you listened and makes them more willing to listen back. It also catches your own misreadings before you argue against something they never said.

回答之前，先把对方的观点复述到他们说“对，就是这个意思”的程度。这不等于同意，而是表明你听了，也让对方更愿意听你说。它还能在你反驳一个对方根本没说过的观点之前，先发现你自己的误读。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Look for what you can accept第 3 步 · 找双方能接受的

Learn先学

Ask what each side needs, not what each side demands. The need behind “we must launch Monday” may be “we must not look late”, which other options can meet. Propose a small trial, a deadline to revisit, or a way to test who is right. If you still cannot agree, decide who decides, and respect it. That is usually the person who owns the task or the manager above them.

问双方真正需要什么，而不是各自要求什么。“我们必须周一上线”背后的需要，可能是“不能显得晚”，别的做法也能满足。可以提议小范围试行、设一个回头再议的期限，或找个办法测一测谁对。如果仍然谈不拢，就先定好由谁拍板，并尊重这个结果。通常是任务的负责人，或再上一级的主管。

Position (what they ask for): we must launch on Monday. Need behind it (why): we must not look late to the client. Options: launch a small part Monday, full launch Thursday. If stuck: the project owner decides on Friday.
