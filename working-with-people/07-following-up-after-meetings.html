Meetings and roles · Lesson 4会议与分工 · 第 4 课

Following up after meetings会后跟进

Write each action with who, what and by when, send short notes the same day and check in on due dates · ~9 min · reading and practice把每项行动写成谁、做什么、何时完成，当天发出简短纪要，并在到期时跟进 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Write down the actions第 1 步 · 把行动写下来

Learn先学

A meeting ends well when everyone knows what happens next. Before people leave, write each action in three parts: who will do it, what exactly, and by when. “We should look into prices” is not an action. “Aisha will list three supplier prices by Thursday” is. If an action has no name, nobody owns it, and it will not be done.

会议结束时，要让每个人都知道接下来会发生什么。大家离开前，把每一项行动写成三部分：谁来做、具体做什么、何时完成。“我们应该了解一下价格”不是行动。“Aisha 周四前列出三家供应商的价格”才是。行动没有名字，就没人负责，也就不会完成。

Step 2 · Send it the same day第 2 步 · 当天发出

Learn先学

Send the notes within a day, while people still remember. Keep them short: the decision, then the actions with names and dates, then anything still open. Put it where people already look, such as the team chat or the same email thread. Ask people to reply only if something is wrong, so a quiet reply means agreement and nobody has to answer every note.

趁大家还记得的时候，一天之内把纪要发出去。保持简短：先写决定，再写带名字和日期的行动，最后写仍未解决的事。发在大家本来就会看的地方，比如团队聊天或同一封邮件的往来中。请大家只在有问题时才回复，这样没有回复就表示同意，也不必每个人都回每一份纪要。

Step 3 · Check in on the due dates第 3 步 · 到期时跟进

Learn先学

Notes only help if actions get done. On the due date, or a day before, check in with a short message: “Is the supplier list ready for Friday?” At the next meeting, start by going through last time’s actions. Say 8 were agreed and 6 are done: 6 ÷ 8 = 75%. Ask about the other 2 without blame: was the task unclear, too big, or blocked? Fix that, not the person.

纪要只有在行动完成时才有用。到期当天或前一天，用一条简短的消息跟进：“供应商名单周五前能准备好吗？”下次会议先过一遍上次的行动。假设商定了 8 项，完成了 6 项：6 ÷ 8 = 75%。对另外 2 项，不带责备地问一问：是任务不清楚、太大，还是被卡住了？要解决的是这个问题，而不是怪某个人。

Sort each habit as a good way to follow up a meeting or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“会议跟进的好方式”或“弱方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write each action as who, what and by when; an action with no name is not owned.
• Send short notes within a day: decision, actions, open points.
• Ask for a reply only if something is wrong.
• Check in on the due date and ask about missed actions without blame.
• 8 agreed and 6 done is 6 ÷ 8 = 75%: fix what blocked the other 2.

• 把每项行动写成谁、做什么、何时完成；没有名字的行动没人负责。
• 一天之内发出简短纪要：决定、行动、未决事项。
• 只在有问题时才请大家回复。
• 到期时跟进，并不带责备地询问未完成的行动。
• 商定 8 项完成 6 项，就是 6 ÷ 8 = 75%：去解决另外 2 项被卡住的原因。
