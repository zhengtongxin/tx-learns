People and feedback · Lesson 1人与反馈 · 第 1 课

Giving feedback给出反馈

Give feedback that is specific, kind and useful: describe what you saw, its effect and what to try next · ~10 min · reading and practice给出具体、善意又有用的反馈：说你看到了什么、有什么影响、下次可以怎么试 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · What you saw第 1 步 · 你看到了什么

Learn先学

Start with something you can point to: “in the meeting you spoke over Mei twice”, not “you are rude”. Facts about behaviour are easier to hear and to act on than labels about character. If you cannot give an example, you may not be ready to give the feedback yet.

从你能指出来的事情说起：“会上你有两次打断了 Mei”，而不是“你很没礼貌”。对行为的事实描述，比对品格的标签更容易听进去，也更容易照着改。如果举不出例子，可能说明你还没准备好给这个反馈。

Step 2 · The effect第 2 步 · 造成的影响

Learn先学

Then say what it did: “she stopped sharing ideas, and we lost her point on the budget.” The effect explains why it matters, so the feedback is not just your personal preference. Good news deserves the same treatment: “your summary saved us an hour of reading”.

再说它造成了什么：“她后来不再发言，我们错过了她对预算的看法。”影响说明了为什么重要，这样反馈就不只是你的个人喜好。好消息也值得同样的做法：“你的总结让我们省下一小时阅读。”

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · One thing to try第 3 步 · 一件可以试的事

Learn先学

End with one concrete suggestion or question: “next time, could you wait until she finishes?” One thing at a time is enough; a list of ten faults only produces defence. Give feedback soon after the event, in private for criticism, and ask how they see it.

最后给一个具体的建议或问题：“下次能不能等她说完再讲？”一次一件就够了；列出十条毛病只会引来防备。反馈要在事情发生后不久给出，批评要私下说，并问问对方怎么看。

Saw: you spoke over Mei twice in the meeting. Effect: she went quiet and we lost her budget point. Try: wait until she finishes next time. Question: how did it feel from your side?

Takeaways要点回顾

• Describe behaviour, not character.
• Name the effect.
• Suggest one thing to try.

• 描述行为，而不是品格。
• 说明影响。
• 建议一件可以试的事。
