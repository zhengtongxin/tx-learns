Asking for help开口求助

Ask for help in a way that is easy to say yes to: be specific, give context and respect the other person’s time · ~10 min · reading and practice让别人容易答应你的求助：说得具体、给出背景、尊重对方的时间 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Be specific第 1 步 · 说具体

Learn先学

A vague ask such as “can you help me with the report?” makes the other person do the work of guessing what you need. Say exactly what you want: “could you read pages 2 and 3 and tell me whether the numbers make sense?” A small, clear ask is quick to answer and much more likely to get a yes.

“你能帮我看看报告吗？”这类含糊的请求，会让对方先花力气去猜你要什么。直接说清楚：“你能看看第 2、3 页，告诉我数字合不合理吗？”小而清楚的请求回答得快，也更容易得到同意。

Step 2 · Give enough context第 2 步 · 给够用的背景

Learn先学

Add two or three lines of context: what you are trying to do, what you already tried and where you are stuck. This lets the person answer without a round of questions and shows you did your own thinking first. Keep it short; a long story hides the question.

补上两三行背景：你想做什么、已经试过什么、卡在哪里。这样对方不用来回追问就能回答，也说明你先自己动过脑。要简短，故事太长会把问题埋掉。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Leave room to say no第 3 步 · 给对方说“不”的余地

Learn先学

Say when you need the answer, and that “not this week” is a fine reply. A request with room to decline is easier to accept, and people stay willing to help next time. Afterwards, tell them what happened and thank them for the specific thing they did.

说明你什么时候需要答复，并告诉对方“这周不行”也完全可以。留有余地的请求更容易接受，下次人们也还愿意帮你。事后告诉对方结果如何，并具体地感谢对方做的那件事。

Hi Mei, could you read pages 2 and 3 of the report and tell me if the numbers make sense? I checked the totals but not the rates. If you can by Thursday that would help; if not, no problem.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Make the ask specific.
• Give a few lines of context.
• Leave room to say no.

• 把请求说具体。
• 给几行背景。
• 留出说“不”的余地。
