Meetings and roles · Lesson 1会议与分工 · 第 1 课

Useful meetings有用的会议

Decide whether you need a meeting, then run it with a purpose, an agenda and clear next steps · ~10 min · reading and practice先判断要不要开会，再带着目的和议程开，并留下明确的下一步 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Do you need one第 1 步 · 真的需要开会吗

Learn先学

A meeting costs everyone’s time at once, so ask first whether a message would do. Meet when people must decide together, solve something that needs back-and-forth, or work through a sensitive topic. Do not meet just to share information that could be read in two minutes. If you do meet, invite only the people who must decide or who hold the facts.

会议会同时占用所有人的时间，所以先问一句：发条消息行不行？当人们必须一起做决定、要解决需要来回讨论的问题，或要处理敏感话题时，才开会。不要只为了通报两分钟就能读完的信息而开会。要开的话，只请必须做决定或掌握事实的人。

Step 2 · Purpose and agenda第 2 步 · 目的与议程

Learn先学

Write the purpose in one line: “decide which supplier we pick”, not “talk about suppliers”. Then list two or three agenda items with a time for each, and send it before the meeting. People who know the point can prepare, and the person running the meeting has something to steer back to when talk drifts.

用一句话写下目的：“决定选哪家供应商”，而不是“聊聊供应商”。再列出两三项议程，每项标上时间，会前发出去。知道要点的人可以提前准备，话题跑偏时，主持人也有东西可以拉回来。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Close with next steps第 3 步 · 以下一步收尾

Learn先学

Spend the last minutes on three things: what was decided, who does what, and by when. Write them down where everyone can see and send them within a day. A meeting without owners and dates is only a conversation, and the same topic comes back next week.

最后几分钟做三件事：决定了什么、谁做什么、何时完成。写在大家都看得到的地方，一天内发出去。没有负责人和日期的会议只是聊天，同一个话题下周还会回来。

Decided: use the second supplier. Alex: send the order by Friday. Mei: tell the finance team by Monday. Next meeting: only if the order is late.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask whether a message would do.
• Send a purpose and agenda.
• End with owners and dates.

• 先问发消息行不行。
• 发出目的和议程。
• 以负责人和日期收尾。
