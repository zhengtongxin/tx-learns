People and feedback · Lesson 2人与反馈 · 第 2 课

Receiving feedback接受反馈

Listen to feedback without defending, find the useful part and decide what to do with it · ~10 min · reading and practice听反馈时不急着辩解，找出有用的部分，再决定怎么做 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Listen first第 1 步 · 先听

Learn先学

When feedback stings, the first reflex is to explain or defend. Breathe, and just listen to the end. You can say “thank you, let me think about that” without agreeing or arguing. Defending too early tells the other person that giving feedback is not worth the trouble, and then you stop hearing the truth.

反馈刺痛人的时候，第一反应是解释或辩护。先深呼吸，认真听完。你可以只说“谢谢，让我想一想”，既不同意也不争辩。过早辩护等于告诉对方“给你反馈不值得”，久了你就听不到实话了。

Step 2 · Ask for an example第 2 步 · 请对方举例

Learn先学

If the point is vague, ask: “could you give me an example?” or “what would better look like?” Examples turn a feeling into something you can work with, and show you take it seriously. Do not ask why they said it; ask what they saw.

要点含糊时，问一句：“能举个例子吗？”或“更好的样子是什么？”例子把一种感觉变成可以着手的东西，也表明你认真对待。别问“你为什么这么说”，而是问“你看到了什么”。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Decide what to do第 3 步 · 决定怎么做

Learn先学

You do not have to accept all feedback. Sort it: what is clearly true, what is partly true, and what you disagree with. Act on the first, test the second, and thank the person for the third. Then tell them what you will change, so they know it landed.

并非所有反馈都要接受。把它分成：明显对的、部分对的、你不同意的。对第一类行动，对第二类试一试，对第三类也谢谢对方。然后告诉对方你打算改什么，让对方知道反馈有效。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Listen to the end.
• Ask for an example.
• Decide and say what you will do.

• 听完。
• 请对方举例。
• 决定，并说出你会做什么。
