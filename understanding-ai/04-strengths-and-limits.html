Strengths and limits长处与局限

Know what a chat assistant does well, where it struggles, and how to match a task to it · ~10 min · reading and practice知道聊天助手擅长什么、哪里吃力，并学会把任务和它配对 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · What it does well第 1 步 · 它擅长什么

Learn先学

A chat assistant is strong where the work is about language and patterns: drafting and rewording text, summarising a long page, sorting ideas into groups, explaining a topic at your level, and suggesting options to react to. These tasks have no single exact answer, and a good draft saves real time even when you edit it.

聊天助手在语言和规律方面很强：起草和改写文字、概括长文、把想法分组、按你的水平讲解某个主题、提供可以让你去反应的选项。这些任务没有唯一确切的答案，一份不错的草稿，哪怕还要改，也能省下真实的时间。

Step 2 · Where it struggles第 2 步 · 它哪里吃力

Learn先学

It struggles where the answer must be exactly right or must come from after its training (it only knows what it learned up to some point): precise sums on long numbers (it predicts likely text, not calculates, as the last lesson showed), today’s news, facts about your own private files it was never shown, and anything where a single wrong detail matters. It also cannot tell you how sure it really is, so a shaky answer can sound as firm as a solid one.

凡是答案必须分毫不差，或必须来自训练之后（它只知道训练到某个时间点为止学到的东西）的情况，它就吃力：很长数字的精确运算、今天的新闻、它从没见过的你自己的私人文件里的事实，以及任何一个细节出错就要紧的事。它也说不清自己到底有多确定，所以不稳的回答可能听起来和扎实的一样肯定。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Match the task, then do the sum第 3 步 · 先配对任务，再算一算

Learn先学

Ask two questions of a task. Is it about language and patterns? And is a small mistake cheap to catch? If both are yes, it is a good fit. Then estimate the saving: time without help, minus time with the draft and your check. Count the check, because skipping it can cost you a wrong figure sent to a customer.

对一项任务问两个问题：它是关于语言和规律的吗？小错容易发现吗？两个都是“是”，就很合适。然后估一估省下多少：没有帮助时的用时，减去“有草稿加上你检查”的用时。检查要算进去，因为跳过检查，可能让一个错误的数字发给客户。

20 reports, 15 minutes each by hand 20 x 15 = 300 minutes (5 hours) With a draft (made-up numbers): 3 minutes to get it + 5 to check = 8 minutes each 20 x 8 = 160 minutes Saved: 300 - 160 = 140 minutes

Takeaways要点回顾

• It is strong at language and patterns: drafting, summarising, sorting, explaining.
• It struggles with exact figures and anything after its training.
• Pick tasks where a small mistake is easy to catch.
• Savings = time without help minus time with the draft and the check.

• 它擅长语言和规律：起草、概括、分类、讲解。
• 它在精确数字和训练之后的事上吃力。
• 挑那些小错容易发现的任务。
• 节省 = 没有帮助的用时 − 草稿加检查的用时。
