What AI isAI 是什么

Say what AI is in plain words, and tell it apart from software that follows fixed rules · ~10 min · reading and practice用平常的话说出 AI 是什么，并把它和按固定规则运行的软件区分开 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Rules or examples第 1 步 · 规则，还是例子

Learn先学

Ordinary software follows rules that a person wrote: if the total is over S$100, add free delivery. AI is software that gets its behaviour from examples instead. Nobody writes the rule for spotting a cat in a photo. The program is shown many photos and finds the pattern itself.

普通软件按人写好的规则运行：总额超过 S$100，就加免运费。AI 则是从例子里得到行为的软件。没有人去写“怎样认出照片里的猫”这条规则，而是让程序看大量照片，自己找出规律。

Step 2 · Where you already meet it第 2 步 · 你已经在哪里遇到它

Learn先学

You meet AI more often than you notice: the spam filter in your mailbox, the map that suggests a faster route, the keyboard that guesses your next word, the app that turns speech into text. Most of these learned a pattern from many examples. A chat assistant is the same idea, aimed at writing and answering.

你遇到 AI 的次数比自己察觉的多：邮箱里的垃圾邮件过滤、给你换更快路线的地图、猜你下一个字的键盘、把语音转成文字的应用。它们都是从大量例子里学到某种规律。聊天助手是同一个思路，只是用在写作和回答上。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A simple test第 3 步 · 一个简单的检验

Learn先学

To tell the two apart, ask: could a person write down the exact rule? If yes, it is probably ordinary software. If the task is too fuzzy for a rule, such as “is this email spam?”, the program probably learned from examples. Learning from examples also means it can be wrong, so it deserves a check.

要区分两者，问一句：有人能把确切的规则写下来吗？能，多半是普通软件。如果任务太模糊，写不成规则，比如“这封邮件是不是垃圾邮件”，程序多半是从例子里学的。从例子里学也意味着会出错，所以值得检查一下。

Rule: total over S$100 -> free delivery (a person wrote it) Learned: "is this photo a cat?" (found from many photos) Rule: 3 of 4 items in stock -> say so (a person wrote it) Learned: "is this email spam?" (found from many emails)

Takeaways要点回顾

• AI gets its behaviour from examples, not from hand-written rules.
• You already meet it in filters, maps, keyboards and speech apps.
• If a person cannot write the exact rule, the program probably learned.
• What is learned from examples can be wrong, so check it.

• AI 的行为来自例子，而不是手写规则。
• 你已经在过滤器、地图、键盘和语音应用里遇到它。
• 如果没人能写出确切的规则，程序多半是学出来的。
• 从例子里学来的东西可能出错，所以要检查。
