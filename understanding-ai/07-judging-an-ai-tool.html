Judging an AI tool评估一个 AI 工具

Ask four plain questions about an AI tool, test it on your own examples, and decide in small steps · ~9 min · reading and practice对 AI 工具提四个朴素的问题，用你自己的例子测试，并一步一步地决定 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Four questions第 1 步 · 四个问题

Learn先学

A new AI tool arrives and everyone is excited. Before you rely on it, ask four plain questions. What job does it do? What does it need from me, such as my files or private data? How can it be wrong, and would I notice? What does it cost in money, time and risk? If you cannot answer one of them, you do not know the tool yet.

新的 AI 工具出现，大家都很兴奋。在依赖它之前，先问四个朴素的问题。它做什么工作？它需要我提供什么，比如我的文件或私人数据？它可能怎样出错，我能发现吗？它的代价是什么，包括金钱、时间和风险？如果其中一个答不上来，说明你还不了解这个工具。

Step 2 · Try it on your own work第 2 步 · 用你自己的工作试一试

Learn先学

Claims on a website are not evidence. Test the tool on 20 real examples from your own work, where you already know the right answer. Say it gets 17 right: 17 ÷ 20 = 85%. If you need at least 90% for this job, it falls short by 5 percentage points (90% minus 85%). A percentage point is the plain gap between two percentages. Set the bar before you test, by how much harm a wrong answer would do. Look at the 3 it got wrong. Are they the kind that would cause harm? A tool that is wrong in small, easy-to-spot ways is easier to live with than one that is rarely wrong but wrong in a way you would not notice.

网站上的宣传不是证据。用你自己工作中的 20 个真实例子测试这个工具，这些例子的正确答案你本来就知道。假设它答对 17 个：17 ÷ 20 = 85%。如果这项工作要求至少 90%，它就差 5 个百分点（90% 减 85%）。百分点就是两个百分比之间的直接差。要在测试之前定好这条线，依据是答错会造成多大的伤害。看看它答错的那 3 个：是会造成伤害的那种吗？一个错得小而且容易发现的工具，比一个很少出错、但错了你察觉不到的工具更让人放心。

Step 3 · Deciding and reviewing第 3 步 · 决定与回顾

Learn先学

Decide in small steps. Use the tool on low-risk work first, keep a person checking the results, and write down what you learned. Say you score the tool on the four questions and it passes 3 of 4. This is a checklist, not an accuracy score. Name the one it failed, for example it needs private data, and fix that before you use it more widely. Look again after a few months, because tools and your needs change.

一步一步地决定。先把工具用在低风险的工作上，让人检查结果，并写下你学到的东西。假设你用四个问题给工具打分，它通过了其中 3 个。这是一份检查清单，不是准确率。说出没通过的那一个，例如它需要私人数据，并在更广泛使用之前解决它。几个月后再看一次，因为工具和你的需求都会变。

Sort each step as a good way to judge a tool or a weak one.

把每一步分为“好的评估方式”或“弱的评估方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask four plain questions: the job, what it needs from you, how it can be wrong, what it costs.
• Test on your own examples: 17 of 20 is 85%, which is 5 points short of 90%.
• Look at how it is wrong, not only how often.
• Start small on low-risk work with a person checking.
• Fix a failed question before using the tool more widely.

• 问四个朴素的问题：工作、它需要你什么、可能怎样出错、代价是什么。
• 用你自己的例子测试：20 个中 17 个是 85%，比 90% 差 5 个百分点。
• 要看它怎样出错，而不只是多久出一次错。
• 先在低风险工作上小范围使用，并让人检查。
• 在更广泛使用之前，先解决没通过的问题。
