What it can and cannot do · Lesson 3它能做什么，不能做什么 · 第 3 课

Using it well把它用好

Turn what you know into a short routine: choose the task, ask clearly, check and decide · ~10 min · reading and practice把所学变成一套简短的做法：选任务、问清楚、检查、再决定 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A four-step routine第 1 步 · 四步做法

Learn先学

Use the same routine each time. Choose: is this language work where a mistake is cheap to catch? Ask: say what you want, who it is for and what to use. Check: read it, and verify any fact, number or source that matters. Decide: you edit, accept or throw it away. You are always the one who decides.

每次都用同一套做法。选：这是不是语言类任务，而且错了容易发现？问：说清你要什么、给谁看、依据什么。查：通读一遍，核对要紧的事实、数字和来源。定：由你来改、收下，或扔掉。拿主意的永远是你。

Step 2 · Choose by fit and cost第 2 步 · 按合适程度和代价来选

Learn先学

Put each task in one of three groups. Go ahead: low-cost, easy to check, such as a first draft or a list of ideas. Go carefully: the result will be used, so check every fact. Do not hand over: private details, or a decision about a person, where a mistake would hurt someone. The group sets how much checking you give it.

把每项任务放进三组之一。放心做：代价低、容易检查，比如初稿或点子清单。小心做：结果会被使用，每个事实都要核对。不要交出去：隐私信息，或涉及某个人的决定，出错会伤到人。分到哪一组，决定你要花多少力气检查。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Where to go next第 3 步 · 下一步去哪里

Learn先学

You now know what AI is, how it learns, how a language model writes, what it does well and why it sometimes invents. To get better answers, learn to write prompts (next track: AI Prompting; a prompt is what you type to ask the assistant). To use it with care, learn about risk, fairness and privacy. Both build directly on this track, and neither needs any code.

现在你知道了 AI 是什么、怎样学习、语言模型怎样写作、它擅长什么，以及为什么有时会编造。想得到更好的回答，就去学写提示词（下一条路线“提示词”；提示词就是你输入、用来向助手提问的话）；想用得小心，就去学风险、公平和隐私。两者都直接建立在本路线之上，而且都不需要写代码。

Draft a thank-you note -> go ahead Summarise a report for a boss -> go carefully (check facts) Decide who gets a loan -> do not hand over Ideas for a team lunch -> go ahead Quote a price to a customer -> go carefully (check the number) 2 of 5 tasks are "go ahead": 2 / 5 = 40%

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Choose, ask, check, decide: the same routine every time.
• Go ahead, go carefully, or do not hand over.
• The group sets how much checking a task needs.
• Next: learn to write good prompts, or to use AI with care.

• 选、问、查、定：每次都用同一套做法。
• 放心做、小心做、不要交出去。
• 分到哪一组，决定要花多少力气检查。
• 下一步：学写好的提示词，或学着小心地用 AI。
