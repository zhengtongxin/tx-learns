Why it makes things up它为什么会编造

Understand why an assistant sometimes invents facts, and learn the habits that catch it · ~10 min · reading and practice明白助手为什么有时会编造事实，并学会能抓住它的习惯 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A gap gets filled第 1 步 · 空缺会被填上

Learn先学

When a model does not have the fact, it does not stop. Its job is to continue the text in a likely way, so it produces something that fits the shape of a good answer. When this is wrong it is called a hallucination. It is not lying: there is no intention. It is a likely-sounding continuation that happens to be false.

模型没有掌握某个事实时，不会停下来。它的任务是用很可能的方式续写文字，于是它生成一段符合“好答案的样子”的东西。这种错误叫幻觉。它不是在撒谎，因为并没有意图，只是一段听起来很可能、碰巧是假的续写。

Step 2 · What is most often invented第 2 步 · 最常被编造的是什么

Learn先学

Watch for details that have a shape the model can imitate: names, dates, quotes, page numbers, web links, case numbers and the titles of books or papers. A made-up reference looks exactly like a real one. The more specific and obscure the detail you ask for, the more likely it is a guess.

要留意那些有固定形状、模型容易模仿的细节：人名、日期、引文、页码、网址、案号，以及书和论文的标题。编出来的参考文献，和真的看起来一模一样。你要的细节越具体、越冷门，就越可能是猜的。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Catch it before it costs you第 3 步 · 在它造成损失之前抓住它

Learn先学

Three habits help. Ask for sources, then open them. Check a few items yourself and see how many hold up, which tells you how far to trust the rest. And give the assistant the text to work from, so it quotes your material instead of its memory. Do not use a detail you have not checked in anything that matters.

有三个习惯很管用。要来源，然后真的去打开。自己抽查几项（只查 10 项时，真实比例可能比 20% 高得多或低得多），看有几项站得住，就知道其余部分能信多少。再就是把要处理的文字交给助手，让它引用你的材料，而不是靠它的记忆。凡是重要的内容，没核对过的细节就不要用。

10 sources checked by hand 2 could not be found, or did not say what it claimed -> 2 / 10 = 20% invented If it gave 50 sources: about 50 x 20 / 100 = 10 to expect wrong Checking every one is safest; with only 10 checked, the real share could be well above or below 20%. Or give it the text instead

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• When it lacks a fact, it fills the gap with likely text.
• Names, dates, quotes and references are invented most often.
• Open the sources and check a sample to know how far to trust the rest.
• Give it your own text to work from when accuracy matters.

• 没有某个事实时，它用很可能的文字填补空缺。
• 人名、日期、引文和参考文献最常被编造。
• 打开来源并抽查，才知道其余部分能信多少。
• 准确性要紧时，把你自己的文字交给它去处理。
