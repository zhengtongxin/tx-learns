How it learns它怎样学习

See how a program learns from labelled examples, and why the examples matter more than anything else · ~10 min · reading and practice看程序怎样从带标签的例子里学习，以及为什么例子比什么都重要 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Training is practice with answers第 1 步 · 训练就是带答案的练习

Learn先学

To teach a spam filter, people collect many emails and mark each one spam or not spam. These marks are called labels. The program adjusts itself until its guesses match the labels as often as possible: when it calls a spam email “not spam”, it changes its settings (the program’s internal numbers) a little so that email looks more like spam next time. This practice stage is called training, and the examples used are the training data.

要教会垃圾邮件过滤器，人们先收集大量邮件，给每封标上垃圾或正常。这些标记叫标签。程序不断调整自己，让它的判断尽量多地和标签一致：当它把一封垃圾邮件判成“不是垃圾”，就把设置（程序内部的数字）微调一点，让下次这类邮件更像垃圾。这个练习阶段叫训练，用到的例子叫训练数据。

Step 2 · The examples shape the result第 2 步 · 例子决定结果

Learn先学

A program can only learn what its examples show. If the training emails are mostly in English, it may do badly on Chinese emails. If almost all examples are spam, it may learn to call everything spam. So the first questions about any AI are about its examples: how many, how varied, and who is missing.

程序只能学到例子里有的东西。如果训练邮件大多是英文，它处理中文邮件可能就很差；如果例子几乎全是垃圾邮件，它可能学会把什么都叫垃圾邮件。所以问任何 AI，先问它的例子：有多少、多不多样、缺了谁。

Step 3 · Training, then use第 3 步 · 先训练，再使用

Learn先学

After training, the program is used on new emails it has never seen. How well it does there is what counts, so people test it on a separate set of examples kept aside. Test on the same emails it trained on and it looks better than it really is, like a student who saw the exam questions beforehand.

训练之后，程序要处理它没见过的新邮件。它在新邮件上表现如何才是关键，所以人们会留出另一组例子来测试。如果用训练过的同一批邮件来测，它看起来会比实际好，就像学生提前见过考题。

Training data: 1,000 emails, 300 marked spam Share of spam: 300 / 1,000 = 30% Test set: 200 new emails the program never saw It flags 50 of them: 50 / 200 = 25% People had labelled the 200 test emails; 45 really were spam: 45 / 200 = 22.5% (50 flagged vs 45 real: at least 5 were false alarms, and it may have missed some spam too) Each wrong guess nudges the program a little, and it tries again

Takeaways要点回顾

• Training is practice on examples with correct answers (labels).
• A program can only learn what its examples show.
• Test on new cases, not on the examples it trained on.
• The first questions about any AI are about its examples.

• 训练就是在带正确答案（标签）的例子上练习。
• 程序只能学到例子里有的东西。
• 要用新情况测试，而不是用它训练过的例子。
• 问任何 AI，先问它的例子。
