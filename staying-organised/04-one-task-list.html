Tasks and notes · Lesson 1任务和笔记 · 第 1 课

One task list一张任务清单

Keep every task in one trusted list, so your head is free for the work and nothing slips between notes · ~10 min · reading and practice把所有任务放进一张可信的清单，让脑子腾出来做事，也不会有任务从几份笔记之间漏掉 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One place第 1 步 · 同一个地方

Learn先学

Tasks scattered over sticky notes, chats and memory cause a low, steady worry that you are forgetting something. Pick one list, on paper or an app, and put everything there the moment it comes up. The list only works if you trust it, so it has to hold all of it.

任务散落在便利贴、聊天记录和记忆里，会带来一种低低的、持续的担心：我是不是忘了什么。选一张清单，纸上或应用里都行，任务一出现就记进去。清单只有在你信任它的时候才管用，所以它得装下全部。

Step 2 · A clear next action第 2 步 · 清楚的下一步

Learn先学

“Taxes” on a list is a worry, not a task. Write the very next physical step: “Find last year’s tax letter in the blue folder”. It begins with a verb and can be done in one sitting. When a task feels heavy, the usual reason is that its first step is not written down.

清单上写“税务”，是一种焦虑，而不是任务。要写下紧接着的那个具体动作：“在蓝色文件夹里找出去年的税务信”。它以动词开头，一次就能做完。一个任务让人觉得沉重，通常是因为它的第一步没写下来。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Today’s short list第 3 步 · 今天的短清单

Learn先学

Each morning, pick three things from the master list that matter most today and write them on top. The big list is for keeping tasks safe, and the short one is for doing. Finish the three first; then take more from the list if there is time.

每天早上，从总清单里选出今天最重要的三件事，写在最上面。大清单是用来保管任务的，短清单才是用来做事的。先做完这三件，有时间再从清单里拿别的。

TODAY: 1 call the landlord 2 draft the report outline 3 book the dentist MASTER LIST - Find last year's tax letter in the blue folder - Email Sam the meeting notes - Ask HR how many days' notice for leave Rule: every task starts with a verb and fits one sitting.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Keep one list you trust.
• Write clear next actions.
• Choose three for today.

• 用一张你信任的清单。
• 写清楚的下一步。
• 选三件今天的事。
