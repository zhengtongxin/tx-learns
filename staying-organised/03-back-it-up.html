Back it up做好备份

Keep a second copy of what matters in a different place, so one lost or broken device does not cost you your work · ~10 min · reading and practice把重要的东西再留一份放在另一个地方，这样一台设备丢了或坏了，也不会丢掉你的成果 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · What matters第 1 步 · 什么重要

Learn先学

You cannot back up everything, and you do not need to. Ask what would hurt to lose: documents you made, photos, contracts, anything you cannot download again. Programs and downloads can be fetched again; your own work cannot. Make a short list of those places.

你不可能、也不需要备份所有东西。问问自己：丢了会心疼的是什么？自己做的文档、照片、合同，凡是没法再下载的。程序和下载的文件可以重新取得，你自己的成果却不行。把这些地方列一张短清单。

Step 2 · A second place第 2 步 · 另一个地方

Learn先学

A copy on the same device is not a backup: if the device is lost, both go. Keep the second copy somewhere else, such as an external drive at home or a storage service online. Many people keep one copy on a drive and another online, in case one place fails. Set it to copy by itself, because backups you must remember do not last.

放在同一台设备上的副本不算备份：设备丢了，两份一起没了。第二份要放在别的地方，比如家里的外接硬盘，或在线存储服务。很多人一份放硬盘，一份放在线上，以防其中一处出问题。设成自动复制，因为要靠记性的备份，坚持不了多久。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Try getting it back第 3 步 · 试着取回

Learn先学

A backup you have never tried to restore is a hope. Once in a while, pick one file and bring it back from the copy. If it opens, the backup works. If it does not, you have just found out while there is still time to fix it.

从没试过取回的备份，只是一种希望。隔一段时间，挑一个文件从备份里取回来。打开了，说明备份有效。打不开，那你是在还来得及补救的时候发现的。

Worth keeping: documents, photos, contracts. (Programs can be downloaded again.) Copy 1: working files on the laptop. Copy 2: external drive at home, automatic every night. Copy 3: online storage, automatic. Test: every three months, restore one file and open it.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Back up what you cannot get again.
• Keep the copy somewhere else, and automatic.
• Test the restore now and then.

• 备份没法再取得的东西。
• 副本放在别处，并设成自动。
• 隔一段时间测试取回。
