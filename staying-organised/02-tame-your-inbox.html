Tame your inbox管好收件箱

Decide what to do with each message once, so the inbox is a short list of real work and not a pile of worry · ~10 min · reading and practice每条消息只决定一次怎么处理，让收件箱成为一张简短的真实待办，而不是一堆焦虑 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Touch it once第 1 步 · 只处理一次

Learn先学

Opening a message, thinking “later” and closing it means you will have to read it all over again. When you open a message, decide: what happens to this? Then do it. The aim is to touch each message once, and to leave the inbox with only what truly waits for you.

打开一条消息，想着“待会儿再说”，又关掉，意味着以后还得从头再读一遍。打开消息时就决定：这条怎么处理？然后去做。目标是每条消息只碰一次，让收件箱里只留下真正在等你的东西。

Step 2 · Four answers第 2 步 · 四个答案

Learn先学

Each message gets one of four answers. Do it now, if it takes two minutes or less. Delegate it, if someone else should handle it. Schedule it, if it needs real time: write it on your task list and archive the message. Delete or archive it, if nothing is needed. “Leave it in the inbox” is not an answer.

每条消息得到四个答案之一。现在就做，如果两分钟内能完成。转交，如果该别人处理。安排，如果需要整块时间：写进任务清单，并把消息归档。删除或归档，如果什么都不用做。“留在收件箱里”不是答案。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Set times第 3 步 · 固定的时间

Learn先学

A message that arrives is not a message you must answer this second. Check at set times, say three times a day, and close the inbox in between. This protects your focus, and people soon learn that anything truly urgent reaches you another way.

消息到了，不代表你必须这一秒就回。在固定的时间查看，比如一天三次，其余时间把收件箱关上。这样保护专注力，而且别人很快就会知道，真正紧急的事会通过别的方式找到你。

Check at: 9:00, 13:00, 16:30. Two minutes or less -> do it now. Someone else should do it -> forward it, note "waiting for Sam". Needs real time -> task list, then archive the message. Nothing needed -> archive or delete. Inbox target: only what waits for me.
