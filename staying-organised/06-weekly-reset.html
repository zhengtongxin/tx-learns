Weekly reset每周整理

Spend twenty minutes a week to bring files, inbox, tasks and notes back to a clean state, so small mess never grows · ~10 min · reading and practice每周花二十分钟，把文件、收件箱、任务和笔记带回整洁状态，小小的乱就不会变大 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A fixed slot第 1 步 · 一个固定的时间

Learn先学

Organising systems fail not for lack of rules but because the tidying never happens. Put a weekly slot in your calendar, such as Friday at 4 pm for twenty minutes. A fixed slot beats the intention to “do it when I have time”, because that time never comes.

整理系统失败，往往不是因为没有规则，而是因为从不去整理。在日历里定一个每周的时间，比如周五下午 4 点，二十分钟。固定时间胜过“有空再做”的打算，因为那个空永远不来。

Step 2 · A short checklist第 2 步 · 一张简短清单

Learn先学

Walk through the same few steps each time: empty the inbox with the four answers, move stray files to their folders, tick off done tasks and rewrite vague ones, file or delete the week’s notes, and check that the backup ran. Same steps, same order, so you do not need to decide what to do.

每次走同样的几步：用四个答案清空收件箱，把散落的文件放回文件夹，勾掉做完的任务、改写含糊的任务，归档或删除这一周的笔记，确认备份已经运行。步骤相同、顺序相同，你不用再决定要做什么。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Look ahead第 3 步 · 预看下一周

Learn先学

Finish by looking at next week’s calendar and task list. Pick the three most important things and note when you will do them. You begin Monday with a plan instead of a pile. Ending the reset like this makes the twenty minutes pay for themselves.

最后看一眼下周的日历和任务清单。选出最重要的三件事，写下什么时候做。周一开始时你手里是一个计划，而不是一堆东西。这样收尾，会让这二十分钟物有所值。

Friday 16:00, 20 minutes. 1 Inbox to zero with the four answers. 2 Stray files into their folders. 3 Task list: tick off, rewrite vague ones. 4 Notes: file or delete. 5 Backup ran? Yes / no. 6 Next week: pick three, say when.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Fix a weekly time.
• Use the same short checklist.
• End by planning next week.

• 定一个每周的时间。
• 用同一张简短清单。
• 最后规划下一周。
