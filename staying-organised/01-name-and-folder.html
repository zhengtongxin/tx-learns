Name and folder命名与文件夹

Give files names and homes that you can read in a year, so finding one takes seconds, not minutes · ~10 min · reading and practice给文件起一年后还看得懂的名字，并放在固定的地方，找一个文件只要几秒 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A name that says what and when第 1 步 · 写明内容和时间的名字

Learn先学

A file called “final2” tells you nothing in a month. Put the date, the subject and the version in the name: “2026-03-14 budget draft 2”. Writing the date as year, month, day makes files sort in the order they were made. Choose names for the person who will search in a year: you.

一个叫“final2”的文件，一个月后你就不知道它是什么了。把日期、主题和版本写进名字：“2026-03-14 预算草稿 2”。日期按年、月、日写，文件就会按做出来的先后顺序排列。取名字时，想想一年后来找它的人，也就是你自己。

Step 2 · A shallow tree第 2 步 · 浅一点的文件夹结构

Learn先学

Deep folders hide things; one huge pile hides everything. Aim for two or three levels, with folders named by what the work is for: “Taxes”, “Travel”, “Work reports”. If you hesitate between two folders for a file, the folders overlap; merge them, or pick one simple rule for which folder wins (for example, by the main purpose of the file).

太深的文件夹会藏东西，一大堆文件则什么都藏。目标是两三层，文件夹按用途命名：“税务”“旅行”“工作报告”。如果一个文件你在两个文件夹之间犹豫，说明这两个文件夹有重叠；合并它们，或定一条简单规则决定放哪个（比如按文件的主要用途）。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · One rule, kept第 3 步 · 一条规则，坚持用

Learn先学

A perfect system you do not use is worse than a simple one you do. Write your naming rule in one line and put the file in its folder at the moment you save it. Tidying at the end of a project is slower than naming it well on day one.

用不上的完美系统，不如一个坚持使用的简单系统。把你的命名规则写成一行，保存文件的同时就放进它的文件夹。等项目结束再整理，比第一天就好好命名要慢得多。

Name: year-month-day, subject, version -> 2026-03-14 budget draft 2 Folders: Work reports / Travel / Taxes (two levels at most) Tie-break: file under what it is for, not who sent it. Save in the right folder the first time.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Put what and when in the name.
• Keep folders shallow and by purpose.
• Follow one rule from the first save.

• 名字里写明内容和时间。
• 文件夹浅，并按用途分。
• 从第一次保存起就按一条规则来。
