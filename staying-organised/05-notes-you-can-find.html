Tasks and notes · Lesson 2任务和笔记 · 第 2 课

Notes you can find找得到的笔记

Take notes in a form you can search and reuse later, so what you wrote down helps you again · ~10 min · reading and practice用以后能搜索、能再用的方式记笔记，让你写下的东西能再次帮到你 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A title to search for第 1 步 · 能被搜到的标题

Learn先学

Notes pile up fast, and a note you cannot find is a note you did not take. Give each note a plain title made of the words you would type when looking for it: “Leave notice period” rather than “Notes from Tuesday”. A date alone is not a title.

笔记堆得很快，找不到的笔记等于没记。给每条笔记起一个朴素的标题，用你找它时会输入的词：“请假的提前通知期”，而不是“周二的笔记”。光有日期不是标题。

Step 2 · Short and about one thing第 2 步 · 短，并且只讲一件事

Learn先学

One note, one idea. A long note about five things is hard to search and hard to reuse. Write in your own words, in a few lines. If you copy a lot, you will have a lot to read and nothing you have understood.

一条笔记，一个想法。一条讲五件事的长笔记，既难搜索又难再用。用自己的话，写几行。如果大段照抄，你只会多出很多要读的东西，却没有你真正理解的。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Tie it to the work第 3 步 · 和工作连起来

Learn先学

A note earns its place when it helps some task or decision. At the end of a note, add one line: where I will use this. Then link it from the task or project it belongs to. A note with no link and no use is the first to be forgotten.

一条笔记，只有帮到某个任务或决定，才算有价值。在笔记末尾加一行：我会在哪里用到。然后从它所属的任务或项目链接到它。没有链接、没有用处的笔记，最先被忘掉。

Title: Leave notice period HR said: 14 days for annual leave, 3 days for sick leave with a certificate. Use: planning the March trip; add to the Travel task. Link: Travel / March trip.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Title it with your search words.
• One note, one idea.
• Link every note to some use.

• 用你的搜索词起标题。
• 一条笔记，一个想法。
• 让每条笔记都连到某种用途。
