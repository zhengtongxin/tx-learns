Asking and following up · Lesson 2提问与追问 · 第 2 课

Ask without fear放下顾虑去问

Ask in front of others when you need to, treat a question as normal rather than a weakness, and make it safe for others to ask · ~10 min · reading and practice需要时当着别人的面提问，把提问看作平常事而不是弱点，并让别人也敢问 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · You are rarely the only one第 1 步 · 你很少是唯一的一个

Learn先学

When something is unclear, there are usually others in the room who also do not follow, and they are waiting for someone to speak. Asking is not a sign you are behind. Often it is the most useful thing anyone does in the meeting, because it brings the confusion into the open.

有什么说得不清楚时，房间里通常还有别人也没跟上，他们在等有人开口。提问并不表示你落后了。它往往是会上最有用的举动，因为它把大家的困惑摆到了台面上。

Step 2 · Ask without apology第 2 步 · 不带歉意地问

Learn先学

Skip “sorry, this is probably a silly question”. It tells people to expect a silly question. Use plain openers: “Can I check I understood: you mean the deadline moved to Friday?” “I did not follow the last step. Could you go through it again?” Calm, short and specific.

省掉“不好意思，这可能是个傻问题”。这句话是在让人预期一个傻问题。用朴实的开头：“我确认一下我的理解：你的意思是期限改到周五了？”“最后一步我没跟上，能再讲一遍吗？”平静、简短、具体。

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Make it safe for others第 3 步 · 让别人也敢问

Learn先学

If you lead a group or teach, how you respond decides whether people ask again. Say thanks, answer without sighing, and ask “who else was wondering this?” People learn from what you do when someone is unsure far more than from any rule about questions.

如果你带领一个团队或教别人，你怎么回应，决定了大家以后还问不问。说声谢谢，不叹气地回答，再问一句“还有谁也在想这个？”比起任何关于提问的规定，人们更会从你在别人不确定时的做法里学到东西。

Instead of: "Sorry, silly question, but..." Say: "Can I check I understood: ... ?" Say: "I did not follow the last step. Could you go through it again?" When someone asks you: "Thanks, good question. Who else was wondering that?"

Takeaways要点回顾

• You are rarely the only one confused.
• Ask plainly, without apology.
• Thank people who ask.

• 你很少是唯一困惑的人。
• 平实地问，不带歉意。
• 感谢提问的人。
