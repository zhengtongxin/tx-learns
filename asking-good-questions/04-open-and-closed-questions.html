Open and closed questions开放式与封闭式问题

Use closed questions for facts and open ones for understanding, and choose on purpose · ~10 min · reading and practice用封闭式问题问事实，用开放式问题求理解，并有意识地选择 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Two kinds of question第 1 步 · 两种问题

Learn先学

A closed question has a short answer: “Is the report due Friday?” An open question invites a longer one: “What worries you about the Friday deadline?” Neither is better. They do different jobs, and most people reach for one kind and forget the other.

封闭式问题有简短的答案：“报告周五交吗？”开放式问题邀请更长的回答：“你对周五的期限有什么担心？”两者没有高下之分，它们做不同的事，大多数人只习惯用其中一种，忘了另一种。

Step 2 · Closed to confirm第 2 步 · 用封闭式来确认

Learn先学

When you need a fact, a decision or a commitment, ask closed: “Do you approve the plan?” “Is the file in the shared folder?” You get a clear answer fast. Asking open questions for a simple fact only takes longer, since the answer wanders. Use closed questions to end a vague discussion: “So, are we going with option B?”

需要一个事实、一个决定或一个承诺时，用封闭式：“你批准这个方案吗？”“文件在共享文件夹里吗？”你会很快得到清楚的答案。只要一个事实却用开放式提问，只会更费时间，因为回答会跑题。用封闭式问题来结束含糊的讨论：“那么，我们选方案 B 吗？”

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Open to discover第 3 步 · 用开放式来发现

Learn先学

When you do not yet know what matters, ask open: “How does this step work for you?” “What would make this easier?” The other person can tell you something you did not think to ask. Start with “what”, “how” or “tell me about”, and stay quiet after.

还不知道什么重要时，用开放式：“这一步你这边是怎么做的？”“怎样会更容易？”对方可能告诉你一件你没想到要问的事。用“什么”“怎么”或“给我讲讲”开头，然后安静地听。

Closed: Is the report due Friday? -> yes or no Closed: Do you approve the plan? -> yes or no Open: What worries you about Friday? -> a story Open: How does this step work for you? -> a story Facts and decisions: closed. Understanding: open.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Closed for facts and decisions.
• Open for understanding.
• Stay quiet after an open question.

• 封闭式用于事实和决定。
• 开放式用于理解。
• 问完开放式问题后保持安静。
