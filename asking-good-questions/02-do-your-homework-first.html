Before you ask · Lesson 2提问之前 · 第 2 课

Do your homework first先自己查一查

Spend a few minutes looking yourself and say what you tried, so people can help quickly and respect your question · ~10 min · reading and practice先花几分钟自己找，并说明你试过什么，这样别人才能很快帮上忙，也更尊重你的问题 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A few minutes of looking第 1 步 · 花几分钟找一找

Learn先学

Before you ask, spend a short, fixed time looking yourself: ten minutes in the documents, the search box or the earlier messages. Many answers are right there. If you still find nothing, you have lost little and you now understand the question better.

提问之前，先花一小段固定的时间自己找：十分钟翻翻文档、搜索框或之前的消息。很多答案就在那儿。如果还是找不到，你损失不大，而且对这个问题有了更好的理解。

Step 2 · Show what you tried第 2 步 · 讲出你试过什么

Learn先学

Say what you did and what happened: “I searched the shared folder for the template and found two old versions, but I do not know which is current.” This tells the helper where you are, so they do not repeat what you did, and it shows that you value their time.

说清你做了什么、结果怎样：“我在共享文件夹里搜了模板，找到两个旧版本，但不知道哪个是现行的。”这让帮忙的人知道你到了哪一步，不用重复你做过的事，也表明你重视对方的时间。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Do not wait too long either第 3 步 · 也不要等太久

Learn先学

Looking first is a courtesy, not a test of how long you can struggle. If you have lost an hour on something a colleague could answer in a minute, ask. Set a time limit before you start, and when it is up, send the question you have already written.

先自己找是一种礼貌，不是比谁能硬撑更久。如果你为一件同事一分钟就能回答的事耗掉了一小时，就去问。开始前先设定时间上限，时间到了，就发出你已经写好的问题。

Question: which template is current? I tried: searched the shared folder: found two versions read the last three messages: no mention Time spent: 10 minutes Now asking: which one should I use?

Takeaways要点回顾

• Look for a short, fixed time first.
• Say what you tried and what happened.
• Ask when the time is up.

• 先花一小段固定时间找。
• 说清你试过什么、结果如何。
• 时间到了就问。
