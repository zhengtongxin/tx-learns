Asking and following up · Lesson 3提问与追问 · 第 3 课

Follow up and learn追问并学到东西

Use the answer well: check you understood, ask the next question and tell the helper what happened · ~10 min · reading and practice好好利用答案：确认自己听懂，问下一个问题，并告诉帮忙的人结果如何 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Say it back第 1 步 · 把它说回去

Learn先学

After the answer, say what you understood in your own words: “So I use the March version, and send it to Mei by Friday?” If you got it right, you have confirmed it. If not, the misunderstanding shows now, not after you have done the wrong work.

得到答案后，用自己的话说出你的理解：“所以我用三月版，周五前发给梅？”说对了，你就确认了。说错了，误会现在就暴露出来，而不是等你做完了错的事才发现。

Step 2 · Ask the next question第 2 步 · 问下一个问题

Learn先学

One answer usually raises another question. Before the person leaves, ask the one that comes next: “What should I do if the data is missing?” “Who do I ask if this changes?” A good follow-up often comes from asking “and then what?”

一个答案通常会引出下一个问题。对方走之前，把接下来的问题问出来：“如果数据缺了怎么办？”“如果有变化我该问谁？”好的追问往往来自一句“然后呢？”

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Close the loop第 3 步 · 告诉对方结果

Learn先学

Afterwards, tell the helper how it went: “Used the March version, report sent, thank you.” It takes ten seconds and makes them glad they helped. Write the answer in your notes, so you do not need to ask the same question again.

事后告诉帮忙的人结果如何：“用了三月版，报告已发出，谢谢。”只要十秒钟，却会让对方庆幸自己帮了忙。把答案记进笔记，免得下次再问同一个问题。

Say it back: "So I use the March version and send it by Friday?" Next question: "What if the data is missing?" Close the loop: "Used March, report sent. Thank you!" Notes: March version = current template

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Say the answer back in your words.
• Ask the next question before they leave.
• Tell the helper how it went, and keep notes.

• 用自己的话说回答案。
• 对方走之前问下一个问题。
• 告诉帮忙的人结果，并做好笔记。
