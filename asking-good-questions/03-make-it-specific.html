Before you ask · Lesson 3提问之前 · 第 3 课

Make it specific让问题具体

Turn a broad question into one that has a clear answer, with the context a stranger would need · ~10 min · reading and practice把宽泛的问题变成有明确答案的问题，并附上陌生人也需要的背景 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One question at a time第 1 步 · 一次一个问题

Learn先学

A message with five questions often gets one answer. Ask one thing at a time, or number them so each can be answered. “Which template is current?” is easy to answer. “Can you tell me about templates, the review and the deadline?” is not.

一条消息里有五个问题，常常只得到一个回答。一次问一件事，或者编号，让每个都能被回答。“哪个模板是现行的？”很好回答。“能给我讲讲模板、评审和期限吗？”就不好回答。

Step 2 · Just enough context第 2 步 · 刚好够用的背景

Learn先学

Imagine the person who gets your message knows nothing about your situation. Give two or three sentences: what you are working on, what you are stuck on, what you tried. Not your whole story. If they have to ask “what do you mean?”, the question lost a round trip.

想象收到消息的人对你的情况一无所知。给两三句话：你在做什么、卡在哪、试过什么。不用讲整个来龙去脉。如果对方还得问“你指什么？”，这个问题就白白多绕了一个来回。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Say what answer would help第 3 步 · 说明什么样的答案有用

Learn先学

Tell them the shape you need: “a yes or no is enough”, “a link to the document”, “two options and which you prefer”. This saves them guessing how much to write, and it saves you reading a long answer when a short one would do.

告诉对方你需要的形式：“回答是或否就够了”“给我一个文档链接”“两个选项，以及你更倾向哪个”。这样对方不用猜该写多少，你也不用读长篇大论，而其实一句话就够了。

Broad: "Can you help with the report?" Specific: "I am writing the March report from the shared template and cannot tell which of the two versions is current. A link to the right one is enough."
