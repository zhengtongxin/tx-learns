Before you ask · Lesson 1提问之前 · 第 1 课

Know what you need先想清楚要什么

Decide what you actually want to find out before you ask, so the answer fits the need · ~10 min · reading and practice提问之前先想清楚你到底想知道什么，这样得到的答案才合用 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Start from the use第 1 步 · 从用途出发

Learn先学

A question like “how does this work?” can be answered in a sentence or a semester. These habits work whether you ask a colleague, a teacher or an AI assistant. Ask yourself first: what will I do with the answer? “I need to decide whether to use this tool for next week’s report.” Now the answer can be short and aimed at your decision.

“这个是怎么运作的？”这样的问题，可以用一句话回答，也可以讲一个学期。这些习惯对同事、老师或 AI 助手都适用。先问问自己：我拿这个答案做什么？“我要决定下周的报告用不用这个工具。”这样答案就能又短又对准你的决定。

Step 2 · Split known from unknown第 2 步 · 分清已知和未知

Learn先学

Draw two short lists: what you already know, and what you do not. Often the second list is shorter than the feeling of being lost suggests. You may find you are missing one fact, not the whole subject, and one fact is a much easier thing to ask for.

画两张短清单：已经知道的，和不知道的。第二张通常比“一头雾水”的感觉短得多。你可能发现自己缺的只是一个事实，而不是整个主题，而问一个事实要容易得多。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Write it before you send it第 3 步 · 发出之前先写下来

Learn先学

Writing the question forces it to become clear. Many questions answer themselves halfway through writing. If yours does not, you now have a question the other person can read in a few seconds and answer without a long chat to find out what you meant.

把问题写下来，会逼它变清楚。很多问题写到一半就自己有答案了。如果没有，你也已经有了一个对方几秒钟就能读完的问题，不必先聊半天才知道你想问什么。

What I will do with the answer: decide whether to use the new tool for the report I know: it exports to a spreadsheet I do not know: whether it keeps my data private Question: does the tool keep uploaded data private?

More practice再练一练

Takeaways要点回顾

• Start from what you will do with the answer.
• Split what you know from what you do not.
• Write the question before you send it.

• 从你要拿答案做什么出发。
• 分清知道的和不知道的。
• 发出之前先写下来。
