Governance and flow questions治理与流程题目

A self-check: answer twelve questions on governance, events, flow, AI and the lifecycle, and learn which lesson to revisit for each miss · ~15 min · practice自测：做十二道关于治理、事件、流程、AI 和生命周期的题目，答错时知道该回看哪一课 · 约 15 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · Governance, reports and events第 1 步 · 治理、报告与事件

Learn先学

Governance sets direction and checks progress, a service level agreement (SLA) is a written promise of a service target, a report can be green while users are unhappy (called a watermelon report: green outside, red inside), and events come in three kinds: informational (just a note), warning (a limit is near) and exception (a limit was crossed or something failed). Missed one? Revisit “Governance and improvement” or “Service levels and relationships”.

治理定方向并检查进展，服务水平协议（SLA）是对服务目标的书面承诺，报告可以全绿而用户仍不满（叫西瓜式报告：外绿内红），事件有三种：信息（只是记录）、警告（快到限度）和异常（已越过限度或出了故障）。答错了？回看“治理与持续改进”或“服务水平与关系”。

Step 2 · Flow, AI and simplicity第 2 步 · 流程、AI 与简化

Learn先学

A value stream map (a picture of the steps from request to value) shows waiting, people stay accountable for what AI drafts, and you simplify before you automate. Missed one? Revisit “Value streams”, “AI in service management” or “Guiding principles”.

价值流图（从请求到价值的步骤图）显示等待，AI 起草的内容仍由人负责，先简化再自动化。答错了？回看“价值流”“服务管理中的 AI”或“指导原则”。

Step 3 · Knowledge, lifecycle and DevOps第 3 步 · 知识、生命周期与 DevOps

Learn先学

Written-down know-how is knowledge management, lifecycle activities loop, and DevOps joins development and operations, working toward shared goals with automation and quick feedback. Missed one? Knowledge: “Service desk and requests”. Lifecycle: “Product and service lifecycle”. DevOps: “Working with other frameworks”. Release management: “Change and release”. Suppliers: “Service levels and relationships”.

写下来的经验是知识管理，生命周期活动循环进行，DevOps 把开发和运维连起来，以共同目标、自动化和快速反馈协作。答错了？知识看“服务台与服务请求”；生命周期看“产品与服务生命周期”；DevOps 看“与其他框架配合”；发布管理看“变更与发布”；供应商看“服务水平与关系”。

Step 4 · Principles and requests第 4 步 · 原则与服务请求

Learn先学

Name the principle from the behaviour. The principles are: focus on value; start where you are; progress iteratively with feedback; collaborate and promote visibility; think and work holistically; keep it simple and practical; optimise and automate. Start where you are means look at what already works before building new. Collaborate and promote visibility means share work and information across teams so nobody repeats it or waits blind. A service request is a formal request for something small, routine and pre-agreed, such as a new keyboard, unlike an incident, which is an interruption. Missed one? Revisit “Guiding principles” or “Service desk and requests”.

从行为认出原则。原则共有：聚焦价值、从现状出发、小步迭代并取得反馈、协作并保持透明、整体思考和行动、保持简单实用、优化和自动化。从现状出发是先看已经行之有效的东西，再决定是否新建。协作并保持透明是跨团队分享工作和信息，避免重复劳动或盲目等待。服务请求是对小而常规、已有约定的事物的正式请求，比如换一个新键盘，不同于事件（事件是中断）。答错了？回看“指导原则”或“服务台与服务请求”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Governance evaluates, directs and monitors; management does the daily work.
• Green reports can hide red experience.
• A value stream map (a picture of the steps from request to value) shows waiting, not just work; people stay accountable for what AI drafts.
• Simplify before you automate; start where you are and promote visibility.
• A routine, low-risk ask is a service request, and every miss points to a lesson to revisit.

• 治理评估、指导、监督；管理负责日常工作。
• 绿色报告可能掩盖红色体验。
• 流程图显示等待，而不只是工作；AI 起草的内容仍由人负责。
• 先简化再自动化；从现状出发，并提升可视性。
• 例行、低风险的需求是服务请求；每次答错都指向一课可以回看。
