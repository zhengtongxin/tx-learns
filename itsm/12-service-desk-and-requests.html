Service desk and requests服务台与服务请求

Step 1 · The desk and requests第 1 步 · 服务台与请求

Learn先学

The service desk is the single point of contact between provider and users. It logs, routes and solves incidents and requests, and keeps users informed.

服务台是提供方与用户之间唯一的联络点。它记录、转交并解决事件和请求，并让用户知情。

A service request is a user asking for something to be provided: a new account, software, information. It is not a fault. Requests are usually low risk and follow a known routine.

服务请求是用户要求提供某样东西：新账号、软件、信息。它不是故障。请求通常风险低，并有固定流程。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Knowledge and configuration第 2 步 · 知识与配置

Learn先学

Knowledge management keeps useful know-how findable, so the desk and users can solve things faster. Configuration management keeps records of the pieces that make up a service and how they connect, so you can see what a change will touch.

知识管理让有用的经验容易被找到，使服务台和用户解决问题更快。配置管理记录构成服务的各个部分及其关联，让你看清一次变更会影响什么。

• 请求是要东西，事件是故障。
