Service desk and requests服务台与服务请求

The front door, plus knowledge and configuration · ~12 min · reading and practice第一接触点，以及知识和配置 · 约 12 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The desk and requests第 1 步 · 服务台与请求

Learn先学

The service desk is the single point of contact between provider and users. It logs, routes and solves incidents and requests, and keeps users informed.

服务台是提供方与用户之间唯一的联络点。它记录、转交并解决事件和请求，并让用户知情。

A service request is a user asking for something to be provided: a new account, software, information. It is not a fault. Requests are usually low risk and follow a known routine.

服务请求是用户要求提供某样东西：新账号、软件、信息。它不是故障。请求通常风险低，并有固定流程。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Knowledge and configuration第 2 步 · 知识与配置

Learn先学

Knowledge management keeps useful know-how findable, so the desk and users can solve things faster. Configuration management keeps records of the pieces that make up a service and how they connect, so you can see what a change will touch.

知识管理让有用的经验容易被找到，使服务台和用户解决问题更快。配置管理记录构成服务的各个部分及其关联，让你看清一次变更会影响什么。

Step 3 · One call, two outcomes第 3 步 · 一通电话，两种结果

Learn先学

A user calls: 'I cannot print, and I also need access to the shared folder.' The desk hears two things. The printer failure is an incident: restore it. The folder access is a service request: a standard, pre-agreed way to get something, usually with an approval and a known way of getting it done. Logging them separately keeps the numbers honest: reports then count faults and requests on their own, instead of mixing them.

用户来电：“我打不了印，还需要共享文件夹的权限。”服务台听到了两件事。打印机故障是事件：去恢复。文件夹权限是服务请求：一种标准的、事先约定好的获取方式，通常有审批和已知的办理方式。分开登记，度量才不会失真：报表这样才能把故障和请求分别统计，不会混在一起。

Good desks also use a knowledge base (written-down answers and how-to steps): if the answer was written down last time, the next caller gets it in one minute. And the first contact solving the issue, without passing it on, is a sign the desk is working well.

好的服务台还会用知识库：上次的答案若已写下来，下一位来电者一分钟就能得到。第一次联系就解决问题、不用转交，是服务台运转良好的标志。

Step 4 · Request routines and the configuration record第 4 步 · 请求流程与配置记录

Learn先学

Because requests repeat, each common one gets a request routine (also called a request model): the steps, who approves, who does the work and how long it should take. “New starter laptop” or “reset my access” are typical. A routine makes the result predictable, and many can be offered through a self-service portal, where users ask without calling. Users reach the desk by phone, chat, email or portal, and the desk should treat them alike.

请求会反复出现，所以每个常见请求都有一套请求流程（也叫请求模型）：步骤、谁审批、谁执行、应该多久完成。“新员工笔记本”“重置权限”就是典型例子。流程让结果可预期，很多请求还可以放在自助门户里，用户不用打电话就能提出。用户可以用电话、聊天、邮件或门户联系服务台，服务台应一视同仁。

The configuration record shows what a service depends on. Example: the booking site runs on two web servers (computers that serve the site to users) and one database (where its records are stored), and the database also feeds the invoicing tool. Before anyone changes the database, the record shows that invoicing will be touched too, so it is tested and its users are told.

配置记录显示一项服务依赖什么。例如：预订网站跑在两台网络服务器（向用户提供网站的计算机）和一个数据库（存放它的记录）上，而这个数据库同时供应开票工具。有人要改数据库之前，记录会显示开票也会受影响，于是先测试并通知它的用户。

Takeaways要点回顾

• The service desk is the single point of contact.
• A request asks for something; an incident is a fault.
• Knowledge and configuration records speed up work.
• Common requests follow a routine; the configuration record shows what a change touches.

• 服务台是唯一的联络点。
• 请求是要东西，事件是故障。
• 知识和配置记录让工作更快。
• 常见请求有固定流程；配置记录显示一次变更会影响什么。
