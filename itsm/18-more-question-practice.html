Ten more foundation-style questions, with the lesson to revisit for each miss · ~15 min · practice再来十道基础考试风格的题目，答错时知道该回看哪一课 · 约 15 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · Records, principles and roles第 1 步 · 记录、原则与角色

Learn先学

Know the terms. A record is the entry kept about one piece of work. Incidents, problems and changes are all kinds of record, each with its own record type. An incident is an unplanned interruption to a service; a problem is the underlying cause of one or more incidents. The sponsor authorises the budget, the customer defines the requirements and takes responsibility for the outcome, and the user actually uses the service. The iterative principle (“progress iteratively with feedback”) means working in small steps and using feedback after each; two other principles, “optimise and automate” and “think and work holistically”, are about improving the work and seeing the whole system. Match the situation to the term before you look at the options. Missed one? Revisit “Incidents and problems”, “Guiding principles” or “Value and co-creation”.

先弄清术语。记录是为一项工作留下的条目。事件、问题和变更都是记录，各有各的记录类型。事件是服务的计划外中断；问题是一个或多个事件的根本原因。发起人批准预算，客户定义需求并对结果负责，用户是实际使用服务的人。迭代原则（“迭代推进并获取反馈”）是小步前进，并在每一步之后利用反馈；另外两条原则，“优化并自动化”和“整体思考和工作”，讲的是改进工作和看到整个系统。先把情境对上术语，再看选项。答错了？回看“事件与问题”“指导原则”或“价值与共创”。

Step 2 · Dimensions, streams and improvement第 2 步 · 维度、价值流与改进

Learn先学

The four dimensions are organisations and people, information and technology, partners and suppliers, and value streams and processes; contracts with outsiders sit under partners and suppliers. A value stream is an end-to-end flow of work that creates value, and improvement starts from the vision: the first question of the continual improvement model is “What is the vision?”, and later questions ask where we are now, where we want to be, how we get there, and whether we got there (“Governance and improvement” has the full model). Missed one? Revisit “The four dimensions”, “Value streams” or “Governance and improvement”.

四个维度是组织与人员、信息与技术、合作伙伴与供应商、价值流与流程；与外部方的合同属于合作伙伴与供应商。价值流是创造价值的端到端工作流，改进从愿景开始：持续改进模型的第一问是“愿景是什么？”，之后问现状在哪、想去哪、怎么去、到了没有（完整模型见“治理与持续改进”）。答错了？回看“四个维度”“价值流”或“治理与持续改进”。

Step 3 · Change, value and service levels第 3 步 · 变更、价值与服务级别

Learn先学

Three change types: a standard change is low-risk and pre-approved, a normal change is assessed and authorised before it goes ahead, and an emergency change is urgent and handled fast. Value has two parts: utility is what the service does (fit for purpose), and warranty is how reliably it does it (fit for use); the output is simply what the service produces, so it is not a third part of value. If a service does its job but keeps failing, the weak part is warranty. A service level agreement records the agreed service levels, such as response times, between provider and customer. Missed one? Revisit “Change and release”, “Value and co-creation” or “Service levels and relationships”.

三种变更类型：标准变更风险低且预先批准，常规变更要先评估并授权才能进行，紧急变更很紧迫，需要快速处理。价值有两部分：效用是服务做什么（适合目的），保证是它做得多可靠（适合使用）；产出只是服务产生的东西，它不是价值的第三部分。服务能完成任务却常出故障时，弱的是保证。服务水平协议记录提供方与客户之间约定的服务水平，比如响应时间。答错了？回看“变更与发布”“价值与共创”或“服务水平与关系”。

Step 4 · Reading a question carefully第 4 步 · 仔细读题

Learn先学

Most misses come from reading, not knowledge. Use three habits. Find the ask: read the last sentence first, because it says what is being asked (a type, a record, a role). Strike the distractors: cross out options that name a different kind of thing, such as a role when the question asks for a record. Watch the qualifiers: words like “first”, “best” and “not” change the answer. Example: “Which is NOT one of the four dimensions?” asks for the odd one out, so three options belong to the set and the one that does not belong is the answer. Another example: “Which is NOT a fruit?” with the options apple, banana, carrot and mango has the answer carrot.

多数答错源于读题，而不是知识。用三个习惯。找出所问：先读最后一句，它说明在问什么（类型、记录或角色）。划掉干扰项：划掉属于另一类事物的选项，例如题目问记录，选项却是角色。留意限定词：“首先”“最佳”“不”这样的词会改变答案。例如“下列哪一项不是四个维度之一？”要找的是例外，所以三个选项属于这个集合，不属于的那一个才是答案。再比如“下列哪一项不是水果？”，选项是苹果、香蕉、胡萝卜和芒果，答案就是胡萝卜。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Match the situation to the definition before you look at options.
• The sponsor authorises budget; the customer sets requirements; the user uses the service.
• Value is utility plus warranty.
• A normal change is assessed and authorised, not pre-approved and not urgent.
• Read the ask and the qualifiers (first, best, not) before you choose.
• Every miss points to a lesson to revisit.

• 先把情境对上定义，再看选项。
• 发起人批准预算；客户定需求；用户使用服务。
• 价值是效用加保证。
• 常规变更要评估并授权，不是预先批准的，也不紧急。
• 选之前先读清所问和限定词（首先、最佳、不）。
• 每一次答错都指向一课可以回看。
