More question practice更多题目练习

Nine more foundation-style questions, with the lesson to revisit for each miss · ~15 min · practice再来九道基础考试风格的题目，答错时知道该回看哪一课 · 约 15 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · Records, principles and roles第 1 步 · 记录、原则与角色

Learn先学

Match the situation to the record, link small steps with feedback to the iterative principle, and remember the sponsor holds the budget. Missed one? Revisit “Incidents and problems”, “Guiding principles” or “Key terms”.

把情境对上记录，把带反馈的小步前进对上迭代原则，并记住预算在发起人手里。答错了？回看“事件与问题”“指导原则”或“关键术语”。

Step 2 · Dimensions, streams and improvement第 2 步 · 维度、价值流与改进

Learn先学

Contracts with outsiders sit under partners and suppliers, a value stream is an end-to-end flow of work, and improvement starts from the vision. Missed one? Revisit “The four dimensions”, “Value streams” or “Governance and improvement”.

与外部方的合同属于合作伙伴与供应商，价值流是端到端的工作流，改进从愿景开始。答错了？回看“四个维度”“价值流”或“治理与持续改进”。

Step 3 · Change, value and service levels第 3 步 · 变更、价值与服务级别

Learn先学

Assessed-then-authorised and not urgent means normal change, reliable delivery is warranty, and agreed response times go in a service level agreement. Missed one? Revisit “Change and release”, “Value and co-creation” or “Service levels and relationships”.

先评估再授权且不紧急是常规变更，可靠的交付是保证，约定的响应时间写进服务级别协议。答错了？回看“变更与发布”“价值与共创”或“服务水平与关系”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Match the situation to the definition before you look at options.
• The sponsor authorises budget; the customer sets requirements; the user uses the service.
• Value is utility plus warranty.
• A normal change is assessed and authorised, not pre-approved and not urgent.
• Every miss points to a lesson to revisit.

• 先把情境对上定义，再看选项。
• 发起人批准预算；客户定需求；用户使用服务。
• 价值是效用加保证。
• 常规变更要评估并授权，不是预先批准的，也不紧急。
• 每一次答错都指向一课可以回看。
