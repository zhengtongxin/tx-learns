Deployment and rollout部署与推出

Tell deployment approaches apart, set a stop rule for phased waves, and choose an approach by risk and reach · ~9 min · reading and practice分清各种部署方式，为分批推出设定叫停规则，并按风险和范围选择方式 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Deployment approaches第 1 步 · 部署方式

Learn先学

Deployment moves a new or changed component into the live environment, so people can start using it. It is not the same as release (which makes a service or feature available to users), though on small changes they happen together. Common approaches: big bang (everyone at once), phased (in waves), pilot (a small group first) and continuous (small changes, often, usually automated).

部署是把新的或变更的组成部分放入生产环境，让人们可以开始使用。它与发布（让服务或功能对用户可用）不同，但在小变更中两者常常同时发生。常见方式有：大爆炸式（所有人一次到位）、分阶段（分批推进）、试点（先让一小群人用）和持续式（小改动、高频率，通常自动化）。

Example: a new laptop image goes to the IT team first as a pilot, then to everyone else in waves.

例子：新的笔记本电脑镜像先给 IT 团队做试点，再分批推给其他所有人。

Step 2 · Waves and stop rules第 2 步 · 分批与叫停规则

Learn先学

Phasing limits the damage when something goes wrong. Say 500 laptops are updated in 5 waves of 500 ÷ 5 = 100. Agree a stop rule before you start: for example, stop if more than 10% of a wave has a problem. If wave 2 sees 12 laptops fail, then 12 ÷ 100 = 12%, which is over 10%, so you pause. Only 200 laptops have been touched, not 500, and you have a plan to roll back (return to the earlier version) for them.

分批能在出问题时限制损失。假设 500 台笔记本电脑分 5 批更新，每批 500 ÷ 5 = 100 台。开始之前先商定一条叫停规则：例如，某一批中有问题的超过 10% 就停止。如果第 2 批有 12 台失败，12 ÷ 100 = 12%，超过了 10%，所以暂停。受影响的只有 200 台，而不是 500 台，而且你有一份让它们回退（回到先前版本）的计划。

Step 3 · Choosing the approach第 3 步 · 选择方式

Learn先学

Choose by risk and reach. A change that is easy to undo and affects few people can go big bang. A change that touches many people, or is hard to undo, should be piloted and phased. Whichever you pick, decide the stop rule and the roll-back plan first, and tell users when it will happen.

按风险和范围来选择。容易撤销且影响人少的变更可以大爆炸式。涉及很多人或难以撤销的变更，应先试点再分批。不管选哪种，都要先定好叫停规则和回退计划，并告诉用户何时进行。

Sort each change by the safer way to deploy it.

按更稳妥的部署方式给每个变更分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Deployment moves a change into the live environment; release makes it available to users.
• Big bang, phased, pilot and continuous are the common approaches.
• 500 laptops in 5 waves is 100 a wave; 12 failures is 12%, over a 10% stop rule.
• Agree a stop rule and a roll-back plan before starting.
• Wide reach or hard to undo: pilot and phase; small and easy to undo: big bang is fine.

• 部署把变更放入生产环境；发布让用户可以使用它。
• 大爆炸式、分阶段、试点和持续式是常见方式。
• 500 台笔记本分 5 批每批 100 台；12 台失败是 12%，超过 10% 的叫停规则。
• 开始之前先定叫停规则和回退计划。
• 范围广或难撤销：先试点再分批；很小且易撤销：大爆炸式即可。
