Guiding principles指导原则

Seven habits that shape every decision · ~12 min · reading and practice影响每个决定的七个习惯 · 约 12 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The seven principles第 1 步 · 七条原则

Learn先学

Focus on value; start where you are (reuse what works); progress iteratively with feedback; collaborate and promote visibility; think and work holistically; keep it simple and practical; optimise and automate (only after simplifying).

聚焦价值；从现状出发（复用有效的东西）；小步迭代并取得反馈；协作并保持透明；整体思考和行动；保持简单实用；优化并自动化（先简化再自动化）。

They are guidance, not rules. Several can apply to one situation, and you choose which matter most.

它们是指引而不是规则。同一情境可能适用几条，你要判断哪条最重要。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Order matters第 2 步 · 顺序很重要

Learn先学

The last principle has a catch: automate only what is already simple. Automating a messy process makes the mess run faster.

最后一条原则有个前提：只自动化已经简单的东西。把混乱的流程自动化，只会让混乱跑得更快。

Step 3 · Picking the principle第 3 步 · 挑出对应的原则

Worked example例子

Exam-style cases name a situation and ask which principle applies. Match the key phrase: “we already have a process that mostly works” points to start where you are; “release everything in one big go” points to progress iteratively with feedback; “each team hides its work” points to collaborate and promote visibility; “this form has 30 fields, 20 unused” points to keep it simple and practical.

考试式的案例会描述一个情境，问用哪条原则。抓关键词：“已有一套基本能用的流程”对应从现状出发；“一次性全部上线”对应迭代推进并及时反馈；“各团队把工作藏起来”对应协作并提高透明度；“表单 30 个字段，20 个没人用”对应保持简单实用。

Watch for a trap: several principles can fit. Choose the one that answers the question’s problem most directly, and remember that automation comes last.

注意陷阱：好几条原则可能都沾边，选最直接回应题目问题的那一条；别忘了自动化永远排在最后。
