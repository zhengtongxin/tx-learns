Catalogue and assets目录与资产

Say what a service catalogue entry holds, count idle licences and what dropping them saves, and tell catalogue, asset and configuration questions apart · ~9 min · reading and practice说清服务目录条目包含什么，算出闲置许可证及去掉它们能节省多少，并分清目录、资产与配置的问题 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The service catalogue第 1 步 · 服务目录

Learn先学

A service catalogue lists what the provider offers, in words customers understand. Each entry says what the service is, who can ask for it, how to ask, when it is available and who owns it. The service portfolio is wider: it also holds services being planned and services being retired, so leaders can decide where to invest.

服务目录用客户看得懂的话列出提供方提供的服务。每个条目写明服务是什么、谁可以申请、怎么申请、什么时候可用以及由谁负责。服务组合范围更广：它还包括正在规划的服务和即将退役的服务，让管理者决定把投入放在哪里。

Example: “Laptop setup: new staff only, request through the portal, ready in 2 days, owned by the desk team.”

例子：“笔记本设置：仅限新员工，通过门户申请，2 天内就绪，由服务台团队负责。”

Step 2 · Managing assets第 2 步 · 管理资产

Learn先学

An asset is anything of value the organisation owns or pays for, such as a laptop or a software licence. Asset management follows each one through its life: bought, in use, repaired, retired. It matters most for money. Say you pay for 120 licences and 95 are in use. That is 95 ÷ 120 = about 79% used, and 120 − 95 = 25 are idle. If a licence costs S$80 a year, dropping the idle ones at renewal (when the yearly licence is due to be paid again) saves 25 × 80 = S$2,000.

资产是组织拥有或花钱使用的任何有价值的东西，例如笔记本电脑或软件许可证。资产管理跟踪每一项的整个生命周期：购买、使用、维修、退役。它对金钱最重要。假设你付费购买了 120 个许可证，其中 95 个在用。那就是 95 ÷ 120 ≈ 79% 在用，120 − 95 = 25 个闲置。如果一个许可证每年 S$80，续约时（年度许可证又要付费时）去掉闲置的就能节省 25 × 80 = S$2,000。

Step 3 · Catalogue, assets and configuration together第 3 步 · 目录、资产与配置的关系

Learn先学

They answer different questions. The catalogue asks what do we offer? Asset management asks what do we own, and what does it cost? Configuration records ask what is connected to what? A configuration item is one thing that must be managed to deliver a service, and a configuration record is the map of those items and their links. A laptop is an asset (it was bought and has a value), a configuration item (it links to its user and the services it supports) and part of a catalogue entry (the laptop setup service).

它们回答的是不同的问题。目录问的是我们提供什么？资产管理问的是我们拥有什么，花了多少钱？配置记录问的是什么与什么相连？配置项是为交付服务而必须管理的某一样东西，配置记录则是这些配置项及其关联的地图。一台笔记本电脑既是资产（购买过、有价值），也是配置项（与其使用者和支持的服务相连），还是目录条目（笔记本设置服务）的一部分。

Sort each question by the practice that answers it.

按能回答问题的实践给每个问题分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A catalogue lists what is offered, in customer words, with how and when.
• The portfolio also holds planned and retiring services.
• Asset management follows each asset from purchase to retirement.
• 120 licences, 95 used: 25 idle, and at S$80 that is S$2,000 a year.
• Catalogue: what do we offer? Assets: what do we own? Configuration: what is connected?

• 目录用客户的话列出提供的服务，以及怎么申请、何时可用。
• 服务组合还包括规划中和即将退役的服务。
• 资产管理跟踪每项资产从购买到退役。
• 120 个许可证用了 95 个：闲置 25 个，每个 S$80 就是每年 S$2,000。
