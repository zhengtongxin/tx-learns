Step 1 · The four dimensions第 1 步 · 四个维度

Learn先学

Every product or service should be looked at from four sides: organisations and people (roles, culture, skills), information and technology (data, tools, knowledge), partners and suppliers (other organisations you depend on) and value streams and processes (how work flows).

每个产品或服务都要从四个方面看：组织与人员（角色、文化、技能）、信息与技术（数据、工具、知识）、合作伙伴与供应商（你依赖的其他组织）、价值流与流程（工作如何流动）。

Ignoring one dimension is how good plans fail: a new tool (technology) that nobody was trained to use (people) will not deliver value.

忽略其中一个维度，好计划就会失败：新工具（技术）没人会用（人员），就带不来价值。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Outside factors第 2 步 · 外部因素

Learn先学

Outside factors affect all four dimensions: political, economic, social, technological, legal and environmental, often remembered as PESTLE. You cannot control them, but you must account for them.

外部因素会影响全部四个维度：政治、经济、社会、技术、法律和环境，常记作 PESTLE。你无法控制它们，但必须考虑它们。
