Governance and improvement治理与持续改进

Who directs the work and how it keeps getting better · ~10 min · reading and practice谁来指导工作，以及如何持续变好 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Governance第 1 步 · 治理

Learn先学

Governance is how an organisation is directed and controlled. The governing body evaluates what is going on, directs the plans and policies, and monitors the results.

治理是组织如何被指导和控制。治理机构评估现状，指导计划和政策，监督结果。

It sets direction. Doing the day-to-day work is management, not governance.

它设定方向。做日常工作的是管理，不是治理。

Step 2 · Continual improvement第 2 步 · 持续改进

Learn先学

Improvement is never a one-off. A simple model asks seven questions in order: what is the vision, where are we now, where do we want to be, how do we get there, take action, did we get there, and how do we keep going.

改进从来不是一次性的。一个简单的模型按顺序问七个问题：愿景是什么、现在在哪、想去哪、怎么去、行动、到了吗、如何保持势头。

Everyone takes part, not only a quality team. Improve in small steps and measure each one.

每个人都参与，不只是质量团队。小步改进，每一步都要衡量。

More practice再练一练

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Governance: evaluate, direct, monitor.
• Management does the day-to-day work.
• Improvement is continual and shared by everyone.

• 治理：评估、指导、监督。
• 管理做日常工作。
• 改进是持续的，由所有人共同参与。
