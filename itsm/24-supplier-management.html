Supplier management供应商管理

Explain why suppliers matter, fit supplier agreements to the promise made to customers, and review supplier performance · ~9 min · reading and practice说明供应商为何重要，让供应商协议与对客户的承诺相匹配，并回顾供应商的表现 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Why suppliers matter第 1 步 · 为什么供应商很重要

Learn先学

Few services are built and run by one organisation alone. Cloud platforms, software, networks and specialist support often come from suppliers. Supplier management makes sure those suppliers are chosen, agreed with and followed up so that what they provide supports the services the organisation promises its customers. The organisation stays accountable to its customers even when a supplier causes the problem.

很少有服务完全由一个组织自己建设和运行。云平台、软件、网络和专业支持往往来自供应商。供应商管理确保这些供应商被正确选择、签订协议并持续跟进，使它们提供的东西能支撑组织向客户承诺的服务。即使问题由供应商造成，组织对客户仍然负责。

Step 2 · Contracts that fit the promise第 2 步 · 与承诺相匹配的合同

Learn先学

A supplier’s agreement must fit the service level you promised your customer. Say you promise customers a fix within 8 hours. If the supplier’s part may take up to 6 hours, only 8 − 6 = 2 hours are left for everything you do yourself, which is probably too little. So the supplier’s target must be tighter than your own promise, for example 3 hours, which leaves 8 − 3 = 5 hours for your side. Agreements like this are often called underpinning agreements.

供应商的协议必须与你对客户承诺的服务水平相匹配。假设你向客户承诺 8 小时内修复。如果供应商那部分最多可能要 6 小时，你自己做的所有事情就只剩 8 − 6 = 2 小时，可能太少。所以供应商的目标必须比你自己的承诺更严，例如 3 小时，这样你这边还有 8 − 3 = 5 小时。这类协议常被称为支撑协议。

Step 3 · Watching performance第 3 步 · 关注表现

Learn先学

Review each supplier on a schedule against what was agreed. If a supplier delivered 18 of 20 requests on time, that is 18 ÷ 20 = 90%. If the agreed target is 95%, they missed it by 5 percentage points. Talk about it, find the cause and agree an improvement, rather than only counting. Treat the relationship as a partnership: suppliers who understand your goals usually help more. Also plan for the day a supplier fails or leaves.

按计划对每个供应商对照约定进行回顾。如果供应商按时完成了 20 个请求中的 18 个，就是 18 ÷ 20 = 90%。如果约定目标是 95%，就差了 5 个百分点。要沟通、找原因并商定改进，而不是只数数字。把关系当作伙伴关系：了解你目标的供应商通常帮助更大。也要为供应商失败或退出的那一天做好预案。

Sort each statement as true or false about supplier management.

把每个说法判断为关于供应商管理的“对”或“错”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Suppliers provide parts of services, but the organisation stays accountable to customers.
• A supplier’s target must be tighter than the promise to customers: 8 − 3 = 5 hours left.
• Review suppliers on a schedule against the agreement: 18 ÷ 20 = 90% against a 95% target.
• Talk about causes and improvements, not only numbers.
• Plan for a supplier that fails or leaves.

• 供应商提供服务的一部分，但组织对客户仍然负责。
• 供应商的目标必须比对客户的承诺更严：8 − 3 = 5 小时留给自己。
• 按计划对照协议回顾供应商：目标 95% 时，18 ÷ 20 = 90%。
• 要谈原因和改进，而不只是数字。
• 为供应商失败或退出做好预案。
