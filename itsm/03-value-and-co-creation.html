Value and co-creation价值与共创

What a service is and why value is made together · ~10 min · reading and practice什么是服务，为什么价值是共同创造的 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Service and value第 1 步 · 服务与价值

Learn先学

A service is a way of helping someone get the result they want without them carrying the specific costs and risks themselves. Think of a taxi: you get to the airport, and the driver deals with fuel, repairs and traffic.

服务是帮别人得到想要的结果，而不必自己承担具体的成本和风险。就像出租车：你到了机场，油费、修车和堵车由司机负责。

Value is the benefit, usefulness and importance of something as the person sees it. It is judged by the consumer (the one who uses the service), not the provider (the one who supplies it).

价值是某事物在人看来的好处、用处和重要性，由使用方（使用服务的人）判断，而不是提供方（提供服务的人）。

Step 2 · Co-creation and roles第 2 步 · 共创与角色

Learn先学

Value is co-created: provider and consumer both contribute. A help desk cannot fix what the user never reports, and the best system is worth little if nobody uses it well.

价值是共同创造的：提供方和使用方都要出力。用户不报问题，服务台就修不了；系统再好，没人好好用也没价值。

Three consumer roles: the customer defines requirements and owns the outcome, the user uses the service, and the sponsor authorises the budget. One person can hold all three.

使用方有三种角色：客户定义需求并对结果负责，用户使用服务，发起人批准预算。一个人可以同时担任三种。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A service enables outcomes without the consumer carrying specific costs and risks.
• Value is judged by the consumer.
• Value is co-created.
• Customer, user and sponsor are roles.

• 服务让使用方得到结果，而不必承担具体成本和风险。
• 价值由使用方判断。
• 价值是共创的。
• 客户、用户、发起人是角色。
