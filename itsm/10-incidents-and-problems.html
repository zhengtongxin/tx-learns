Incidents and problems事件与问题

Restore service first, remove the cause second · ~12 min · reading and practice先恢复服务，再消除根因 · 约 12 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Incident versus problem第 1 步 · 事件与问题

Learn先学

An incident is an unplanned interruption to a service or a drop in its quality. The goal is to restore normal service as fast as possible, even if the cause is unknown.

事件是服务的非计划中断或质量下降。目标是尽快恢复正常服务，哪怕还不知道原因。

A problem is a cause, or likely cause, of one or more incidents. Problem work looks for the root cause so the incidents stop coming back.

问题是一个或多个事件的原因或可能原因。问题管理寻找根因，让事件不再重复发生。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Workarounds and known errors第 2 步 · 临时办法与已知错误

Learn先学

A workaround is a way to reduce or avoid an incident’s impact without fixing the cause. A known error is a problem that has been analysed but not yet resolved, and it usually has a documented workaround.

临时解决办法是不修根因、而减少或避免事件影响的做法。已知错误是已分析但尚未解决的问题，通常有记录在案的临时办法。

Record known errors so the service desk can apply the workaround at once.

记录已知错误，服务台就能立即使用临时办法。

Step 3 · From incident to known error第 3 步 · 从事件到已知错误

Learn先学

Monday: the booking site slows down and 12 users call. The desk logs incidents, finds that restarting a service restores speed and applies it. Service is back in 10 minutes: that is the incident goal met. Tuesday and Wednesday: the same slowdown. Three similar incidents are a good reason to open a problem and look for the cause.

周一：预订网站变慢，12 位用户来电。服务台登记事件，发现重启某个服务可恢复速度并执行。10 分钟后服务恢复：事件目标达成。周二、周三：同样变慢。三次类似的事件，就有充分理由开立一个问题去找原因。

Analysis finds a memory leak (a program slowly using up the memory it was given) that needs a code fix due in three weeks. Until then the problem is a known error and the restart is its workaround, written where the desk can find it. The incident team never needs to rediscover it.

分析发现是内存泄漏（程序不断占用内存而不释放），需要三周后发布的代码修复。在此之前，这个问题是已知错误，重启就是它的临时办法，记录在服务台能找到的地方。事件处理的人就不必再去重新发现。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Incident: restore service fast.
• Problem: find and remove the cause.
• Known error: analysed, unresolved, has a workaround.

• 事件：快速恢复服务。
• 问题：找到并消除原因。
• 已知错误：已分析、未解决、有临时办法。
