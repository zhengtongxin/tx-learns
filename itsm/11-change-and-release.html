Change and release变更与发布

Make changes safely without slowing everything down · ~12 min · reading and practice安全地变更，又不拖慢一切 · 约 12 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three kinds of change第 1 步 · 三种变更

Learn先学

A change is adding, modifying or removing anything that could affect a service. Change enablement aims to make as many changes succeed as possible by assessing risk and authorising the right people.

变更是增加、修改或移除任何可能影响服务的东西。变更实施的目的是通过评估风险和授权合适的人，让尽可能多的变更成功。

Standard: low risk, well understood, pre-authorised. Normal: needs assessment and authorisation, with more senior approval for higher risk. Emergency: must be done fast to fix or prevent a serious problem.

标准变更：低风险、已被充分理解、预先授权。常规变更：需要评估和授权，风险越高越需要更高层批准。紧急变更：必须尽快完成以修复或预防严重问题。

Step 2 · Change is not release第 2 步 · 变更不等于发布

Learn先学

A release is a version of a service or component made available for use. Change enablement decides whether a change may go ahead; release management decides what goes live together and when.

发布是让服务或组件的某个版本可供使用。变更实施决定变更能否进行；发布管理决定哪些东西一起、何时上线。
