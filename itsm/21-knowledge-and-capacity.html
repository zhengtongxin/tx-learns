The value system · Lesson 8价值系统 · 第 8 课

Knowledge and capacity知识与容量

Keep knowledge articles fresh, count what self-service saves, and plan capacity ahead of demand · ~9 min · reading and practice让知识文章保持新鲜、算出自助服务节省了多少、并在需求之前规划容量 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Knowledge that gets used第 1 步 · 被用起来的知识

Learn先学

Knowledge management keeps what people learn in a form others can find and reuse. A good knowledge article is short, written for the person who will read it, and says when it was last checked. An article nobody reviews goes stale (out of date), and a wrong answer is worse than none.

知识管理把人们学到的东西保存成别人能找到、能重用的形式。好的知识文章简短，为读它的人而写，并注明上次核对的时间。没人复查的文章会过时（不再准确），而错误的答案比没有答案更糟。

Example: after solving a printer fault, the service desk writes a five-line fix. The next time it happens, anyone can use it, including the customer.

例子：解决一次打印机故障后，服务台写下五行的处理办法。下次再发生时，任何人都能用，包括客户本人。

Step 2 · Self-service and saved effort第 2 步 · 自助服务与节省的工作

Learn先学

Articles also power self-service (it depends on good articles, so it is counted here): customers solve simple things themselves, and the service desk keeps its time for harder ones. To see if it works, count it. Say 200 requests arrive in a month and customers now solve 40% of them from articles. That is 200 × 40 ÷ 100 = 80 requests the desk never handles. At an assumed 5 minutes each, the desk saves 80 × 5 = 400 minutes.

文章也支撑自助服务（它依赖好的文章，所以放在这里讲）：客户自己解决简单的事，服务台把时间留给更难的事。要知道有没有用，就数一数。假设一个月有 200 个请求，客户现在靠文章解决了其中 40%。那就是 200 × 40 ÷ 100 = 80 个请求，服务台根本不用处理。按假设每个 5 分钟，服务台就节省了 80 × 5 = 400 分钟。

Step 3 · Capacity and performance第 3 步 · 容量与性能

Learn先学

Capacity is how much work a service can do; performance is how well it does it, for example how many seconds a page takes to load. Demand grows, so capacity must be planned ahead. Say 4 servers each handle 250 users at the same time, so capacity is 4 × 250 = 1,000 users. Demand is 800 users, which uses 800 ÷ 1,000 = 80% of capacity. Headroom is capacity minus demand: 1,000 − 800 = 200 users. If demand grows by 50 users a month, that headroom lasts 200 ÷ 50 = 4 months, so the team must act before then.

容量是服务能做多少工作；性能是它做得多好，例如一个页面要几秒钟才能加载出来。需求会增长，所以必须提前规划容量。假设 4 台服务器各能同时服务 250 个用户，容量就是 4 × 250 = 1,000 个用户。需求是 800 个用户，占用了 800 ÷ 1,000 = 80% 的容量。余量是容量减去需求：1,000 − 800 = 200 个用户。如果需求每月增加 50 个用户，这些余量能撑 200 ÷ 50 = 4 个月，所以团队必须在那之前行动。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Knowledge management keeps what people learn findable and reusable.
• A stale article is worse than none: show a check date and an owner.
• Self-service saves effort; count it as requests × share solved.
• Capacity is how much a service can do; performance is how well.
• Headroom ÷ monthly growth is how long you have to act.

• 知识管理让人们学到的东西可以被找到、被重用。
• 过时的文章比没有更糟：注明核对日期和负责人。
• 自助服务节省工作；用请求数 × 解决比例来计算。
• 容量是服务能做多少；性能是做得多好。
• 余量 ÷ 每月增长量，就是你行动的期限。
