Mixed question drill综合题练习

Practise nine mixed foundation-style questions and name the lesson to revisit for each miss, then turn your score into a plan · ~15 min · practice练习九道混合的基础级考试风格题，答错时知道该回看哪一课，再把得分变成计划 · 约 15 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · Incidents, problems and changes第 1 步 · 事件、问题与变更

Learn先学

Match the situation to the definition. An incident is an unplanned interruption to a service, a problem is the cause behind incidents, and a known error is a problem with a documented workaround. A standard change is low-risk with a pre-approved procedure, a normal change is assessed and approved first, and an emergency change is urgent. So restoring service is an incident, finding the cause is a problem, and the risk of a change decides its type. Missed one? Revisit “Incidents and problems” or “Change and release”.

把情境对上定义。事件是服务的计划外中断，问题是事件背后的原因，已知错误是有记录临时办法的问题；标准变更低风险且有预先批准的流程，常规变更要先评估批准，紧急变更则十万火急。所以恢复服务是事件，找原因是问题，变更的风险决定它的类型。答错了？回看“事件与问题”或“变更与发布”。

Step 2 · Requests, dimensions and principles第 2 步 · 请求、维度与原则

Learn先学

Ask whether anything broke (incident) or something is simply wanted (request), and which of the four dimensions the gap sits in: organisations and people, information and technology, partners and suppliers, or value streams and processes. Missed one? Revisit “Service desk and requests”, “The four dimensions” or “Guiding principles”.

先问是不是有东西坏了（事件），还是单纯想要什么（请求），并看缺口落在四个维度中的哪一个：组织与人员、信息与技术、合作伙伴与供应商，还是价值流与流程。答错了？回看“服务台与服务请求”“四个维度”或“指导原则”。

Step 3 · Value and the service desk第 3 步 · 价值与服务台

Learn先学

Value is created together by provider and consumer, and the service desk is the front door. Missed one? Revisit “Value and co-creation” or “Service desk and requests”.

价值由提供方和消费方共同创造，服务台是前门。答错了？回看“价值与共创”或“服务台与服务请求”。

Step 4 · Turn your score into a plan第 4 步 · 把得分变成计划

Learn先学

A total score hides where you are weak. After the nine questions, write your result for each step as a fraction (Step 1 out of 4, Step 2 out of 3, Step 3 out of 2), turn it into a percentage (right ÷ asked × 100), and revisit the step with the lowest percentage first. Compare by rate, not by the number missed: one miss out of two questions is worse than one miss out of four.

总分会掩盖你弱在哪里。做完九道题后，把每一步的结果写成分数（第 1 步满分 4，第 2 步 3，第 3 步 2），换算成百分比（答对 ÷ 题数 × 100），先回看百分比最低的那一步。要比正确率，不要比错了几题：两题错一题比四题错一题更糟。

Step 1 3 of 4 right = 75% Step 2 3 of 3 right = 100% Step 3 1 of 2 right = 50% <- revisit first Total 7 of 9 right = 78%

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Match the situation to the definition before you look at options.
• Incident restores service; problem finds the cause; known error holds the workaround.
• Standard, normal and emergency changes differ by risk and urgency.
• Value is co-created by provider and consumer.
• Every miss points to a lesson to revisit; compare steps by percentage and start with the lowest.

• 先把情境对上定义，再看选项。
• 事件恢复服务；问题找原因；已知错误记着临时办法。
• 标准、常规、紧急变更按风险和紧迫程度区分。
• 价值由提供方和消费方共创。
• 每一次答错都指向一课可以回看；按百分比比较各步，先回看最低的。
