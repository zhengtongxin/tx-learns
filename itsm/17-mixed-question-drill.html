Mixed question drill综合题练习

Practise nine mixed foundation-style questions and name the lesson to revisit for each miss · ~15 min · practice练习九道混合的基础级考试风格题，答错时知道该回看哪一课 · 约 15 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · Incidents, problems and changes第 1 步 · 事件、问题与变更

Learn先学

Match the situation to the definition: restoring service is an incident, finding the cause is a problem, and the risk of a change decides its type. Missed one? Revisit “Incidents and problems” or “Change and release”.

把情境对上定义：恢复服务是事件，找原因是问题，变更的风险决定它的类型。答错了？回看“事件与问题”或“变更与发布”。

Step 2 · Requests, dimensions and principles第 2 步 · 请求、维度与原则

Learn先学

Ask whether anything broke (incident) or something is simply wanted (request), and which of the four dimensions the gap sits in. Missed one? Revisit “Service desk and requests”, “The four dimensions” or “Guiding principles”.

先问是不是有东西坏了（事件），还是单纯想要什么（请求），并看缺口落在四个维度中的哪一个。答错了？回看“服务台与服务请求”“四个维度”或“指导原则”。

Step 3 · Value and the service desk第 3 步 · 价值与服务台

Learn先学

Value is created together by provider and consumer, and the service desk is the front door. Missed one? Revisit “Value and co-creation” or “Service desk and requests”.

价值由提供方和消费方共同创造，服务台是前门。答错了？回看“价值与共创”或“服务台与服务请求”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Match the situation to the definition before you look at options.
• Incident restores service; problem finds the cause; known error holds the workaround.
• Standard, normal and emergency changes differ by risk and urgency.
• Value is co-created by provider and consumer.
• Every miss points to a lesson to revisit.

• 先把情境对上定义，再看选项。
• 事件恢复服务；问题找原因；已知错误记着临时办法。
• 标准、常规、紧急变更按风险和紧迫程度区分。
• 价值由提供方和消费方共创。
• 每一次答错都指向一课可以回看。
