Service levels and relationships服务水平与关系

Agreeing targets, watching events, managing suppliers · ~12 min · reading and practice约定目标、监控事件、管理供应商 · 约 12 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Service levels第 1 步 · 服务水平

Learn先学

Service level management sets clear, business-based targets and checks they are met. A service level agreement is a documented agreement between provider and customer that names the services and their targets.

服务水平管理设定清晰的、以业务为基础的目标，并检查是否达成。服务水平协议是提供方与客户之间的书面约定，写明服务及其目标。

Measure from the customer’s side. A watermelon report is green outside (every internal target met) and red inside (customers are unhappy).

要从客户的角度衡量。“西瓜”式报告是外面绿（内部指标全部达标）、里面红（客户不满意）。

Step 2 · Events and suppliers第 2 步 · 事件监控与供应商

Learn先学

Monitoring and event management watches for changes of state. An event can be informational, a warning or an exception; only some need action.

监控与事件管理关注状态变化。事件可以是信息性、警告或异常；只有部分需要处理。

Supplier management makes sure suppliers deliver what they promise. Relationship management builds links with stakeholders and understands their needs.

供应商管理确保供应商兑现承诺。关系管理与相关方建立联系并了解他们的需求。

Takeaways要点回顾

• An SLA names services and targets.

• Events: informational, warning, exception.
• Suppliers must deliver what they promised.

• SLA 写明服务及目标。
• 西瓜效应：外绿内红。
