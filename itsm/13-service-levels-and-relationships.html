The value system · Lesson 6价值系统 · 第 6 课

Service levels and relationships服务水平与关系

Agreeing targets, watching events, managing suppliers · ~12 min · reading and practice约定目标、监控事态、管理供应商 · 约 12 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Service levels第 1 步 · 服务水平

Learn先学

A service level is the target for how well a service performs. Service level management sets clear, business-based targets and checks they are met. A service level agreement is a documented agreement between provider and customer that names the services and their targets.

服务水平是对服务表现好坏的目标。服务水平管理设定清晰的、以业务为基础的目标，并检查是否达成。服务水平协议是提供方与客户之间的书面约定，写明服务及其目标。

Measure from the customer’s side. The watermelon effect is a report that looks green outside (every internal target met) and red inside (customers are unhappy).

要从客户的角度衡量。“西瓜”式报告是外面绿（内部指标全部达标）、里面红（客户不满意）。

Step 2 · Events and suppliers第 2 步 · 事态监控与供应商

Learn先学

Monitoring and event management watches for changes of state. An event can be informational (for the record), a warning (a risk before failure) or an exception (something has failed); only some need action.

监控与事态管理关注状态变化。事态可以是信息性（仅作记录）、警告（失败之前的风险）或异常（已经出了问题）；只有部分需要处理。

Supplier management makes sure suppliers deliver what they promise. Relationship management builds links with stakeholders (the customers, users and managers who care about the service) and understands their needs.

供应商管理确保供应商兑现承诺。关系管理与相关方（关心这项服务的客户、用户和管理者）建立联系并了解他们的需求。

Step 3 · Reading a service level第 3 步 · 读懂一条服务水平

Learn先学

A service level agreement says: 'Priority 1 incidents are answered within 15 minutes and resolved within 4 hours, measured monthly.' Of 20 such incidents, 18 met both targets. The result is 18 ÷ 20 = 90%. If the agreed level was 95%, the target was missed even though most incidents were fine.

一份服务水平协议写道：“一级事件 15 分钟内响应，4 小时内解决，按月度衡量。”20 个这样的事件中，18 个同时达标。结果是 18 ÷ 20 = 90%。若约定的水平是 95%，即使大多数事件处理得不错，目标仍然没有达成。

Read each level for three parts: what is measured, the target, and the period. Then ask whether it reflects what the customer actually cares about, not only what is easy to count.

读每条水平要看三部分：衡量什么、目标是多少、统计周期。再问问它是否反映了客户真正在意的，而不只是容易数的。

Step 4 · Turn a target into minutes第 4 步 · 把目标换算成分钟

Learn先学

Availability is the share of time the service works; downtime is the rest. Say the agreement promises 99.5% availability in a 30-day month. The month has 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200 minutes, so the allowed downtime is the part not promised: 100% − 99.5% = 0.5%, and 43,200 × 0.5 ÷ 100 = 216 minutes. If the service was down 180 minutes, it stayed within the target. If it was down 300 minutes, it went over by 300 − 216 = 84 minutes and the level was missed.

可用率是服务能正常工作的时间占比，停机时间就是其余部分。假设协议承诺 30 天的月份里可用率 99.5%。一个月有 30 × 24 × 60 = 43,200 分钟，所以允许的停机时间就是没有承诺的那部分：100% − 99.5% = 0.5%，43,200 × 0.5 ÷ 100 = 216 分钟。如果服务停了 180 分钟，仍在目标之内；如果停了 300 分钟，就超出 300 − 216 = 84 分钟，水平未达成。

Turning a percentage into minutes makes a target easy to check, and easy to explain to a customer.

把百分比换算成分钟，目标就容易核对，也容易向客户解释。

Takeaways要点回顾

• An SLA names services and targets.
• Watermelon effect: green outside, red inside.
• Events: informational, warning, exception.
• Suppliers must deliver what they promised.
• Turn a percentage into minutes to check it.

• SLA 写明服务及目标。
• 西瓜效应：外绿内红。
• 事态：信息性、警告、异常。
• 供应商必须兑现承诺。
• 把百分比换算成分钟再核对。
