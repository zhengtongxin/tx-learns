Products and services产品与服务

Offerings, relationships, utility and warranty · ~10 min · reading and practice服务内容、服务关系、效用和保障 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Product, offering, relationship第 1 步 · 产品、内容、关系

Learn先学

A product is a set of an organisation’s resources, designed to offer value to a consumer. A service offering is the formal description of what is on offer: goods, access to resources, or actions the provider performs.

产品是组织资源的一种组合，目的是给使用方带来价值。服务内容是对所提供内容的正式描述：商品、资源访问权限，或提供方执行的行动。

A service relationship has three parts: provision (the provider’s activities), consumption (the consumer’s activities) and relationship management (the joint work that keeps it healthy).

服务关系有三部分：提供（提供方的活动）、消费（使用方的活动）和关系管理（让关系保持健康的共同工作）。

Step 2 · Utility and warranty第 2 步 · 效用与保障

Learn先学

Utility is what the service does: it is “fit for purpose”. Warranty here does not mean a guarantee card. It is how dependable the service is: it is “fit for use”, covering availability, capacity, security and continuity.

效用是服务能做什么：“适合目的”。保障在这里不是保修卡，而是服务有多可靠：“适合使用”，包括可用性、容量、安全和持续性。

Both are needed. A tool with every feature that is down every afternoon has utility without warranty.

两者缺一不可。功能齐全却每天下午宕机的工具，有效用没保障。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Product or offering?第 3 步 · 是产品还是服务内容？

Worked example例子

Take a clinic’s online booking system in Singapore. The product is the system itself: servers, software, the booking team. The clinic then packages it into two service offerings: “book online for free” for patients and “booking API plus support” for partner pharmacies. One product, two offerings, each described to a different consumer group.

以新加坡一家诊所的线上预约系统为例。产品是系统本身：服务器、软件、运维团队。诊所把它包装成两个服务内容：给病人的“免费线上预约”，和给合作药房的“预约接口加支持”。一个产品，两个服务内容，各自面向不同的使用方。

A common slip: calling the offering “the thing we built”. What you built is the product. The offering is how you describe it to someone who might use it, including what they get and what they must do.

常见误区：把服务内容说成“我们做的那个东西”。做出来的是产品；服务内容是你对潜在使用方的说明，包括他们能得到什么、需要做什么。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A product is a set of resources designed to offer value.
• Provision, consumption, relationship management.
• Utility = fit for purpose.
• Warranty = fit for use.

• 产品是为提供价值而设计的资源组合。
• 提供、消费、关系管理。
• 效用＝适合目的。
• 保障＝适合使用。
