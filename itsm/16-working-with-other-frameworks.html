Working with other frameworks与其他框架配合

How service management fits DevOps and project methods · ~8 min · reading and practice服务管理如何与 DevOps 和项目方法配合 · 约 8 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · DevOps and project methods第 1 步 · DevOps 与项目方法

Learn先学

DevOps joins development and operations through shared goals, automation and quick feedback, using continuous integration (merge and test every change automatically) and continuous delivery (keep every tested change ready to release) to ship small changes often. Site reliability engineering treats reliability as an engineering job with measurable targets.

DevOps通过共同目标、自动化和快速反馈，把开发和运维连在一起，用持续集成（每个改动都自动合并并测试）和持续交付（让每个测过的改动随时可以发布）频繁发布小改动。站点可靠性工程把可靠性当作有可衡量目标的工程工作。

A project method such as PRINCE2 organises one-off work with a start, an end and a defined result, often in stages with an approval before each next stage. Service management keeps the value flowing after the project ends.

项目方法（如 PRINCE2）组织有开始、有结束、有明确成果的一次性工作，常分成阶段，进入下一阶段前先要批准。服务管理则在项目结束后让价值持续流动。

Step 2 · A project hands over第 2 步 · 项目移交

Learn先学

A team runs a project to launch a booking service. The project ends on launch day. Without a handover, nobody owns incidents on day one. With one, the service owner accepts the service, support has runbooks (the steps to follow) and known errors (faults whose cause is understood, with a workaround), and improvement continues in service management. The project delivers the change, service management keeps the value flowing.

一个团队开展项目来上线预订服务。项目在上线当天结束。没有移交，上线第一天就没人负责事件。有了移交，服务负责人接手服务，支持团队拿到操作手册（照着做的步骤）和已知错误（原因已弄清、带有临时办法的故障），改进在服务管理中继续。项目交付变更，服务管理让价值持续流动。

DevOps shortens the loop in the middle (release, test, feedback): small releases, automated tests and quick feedback mean problems show up earlier. None of these replace the others; they cover different parts of the same journey.

DevOps 缩短了中间的循环（发布、测试、反馈）：小步发布、自动化测试和快速反馈，让问题更早暴露。它们不是互相替代，而是覆盖同一旅程的不同部分。

Step 3 · Check the handover with a list第 3 步 · 用清单检查移交

Learn先学

Before a new service goes live, check the handover against a short list: a named service owner, a runbook (the steps support follows), known errors with workarounds, a contact list, monitoring (a way to see that it is working) and trained support staff. Count what is ready. If the owner is missing, the service should not go live yet, however many other items are ready.

新服务上线前，对照一张短清单检查移交：明确的服务负责人、操作手册（支持团队照着做的步骤）、带临时办法的已知错误、联系人名单、监控（能看出它在正常工作的办法），以及受过培训的支持人员。数一数哪些已就绪。如果缺了负责人，不管其他项多齐，服务都还不该上线。

Handover list: 6 items Ready: runbook, known errors, contact list, monitoring = 4 of 6 4 / 6 is about 67% Missing: owner, trained staff Decision: hold the go-live until the owner and trained staff are in place
