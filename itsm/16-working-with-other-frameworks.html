Step 1 · DevOps and project methods第 1 步 · DevOps 与项目方法

Learn先学

DevOps joins development and operations through shared goals, automation and quick feedback, using continuous integration and delivery to ship small changes often. Site reliability engineering treats reliability as an engineering job with measurable targets.

DevOps通过共同目标、自动化和快速反馈，把开发和运维连在一起，用持续集成和持续交付频繁发布小改动。站点可靠性工程把可靠性当作有可衡量目标的工程工作。

A project method such as PRINCE2 organises one-off work with a start, an end and a defined result. Service management keeps the value flowing after the project ends.

项目方法（如 PRINCE2）组织有开始、有结束、有明确成果的一次性工作。服务管理则在项目结束后让价值持续流动。
