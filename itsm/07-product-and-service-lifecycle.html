Product and service lifecycle产品与服务生命周期

The activities a service goes through, in order of thinking · ~10 min · reading and practice服务经历的各项活动 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The activities第 1 步 · 各项活动

Learn先学

The newer edition describes eight activities: discover (find needs and ideas), design, acquire (buy or license), build, transition (move into use), operate, deliver and support.

新版本描述了八项活动：发现（找到需求和想法）、设计、获取（购买或授权）、构建、过渡（投入使用）、运营、交付和支持。

They are not a straight line. Feedback from support can send you back to discover or design. The older edition covered similar ground in six value chain activities: plan, improve, engage, design and transition, obtain or build, and deliver and support. Check which one your syllabus uses.

它们不是一条直线。来自支持的反馈可以把你送回发现或设计。旧版本用六个价值链活动覆盖类似内容：计划、改进、参与、设计与过渡、获取或构建、交付与支持。请核对你的大纲用哪一种。

Discover find needs and ideas Design shape the solution Acquire buy or license parts Build make and test it Transition move it into live use Operate keep it running Deliver provide it to consumers Support help users and learn

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Iterating第 2 步 · 迭代

Learn先学

Because feedback keeps arriving, the lifecycle runs in loops. A change after launch starts a small discover and design step again.

因为反馈不断到来，生命周期是循环运转的。上线后的一次改动，会重新开始一轮小小的发现和设计。
