AI in service management服务管理中的 AI

Where AI helps and where people stay accountable · ~8 min · reading and practiceAI 有哪些用处，以及谁仍要负责 · 约 8 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Kinds of AI第 1 步 · 几种 AI

Learn先学

AI is software that does tasks that normally need human judgement. Generative AI produces new text, images or code. Agentic AI pursues a goal and takes actions with some autonomy.

AI是能完成通常需要人类判断的任务的软件。生成式 AI生成新的文字、图片或代码。代理式 AI追求某个目标，并能在一定程度上自主采取行动。

Classifying puts things into groups. It is AI, but not generative. Service uses: sorting tickets, summarising a long case, suggesting knowledge articles, drafting replies. These kinds can overlap: ask which one does the main work (drafting a reply is mainly generative).

分类是把事物归入不同的组，它是 AI，但不是生成式。在服务工作中的用途：给工单分类、总结冗长的案例、推荐知识文章、起草回复。这几类可以重叠：问一问哪一种起主要作用（起草回复主要是生成式）。

Step 2 · Governing it第 2 步 · 治理 AI

Learn先学

AI does not remove accountability. People stay accountable for its results, so you need clear ownership, human oversight for risky actions, good data and a record of what it did. The official foundation outline (see “The exam shape”) also names a capability model for AI (a checklist of what an organisation needs in place to use AI well); check its exact wording in the outline before the exam.

AI 不会免除责任。人仍要对其结果负责，所以需要明确的负责人、对高风险动作的人工监督、良好的数据，以及对其行为的记录。官方基础大纲（见“考试的样子”）还提到一个 AI 能力模型（一份清单，列出组织要用好 AI 需要具备什么）；考前请在大纲里核对它的确切说法。

Step 3 · AI on the service desk第 3 步 · 服务台上的 AI

Learn先学

A desk adds an assistant that suggests replies. It helps on routine, well-documented questions and saves minutes. It must not decide access to sensitive data or close a complaint alone. A person checks anything with real consequences, and the team keeps a record of what the assistant suggested so mistakes can be traced.

服务台加入一个提供回复建议的助手。它对常规、有文档的问题很有帮助，能节省时间。但不能由它单独决定敏感数据的访问，或关闭投诉。有实际后果的事项由人来检查，团队还要记录助手给过什么建议，以便追溯错误。

The test for any AI use is the same as for any change to a service (a deliberate alteration that could affect it; see “Change and release”): what value does it create, what risk does it add, and who is accountable? AI changes how work is done, not who is responsible for it.

任何 AI 用法的检验标准，与对服务做任何变更（可能影响服务的有意改动，见“变更与发布”）相同：创造什么价值，增加什么风险，谁来负责？AI 改变的是工作方式，不是责任归属。

Step 4 · Start small and measure第 4 步 · 从小处开始并度量

Learn先学

Rate each possible AI use by the cost of a mistake. Sorting tickets: a wrong category is easy to spot and fix, so risk is low. Summarising a long case: a missed detail can mislead, so a person reads the summary before relying on it. Approving access to sensitive data: a mistake is a security incident, so a person decides. Start with the low-risk use as a pilot (a small, time-limited trial), not with the whole desk.

按出错的代价给每种可能的 AI 用法评级。给工单分类：分错了容易发现和纠正，风险低。总结冗长的案例：漏掉的细节可能误导人，所以先由人读过摘要再采信。批准敏感数据的访问：出错就是安全事件，所以由人来决定。先拿低风险的用法做试点（小范围、有时限的试用），而不是一下子铺到整个服务台。

Then measure. In a two-week pilot the assistant sorts 200 tickets and a person finds 10 in the wrong category: 190 ÷ 200 = 95% correct, 5% wrong. Whether that is good enough depends on the cost of the 5%, so the team agrees the acceptable error rate and fix time before the pilot starts, for each use separately. For example, if a wrong ticket is moved to the right team within an hour, keep going (the one-hour limit is only an example; set your own). Both checks must pass: the error rate is acceptable and wrong tickets are fixed in time. If not, fix the instructions you gave the assistant, or stop. Before any action that changes something (a password reset, say), a person approves it.

然后度量。在为期两周的试点里，助手给 200 张工单分类，人发现有 10 张分错：190 ÷ 200 = 95% 正确，5% 错误。够不够好，取决于这 5% 的代价，所以团队要在试点开始前，为每一种用法分别商定可接受的错误率和纠正时间。例如，如果分错的工单能在一小时内被转到正确的团队，就继续（一小时只是示例，请定你自己的标准）。两项检查都要通过：错误率可以接受，且错分的工单能及时纠正。否则，改进你给助手的指令，或停止。任何会改变现状的操作（比如重置密码），都先由人批准。

Takeaways要点回顾

• AI, generative AI and agentic AI differ.
• Good uses: sort, summarise, suggest, draft.
• People stay accountable.
• Pilot the low-risk use first and measure the errors.

• AI、生成式 AI、代理式 AI 各不相同。
• 适合的用途：分类、总结、推荐、起草。
• 人仍要负责。
• 先试点低风险用法，并度量错误。
