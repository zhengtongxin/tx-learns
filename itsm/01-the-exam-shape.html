Step 1 · Format and pass mark第 1 步 · 题型和及格线

Learn先学

The foundation exam is 40 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, closed book. Each question is one mark and you need 26 of 40 (65%) to pass. It tests recall and understanding, not long scenarios.

基础级考试是 40 道选择题，限时 60 分钟，闭卷。每题 1 分，40 题中答对 26 题（65%）即通过。它考记忆和理解，不考很长的情境。

These figures come from training providers, not from the exam body. Confirm them on the official syllabus before you book.

这些数字来自培训机构，而不是考试方。报名前请对照官方大纲确认。

Step 2 · Two editions, one core第 2 步 · 两个版本，同一个核心

Learn先学

Version 4 of the framework is being succeeded by version 5, and the foundation syllabus for version 5 is dated February 2026. Training providers report that the older exam is being retired at the end of 2027.

框架的第 4 版正被第 5 版接替，第 5 版基础级大纲的日期是 2026 年 2 月。培训机构称旧版考试将在 2027 年底停用。

Most ideas are shared: value, outcomes, the four dimensions, guiding principles and the main practices. This track teaches the shared core and flags what version 5 adds. Its weights are the version 5 ones as reported by providers, and I have not checked them against the official file.

大部分概念是通用的：价值、成果、四个维度、指导原则和主要实践。本路线讲通用核心，并标出第 5 版新增的内容。占比采用培训机构报告的第 5 版数字，没有对照官方文件核实。
