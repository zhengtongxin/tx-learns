Step 1 · Format and pass mark第 1 步 · 题型和及格线

Learn先学

The foundation exam, the entry-level exam in this field, is 40 multiple-choice questions in 60 minutes, closed book. Each question is one mark and you need 26 of 40 (65%) to pass. It tests recall and understanding, not long scenarios.

基础级考试是该领域的入门级考试，共 40 道选择题，限时 60 分钟，闭卷。每题 1 分，40 题中答对 26 题（65%）即通过。它考记忆和理解，不考很长的情境。

These figures come from training providers, not from the exam body (the organisation that sets and runs the exam). Confirm them on the official syllabus before you book.

这些数字来自培训机构，而不是考试方（负责制定和举办考试的机构）。报名前请对照官方大纲确认。

Key terms 30% · 12 marks (what a service, value, outcome are) Four dimensions 10% · 4 (people, technology, partners, and the flow of work) Lifecycle 10% · 4 (the activities a service goes through) Value system 40% · 16 (principles, governance, practices) Value streams 5% · 2 (drawing the steps that turn a request into value) AI 2.5% · 1 Other frameworks 2.5% · 1

Step 2 · Two editions, one core第 2 步 · 两个版本，同一个核心

Learn先学

IT service management (ITSM) is the practice of designing, running and improving the IT services people rely on. The best-known framework for it, ITIL (a widely used set of good practices, called a framework, for running IT services), has a version 4 and a newer version 5, and the version 5 foundation syllabus is dated February 2026. Training providers report that the older exam is being retired at the end of 2027.

IT 服务管理（ITSM）是设计、运行和改进人们所依赖的 IT 服务的做法。最知名的框架 ITIL（一套被广泛使用的 IT 服务管理好做法，称为框架）有第 4 版和较新的第 5 版，第 5 版基础级大纲的日期是 2026 年 2 月。培训机构称旧版考试将在 2027 年底停用。

Most ideas are shared: value, outcomes, the four dimensions, guiding principles and the main practices. This track teaches the shared core and flags what version 5 adds: a lifecycle of eight activities, value stream mapping and a section on AI. The weights here are the version 5 ones as reported by providers, and they have not been checked against the official file.

大部分概念是通用的：价值、成果、四个维度、指导原则和主要实践。本路线讲通用核心，并标出第 5 版新增的内容：由八项活动组成的生命周期、价值流图和关于 AI 的部分。这里的占比采用培训机构报告的第 5 版数字，尚未对照官方文件核实。

Step 3 · Share study time by marks第 3 步 · 按分值分配学习时间

Learn先学

Marks tell you where study time pays off. Take the marks of one section, divide by the 40 in total, and give it that share of your hours. With 10 hours, the value system (16 of 40 marks) gets 10 × 16 ÷ 40 = 4 hours, and the AI section (1 mark) gets 15 minutes. Treat the result as a first draft: add time to any section where your practice scores are weak.

分值告诉你时间花在哪里最划算。用某一部分的分数除以总共的 40 分，就是它应得的学习时间比例。若有 10 小时，价值系统（40 分中的 16 分）得到 10 × 16 ÷ 40 = 4 小时，AI 部分（1 分）得到 15 分钟。把结果当作初稿：哪一部分练习得分低，就再给它加时间。

10 hours, shared by marks (out of 40) Key terms 12 marks -> 3 hours Four dimensions 4 marks -> 1 hour Lifecycle 4 marks -> 1 hour Value system 16 marks -> 4 hours Value streams 2 marks -> 30 minutes AI 1 mark -> 15 minutes Other frameworks 1 mark -> 15 minutes Total: 3 + 1 + 1 + 4 + 0.5 + 0.25 + 0.25 = 10 hours

More practice再练一练

Sort each one: a section is big if it carries over 25% of the marks and small if it carries under 15%.

给每一项分类：占分超过 25% 的部分算大块，低于 15% 的算小块。
