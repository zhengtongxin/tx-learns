Question tactics答题方法

Read the four question types and choose faster · ~8 min · reading and practice认识四种题型，选得更快 · 约 8 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Four question types第 1 步 · 四种题型

Learn先学

Standard: four options, one right. Missing word: pick the word that completes a sentence. List: four statements, pick the two that are true. Negative: pick the option that is not true. Negative questions are rare, but read the stem (the question text) twice.

标准题：四个选项选一个。填空题：选出补全句子的词。列表题：四句话选出两句正确的。否定题：选出不正确的那项。否定题很少，但要把题干（题目的文字部分）读两遍。

There is no negative marking, so never leave a question blank: rule out the options you know are wrong, then guess among the rest, because a guess cannot cost you marks.

答错不扣分，所以不要留空：先排除明显错误的选项，再从剩下的里面猜，因为猜错不会扣分。

Step 2 · Choose faster第 2 步 · 选得更快

Learn先学

Exam answers are mostly definitions and plain ideas. Options with words like “always”, “never” or “only” are usually wrong, because real practice has exceptions.

考题多是定义和朴素的道理。带“总是”“从不”“只有”的选项通常是错的，因为实际做法总有例外。

With 40 questions in 60 minutes you have about 90 seconds each. Answer the sure ones first, flag the rest (or note their numbers on paper) and come back.

60 分钟 40 题，平均每题约 90 秒。先答有把握的，标记其余的（或在纸上记下题号）再回来。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Standard, missing word, list and negative.
• Never leave a blank.
• Absolute words are a warning.
• About 90 seconds a question.

• 标准、填空、列表、否定。
• 不要留空。
• 绝对化用词是警示。
• 每题约 90 秒。
