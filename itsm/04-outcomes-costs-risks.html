Outcomes, costs and risks成果、成本与风险

Four terms that most questions lean on · ~10 min · reading and practice大部分题目都会用到的四个术语 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Output and outcome第 1 步 · 产出与成果

Learn先学

An output is something an activity produces: a report, a fixed laptop, a closed ticket. An outcome is the result for a stakeholder (anyone with an interest in it) that outputs help to bring about: the employee can work again.

产出是活动生产出来的东西：一份报告、一台修好的电脑、一张关闭的工单。成果是产出带来的、对相关方（对结果有利害关系的人）的结果：员工又能工作了。

A team can hit every output target and still miss the outcome. Exams love this contrast.

团队可能完成了所有产出指标，却没达成成果。考试很喜欢考这个对比。

Step 2 · Cost and risk第 2 步 · 成本与风险

Learn先学

Cost is the money spent on a specific activity or resource. A service removes some costs from the consumer, but the consumer still pays for the service itself.

成本是花在某项活动或资源上的钱。服务替使用方承担了一些成本，但使用方仍要为服务本身付费。

Risk is a possible event that could cause harm or loss, or make goals harder to reach. Providers take on some risks for the consumer, such as hardware failure, and the consumer keeps others.

风险是可能造成伤害或损失、或让目标更难实现的事件。提供方替使用方承担部分风险（如硬件故障），使用方保留另一些。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Output: what is produced.
• Outcome: the result for the stakeholder.
• Cost: money spent on activities or resources.
• Risk: an event that could cause harm or loss.

• 产出：生产出来的东西。
• 成果：对相关方的结果。
• 成本：花在活动或资源上的钱。
• 风险：可能造成伤害或损失的事件。
