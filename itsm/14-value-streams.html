Value streams价值流

Map the steps from request to value and find the waiting · ~10 min · reading and practice画出从请求到价值的步骤，找出等待 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Mapping第 1 步 · 画价值流图

Learn先学

A value stream is the series of steps an organisation takes to create and deliver a product or service. Mapping shows each step, who does it, and how long work waits between steps.

价值流是组织创造和交付产品或服务所经过的一系列步骤。画图能显示每一步、谁来做，以及工作在步骤之间等待多久。

The newer edition also separates core streams, which serve consumers directly, from enabling ones, which support them.

新版本还区分直接服务使用方的核心价值流，和为其提供支撑的支撑价值流。

Step 2 · Where the time goes第 2 步 · 时间花在哪里

Worked example例子

Use the map above. Work time is 5 + 2 + 20 = 27 minutes. Waiting is 4 hours + 2 days + 1 hour, about 53 hours. So the request takes roughly 53.5 hours and only 27 minutes of that is anyone working: about 1%. Making the setup step twice as fast saves 10 minutes. Removing the two-day wait for approval saves two days.

用上面的图算：工作时间是 5 + 2 + 20 = 27 分钟；等待是 4 小时 + 2 天 + 1 小时，约 53 小时。整个请求大约 53.5 小时，真正有人在做事的只有 27 分钟，约 1%。把“安装”那一步提速一倍，只省 10 分钟；去掉审批的两天等待，省两天。

Lesson: improve the longest wait first, not the busiest step. Typical fixes are pre-approving routine requests, batching approvals into set times, or removing a step nobody needs.

结论：先改最长的等待，不是最忙的步骤。常见做法：对常规请求预先批准、把审批集中到固定时段、删掉没人需要的步骤。

Step 3 · Another map to read第 3 步 · 再读一张图

Learn先学

A refund request goes through three steps. Log it (3 minutes), check it (10 minutes), approve it (5 minutes). Work time is 3 + 10 + 5 = 18 minutes. But it waits 1 day before the check and 3 hours before approval: 24 + 3 = 27 hours of waiting. The refund takes about 27 hours 18 minutes, and only 18 minutes of that is anyone working.

一个退款请求要经过三步。登记（3 分钟）、核查（10 分钟）、批准（5 分钟）。工作时间是 3 + 10 + 5 = 18 分钟。但它在核查前等了 1 天，批准前等了 3 小时：24 + 3 = 27 小时的等待。退款总共约 27 小时 18 分钟，其中真正有人在干活的只有 18 分钟。

Making the check 2 minutes faster saves almost nothing. Removing or shortening the 1-day wait before the check saves the most, for example by checking routine refunds the same morning.

把核查提速 2 分钟几乎没有收益。取消或缩短核查前的 1 天等待收益最大，例如当天上午就核查常规退款。
