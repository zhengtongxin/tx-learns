Step 1 · Brief it like a colleague第 1 步 · 像对同事一样交代

Learn先学

An assistant can only work with what you paste. “It does not work” gets a generic answer. Give it what you gathered in this track: the exact error (the full last lines), the smallest code that shows it, what you expected, what you got and what you already tried. A minute spent writing that is usually a better answer, and sometimes you solve it while writing.

助手只能用你贴给它的东西。“它不能用”只会得到泛泛的回答。把你在本路线里收集的东西给它：确切的报错（完整的最后几行）、能显示问题的最小代码、你预期什么、得到什么，以及你已经试过什么。花一分钟写这些，通常换来更好的回答，有时写着写着你自己就解决了。

Step 2 · A suggestion is a guess第 2 步 · 建议只是猜测

Learn先学

A suggested fix sounds confident whether it is right or not. Treat it as a guess in the same way as your own: ask what it predicts, then test that. Run your reproduction before and after. If the assistant changes five things at once, ask for one change at a time, so you can tell which one mattered. Watch for fixes that hide the error instead of removing the cause.

建议的修复无论对错，听起来都很有把握。要像对待你自己的猜测一样对待它：问它预测了什么，然后去验证。在修改前后各运行一次你的复现。如果助手一次改了五样，就让它一次只改一处，这样你才知道哪一处起了作用。留意那些只是掩盖错误而不是消除原因的修复。

Step 3 · Keep the understanding第 3 步 · 把理解留在自己这里

Learn先学

The goal is not a green test but knowing why the bug happened. After a fix, say the cause in one sentence in your own words. If you cannot, ask the assistant to explain it, and then check that explanation against the code. Add the test, note the cause in the commit message (the short note you write when you save a change) and move on. Next time, you will recognise the family of problem without help. Sort each habit.

目标不是让测试变绿，而是弄清问题为什么发生。修好之后，用自己的话用一句话说出原因。如果说不出，就让助手解释，再拿解释去对照代码。加上测试，在提交信息（保存一次改动时写的那条简短说明）里写下原因，然后继续。下一次，你自己就能认出这类问题。给每个习惯分类。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Give the assistant the error, the smallest code, expected, got and tried.
• Treat a suggested fix as a guess and test it.
• Keep the understanding: say the cause yourself.

• 给助手报错、最小代码、预期、实际和已试过的。
• 把建议的修复当作猜测，并去验证。
• 把理解留给自己：亲口说出原因。
