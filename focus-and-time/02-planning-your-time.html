Planning your time规划时间

Estimate how long things really take, add a buffer, and make a plan that survives surprises · ~10 min · reading and practice估算事情真正要多久，留出缓冲，做出经得起意外的计划 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · We guess too short第 1 步 · 我们总估得太短

Learn先学

People tend to guess how long a task will take by picturing everything going well. Real work includes waiting, mistakes, interruptions and rework, so it usually takes longer. The fix is not to try harder to guess; it is to look at how long similar tasks really took last time, and use that instead of the hopeful picture. If you have no record yet, time this task as you go and use that number next time.

人们估算时间，往往想象一切顺利。真实的工作里有等待、出错、打断和返工，所以通常更久。解决办法不是更用力地猜，而是看看上次类似的任务实际花了多久，用它代替那个乐观的画面。如果还没有记录，就在做这项任务时计时，下次用这个数字。

Step 2 · Buffer and breaks第 2 步 · 缓冲与间隔

Learn先学

Add a buffer to each task’s estimate, for example a 2-hour guess becomes 3 hours (start from the real past time if you have one, and add the buffer on top), and leave gaps between tasks for moving and thinking. A plan with no slack breaks at the first delay and drags everything after it. Split big tasks into pieces of an hour or two, since small pieces are easier to estimate honestly.

给每项任务的估算加上缓冲，比如估 2 小时就按 3 小时算（有真实的过去用时就以它为起点，再在上面加缓冲），并在任务之间留出转换和思考的空隙。没有余地的计划，第一次延误就会垮，还拖累后面所有事。把大任务拆成一两个小时的小块，小块更容易老实估算。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Plan less than fits第 3 步 · 少排一点

Learn先学

A day packed to the edge has no room for the unplanned, and some of it always comes. The buffer covers each task; a free third of the day covers surprises across the whole day. For example, in an 8-hour day: two tasks of 3 hours each (2 hours of work plus buffer) fill 6 hours, and the last 2 hours stay free. So fill about two thirds of your time with planned work and keep the rest free. If you finish early you gain time; if not, the plan still holds. After each plan, note the real time taken, so next time your guess has data behind it.

排得满满的一天，容不下计划外的事，而计划外的事总会来。缓冲管每一项任务，留空的三分之一管一整天里的意外。比如 8 小时的一天：两项各排 3 小时（2 小时的活加缓冲）共 6 小时，最后 2 小时留空。所以只用大约三分之二的时间排计划，其余留空。提前完成就是赚到；没提前完成，计划也不会垮。每次做完记下实际用时，下次的估算就有了依据。
