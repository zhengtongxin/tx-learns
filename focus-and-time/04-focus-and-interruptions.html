Focus and interruptions专注与打断

Do deep work in protected blocks, cut the interruptions you control and recover after the ones you cannot · ~10 min · reading and practice在受保护的时间块里做需要深度思考的事，减少你能控制的打断，并在无法避免的打断后恢复状态 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Switching is costly第 1 步 · 切换有代价

Learn先学

Each interruption costs more than the seconds it takes: you must find your place again and rebuild your train of thought. Several small checks of a phone can wreck an hour of thinking. Work that needs real thought, such as writing, planning or learning, suffers most. Routine work copes with interruptions better, so keep the thoughtful work for the protected time.

每次被打断，代价远不止那几秒：你要重新找到进度，重建思路。几次小小的看手机，就可能毁掉一个小时的思考。需要真正思考的工作，比如写作、规划、学习，受影响最大。例行的事对打断的耐受度更高，所以把需要思考的工作留给受保护的时间。

Step 2 · A protected block第 2 步 · 受保护的时间块

Learn先学

Choose a block of 45 to 90 minutes, name the one task, and remove what pulls you away: silence notifications, close extra tabs, put the phone out of reach, and tell others when you will be back. Short blocks with a clear end are easier to keep than a vague plan to focus all day. Take a real break afterwards.

选一块 45 到 90 分钟的时间，写明唯一的任务，并去掉会拉走你的东西：关掉通知、关掉多余的标签页、把手机放到够不着的地方，并告诉别人你什么时候回来。有明确终点的短时间块，比“整天专注”这种模糊的打算更容易坚持。结束后好好休息一下。

Step 3 · The ones you cannot avoid第 3 步 · 避免不了的打断

Learn先学

Some interruptions are real: a colleague, an urgent call. Handle them without losing the thread: before you switch, write one line on where you were and the next step. When you return, that note gets you going in seconds. Batch the low-value checks, such as messages, into a few set times rather than answering as they arrive.

有些打断是真的：同事来问、紧急电话。应对时别丢了线头：切换前先写一行字，记下做到哪里、下一步是什么。回来时，这张便条能让你几秒内重新进入状态。把价值不高的检查，比如消息，集中到几个固定时段，而不是来一条回一条。

Block: 9:00-10:15, report draft only. Phone: in the bag. Messages: checked at 10:30 and 12:00. If interrupted, write: "Was on section 2; next: add the table."
