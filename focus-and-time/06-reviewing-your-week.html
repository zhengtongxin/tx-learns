Reviewing your week回顾一周

Take a short regular look back to see what worked, clear loose ends and choose the next priorities · ~10 min · reading and practice定期做一次简短的回顾，看哪些有效，清掉遗留的事，选出接下来的重点 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A short look back第 1 步 · 简短回顾

Learn先学

Set a fixed time, about twenty minutes, once a week. Ask three questions: what got done, what did not and why, and what surprised me? Write your three answers down, even in a few lines, so you have notes to reread later. Keep it honest and free of blame. The goal is not to judge yourself but to notice patterns, such as always underestimating one kind of task, so that next week’s plan is better than this week’s.

定一个固定时间，每周一次，大约二十分钟。问三个问题：做成了什么？没做成什么、为什么？有什么出乎意料？把三个答案写下来，哪怕只有几行，以后才有笔记可重读。保持诚实，不要自责。目的不是评判自己，而是发现规律，比如总是低估某一类任务，这样下一周的计划就比这一周的好。

Step 2 · Clear the loose ends第 2 步 · 清理遗留事项

Learn先学

Gather everything still open: notes, open tabs, half-finished lists, promises you made. For each, do one of four things: do it now if it takes two minutes, schedule it, hand it over, or delete it. An empty head and a clean list let you think about the future instead of trying to remember the past.

把所有还没处理完的东西收拢起来：笔记、开着的标签页、写了一半的清单、你许下的承诺。每一项做四件事之一：两分钟能做完就立刻做、排进日程、交给别人，或者删掉。头脑空了、清单干净了，你才能思考未来，而不是努力记住过去。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Choose what is next第 3 步 · 选出下一步

Learn先学

Finish by choosing the one to three priorities for the coming week, and put them in your calendar before anything else fills it. Look at your fixed commitments first (meetings and deadlines you cannot move), then place the important blocks. Keep the plan light and leave slack. Every month or so, reread your last few weekly notes in one sitting: if the same problem shows up more than once, change one habit and see whether it goes away.

最后选出接下来一周的一到三个重点，趁别的事还没把日历填满，先把它们放进去。先看固定的安排（挪不动的会议和截止日期），再放入重要的时间块。计划保持轻盈，留出余地。每隔一个月左右，一次坐下来把最近几次每周回顾的笔记重读一遍：同一个问题出现不止一次，就改一个习惯，看它会不会消失。

Done: draft report, booked venue. Not done: invoice (waited for numbers). Pattern: I wait for others too long. Next: Tue 9:00 report final; Wed: ask for the numbers before you start.
