Choosing what matters · Lesson 1先选重要的事 · 第 1 课

Urgent and important紧急与重要

Tell apart what shouts for attention from what really matters, and protect time for the second kind · ~10 min · reading and practice分清“吵着要你注意”的事和“真正重要”的事，并为后者留出时间 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Two different questions第 1 步 · 两个不同的问题

Learn先学

Urgent asks “does this need action soon?” Important asks “does this move me toward what I care about?” They are not the same. A buzzing phone is urgent and often unimportant; learning a skill, a health check or planning a project is important and rarely urgent. When everything is treated as urgent, the important things are the ones that wait forever.

紧急问的是“这事要不要很快处理？”重要问的是“这事能不能让我更接近真正在乎的目标？”两者不是一回事。响个不停的手机很紧急，却常常不重要；学一项技能、做健康检查、规划一个项目很重要，却很少紧急。把一切都当作紧急，重要的事就会永远在等。

Step 2 · Four groups第 2 步 · 四个分组

Learn先学

Put each task in one of four groups. Urgent and important: do it now. Important but not urgent: schedule it. Urgent but not important: shorten, hand over or say a quick no. Neither: drop it. Most people spend their days in the urgent groups, so the second group, the important and not urgent one, is where a deliberate choice pays off most.

把每项任务放进四组之一。紧急且重要：马上做。重要但不紧急：排进日程。紧急但不重要：缩短、交给别人或简短拒绝。两者都不是：放弃。大多数人的日子都花在紧急的两组里，所以“重要但不紧急”这一组，是主动选择回报最大的地方。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Protect the important第 3 步 · 守住重要的事

Learn先学

Important work does not announce itself, so give it a slot: put a named block in your calendar and treat it like a meeting with someone you respect. Start the day, or the week, by choosing the one or two things that matter most. Tasks you chose beat tasks that merely arrived.

重要的事不会自己跳出来，所以要给它一个时段：在日历里留一块写明名称的时间，像对待一场重要的会面那样对待它。一天或一周开始时，先选出最重要的一两件。你自己选的任务，胜过只是碰巧送来的任务。

Important, not urgent: - Finish the first draft of the report (Tue 9:00-10:30) - Book the health check (this month) Urgent, not important: - Reply to the group chat (once, at 12:00) Drop: - Reorganise old photos

Takeaways要点回顾

• Urgent and important differ.
• Sort into four groups.

• 分成四组。
• 为重要的事留时间。
