Batching small tasks集中处理小事

Handle small tasks together in one short slot so each one does not cost you a restart · ~9 min · reading and practice把小任务集中在一个短时段里处理，让每个任务不再各自消耗一次重新进入的代价 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · See the small tasks第 1 步 · 看见那些小任务

Learn先学

Small tasks (a quick reply, a form, a call to book) feel harmless, but each one pulls you out of deeper work and costs a restart. Write down the small tasks that wait for you. Seeing them in one list is the first step to handling them well.

小任务（回一条消息、填一张表、预约一个电话）看起来无害，但每一个都会把你从深度工作中拉出来，还要付出重新进入的代价。先把等着你的小任务写下来。看到它们在同一张清单上，是处理好它们的第一步。

Step 2 · Batch them第 2 步 · 把它们集中处理

Learn先学

Batching means doing similar small tasks together in one set time. Say you have 4 tasks of 5 minutes each. Done in one go they take 4 × 5 = 20 minutes. Done one by one between other work, each costs about 5 more minutes to get back into what you were doing: 4 × 5 = 20 extra, so 40 in all. Batching halves the time (20 ÷ 40 = 50%).

集中处理是指在一个固定的时段里，把相似的小任务一起做完。假设有 4 个各 5 分钟的任务，一口气做完要 4 × 5 = 20 分钟。若在别的工作之间一件件做，每件还要多花约 5 分钟重新进入原来的状态：4 × 5 = 20 分钟额外，总共 40 分钟。集中处理省下一半的时间（20 ÷ 40 = 50%）。

Step 3 · Keep it small第 3 步 · 保持它小

Learn先学

Pick a fixed slot, such as 20 minutes after lunch, and keep it short so the batch does not grow into a whole afternoon. If a task turns out to be bigger than a few minutes, take it out and give it a proper place in your plan. The batch is for small things only.

选一个固定的时段，比如午饭后的 20 分钟，并保持它短小，免得集中处理拖成一整个下午。如果某个任务发现比几分钟大得多，就把它拿出来，在计划里给它一个正式的位置。这个时段只用于小事。

To finish Step 3, sort each habit as good or weak: tap a habit, then tap its group.

完成第 3 步：把每个做法分为好或弱。先点一个做法，再点它所属的组。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Small tasks each cost a restart, so list them together.
• Batching similar tasks in one slot can save a lot of time: 20 minutes instead of 40 in the example.
• Keep the slot short and fixed.
• A task that grows past a few minutes leaves the batch and gets its own plan.
• The batch is for small things only.

• 小任务各自都有重新进入的代价，所以把它们列在一起。
• 把相似的任务集中在一个时段，能省下很多时间：例子里是 20 分钟而不是 40 分钟。
• 让时段短小而固定。
• 超过几分钟的任务要离开集中时段，另做计划。
• 集中时段只用于小事。
