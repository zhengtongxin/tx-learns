Saying no学会拒绝

Protect your time by saying no, or not now, kindly and clearly, and by offering a smaller yes when it fits · ~10 min · reading and practice用友好而清楚的方式说“不”或“现在不行”，并在合适时给出一个更小的“可以”，以守住自己的时间 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Every yes costs something第 1 步 · 每个“好”都有代价

Learn先学

Time is fixed, so each yes is also a no to something else, usually the quiet important work. Before agreeing, ask what you would stop doing to fit it in. If the honest answer is “my own priority”, the cost is real. Seeing the trade makes it easier to decide, and to explain a no without feeling guilty.

时间是固定的，所以每个“好”同时也是对别的事说“不”，通常是安静的重要工作。答应之前，先问：为了挤进这件事，我要放下什么？如果诚实的答案是“我自己的重点”，代价就是真的。看清这笔交换，决定更容易，说“不”也不必内疚。

Step 2 · A kind, clear no第 2 步 · 友好而清楚的拒绝

Learn先学

Say no early, in one or two sentences, with a short reason or none: “I cannot take this on this month.” Thank them for asking, do not apologise at length, and do not invent excuses that invite a counter-offer. If it is only a matter of timing, say “not now” and give a date when you can look again.

尽早拒绝，用一两句话，说一个简短的理由，或者不说理由：“这个月我接不下来。”感谢对方想到你，别长篇道歉，也别编出会招来反提议的借口。如果只是时间问题，就说“现在不行”，并给一个可以再看的日期。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A smaller yes第 3 步 · 更小的“可以”

Learn先学

Sometimes you can help without taking the whole task. Offer something smaller: ten minutes to review it, a pointer to a person or document, or a later date. When the request comes from your manager, ask which matters more. For example: “I can do X or Y by Friday. Which first?” That keeps the decision where it belongs.

有时你不必接下整件事也能帮忙。提供一个更小的选项：花十分钟看一眼、指给对方一个人或一份文件，或者约一个晚些的日期。要求来自上级时，问他哪件更重要，比如：“周五前我能做 X 或 Y，先做哪个？”这样决定就留在该做决定的人手里。

Thanks for thinking of me. I cannot take on the full report this month. I can review your draft for 20 minutes on Thursday. If it can wait until next month, I would be glad to look again.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Each yes costs time.
• Say no early and kindly.
