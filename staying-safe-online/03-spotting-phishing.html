Spotting phishing识别钓鱼信息

Notice messages that try to rush you into clicking, paying or signing in, and check them another way before acting · ~10 min · reading and practice识别那些催你点击、付款或登录的信息，行动之前先用另一种方式核实 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The pattern behind the trick第 1 步 · 骗局背后的套路

Learn先学

Phishing messages pretend to be someone you trust: a bank, a courier, your boss. They share a pattern: urgency (“act within one hour”), a threat or a prize, and a link or attachment you did not expect. The goal is to make you act before you think. A message that makes you feel rushed deserves a pause.

钓鱼信息会假扮你信任的人：银行、快递、你的老板。它们有共同的套路：催促（“一小时内处理”）、威胁或奖励，再加一个你没想到的链接或附件。目的是让你来不及思考就行动。让你觉得被催的信息，值得停一停。

Step 2 · Check the small details第 2 步 · 看清小细节

Learn先学

Look at the sender’s full address, not just the display name. Hover over a link to see where it really goes. Watch for spelling slips, odd greetings and requests that do not fit how that person normally writes. Real organisations rarely ask for passwords or codes by message. Be careful with unexpected attachments, even from a known name.

看发件人的完整地址，而不只是显示的名字。把鼠标停在链接上，看它真正通向哪里。留意拼写错误、奇怪的称呼，以及不符合对方平时说话方式的请求。正规机构很少通过消息索要密码或验证码。对意外的附件要小心，哪怕名字看起来熟悉。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Verify another way第 3 步 · 换一种方式核实

Learn先学

Do not reply to the message or use its link. Open the site by typing its address or using your own bookmark, or call the number on the back of your card or on the official site. If your “boss” asks for gift cards or an urgent transfer, call them. Reporting the message helps others, then delete it. If you already clicked and typed a password, go to the last lesson’s steps now.

不要回复这条信息，也不要点它的链接。自己输入网址或用书签打开网站，或拨打银行卡背面、官方网站上的号码。如果“老板”要你买礼品卡或紧急转账，打电话找他本人。举报这条信息能帮到别人，然后删除。如果你已经点了并输入了密码，现在就照最后一课的步骤做。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Pressure is the warning sign.
• Check the sender and the real link.
• Verify another way before you act.

• 被催促就是警告信号。
• 看清发件人和真实链接。
