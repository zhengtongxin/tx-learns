Two-step sign-in两步验证

Add a second check to important accounts so a stolen password alone is not enough to get in · ~10 min · reading and practice给重要账号加上第二道验证，这样光有被盗的密码也进不去 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A second lock on the door第 1 步 · 门上的第二把锁

Learn先学

Two-step sign-in asks for something more than your password: a code, a prompt on your phone or a physical key. If someone steals your password, they still cannot get in without that second item. It is one of the most effective things you can do for an account, and it takes a few minutes.

两步验证在密码之外再要求一样东西：一个验证码、手机上的确认提示，或一把实体密钥。即使有人偷了你的密码，没有第二样东西也进不去。这是给账号加固最有效的做法之一，几分钟就能完成。

Step 2 · Choose the stronger kind第 2 步 · 选更强的方式

Learn先学

A code sent by text message is better than nothing, but someone can take over your number or read the text. A code from an authenticator app (an app on your phone that makes the code) is stronger, and a physical security key (a small device you plug in or tap) is stronger still. Start with your email, then banking and anything that holds payment details. Never read a code out to someone who calls you, even if they say they are from the company.

短信验证码总比没有好，但有人可能接管你的号码或读到短信。验证器应用（手机上生成验证码的应用）的验证码更强，实体安全密钥（插上或轻触的小设备）更强。先从邮箱开始，再到银行和保存了付款信息的账号。无论谁打电话来、说自己来自哪家公司，都不要把验证码念给对方。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Plan for losing your phone第 3 步 · 想好手机丢了怎么办

Learn先学

The second step can lock you out too. When you switch it on, the service usually gives you backup codes: one-time codes for signing in when you cannot get your usual second code. Save them offline, such as printed and kept in a drawer, not only in the phone that might be lost. Also keep a recovery email or number up to date. Check once a year that they still work.

第二步也可能把你自己挡在外面。开启时，服务通常会给你一组备用码，这是一次性的验证码，在拿不到平时的第二个验证码时用来登录。把它们存在离线的地方，比如打印出来放进抽屉，而不是只存在可能丢失的手机里。也要保持恢复邮箱或号码是最新的。每年检查一次它们还能用。

Turn on, in this order: 1. email 2. bank and payment accounts 3. social and shopping accounts Second step: authenticator app or security key Backup codes: printed, in a drawer Never read a code to a caller

Takeaways要点回顾

• Two checks beat one.
• Email first, then money.
• Save backup codes offline; never share a code.

• 两道验证胜过一道。
• 先邮箱，再涉及钱的账号。
• 备用码离线保存，验证码绝不外传。
