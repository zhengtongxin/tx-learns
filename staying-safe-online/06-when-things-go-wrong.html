Guarding your devices and data · Lesson 3守护设备和数据 · 第 3 课

When things go wrong出了问题怎么办

Act calmly and in order when an account is taken over, a device is lost or you clicked something you should not have · ~10 min · reading and practice账号被盗、设备丢失，或点了不该点的东西时，冷静而有序地处理 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · First minutes: cut off access第 1 步 · 最初几分钟：切断访问

Learn先学

If you think an account is taken, change its password at once from a device you trust, using a new, unique one. Turn on two-step sign-in if it is off, and use the option to sign out all other devices. Do your email first, since the “forgot password” links for the rest are sent there. If you used the same password elsewhere, change those too.

如果觉得账号被盗，马上用可信的设备换掉它的密码，用一个全新的、独一无二的密码。如果没开两步验证，就开启它，并用“退出所有其他设备”的选项。先处理邮箱，因为其他账号的“忘记密码”链接都发到那里。如果别处用过同样的密码，也要一起换。

Step 2 · Then tell the right people第 2 步 · 然后告诉该告诉的人

Learn先学

If money or a card is involved, contact the bank or card provider right away, using the number on your card. Tell the service itself, and warn friends or colleagues if scam messages may have gone out in your name. If a device is lost, use its “find” option, and “erase” (wipes everything, so it is the last resort) if it cannot be found. At work, tell the person responsible for security quickly. Speed matters more than pride.

涉及钱或银行卡，立即用卡上的号码联系银行或发卡机构。告诉相关服务本身，如果可能有骗局信息以你的名义发出，也提醒朋友或同事。设备丢了，就用它的“查找”功能，找不到再用“抹除”（会清空所有内容，是最后手段）。在工作中，尽快告诉负责安全的人。速度比面子更重要。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Recover and learn第 3 步 · 恢复，并从中学习

Learn先学

Check the account for changes you did not make: forwarding rules (a rule that copies your new mail to another address), new devices, odd messages. Restore anything lost from your backup. Be wary of people who contact you offering to “fix” it for a fee, or who threaten you to pay. Then ask what let it happen, whether a reused password or a rushed click, and fix that one thing. Anyone can be caught out; the response is what counts.

检查账号里有没有不是你做的改动：转发规则（把你的新邮件复制到另一个地址的规则）、新设备、奇怪的信息。从备份里恢复丢失的东西。提防主动联系你、收费“帮你修复”，或者威胁你付钱的人。然后想想是什么让它发生，是重复的密码，还是匆忙的一次点击，把这一点改掉。谁都可能中招；重要的是之后怎么处理。

1. Change the password (email first), from a trusted device 2. Turn on two-step; sign out other devices 3. Call the bank if money is involved 4. Tell the service, and warn contacts 5. Check for changes you did not make 6. Restore from backup; fix the cause

Takeaways要点回顾

• Cut off access first, quickly.
• Tell the bank, the service and your contacts.
• Fix the cause; do not blame yourself.

• 先切断访问，要快。
• 告诉银行、服务方和联系人。
• 改掉原因，不责怪自己。
