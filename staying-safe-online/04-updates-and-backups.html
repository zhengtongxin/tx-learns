Updates and backups更新与备份

Keep software patched and keep copies of what you cannot afford to lose · ~10 min · reading and practice让软件保持最新，并为不能丢的东西留好副本 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Updates close known holes第 1 步 · 更新堵住已知的漏洞

Learn先学

Many break-ins use weaknesses that are already known and already fixed in a newer version. An update is that fix. Turning on automatic updates for your phone, computer, browser and apps is one of the easiest protections you can add. Restart when it asks, instead of postponing for weeks. Remove apps you no longer use, since each is one more thing to keep updated.

很多入侵利用的是已知、且新版本早已修复的弱点。更新就是那个修复。给手机、电脑、浏览器和应用开启自动更新，是最容易加上的保护之一。需要重启时就重启，别一拖几周。把不再使用的应用删掉，每多一个就多一样要更新的东西。

Step 2 · Keep more than one copy第 2 步 · 留不止一份副本

Learn先学

Phones get lost, drives fail and files get locked by malware (harmful software, and some of it locks your files). If your only copy is on one device, one accident ends it. A simple rule: keep the original, a second copy on another device or drive, and a third somewhere else, such as an online storage service or a drive kept elsewhere. Copy what matters: photos, documents and anything you cannot recreate.

手机会丢，硬盘会坏，文件还可能被恶意软件（有害的软件，有些会把你的文件锁住）锁住。如果唯一的副本只在一台设备上，一次意外就全没了。一个简单的规则：留着原件，在另一台设备或硬盘上放第二份，再在别处放第三份，比如在线存储服务，或放在别处的硬盘。备份重要的东西：照片、文档，以及无法重做的一切。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Prove it works第 3 步 · 证明它真的能用

Learn先学

A backup you have never opened is a hope, not a plan. Set it to run automatically, so you do not rely on memory. A synced folder is not a backup by itself, because it copies deletions too. Then, once or twice a year, try restoring one file from it. Keep a backup drive unplugged when it is not in use, so that malware on your computer cannot reach it too.

从没打开过的备份只是希望，不是计划。把它设成自动运行，别靠记性。同步文件夹本身不算备份，因为它连删除也会一起同步。然后每年试一两次，从中还原一个文件。备份硬盘不用时拔掉，这样电脑上的恶意软件也碰不到它。

Updates: automatic, on every device Restart when asked Backups (three places): 1. the device 2. a second drive 3. online storage or a drive kept elsewhere Test: restore one file twice a year

Takeaways要点回顾

• Updates fix known holes.
• Keep copies in more than one place.
• Test the restore.

• 更新修复已知的漏洞。
• 副本放在不止一个地方。
• 测试还原。
