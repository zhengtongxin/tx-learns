Guarding your accounts · Lesson 1守护你的账号 · 第 1 课

Strong passwords强密码

Make every account hard to break into: long, different for each account, and kept in a password manager · ~10 min · reading and practice让每个账号都难以攻破：够长、各不相同，并存进密码管理器 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Long beats clever第 1 步 · 长比花哨更重要

Learn先学

A password is hard to guess mainly because it is long and unpredictable. Swapping an “a” for an “@” adds little. Four or five words picked by chance (roll dice against a word list, or let a password manager’s generator pick them, not words you like) strung together are far harder to crack than a short word with symbols, and easier to remember. Avoid names, birthdays and anything about you that others could find.

密码难猜，主要靠又长又没有规律。把“a”换成“@”几乎没用。四五个靠运气选出的词（对着词表掷骰子，或让密码管理器的生成器来选，不是你喜欢的词）连在一起，比带符号的短单词难破得多，也更好记。避开名字、生日，以及别人能查到的个人信息。

Step 2 · One password, one account第 2 步 · 一个账号，一个密码

Learn先学

When a site leaks passwords, attackers try the same email and password on other sites. If you reuse a password, one leak opens many doors. A different password for each account keeps a leak small. Your email account matters most, because the “forgot password” link for other accounts is sent to your email.

网站泄露密码后，攻击者会拿同一组邮箱和密码去试别的网站。重复使用密码，一次泄露就能打开很多扇门。每个账号一个不同的密码，就能把损失限制在一处。邮箱账号最重要，因为其他账号的“忘记密码”链接都发到那里。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Let a manager remember第 3 步 · 让管理器替你记

Learn先学

You cannot memorise dozens of different long passwords, and you should not try. A password manager is an app that creates them, stores them and fills them in, and its generator can make passphrases too. You remember one strong passphrase, a password made of several words, for the manager itself. Writing passwords on a sticky note beside the screen, or in a plain note on your phone, is the habit to drop.

你记不住几十个不同的长密码，也不必去记。密码管理器是一个会生成、保存并自动填写密码的应用，它的生成器也能生成口令。你只需记住管理器自己的那一句强口令（由几个词组成的密码）。把密码写在屏幕旁的便利贴上，或存在手机的普通备忘录里，这些习惯要改掉。

Remember (one passphrase, four or five words): river-lamp-orbit-tulip-market Let the manager store: email, bank, shopping, social, work each one: long, random, different Never: reuse a password share it in chat or email keep it on a sticky note

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Long and random beats short and clever.
• One password for each account.
• A manager remembers; you keep one passphrase.

• 又长又随机，胜过又短又花哨。
• 一个账号一个密码。
• 让管理器去记，你只记一句口令。
