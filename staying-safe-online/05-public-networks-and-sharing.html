Public networks and sharing公共网络与分享

Use shared Wi-Fi with care and think before you share links, files and personal details · ~10 min · reading and practice谨慎使用公共 Wi-Fi，并在分享链接、文件和个人信息之前先想一想 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Shared Wi-Fi is shared第 1 步 · 共用的 Wi-Fi 是大家共用的

Learn先学

On a public network, you do not know who runs it or who else is on it. A fake network can copy a real name. Most sites protect your data while it travels between your device and the site, but the safest habit is simple: keep banking and other sensitive sign-ins for your own connection, such as your mobile data. If you must use public Wi-Fi, check the site address, keep your device updated and turn off automatic joining of open networks (networks with no password).

在公共网络上，你不知道谁在运营，也不知道还有谁在用。假网络可以仿冒真名字。多数网站会在数据往来于你的设备和网站之间时保护它，但最稳妥的习惯很简单：银行等敏感登录留给自己的网络，比如手机流量。非用公共 Wi-Fi 不可，就确认网址、保持设备更新，并关闭自动加入开放网络（没有密码的网络）。

Step 2 · Share with the smallest group第 2 步 · 只分享给最小的范围

Learn先学

A link set to “anyone with the link” can be forwarded and found by people you never meant. Share with named people where you can, and choose view-only unless they need to edit. Remove access when the work is done. Before you send a file, ask what is inside it: hidden pages, comments and personal details travel along.

设成“知道链接的任何人”的链接，可能被转发，被你从没想到的人看到。尽量分享给指定的人，除非对方需要编辑，否则选只读。事情做完就收回权限。发文件之前，想想里面有什么：隐藏的页面、批注和个人信息都会一起带走。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Post as if everyone can see第 3 步 · 发帖时当作所有人都看得到

Learn先学

Details people post for fun add up: your birthday, your pet’s name, your street, a photo of a boarding pass. Together they help someone answer your security questions or pretend to be you. Check who can see your posts, and keep addresses, ID numbers and travel dates out of public ones.

大家随手发的小细节会叠加起来：生日、宠物的名字、住的街道、一张登机牌的照片。合在一起，别人就能回答你的安全问题，或者冒充你。检查谁能看到你的帖子，地址、证件号码和出行日期别放进公开的帖子。

Before you share: Who needs this? -> name them Can they just view? -> view-only When is it done? -> remove access What is inside? -> hidden pages, comments Public posts: no address, ID number, boarding pass

Takeaways要点回顾

• Do sensitive things on your own connection.
• Share with the smallest group.
• Post as if everyone can see.

• 敏感的事用自己的连接。
• 分享给最小的范围。
• 发帖时当作所有人都看得到。
