What a risk is什么是风险

Tell a risk from a problem you already have, and describe it as a cause, an event and an effect · ~10 min · reading and practice分清风险和已经发生的问题，并用原因、事件、影响三部分来描述 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A risk has not happened yet第 1 步 · 风险还没有发生

Learn先学

A risk is something uncertain that, if it happens, would change what the project achieves. The key word is uncertain: it might happen and it might not. Once it has happened it is no longer a risk but an issue, a problem you must deal with now. Heavy rain on the day of an outdoor event is a risk the week before and an issue when the clouds arrive.

风险是一件不确定的事：如果发生，会改变项目的结果。关键词是“不确定”：可能发生，也可能不发生。一旦已经发生，它就不再是风险，而是问题，是必须马上处理的事。户外活动当天下大雨，在前一周是风险，乌云压来时就是问题。

Step 2 · Cause, event, effect第 2 步 · 原因、事件、影响

Learn先学

A useful risk statement has three parts: the cause (a fact that is true today), the event (what might happen) and the effect (what it would do to the project). Writing all three keeps the risk specific. “Weather” is vague; “because the event is outdoors, rain might fall, and then the talks would move indoors and cost an extra day” can be sized and answered.

一条有用的风险描述有三部分：原因（今天就成立的事实）、事件（可能发生什么）和影响（会让项目怎样）。三部分写全，风险才具体。“天气”太含糊；“因为活动在户外，可能下雨，那样讲座就得挪进室内，多花一天”，才能评估大小、才能应对。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Some risks are chances第 3 步 · 有的风险是机会

Learn先学

Not every uncertainty is bad. A risk that would help the project, such as a supplier offering an early-order discount, is an opportunity. The same habits apply: describe it, size it, and decide whether to chase it. Looking only for threats means missing things worth having.

不是所有不确定的事都是坏事。会对项目有利的风险，比如供应商给提前下单的折扣，叫机会。做法是一样的：描述它、评估它、决定要不要去争取。只盯着威胁，就会错过值得拿的东西。

Cause : the event is outdoors Event : rain may fall on the day Effect : talks move indoors, one extra day, S$800 more Type : threat

Takeaways要点回顾

• A risk is uncertain and has not happened yet; once it has, it is an issue.
• Write cause, event and effect so the risk is specific.
• Some risks are opportunities worth chasing.

• 风险是不确定且尚未发生的；已经发生，就是问题。
• 写出原因、事件、影响，风险才具体。
• 有些风险是值得争取的机会。
