Acting on risks · Lesson 3应对风险 · 第 3 课

Keeping the register alive让登记表活下去

Keep a short risk register with owners and triggers, review it on a rhythm and close risks properly · ~10 min · reading and practice维护一张简短的风险登记表，写明负责人和触发信号，定期复查，并正确地关闭风险 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A short register with owners第 1 步 · 带负责人的简短登记表

Learn先学

A risk register is a simple list: the risk, its score, the response, an owner, a trigger and the date by which it can no longer happen. The owner is one named person who watches the risk and acts. The trigger is an early sign that the risk is about to happen, such as “supplier misses its first weekly check-in”. Without an owner nobody watches; without a trigger you notice only when it is too late.

风险登记表就是一张简单的清单：风险、分数、应对办法、负责人、触发信号，以及它不再可能发生的日期。负责人是一位具体的人，负责盯着这个风险并采取行动。触发信号是风险快要发生的早期迹象，比如“供应商错过了第一次每周对接”。没有负责人，没人盯；没有触发信号，等你发现已经来不及了。

Step 2 · Review on a rhythm第 2 步 · 按节奏复查

Learn先学

A register that is written once and filed is useless. Look at it at a fixed rhythm, such as ten minutes in the weekly meeting. Ask three things: did any trigger fire, did any score change, and is there a new risk? Re-score after a response too. A risk reduced from 12 to 4 is still on the list (in the box below, a backup print run means a late supplier no longer hurts much, so impact falls from 3 to 1 and the score from 9 to 3), now with a smaller number, which is called the residual risk.

写完就归档的登记表没用。要按固定的节奏看，比如每周例会里花十分钟。问三件事：有没有触发信号出现？有没有分数变了？有没有新风险？应对之后也要重新打分。一个从 12 降到 4 的风险仍然在表里（下面的框里，备用印刷让供应商延误不再有大影响，所以影响从 3 降到 1，分数从 9 降到 3），只是数字小了，这叫剩余风险。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Close it, or turn it into an issue第 3 步 · 关闭，或转成问题

Learn先学

A risk leaves the list in one of two ways. If that date has passed without it happening, close it and note why. If it happens, it becomes an issue: move it to the issues list, use the plan you made and note what you learned for next time. Either way the register shows how the risk story ended, which is what the next project needs.

一个风险离开清单有两种方式。如果那个日期过了它也没发生，就关闭并记下原因。如果它发生了，就变成问题：移到问题清单，用你事先备好的办法，并记下这次学到的东西，供下次使用。两种方式都能让登记表交代这个风险的结局，而这正是下一个项目需要的。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A register has owner and trigger for each risk.
• Review it on a rhythm: triggers, score changes, new risks.
• Close with a note, or turn a happened risk into an issue.

• 登记表里每个风险都有负责人和触发信号。
• 按节奏复查：触发信号、分数变化、新风险。
• 关闭时写明原因；已发生的风险转成问题。
