Seeing risks · Lesson 2看见风险 · 第 2 课

Finding risks找出风险

Use checklist questions, past projects and other people to find risks you would otherwise miss · ~10 min · reading and practice用提示清单、过去的项目和别人的眼睛，找出你自己会漏掉的风险 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Walk through the areas第 1 步 · 按领域逐个走一遍

Learn先学

Staring at a blank page finds few risks. Instead, walk through a list of areas and ask “what could go wrong here?” for each: people (who might leave or be unavailable), money (costs that might rise), time (steps that might slip), suppliers (late or wrong deliveries) and things outside the project (rules, weather, other projects). Each area is a question to ask that pulls out a risk you would not have thought of.

对着空白页发呆，找不出几条风险。更好的办法是列出几个领域，每个都问一句“这里可能出什么错？”：人（谁可能离开或没空）、钱（哪些成本可能涨）、时间（哪些步骤可能延误）、供应商（送货迟了或错了）和项目之外的事（规定、天气、别的项目）。每个领域都是一条提示，能带出你原本想不到的风险。

Step 2 · Learn from before第 2 步 · 向以往学习

Learn先学

Past projects are the cheapest source of risks. Look at an earlier similar project and ask what went wrong, what nearly went wrong and what was luck. If the last event ran late because the printer delivered posters late, “late printing” goes on the new list on day one. Even a short note from a past project beats memory alone.

以往的项目是最便宜的风险来源。看看以前类似的项目，问问哪里出过错、哪里差点出错、哪些只是运气好。如果上次活动因为印刷厂晚交海报而延误，那“印刷延误”从第一天起就该上新的清单。哪怕只有一小段以往项目的记录，也比光靠记忆强。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Ask more than one person第 3 步 · 不要只靠一个人

Learn先学

One person sees a project from one side. A short session with the people who will do the work, plus someone who has done something similar, finds more risks than the project lead alone. Ask a simple question: “If this fails, what will the reason have been?” Everyone knows a worry they have not said out loud.

一个人只能从一个角度看项目。让将要做事的人再加上一位做过类似事情的人聊一小会儿，找到的风险比项目负责人一个人想多得多。问一个简单的问题：“如果这件事失败了，原因会是什么？”每个人心里都有一个没说出口的担心。

People : who could be away or leave? Money : which costs could rise? Time : which steps could slip? Suppliers : what could arrive late or wrong? Outside : rules, weather, other projects?

Takeaways要点回顾

• Walk through areas instead of staring at a blank page.
• Past projects are the cheapest source of risks.
• Ask more than one person: everyone has an unspoken worry.

• 按领域走一遍，不要对着空白页发呆。
• 以往的项目是最便宜的风险来源。
• 不要只靠一个人：每个人都有没说出口的担心。
