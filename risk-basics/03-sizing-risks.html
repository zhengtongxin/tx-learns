Sizing risks评估风险大小

Score risks by how likely and how big, and use the score to decide which to deal with first · ~10 min · reading and practice按可能性和影响大小给风险打分，用分数决定先处理哪几个 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Probability and impact第 1 步 · 可能性和影响

Learn先学

Two questions size a risk. Probability: how likely is it to happen? Impact: how much would it hurt if it did? Use a small scale, such as 1 to 5 for each, where 1 is very low and 5 is very high. A rain shower that is likely (probability 4) but only moves a talk indoors (impact 2) is a different animal from a rare failure (probability 2) that would cancel the event (impact 5).

两个问题就能给风险定大小。可能性：它有多可能发生？影响：一旦发生会有多痛？用一个小的刻度，比如各自 1 到 5 分，1 是很低，5 是很高。一场很可能下的阵雨（可能性 4）只是让一场讲座挪进室内（影响 2），和一个罕见却会让活动取消的故障（可能性 2、影响 5）完全是两种东西。

Step 2 · Multiply to rank第 2 步 · 相乘来排序

Learn先学

Multiply the two scores to get one number: probability × impact. Rain: 4 × 2 = 8. The rare cancelling failure: 2 × 5 = 10. A late supplier rated 3 and 3 scores 9. Sorted from the top, the cancelling failure (10) comes before the supplier (9), which comes before the rain (8). The score is a way to order the list, not a precise measurement.

把两个分数相乘，得到一个数：可能性 × 影响。下雨：4 × 2 = 8。罕见却会取消活动的故障：2 × 5 = 10。供应商延误，两项都评 3，得 9。从高到低排，会取消活动的故障（10）排在供应商（9）之前，供应商又排在下雨（8）之前。分数是用来给清单排序的，并不是精确的测量。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Keep it simple and shared第 3 步 · 简单，并且大家一致

Learn先学

Scores are guesses, so keep the method light and agree what each number means before you start. Write one line for each score, such as “impact 5 means the event is cancelled”. Then two people rating the same risk land close to each other. If they differ a lot, that is useful: they know different things, so talk about it.

分数是猜出来的，所以方法要轻，开始前先约定每个数字的含义。给每个分数写一句话，比如“影响 5 表示活动被取消”。这样两个人给同一个风险打分，结果会很接近。如果差得很远，也有用：说明他们知道的东西不同，值得聊一聊。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Size a risk with probability and impact on a small scale.
• Multiply to rank: the score orders the list.
• Agree what each score means so ratings can be compared.

• 用小刻度的可能性和影响给风险定大小。
• 相乘来排序：分数用来给清单排顺序。
• 约定每个分数的含义，打分才可比。
