Choosing a response选择应对方式

Pick avoid, reduce, transfer or accept for a threat, and compare the cost of a response with what it saves · ~10 min · reading and practice为威胁选择规避、降低、转移或接受，并把应对的成本和它省下的钱作比较 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Four ways to answer a threat第 1 步 · 应对威胁的四种方式

Learn先学

For a threat there are four basic answers. Avoid: change the plan so the risk cannot happen, such as dropping an outdoor stage. Reduce: make it less likely or smaller, such as booking a backup tent. Transfer: pass the cost to someone else, such as buying insurance or a fixed-price contract. Accept: do nothing now and carry on, perhaps with a plan for if it happens.

威胁基本有四种应对方式。规避：改变计划，让风险不可能发生，比如取消户外舞台。降低：让它更不可能或更小，比如预订一个备用帐篷。转移：把代价转给别人，比如买保险或签固定价合同。接受：现在什么都不做，继续往前走，也许备好一个“万一发生”的办法。

Step 2 · Is the response worth it?第 2 步 · 这个应对值得吗？

Learn先学

A response costs something, so compare it with what it saves. Say a risk has a 40% chance of costing S$5,000: its expected cost (chance × cost) is 0.4 × 5,000 = S$2,000. A S$300 fix that cuts the chance to 10% leaves 0.1 × 5,000 = S$500. It saves 2,000 − 500 = S$1,500 for S$300, a net gain of S$1,200, so it is worth doing. A tiny risk, say 2% of S$1,000, is only S$20, so simply accept it.

应对是要花钱的，所以要拿它和省下的钱比。假设一个风险有 40% 的可能让你损失 5,000 新元：预期成本（可能性 × 成本）是 0.4 × 5,000 = 2,000 新元。一笔 300 新元的措施，把可能性降到 10%，剩下 0.1 × 5,000 = 500 新元。它花 300 新元省下 2,000 − 500 = 1,500 新元，净赚 1,200 新元，值得做。很小的风险，比如 2% 乘 1,000 新元，只有 20 新元，直接接受就好。

Step 3 · Do not forget the good ones第 3 步 · 别忘了好的那类

Learn先学

Opportunities have their own answers: exploit (make sure it happens), enhance (make it more likely or bigger) and share (team up with someone who can capture it). Whatever you choose, give each response an owner and a date, or it stays a good idea on paper.

机会也有自己的应对：利用（确保它发生）、增强（让它更可能或更大）和分享（和能抓住它的人合作）。不管选哪种，每项应对都要有负责人和日期，否则只是纸上的好主意。

Risk: 40% chance of S$5,000 loss -> expected cost S$2,000 Reduce: spend S$300, chance falls to 10% -> S$500 Saving: 2,000 - 500 = S$1,500 for S$300 -> do it Risk: 2% chance of S$1,000 -> S$20 -> accept

Takeaways要点回顾

• Four answers to a threat: avoid, reduce, transfer, accept.
• Compare what a response costs with what it saves.
• Every response needs an owner and a date.

• 威胁的四种应对：规避、降低、转移、接受。
• 拿应对的成本和它省下的钱比一比。
• 每项应对都要有负责人和日期。
