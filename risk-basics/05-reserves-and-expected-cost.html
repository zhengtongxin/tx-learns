Reserves and expected cost储备与预期成本

Turn risk scores into money, and set aside a reserve that fits the risks you carry · ~10 min · reading and practice把风险分数换成钱，并留出和所承担风险相称的储备 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Expected cost第 1 步 · 预期成本

Learn先学

The expected cost of a risk is its probability times what it would cost: probability × cost. A supplier delay with a 30% chance of costing S$6,000 has an expected cost of 0.3 × 6,000 = S$1,800. You will not lose exactly S$1,800: either nothing happens or S$6,000 is lost. But across many risks, the expected costs add up to a sensible amount to set aside. That money is called a reserve.

一个风险的预期成本是它的可能性乘以会花的钱：可能性 × 成本。一个有 30% 可能造成 6,000 新元损失的供应商延误，预期成本是 0.3 × 6,000 = 1,800 新元。你不会正好损失 1,800 新元：要么什么都没发生，要么损失 6,000。但很多风险放在一起，预期成本加起来就是一笔合理的预留额。这笔钱叫储备。

Step 2 · Add them up into a reserve第 2 步 · 加起来形成储备

Learn先学

List each risk with its expected cost and add them. A supplier delay is S$1,800. A key person falling sick, 10% of S$9,000, is S$900. A price rise, 50% of S$2,000, is S$1,000. Together 1,800 + 900 + 1,000 = S$3,700. An opportunity with a 20% chance of saving S$1,500 is worth 0.2 × 1,500 = S$300, so the net reserve is 3,700 − 300 = S$3,400. Time works the same way: a 30% chance of a 5-day delay is 0.3 × 5 = 1.5 days.

把每个风险和它的预期成本列出来再加总。供应商延误是 1,800 新元。关键的人生病，9,000 新元的 10%，是 900 新元。价格上涨，2,000 新元的 50%，是 1,000 新元。合计 1,800 + 900 + 1,000 = 3,700 新元。一个有 20% 可能省下 1,500 新元的机会，价值 0.2 × 1,500 = 300 新元，所以净储备是 3,700 − 300 = 3,400 新元。时间也一样：有 30% 的可能延误 5 天，就是 0.3 × 5 = 1.5 天。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · A reserve is not padding第 3 步 · 储备不是水分

Learn先学

A reserve is tied to named risks, kept visible and spent by a clear rule. Padding is a hidden “just in case” added to every task, which hides real problems and tends to get used up anyway. Show the reserve as its own line and say what it covers, so the sponsor (the person who backs the project) can agree to it and you can report how it was used.

储备和有名有姓的风险挂钩，是公开可见的，并按明确的规则动用。水分是悄悄加在每个任务上的“以防万一”，它会掩盖真正的问题，而且总会被用光。把储备单独列一行并说明它覆盖什么，发起人（支持这个项目的人）才能同意，你也才能汇报它用在了哪里。

Supplier delay 30% x 6,000 = 1,800 Key person sick 10% x 9,000 = 900 Price rise 50% x 2,000 = 1,000 Opportunity 20% x 1,500 = -300 (a saving) Reserve: 1,800 + 900 + 1,000 - 300 = S$3,400

Takeaways要点回顾

• Expected cost is probability times cost.
• Add the expected costs, less any gains, to size a reserve.
• A reserve is visible and named; padding is hidden.

• 预期成本是可能性乘以成本。
• 把预期成本加起来，减去收益，就得到储备的大小。
• 储备公开、有名目；水分则是隐藏的。
