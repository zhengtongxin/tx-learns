Acting on risks · Lesson 4应对风险 · 第 4 课

Learning from risks从风险中学习

Look back at a finished project's risk register, check the reserve and the surprises, and pass the lessons on · ~9 min · reading and practice回看已结束项目的风险登记表，核对储备和意外，并把经验传下去 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Look back at the register第 1 步 · 回看风险登记表

Learn先学

When a project ends, the risk register (the list of risks, what each could cost and what you planned to do about it) still holds a lesson. Open it and ask three plain questions. Which risks actually happened? Which happened that we never listed? Which responses worked (what you did about each risk)? Say the register had 10 risks and 3 of them happened: 3 ÷ 10 = 30%. That tells you how often your list turned into real trouble.

项目结束时，风险登记表（列出风险、每个风险可能的代价以及你计划怎么应对的清单）里仍有可学的东西。打开它，问三个朴素的问题。哪些风险真的发生了？哪些发生了却从没列出来？哪些应对方式管用（你对每个风险做了什么）？假设登记表里有 10 个风险，其中 3 个发生了：3 ÷ 10 = 30%。这说明你列出的风险有多大比例变成了真正的麻烦。

Step 2 · Check the money and the surprises第 2 步 · 核对金额与意外

Learn先学

Compare the reserve, the money set aside to pay for risks, with what was used. Say the reserve was S$3,400 and the team used S$2,720: 2,720 ÷ 3,400 = 80%, with S$3,400 − S$2,720 = S$680 left. A reserve that is always empty was too small, and one that is always untouched may have been too big. Then count the surprises. Say a fourth risk also happened that was never on the register. Then 3 of the 4 that happened were listed, so 3 ÷ 4 = 75% were seen in advance, and the one that was not points to a gap in how you looked for risks.

把储备（为应对风险预留的钱）与实际用掉的做比较。假设储备是 3,400 新元，团队用了 2,720 新元：2,720 ÷ 3,400 = 80%，还剩 3,400 − 2,720 = 680 新元。总是用光的储备太小了，总是原封不动的储备可能太大。然后数一数意外。假设还有第 4 个风险也发生了，而它从没列在登记表上。那么发生的 4 个风险中有 3 个在登记表上，那么 3 ÷ 4 = 75% 是事先看到的，没看到的那一个说明你找风险的方法有缺口。

Step 3 · Pass it on第 3 步 · 传给下一个项目

Learn先学

Write down what you learned in a few short lines: the risk, what happened, what worked and what to do next time. Keep the notes where the next team will find them, for example with the project’s files, and start the next register from them, instead of from a blank page. Share the lessons without blame, so people report honestly. A lesson nobody reads changes nothing.

用几行简短的话写下你学到的东西：风险、发生了什么、什么管用、下次怎么做。把笔记放在下一个团队找得到的地方，例如项目文件旁，并以它们为起点建立下一份登记表，而不是从空白开始。分享经验时不要责怪任何人，这样大家才会如实报告。没人读的经验改变不了任何事。

Sort each habit as a good way to learn from risks or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“好的学习方式”或“弱的学习方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Look back at the register: which risks happened, which were never listed, which responses worked.
• 3 of 10 listed risks happened is 30%; if a fourth was never listed, 3 of 4 is 75% seen in advance.
• Compare the reserve with what was used: 2,720 of 3,400 is 80%, S$680 left.
• A surprise points to a gap in how you look for risks, not a person to blame.
• Write short lessons and keep them where the next team will find them.

• 回看登记表：哪些风险发生了、哪些从没列出、哪些应对方式管用。
• 列出的 10 个风险中 3 个发生是 30%；如果第 4 个从没列过，4 个中的 3 个就是 75% 事先看到了。
• 把储备和用掉的做比较：3,400 中用了 2,720 是 80%，还剩 680 新元。
• 意外说明找风险的方法有缺口，而不是有人该被责怪。
• 写下简短的经验，并放在下一个团队找得到的地方。
