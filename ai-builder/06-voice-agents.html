Step 1 · Three jobs in a loop第 1 步 · 循环里的三件事

Learn先学

A voice agent is a chat agent with ears and a mouth. Speech goes to text, a model decides what to say, and text goes back to speech. Then it listens again.

语音智能体就是装上耳朵和嘴巴的聊天智能体：语音转成文字，模型决定说什么，文字再转回语音，然后它继续听。

Two things make voice harder than chat: speed (a pause of two seconds feels broken) and turn-taking (it must know when you have finished, and stop when you interrupt).

语音比聊天难在两点：速度（停顿两秒就像坏了）和轮流说话（要知道你什么时候说完，你插嘴时它要停下）。

Step 3 · Write for the ear第 3 步 · 为耳朵而写

Learn先学

• Short: two sentences, then let the caller talk.
• Plain: no bullets, tables or markdown. They get read aloud, or skipped.
• Spelled for speech: numbers, dates and codes written the way you would say them.
• Honest: a safe line for "I do not know".

• 短：说两句，就让对方开口。
• 平实：不要项目符号、表格和 markdown，它们要么被念出来，要么被跳过。
• 按说法写：数字、日期和编号写成你会说的样子。
• 诚实：为“我不知道”准备一句稳妥的话。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 4 · Rewrite an answer for the ear第 4 步 · 把答案改写成适合听的

Worked example例子

A text answer often sounds wrong when read aloud. Here is the same fact both ways. The first is fine on a screen and awful on a phone; the second is what you want the agent to say.

文字答案念出来常常不对劲。下面是同一件事的两种写法：第一种放在屏幕上没问题，打电话时很糟；第二种才是你要智能体说的话。

On screen: Hours: Tue-Sun 10:00-19:00 | Basic service $42 | Full service $89 For the ear: We are open Tuesday to Sunday, from ten in the morning to seven in the evening. A basic service is forty-two dollars, and a full service is eighty-nine.

Put a rule like this in the system prompt: “Say times and prices in words. Never read out symbols such as dashes or slashes.” Then test by asking the agent for the opening hours and listen for anything that sounds like reading a table.

在系统提示里写一条类似的规则：“用文字说出时间和价格，不要念出破折号、斜杠等符号。”然后问它营业时间，听有没有哪里像是在念表格。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A voice agent is listen, think, speak, in a loop.
• Speed and turn-taking are what make voice harder than chat.
• The system prompt sets the job, the tone and the limits.
• Write short, plain, speakable sentences. No symbols.
• Always give it a safe line for what it does not know.

• 语音智能体就是“听、想、说”的循环。
• 速度和轮流说话，是语音比聊天难的地方。
• 系统提示词规定任务、语气和边界。
• 句子要短、平实、能顺口说出来，不要符号。
• 永远给它一句“不知道时怎么说”的稳妥话。
