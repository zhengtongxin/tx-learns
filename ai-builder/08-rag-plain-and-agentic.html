Step 1 · Look it up, then answer第 1 步 · 先查，再答

Learn先学

A model only knows what it was trained on. It has never seen your price list. RAG (retrieval-augmented generation) fixes that: first retrieve the few passages that matter, then give them to the model and ask it to answer from them.

模型只知道训练时见过的东西，没见过你的价目表。RAG（检索增强生成）解决这个问题：先检索出最相关的几段，再交给模型，让它依据这些回答。

Your documents are cut into chunks, turned into number lists called embeddings that capture meaning, and stored in a vector store. A question is embedded the same way and matched to the closest chunks.

文档被切成块，转成捕捉语义的数字列表（叫嵌入），存进向量库。问题也用同样的方式转换，再去匹配最近的几块。

Step 2 · Let the agent search第 2 步 · 让智能体自己搜

Learn先学

Agentic RAG makes search a tool. The agent decides whether to search, what to search for, whether the result is good enough, and whether to search again with different words.

智能体式 RAG 把搜索变成一个工具。由智能体决定要不要搜、搜什么、结果够不够好、要不要换个说法再搜一次。

• Plain RAG: one fixed search per question. Fast, cheap, predictable.
• Agentic RAG: zero, one or several searches, possibly across different sources. Slower and dearer, but copes with vague or multi-part questions.

• 普通 RAG：每个问题固定搜一次。快、便宜、可预测。
• 智能体式 RAG：搜零次、一次或多次，还可能跨不同来源。更慢更贵，但能应付含糊或多部分的问题。

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · When RAG goes wrong第 3 步 · RAG 出错的时候

Learn先学

• Wrong chunk: the search returned something nearby, not the answer. Fix the chunking or the question.
• Missing chunk: the answer is not in your documents. The agent must say "I do not know", not invent one.
• Stale chunk: an old price list is still stored. Re-ingest when documents change.

• 块不对：搜到的是附近的内容，不是答案。改切块方式或改问法。
• 缺块：答案根本不在你的文档里。智能体必须说“我不知道”，不能编。
• 块过期：旧价目表还存着。文档变了就要重新入库。

Tell the model to answer only from the passages, and to say so when they are not enough.

要告诉模型：只能依据这些段落回答，不够时要直说。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• RAG means retrieve the right passages first, then answer from them.
• Documents become chunks, embeddings and a vector store.
• Plain RAG is one fixed search; agentic RAG lets the agent choose.
• Use agentic RAG for vague or multi-part questions, plain RAG for simple lookups.
• If the passages do not hold the answer, say so.

• RAG 就是先检索出对的段落，再依据它们回答。
• 文档变成块、嵌入，再存进向量库。
• 普通 RAG 固定搜一次；智能体式 RAG 由智能体自己选。
• 含糊或多部分的问题用智能体式，简单查询用普通 RAG。
• 段落里没有答案，就直说。
