Step 1 · Greedy by default第 1 步 · 默认是贪婪的

Learn先学

Quantifiers are greedy: they grab as much as they can. <.+> on <b>hi</b> matches the whole string, not just the first tag. Add ? to make it lazy, taking as little as possible: <.+?>. Greedy versus lazy is the most common regex surprise.

量词默认是贪婪的：能多吃就多吃。<.+> 作用在 <b>hi</b> 上会匹配整个字符串，而不只是第一个标签。加上 ? 变成懒惰匹配，尽可能少取：<.+?>。贪婪与懒惰是正则里最常见的意外。

import re s = "<b>hi</b>" print(re.findall(r"<.+>", s)) print(re.findall(r"<.+?>", s))

Step 2 · Regex for AI output第 2 步 · 用正则处理 AI 输出

Learn先学

Models often wrap what you want in chatter: “Sure! The answer is 42.” A pattern can pull out just the number, or the text between code fences. Always code the miss: if there is no match, retry or report it, never assume. And test the pattern on the odd cases (no number, two numbers, a negative) before you trust it.

模型常常把你要的东西包在闲聊里：“好的！答案是 42。”一个模式就能只取出那个数字，或取出代码围栏之间的文本。一定要处理“没匹配到”的情况：没有匹配就重试或报告，绝不要想当然。信任模式之前，先用怪情况测一测（没有数字、两个数字、负数）。

Step 3 · Know when to stop第 3 步 · 知道何时停手

Learn先学

Regex is for flat patterns. Nested or recursive formats (HTML, JSON, code) need a real parser, such as json.loads. A giant pattern nobody can read is also a sign to stop: split the job into smaller steps. Decide for each task.

正则适合扁平的模式。嵌套或递归的格式（HTML、JSON、代码）需要真正的解析器，比如 json.loads。一个没人看得懂的巨大模式也是该停手的信号：把任务拆成更小的步骤。为每个任务做判断。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Quantifiers are greedy; add ? to make them lazy.
• Always code the no-match case and test odd inputs.
• Regex for flat patterns, a parser for nested formats.

• 量词是贪婪的；加 ? 变成懒惰。
• 永远要处理没有匹配的情况，并测试怪输入。
• 正则用于扁平模式，嵌套格式用解析器。
