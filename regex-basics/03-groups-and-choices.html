Step 1 · Parentheses group第 1 步 · 括号分组

Learn先学

Parentheses do two jobs. They group pieces so a quantifier covers all of them: (ha)+ matches “ha”, “haha”, “hahaha”. And they capture what matched, so you can pull a part out afterwards: in (\d+)-(\d+) the two number runs become group 1 and group 2.

括号有两个用途。一是分组，让量词作用于整组：(ha)+ 匹配“ha”“haha”“hahaha”。二是捕获匹配到的内容，之后可以取出其中一部分：在 (\d+)-(\d+) 里，两段数字分别是第 1 组和第 2 组。

import re m = re.search(r"(\d+)-(\d+)", "pages 12-48") print(m.group(1), m.group(2))

Step 2 · Either or第 2 步 · 二选一

Learn先学

A vertical bar | means “or”: cat|dog matches either word. Put it inside parentheses to limit its reach: gr(a|e)y matches “gray” and “grey”, while gra|ey would mean “gra” or “ey”.

竖线 | 表示“或”：cat|dog 两个词都能匹配。放进括号里可以限定它的范围：gr(a|e)y 匹配“gray”和“grey”，而 gra|ey 的意思是“gra”或“ey”。
