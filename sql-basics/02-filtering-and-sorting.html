Filtering and sorting筛选与排序

Keep only the rows you want with WHERE and put them in order with ORDER BY · ~10 min · reading and practice用 WHERE 只留下想要的行，用 ORDER BY 给它们排序 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · WHERE第 1 步 · WHERE

Learn先学

WHERE keeps only the rows that make a condition true: WHERE price > 4. Comparisons are =, <> (not equal), <, >, <=, >=. Text goes in single quotes: WHERE kind = 'food'. LIKE 'c%' matches text starting with c.

WHERE 只保留让条件为真的行：WHERE price > 4。比较符有 =、<>（不等于）、<、>、<=、>=。文本要放在单引号里：WHERE kind = 'food'。LIKE 'c%' 匹配以 c 开头的文本。

Step 2 · AND, OR and ORDER BY第 2 步 · AND、OR 与 ORDER BY

Learn先学

AND needs both conditions, OR needs either. Use brackets when you mix them. ORDER BY price sorts from low to high, and ORDER BY price DESC from high to low. Without ORDER BY the database promises no particular order.

AND 要求两个条件都满足，OR 满足其一即可。混用时加括号。ORDER BY price 从小到大排，ORDER BY price DESC 从大到小排。不写 ORDER BY，数据库不保证任何顺序。

SELECT name, price FROM menu WHERE kind = 'drink' AND price < 4.5 ORDER BY price DESC;

Takeaways要点回顾

• WHERE keeps rows that make a condition true.
• AND needs both, OR needs either.
• ORDER BY sorts; DESC reverses it.

• WHERE 保留让条件为真的行。
• AND 要两者都满足，OR 满足其一即可。
• ORDER BY 排序，DESC 反过来。
