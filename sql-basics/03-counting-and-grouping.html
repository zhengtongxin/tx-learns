Counting and grouping计数与分组

Summarise many rows into one answer with COUNT, SUM and AVG, and split the answer by group · ~10 min · reading and practice用 COUNT、SUM、AVG 把很多行汇总成一个答案，并按组拆开 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One answer from many rows第 1 步 · 多行变一个答案

Learn先学

An aggregate function squeezes many rows into one value: COUNT(*) counts rows, SUM(price) adds, AVG(price) averages, MIN and MAX find the smallest and largest. Put a WHERE first to summarise only some rows.

聚合函数把很多行压成一个值：COUNT(*) 数行数，SUM(price) 求和，AVG(price) 求平均，MIN 和 MAX 找最小和最大。先加一个 WHERE，就只汇总其中一部分行。

Step 2 · GROUP BY第 2 步 · GROUP BY

Learn先学

GROUP BY kind splits the rows into groups with the same kind and gives each group its own summary: SELECT kind, COUNT(*) FROM menu GROUP BY kind. HAVING filters the groups after they are summed, the way WHERE filters rows before.

GROUP BY kind 把 kind 相同的行分成一组，并给每组各自汇总：SELECT kind, COUNT(*) FROM menu GROUP BY kind。HAVING 在汇总之后筛选分组，就像 WHERE 在汇总之前筛选行。

SELECT kind, COUNT(*), AVG(price) FROM menu GROUP BY kind HAVING COUNT(*) > 2;

Takeaways要点回顾

• COUNT, SUM, AVG, MIN and MAX turn many rows into one value.
• GROUP BY gives each group its own summary.
• WHERE filters rows, HAVING filters groups.

• COUNT、SUM、AVG、MIN、MAX 把很多行变成一个值。
• GROUP BY 让每组各有各的汇总。
• WHERE 筛选行，HAVING 筛选分组。
