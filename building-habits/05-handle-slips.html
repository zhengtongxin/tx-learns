Keeping it going · Lesson 2坚持下去 · 第 2 课

Handle slips应对中断

Plan for the days you miss, so one slip does not become a stop, with a rule of never missing twice · ~10 min · reading and practice为漏掉的日子做好准备，让一次中断不变成放弃，规则是不连续漏两次 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Slips are normal第 1 步 · 中断很正常

Learn先学

Everyone misses days: illness, travel, a very busy week. A slip is not a verdict on you, it is information about a day your plan did not fit. Decide in advance that slips will happen, so that when one does, it is not a surprise.

人人都会漏掉几天：生病、出差、特别忙的一周。中断不是对你的判决，而是“这一天计划没配上”的信息。提前就决定“中断会发生”，真发生时就不会觉得意外。

Step 2 · Never miss twice第 2 步 · 不连续漏两次

Learn先学

One missed day barely changes anything. Two in a row starts a new habit: not doing it. Make a rule that after a miss, the next day you do at least the tiny version, even one page. This keeps your run of days in a row from breaking.

漏一天几乎不影响什么。连漏两天，就开始养成新的习惯：不做。立一条规矩：漏了之后，第二天至少做小版本，哪怕一页。这样你连续的天数就不会断。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Restart kindly第 3 步 · 善意地重新开始

Learn先学

Guilt makes people avoid the habit that reminds them of it. When you slip, skip the speech and restart with the tiny version. Note what got in the way, change one thing in the plan if needed, and move on.

内疚会让人躲开那个提醒他们的习惯。中断了，别自责，用小版本重新开始。记下是什么挡了路，必要时改计划里的一点，然后继续。

Missed Tuesday (travel). Rule: never miss twice. Wednesday: tiny version only, one page. Note: on travel days, read one page on the phone.
