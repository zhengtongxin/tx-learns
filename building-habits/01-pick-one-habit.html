Starting a habit · Lesson 1开始一个习惯 · 第 1 课

Pick one habit选一个习惯

Choose a single habit that matters to you, say why it matters and make it specific enough to know when you have done it · ~10 min · reading and practice选一个对你重要的习惯，说清为什么重要，并具体到能判断做没做 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One at a time第 1 步 · 一次只选一个

Learn先学

Most people start by promising to change everything at once: exercise, sleep, study and eat better, all from Monday. Each habit takes attention, and attention runs out. Pick one habit, build it until it feels ordinary, and only then add another.

大多数人一上来就想一口气改变所有事：运动、睡觉、学习、饮食，都从周一开始。每个习惯都要花注意力，而注意力是有限的。先选一个，养到觉得平常了，再加下一个。

Step 2 · Your reason第 2 步 · 你的理由

Learn先学

A habit that you do only because you should is easy to drop. Ask yourself why this one matters to you, in one sentence, such as “I want to understand what I study before the exam”. A reason you believe in carries you through the days when you do not feel like it.

只因为“应该”才做的习惯很容易放弃。用一句话问自己：这个习惯为什么对我重要？比如“我想在考试前真正弄懂学过的内容”。一个你自己相信的理由，会在你不想做的日子里推着你走。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Make it specific第 3 步 · 写得具体

Learn先学

“Study more” cannot be checked. “Review my notes for ten minutes” can: at the end of the day it is either done or not done. Write the habit as an action with an amount, such as ten minutes or one page, so you never have to argue with yourself about whether it counted.

“多学习”没法检查，“复习笔记十分钟”可以：一天结束时，要么做了，要么没做。把习惯写成有分量的动作，比如十分钟或一页，就不必再和自己争论算不算数。

Habit: review my notes for 10 minutes. Why: I want to understand the lessons before the exam. Done today? yes / no One habit only. Next one: not before it feels ordinary.
