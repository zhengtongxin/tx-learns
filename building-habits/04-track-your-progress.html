Keeping it going · Lesson 1坚持下去 · 第 1 课

Track your progress记录进度

Mark each day you do the habit and read the record kindly, so you can see the pattern without being harsh on yourself · ~10 min · reading and practice每天做了就标一下，并善意地读这份记录，看出规律而不苛责自己 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A record you will keep第 1 步 · 能坚持的记录

Learn先学

Use the simplest tool you have: a paper calendar and a pen, or a note on your phone. Put a mark, an x or a tick, on each day you do the habit. If the record takes more effort than the habit, you will stop keeping it, so keep it to a few seconds.

用你手边最简单的工具：纸质日历加一支笔，或手机里的一条备忘。每天做了习惯就标一个 x 或勾。如果记录比习惯还费劲，你就会不记了，所以让它只花几秒。

Step 2 · Days done out of days possible第 2 步 · 做了几天，共几天

Learn先学

Count what you did against what was possible: 5 of 7 days is a good week, even though it is not perfect. A count shows progress that “I failed twice” hides. Watch the trend over several weeks rather than any single day.

用“做了的”对比“可能做的”来数：七天里做了五天是不错的一周，尽管不完美。数字能显示“我失败了两次”所掩盖的进步。看几周的趋势，而不是某一天。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Look for patterns第 3 步 · 找规律

Learn先学

Once a week, read the record and ask what the gaps have in common. Maybe the missed days are all Fridays, or all days with an early meeting. A pattern tells you what to change in the plan, so you fix the setup instead of blaming yourself.

每周读一次记录，问问缺的那几天有什么共同点。也许漏掉的都是周五，或者都是有早会的日子。规律告诉你该改计划的哪里，让你修的是安排，而不是责怪自己。

x = done, - = not done Mon x Tue x Wed x Thu - Fri - Sat x Sun x Done: 5 of 7 days. Gaps: Thursday and Friday, both early-meeting days. Change: do the page before the meeting, not after.
