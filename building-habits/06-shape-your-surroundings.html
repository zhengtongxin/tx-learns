Keeping it going · Lesson 3坚持下去 · 第 3 课

Shape your surroundings改造环境

Make the habit easy and the distractions harder by changing your space and your phone, so you rely less on willpower · ~10 min · reading and practice让习惯变容易、让干扰变难：调整你的空间和手机，少靠意志力 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Make it easy第 1 步 · 让它容易

Learn先学

Remove steps between you and the habit, and keep the room tidy enough that nothing else is in the way. Leave the notes open on the desk, put the book on the pillow, lay out the running shoes. Every small step you take away makes starting a little more likely, especially when you are tired.

把你和习惯之间的步骤去掉，房间也收拾到没有别的东西挡路。笔记摊开在桌上，书放在枕头上，跑鞋摆在门口。你去掉的每一小步，都让开始多一点可能，尤其是累的时候。

Step 2 · Make the distraction hard第 2 步 · 让干扰变难

Learn先学

Do the reverse for what pulls you away, which means adding friction, a few extra steps in the way: move the game off the home screen, charge the phone in another room, log out of the feed. A few extra steps are often enough to turn an automatic scroll into a choice you can say no to.

对拉你走的东西反着来，也就是增加阻力，在路上多加几步：把游戏移出主屏幕，让手机在另一个房间充电，退出信息流的登录。多几步往往就能把自动的刷屏变成一个你可以拒绝的选择。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Tell someone第 3 步 · 告诉一个人

Learn先学

Tell a friend what you are building and what the tiny version is. You do not need to be policed, only seen: a short message at the end of the week is enough. Choose someone kind who will ask how it went, not whether you failed.

告诉一位朋友你在养什么习惯、小版本是什么。你不需要被监督，只需要被看见：周末发一条短消息就够了。选一个善意的人，他会问“怎么样”，而不是“是不是失败了”。

Make it easy: notes open on the desk the night before. Make distraction hard: phone charges in the hall. Tell someone: message Mei on Sunday, "5 of 7 this week".
