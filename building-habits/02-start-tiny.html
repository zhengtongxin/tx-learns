Starting a habit · Lesson 2开始一个习惯 · 第 2 课

Start tiny从小处开始

Shrink a habit until it takes two minutes or less, so starting is easy even on a bad day, then let it grow · ~10 min · reading and practice把习惯缩小到两分钟以内，让你在糟糕的日子也能开始，然后再慢慢变大 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The smallest version第 1 步 · 最小版本

Learn先学

Take your habit and ask: what is the smallest version I would do even when tired? “Review notes for ten minutes” becomes “open my notes and read one page”. Aim for two minutes or less. If the small version feels almost silly, it is about the right size.

拿出你的习惯，问自己：累的时候我也肯做的最小版本是什么？“复习笔记十分钟”变成“打开笔记读一页”。目标是两分钟以内。如果小版本小到有点可笑，那大小就差不多了。

Step 2 · Why tiny works第 2 步 · 为什么小有效

Learn先学

Most of the effort in a habit is starting. Once the notes are open, reading a second page is easy. A tiny version removes the biggest obstacle, which is the decision to begin, and it keeps your run of days in a row going on days when a full version would be skipped.

习惯里最费力的往往是开始。笔记一旦打开，再读第二页就容易了。小版本去掉了最大的障碍，也就是“开始”的那个决定，让你在做不了完整版的日子里也能连着做下去。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Let it grow第 3 步 · 让它慢慢变大

Learn先学

Once the tiny version feels easy for a couple of weeks, add a little: two pages, then three. Grow in small steps and let a bad week shrink it back without guilt. The goal is a habit that survives, not a heroic start.

小版本轻松坚持了几周后，再加一点：两页，然后三页。步子要小，糟糕的一周可以缩回去，不必内疚。目标是一个能活下来的习惯，而不是英雄式的开头。

Habit: review my notes. Full version: 10 minutes. Tiny version: open my notes and read one page. First two weeks: one page. Third week: two pages. Later: three pages. After a bad week: back to one page.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Shrink it to two minutes.
• Start is the hard part.
• Grow slowly and shrink without guilt.

• 缩到两分钟。
• 最难的是开始。
• 慢慢变大，缩回去不内疚。
