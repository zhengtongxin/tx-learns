Starting a habit · Lesson 3开始一个习惯 · 第 3 课

Cue and routine提示与流程

Tie your habit to something you already do, with a clear cue, so you do not have to remember or decide each time · ~10 min · reading and practice把习惯接在你已经在做的事后面，用清楚的提示触发，不必每次靠记忆或决定 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A cue that already exists第 1 步 · 已有的提示

Learn先学

Habits that depend on remembering tend to fail, and “sometime today” is the usual way they get lost. A cue is something that already happens every day, such as pouring your morning drink or sitting at your desk. Link the habit to it, so the cue reminds you instead of your memory having to.

靠记忆的习惯容易失败，“今天找时间”就是它们最常见的走丢方式。提示是每天本来就会发生的事，比如倒早上的饮料、坐到书桌前。把习惯和它连起来，让提示来提醒你，而不是靠记性。

Step 2 · After X, I do Y第 2 步 · 做完 X 之后，我做 Y

Learn先学

Write the plan as one sentence: “After I pour my coffee, I open my notes and read one page.” The cue is clear, the action is small and there is nothing left to decide. The more exact the cue, the less you rely on motivation.

把计划写成一句话：“倒完咖啡之后，我打开笔记读一页。”提示清楚，动作很小，没什么要再决定的。提示越具体，你越不用依赖干劲。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Stack habits第 3 步 · 把习惯叠起来

Learn先学

Once a habit is steady, it can become the cue for the next: after I read one page, I write one line about it. Add one link at a time. A chain here means habits that follow each other in a row. One built slowly holds; one built in a week usually snaps.

习惯稳定之后，它可以成为下一个习惯的提示：读完一页之后，我写一句总结。一次只加一环。这里的“链”指一个接一个的习惯。慢慢搭起的结实，一周内搭起的通常会断。

After I pour my coffee, I open my notes and read one page. Cue: pouring coffee (happens every day). Action: one page (tiny). Later: after the page, I write one line about it.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Use a cue that already exists.
• Write “after X, I do Y”.
• Add one link at a time.

• 用已有的提示。
• 写成“做完 X 之后，我做 Y”。
• 一次加一环。
