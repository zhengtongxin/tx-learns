# Story for the AI Production track

Setting: Pasir Panjang Wharf 7, a busy container terminal on Singapore's southern coast, seen from a small IT office above the gantry cranes. (AI Agentic uses the Singapore kopitiam and AI Engineer the heartland HDB estate; do not mix them.)

Cast:
- Mei Ling: the builder. A junior IT officer at the wharf who teaches herself to put AI tools in front of real people.
- Mr Ong: the wharf supervisor; watches every dollar (budgets, cost alarms, "who approved this?").
- Kumar: the crane operator; likes things repeatable and automatic (scripts, pipelines, one button).
- Sergeant Lee: the port security officer at the gate; passes and permissions (sign-in, IAM, keys).
- Auntie Siti: runs the canteen on the wharf; the friendly first customer who tests every screen.

Props: shipping containers (Docker), the gate pass check, the harbour control tower (monitoring), the berth plan pinned to the wall (infrastructure as code), the night shift handover book (memory), the conveyor belt (CI/CD), the port invoice (cloud bill).

Rule: each lesson ends with one short "At the wharf" callout (.callout tip, EN and 中文) just before the takeaways, tying its idea to the terminal.

## One thread: the wharf product

Mei Ling builds one product across the track: a small AI service for the wharf, starting as one page and ending as a deployed, tested, agent-powered product. Constraint: Mr Ong's budget and Sergeant Lee's permissions. Success: it runs live, costs what was planned, and redeploys from one push.

- Arc by topic: (1) a first live page, its two halves and streaming answers; (2) sign-in and paid features, then the consultation notes assistant; (3) a safe cloud account, containers, serverless, the product on serverless; (4) the twin with memory, personality and a live deploy; (5) managed models, picked by cost and speed; (6) the same stack as code (state, environments, scripts); (7) deploys on every push to dev, test, prod; (8) the agent as a managed service that plans and checks itself.
- Every lesson: a **Where we left off** box after the hero ("Last time the product could ___. Today it fails when ___. We fix it by ___.") and a **What changed** box replacing "At the wharf" ("Now the product can ___. The next problem is ___.").
