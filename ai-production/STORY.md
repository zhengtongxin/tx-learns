# Story for the AI Production track

Setting: Pasir Panjang Wharf 7, a busy container terminal on Singapore's southern coast, seen from a small IT office above the gantry cranes. (Agentic AI uses the Penang kopitiam and AI Engineer the heartland HDB estate; do not mix them.)

Cast:
- Mei Ling: the builder. A junior IT officer at the wharf who teaches herself to put AI tools in front of real people.
- Mr Ong: the wharf supervisor; watches every dollar (budgets, cost alarms, "who approved this?").
- Kumar: the crane operator; likes things repeatable and automatic (scripts, pipelines, one button).
- Sergeant Lee: the port security officer at the gate; passes and permissions (sign-in, IAM, keys).
- Auntie Siti: runs the canteen on the wharf; the friendly first customer who tests every screen.

Props: shipping containers (Docker), the gate pass check, the harbour control tower (monitoring), the berth plan pinned to the wall (infrastructure as code), the night shift handover book (memory), the conveyor belt (CI/CD), the port invoice (cloud bill).

Rule: each lesson ends with one short "At the wharf" callout (.callout tip, EN and 中文) just before the takeaways, tying its idea to the terminal.
