Building the base · Lesson 3打好基础 · 第 3 课

Grammar in use用中学语法

Learn patterns from examples first, make your own sentences and treat mistakes as useful information · ~10 min · reading and practice先从例子里学句型，自己造句，并把错误当作有用的信息 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Patterns from examples第 1 步 · 从例子里学句型

Learn先学

Meet a pattern in several real sentences before you study the rule. When you have seen “I want to eat”, “I want to go” and “I want to learn”, the rule “want to plus a verb” (a verb is an action word, such as eat, go or learn) is easy to understand. The examples are in English, but the same steps work for any language. Rules are a way to notice patterns, not a replacement for them.

先在几个真实的句子里遇到某个句型，再去研究规则。当你见过“I want to eat”“I want to go”“I want to learn”之后，“want to 加动词”（动词就是表示动作的词，比如 eat、go、learn）这条规则就很容易理解。例子用的是英语，但同样的步骤适用于任何语言。规则是帮助你发现句型的方法，而不是句型的替代品。

Step 2 · Make your own第 2 步 · 自己造句

Learn先学

Take a pattern and write three sentences about your own life. Using it yourself is what turns recognising into using. Start with the most common patterns, such as asking, wanting and past events, and leave rare exceptions until later.

拿一个句型，写三个关于你自己生活的句子。亲自使用，才能把“认得”变成“会用”。先学最常见的句型，比如提问、表达想要、讲过去的事，生僻的例外留到以后。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Mistakes are information第 3 步 · 错误是信息

Learn先学

A mistake shows exactly where your understanding is thin. Keep a short list of your own repeated mistakes and fix one at a time. Ask a teacher or a speaker to correct you, and keep going when you are not sure; waiting until you are perfect means never starting.

一个错误准确指出你的理解薄弱在哪里。记下你自己反复犯的错误，一次改正一个。请老师或母语者纠正你，拿不准时也要继续说；等到完美才开口，就等于永远不开始。

Pattern: I want to + verb Examples: I want to eat. I want to go. I want to learn. Mine: I want to ______ . (write three about your own life)
