Learning from corrections从纠正中学习

Welcome corrections, keep a short list of mistakes that repeat and use each corrected form again in your own words · ~9 min · reading and practice欢迎被纠正，记一份反复出现的错误的短清单，并用自己的话再用一次纠正后的形式 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Welcome corrections第 1 步 · 欢迎被纠正

Learn先学

When someone corrects you, it is a gift: it shows exactly where your language differs from theirs. Mistakes mean you are trying things, and a learner who never makes mistakes is probably not speaking much. Say thanks, repeat the corrected phrase once, and carry on.

有人纠正你，是一份礼物：它准确地告诉你，你的说法和对方的不同在哪里。犯错说明你在尝试，从不犯错的学习者很可能说得不多。说声谢谢，把纠正后的说法重复一遍，然后继续。

Step 2 · Keep a short list第 2 步 · 记一份短清单

Learn先学

After a conversation or a piece of writing, note the corrections that came up. Say 8 sentences were written and 2 were corrected: 2 ÷ 8 = 25%. Do not copy every correction. Keep a list of only the repeat mistakes, maybe 3, and read them for 2 minutes a day. Three items at 2 minutes is a small load that you can actually keep up.

一次对话或一段写作之后，记下出现过的纠正。假设写了 8 个句子，被纠正了 2 个：2 ÷ 8 = 25%。不用抄下每一条纠正，只记反复出现的错误，大约 3 条，每天读 2 分钟。三条、每天 2 分钟，是一个你真能坚持的小负担。

Step 3 · Use it again第 3 步 · 再用一次

Learn先学

Reading a correction is not enough. Within a day or two, say or write a new sentence that uses the corrected form. Using it once in your own words moves it from something you read to something you can say. Check the list again after a week: a mistake that no longer appears can leave the list.

光读纠正是不够的。一两天内，用纠正后的形式说或写一个新句子。用自己的话用一次，才能把它从“读过的东西”变成“能说出来的东西”。一周后再看一遍清单：不再出现的错误就可以从清单里划掉。

To finish Step 3, sort each habit as good or weak: tap a habit, then tap its group.

完成第 3 步：把每个做法分为好或弱。先点一个做法，再点它所属的组。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A correction is useful: it shows where your language differs.
• Say thanks, repeat the corrected phrase and carry on.
• Keep a short list of repeat mistakes, about 3, and read it for 2 minutes a day.
• In the example, 2 of 8 sentences corrected is 25%.
• Use the corrected form in a new sentence within a day or two.

• 纠正很有用：它指出你的说法在哪里不同。
• 道谢，重复纠正后的说法，然后继续。
• 记一份反复出现的错误的短清单，大约 3 条，每天读 2 分钟。
• 例子里 8 个句子中 2 个被纠正是 25%。
• 一两天内用纠正后的形式造一个新句子。
