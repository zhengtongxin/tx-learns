Words that stick记得住的词汇

Learn words inside phrases, test yourself instead of rereading and review them on a spaced schedule · ~10 min · reading and practice在短语里学词，用自测代替重读，并按间隔安排复习 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Words travel in phrases第 1 步 · 词语成串出现

Learn先学

A word on its own is easy to forget and hard to use. Learn it inside a short phrase or sentence, so you also learn what it goes with and when people say it. Start with the common words: a small set of everyday words covers most of what you will meet.

单独的一个词既容易忘，也不好用。把它放在短语或句子里学，你就同时学到了它的搭配和使用场合。先学常用词：一小批日常词汇就覆盖了你会遇到的大部分内容。

Step 2 · Recall beats rereading第 2 步 · 回忆胜过重读

Learn先学

Looking at a word list again feels productive but teaches little. Cover the meaning and try to recall it, then check. The effort of pulling a word out of memory is what makes it stick. Wrong answers are fine: they show what to review.

再看一遍词汇表感觉很有效，其实学不到多少。盖住意思，先尝试回忆，再核对。把一个词从记忆里“拉”出来的这份努力，才让它记得牢。答错没关系：它指出了该复习什么。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Space the reviews第 3 步 · 把复习隔开

Learn先学

Review a word just before you would forget it, and leave longer gaps each time it goes well. A simple schedule is one day, three days, then seven, then longer. This is the same spacing the study coach on this site uses to schedule reviews, so you can use it for any language material.

在快忘记之前复习一个词，每次顺利就把间隔拉长。一个简单的安排是：一天后、三天后、再七天后，之后更久。本站的学习教练安排复习用的也是这种间隔，所以你可以把它用在任何语言材料上。

Learn a new word: today First review: 1 day later Second review: 3 days later Third review: 7 days later Then leave longer gaps.
