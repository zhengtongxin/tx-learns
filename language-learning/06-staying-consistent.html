Staying consistent坚持下去

Plan small, regular sessions, tie them to a habit, mix the skills and keep a simple record of progress · ~10 min · reading and practice安排小而规律的练习，把它绑定在习惯上，混合各项技能，并简单记录进步 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Small and regular第 1 步 · 小而规律

Learn先学

Short sessions on most days beat one long session now and then, because memory needs repeated contact and small sessions are easier to keep. Twenty minutes you actually do is worth more than two hours you plan and skip. Pick an amount you could do even on a bad day.

大多数日子里的短时练习，胜过偶尔一次的长时间学习，因为记忆需要反复接触，小段练习也更容易坚持。你真正做到的二十分钟，比你计划却没做的两小时有价值得多。选一个即使状态差的日子也能做到的量。

Step 2 · Tie it to a habit and mix it up第 2 步 · 绑在习惯上，并且换着练

Learn先学

Attach practice to something you already do, such as a clip on the commute or five words with your morning drink. Mix listening, speaking, reading and writing across the week so that no skill is left behind, and rest when you need to; one missed session is not a failure.

把练习绑在你已有的习惯上，比如通勤时听一段、早上喝饮料时学五个词。一周内把听、说、读、写交替安排，不让任何一项落下，需要时就休息；错过一次练习不算失败。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Track simply第 3 步 · 简单记录

Learn先学

Keep a plain log: the date, what you did and one line on what was hard, such as “12 March: 20 minutes of listening; the fast clip was hard”. Look back once a month and notice what changed, such as a clip you now follow. Seeing your own progress is the best reason to carry on.

做一份朴素的记录：日期、做了什么，以及一行写下哪里难，比如“3 月 12 日：听了 20 分钟；那段快的很难”。每个月回看一次，留意发生了什么变化，比如你现在能听懂的某个片段。看到自己的进步，是继续下去最好的理由。

Plan: 20 minutes a day on 5 days = 100 minutes a week. One 100-minute session once a week is also 100 minutes, but with six days between contacts.
