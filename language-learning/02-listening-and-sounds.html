Listening and sounds听力与发音

Train your ear with short clips you mostly understand, notice new sounds and repeat after the speaker · ~10 min · reading and practice用大致听得懂的短片段训练耳朵，注意新的发音，并跟着说话人重复 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Easy to follow, a little hard第 1 步 · 好懂，又略有挑战

Learn先学

Choose audio (podcasts for learners, short videos, recordings from a textbook) where you understand most of what is said, so you can follow the story and pick up the rest from context. Material that is all noise teaches little. Short clips are best, because you can replay them until the details appear.

选择你大部分听得懂的音频（学习者播客、短视频、教材配套录音），这样你能跟上内容，并从语境里猜出其余部分。全是噪音的材料学不到什么。短片段最合适，因为你可以反复播放，直到细节浮现。

Step 2 · Notice the sounds第 2 步 · 留意发音

Learn先学

Every language has sounds, rhythm and stress that your own language lacks, and your ear will skip them at first. Listen for pairs of words that sound almost alike (in English, “ship” and “sheep”) and say them aloud slowly. Repeating right after the speaker, called shadowing, trains your mouth and ear together.

每种语言都有你母语里没有的发音、节奏和重音，你的耳朵起初会略过它们。留意听起来几乎一样的词对（英语里比如 ship 和 sheep），并慢慢大声说出来。紧跟说话人重复，也叫“跟读”，能同时训练嘴和耳朵。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · A clip routine第 3 步 · 片段练习流程

Learn先学

Use a short routine: listen once with no text, listen with the text, repeat aloud, then listen again with no text. The last pass shows what you now catch. Use subtitles as a step, not a crutch.

用一个简短的流程：先不看文字听一遍，再对着文字听，然后大声重复，最后不看文字再听一遍。最后一遍能显示你现在抓到了多少。字幕只是其中一步，不是拐杖。

1. Listen once with no text. 2. Listen with the transcript or subtitles. 3. Repeat aloud, right after the speaker. 4. Listen again with no text.

Takeaways要点回顾
