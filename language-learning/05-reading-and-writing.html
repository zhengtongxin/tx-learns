Reading and writing阅读与写作

Read material you enjoy and mostly understand, look up only the words that matter and write short notes to get corrected · ~10 min · reading and practice读你喜欢又大致读得懂的材料，只查要紧的词，并写简短的文字请人纠正 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Read what you enjoy第 1 步 · 读你喜欢的

Learn先学

Choose texts about things you care about and in which you understand most of the words, such as short stories, news for learners or messages from friends. Reading a lot of easy text builds words and patterns steadily. A page where you understand most of it teaches more than one that needs a dictionary every line.

选你关心的主题、并且大部分词你都认识的文本，比如短篇故事、面向学习者的新闻或朋友的消息。大量阅读容易的文本，能稳步积累词汇和句型。大部分读得懂的一页，比每行都要查词典的一页更有收获。

Step 2 · Look up less第 2 步 · 少查一点

Learn先学

Do not stop for every unknown word. Guess from context, and look up only the words that block the meaning or that keep coming back. Keep a short list of those and review them with the spaced method from Words that stick.

不要每个生词都停下来查。先根据上下文猜，只查那些妨碍理解、或反复出现的词。把这些词记成一份简短的清单，并用“记得住的词汇”那一课里的间隔法复习。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Write short and get corrected第 3 步 · 写短文，请人纠正

Learn先学

Write a few lines each time: what you did, what you plan or what you think. Short and regular beats long and rare. Ask a teacher, a partner or a tool to point out mistakes, then rewrite the corrected lines once so you remember them.

每次写几行：你做了什么、打算做什么、想到了什么。短而规律，胜过长而稀少。请老师、伙伴或工具指出错误，然后把改过的句子自己重写一遍，这样才记得住。

A page has 100 words and 5 are new to you. You know 95 of 100 words: 95%. Guess most from context; look up only the few that block meaning.
