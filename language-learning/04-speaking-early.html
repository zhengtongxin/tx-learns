Speaking early尽早开口

Start speaking while your language is still small: use a short script, find a partner and record yourself · ~10 min · reading and practice趁你的语言还很少时就开口：用一段简短的话、找个练习对象，并录下自己 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Start before you feel ready第 1 步 · 不必等到觉得准备好

Learn先学

Speaking is a skill you only build by speaking. Start with the few sentences you will use most, even if they are simple. A small language used often beats a large one kept in your head. Mistakes are the normal price of practice.

口语是一项只有靠开口才能练出来的技能。从你最常用的几句话开始，哪怕很简单。常用的一点点语言，胜过只存在脑子里的一大堆语言。错误是练习的正常代价。

Step 2 · A small script and a partner第 2 步 · 一小段话和一个伙伴

Learn先学

Prepare a short script you can say almost without thinking: greet, say who you are, ask a question and thank them. Then find someone to use it with, such as a teacher, a language partner or a patient friend. If you do not know a word, describe it with simpler words and keep going.

准备一小段你几乎不用想就能说出口的话：问候、介绍自己、提一个问题、道谢。然后找人一起用它，比如老师、语言伙伴或有耐心的朋友。遇到不会的词，就用更简单的话描述，继续往下说。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Record and listen第 3 步 · 录下来再听

Learn先学

Record yourself saying the script and listen back. You will hear gaps you cannot notice while talking: a missing word, a sound that is off, a long pause. Compare it with a recording of a speaker, then try again and keep the better version.

把自己说这段话录下来，再听回放。你会听到说话时察觉不到的问题：漏掉的词、不准的发音、很长的停顿。拿它和母语者的录音对比，再试一次，留下更好的那一版。

1. Greet. 2. Say who you are. 3. Ask a question. 4. Thank them.

More practice再练一练
