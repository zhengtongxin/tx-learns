Paying attention · Lesson 1专心听 · 第 1 课

Be present全神贯注

Give the speaker your full attention for a few minutes, because people can tell when you are half there · ~10 min · reading and practice给说话的人几分钟的全部注意力，因为人们看得出你只来了一半 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Clear the distractions第 1 步 · 清掉干扰

Learn先学

Put your phone face down, close the laptop lid, turn to the person. Half-listening while you type is still half-listening, and people can tell. If you truly cannot give your attention now, say so and set a time: “I want to hear this properly. Can we talk at three?”

把手机扣在桌上，合上笔记本电脑，转向对方。一边打字一边听，还是只听了一半，人家看得出来。如果你现在真的无法专心，就直说并约个时间：“我想好好听你说，我们三点谈可以吗？”

Step 2 · Show you are listening第 2 步 · 表现出你在听

Learn先学

Face the speaker, keep your eyes on them without staring, and nod or say a small “mm” now and then. These small signals tell the speaker to keep going. Do not fake them: they work because you are really following what is said.

面向说话的人，目光落在对方身上但不盯着看，偶尔点头或“嗯”一声。这些小信号告诉对方可以继续讲。别装：它们有用，是因为你真的在跟着内容走。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Come back when you drift第 3 步 · 走神了就回来

Learn先学

Everyone’s mind wanders, especially while someone else talks, because we think faster than they speak. When you notice it, do not blame yourself. Return to their last sentence. If you lost the thread, say so: “Sorry, I drifted. Could you say that last part again?”

每个人都会走神，别人说话时尤其如此，因为我们思考得比对方说话快。发觉走神时，不必自责，回到对方的上一句话。如果跟丢了，就直说：“不好意思，我走神了，最后那部分能再说一遍吗？”

Before the talk: phone face down, laptop closed. During: eyes on them, a small nod. If I drift: return to their last sentence. If I lost the thread: “Could you say that last part again?”

Takeaways要点回顾

• Clear distractions first.
• Show you follow, for real.
• Return when you drift.

• 先清掉干扰。
• 真实地表明你在跟。
• 走神就回来。
