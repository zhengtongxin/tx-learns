Paying attention · Lesson 3专心听 · 第 3 课

Hold your reply先别急着回应

Pause before you answer, so you do not interrupt, fix or compare when the other person only needs to be heard · ~10 min · reading and practice回答之前先停一停，别打断、别急着解决、别急着比较，对方可能只是需要被听见 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Let them finish第 1 步 · 让他们说完

Learn先学

Interrupting, even to agree or to help, tells people their thought was not worth finishing. Wait until they stop. If you are afraid you will forget your point, jot a word down. Most of the time, what they say next changes what you wanted to say.

打断别人，即使是为了附和或帮忙，也等于说他们的想法不值得讲完。等他们停下来。如果怕忘了自己的观点，就记下一个词。多数时候，他们接下来说的话，会改变你本来要说的。

Step 2 · Do not rush to fix第 2 步 · 别急着解决

Learn先学

When someone shares a problem, the urge is to give advice at once. But often they want to be heard first, and your quick fix says “your feelings are small, here is the answer”. Ask: “Do you want ideas, or do you want me to listen?” Then do that.

别人讲到问题时，人总想立刻给建议。可是他们常常先要的是被听见，而你的快速办法等于在说“你的感受不重要，答案在这”。可以问：“你想要建议，还是想让我听你说？”然后照做。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Leave a pause第 3 步 · 留一小段停顿

Learn先学

After they stop, count a slow two before you reply. The pause lets you think, and often they add the most important part in the silence. Silence feels long to you and short to them. Do not fill it just because it feels uncomfortable.

对方停下来后，慢慢数两下再回答。停顿让你思考，而他们往往会在沉默里补上最重要的部分。沉默对你来说很长，对他们来说很短。不要只因为尴尬就去填满它。

1 Let them finish. Jot a word if I might forget. 2 Ask: ideas, or just listening? 3 Count a slow two before I reply. 4 If I interrupted: “Sorry, please go on.”
