Showing you heard · Lesson 1让对方知道你听到了 · 第 1 课

Say it back把话说回去

Repeat what you heard in your own words, so the speaker knows you understood and can correct you · ~10 min · reading and practice用你自己的话复述你听到的，让说话的人知道你听懂了，也能纠正你 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Say it in your words第 1 步 · 用你的话说

Learn先学

After they finish, say the main point back: “So you are worried the deadline is too tight because the data arrives late.” Parroting their exact words sounds mechanical; your own words show you understood the meaning. Keep it short, one or two sentences.

对方说完后，把主要意思说回去：“所以你担心截止日期太紧，因为数据到得晚。”一字不差地鹦鹉学舌听着机械；用自己的话才表明你懂了意思。简短点，一两句就好。

Step 2 · Check it第 2 步 · 核对一下

Learn先学

End with a small question: “Did I get that right?” or “Is that it?” It makes the summary an offer, not a verdict. If you are wrong, they will say so, and a misunderstanding is fixed in ten seconds instead of ten days.

最后加一个小问题：“我理解得对吗？”或者“是这样吗？”这让复述成了一个提议，而不是定论。如果你错了，他们会说，一个误会十秒钟就能纠正，而不是拖上十天。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Hold your opinion第 3 步 · 先别发表意见

Learn先学

Saying it back is not agreeing and not arguing. It only shows what you heard. Resist adding “but” straight after. Say it back, wait for their yes, and only then give your own view. People hear you better when they feel heard first.

复述不是同意，也不是争辩，它只表明你听到了什么。别紧接着加一个“但是”。复述，等他们说“对”，然后再说你的看法。人在先感到被听见之后，才更听得进你的话。

So you are saying ______ because ______. Did I get that right? (wait for yes, then my view)

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Say it back in your words.
• Check you got it.
• Hold your view until they say yes.

• 用你的话复述。
• 确认你听对了。
• 等他们说“对”再说你的看法。
