When it is hard听不下去的时候

Keep listening when you disagree, feel attacked or are in a hurry, by slowing down and protecting the conversation · ~10 min · reading and practice当你不同意、觉得被攻击或赶时间时，放慢下来，保护这场谈话，继续听下去 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Notice your own reaction第 1 步 · 发现自己的反应

Learn先学

When you disagree or feel criticised, your body reacts first: a hot face, a tight jaw, an urge to defend. Name it silently: “I am getting defensive.” Once you notice, you can choose. Without noticing, the reaction chooses for you.

当你不同意或觉得被批评时，身体先有反应：脸发热、下巴绷紧、想要辩解。默默给它起个名字：“我开始防御了。”一旦你察觉，就可以选择。没察觉，就是反应在替你选择。

Step 2 · Slow down第 2 步 · 放慢

Learn先学

Take one slow breath. Drop your shoulders. Slow your voice. Then say back the part you can honestly agree with: “You are right that I was late with the report.” Listening to the criticism is not accepting all of it; you can answer later, calmly.

深呼吸一次，放下肩膀，放慢语速。然后把你能真心同意的部分说回去：“你说得对，我的报告确实交晚了。”听批评不等于接受全部，你可以稍后冷静地回应。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Close well第 3 步 · 好好收尾

Learn先学

Even a hard talk can end well. Thank them for telling you, state what you will do, and agree a next step: “Thanks for raising it. I will send the figures by Friday. Can we check in then?” If you are in a hurry, say so early and offer a time instead of cutting them off.

再难的谈话也可以好好收尾。感谢他们告诉你，说明你会怎么做，约好下一步：“谢谢你提出来。我周五前把数字发给你，到时我们再看看好吗？”如果你赶时间，就早点说，并提出另一个时间，而不是中途把人打断。

Notice: “I am getting defensive.” Breathe, drop shoulders, slow voice. Say back what I agree with. Close: thanks + what I will do + when we check in.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Notice your own reaction.
• Slow down, agree where true.
• Close with a next step.

• 发现自己的反应。
• 放慢，真心同意的地方就同意。
