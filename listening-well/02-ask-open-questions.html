Paying attention · Lesson 2专心听 · 第 2 课

Ask open questions问开放式问题

Ask questions that invite a story, not a yes or no, so the speaker tells you what really matters · ~10 min · reading and practice问能引出一段话的问题，而不是“是”或“不是”，让说话的人讲出真正重要的事 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Open and closed第 1 步 · 开放与封闭

Learn先学

A closed question has a short answer: “Did the meeting go well?” An open one starts with “what”, “how” or “tell me about”: “How did the meeting go?” Open questions get you the story, the worry and the detail that a yes or no hides.

封闭式问题只有简短的答案：“会开得顺利吗？”开放式的以“什么”“怎么”“说说看”开头：“会开得怎么样？”开放式问题能带出故事、担忧和细节，而“是”或“不是”会把这些藏起来。

Step 2 · Follow their thread第 2 步 · 顺着他们的线索

Learn先学

Your next question should come from what they just said, not from the list in your head. If they say “it was a rough week”, ask “what made it rough?” Using their own words shows you heard them and lets them lead to what matters to them.

你的下一个问题应该来自对方刚说的话，而不是你脑子里的清单。如果他们说“这周很难熬”，就问“是什么让它很难熬？”用他们自己的词，表明你听到了，也让他们带你去他们在意的地方。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Short prompts第 3 步 · 简短的鼓励

Learn先学

You do not always need a question. “Tell me more.” “What happened next?” “And then?” keep people going without steering them. Avoid “why” when it sounds like blame: “Why did you do that?” can feel like an accusation; “What led to that?” feels like interest.

不一定要提问。“多说说。”“后来呢？”“然后呢？”可以让人继续讲，又不会引导他们。“为什么”听起来像指责时要避免：“你为什么这样做？”可能像质问；“是什么促成了这件事？”则像有兴趣。

Closed: Did it go well? -> Open: How did it go? Follow their words: “rough week” -> “What made it rough?” Prompts: Tell me more. / And then? Soften “why”: What led to that?

Takeaways要点回顾

• Prefer what and how.
• Follow their words.
• Use short prompts.

• 多用“什么”和“怎么”。
• 顺着他们的话。
• 用简短的鼓励。
