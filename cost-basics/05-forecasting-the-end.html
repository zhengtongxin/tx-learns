Forecasting the end预测最终成本

Forecast what a project will cost in the end under two simple assumptions, and read the range · ~10 min · reading and practice在两种简单假设下预测项目最终成本，并读懂这个区间 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Look ahead, not only back第 1 步 · 不只回头，也要向前看

Learn先学

Tracking tells you what has been spent. A forecast says what the whole project will cost. Take the project with a budget of 4,000: 1,200 spent so far against 1,000 planned, and 3,000 of planned work still to do. Everything depends on what you assume about that remaining work.

记录花费告诉你已经花了多少。预测则说整个项目最终要花多少。还是那个预算 4,000 的项目：计划 1,000，实际已花 1,200，还有 3,000 的计划工作没做。一切取决于你对剩下工作的假设。

Step 2 · If the rest goes to plan第 2 步 · 假设剩下的按计划进行

Learn先学

The simple, hopeful assumption is that the overspend was a one-off and the rest costs what was planned. Then the forecast is what is spent plus what remains: 1,200 + 3,000 = 4,200. Against the budget of 4,000 that is 200 over.

最简单也最乐观的假设是：超支只是一次性的，剩下的按计划花。那么预测就是已花的加剩余的：1,200 + 3,000 = 4,200。对照 4,000 的预算，超出 200。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · If the overspend continues第 3 步 · 假设超支会持续

Learn先学

The cautious assumption is that the same pattern carries on. Actual was 1,200 for 1,000 planned, so work costs 20% more. The remaining 3,000 of work would cost 3,000 × 1.2 = 3,600, and the forecast is 1,200 + 3,600 = 4,800, which is 800 over. Show both: the final cost likely lies between 4,200 and 4,800.

谨慎的假设是同样的情形会延续。计划 1,000 实际 1,200，说明做事贵了 20%。剩下 3,000 的工作就要花 3,000 × 1.2 = 3,600，预测是 1,200 + 3,600 = 4,800，超出 800。两个都展示：最终成本很可能在 4,200 到 4,800 之间。

Budget 4,000. Spent 1,200 (planned 1,000). Remaining plan 3,000. Rest goes to plan: 1,200 + 3,000 = 4,200 (200 over) Overspend continues: 1.2 x 3,000 = 3,600; 1,200 + 3,600 = 4,800 (800 over) Range: 4,200 to 4,800

Takeaways要点回顾

• A forecast says where the project will end up.
• If the rest goes to plan: spent plus remaining plan.
• If the overspend continues: scale the rest by the same ratio.
• Show the range and update it with every new actual.

• 预测说的是项目最终会到哪里。
• 剩下的按计划：已花加上剩余计划。
• 超支持续：剩下的按同样比例放大。
• 展示区间，并随每个新的实际数字更新。
