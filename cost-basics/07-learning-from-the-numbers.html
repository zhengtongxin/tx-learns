Watching the money · Lesson 4盯住资金 · 第 4 课

Learning from the numbers从数字中学习

Compare planned and actual cost, name the cause of a gap and carry the lesson into the next estimate · ~9 min · reading and practice对比计划与实际成本，说出差额的原因，并把经验带进下一次估算 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Plan against actual第 1 步 · 把计划和实际对比

Learn先学

When a project or event ends, compare what you planned with what you actually spent, line by line. Say a party was planned at food 800 (40 guests at 20 each), venue 600, supplies 400 and other 200, which is 2,000 in all. It actually cost food 1,000, venue 600, supplies 400 and other 200, which is 2,200. That is 2,200 − 2,000 = 200 over, or 200 ÷ 2,000 = 10%. The lines show where: only food moved, by 200.

项目或活动结束时，把计划花多少和实际花了多少逐行对比。比如一场聚会计划：食物 800（40 位客人，每位 20）、场地 600、用品 400、其他 200，共 2,000。实际：食物 1,000、场地 600、用品 400、其他 200，共 2,200。2,200 − 2,000 = 超出 200，即 200 ÷ 2,000 = 10%。逐行看就知道在哪里：只有食物变了，多了 200。

Step 2 · Find the cause第 2 步 · 找出原因

Learn先学

A gap is a number, not an answer. Ask why. Costs move for two main reasons: the amount changed (more of the thing) or the price changed (each costs more). Food was planned as 40 guests × 20 = 800. If 50 guests came at 20 each, that is 50 × 20 = 1,000: the amount grew by 10 guests, and 10 × 20 = 200 explains the whole gap. The price did not move. Name the cause in one plain sentence.

差额只是一个数字，不是答案。要问为什么。成本变动主要有两个原因：数量变了（东西多了）或单价变了（每个更贵）。食物原计划是 40 位客人 × 20 = 800。如果来了 50 位、每位仍是 20，就是 50 × 20 = 1,000：数量多了 10 位，10 × 20 = 200 正好解释全部差额，单价没变。用一句平实的话写出原因。

Step 3 · Feed it forward第 3 步 · 用到下一次

Learn先学

A lesson learned that stays in your head is lost. Write it where the next estimate will find it: the guest count grew 25% from the first count (10 more on 40), so next time add a few spare guests to the count or ask for a firm number earlier. Share the numbers and the cause with the person who paid, and keep the actual costs as a record to estimate from. Then the next plan starts from what really happened, not from a guess.

只留在脑子里的经验会丢失。把它写在下次估算会看到的地方：客人人数比最初数字增加了 25%（40 人多了 10 人），所以下次要在人数上多留几位，或更早要一个确定的数字。把数字和原因告诉出钱的人，并把实际成本留作今后估算的依据。这样下一份计划就从真实发生的事出发，而不是猜测。

Sort each habit as a good way to learn from the final costs or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“从最终成本中学习的好方式”或“弱方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Compare planned and actual line by line: 2,200 against 2,000 is 200 over, or 10%.
• A gap needs a cause: the amount changed or the price changed.
• 40 guests planned and 50 came: 10 × 20 = 200 is the whole gap, and the count grew 25%.
• Write the lesson where the next estimate will find it, and share it with the person paying.
• Keep the actual costs as data for the next estimate (an estimate is your best guess of a cost before you spend it).

• 逐行对比计划和实际：2,200 对 2,000 是超出 200，即 10%。
• 差额需要原因：是数量变了，还是单价变了。
• 计划 40 位而来了 50 位：10 × 20 = 200 正好是全部差额，人数增加了 25%。
• 把经验写在下次估算会看到的地方，并告诉出钱的人。
• 把实际成本留作下次估算的数据（估算就是花钱之前对成本的最佳猜测）。
