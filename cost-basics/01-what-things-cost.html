Planning the money · Lesson 1规划资金 · 第 1 课

What things cost成本有哪些

Name the costs of a small project and sort them into direct and indirect, fixed and variable · ~10 min · reading and practice说出一个小项目的各项成本，并分成直接与间接、固定与可变 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Direct and indirect第 1 步 · 直接与间接

Learn先学

A direct cost belongs to one project: the venue for a 50-person event, the food, the printing. An indirect cost is shared with other work, such as a share of office admin. Counting only the direct costs is the most common way to under-state what a project costs.

直接成本只属于一个项目：50 人活动的场地、餐食、印刷。间接成本与其他工作共同承担，比如分摊的办公行政费用。只算直接成本，是低估项目成本最常见的原因。

Step 2 · Fixed and variable第 2 步 · 固定与可变

Learn先学

A fixed cost stays the same however many people come: the venue is S$800 for 30 guests or 60. A variable cost moves with the amount: food at S$12 per person costs more as the guest list grows. Knowing which is which tells you what happens to the total when the plan changes.

固定成本不随人数变化：场地不管来 30 人还是 60 人都是 S$800。可变成本随数量变化：每人 S$12 的餐食，名单越长越贵。分清哪种是哪种，才知道计划一变，总额会怎么变。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · A cost list第 3 步 · 一张成本清单

Learn先学

Write every cost on one list with its kind. Add the direct costs first, then the indirect share, and the total is what the project really costs. A list you can read at a glance also shows where the money goes and which line to look at first when the total is too high.

把每项成本连同类别写在一张清单上。先加直接成本，再加分摊的间接成本，总和才是项目真正的成本。一眼能读懂的清单，也能看出钱花在哪里，总额太高时先看哪一行。

Venue (direct, fixed) 800 Food 50 x 12 (direct, variable) 600 Printing (direct) 100 Direct total 1,500 Share of admin (indirect) 150 Total 1,650 (x means times: 50 guests x S$12 = 600)

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Direct costs belong to one project; indirect costs are shared.
• Fixed costs stay put; variable costs move with the amount.
• The real cost is direct plus the indirect share.
• One list, one kind per line, shows where the money goes.

• 直接成本只属于一个项目，间接成本是共同分摊的。
• 固定成本不变，可变成本随数量变化。
• 真实成本是直接成本加上分摊的间接成本。
• 一张清单、每行一个类别，能看出钱花在哪里。
