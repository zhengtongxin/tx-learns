Estimating costs估算成本

Estimate a cost three ways, bottom-up, from a past project and as a range, and use them to check each other · ~10 min · reading and practice用三种方法估算成本：自下而上、参照过去的项目、给出区间，并让它们互相印证 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Add up the pieces第 1 步 · 把各部分加起来

Learn先学

A bottom-up estimate prices each piece of work and adds them. A 50-person event: venue 800, food 600, printing 100, admin share 150. The sum is 1,650. The smaller the pieces, the easier each is to price, so break big lines into smaller ones before you guess.

自下而上的估算，是给每一块工作定价再相加。50 人的活动：场地 800、餐食 600、印刷 100、行政分摊 150，合计 1,650。块越小越容易定价，所以先把大项拆小，再去估。

Step 2 · Use what happened before第 2 步 · 参照过去的项目

Learn先学

If a similar 40-person event cost 1,400 last year, that is 1,400 ÷ 40 = 35 a head. For 50 people, 35 × 50 = 1,750. A past project is quick and grounded in real money, but only as good as the match: a different venue or season can change the answer.

如果去年类似的 40 人活动花了 1,400，那就是每人 1,400 ÷ 40 = 35。50 人就是 35 × 50 = 1,750。参照过去的项目既快又有真实花费作基础，但只在两者相近时才靠谱：场地或季节不同，结果可能就变了。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Give a range第 3 步 · 给一个区间

Learn先学

An estimate is a guess about the future, so give a range. Say the best case is 1,500, the likely case 1,650 and the worst case 2,100. A simple average of the three is (1,500 + 1,650 + 2,100) ÷ 3 = 1,750. Two methods that land near each other, here 1,650 and 1,750, make you more confident.

估算是对未来的猜测，所以要给一个区间。比如最好情况 1,500、最可能 1,650、最坏 2,100。三者的简单平均是 (1,500 + 1,650 + 2,100) ÷ 3 = 1,750。两种方法的结果很接近（这里是 1,650 和 1,750），就更让人放心。

Bottom-up: 800 + 600 + 100 + 150 = 1,650 Past project: 1,400 / 40 = 35 a head; 35 x 50 = 1,750 Range: best 1,500, likely 1,650, worst 2,100 Average of three: (1,500 + 1,650 + 2,100) / 3 = 1,750

Takeaways要点回顾

• Bottom-up: price small pieces and add them.
• Past projects give a quick check, if they are a close match.
• Give a range, not one exact figure.
• Two methods that agree make the figure easier to trust.

• 自下而上：给小块定价再相加。
• 过去的项目能快速印证，前提是两者相近。
• 给区间，而不是一个确切数字。
• 两种方法一致，数字更可信。
