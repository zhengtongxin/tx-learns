Building a budget做预算

Turn an estimate into a budget with a reserve, spread it across phases, and agree it as the baseline · ~10 min · reading and practice把估算变成带储备金的预算，分摊到各阶段，并约定为基线 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · An estimate is not yet a budget第 1 步 · 估算还不是预算

Learn先学

An estimate is what you think the work will cost. A budget is the amount agreed to be spent. The step between them is a reserve, extra money set aside for things that go wrong. With a 1,650 estimate and a 10% reserve, 1,650 × 10 ÷ 100 = 165, so the budget is 1,650 + 165 = 1,815.

估算是你认为这项工作要花多少。预算是约定可以花的金额。两者之间的一步是储备金，即为出状况而预留的额外资金。估算 1,650、储备 10%：1,650 × 10 ÷ 100 = 165，所以预算是 1,650 + 165 = 1,815。

Step 2 · Spread it over the phases第 2 步 · 分摊到各阶段

Learn先学

Split the budget by phase so you can check spending as you go. Say planning takes 20%, running 60% and wrap-up 20%. Of 1,815 that is 363, 1,089 and 363, and 363 + 1,089 + 363 = 1,815. If the parts do not add back to the whole, a number is wrong.

把预算按阶段拆开，这样边做边能核对花费。比如策划占 20%、执行占 60%、收尾占 20%。1,815 就拆成 363、1,089 和 363，363 + 1,089 + 363 = 1,815。各部分加起来对不上总数，就说明有数字错了。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Agree a baseline第 3 步 · 约定基线

Learn先学

Once the people paying agree the budget, it becomes the baseline: the figure you measure against. Changing the baseline is a decision, not something that drifts quietly. A reserve is part of the baseline, but only the person who owns it should decide when it is used.

出钱的人认可预算后，它就成为基线：之后拿来对照的数字。改基线是一项决定，不能悄悄地漂移。储备金是基线的一部分，但什么时候动用，应由负责它的人来决定。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A budget is an estimate plus a reserve.
• Split it by phase and check the parts add up.
• Once agreed, the budget is the baseline.
• Only its owner should decide to use the reserve.

• 预算是估算加储备金。
• 按阶段拆分，并核对各部分之和。
• 一经约定，预算就是基线。
• 储备金只应由负责人决定是否动用。
