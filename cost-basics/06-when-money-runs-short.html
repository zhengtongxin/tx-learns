Watching the money · Lesson 3盯住资金 · 第 3 课

When money runs short钱不够用时

Compare a forecast with the reserve, choose among honest responses and bring a decision to the person paying · ~10 min · reading and practice把预测和储备金对照，在几种诚实的应对中选择，并把决定交给出钱的人 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Is the reserve enough?第 1 步 · 储备金够不够？

Learn先学

A reserve is money kept aside inside the budget for surprises. “Over budget” is measured against the whole 4,000, reserve included. Say the 4,000 budget includes a reserve of 400. If the forecast is 200 over the budget, that is 200 against a reserve of 400, so the reserve still covers it and 400 − 200 = 200 is left. If the forecast is 800 over, the reserve is used up and 800 − 400 = 400 is still uncovered. Which case you are in decides what you do next: if the reserve covers it, use it with its owner’s approval and note it, and if not, choose a response below.

储备金是预算里专门留出来应付意外的钱。“超出预算”是对照整个 4,000 来算的，储备金也包括在内。假设 4,000 的预算里含有 400 的储备金。如果预测超出预算 200，那么 200 对 400 的储备，储备仍然够，还剩 400 − 200 = 200。如果预测超出 800，储备用光，还有 800 − 400 = 400 没有着落。你处在哪种情形，决定下一步做什么：储备够用，就在负责人同意后动用并记下来；不够，就从下面的应对办法里选。

Step 2 · Ways to respond第 2 步 · 应对办法

Learn先学

There are four honest ways. Cut scope: do less. Save elsewhere: find cheaper ways to do the same. Use the reserve, with its owner’s approval. Ask for more money, with the reason and the figure. Each has a price, so say what it costs the project, not only what it saves.

有四种诚实的办法。削减范围：少做一些。别处节省：用更省钱的方式做同样的事。动用储备金，要有负责人同意。申请追加资金，说明原因和数字。每种都有代价，要说清它给项目带来什么，而不只说省了多少。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Bring a choice, early第 3 步 · 尽早带着选择去找人

Learn先学

Tell the person paying (whoever funds the project: the client, sponsor or your own manager) as soon as the forecast moves, not when the money is gone. Bring the figures and two or three options, with your recommendation: the forecast is 800 over, 400 is covered by the reserve, so the choice is to cut work worth 400 or ask for 400 more. 800 ÷ 4,000 = 20% over the budget, a figure they will want to see.

预测一变动就告诉出钱的人（出资方：客户、发起人或你的上司），而不是等钱花光再说。带上数字和两三个方案，再加上你的建议：预测超出 800，储备金盖得住 400，所以要么削减价值 400 的工作，要么追加 400。800 ÷ 4,000 = 20%，超出预算的比例，他们会想看到。

Budget 4,000 (reserve 400 included) Forecast A: 200 over -> reserve left 400 - 200 = 200 Forecast B: 800 over -> uncovered 800 - 400 = 400 Share over budget in B: 800 / 4,000 = 20% Options for B: cut work worth 400, or ask for 400 more

Takeaways要点回顾

• Compare the forecast overspend with the reserve first.
• Four honest responses: cut scope, save elsewhere, use the reserve, ask for more.
• Tell the person paying early, with figures and options.
• Never hide an overspend.

• 先把预测的超支和储备金对照。
• 四种诚实的办法：削减范围、别处节省、动用储备、申请追加。
• 尽早告诉出钱的人，带上数字和方案。
• 不要隐瞒超支。
