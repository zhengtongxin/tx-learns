Watching the money · Lesson 1盯住资金 · 第 1 课

Tracking spend记录花费

Compare planned with actual spending piece by piece, and read what the difference tells you · ~10 min · reading and practice把计划花费和实际花费逐项对照，读懂差额在说明什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Plan next to actual第 1 步 · 计划与实际并排

Learn先学

Keep two numbers for every piece of work: what was planned to be spent so far and what was actually spent. Say the project has a budget of 4,000. Three pieces of work should so far have cost 300, 500 and 200, which is 1,000 in all. Record real figures as soon as they are known, not at the end of the month.

每一项工作记两个数：到目前为止计划花多少，实际花了多少。假设项目预算 4,000。到现在为止，三项工作按计划应该花 300、500 和 200，共 1,000。实际数字一知道就记下来，不要等到月底。

Step 2 · The difference第 2 步 · 差额

Learn先学

The difference is planned minus actual. A positive number is money saved, a negative number is an overspend. If the actual figures are 280, 720 and 200, the differences are +20, −220 and 0. Add them and the total is −200: the project has spent 200 more than planned so far.

差额是计划减实际。正数表示省了钱，负数表示超支。如果实际数字是 280、720 和 200，差额就是 +20、−220 和 0。加起来总差额是 −200：到目前为止，项目比计划多花了 200。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Read it honestly第 3 步 · 如实解读

Learn先学

A total can hide a problem: one piece 220 over and another 20 under still leaves the project over by 200. Look at each line, not only the total, and ask why. A small saving in one place is not a reason to ignore a large overspend in another.

总数可能把问题藏起来：一项超了 220、另一项省了 20，项目仍然超了 200。要看每一行，而不只看总数，并追问原因。某处省了一点点，不是无视另一处大幅超支的理由。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Keep planned and actual side by side.
• Difference = planned minus actual; negative means overspent.
• Look at each line, not only the total.
• Record actuals as soon as they are known.

• 把计划和实际并排放着。
• 差额 = 计划减实际；负数表示超支。
• 要看每一行，而不只看总数。
• 实际数字一知道就记录。
