# Tampines Cycle Works: product sheet (made-up shop)

## City Bike Blue
Price is 389 dollars. Sizes are S, M and L. The frame is aluminium with 7 gears. It weighs 14 kilograms. Warranty is 2 years on the frame and 6 months on parts. Two bikes in size M are in stock.

## Folding Bike Mini
Price is 459 dollars. It folds in 10 seconds and fits in a car boot. It has 6 gears and 20 inch wheels. Warranty is 1 year on the frame and 6 months on parts. It is out of stock until the end of the month.

## Kids Bike 16 Inch
Price is 169 dollars. Suitable for ages 4 to 6. It comes with training wheels that you can take off. Warranty is 1 year.

## Repairs and servicing
A basic service costs 35 dollars and takes one day. A full service costs 80 dollars and takes two days. Flat tyre repair is 12 dollars while you wait. We do not repair electric bikes.

## Opening hours and returns
Open 10am to 7pm, Tuesday to Sunday. Closed on Mondays. Unused bikes can be returned within 14 days with the receipt. Sale items cannot be returned.
