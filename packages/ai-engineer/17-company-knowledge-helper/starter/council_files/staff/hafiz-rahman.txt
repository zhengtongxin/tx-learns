# Hafiz Rahman, Lift Officer

Hafiz Rahman is the lift officer for Block 12 and the neighbouring blocks. He handles all lift repairs and the yearly servicing schedule.

Before joining the town council eleven years ago, Hafiz drove public buses on the interchange routes. He says reading a timetable taught him to plan lift servicing so that no block is left without a working lift.

He reports to the estate manager, Ms Grace Ng. Residents can reach him through the estate office on weekdays from 9 am to 5 pm.
