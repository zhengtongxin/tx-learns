# Siti Aminah, Cleaning Contracts Officer

Siti Aminah looks after the cleaning contractors for the estate: who cleans which block, how often, and what happens when a stairwell is left dirty.

She reports to the estate manager, Ms Grace Ng. Before joining the council she inspected hawker centres for hygiene, so she is firm about bins and drains.

She also schedules the bulky waste pickup days (see the bulky waste service page).
