# Grace Ng, Estate Manager

Ms Grace Ng is the estate manager of Block 12 and its surrounding blocks. She runs the estate office and decides the yearly budget for repairs, cleaning and events.

The lift officer (Hafiz Rahman) and the cleaning contracts officer (Siti Aminah) both report to her. Before this role she worked as a school administrator for six years.

She approves every request to use the void deck for a private event.
