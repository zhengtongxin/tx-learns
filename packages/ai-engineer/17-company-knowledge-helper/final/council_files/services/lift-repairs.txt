# Lift repairs

If a lift is stuck, makes a strange noise or has a broken button, report it to the estate office. The officer in charge is Hafiz Rahman, the lift officer.

There is no fee for repairs. For an emergency (someone trapped inside) press the alarm button in the lift and call the 24-hour hotline printed on the lift wall.

Planned servicing is announced on the void deck noticeboard at least three days before.
