KAYA TRAVEL COVER: PLAIN WORDING (invented sample)

Delays and cancellations: covered when caused by an official weather warning, an airline fault or the traveller's illness. Receipts are required. Not covered: a delay under six hours, a strike that was announced before the policy was bought, or a cost the airline already paid.

Lost bags: paid at current value, not the price when new (a bag two years old is paid at about half), up to the baggage limit. The airline's loss report must be attached. Items left unattended are not covered.

Medical: covered for illness or injury during the trip, up to the medical limit, with a doctor's or hospital invoice. Existing conditions are not covered unless they were declared when the policy was bought.

Payout: the amount claimed, capped at the limit for that kind of claim, minus the excess. Any payout over S$800 needs a handler's signature. When unsure, refer the claim to a handler.
