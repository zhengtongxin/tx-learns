Editing your draft修改你的草稿

Edit in short passes (cut, tighten, reorder), then check the text like a reader before you send it · ~9 min · reading and practice分几轮短的修改（删减、收紧、调整顺序），然后像读者一样检查再发出 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Write first, edit after第 1 步 · 先写，后改

Learn先学

Do not try to write well and edit in the same breath. Write a rough draft first, then come back to it as a reader. A first draft is rarely short enough: a message of 120 words often says the same in 90. Cutting 30 words is 30 ÷ 120 = 25% shorter, and the reader loses nothing. Leave the draft for a few minutes if you can, because you see more after a break.

不要一边写一边改。先写一份粗糙的草稿，再以读者的身份回头看。第一稿很少够短：120 个字的消息，往往 90 个字就能说清。删掉 30 个字，就是 30 ÷ 120 = 短了 25%，读者什么也没损失。如果可以，把草稿放几分钟再看，休息之后你会看到更多。

Step 2 · Cut and tighten第 2 步 · 删减与收紧

Learn先学

Edit in short passes, each with one job. Pass one: cut any sentence that does not help the reader act or understand. Pass two: tighten long phrases, so “in order to” becomes “to” and “at this point in time” becomes “now”. Pass three: reorder so the point comes first. Three passes of one job each beat one pass that tries to fix everything.

分几轮短的修改，每轮只做一件事。第一轮：删掉对读者行动或理解没有帮助的句子。第二轮：收紧冗长的说法，把“为了能够”改成“为了”，把“在这个时间点上”改成“现在”。第三轮：调整顺序，让结论排在最前面。三轮各做一件事，比一轮想修所有问题要好。

Step 3 · Check it like a reader第 3 步 · 像读者一样检查

Learn先学

Finish by reading the text aloud, or imagine the reader opening it with one minute to spare. Where you stumble, the reader will too. Check the facts and the numbers against your source: a date, a name or an amount that is wrong costs more than any clumsy sentence. Then ask one last question: what should the reader do after reading this? If you cannot say it in one line, the text is not finished.

最后把文字读出声来，或者想象读者只有一分钟时打开它。你读得磕磕绊绊的地方，读者也会。把事实和数字对照原始资料核对：一个错误的日期、名字或金额，比任何拗口的句子代价都大。最后再问一句：读完之后，读者该做什么？如果你不能用一句话说出来，这段文字就还没写完。

Sort each habit as a good way to edit a draft or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“修改草稿的好方式”或“弱方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write a rough draft first, then edit as a reader.
• Edit in short passes: cut, tighten, reorder, one job each.
• Cutting 30 words from 120 makes a text 25% shorter and loses nothing.
• Read it aloud and check dates, names and amounts against the source.
• Ask last: what should the reader do after reading this?

• 先写粗糙的草稿，再以读者的身份修改。
• 分几轮短的修改：删减、收紧、调整顺序，每轮一件事。
• 120 个字删掉 30 个字，短了 25%，却什么也没损失。
• 读出声，并对照原始资料核对日期、名字和金额。
• 最后问：读完之后，读者该做什么？
