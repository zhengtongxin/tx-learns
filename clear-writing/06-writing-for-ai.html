Writing for AI给 AI 写指令

Give a model the same things a good colleague needs: the goal, the context and what a good answer looks like · ~10 min · reading and practice给模型和给好同事一样的东西：目标、背景，以及好答案的样子 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A model is a reader too第 1 步 · 模型也是读者

Learn先学

Everything in the earlier lessons applies to a prompt too. The model cannot see your screen, your meeting or your last week. It knows only what you wrote. State the goal, who the answer is for, what it should use and what it must avoid. A prompt that a new colleague could not follow is a prompt a model will guess at.

前面几课讲的一切同样适用于提示词。模型看不到你的屏幕、你的会议或你上周的工作，它只知道你写下的。说出目标、答案给谁看、要用什么材料、要避免什么。一位新同事看不懂的提示，模型也只能靠猜。

Goal: summarise the report for a manager. Audience: busy, not technical. Use: only the text below. Avoid: jargon and guesses. Format: three bullets, then one recommendation.

Step 2 · Show what good looks like第 2 步 · 展示好结果的样子

Learn先学

Describing the output you want is harder than showing it. Include one short example of a good answer, or the exact format: “three bullets, each under 15 words”. Say how long, in what tone and in what layout. If the first answer misses, compare it with what you pictured and add the missing instruction, rather than only writing “try again”.

描述想要的结果，比展示出来更难。给一个简短的好答案示例，或者给出确切的格式：“三条要点，每条不超过 15 个词”。说明多长、什么语气、什么版式。如果第一个答案不对，就拿它和你心里想的比一比，把缺的那条指令补上，而不是只写“再来一次”。

Step 3 · Change one thing at a time第 3 步 · 每次只改一处

Learn先学

Treat a prompt like code you are debugging. Change one thing, run it again and compare. If you change five things at once, you cannot tell which one helped. Keep the versions that worked, and note why. Always check the answer against the original text or data yourself, because a clear prompt makes a good answer more likely, not certain. Sort each habit.

把提示当作你正在调试的代码。改一处，重新运行，对比。一次改五处，就不知道哪一处起了作用。把有效的版本保留下来，并记下原因。始终自己对照原文或原始数据核对答案，因为清楚的提示只会让好答案更有可能，而不是必然。给每个习惯分类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A model is a reader that knows only what you wrote.
• Show the format and what good looks like.
• Change one thing at a time, and check the answer yourself.

• 模型是只知道你所写内容的读者。
• 展示格式和好结果的样子。
• 每次只改一处，并自己核对答案。
