Writing for a reader · Lesson 2为读者写作 · 第 2 课

Point first先说结论

Open with the answer, then give the support in order of importance · ~10 min · reading and practice开头就给答案，再按重要性给出支持 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The answer comes first第 1 步 · 答案放最前面

Learn先学

Readers are busy and read the top most carefully. Put the answer, the decision or the request there, then the reasons. A story told in the order it happened makes the reader wait for the point. Writers often do it because that is how they lived it, but the reader does not need your journey, only its result.

读者很忙，读得最仔细的是开头。把答案、决定或请求放在那里，然后是理由。按事情发生的顺序讲，会让读者一直等到结论。写的人常这样做，因为他们就是这样经历的，但读者不需要你的经历，只需要结果。

Step 2 · Order the support第 2 步 · 给支持内容排序

Learn先学

After the point, give the two or three reasons that matter most, strongest first. Group related facts together and give each group a short label. A reader who stops after one paragraph should still leave with the main message, and a reader who continues can safely skip the later paragraphs, because they matter less.

说完结论，给出两三个最重要的理由，最强的放前面。把相关的事实放在一起，并给每组一个简短的标签。只读了一段就停下的读者，也应该带走主要信息；继续读的读者可以放心跳过后面的段落，因为它们没那么重要。

Point: the launch will slip by one week. Reason 1: the supplier is late. Reason 2: testing found two problems. Detail: the test notes are attached.

Step 3 · Cut the rest第 3 步 · 删掉其余的

Learn先学

For each sentence ask: would the reader miss it if it were gone? If not, cut it. Warm-up sentences, repeated points and every detail that does not change a decision can go. Short is not the goal; every sentence earning its place is. Keep the cut material in your notes in case someone asks.

对每一句话问：删掉后读者会觉得少了什么吗？不会就删。热身句、重复的内容、不会改变任何决定的细节都可以去掉。目标不是短，而是每句话都有存在的理由。把删掉的材料留在笔记里，以防有人问起。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Put the answer or request first.
• Give the strongest reasons next, in order.
• Cut what the reader would not miss.

• 答案或请求放最前面。
• 接着按强弱给出理由。
• 删掉读者不会觉得少了的内容。
