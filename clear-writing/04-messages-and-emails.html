Writing at work · Lesson 1工作中的写作 · 第 1 课

Messages and emails消息与邮件

Write short messages that get an answer: a clear subject, the ask first and the details after · ~10 min · reading and practice写能得到回复的短消息：清楚的主题、先写请求、再给细节 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The subject line is the first message第 1 步 · 主题行就是第一条消息

Learn先学

Many people read only the subject and the first line. Make the subject carry the point: “Approval needed by Friday: Q3 budget” beats “Question”. If the matter changes, change the subject, so that old threads do not hide the new ask.

很多人只看主题和第一行。让主题带上重点：“周五前需要批准：第三季度预算”比“请教一个问题”好。事情变了，就换主题，免得旧邮件串把新的请求藏起来。

Step 2 · One message, one ask第 2 步 · 一条消息，一个请求

Learn先学

Three questions in one message usually get one answer. Ask one thing, or number the questions so each can be answered separately. State your ask in the first or second line, then give what the reader needs to answer: the background, the options, and your recommendation. A reader who must hunt for the question tends to postpone it.

一条消息里三个问题，通常只得到一个回答。只问一件事，或者给问题编号，让每个都能单独回答。在第一或第二行说出请求，然后给出读者回答所需要的：背景、选项和你的建议。需要去找问题在哪的读者，往往会往后拖。

Subject: Approval needed by Friday: Q3 budget Hi Dana, can you approve the Q3 budget by Friday? Why now: the supplier needs our order on Monday. What changed: the total is 5% below plan. My suggestion: approve as is.

Step 3 · Say what you need and when第 3 步 · 说明需要什么、何时要

Learn先学

Give a date and, where it matters, the reason for it: people prioritise what has a deadline they understand. Make the next step easy: attach the file, link the document, name the decision in one line. Then read it once as the reader, who has ten other messages open, and cut whatever they do not need to answer.

给出日期，必要时说明原因：人们会优先处理自己理解的截止时间。让下一步很容易：附上文件、放上文档链接、用一句话说出要做的决定。然后以读者的身份读一遍，他们还开着另外十封邮件，把他们回答用不着的内容删掉。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Make the subject carry the point.
• One message, one ask; number the rest.
• Give a date and make the next step easy.

• 让主题带上重点。
• 一条消息一个请求；其余编号。
• 给出日期，让下一步很容易。
