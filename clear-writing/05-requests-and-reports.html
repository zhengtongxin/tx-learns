Writing at work · Lesson 2工作中的写作 · 第 2 课

Requests and reports请求与报告

Write a problem report or a status update that someone else can act on without asking more · ~10 min · reading and practice写出别人不必再追问就能处理的问题报告和进度汇报 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A report someone can act on第 1 步 · 别人能据以行动的报告

Learn先学

A good problem report has five parts: what you did, what you expected, what happened instead, how to make it happen again (reproduce it), and the details that matter (version, time, any error message). It lets the receiver start work at once instead of replying with questions. Each missing part costs one more back-and-forth message.

好的问题报告有五个部分：你做了什么、预期什么、实际发生了什么、怎样让它再次出现（复现），以及重要的细节（版本、时间、报错信息）。它让接收的人立刻开始工作，而不是回一堆问题。缺哪一部分，就要多来回一次消息。

What I did: exported the March report as PDF. Expected: a 4-page file. Got: a blank page, no error shown. See it again: any report over 100 rows. Details: app version 2.3, 14:05 today.

Step 2 · Status in three lines第 2 步 · 三句话的进度

Learn先学

A status update answers three questions: what is done, what is next, and what is blocking. Add the date you expect to finish, and say plainly if it has moved. Bad news early is cheap and bad news late is expensive. If you need a decision or help, put it first, not at the end of the update.

进度汇报回答三个问题：完成了什么、下一步是什么、什么在卡住。加上预计完成的日期，如果日期变了就直说。坏消息早说代价小，晚说代价大。如果你需要决定或帮助，放在最前面，而不是汇报的最后。

Step 3 · Fact and opinion第 3 步 · 事实与看法

Learn先学

Keep what you saw apart from what you think. “The export failed twice” is a fact. “The export is broken” is a conclusion that may be wrong. Give the facts first, then label your reading of them: “I think it is the file size, because…”. The reader can then check your reasoning instead of having to trust it. Sort each sentence.

把你看到的和你想的分开。“导出失败了两次”是事实；“导出坏了”是可能出错的结论。先给事实，再标明你对事实的解读：“我认为是文件太大，因为……”。这样读者能检查你的推理，而不是只能相信。给每句话分类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A problem report: did, expected, got, how to repeat, details.
• A status update: done, next, blocked, and the date.
• Give facts first, then label your reading of them.

• 问题报告：做了什么、预期、实际、如何重现、细节。
• 进度汇报：完成、下一步、卡住的事，以及日期。
• 先给事实，再标明你的解读。
