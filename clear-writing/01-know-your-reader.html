Writing for a reader · Lesson 1为读者写作 · 第 1 课

Know your reader认清读者

Decide who will read your text, what they know and what they must do afterwards · ~10 min · reading and practice先想清楚谁会读、他们知道什么、读完要做什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Write for one person第 1 步 · 为一个人写

Learn先学

Most unclear writing has no clear reader. Before you type, name the person who will read it: your manager, a new teammate, a customer, an AI model. Different readers need different texts, even about the same facts. Picture one real person, because “everyone” is a reader who does not exist.

大多数不清楚的文字，是因为心里没有明确的读者。动笔之前，先说出会读它的那个人：你的主管、新同事、客户，或一个 AI 模型。即使事实相同，不同的读者也需要不同的文字。想象一个具体的人，因为“所有人”是不存在的读者。

Step 2 · What do they already know第 2 步 · 他们已经知道什么

Learn先学

Ask what your reader knows before they start. If you skip a step they lack, they get lost. If you explain a step they already know, they get bored and skim, and may miss the part that matters. Write at their level: use their words, and explain any term they have not met. Sort each choice.

先问读者开始读之前已经知道什么。你跳过他们缺的一步，他们就迷路；你解释他们早就知道的一步，他们会觉得无聊、开始扫读，甚至错过重要的部分。按他们的水平写：用他们的词，对没见过的术语加以解释。给每个选择分类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · What should they do next第 3 步 · 读完要做什么

Learn先学

Every text should lead to something: a decision, an answer, an action, or simply understanding. Decide which before you write, and say it. “Please approve by Friday” is better than a description that leaves the reader guessing why you wrote. If you cannot say what you want them to do, you are not ready to write.

每篇文字都应指向某件事：一个决定、一个答复、一个行动，或者单纯的理解。动笔之前先定下是哪一种，并明确说出来。“请在周五前批准”比一段让读者猜你为什么写的描述好得多。如果你说不出希望对方做什么，说明你还没准备好写。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write for one real reader.
• Match what they already know.
• Say what you want them to do.

• 为一个具体的读者写。
• 配合他们已有的知识。
• 说出你希望他们做什么。
