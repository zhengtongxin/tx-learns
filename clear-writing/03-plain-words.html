Writing for a reader · Lesson 3为读者写作 · 第 3 课

Plain words朴素的词句

Use short sentences, concrete words and “who did what” wording so the meaning is hard to miss · ~10 min · reading and practice用短句、具体的词和“谁做了什么”的写法，让意思不容易被误解 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One idea per sentence第 1 步 · 一句一个意思

Learn先学

Long sentences hold several ideas, and the reader must keep them all in mind until the end. Try one idea per sentence, usually under twenty words. You can check your own writing: count the words in each sentence, and look again at any very long one. Variety is fine; a sentence that needs two readings is not.

长句子里装着好几个意思，读者得一直记着，直到句末。试试一句一个意思，通常不超过二十个词。你可以检查自己的文字：数一数每句的词数，再看一眼特别长的。有长有短没问题；需要读两遍才懂的句子才是问题。

Hard to follow (22 words, several ideas tangled together): We shipped it after the team, which tested it twice, having found, as far as anyone could tell, no problems, signed off. Easier (two sentences, one idea each): We shipped it. The team tested it twice and found no problems.

Step 2 · Concrete beats vague第 2 步 · 具体胜过含糊

Learn先学

“Soon”, “some issues” and “improve performance” mean different things to different people. Replace them with what you can measure or point at: “by Friday”, “two failed tests”, “cut load time from 4 seconds to 2”. A concrete sentence can be checked, and a vague one cannot, which is often why it was written that way. Sort each phrase.

“很快”、“一些问题”、“提升性能”对不同的人意思不同。把它们换成你能测量或指出的东西：“周五之前”、“两个失败的测试”、“把加载时间从 4 秒降到 2 秒”。具体的句子可以被检验，含糊的不行，这往往也是它被写成那样的原因。给每个说法分类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Say who does what第 3 步 · 说清谁做了什么

Learn先学

Active voice names the doer first: “The team fixed the bug”. Passive voice (a form of “to be” plus a past-tense verb, such as “was fixed” or “were sent”) can hide it: “The bug was fixed”. Passive is fine when the doer does not matter, but when a reader needs to know who must act, name them. “Someone needs to approve this” gets nothing done; “Dana, please approve this by Friday” does.

主动语态先点出做事的人：“团队修好了这个问题”。被动语态（“被……”这类写法，如“被修好了”“被发送了”）可能把人藏起来：“问题被修好了”。做事的人无关紧要时，被动没问题；但读者需要知道该谁行动时，就要点名。“需要有人批准”什么也推动不了；“Dana，请在周五前批准”才行。

Takeaways要点回顾

• One idea per sentence, usually under twenty words.
• Swap vague words for things you can check.
• Name who does what.

• 一句一个意思，通常不超过二十个词。
• 把含糊的词换成可以核对的东西。
• 点明谁做什么。
