Summaries汇总

Boil a column down to a total, an average and a middle value · ~10 min · reading and practice把一列数据浓缩成总和、平均值和中间值 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Total, count, range第 1 步 · 总和、个数、范围

Learn先学

A summary squeezes many numbers into a few you can hold in your head. Start with len(x) (how many), sum(x) (total), min(x) and max(x) (the range). Always look at these first: an unexpected maximum or a count that is too low often reveals a cleaning problem.

汇总把很多数字压缩成几个你记得住的。先从 len(x)（有多少个）、sum(x)（总和）、min(x) 和 max(x)（范围）开始。总要先看这些：出乎意料的最大值或偏少的个数，常常暴露清理上的问题。

Step 2 · Mean and median第 2 步 · 平均值与中位数

Learn先学

The mean is the sum divided by the count. The median is the middle value once sorted; half the values are below it. One huge value drags the mean up but barely moves the median, so for pay, prices and response times the median is usually the honest “typical”. The statistics module has both.

平均值是总和除以个数。中位数是排序后正中间的那个值，一半的值比它小。一个特别大的值会把平均值拉高，却几乎不影响中位数，所以对工资、价格和响应时间，中位数通常才是诚实的“典型值”。statistics 模块两者都有。

import statistics sales = [100, 120, 80, 95, 2000] print(sum(sales) / len(sales)) print(statistics.median(sales))

Takeaways要点回顾

• Look at count, total, min and max first.
• The mean is dragged by extremes; the median is not.
• For pay, prices and times, prefer the median.

• 先看个数、总和、最小值和最大值。
• 平均值会被极端值拉动；中位数不会。
• 对工资、价格和时间，优先用中位数。
