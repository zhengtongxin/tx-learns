Reading results honestly诚实地解读结果

Spot the traps that make a neat number mislead · ~10 min · reading and practice识破让整齐的数字误导人的陷阱 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · How many, and which ones?第 1 步 · 有多少，是哪些

Learn先学

A number is only as good as the rows behind it. Ask three things. How many? An average of 4 reviews is a rumour; one of 400 is evidence. Which ones? If only unhappy customers write feedback, the average is not “our customers”. What is missing? Rows you skipped or never collected can all lean the same way.

数字的可靠程度取决于它背后的行。问三件事。有多少？4 条评价的平均是传闻；400 条的平均才是证据。是哪些？如果只有不满意的客户才写反馈，这个平均就不代表“我们的客户”。缺了什么？你跳过或根本没收集的行，可能全都偏向同一边。

Step 2 · Together is not because第 2 步 · 一起变化不等于因果

Learn先学

Two things that move together are correlated; that does not mean one causes the other. Ice cream sales and drownings both rise in hot months, and neither causes the other: the weather drives both. Before you act on a pattern, ask what else could produce it, and whether you can test the cause directly.

一起变化的两件事叫相关；这不代表其中一件导致另一件。冰淇淋销量和溺水人数在炎热的月份都会上升，但谁也不是谁的原因：是天气同时推动了两者。按某个规律行动之前，先问还有什么可能造成它，能不能直接检验因果。

Step 3 · Trust it or check it?第 3 步 · 相信还是再查

Learn先学

Decide for each claim whether to trust it as stated or check it first.

为每个说法判断：照字面相信，还是先核查。

Sort each one.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask how many rows, which ones and what is missing.
• Correlation is not cause: look for what else drives both.
• Check outliers and prefer the median when they exist.

• 问有多少行、是哪些、缺了什么。
• 相关不是因果：找找还有什么同时推动两者。
• 检查离群值，有离群值时优先用中位数。
