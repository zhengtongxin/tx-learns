Cleaning values清理数据

Turn messy text into clean numbers and deal with blanks and typos · ~10 min · reading and practice把凌乱的文本变成干净的数字，并处理空白和错别字 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Real data is messy第 1 步 · 真实数据很凌乱

Learn先学

Real tables hold blanks, stray spaces, “N/A”, a number typed as 1,200, the same city spelled three ways. Analysis on dirty data gives confident wrong answers, so cleaning comes first. The goal is not perfect data but known data: you can say what you changed and what you dropped.

真实的表格里有空白、多余的空格、“N/A”、写成 1,200 的数字、同一个城市的三种拼法。在脏数据上分析，会得出自信满满的错误答案，所以先清理。目标不是完美的数据，而是心里有数的数据：你能说清改了什么、丢了什么。

Step 2 · Strip, convert, skip第 2 步 · 去空格、转换、跳过

Learn先学

.strip() removes stray spaces, .lower() evens out capitals, and int() / float() convert text to numbers. A blank or “N/A” makes int() raise ValueError, so wrap it in try and decide: skip the row, use a default or stop. Whatever you pick, count what you skipped.

.strip() 去掉多余空格，.lower() 统一大小写，int() / float() 把文本转成数字。空白或“N/A”会让 int() 抛出 ValueError，所以用 try 包起来，并决定：跳过这行、用默认值，还是停下。无论怎么选，都要数一数跳过了多少。

raw = [" 120", "80 ", "N/A", "100"] clean, skipped = [], 0 for s in raw: try: clean.append(int(s.strip())) except ValueError: skipped += 1 print(clean, skipped)

Takeaways要点回顾

• Clean first: dirty data gives confident wrong answers.
• strip, lower and int or float fix most text problems; try handles the rest.
• Always count what you skipped.

• 先清理：脏数据会给出自信的错误答案。
• strip、lower 和 int 或 float 能解决多数文本问题；其余交给 try。
• 永远要数一数跳过了多少。
