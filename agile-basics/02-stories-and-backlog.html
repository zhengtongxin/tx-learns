Stories and backlog故事与待办列表

Write what users want as short stories, keep them in one ordered list and make each one small enough to finish and check · ~10 min · reading and practice把用户想要的写成简短的故事，放进一张有顺序的清单，并让每个故事小到能做完、能检验 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · User stories第 1 步 · 用户故事

Learn先学

A user story is one short sentence about what a person wants and why: “As a regular customer, I want to reorder my last drink in one tap, so that ordering is quick.” It keeps the team looking at the user’s benefit, not at a list of technical jobs.

用户故事是一句简短的话，说明某个人想要什么、为什么：“作为常客，我想一键再点上次的饮品，这样点单更快。”它让团队盯着用户的好处，而不是一串技术工作。

Step 2 · The backlog第 2 步 · 待办列表

Learn先学

The backlog is one ordered list of everything the project may still do. The top is the most valuable and is described in detail, because it will be picked up next. The bottom is rough, because it may change or never happen. The person who decides what gets built first (the product owner) can reorder the list at any time as new facts arrive.

待办列表是一张有顺序的清单，列出项目可能还要做的所有事。最上面的最有价值，写得详细，因为接下来就要做它。最下面的写得粗略，因为它可能变化，甚至永远不会做。决定先做什么的人（产品负责人）可以随着新情况随时调整顺序。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Small, with checks第 3 步 · 小而可检验

Learn先学

A story should be small enough to finish in one iteration (a short, fixed work cycle, often one or two weeks, explained in the next lesson). If it is too big, split it into smaller stories that each still give the user something. Add two or three acceptance checks: plain statements that tell you when the story is done. Without checks, “done” becomes an argument.

一个故事应该小到能在一次迭代（一个短而固定的工作周期，常见为一到两周，下一课会讲）里做完。如果太大，就拆成几个更小的故事，每一个仍然给用户带来点什么。再加两三条验收条件：用平实的话说明故事做到什么程度算完成。没有验收条件，“完成”就会变成争论。

Story: As a customer, I want to book a table online, so that I do not have to wait on the phone. Checks: 1. I can pick a date and a time. 2. I get a confirmation email. 3. If the time is full, I see a clear message.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A story says who wants what and why.
• The backlog is one ordered list, detailed at the top.
• Split big stories into small ones that still give value.
• Acceptance checks say when a story is done.

• 故事说明谁想要什么、为什么。
• 待办列表是一张有顺序的清单，上面写得详细。
• 把大故事拆成仍然带来价值的小故事。
• 验收条件说明故事何时完成。
