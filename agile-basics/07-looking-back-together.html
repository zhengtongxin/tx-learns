Working in agile teams · Lesson 4在敏捷团队里工作 · 第 4 课

Looking back together一起回顾

Look back on how the team worked, pick a few owned actions, and check them next time · ~9 min · reading and practice回顾团队的工作方式，选少量有负责人的行动，并在下次检查它们 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Why look back第 1 步 · 为什么要回顾

Learn先学

At the end of each iteration, the team takes a short, regular look back at how it worked, not at the product. Three plain questions are enough: What went well? What got in the way? What will we try next time? Keep it to about 30 to 45 minutes, and let everyone speak. The point is to improve the way the team works, a little each iteration.

每个迭代结束时，团队会定期做一次简短的回顾，看的是工作的方式，而不是产品。三个朴素的问题就够了：什么做得好？什么成了阻碍？下次我们想试什么？控制在 30 到 45 分钟左右，并让每个人都发言。目的是每个迭代把团队的工作方式改进一点点。

Step 2 · Pick few actions第 2 步 · 只选少量行动

Learn先学

A look back often produces a long list. Say the team lists 8 ideas. Trying all 8 at once means none gets done, so pick 2 (2 ÷ 8 = 25% of the list) and give each one an owner and a date, for example “Mei tries a 10-minute stand-up limit this week”. Write the actions where the team will see them every day, such as on the board.

回顾常常会列出一长串。假设团队列出了 8 个想法。同时尝试 8 个，结果一个也做不成，所以只选 2 个（2 ÷ 8 = 列表的 25%），并给每个行动指定负责人和日期，例如“小美这周试行 10 分钟的站会时限”。把行动写在团队每天都能看到的地方，例如看板上。

Step 3 · Check what happened第 3 步 · 检查结果

Learn先学

Start the next look back with last time’s actions. Say the team picked 5 actions and finished 4: 4 ÷ 5 = 80%. If the iteration before only finished 2 of 4 (50%), the rate rose by 30 percentage points (80% minus 50%). A drop in the rate is a signal: too many actions, no owners or no time set aside. Keep the tone kind and about the work, so that people say what really happened.

下一次回顾先从上次的行动开始。假设团队选了 5 个行动，完成了 4 个：4 ÷ 5 = 80%。如果上一个迭代 4 个只完成了 2 个（50%），完成率就提高了 30 个百分点（80% 减 50%）。完成率下降是个信号：行动太多、没有负责人，或者没有留出时间。语气要友善，针对工作，这样大家才会说出真实情况。

Sort each habit as a good way to look back or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“好的回顾方式”或“弱的回顾方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Look back at how the team worked: what went well, what got in the way, what to try.
• Pick few actions: 2 of 8 ideas, each with an owner and a date.
• Put actions where the team sees them every day, such as the board.
• Check last time’s actions first: 4 of 5 is 80%, up 30 points from 50%.
• Keep the tone kind and about the work, not about blame.

• 回顾团队的工作方式：什么做得好、什么成了阻碍、想试什么。
• 只选少量行动：8 个想法选 2 个，每个有负责人和日期。
• 把行动放在团队每天都能看到的地方，例如看板。
• 先检查上次的行动：5 个中 4 个是 80%，比 50% 提高 30 个点。
• 语气要友善，针对工作，而不是责怪。
