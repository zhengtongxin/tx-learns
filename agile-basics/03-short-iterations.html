Short iterations短迭代

Work in fixed, short cycles: plan a few stories, build them, show what is finished and look back at how it went · ~10 min · reading and practice按固定的短周期工作：计划几个故事、做出来、展示做完的成果、再回顾做得怎样 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A fixed-length cycle第 1 步 · 固定长度的周期

Learn先学

An iteration is a short cycle of fixed length, often one to four weeks. In each one the team plans a few stories, builds them, shows what is finished and then looks back. Keeping the length fixed gives the team a steady rhythm and makes its results comparable from one cycle to the next.

迭代是一个长度固定的短周期，通常一到四周。每个迭代里，团队计划几个故事、把它们做出来、展示做完的成果，然后回顾。长度固定，团队就有稳定的节奏，各个周期的结果也有可比性。

Step 2 · Planning and review第 2 步 · 计划与评审

Learn先学

At the start the team holds planning: it picks the stories from the top of the backlog that it believes will fit, judging from what it finished before. At the end comes the review: the team shows only finished work to the people who care and listens to their reactions. Those reactions feed the backlog.

开始时团队开计划会：根据以往做完的量，从待办列表顶部挑出它认为放得下的故事。结束时开评审：团队只向相关的人展示做完的成果，并听他们的反应。这些反应会反馈到待办列表里。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Looking back第 3 步 · 回顾

Learn先学

After the review the team holds a retrospective, a look-back on how it worked rather than on what it built: what went well, what got in the way, and one change to try next time. Unfinished stories are not stretched into the next cycle quietly. They go back to the backlog and are re-judged with fresh facts.

评审之后团队开回顾会，回头看的是自己怎么工作，而不是做出了什么：什么顺利、什么碍事，以及下次尝试的一项改变。没做完的故事不会悄悄延到下一个周期，而是回到待办列表，用新的事实重新评估。

Takeaways要点回顾

• An iteration is a short cycle of fixed length.
• Planning picks stories; the review shows finished work.
• The look-back is about how the team worked.
• Unfinished stories go back to the backlog.

• 迭代是长度固定的短周期。
• 计划会挑故事；评审展示做完的成果。
• 回顾说的是团队怎么工作。
• 没做完的故事回到待办列表。
