Why agile为什么用敏捷

See why teams plan in small steps, show real work early and change direction when they learn something · ~10 min · reading and practice看团队为什么分小步计划、尽早展示真实成果，并在有新发现时调整方向 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Plan in small steps第 1 步 · 分小步计划

Learn先学

Planning everything up front assumes you know everything at the start, and that nothing will change. Real projects rarely work that way. Agile is a way of working in short steps: deliver something usable, learn from what people say about it, then plan the next step with that knowledge.

一开始就把所有事情计划好，等于假设你一开始什么都知道、而且什么都不会变。真实的项目很少是这样。敏捷是一种分短步骤的工作方式：交付一点能用的东西，听大家的反馈，再带着新认识去计划下一步。

Step 2 · Feedback beats guessing第 2 步 · 反馈胜过猜测

Learn先学

Imagine a café chain building an ordering app. In week two the team shows a rough menu screen, and customers say they mostly want to choose a pickup time. Learning that in week two costs a small change. Learning it in month six, after everything is built, costs a rebuild. Showing work early turns guesses into facts while change is still cheap.

设想一家咖啡连锁店在做点单应用。第二周团队给顾客看一个粗糙的菜单界面，顾客说他们最想选取餐时间。第二周得知，只需小改一下；等到第六个月、全部做完才得知，就得重做。尽早展示成果，能在改动还便宜的时候，把猜测变成事实。

Step 3 · When a fixed plan still fits第 3 步 · 什么时候固定计划仍然合适

Learn先学

Agile does not mean no planning. When the work is well known and stable, such as laying a standard pipe along a surveyed route, planning it all first is sensible. The more that is unknown or likely to change, the more small steps and feedback pay off. Pick the approach by how much you do not yet know.

敏捷不等于不做计划。当工作已被充分了解、又很稳定（比如沿着勘测好的路线铺一条标准管道），先把一切计划好是合理的。未知和可能变化的部分越多，小步加反馈就越划算。要按“你还有多少不知道”来选方法。

Known and stable -> plan it all first Unknown or changing -> small steps, feedback after each Most real work -> some of both

Takeaways要点回顾

• Agile means short steps with feedback after each.
• Early feedback is cheap to act on.
• A full plan still fits known, stable work.
• Choose by how much you do not yet know.

• 敏捷是短步骤加每步之后的反馈。
• 早反馈便宜，容易据此行动。
• 已知且稳定的工作，仍适合完整计划。
• 按“还有多少不知道”来选择方法。
