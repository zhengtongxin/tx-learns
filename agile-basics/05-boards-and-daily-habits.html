Working in agile teams · Lesson 2在敏捷团队里工作 · 第 2 课

Boards and daily habits看板与每日习惯

Make work visible on a simple board, check in briefly each day and limit how much is started at once · ~10 min · reading and practice用一块简单的看板让工作看得见，每天简短碰头，并限制同时开工的数量 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The short daily check-in第 1 步 · 简短的每日碰头

Learn先学

Once a day the team meets for about fifteen minutes, often standing up. Each person answers three questions: what did I finish, what will I do next, and is anything stopping me? It is not a report to a boss. It is for the team to line up its day and to bring blockers (anything that stops you moving forward) into the open early.

团队每天碰头一次，大约十五分钟，常常站着开。每个人回答三个问题：我完成了什么、接下来做什么、有什么挡着我？这不是向老板汇报，而是让团队排好当天的事，并尽早把障碍（任何挡住你前进的事）摆到桌面上。

Step 2 · A visible board第 2 步 · 看得见的看板

Learn先学

A board shows the work as cards in columns, usually To do, Doing and Done. Anyone can see at a glance what is waiting, what is being worked on and what is finished. A card that sits in Doing for days stands out, and that is the point: problems become visible before they become late.

看板把工作画成放在各列里的卡片，通常分为“待做、进行中、完成”。任何人一眼就能看到什么在等、什么在做、什么已完成。在“进行中”停了好几天的卡片会很显眼，这正是目的：问题在变成延误之前就被看见。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Limit work in progress第 3 步 · 限制在制工作

Learn先学

Starting everything at once feels busy but finishes little. If one person switches between five two-day stories, none may be done until close to day ten (5 stories × 2 days = 10 days), because each switch costs time to get back into a story. Working on one at a time, the first is done on day two, the next on day four, and so on. Work in progress (often shortened to WIP) is the cards now in Doing. A limit on that column, say three cards at once, forces the team to finish something before it starts something new.

同时开工显得很忙，却很少做完东西。如果一个人在五个各需两天的故事之间来回切换，可能要到接近第十天（5 × 2 = 10 天）才有一个做完（每次切换都要花时间重新进入状态）；一次只做一个，第二天做完第一个，第四天做完下一个，依此类推。“在制工作”（常缩写为 WIP）就是现在在“进行中”的卡片。给这一列设一个上限（比如最多同时三张卡片），迫使团队先做完一件，再开始新的。

To do Doing (limit 3) Done [story 6] [story 3] [story 1] [story 7] [story 4] [story 2] [story 8] [story 5]

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• The check-in has three questions: done, next, blocked.
• A board makes work visible.
• Limit work in progress; finish before starting.
• Visible problems get fixed sooner.

• 碰头有三个问题：做完了什么、接下来做什么、被什么挡着。
• 看板让工作看得见。
• 限制在制工作；先做完再开工。
• 看得见的问题会更早被解决。
