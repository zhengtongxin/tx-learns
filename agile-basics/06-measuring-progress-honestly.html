Measuring progress honestly如实衡量进展

Count only finished work, use recent results to forecast and report a range instead of a promise · ~10 min · reading and practice只算做完的工作，用近期的结果来预测，并给出范围而不是承诺 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · What done means第 1 步 · “完成”是什么意思

Learn先学

A story counts only when it is done, which the team defines in advance as a short checklist: built, checked against its acceptance checks, shown to the owner, nothing left to fix. A story that is 90% finished counts as nothing yet. That sounds harsh, but it stops a project from reporting progress that is not real.

一个故事只有在完成时才算数，而“完成”由团队事先定成一张简短的清单：做出来了、对照验收条件检查过了、给负责人看过了、没有遗留要修的东西。做了九成的故事暂时算零。这听起来苛刻，但能防止项目汇报不真实的进展。

Step 2 · Velocity第 2 步 · 速度

Learn先学

Teams often give each story a size in points, a scale each team sets for itself, so points only mean something inside one team. Velocity is the number of points the team finishes in one iteration. It is measured, not wished for. If the last three cycles finished 18, 22 and 20 points, the average is (18 + 22 + 20) ÷ 3 = 20. That average is the best guide to how much the team can take next time.

团队常常用点数给每个故事估一个大小。点数是各个团队自己定的尺度，只在同一个团队内部才有意义。速度是团队在一次迭代里做完的点数，是量出来的，不是愿望。如果最近三个周期做完了 18、22、20 点，平均值就是 (18 + 22 + 20) ÷ 3 = 20。这个平均值是判断团队下次能接多少的最好依据。

Step 3 · Forecast with a range第 3 步 · 用范围来预测

Learn先学

With 100 points left and an average of 20, the work needs about 100 ÷ 20 = 5 iterations. But the results ranged from 18 to 22: at 22, 100 ÷ 22 is about 4.5, so 5 cycles (you cannot finish half a cycle, so round up); at 18, 100 ÷ 18 is about 5.6, so 6 cycles. Say “five to six iterations”. A range is honest. A single date is a promise nobody can keep.

剩下 100 点，平均速度 20，大约需要 100 ÷ 20 = 5 次迭代。但结果在 18 到 22 之间波动：按 22 算，100 ÷ 22 约 4.5，即 5 个周期（不能只做半个周期，所以向上取整）；按 18 算，100 ÷ 18 约 5.6，即 6 个周期。所以说“五到六次迭代”。范围是诚实的，单一日期则是没人能守住的承诺。

Last three iterations: 18, 22, 20 points Average velocity: (18 + 22 + 20) / 3 = 20 Points left: 100 Forecast: 100 / 20 = 5 iterations (5 to 6 with the range)

Takeaways要点回顾

• Count only work that meets the done checklist.
• Velocity is measured: here (18 + 22 + 20) ÷ 3 = 20.
• Forecast = points left ÷ velocity, shown as a range.
• A range is honest; a single date is a promise.

• 只算满足“完成”清单的工作。
• 速度是量出来的：这里 (18 + 22 + 20) ÷ 3 = 20。
• 预测 = 剩余点数 ÷ 速度，用范围表示。
• 范围是诚实的；单一日期是承诺。
