Roles in the team团队里的角色

Step 1 · Three roles第 1 步 · 三种角色

Learn先学

An agile team usually has three roles. The product owner speaks for the people who will use the result and decides what is built and in what order. The team is the people who do the work together. The facilitator looks after the way of working: it keeps the rhythm and helps remove what is in the way. One person fills one role, but a role is not a job title.

敏捷团队通常有三种角色。产品负责人代表将使用成果的人，决定做什么、按什么顺序做。团队是一起做事的人。协调者照看工作方式：维持节奏，帮忙清除障碍。一个人担任一个角色，但角色并不等于职位头衔。

Step 2 · Who decides what第 2 步 · 谁决定什么

Learn先学

The product owner decides what and in what order, and may change the order at any time. The team decides how to build it and how much fits in a cycle, because the people doing the work know best how long it takes. The facilitator decides nothing about the content. Splitting decisions this way keeps them close to the people who know the most.

产品负责人决定做什么以及顺序，并且可以随时调整顺序。团队决定怎么做，以及一个周期放得下多少，因为做事的人最清楚要花多久。协调者不决定内容。这样分工，让决定靠近最了解情况的人。

Step 3 · Clearing blockers第 3 步 · 清除障碍

Learn先学

A blocker is anything that stops work and that the team cannot fix on its own: a missing approval, a broken tool, a person who has not replied. The rule is to raise it the day it appears, not at the end of the cycle. The facilitator then works to clear it, so the team can keep building.

障碍是指阻碍工作、而团队自己无法解决的东西：缺少批准、工具坏了、某人没有回复。规则是出现当天就提出来，而不是等到周期结束。协调者随后去设法清除它，让团队继续做事。

Takeaways要点回顾

• The owner decides what and in what order.
• The team decides how and how much.
• The facilitator keeps the rhythm and clears blockers.
• Raise blockers the day they appear.

• 负责人决定做什么、什么顺序。
• 团队决定怎么做、做多少。
• 协调者维持节奏、清除障碍。
• 障碍出现当天就提出来。
