Step 1 · Cells, rows and columns第 1 步 · 单元格、行和列

Learn先学

A sheet is a grid. Columns have letters (A, B, C) and rows have numbers (1, 2, 3). Each box is a cell, named by its column letter then its row number: the cell in column C, row 4 is C4. A cell holds one thing: a number, a piece of text or a formula (a calculation that works out its answer from other cells).

表格是一张网格。列用字母（A、B、C），行用数字（1、2、3）。每个格子叫单元格，名字是先列字母再行数字：C 列第 4 行就是 C4。一个单元格里放一样东西：数字、文字或公式（用其他单元格算出答案的计算式）。

Step 2 · Point, do not type第 2 步 · 指向，而不是手填

Learn先学

A formula starts with = and can use other cells instead of typed numbers. If the total is =B2+B3, it follows B2 and B3 whenever they change. Typing the answer by hand freezes it: when an input changes, the total is silently wrong.

公式以 = 开头，可以用其他单元格代替手敲的数字。如果合计是 =B2+B3，B2 和 B3 一变，它就跟着变。把答案手敲进去就被“冻住”了：输入一变，合计就悄悄错了。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A first sheet第 3 步 · 第一张表

Learn先学

Put labels in the first row, inputs below them and the formula where the answer belongs. To enter a formula, click the cell, type = and the formula, then press Enter. Then change one input and watch the answer move. If it does not move, the formula is not pointing at that cell.

第一行放标签，下面放输入，答案所在的格子放公式。输入公式的方法：点一下单元格，输入 = 和公式，再按回车。然后改动一个输入，看答案是否跟着动。如果没动，说明公式没有指向那个格子。

A1: Item B1: Price A2: Tea B2: 3 A3: Rice B3: 5 A4: Total B4: =B2+B3 (shows 8)

Takeaways要点回顾

• Cells are named column then row.
• A formula starts with =.
• Point at cells, do not retype.

• 单元格先列后行命名。
• 公式以 = 开头。
• 指向单元格，不要重敲数字。
