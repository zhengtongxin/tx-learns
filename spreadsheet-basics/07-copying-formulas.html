Copying formulas复制公式

Know which cell names move and which stay when you copy a formula, and check the copy before you trust it · ~9 min · reading and practice弄清复制公式时哪些单元格名称会移动、哪些保持不变，并在信任结果之前核对复制出来的公式 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Relative references第 1 步 · 相对引用

Learn先学

When you copy a formula to another row, the cell names inside it move with it. This is a relative reference. A formula in row 3 that reads the price in A3 becomes a formula that reads A4 when copied one row down. This is usually what you want, because each row should use its own price.

把公式复制到另一行时，公式里的单元格名称会跟着移动，这叫相对引用。第 3 行里读取 A3 价格的公式，向下复制一行后，就变成读取 A4。这通常正是你想要的，因为每一行应当用自己的价格。

Step 2 · Fixing a cell第 2 步 · 锁定一个单元格

Learn先学

Sometimes every row must use the same cell, such as one tax rate. Put the rate in C1 (10%) and write the formula as price × $C$1. The dollar signs make an absolute reference, which does not move when copied. With prices 40, 60 and 100 the results are 4, 6 and 10, and 4 + 6 + 10 = 20, which is 10% of 40 + 60 + 100 = 200. Without the dollar signs, the copy would read C2, C3 and so on, which are empty, and show 0.

有时每一行都必须用同一个单元格，比如同一个税率。把税率放在 C1（10%），公式写成 价格 × $C$1。美元符号让它成为绝对引用，复制时不会移动。价格是 40、60、100 时，结果是 4、6、10，4 + 6 + 10 = 20，正好是 40 + 60 + 100 = 200 的 10%。如果没有美元符号，复制出去的公式会读取 C2、C3 等空单元格，显示 0。

Step 3 · Check after copying第 3 步 · 复制后要核对

Learn先学

After you copy a formula down, click a cell in the last row and read its formula. Check that the part that should move did move, and the part that should stay did stay. Then check the total against a quick sum in your head. Put a rate or any other number that many formulas use in one labelled cell, never typed inside each formula.

把公式向下复制后，点一下最后一行的单元格，看看它的公式。检查该移动的部分有没有移动，该固定的部分有没有固定。再用心算粗略核对总数。许多公式共用的税率或其他数字，要放在一个有标签的单元格里，不要在每个公式里手打一遍。

To finish Step 3, sort each habit as good or weak: tap a habit, then tap its group.

完成第 3 步：把每个做法分为好或弱。先点一个做法，再点它所属的组。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Relative references move when you copy a formula.
• Dollar signs make an absolute reference that stays put.
• With a 10% rate, prices 40, 60 and 100 give 4, 6 and 10, a total of 20.
• After copying, read a formula in the last row.
• Keep a shared rate in one labelled cell.

• 相对引用在复制公式时会移动。
• 美元符号让引用成为绝对引用，固定不动。
• 税率 10%，价格 40、60、100 得到 4、6、10，合计 20。
• 复制之后，读一读最后一行的公式。
• 把共用的税率放在一个有标签的单元格里。
