Summaries and lookups汇总与查找

Step 1 · Totals by group第 1 步 · 按组汇总

Learn先学

Often you want a total for one group, such as how much was spent on tea. A conditional sum adds only the rows that match a test (SUMIF), and a conditional count counts them (COUNTIF). Both read the test column and the amount column row by row.

你经常需要某一组的合计，比如茶一共花了多少。条件求和只加符合条件的行（SUMIF），条件计数统计它们的个数（COUNTIF）。两者都会逐行读取条件列和金额列。

Step 2 · Match exactly第 2 步 · 精确匹配

Learn先学

A match is exact: “Tea ” with a trailing space is not “Tea”, and “tea” may or may not equal “Tea” depending on the tool. A lookup finds a value by name in the same exact way, and gives an error when the name is not found. Clean the spelling first; do not hide the error.

匹配是精确的：末尾带空格的“Tea ”不等于“Tea”；“tea”是否等于“Tea”取决于工具。按名称查找数值用的是同样的精确匹配，找不到名称时会给出错误。先把拼写整理干净，不要把错误掩盖掉。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Do the parts add up?第 3 步 · 各部分加起来对吗？

Learn先学

After grouping, add the group totals and compare with the overall total. If they differ, some rows were missed: a misspelt name, a stray space or a blank. That one comparison catches many mistakes.

分组之后，把各组合计加起来，与总合计对比。如果不一致，说明有些行被漏掉了：拼错的名称、多余的空格或空白。这一次对比就能抓住很多错误。
