Ranges and functions区域与函数

Use a range and a built-in function to total, average and count a column in one step instead of cell by cell · ~10 min · reading and practice用区域和内置函数一步完成对一列的求和、平均和计数，而不是一格一格相加 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Ranges第 1 步 · 区域

Learn先学

A range is a block of cells written with a colon: B2:B6 means every cell from B2 down to B6. A function takes a range and does a job on it. =SUM(B2:B6) adds them all, so you do not write B2+B3+B4+B5+B6. If you insert a new row inside the range (right-click a row number and choose insert), the range stretches to include it.

区域是用冒号写出的一片单元格：B2:B6 表示从 B2 到 B6 的每一格。函数接收一个区域并对它做一件事。=SUM(B2:B6) 把它们全部相加，所以你不必写 B2+B3+B4+B5+B6。如果在区域内部插入新的一行（右键点行号，选择插入），区域会自动把它包含进去。

Step 2 · The everyday five第 2 步 · 常用五个

Learn先学

Five functions cover most day-to-day work: SUM adds, AVERAGE gives the mean (add the values, divide by how many there are), MIN and MAX find the smallest and largest, COUNT counts the cells that hold numbers. Start the range just below the header row, because text in a header is not a number.

五个函数涵盖大部分日常工作：SUM 求和，AVERAGE 求平均（把数值相加，再除以个数），MIN 和 MAX 找最小和最大，COUNT 统计有数字的单元格个数。区域要从表头下面一行开始，因为表头里的文字不是数字。

Step 3 · Order of operations第 3 步 · 运算顺序

Learn先学

When a formula mixes operations, the order decides the answer. It multiplies and divides before it adds and subtracts, so =2+3*4 is 14, not 20. Brackets go first: =(2+3)*4 is 20. When a result looks odd, check the order, then check the range.

公式里混用多种运算时，顺序决定答案。它先乘除、后加减，所以 =2+3*4 是 14，不是 20。括号最先算：=(2+3)*4 是 20。结果看着奇怪时，先查运算顺序，再查区域。

B2:B6 holds 3, 5, 4, 8, 10 =SUM(B2:B6) -> 30 =AVERAGE(B2:B6) -> 6 =MIN(B2:B6) -> 3 =MAX(B2:B6) -> 10 =COUNT(B2:B6) -> 5
