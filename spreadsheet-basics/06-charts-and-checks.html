Charts and checks图表与核对

Pick a chart that fits the question, draw it honestly and run a few quick checks before anyone relies on the sheet · ~10 min · reading and practice选一种适合问题的图表，诚实地画出来，并在别人依赖这张表之前做几项快速核对 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The right chart第 1 步 · 合适的图表

Learn先学

Ask what the reader should see. Change over time is a line chart. Comparing a few categories is a bar chart. Parts of one whole can be a pie chart, but only with a few slices, since many slices are hard to compare. If a table shows the point better, use the table.

先想读者应该看到什么。随时间的变化用折线图。比较几个类别用柱状图。一个整体的组成部分可以用饼图，但只适合少数几块，块多了很难比较。如果表格更能说明问题，就用表格。

Step 2 · Draw it honestly第 2 步 · 诚实地画

Learn先学

Bars show size by length, so start their axis at zero; cutting the axis makes a small gap look huge. Label the axes and units, give the chart a title that states the point and keep the colours few.

柱子靠长度表示大小，所以坐标轴要从零开始；截断坐标轴会让小差距看起来很大。给坐标轴和单位加标签，标题直接写出结论，颜色要少。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Check before sharing第 3 步 · 分享前核对

Learn先学

Build a small habit: add a few numbers by hand and compare, look for error messages such as a division by zero, scan for blank cells inside ranges, and change one input to see that the output moves. Then keep the inputs in one clearly marked place.

养成一个小习惯：手算几个数字来对比；查找错误提示，比如除以零；扫一眼区域里有没有空白单元格；改动一个输入，看输出是否跟着动。然后把输入集中放在一个标记清楚的地方。

1. Add three numbers by hand: matches the sheet? 2. =B2/B3 with B3 = 0 gives an error: fix the cause 3. Any blank cell inside a range? 4. Change one input: does the answer move? 5. Inputs in one marked place; formulas elsewhere
