Shaping ideas · Lesson 2打磨想法 · 第 2 课

Test small小测试

Try an idea in the cheapest, quickest way before you build it, so you learn what works while it is still easy to change · ~10 min · reading and practice在动手做之前，用最便宜、最快的办法试一试，趁着还容易改，先弄清什么行得通 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The riskiest guess第 1 步 · 最冒险的猜测

Learn先学

Every idea rests on guesses. “New staff will read a welcome pack” is a guess. Write down the guesses and choose the one that would sink the idea if it were wrong. That is what you test first, not the details of how it will look.

每个点子都建立在若干猜测上。“新同事会读迎新包”就是一个猜测。写下这些猜测，选出一旦错了就会让点子垮掉的那个。先测它，而不是测外观细节。

Step 2 · Rough and quick第 2 步 · 粗糙而快

Learn先学

A test can be tiny: a paper sketch, a one-page draft, five minutes with two people. Do not build the whole thing. The aim is a quick answer to one question, so rough is fine. If it takes weeks, it is a project and not a test.

测试可以很小：一张纸上的草图、一页草稿、和两个人谈五分钟。不要把整个东西做出来。目的是对一个问题快速得到答案，所以粗糙也行。如果要花几周，那是一个项目，不是测试。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Honest results第 3 步 · 诚实地看结果

Learn先学

Decide beforehand what would count as success, such as “at least three of five new staff find their form without help”. Then look at what happened and not at what you hoped. A test that disproves your idea has done its job: it saved you from building the wrong thing.

事先定好什么算成功，比如“五位新同事里至少三位无需帮助就找到表格”。然后看实际发生了什么，而不是你希望发生什么。推翻了你点子的测试，已经完成了它的任务：它让你没有去做一件错的东西。

Idea: welcome pack sent a week before. Riskiest guess: new staff will read it. Test: send a one-page draft of the pack to five people; ask whether they would read it and what they remember. Success: at least 3 of 5 read it and can say two things from it. Result: 2 of 5. Next: shorten, add a picture, try again.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Test the riskiest guess first.
• Keep the test rough and quick.
• Fix the success bar before you look.

• 先测最冒险的猜测。
• 让测试粗糙而快。
• 看结果前先定成功标准。
