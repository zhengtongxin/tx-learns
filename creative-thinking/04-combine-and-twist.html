Shaping ideas · Lesson 1打磨想法 · 第 1 课

Combine and twist组合与变形

Make new ideas by joining two old ones, or by changing one part of an idea you already have · ~10 min · reading and practice把两个旧点子拼起来，或改变已有点子的某一部分，得到新点子 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Join two ideas第 1 步 · 把两个点子合在一起

Learn先学

Take two ideas from your list and ask what happens if you put them together. A buddy for each new person plus a colour-coded folder becomes a buddy who hands over the folder on day one. Many good ideas are two plain ones that no one had put side by side.

从清单里拿两个点子，问问放在一起会怎样。“给每个新人配一个伙伴”加上“按颜色分类的文件夹”，就成了“伙伴在第一天把文件夹交给新人”。很多好点子，其实是两个普通点子，只是以前没人把它们放在一起。

Step 2 · Change one part第 2 步 · 改变一个部分

Learn先学

Take one idea and change one part at a time: make it bigger or smaller, move it earlier or later, give it to someone else, take something away, reverse it. “A welcome pack on day one” becomes “a welcome pack sent a week before”. One small change can make it a different idea.

拿一个点子，一次只改一个部分：变大或变小，提前或推后，换给别人，拿掉一样东西，反过来。“第一天发迎新包”变成“提前一周寄出迎新包”。一个小改动，就可能变成另一个点子。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Keep what fits第 3 步 · 留下合适的

Learn先学

Most combinations and twists will not work, and that is expected. After you have made a batch, go back to your brief (the question, the person and the limits). Keep the few that fit and meet the need of the person you wrote down. Drop the rest without regret.

多数组合和变形行不通，这很正常。做出一批之后，回到你的题目（问题、对象和限制）。留下合适、能满足你写下的那个人的需要的几个，其余的毫不惋惜地放掉。

Join: buddy + colour-coded folder = the buddy hands over the folder on day one. Twist: welcome pack on day one -> sent a week before. Twist: give it to the manager -> give it to the team. Check against the brief: free, ready in two weeks, helps new staff. Keep: buddy + folder, pack a week before.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Join two ideas.
• Change one part at a time.
• Keep only what fits the brief.

• 把两个点子合起来。
• 一次改一个部分。
• 只留下符合题目的。
