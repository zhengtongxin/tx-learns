Finding ideas · Lesson 3找到想法 · 第 3 课

Borrow from elsewhere向别处借

Look at how other fields solve a similar problem and carry the idea over, instead of waiting for a new one · ~10 min · reading and practice看看别的领域怎样解决相似的问题，把做法借过来，而不是干等一个全新的 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The problem underneath第 1 步 · 问题背后的问题

Learn先学

Most problems are a version of a more general one. “New staff cannot find the right form” is really “people need to find the right thing in a crowded place”. Say it in general words: it frees you from the details that make your case look unique.

多数问题都是某个更一般的问题的一种。“新同事找不到对的表格”，其实是“人在拥挤的地方要找到对的东西”。用一般的话说出来，就能摆脱那些让你的情况显得独一无二的细节。

Step 2 · Who else has this?第 2 步 · 还有谁有这个问题？

Learn先学

Ask where else people face the general problem. Shops guide shoppers to the right shelf, hospitals guide visitors to the right ward, airports guide travellers to the right gate. Make a short list. Each one is a ready-made set of ideas, such as signs, colours and guides, that people already use every day.

问问还有哪里的人面对这个一般问题。商店把顾客引到对的货架，医院把访客引到对的病房，机场把旅客引到对的登机口。列一张短清单。每一个都是别人早就试过、可以借来用的做法。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Carry it over第 3 步 · 借过来，再调整

Learn先学

Pick one example, write how it works in two lines, then change it to fit your limits. The borrowed part is a visible guide that lets people choose their own way. Floor arrows in a hospital might become a colour-coded folder in your shared drive. A borrowed idea rarely fits as it is; the adjusting is where your own contribution lies.

选一个例子，用两行写出它怎么运作，再按你的限制改一改。借来的部分是：一个让人自己选路的、看得见的指引。医院地上的箭头，可能变成共享文件夹里按颜色分类的文件夹。借来的点子很少原样合适；调整的过程，就是你自己的贡献。

Our problem: new staff cannot find the right form. Same problem, in general words: finding the right thing in a crowded place. Who else has it: shops (shelf signs), hospitals (floor arrows), airports (gate boards). Borrow: floor arrows. Adjust: colour-coded folders, one colour per task.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Say your problem in general words.
• Look at who else has it.
• Adjust what you borrow.

• 用一般的话说出问题。
• 看看还有谁有它。
• 调整借来的东西。
