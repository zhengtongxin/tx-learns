Finding ideas · Lesson 1找到想法 · 第 1 课

Name the brief写清题目

Say what you need ideas for, who it is for and what limits apply, so the ideas have somewhere to land · ~10 min · reading and practice先说清你要为什么找点子、为谁、有什么限制，点子才有落脚的地方 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A question you can answer第 1 步 · 一个能回答的问题

Learn先学

A brief is the question you want ideas for, the person it is for and the limits that apply. “We need something new” gives your mind nothing to work on. Turn it into a question that begins “How could we ...” and names who and what: “How could we help new staff find the right form in their first week?” A clear question points the search; a vague one makes every idea seem equally good or equally useless.

“题目”（brief）就是：你要为什么找点子、为谁、有什么限制。“我们需要点新东西”，让大脑无从下手。把它变成以“我们怎样才能……”开头、写清对象和目标的问题：“我们怎样才能帮新同事在第一周找到对的表格？”清楚的问题给搜索指方向，含糊的问题让每个点子看起来要么一样好，要么一样没用。

Step 2 · Limits help第 2 步 · 限制有帮助

Learn先学

List the limits before you start: money, time, tools, rules. This sounds like it would shrink your ideas, but it does the opposite. With a clear limit, such as “it must cost nothing”, the mind looks for clever routes instead of the obvious one. Open choices feel free but often lead to the same safe idea.

开始前列出限制：钱、时间、工具、规定。这听起来会让点子变少，实际相反。有了清楚的限制，比如“不能花钱”，大脑就会去找巧妙的路，而不是最明显的那条。完全开放的选择看似自由，常常只会得到同一个保险的点子。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Who is it for?第 3 步 · 给谁用？

Learn先学

An idea is good only for someone. Write one line about the person who will use it and what they struggle with now. When you later judge ideas, ask “would this help that person?” and not “do I like it?”.

点子是对某个人才算好。写一行字，说明谁会用它、他现在卡在哪里。以后评判点子时，问“这能帮到那个人吗？”，而不是“我喜不喜欢？”。

Question: How could we help new staff find the right form in their first week? Who: new staff, busy, unsure who to ask. Limits: no budget, ready within two weeks, no new software. A good idea: a new person finds the form alone in under five minutes.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Turn a wish into a “How could we ...” question.
• Write the limits first.
• Keep one real person in mind.

• 把愿望变成“我们怎样才能……”的问题。
• 先写限制。
• 心里装着一个真实的人。
