Shaping ideas · Lesson 3打磨想法 · 第 3 课

Make room for ideas给点子留空间

Catch ideas when they come and give your mind rest and variety, because good ideas often arrive when you are not forcing them · ~10 min · reading and practice点子来时把它接住，也给大脑休息和变化，因为好点子常在你不硬逼的时候出现 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Catch it第 1 步 · 把它接住

Learn先学

Ideas often arrive at odd moments: in the shower, on a walk, half asleep. They also vanish quickly. Keep one place to catch them, such as a small notebook or a note on your phone. Write one line, no more. You can sort them later; the point is that nothing is lost.

点子常在奇怪的时刻冒出来：洗澡时、散步时、半睡半醒时，也很快就消失。留一个接住它们的地方，比如一本小本子或手机上的一条备忘。只写一行。以后再整理；要点是什么都不丢。

Step 2 · Step away第 2 步 · 有意走开

Learn先学

After you have worked hard on a question, leave it. Take a walk, do something plain with your hands, sleep on it. Your mind keeps working in the background, and you often return with a new angle. Stepping away is not the opposite of working on the problem; it is part of it.

在你为一个问题下过一番功夫之后，把它放一放。去散个步，做点简单的手工活，睡一觉再说。大脑会在背景里继续工作，你回来时常常带着新角度。走开不是不处理问题，而是处理问题的一部分。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Feed your mind第 3 步 · 给大脑新的“养料”

Learn先学

New ideas are made from what you already know, so widen it. Talk to someone from another job, read something outside your field, walk a different route. A small, regular dose of variety gives you more to join and borrow next time.

新点子是用你已有的东西做出来的，所以要拓宽它。和做别的工作的人聊聊，读一点你领域之外的东西，走一条不同的路。少量而经常的变化，会让你下次有更多可以组合和借用的东西。

Catch: pocket notebook, one line per idea, sorted on Friday (any day works). Step away: 10-minute walk after each hard hour. Feed: one conversation a week with someone from a different team. Read: one article a week outside my field.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Catch ideas in one place.
• Step away on purpose.
• Feed your mind with variety.

• 在一个地方接住点子。
• 有意走开。
• 用变化养大脑。
