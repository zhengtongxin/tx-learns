Finding ideas · Lesson 2找到想法 · 第 2 课

Quantity first先要数量

Collect many ideas before you judge any of them, because the first ideas are usually the obvious ones · ~10 min · reading and practice先收集很多点子，再评判，因为最先冒出来的通常是最明显的 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Make, then judge第 1 步 · 先想，再评判

Learn先学

Thinking up ideas and judging ideas are two different jobs, and they get in each other’s way. If you judge each idea the moment it appears, you stop the next one coming. Do them in order: first list everything without comment, then pick. Even a silly idea can lead to a good one.

想点子和评判点子是两件不同的事，而且会互相妨碍。每个点子一出现就评判，会挡住下一个。按顺序做：先不加评论地列出所有，再挑选。哪怕一个傻点子，也可能引出一个好点子。

Step 2 · A number and a clock第 2 步 · 一个数量和一个计时

Learn先学

Give yourself a target, such as twenty ideas in ten minutes. A number turns a vague feeling of “I am out of ideas” into a task you can finish. Stick to it even when it gets hard. The ideas that come after you thought you were done are often the more original ones.

给自己定一个目标，比如十分钟二十个点子。数字把“我没点子了”这种含糊的感觉，变成可以完成的任务。再难也坚持。你以为已经想完之后冒出来的点子，往往更有新意。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Past the obvious第 3 步 · 越过明显的

Learn先学

The first few ideas are usually the ones everybody would have. Mark them and keep going. To keep moving, change the angle: “What if we had no money?”, “What if a child had to do it?”, “What would be the opposite?”.

最先冒出来的几个，通常是人人都会想到的。标出来，继续往下。想继续，就换个角度：“如果没有钱呢？”“如果让小孩来做呢？”“反过来会怎样？”

Target: 20 ideas in 10 minutes. No judging yet. 1* a printed map 2* a buddy for each new person 3* a short video (* = obvious, everyone thinks of these) ... 11 a form-finder chat 12 a colour code for forms Angle: what if a child had to do it? Then pick: keep three, drop the rest.
