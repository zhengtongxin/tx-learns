Budget and risks预算与风险

Add up what the project will cost with a small reserve, and list what might go wrong and what you will do about it · ~10 min · reading and practice算出项目的成本并留一小笔储备，列出可能出什么问题，以及你打算怎么应对 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Build the budget第 1 步 · 做预算

Learn先学

Cost each task as quantity times rate. Two people working 5 days at 200 a day is 2 × 5 × 200 = 2,000. Add materials of 600, and the base cost is 2,600. Writing the sum down shows where the money goes and lets someone check it.

每个任务的成本等于数量乘单价。两个人干 5 天，每天 200，就是 2 × 5 × 200 = 2,000。再加材料费 600，基础成本是 2,600。把算式写下来，钱花在哪里一目了然，也方便别人核对。

Step 2 · Add a reserve第 2 步 · 加一笔储备

Learn先学

Estimates are never exact, so add a reserve for the unknown, often a percentage. A 10% reserve on 2,600 is 260, so the budget is 2,860. Keep the reserve visible, and spend it only on surprises that are agreed, not on extra wishes.

估算从来不会完全准确，所以要为未知加一笔储备，常常按百分比。2,600 的 10% 是 260，所以预算是 2,860。让储备保持可见，只用于经同意的意外，不用于额外的愿望。

Is each one good practice or weak practice?

每一项是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Risks and responses第 3 步 · 风险与应对

Learn先学

A risk is something that might happen and would matter. Score each on likelihood and impact (1 to 3 each) and multiply: a late supplier, likelihood 2 and impact 3, scores 6; rain on launch day, likelihood 1 and impact 2, scores 2. Deal with the highest first. Choose a response: avoid it, reduce it, share it (for example, pay an insurer to carry the cost) or accept it, and give it an owner. Once a risk happens it is an issue.

风险是可能发生、而且会有影响的事。按可能性和影响各打 1 到 3 分，再相乘：供应商延迟，可能性 2、影响 3，得 6 分；发布日下雨，1 和 2，得 2 分。先处理分数最高的。选一种应对方式：回避、降低、转移（比如付费让保险公司承担成本）或接受，并指定负责人。风险一旦发生，就成了问题。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Cost = quantity × rate, written down.
• Add a visible reserve for surprises.
• Risk score = likelihood × impact.
• Give each risk a response and an owner.
• A risk that happens becomes an issue.

• 成本 = 数量 × 单价，要写下来。
• 为意外加一笔可见的储备。
• 风险分数 = 可能性 × 影响。
• 每个风险都有应对和负责人。
• 发生了的风险就成了问题。
