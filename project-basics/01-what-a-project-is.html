What a project is什么是项目

Tell a project from routine work: it is temporary, it makes something unique, and it ends when its goal is met · ~10 min · reading and practice分清项目和日常工作：项目是临时的，做出独一无二的东西，目标达成就结束 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Temporary and unique第 1 步 · 临时而独特

Learn先学

A project is temporary work that makes something unique: a product, a service or a result. It has a start, an end and a purpose. Organising a school fair, moving an office or launching a small shop website are all projects, because each one happens once and then it is over.

项目是为了做出独一无二的东西（产品、服务或成果）而进行的临时工作。它有开始、有结束、有目的。办一场学校义卖、搬办公室、上线一个小店网站都是项目，因为每一件都只发生一次，做完就结束。

Step 2 · Routine work repeats第 2 步 · 日常工作是重复的

Learn先学

Routine work (also called operations) repeats: answering the same support emails, paying monthly bills, cleaning the shop each night. It has no planned end and it keeps the organisation running. Projects change things; routine work keeps them going. Many jobs mix both.

日常工作（也叫运营）是重复的：回复同类客服邮件、每月付账单、每晚打扫店面。它没有计划好的终点，让组织持续运转。项目改变现状，日常工作维持现状。很多岗位两者兼有。

Is each one good practice or weak practice?

每一项是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Why an end matters第 3 步 · 为什么要有终点

Learn先学

A project is finished when its goal is met, not when people run out of energy. Writing down what “finished” means at the start lets you stop at the right time, hand over the result and move on. Work with no end in sight is usually routine work wearing a project’s name.

项目是在目标达成时结束，不是等大家没力气了才结束。一开始就写下“完成”是什么样子，你才能在合适的时候停下来，交接成果，转向下一件事。看不到终点的工作，往往是披着项目名字的日常工作。

Project (once, has an end): open a new shop website Project (once, has an end): move the office to a new floor Routine (repeats, no end): answer customer emails every day Routine (repeats, no end): pay suppliers every month

Takeaways要点回顾

• A project is temporary work that makes something unique.
• Routine work repeats and keeps things running.
• A project ends when its goal is met.
• Write down what “finished” means at the start.
• Work with no end is usually routine work with a project label.

• 项目是做出独特成果的临时工作。
• 日常工作重复进行，让事情持续运转。
• 项目在目标达成时结束。
• 一开始就写下“完成”的标准。
• 没有终点的工作，通常是贴着项目标签的日常工作。
