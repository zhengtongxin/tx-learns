Writing a status report写一份状态报告

Report progress with three plain numbers, compare them with the plan and end with one clear ask · ~9 min · reading and practice用三个简单的数字汇报进展，与计划比较，并以一个明确的请求结尾 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three plain numbers第 1 步 · 三个简单的数字

Learn先学

A status report is a short note that tells people where the project stands. Start with three numbers: how much work is done, how much money is spent and how much time is gone. Say a project has 20 tasks and 12 are finished: 12 ÷ 20 = 60% done. Its budget is 5,000 and 2,000 is spent: 2,000 ÷ 5,000 = 40% spent. When 9 of the 30 days of a job have passed, 9 ÷ 30 = 30% of the time is gone. Numbers beat words like “going well”, because anyone can check them.

状态报告是一份简短的说明，告诉大家项目现在到了哪一步。先给三个数字：完成了多少工作、花了多少钱、用掉了多少时间。假设项目有 20 项任务，已完成 12 项：12 ÷ 20 = 完成 60%。预算 5,000，已花 2,000：2,000 ÷ 5,000 = 花了 40%。一项 30 天的工作过去了 9 天，9 ÷ 30 = 用掉了 30% 的时间。数字胜过“进展顺利”这类话，因为人人都能核对。

Step 2 · Compare with the plan第 2 步 · 和计划比一比

Learn先学

A number alone says little. Put it next to the plan. If the plan said 10 of the 20 tasks would be done by now and 12 are done, you are 12 − 10 = 2 tasks ahead. If 60% of the work is done and only 40% of the money is spent, the money is holding up well, because money spent should not run ahead of work done. Mark each line with a plain sign: on track, at risk or off track. Do not hide a bad line among good ones.

单独一个数字说明不了什么，要把它放在计划旁边。如果计划说现在 20 项任务应完成 10 项，而你完成了 12 项，就是领先 12 − 10 = 2 项。如果工作完成了 60%，钱只花了 40%，说明资金控制得不错，因为花钱的速度不应该超过工作的进度。给每一行打上简单的标记：正常、有风险或偏离。不要把坏消息藏在好消息中间。

Step 3 · End with one ask第 3 步 · 以一个请求结尾

Learn先学

A good report is short and ends with what you need. Name the top risk and one ask: a decision, some help or a date. For example: “60% done, 40% spent, 2 tasks ahead. Risk: the supplier may be late. Ask: please confirm the order by Friday.” Keep it to a few lines, send it on the same day each week and use the same shape every time, so readers know where to look.

好的报告很短，并以你需要什么结尾。点出最大的风险和一个请求：一个决定、一些帮助或一个日期。比如：“完成 60%，花费 40%，领先 2 项任务。风险：供应商可能迟到。请求：请在周五前确认订单。”控制在几行内，每周同一天发出，每次用同样的格式，读者就知道该看哪里。

Sort each habit as a good way to report progress or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“汇报进展的好方式”或“弱方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Report three plain numbers: work done, money spent, time gone (12 of 20 tasks is 60%).
• Compare each with the plan: 12 done against 10 planned is 2 tasks ahead.
• Mark each line on track, at risk or off track, and do not hide bad news.
• End with the top risk and one ask.
• Keep it short and send it on the same day each week.

• 汇报三个简单的数字：完成的工作、花掉的钱、用掉的时间（20 项完成 12 项是 60%）。
• 把每个数字和计划比较：完成 12 项而计划是 10 项，就是领先 2 项。
• 给每一行标上正常、有风险或偏离，不要藏起坏消息。
• 以最大的风险和一个请求结尾。
• 保持简短，每周同一天发出。
