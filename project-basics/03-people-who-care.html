Starting a project · Lesson 3开始一个项目 · 第 3 课

People who care关心项目的人

Find everyone who is affected by the project or can affect it, learn what each wants, and keep them informed · ~10 min · reading and practice找出受项目影响、或能影响项目的所有人，弄清各自想要什么，并让他们保持知情 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Stakeholders第 1 步 · 相关方

Learn先学

A stakeholder is anyone who is affected by the project or can affect it. The sponsor pays for it and has the final say, the team does the work, users live with the result, and others such as neighbours, regulators or suppliers may care too. Missing one is a classic way to be surprised late.

相关方是受项目影响、或能影响项目的任何人。发起人出钱并拥有最终决定权，团队做事，用户要用成果，另外邻居、监管方、供应商等也可能在意。漏掉一个，是后期出意外的典型原因。

Step 2 · What each one wants第 2 步 · 各自想要什么

Learn先学

Make a short table with one line per person: what they want, what worries them and how much power to decide they have (high means they can approve or stop the work; low means they can only give an opinion). Ask them rather than guessing. A sponsor may care about cost, users about ease of use, the team about a fair workload. You cannot satisfy everyone, but you can know what you are trading.

做一张简短的表，每人一行：他们想要什么、担心什么、有多大话语权（高表示他们能批准或叫停工作，低表示只能提意见）。去问他们，不要猜。发起人可能在意成本，用户在意好不好用，团队在意工作量是否合理。你没法让所有人满意，但你可以知道自己在权衡什么。

Is each one good practice or weak practice?

每一项是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Keep them informed第 3 步 · 让他们保持知情

Learn先学

Match the update to the person. Someone with a lot of power to decide and high interest gets regular, short updates and a voice in decisions. Someone with little power to decide and little interest needs only a short notice, such as one at the start. Fewer surprises mean fewer arguments. Write the plan down, with who, how often and by what channel.

更新方式要因人而异。话语权大又很关心的人，得到定期的简短更新，并参与决定；话语权小又不太关心的人，一份简短的通知（比如开始时的一份）就够了。惊喜越少，争吵越少。把计划写下来：对谁、多久一次、用什么渠道。

Sponsor wants low cost power: high update: every week, short Users want ease of use power: medium update: demo before launch Team want a fair workload power: medium update: daily check-in Neighbour worried about noise power: low update: one notice at the start

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A stakeholder is anyone who affects or is affected.
• The sponsor pays and has the final say.
• Ask people what they want.
• Match each update to the person.
• Late surprises come from missed stakeholders.

• 相关方是影响项目或受项目影响的人。
• 发起人出钱并拥有最终决定权。
• 去问人家想要什么。
• 更新方式因人而异。
• 后期的意外来自被漏掉的相关方。
