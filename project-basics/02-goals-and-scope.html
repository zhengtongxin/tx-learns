Goals and scope目标与范围

Say in one sentence why the project exists, list what is in and out, and spot scope creep before it grows · ~10 min · reading and practice用一句话说明项目为什么存在，列出做什么和不做什么，在范围蔓延之前发现它 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A goal you can check第 1 步 · 能检验的目标

Learn先学

A good goal says what will be different and how you will know. “Make the website better” cannot be checked. “Let customers book a table online, and get at least 20 bookings in the first month” can. Add a date, and the goal tells you when the project is done.

好的目标说明会有什么不同、你怎么知道。“把网站做得更好”无法检验；“让顾客能在线订位，并且第一个月至少有 20 笔订位”就可以。再加上日期，目标就告诉你项目什么时候算完成。

Step 2 · In and out of scope第 2 步 · 范围内与范围外

Learn先学

Scope is the work the project will do. Write it as two short lists: in scope and out of scope. The out-of-scope list is just as useful, because it settles arguments later: “Payment by card is out of scope for this first version.” Everyone sees the same boundary.

范围是项目要做的工作。把它写成两张短清单：范围内和范围外。范围外的清单同样有用，因为它能平息以后的争论：“刷卡付款不在第一版范围内。”大家看到的是同一条边界。

Is each one good practice or weak practice?

每一项是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Scope creep第 3 步 · 范围蔓延

Learn先学

Scope creep is when extra work slips in unnoticed: “while you are at it, could you also…?” Each small extra feels free, but together they eat time and money. The answer is not always no. Ask what the extra costs, and let the person who owns the goal (usually the sponsor, who pays for it) decide, in the open.

范围蔓延是指额外的工作悄悄混进来：“你顺便再做一下……行吗？”每一项小额外看着不花钱，加起来却吞掉时间和预算。答案不一定是不行。问清这个额外要花什么，再由负责目标的人（通常是出钱的发起人）公开地决定。

Goal: Customers can book a table online; 20 bookings in month one. In: booking page, confirmation email, a list for the staff Out: card payment, loyalty points, a phone app New ask: “Add loyalty points?” -> cost it, then the sponsor (who pays for it) decides.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A goal says what changes and how you will know.
• Add a number and a date.
• Write what is in scope and what is out.
• Scope creep is extra work slipping in unnoticed.
• Cost the extra, then decide in the open.

• 目标说明什么会改变，以及你怎么知道。
• 加上数字和日期。
• 写下范围内和范围外的内容。
• 范围蔓延是额外工作悄悄混进来。
• 先算成本，再公开决定。
