Working at a distance · Lesson 2远距离协作 · 第 2 课

Make calls work把线上会议开好

Run a video call so that it has a purpose, everyone can join in, and it ends with clear next steps · ~10 min · reading and practice让线上会议有目的、人人能参与，并以清楚的下一步结束 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Is a call needed?第 1 步 · 需要开会吗？

Learn先学

Before you invite people, ask what a call would do that a clear message cannot. Good reasons: a decision with real disagreement, a delicate topic, a quick back-and-forth that would take twenty messages. If the answer is only sharing information, write it down instead and give people their time back.

邀请别人之前，先问一句：会议能做到什么是一条清楚的消息做不到的？好的理由有：有真正分歧的决定、敏感的话题、需要快速来回而发消息要二十条的事。如果只是分享信息，就写下来，把时间还给大家。

Step 2 · Purpose and agenda第 2 步 · 目的和议程

Learn先学

Send two lines ahead: what the call is for and what you want out of it. List the two or three things to cover, with who leads each. People then arrive ready, and you can say “that is for another time” when the talk wanders.

提前发两行：会议是为了什么，你想得到什么结果。列出要谈的两三件事，以及各由谁负责。大家就能有准备地来，谈偏了你也可以说“这个以后再谈”。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Include everyone, then close第 3 步 · 让每个人参与，然后收尾

Learn先学

On a call it is easy for quiet people to vanish. Ask each person by name for their view, and leave a pause. Finish five minutes early with a recap: who does what by when. Send it in writing straight after, so the people who could not join know too.

线上会议里，安静的人很容易消失。点名问每个人的看法，并留出停顿。提前五分钟收尾，复述：谁在何时做什么。会后马上以文字发出，不能参加的人也能知道。

Purpose: choose the date for the launch. Want out: one date everyone can meet. Cover: (1) the two options, led by Mei; (2) risks, led by Raj. Ends 5 minutes early with a recap. Notes sent the same day: who does what by when.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask whether a call is needed.
• Share the purpose and agenda first.
• Include everyone, then recap who does what by when.

• 先问是否需要开会。
• 先分享目的和议程。
• 让每个人参与，再复述谁在何时做什么。
