Setting yourself up · Lesson 2先把自己安顿好 · 第 2 课

Keep a routine保持作息

Anchor your day with a few fixed points, such as a start time, breaks and a stop time, so it does not drift · ~10 min · reading and practice用几个固定点来撑住一天，比如开始、休息和收工时间，这样它就不会漂移 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three anchors第 1 步 · 三个固定点

Learn先学

You do not need a timetable for every hour. An anchor is a fixed point in the day that stays put while the rest moves. Pick three: when you start, when you take your main break and when you stop. Everything else can move around them. With no anchors, work leaks into the evening and rest leaks into the morning.

你不需要给每个小时排课表。“固定点”是一天里不动的时间点，其余的围着它动。选三个：几点开始、几点吃主要的休息、几点收工。其余的可以围着它们动。没有固定点，工作会漏进晚上，休息会漏进早上。

Step 2 · Real breaks第 2 步 · 真正的休息

Learn先学

A break means leaving the screen: walk, stretch, look out of the window, drink water. Checking a phone is a different kind of screen and does not rest your eyes or your head. Short breaks every hour or so, and a longer one at midday, keep the afternoon from collapsing.

休息指离开屏幕：走一走、拉伸、看看窗外、喝水。看手机是另一种屏幕，眼睛和脑子都没休息。大约每小时一次的短休息，加上中午一次长休息，能让下午不垮掉。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Best hours, hardest work第 3 步 · 最好的时段做最难的事

Learn先学

Notice when you think most clearly: for three days, note when your energy is high and when it drops. Put the work that needs thought there, and leave email and small admin for the slow hours. Guard those best hours: keep them free of meetings where you can, and close chat while you work.

留意自己什么时候思路最清楚：连续三天记下精力什么时候高、什么时候低。把需要动脑的工作放在那时，把邮件和杂务留给低效的时段。守住最好的时段：尽量不安排会议，工作时把聊天关掉。

Start: 9:00, top three tasks first. Main break: 12:30, away from the screen. Stop: 17:30, write tomorrow's first task. Best hours: 9:00 to 11:00, writing and planning only. Slow hours: 14:00 to 15:00, email and admin.
