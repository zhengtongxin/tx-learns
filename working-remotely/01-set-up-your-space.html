Setting yourself up · Lesson 1先把自己安顿好 · 第 1 课

Set up your space布置工作空间

Give your work one steady place, with what you need in reach, so you can start quickly and stop cleanly · ~10 min · reading and practice给工作一个固定的地方，需要的东西触手可及，这样开始得快，也能干净地收工 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One place for work第 1 步 · 一个固定的工作地方

Learn先学

Choose one spot you use only for work, even if it is a corner of a table. Your brain learns the link: sitting here means working. A sofa or a bed sends mixed signals, and it also makes it harder to switch off later. If space is tight, a small ritual such as clearing the table and opening the same notebook can do the same job.

选一个只用来工作的位置，哪怕只是桌子的一角。大脑会学到这种联系：坐在这里就是工作。沙发或床发出的信号是混乱的，下班后也更难放松。如果空间紧张，一个小仪式也行，比如清空桌面、翻开同一本笔记本。

Step 2 · Everything within reach第 2 步 · 需要的东西触手可及

Learn先学

List what you reach for every day: charger, water, notes, headset. Put those within arm’s length. Each time you stand up to look for something, you lose a little focus and it takes a moment to get back. Fix the things you do daily first, and ignore the rare ones.

列出你每天要用的东西：充电器、水、笔记、耳机。把它们放在一臂以内。每次站起来找东西，注意力就会掉一点，回来还要一会儿。先解决每天要做的事，少见的不用管。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Start and stop on purpose第 3 步 · 有意地开始和收工

Learn先学

At home the day can blur. Pick a start action, such as making a drink and writing the top three tasks, and an end action, such as writing tomorrow’s first task and closing the laptop. Both take two minutes and they mark the edges of the day.

在家里，一天容易糊成一团。选一个开始动作，比如泡杯饮料、写下最重要的三件事；再选一个收工动作，比如写下明天第一件事、合上电脑。两个都只要两分钟，却标出了一天的边界。

Place: the small table by the window, work only. Within reach: charger, water, notebook, headset. Start: make tea, write the top three tasks. End: write tomorrow's first task, close the laptop.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Pick one place that means work.
• Keep daily things within reach.
• Mark the start and the end of the day.

• 选一个代表工作的地方。
• 每天用的东西放在手边。
• 标出一天的开始和结束。
