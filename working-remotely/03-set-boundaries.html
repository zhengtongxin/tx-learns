Setting yourself up · Lesson 3先把自己安顿好 · 第 3 课

Set boundaries划清界限

Say when you are available and when you are not, so work and home stay out of each other’s way · ~10 min · reading and practice说清你什么时候能找到、什么时候不能，让工作和家里互不侵占 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Share your hours第 1 步 · 让别人知道你的时间

Learn先学

People cannot guess when you are working. Put your hours where others will see them: in your chat status, your calendar and your email signature. Say it once and plainly: “I am online 9 to 5:30 and reply within a few hours.” Most people then plan around it without being asked.

别人猜不到你什么时候在工作。把你的时间放在别人看得到的地方：聊天状态、日历和邮件签名。清楚地说一次：“我 9 点到 5:30 在线，几小时内回复。”多数人看到后就会自己安排，不用你再提。

Step 2 · The people at home第 2 步 · 家里的人

Learn先学

The people you live with need a simple signal too. It could be a closed door, a sign, or headphones on. Agree what it means and what counts as urgent, so they do not have to guess and you do not have to snap. Boundaries work best when they are agreed, not announced.

和你同住的人也需要一个简单的信号。可以是关着的门、一张牌子，或戴着耳机。约好它代表什么，什么算紧急，这样他们不用猜，你也不用发火。界限最好是商量好的，而不是宣布出来的。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A kind no第 3 步 · 礼貌地说不

Learn先学

A message arrives at 9 pm. Decide whether it is truly urgent. Urgent means a service is down or a person is at risk; anything else can wait. If it can wait, reply in the morning, or say: “I will pick this up first thing tomorrow.” If you always answer at once, people learn that you are always on. A calm, steady answer teaches the opposite without any argument.

晚上 9 点来了一条消息。先判断它是否真的紧急。紧急指服务出了故障或有人面临风险，其余的都可以等。可以等的，就第二天早上回，或者说：“我明天一早处理。”如果你总是马上回，别人就会认为你随时都在。平静、稳定的回应，不用争吵就能教会别人相反的事。

Status: online 9:00 to 17:30, replies within a few hours. At home: door closed means focus, knock only for urgent. After hours: "I will pick this up first thing tomorrow." Urgent means: a service is down or a person is at risk.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Tell people your hours.
• Agree a signal with people at home.
• Answer out-of-hours messages calmly and later.

• 告诉别人你的时间。
• 和家里人约好一个信号。
• 平静地、稍后回复下班后的消息。
