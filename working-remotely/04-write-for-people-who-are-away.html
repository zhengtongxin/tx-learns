Working at a distance · Lesson 1远距离协作 · 第 1 课

Write for people who are away给不在场的人写清楚

Write messages that can be understood and acted on without a follow-up, so work keeps moving across hours and places · ~10 min · reading and practice写出不用追问就能看懂、能照着做的消息，让工作在不同时间和地点之间照样推进 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Ask first, with a date第 1 步 · 先写请求，带上日期

Learn先学

The reader may see your message hours later and be busy. Lead with what you need and when: “Please review the budget table by Thursday noon.” Then give the reason. A message that begins with three paragraphs of history hides the ask, and the reply is slower.

读者可能几个小时后才看到消息，而且很忙。开头就写你需要什么、什么时候要：“请在周四中午前审阅预算表。”然后再讲原因。一上来就是三段背景的消息会藏起请求，回复也就更慢。

Step 2 · Enough context to stand alone第 2 步 · 自带足够的背景

Learn先学

A message stands alone when the reader can act on it without asking you anything. Write as if the reader has not been in your head. Name the file, add the link, say what you already tried, and say what “done” looks like. One complete message is better than five short ones that need answers before anyone can act.

消息“自成一体”，是指读者不用问你任何问题就能照着做。写的时候假设读者不在你脑子里。写出文件名，附上链接，说明你已经试过什么，说清“完成”是什么样子。一条完整的消息，胜过五条要先回答才能行动的短消息。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Pick the right channel第 3 步 · 选对渠道

Learn先学

Use chat for quick, small things that can wait a little. Use email or a shared document for anything someone must keep, find later or read carefully. Use a call when there is real disagreement or feelings are involved. Then write down what you decided so people who were away can see it.

聊天适合快速、小而且可以稍等的事。需要留存、以后要查或要细读的内容，用邮件或共享文档。有真正分歧或涉及情绪时，用通话。然后把决定写下来，让不在场的人也能看到。

Please review the budget table by Thursday noon. Why: it goes to the client on Friday. File: budget-v3 in the shared folder, sheet "Summary". I already checked the totals; I am unsure about row 12. Done means: you reply "approved" or list changes.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Put the ask and the date first.
• Add enough context to stand alone.
• Choose the channel on purpose and record decisions.

• 请求和日期放最前。
• 补足背景，自成一体。
• 有意选渠道，并记录决定。
