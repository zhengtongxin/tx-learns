Working at a distance · Lesson 3远距离协作 · 第 3 课

Stay connected保持联系

Build small, steady contact with the people you work with, so trust and help are there when you need them · ~10 min · reading and practice和一起工作的人保持细小而稳定的联系，需要时信任和帮助就在那里 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Make your work visible第 1 步 · 让你的工作被看见

Learn先学

In an office people see you at your desk. At a distance they see nothing unless you show it. A short note at the start or end of the day, such as “today: report draft, tomorrow: review”, keeps others informed and saves them from asking. It also helps you notice when you are stuck.

在办公室里，别人看得到你在桌前。远距离时，除非你展示，否则别人什么也看不到。在一天开始或结束时写一条简短的话，比如“今天：报告初稿；明天：审阅”，别人就知道进展，也省得问你。它还能帮你发现自己卡住了。

Step 2 · Time for people第 2 步 · 给人留时间

Learn先学

The casual talk you get in an office hallway does not happen online, so make a little room for it. Start a call with a minute of how things are. Have an optional coffee call now and then. Ask about something that is not work. These small moments build the trust that makes harder talks easier.

办公室走廊里的随口闲聊在线上不会自然发生，所以要给它留一点空间。会议开头花一分钟问问大家近况。偶尔来一次自愿参加的咖啡聊天。问一句和工作无关的事。这些小片刻建立起信任，让难谈的话变得容易。

Step 3 · Notice and reach out第 3 步 · 留意并伸出手

Learn先学

Distance hides trouble. Signs can be short replies, missed deadlines, a camera always off or going quiet. Do not guess what is wrong; just ask: “How are you doing with the workload this week?” and listen. Offer one small, specific piece of help. A calm check-in costs a minute and may matter a lot.

距离会掩盖困难。迹象可能是回复变短、错过截止日期、摄像头一直关着，或变得沉默。不用猜原因，只要问：“这周的工作量你还好吗？”然后认真听。提供一个具体的小帮助。一次平静的问候只花一分钟，却可能很重要。

Daily note: today: report draft. tomorrow: review. blocked: waiting for data. Weekly: 15-minute optional coffee call, no agenda. Check-in line: "How are you doing with the workload this week?" Then: listen, offer one small thing.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Make your work visible.
• Make time for people, not only tasks.
• Notice, ask, listen and offer one small help.

• 让工作被看见。
• 给人留时间，不只给任务。
• 留意、询问、倾听，再给一个小帮助。
