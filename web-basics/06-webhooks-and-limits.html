Webhooks and limitsWebhook 与限额

Let a service call you instead of polling, and live within rate limits and pagination · ~10 min · reading and practice让服务来调用你而不是你去轮询，并在限速和分页之内工作 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Do not keep asking第 1 步 · 别一直问

Learn先学

Asking “is it done yet?” every few seconds is polling: wasteful and slow. A webhook flips it: you give the service a URL of yours, and when something happens it sends you a POST. Your server answers 200 quickly and does the real work afterwards. Because anyone can post to a public URL, check the signature or secret the service sends before trusting the message.

每隔几秒问一次“好了没”叫轮询：浪费又慢。Webhook 把它倒过来：你把自己的一个网址交给服务，事情一发生，它就给你发一个 POST。你的服务器迅速回答 200，之后再做真正的工作。因为任何人都能向公开网址发 POST，所以相信消息之前，要先核对服务随消息发来的签名或密钥。

Step 2 · Rate limits第 2 步 · 限速

Learn先学

Services cap how many calls you may make, for example 60 a minute. Go over and you get 429, often with a Retry-After header saying how many seconds to wait. Respect it: wait that long, or space your calls out, instead of hammering.

服务会限制你的调用次数，比如每分钟 60 次。超了就会得到 429，往往还带一个 Retry-After 头，说明要等多少秒。请遵守：等够这么久，或把调用间隔拉开，而不是猛敲。

Step 3 · Long lists第 3 步 · 很长的列表

Learn先学

A list of thousands is returned in pages. Each response holds a chunk and a way to the next: a page number or a next cursor. Loop until there is no next, and collect as you go.

几千条的列表会分页返回。每个响应里有一块数据，以及去下一页的方式：页码或 next 游标。循环到没有下一页为止，边走边收集。
