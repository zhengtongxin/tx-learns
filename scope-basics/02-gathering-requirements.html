Defining the work · Lesson 2把工作讲清楚 · 第 2 课

Gathering requirements收集需求

Find out what the result must do by asking and watching, then sort it by how much it matters · ~10 min · reading and practice通过询问和观察弄清成果必须做到什么，再按重要程度分类 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Ask and watch第 1 步 · 问和看

Learn先学

A requirement is something the result must do or have. Find them by asking people what would make the result a success, and by watching how they work today. Visitors may say “easy to find the right classroom”. A teacher may be seen ticking names on a paper list. Both are clues to requirements.

需求是成果必须做到或必须具备的东西。找需求有两个办法：问人们怎样才算成功，以及看他们现在是怎么工作的。访客可能说“容易找到对的教室”；老师可能在一张纸上逐个打勾点名。两者都是需求的线索。

Step 2 · Must, should or nice第 2 步 · 必须、应该还是锦上添花

Learn先学

You will rarely have time for everything. Sort each requirement as a must have (the project fails without it), a should have (important, but there is a way around it) or a nice to have (good if time allows). Do all the musts first. Of 12 requirements, say 5 are musts, 4 are shoulds and 3 are nice: 5 + 4 + 3 = 12.

你很少有时间做完所有事。把每条需求分成必须有（没有它项目就失败）、应该有（重要，但有办法绕过）和锦上添花（有时间才做）。先把“必须有”全部做完。假设 12 条需求里，5 条必须、4 条应该、3 条锦上添花：5 + 4 + 3 = 12。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Write it so it can be checked第 3 步 · 写到能检验

Learn先学

A requirement such as “good tours” cannot be checked. “A tour starts every 20 minutes between 9 and 11” can. Put a number, a time or a yes/no test in each one. A checkable requirement saves arguments later, because you can both see whether it was met.

“好的导览”这样的需求没法检验；“9 点到 11 点之间每 20 分钟出发一个导览”就可以。每一条都要有数字、时间或是/否的检验方式。能检验的需求能省去日后的争执，因为双方都看得出有没有达到。

Must : a tour starts every 20 minutes, 9 to 11 Must : a sign-up sheet at the front gate Should : name badges for the tour guides Nice : a photo wall Count : 5 must + 4 should + 3 nice = 12

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A requirement is something the result must do or have.
• Find them by asking and watching.
• Sort into must, should and nice, and do the musts first.
• Write each so it can be checked with a number or a yes/no test.

• 需求是成果必须做到或具备的东西。
• 通过问和看来找需求。
• 分成必须、应该、锦上添花，先做必须的。
• 每条都写到能用数字或是/否检验。
