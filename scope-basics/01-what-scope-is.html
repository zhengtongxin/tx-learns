What scope is范围是什么

Draw a clear line around what a project will do and what it will not do · ~10 min · reading and practice在项目要做的事和不做的事之间划一条清楚的线 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Scope is the edge of the work第 1 步 · 范围是工作的边界

Learn先学

Scope is everything a project will deliver, plus the work needed to deliver it. Take a school open-house day for 300 visitors. The scope might be guided tours, a welcome desk and a sign-up sheet. A food stall may be a good idea, but if it is not in the scope it is not part of this project.

范围是项目要交付的一切，加上为交付所需做的工作。以一所学校的开放日（300 位访客）为例：范围可能是导览、接待台和一张报名表。设一个美食摊也许是个好主意，但不在范围里，就不属于这个项目。

Step 2 · Write what is out as well第 2 步 · 把不做的也写下来

Learn先学

Most people write only what is in. Add an out list too: no food, no transport, no live music. Without it, each person fills the gaps with their own hopes, and the surprise comes late. “Out for now” is fine: it says the idea is not lost, only not part of this project.

多数人只写“做什么”。再加一份不做清单：不供应食物、不安排交通、不请现场音乐。没有它，每个人都会用自己的期望去填补空白，惊讶来得很晚。“暂时不做”也可以：它说明这个想法没有丢，只是不属于这个项目。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · One sentence that sums it up第 3 步 · 一句话概括

Learn先学

Finish with one scope sentence that names what, who for, by when, and what is left out. It should fit in a message. If you cannot say it in a sentence, the scope is still fuzzy. Share it with the people who decide and ask: “Is this what you expect?”

最后写一句范围说明：做什么、为谁、何时之前、什么不做。它应该能放进一条消息里。一句话说不清，说明范围还模糊。把它发给拍板的人，问一句：“这是你期望的吗？”

In : tours, a welcome desk, a sign-up sheet Out: food, transport, live music (out for now) Scope: We will run tours, a welcome desk and a sign-up sheet for up to 300 visitors on 14 March. We will not provide food, transport or music.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Scope is everything the project will deliver, plus the work to deliver it.
• Write what is out as well as what is in.
• “Out for now” keeps an idea without adding it.
• Sum it up in one sentence the deciders can check.

• 范围是项目要交付的一切，加上交付所需的工作。
• 不做的也要写，和做的一样。
• “暂时不做”能留住想法，又不把它加进来。
• 用一句拍板的人能核对的话概括。
