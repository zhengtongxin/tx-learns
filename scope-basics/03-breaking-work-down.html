Defining the work · Lesson 3把工作讲清楚 · 第 3 课

Breaking work down把工作拆开

Split the scope into small pieces that can each be estimated and owned, and check nothing is missing · ~10 min · reading and practice把范围拆成每块都能估算、有人负责的小块，并检查没有遗漏 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Break it into pieces第 1 步 · 拆成小块

Learn先学

Big work hides surprises. Split it into parts, then split each part, until a piece is small enough to estimate and to give to one person. “Run the open house” is too big. “Print 300 sign-up sheets” is a piece. Stop splitting when a piece is clear.

大工作会藏着意外。把它拆成几部分，再把每部分继续拆，直到每一块小到能估算、能交给一个人。“办开放日”太大；“打印 300 张报名表”就是一块。拆到清楚了就停。

Step 2 · The pieces must add up to the whole第 2 步 · 各块要加起来等于整体

Learn先学

Check that the pieces together make exactly the whole job: nothing missing, nothing extra. Say the open house takes 20 days of effort in total: tours 8, welcome desk 4, sign-up 3, publicity 5. 8 + 4 + 3 + 5 = 20, so it adds up. If the parts add to 17 and the total says 20, three days are hiding somewhere.

检查各块合起来正好是整件工作：不缺、不多。假设开放日总共需要 20 天工作量：导览 8、接待台 4、报名 3、宣传 5。8 + 4 + 3 + 5 = 20，对得上。如果各块加起来是 17，而总数写的是 20，就有三天藏在某个地方。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · One owner for each piece第 3 步 · 每块一位负责人

Learn先学

Put one name beside each piece. A piece with two owners often has none, because each person thinks the other has it. A piece with no name will not get done. Names also make it easy to ask “how is it going?”.

每一块旁边写一个名字。有两位负责人的块，往往等于没人负责，因为各人都以为对方在做。没有名字的块不会有人做。有了名字，也容易问一句“进展如何？”。

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Split work until a piece can be estimated and owned by one person.
• The pieces must add up to the whole.
• A gap between the parts and the total means something is missing.
• One name per piece.

• 拆到一块能估算、能由一个人负责。
• 各块必须加起来等于整体。
• 各块和总数之间有缺口，说明漏了东西。
• 每块一个名字。
