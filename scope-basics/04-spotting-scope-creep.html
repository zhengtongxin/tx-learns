Spotting scope creep发现范围蔓延

Notice small extras that slip into a project without a decision, before they add up · ~10 min · reading and practice在小额外要求累积起来之前，发现那些没经过决定就溜进项目的小额外 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Small extras add up第 1 步 · 小额外会累积

Learn先学

Scope creep is work added to a project without anyone deciding to add it. Each extra looks small: “while you are at it, could you also…”. Five extras of one day each on a 20-day project add 5 days, which is 25% more work, with no new time or money.

范围蔓延是指在没人决定要加的情况下，项目里被加进的工作。每个额外要求看起来都很小：“顺便，能不能也……”。20 天的项目上加五个各一天的额外要求，就多了 5 天，也就是多了 25% 的工作，却没有新增时间或预算。

Step 2 · The usual signs第 2 步 · 常见的迹象

Learn先学

Watch for: a request that begins “can you just…”, work that is not on the breakdown, asks that arrive in chat rather than through the plan, and nobody saying what will be pushed out to make room. The team is busier, but the finish date has not moved, and that gap is the signal.

留意这些迹象：以“你能不能就……”开头的请求；不在拆分清单上的工作；通过聊天而不是计划提出的要求；没人说为了腾出位置要把什么推后。团队更忙了，完成日期却没动，这个落差就是信号。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · The in-list test第 3 步 · “做清单”测试

Learn先学

When an ask arrives, check the in list and the out list. If it is on the in list, it is normal work. If it is on neither list, or on the out list, it is a change, and it should not be quietly done. Say: “That is outside the scope we agreed. Let me see what it would take.”

有要求进来时，查一下“做清单”和“不做清单”。在“做清单”上，就是正常工作。两张清单上都没有，或者在“不做清单”上，它就是一个变更，不该被悄悄做掉。可以说：“这超出了我们商定的范围，让我看看需要什么。”

Takeaways要点回顾

• Scope creep is work added without anyone deciding.

• Check an ask against the in and out lists.
• Small extras add up to big ones.
