Handling a change request处理变更请求

Weigh a requested change by its effect on time, cost and quality, then decide and record it · ~10 min · reading and practice按变更对时间、成本和质量的影响来权衡，再做决定并记录 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Ask for it in writing第 1 步 · 请对方写下来

Learn先学

A change request is a short written ask to change the scope. Write three lines: what is wanted, why it matters and who asked. Writing it slows a casual “just add it” into a real question, and it leaves a record that anyone can read later.

变更请求是一份要求改变范围的简短书面请求。写三行：要什么、为什么重要、谁提出。写下来能把随口一句“加上就行”变成一个正式问题，也留下一份日后谁都能读到的记录。

Step 2 · Weigh the effect第 2 步 · 权衡影响

Learn先学

For each request work out the effect on time, cost and quality or risk. Say live music would take 3 more days at $400 a day: 3 × 400 = $1,200 more. The job grows from 20 to 23 days, a 15% rise (3 ÷ 20). Putting numbers beside the request makes it easier to decide.

对每个请求算出它对时间、成本和质量或风险的影响。假设请现场音乐要多花 3 天，每天 400 新元：3 × 400 = 1,200 新元。工作量从 20 天涨到 23 天，上升 15%（3 ÷ 20）。把数字摆在请求旁边，更容易做决定。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Decide and record第 3 步 · 决定并记录

Learn先学

The person who owns the budget or the goals decides: approve, defer to later, or decline. If approved, update the plan, the budget and the dates, and tell everyone. If declined, say why. Either way write the answer in the change record, so the same ask does not come back as a surprise.

由掌管预算或目标的人决定：批准、推迟到以后，或拒绝。批准了，就更新计划、预算和日期，并通知所有人；拒绝了，要说明理由。无论哪种，都把结果写进变更记录，免得同一个要求日后又作为意外冒出来。

What : add live music for two hours Why : visitors stay longer Who : the parents’ group Time : +3 days (20 -> 23, up 15%) Cost : +3 x $400 = $1,200 Risk : needs a power point and a noise check Decision: defer to next year (principal)

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask for a change in writing: what, why, who.
• Weigh its effect on time, cost and risk with numbers.
• Approve, defer or decline, and say why.
• Record it and update the plan.

• 让对方书面提出变更：什么、为什么、谁。
• 用数字权衡它对时间、成本和风险的影响。
• 批准、推迟或拒绝，并说明理由。
• 记录下来，并更新计划。
