Keeping it in bounds · Lesson 4把它控制在范围内 · 第 4 课

Trading scope for time用范围换时间

Show what a late request costs in time and offer a plain choice: trade, move the date or add help · ~9 min · reading and practice算出迟到请求在时间上的代价，并给出清楚的选择：交换、推迟日期或增加人手 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The budget of time第 1 步 · 时间这笔预算

Learn先学

Scope, time and effort pull against each other. Say the work is planned at 20 days and the deadline is 20 days. A late request adds 4 days of work. Now it is 20 + 4 = 24 days, which is 4 ÷ 20 = 20% more. If the deadline and the team stay the same, 4 days do not fit. Something has to give, and it is better to say so than to quietly work late.

范围、时间和人力互相牵制。假设工作计划需要 20 天，截止期也是 20 天。一个迟到的请求增加了 4 天的工作量。现在是 20 + 4 = 24 天，即 4 ÷ 20 = 多了 20%。如果截止期和团队都不变，这 4 天就放不下。总得有东西让步，直接说出来，比悄悄加班好。

Step 2 · Three honest ways out第 2 步 · 三种坦诚的出路

Learn先学

Offer the person asking a plain choice. Trade: drop something of the same size. Move the date: accept 24 days instead of 20. Add help: bring in more people, knowing a new person needs time to learn first. Never say yes to all three at once, and never pretend the extra 4 days cost nothing.

给提出请求的人一个清楚的选择。交换：放弃一件同样大的事。推迟日期：接受 24 天而不是 20 天。增加人手：加人，但要知道新人先需要时间学习。不要对三样都说好，也不要假装多出的 4 天不花任何代价。

Step 3 · Trade the least important第 3 步 · 先换掉最不重要的

Learn先学

Rank the work as must, should or nice to have, as you did in the requirements. Suppose the plan holds a must of 10 days, a should of 6 days and a nice-to-have of 4 days: 10 + 6 + 4 = 20. Dropping the nice-to-have makes room for the new 4 days: 10 + 6 + 4 (new) = 20. Cut from the bottom first, and keep the musts. Write the trade down so everyone sees what moved.

像收集需求时那样，把工作分为必须、应该和锦上添花。假设计划里必须的是 10 天，应该的是 6 天，锦上添花的是 4 天：10 + 6 + 4 = 20。放弃锦上添花的部分，就给新增的 4 天腾出了位置：10 + 6 + 4（新）= 20。先从最下面砍，保住必须的。把这次交换写下来，让大家都看到什么变了。

Sort each habit as a good way to answer a late request or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“回应迟到请求的好方式”或“弱方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A late request has a cost in time: 4 days on 20 is 24 days, or 20% more.
• Offer a plain choice: trade, move the date or add help.
• Never say yes to everything or hide the extra days.
• Rank the work as must, should and nice to have, and cut from the bottom first.
• Write the trade down so everyone sees what moved.

• 迟到的请求在时间上有代价：20 天加 4 天是 24 天，多了 20%。
• 给出清楚的选择：交换、推迟日期或增加人手。
• 不要对所有事都说好，也不要藏起多出的天数。
• 把工作分为必须、应该和锦上添花，先从最下面砍。
• 把交换写下来，让大家看到什么变了。
