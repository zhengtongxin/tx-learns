Acceptance and sign-off验收与签收

Agree how you will know the work is done before you start, then check it against that list · ~10 min · reading and practice开工之前约定怎样才算完成，之后对照清单检查 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Write the finish line first第 1 步 · 先画终点线

Learn先学

Acceptance criteria are the checks that say a piece of work is done. Write them before the work starts, with the person who will accept it. For the sign-up sheet: “Has a line for name and class. Fits on one page. 300 copies printed.” Each can be answered yes or no.

验收标准是说明一项工作已经完成的检验条件。在工作开始之前，和将要验收的人一起写下来。以报名表为例：“有姓名和班级栏。一页纸装得下。印好 300 份。”每一条都能回答“是”或“否”。

Step 2 · Check against the list第 2 步 · 对照清单检查

Learn先学

When the work is ready, go through the criteria one by one. Say 8 of 10 are met: that is 80%, which is not yet done. Do not accept on a feeling of “nearly there”. Either fix the other 2 or agree openly, in writing, to accept without them.

工作完成后，把标准逐条过一遍。假设 10 条里达到 8 条：那是 80%，还不算完成。不要凭“差不多了”的感觉就接受。要么把剩下 2 条做好，要么公开、书面地同意不含这 2 条也接受。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Sign off and close第 3 步 · 签收并关闭

Learn先学

Ask the accepting person to sign off: a short note saying the criteria are met. Record who, what and when. Sign-off closes that part of the scope, so later asks about it are changes, not “you forgot”. It protects both sides.

请验收人签收：一条简短的话，说明标准都已达到，并记下谁、什么、何时。签收关闭了这部分范围，之后再提出的要求就是变更，而不是“你忘了”。这对双方都是保护。

Sign-up sheet: accepted when [x] has a line for name and class [x] fits on one page [x] 300 copies printed Met 3 of 3. Signed off by Ms Tan, 12 March.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write acceptance criteria before the work starts.
• Check the result against the list, one by one.
• 80% met is not yet done.
• Sign off in writing to close that part of the scope.

• 工作开始前写好验收标准。
• 把成果对照清单逐条检查。
• 达到 80% 还不算完成。
• 书面签收，关闭这部分范围。
