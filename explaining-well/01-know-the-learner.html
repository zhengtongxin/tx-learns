Know the learner了解听的人

Find out what the other person already knows and wants, so you start from where they are · ~10 min · reading and practice先弄清对方已经知道什么、想要什么，从他们所在的地方开始讲 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Start from them, not from you第 1 步 · 从对方出发，而不是从你出发

Learn先学

An explanation that suits an expert fails a beginner, and the reverse is just as bad. Before you start, ask one or two plain questions: what have you already seen of this, and what do you want to do with it? The answers tell you where to begin and what to leave out.

适合专家的讲法对初学者行不通，反过来也同样糟糕。开始之前，先问一两个简单的问题：你对这件事已经见过些什么？你想用它来做什么？答案会告诉你从哪里开始、哪些可以省略。

Step 2 · Words they already own第 2 步 · 他们已经懂的词

Learn先学

Use the words your listener already uses. Jargon you picked up without noticing is the most common reason an explanation fails. When you must use a technical word, say what it means in everyday words the first time, then use it consistently.

用听的人已经在用的词。你不知不觉学来的行话，是讲解失败最常见的原因。非用专业词不可时，第一次出现就用日常话说明它的意思，之后保持用法一致。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · One step beyond第 3 步 · 比他们多一步

Learn先学

Aim to start one step beyond what they know. Too close and they are bored; too far and they are lost. If you are unsure, begin simple and move faster when they keep up. Their face and their questions tell you more than your plan does. For example, if they know what a file is, the next step is a program that reads one.

目标是从比他们现有知识多一步的地方开始。太近会无聊，太远会迷路。拿不准时，先从简单的讲起，他们跟得上再加快。他们的表情和问题，比你的计划更能说明问题。比如，他们已经知道文件是什么，下一步就是读取文件的程序。

Ask: What have you seen of this so far? Ask: What do you want to do with it? Then start one step beyond the answer.
