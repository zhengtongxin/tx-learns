Handling questions应对提问

Welcome questions, find the gap behind them and answer in a new way instead of repeating yourself · ~10 min · reading and practice欢迎提问，找出问题背后的缺口，并换一种方式回答，而不是原样重复 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Welcome the question第 1 步 · 欢迎提问

Learn先学

A question means the listener is thinking. Thank them for it, and make it easy to ask more: pause after each part and ask what is unclear. If a question feels like a challenge, answer the honest part of it, not the tone.

提问说明听者在思考。要感谢他们，并让后续提问变得容易：每一部分之后停一下，问哪里不清楚。如果一个问题听起来像挑战，就回答其中真诚的部分，而不是回应语气。

Step 2 · Find what is behind it第 2 步 · 找出背后的原因

Learn先学

The question asked is not always the question meant. Check by saying it back in your own words: “So you are asking whether…?”. Often the real gap is one step earlier than the question suggests, in a word or idea you skipped over.

被问出来的问题，并不总是真正想问的问题。你可以用自己的话复述一遍来确认：“你是想问……吗？”。真正的缺口往往比问题暗示的更早一步，藏在你略过的某个词或想法里。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A new way, and honesty第 3 步 · 换个方式，并保持诚实

Learn先学

Saying the same words again, louder, rarely helps. Try another example, a picture or a smaller step. If you do not know the answer, say so, promise to find out and follow up. “I do not know yet” keeps trust; a bluff loses it.

把同样的话再说一遍、说得更大声，很少有用。换一个例子、一幅图，或者一个更小的步骤。如果你不知道答案，就直说，并承诺去查清楚、之后回复。“我还不知道”能保住信任，硬编则会失去它。

Listener: Is a recipe the same as an outline? You: So you are asking how they differ? You: Here is a different example... If unsure: I do not know yet; I will find out and tell you.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Welcome questions.
• Find the gap behind them.
• Answer in a new way.

• 欢迎提问。
• 找出问题背后的缺口。
