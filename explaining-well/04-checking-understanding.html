While you explain · Lesson 1讲的时候 · 第 1 课

Checking understanding检查理解

Find out whether it landed by asking the listener to explain it back or try it, instead of asking if they understood · ~10 min · reading and practice让听者复述或亲手试一试，来确认他们是否真的懂了，而不是问“懂了吗” · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · “Got it?” tells you little第 1 步 · “懂了吗？”几乎没有信息

Learn先学

Most people say yes to “do you understand?” even when they do not, because nobody wants to look slow. Ask something that shows it instead: “How would you explain this to a friend?” or “What would you do first?”. An answer reveals what a yes hides.

“你懂了吗？”这个问题，多数人即使没懂也会说“懂了”，因为谁都不想显得慢。改问能暴露真实情况的问题：“你会怎么讲给朋友听？”或“你第一步会做什么？”。一个回答能揭示“是”所隐藏的东西。

Step 2 · Let them try第 2 步 · 让他们试一试

Learn先学

The best check is a small task: let them do one step, or solve a similar example, while you watch. Do not rescue them too fast; a short struggle shows you exactly where the gap is and teaches them more than being told.

最好的检查是一个小任务：让他们做一步，或解一个类似的例子，你在一旁看着。不要太快救场；短暂的挣扎能让你看清缺口究竟在哪，也比直接被告知学得更多。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Read the gaps第 3 步 · 读出缺口

Learn先学

Treat a wrong or partial answer as information about your explanation, not about the listener. Note which part they missed, and go back to that part with a different example. If most of a short check is right, the rest is a gap to close. As a rule of thumb, 4 of 5 right means move on and 3 of 5 means go back to the missed part, but it is your call.

把错误或不完整的回答看作关于你讲解的信息，而不是关于听者的评判。记下他们漏掉了哪一部分，用另一个例子回到那一部分。如果一个简短的检查大部分答对了，其余的就是要补上的缺口。经验上，5 题对 4 题可以往下讲，对 3 题就回到没懂的那部分，但最终由你判断。

Ask: How would you explain this to a friend? Ask: What would you do first? 5 short questions: 3 right, 2 wrong. 3 out of 5 = 60%. Go back to the 2 gaps.
