Before you explain · Lesson 2讲之前 · 第 2 课

One idea at a time一次讲一个点

Break an explanation into small parts, put the main point first and keep each part to one idea · ~10 min · reading and practice把讲解拆成小块，先讲要点，并让每一块只讲一个想法 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The point first第 1 步 · 先讲要点

Learn先学

Begin with the one thing you want them to take away, in a sentence. Then explain it. A listener who knows where you are going can fit each part into the picture, while a listener who does not must hold everything in mind until the end.

先用一句话说出你希望他们带走的那一件事，然后再展开。知道你要去哪里的听者，能把每一部分放进整体图景；不知道的听者，则要把所有东西记在脑子里直到最后。

Step 2 · Small parts第 2 步 · 小块

Learn先学

People hold only a few new things in mind at once. Split a long topic into short parts, each with one idea, and give each a plain name. Twelve loose steps are hard to follow; the same twelve in three groups of four are easy.

人一次只能记住少数几件新东西。把长主题拆成简短的几块，每块只有一个想法，并给每块起个朴素的名字。十二个零散的步骤很难跟上，同样的十二步分成三组、每组四步，就容易多了。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Order and pauses第 3 步 · 顺序与停顿

Learn先学

Put the parts in the order a newcomer would meet them, simple before complex, and pause between parts so each one can settle. Say what the next part is before you start it, so the listener always knows where they are.

把各块按新手会遇到的顺序排列，先简单后复杂，并在各块之间停一停，让每块沉淀下来。开始下一块之前先说它是什么，让听的人始终知道自己在哪里。

Point: a recipe is a list of steps in order. Part 1: gather what you need. Part 2: do the steps in order. Part 3: check the result. 12 steps in groups of 4 make 3 groups. Each group is one part.
