While you explain · Lesson 3讲的时候 · 第 3 课

Practice and feedback练习与反馈

Improve how you explain by practising small, asking for feedback and noting what to change next time · ~10 min · reading and practice通过小范围练习、请人反馈，并记下下次要改的地方，不断提高你的讲解能力 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Practise small第 1 步 · 小范围练习

Learn先学

Explaining is a skill, and it improves with repetition on small things: how to make a drink, how to find a file, how to read a bill. Choose a short topic, explain it to a real person, and keep it under a few minutes so you can repeat it often.

讲解是一项技能，在小事上反复练习就会进步：怎么冲一杯饮料、怎么找到一个文件、怎么看一份账单。选一个简短的主题，讲给真人听，控制在几分钟之内，这样就能经常重复。

Step 2 · Ask for specific feedback第 2 步 · 请人给具体的反馈

Learn先学

General praise teaches little. Ask specific questions: “Where did you get lost?”, “Which part was clearest?”. Specific questions get specific answers you can use. Listen without defending; thank them and decide later what to change.

笼统的夸奖教不了什么。要问具体的问题：“你在哪里开始迷糊？”“哪一部分最清楚？”。具体的问题才能换来你用得上的具体回答。先听，不辩解；道谢，之后再决定改什么。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · One change第 3 步 · 只改一件事

Learn先学

After each try, write one thing that worked and one thing to change next time. Changing one thing at a time lets you see its effect. Keep the notes; reading them back after a month shows how much better you have become.

每次尝试之后，写下一件有效的事和一件下次要改的事。一次只改一件，就能看出它的效果。把笔记留着；一个月后回头翻看，你会发现自己进步了多少。

Worked: the labelled-box analogy for variables landed. Change: slow down after the second part. Then: ask "what would you do first?" at the end.
