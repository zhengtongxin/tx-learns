Examples and analogies例子与类比

Make an idea concrete with a worked example and an analogy from something familiar, and say where the analogy stops · ~10 min · reading and practice用一个演算过的例子和一个熟悉事物的类比让想法变具体，并说明类比在哪里不再适用 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A worked example第 1 步 · 一个演算过的例子

Learn先学

A rule alone is abstract; an example shows it working. Pick one small, real example and walk through it step by step, saying what you do and why. Let the listener see a finished example before they try one of their own.

光有规则是抽象的，例子才能让规则“动起来”。挑一个小而真实的例子，一步一步走一遍，说明你做什么、为什么这样做。先让听的人看到一个完整的例子，再让他们自己试。

Task: store a person's age. Step 1: choose a name for it: age. Step 2: store the value: age = 12. Why: the name lets us find the value again later.

Step 2 · An analogy第 2 步 · 一个类比

Learn先学

An analogy explains something new by comparing it to something the listener already knows, such as “a variable is a labelled box”. It gives them a picture to hang the idea on. Choose something from their world, not yours.

类比是把新东西与听的人已经熟悉的东西作比较来解释，比如“变量就像贴了标签的盒子”。它给听者一幅可以挂住这个想法的画面。要选他们世界里的东西，而不是你的。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Where it stops第 3 步 · 它在哪里不再适用

Learn先学

Every analogy breaks somewhere, and a listener may carry it too far. Say plainly where it stops, then give one example that shows the real thing. Two or three examples that differ a little show what stays the same.

每个类比都会在某处失效，听的人也可能把它推得太远。要直接说明它在哪里不再适用，再给一个展示真实情形的例子。两三个略有差异的例子，能显示什么是不变的。

Idea: a variable stores a value (a piece of information, such as 12). Analogy: a labelled box. Where it stops: a box can hold anything, but a variable keeps one value and loses the old one when you store a new one. Example: age = 12, then age = 13.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Show a worked example.
• Use a familiar analogy.
• Say where it stops.

• 展示演算过的例子。
• 用熟悉的类比。
• 说明它的局限。
