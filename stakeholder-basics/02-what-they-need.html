Knowing them · Lesson 2认识他们 · 第 2 课

What they need他们需要什么

Find what each stakeholder wants and worries about by asking, not by guessing · ~10 min · reading and practice靠询问而不是猜测，弄清每位干系人想要什么、担心什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Ask instead of guess第 1 步 · 靠问，不要猜

Learn先学

It is tempting to decide what people want from your seat. Do not. Ask each one two short questions: What do you need from this? and What worries you about it? A librarian may need fewer paper slips; the same librarian may worry that the app will break during exam week. It is easy to miss either one.

很容易坐在自己的位置上替别人决定他们想要什么。不要这样。对每个人问两个简短的问题：你需要这件事带来什么？以及你担心什么？图书管理员可能需要少填几张纸条，同一个人也可能担心应用在考试周出故障。这两点你都很容易漏掉。

Step 2 · Position or interest第 2 步 · 立场和利益

Learn先学

What people say is often a position: “I do not want the new app.” Behind it is an interest, the real need: “I do not want to lose the lending records I trust.” Ask “what would make that fine for you?” Positions are hard to move; interests can often be met in more than one way, such as keeping a printed backup.

人们说出口的往往是立场：“我不要这个新应用。”立场背后是利益，也就是真正的需要：“我不想丢掉我信得过的借阅记录。”可以问一句“怎样你才觉得没问题？”立场很难动，而利益往往有不止一种满足的办法，比如保留一份纸质备份。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Write them side by side第 3 步 · 并排写下来

Learn先学

For each stakeholder write one line: needs, worries and what you will do about it. Needs and worries side by side show where the project helps and where it threatens. A worry with no planned action is a gap, and the person who has it will notice the gap before you do.

为每位干系人写一行：需要、担心，以及你准备怎么回应。需要和担心并排写，能看出项目在哪里帮到人、哪里威胁到人。有担心却没有对应行动，就是一个缺口，而那位担心的人会比你更早发现这个缺口。

Librarian : needs fewer paper slips worries the app breaks in exam week action: keep a printed backup list Parent : needs to see what the child borrowed worries about privacy action: show titles only to the child

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask what people need and what worries them; do not guess.
• A position is what they say; an interest is the need behind it.
• Write needs, worries and a planned action on one line.
• A worry with no action is a gap.

• 问人们需要什么、担心什么，不要猜。
• 立场是他们说的话，利益是背后的需要。
• 把需要、担心和计划的行动写在一行。
• 没有行动的担心是一个缺口。
