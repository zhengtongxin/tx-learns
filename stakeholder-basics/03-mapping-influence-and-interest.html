Mapping influence and interest按影响力和关注度分类

Score each stakeholder on influence and interest, then choose how much attention each one gets · ~10 min · reading and practice给每位干系人的影响力和关注度打分，再决定各自要给多少关注 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Two scores第 1 步 · 两个分数

Learn先学

Influence is how much a person can change what happens to the project. Interest is how much they care about it. Score each from 1 (low) to 5 (high). The principal may be influence 5, interest 3. A student may be influence 1, interest 5. The scores are your honest estimate, so they can be revised.

影响力是一个人能在多大程度上改变项目的走向。关注度是他们有多在乎这个项目。各按 1（低）到 5（高）打分。校长可能是影响力 5、关注度 3；学生可能是影响力 1、关注度 5。分数是你诚实的估计，所以可以修改。

Step 2 · Four boxes第 2 步 · 四个格子

Learn先学

Call a score of 4 or 5 high and 1 to 3 low (a 3 is only middling, so it is not yet a strong pull one way). High influence and high interest: manage closely. High influence, low interest: keep satisfied. Low influence, high interest: keep informed. Low influence, low interest: watch. The principal at 5 and 3 lands in keep satisfied. The student at 1 and 5 lands in keep informed.

4 或 5 算高，1 到 3 算低（3 只是居中，还谈不上明显偏向）。影响力高、关注度高：密切管理。影响力高、关注度低：保持满意。影响力低、关注度高：随时告知。影响力低、关注度低：留意观察。校长是 5 和 3，落在“保持满意”；学生是 1 和 5，落在“随时告知”。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Attention follows the box第 3 步 · 关注跟着格子走

Learn先学

The box tells you how much effort to spend. Close management means regular talks and a say in decisions. Keep satisfied means short, well-timed updates before big choices. Keep informed means clear news and a way to ask questions. Watch means check now and then. Do not give everyone the same attention: that wastes yours and bores them.

格子告诉你该花多少力气。密切管理意味着常常沟通、让他们参与决定。保持满意意味着在大决定前给简短而及时的更新。随时告知意味着清楚的消息和提问的渠道。留意观察意味着时不时看一眼。不要给每个人同样的关注：既浪费你的精力，也让对方腻烦。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Score influence and interest from 1 to 5; 4 or 5 is high.
• Four boxes: manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, watch.
• Attention follows the box, not the person’s title.
• Scores change, so look again.

• 影响力和关注度各按 1 到 5 打分，4 或 5 算高。
• 四个格子：密切管理、保持满意、随时告知、留意观察。
• 关注跟着格子走，而不是跟着头衔走。
• 分数会变，所以要再看。
