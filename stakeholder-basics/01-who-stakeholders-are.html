Knowing them · Lesson 1认识他们 · 第 1 课

Who stakeholders are谁是干系人

Find everyone a project affects or depends on, not only the people who are easy to remember · ~10 min · reading and practice找出项目影响到或依赖的所有人，而不只是容易想起的那几位 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A stakeholder is anyone affected第 1 步 · 干系人是受影响的人

Learn先学

A stakeholder is a person or group who is affected by a project, can affect it, or both. Take a school library that moves to a new booking app. Students and librarians use it every day. The principal pays for it. A parent who lends books to a child is affected too, even though they never open the app.

干系人是受项目影响、或能影响项目、或两者皆是的人或群体。以一所学校图书馆改用新的预约应用为例：学生和图书管理员每天都要用，校长出钱，替孩子借书的家长虽然从不打开这个应用，也受到影响。

Step 2 · Four questions find them第 2 步 · 四个问题把他们找出来

Learn先学

Ask four questions about the project. Who is affected? Who decides or approves? Who helps or supplies something? Who could block or complain? Write every name or group that comes up. A list built from questions is longer and more honest than one written from memory.

围绕项目问四个问题。谁受影响？谁拍板或批准？谁帮忙或提供东西？谁可能阻拦或投诉？把冒出来的每个人或群体都写下来。用问题列出来的名单，比凭记忆写的更长，也更诚实。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Watch for the quiet ones第 3 步 · 留意不出声的人

Learn先学

The people you forget are often the ones who cause trouble later: the IT helper who must set up the app, the cleaner whose evening routine changes, the neighbouring school that shares the system. They are not loud, so they are easy to leave out. Add a line to your list for each group that is affected but not in the room.

被你忘掉的人，往往后来最容易出麻烦：要装应用的信息技术协助人员、晚间作息要变的清洁工、共用系统的邻校。他们不吵，所以容易漏掉。名单上要为每个受影响却不在场的群体加一行。

Project : new library booking app Affected: students, librarians, parents Decides : principal Helps : IT helper, vendor support Could block: teachers worried about class time

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A stakeholder is anyone affected by a project or able to affect it.
• Ask who is affected, who decides, who helps and who could block.
• Add the quiet groups who are affected but not in the room.
• Questions find more people than memory does.

• 干系人是受项目影响或能影响项目的人。
• 问谁受影响、谁拍板、谁帮忙、谁可能阻拦。
• 把受影响却不在场的不出声群体加进来。
• 用问题找到的人比凭记忆找到的多。
