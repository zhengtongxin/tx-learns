Working with them · Lesson 4与他们合作 · 第 4 课

When people change当人员变动时

Notice changes in who is involved, welcome new people and hand over from leavers, and update the plan · ~9 min · reading and practice留意参与人员的变化，迎接新人并让离开的人做交接，并更新计划 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · People come and go第 1 步 · 人员来来去去

Learn先学

Over a project, stakeholders (the people who care about it or are affected by it) change. A sponsor moves on, a new manager arrives, a team is merged. Each change can shift who has influence, who is interested and what they expect. Do not wait to be surprised: ask at each review, who is new, who has gone, and whose role has changed? Then update the stakeholder list.

在项目期间，干系人（关心项目或受项目影响的人）会发生变化。发起人调走了，新经理来了，团队合并了。每一次变化都可能改变谁有影响力、谁有兴趣以及他们的期望。不要等到被意外击中：每次回顾时都问一问，谁是新来的、谁走了、谁的角色变了？然后更新干系人清单。

Step 2 · Welcome the new, thank the leaving第 2 步 · 迎接新人，感谢离开的人

Learn先学

A new stakeholder starts with no background. Meet them early, share a short summary of the goal, the plan and the open risks, and ask what they expect. Do not assume they agree with earlier decisions. When someone leaves, ask them for a short handover: what is agreed, what is open and who else to talk to. Say thank you, because they may return in another role. Note who now holds the decisions they used to make.

新的干系人一开始没有背景。尽早与他们见面，分享一份关于目标、计划和未解决风险的简短摘要，并询问他们的期望。不要假设他们同意以前的决定。有人离开时，请他们做一份简短的交接：已经商定了什么、还有什么未决、还应该找谁谈。说声谢谢，因为他们可能以别的角色回来。记下现在由谁来做他们过去做的决定。

Step 3 · Update the plan第 3 步 · 更新计划

Learn先学

Every person added creates more ways to talk. The number of channels between n people is n × (n − 1) ÷ 2. With 6 people that is 6 × 5 ÷ 2 = 15 channels. Add 1 person and it is 7 × 6 ÷ 2 = 21, which is 6 more. So revisit your communication plan when the group grows: who needs what, how often and by which route. When the group shrinks, remove what nobody reads, so the plan stays light.

每增加一个人，就多出更多交流的渠道。n 个人之间的渠道数是 n × (n − 1) ÷ 2。6 个人是 6 × 5 ÷ 2 = 15 条渠道。再加 1 个人就是 7 × 6 ÷ 2 = 21 条，多了 6 条。所以当群体变大时，要重新审视沟通计划：谁需要什么、多久一次、通过什么途径。当群体变小时，去掉没人看的内容，让计划保持轻便。

Sort each habit as a good way to handle changes in people or a weak one.

把每个做法分为“应对人员变动的好方式”或“弱方式”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Stakeholders change: ask who is new, who has gone and whose role has changed.
• Meet new people early with a short summary; do not assume they agree.
• Ask leavers for a handover: what is agreed, what is open, who to talk to.
• Channels between n people are n × (n − 1) ÷ 2: 6 people make 15, 7 make 21, so 6 more.
• Revisit the communication plan when the group grows or shrinks.

• 干系人会变：问问谁是新来的、谁走了、谁的角色变了。
• 尽早与新人见面并给出简短摘要；不要假设他们同意。
• 请离开的人做交接：什么已商定、什么未决、还应找谁。
• n 个人之间的渠道数是 n × (n − 1) ÷ 2：6 个人 15 条，7 个人 21 条，多了 6 条。
• 群体变大或变小时，重新审视沟通计划。
