Handling resistance应对抵触

Meet pushback with listening and a real answer, not with pressure or a label · ~10 min · reading and practice用倾听和实在的回应来面对反对，而不是施压或贴标签 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Resistance has reasons第 1 步 · 抵触有原因

Learn先学

People rarely resist for no reason. The usual reasons are loss (I will lose something I value), unclear (I do not understand what changes) and no say (this was decided without me). Calling someone “difficult” hides the reason. Treat resistance as information about what the plan is missing.

人们很少无缘无故地抵触。常见的原因有：失去（我会失去看重的东西）、不清楚（我不明白会变什么）和没发言权（这件事没问过我就定了）。给人贴上“难搞”的标签，就掩盖了原因。把抵触看作一条信息：计划里缺了什么。

Step 2 · Listen first第 2 步 · 先听

Learn先学

Start by listening, and say back what you heard: “You are worried the app will fail in exam week, is that right?” This does not mean you agree. It means the person feels heard, and people who feel heard argue less and explain more. Only then explain your side, briefly, and ask what would help.

先从倾听开始，并复述你听到的：“你担心应用在考试周出故障，对吗？”这不代表你同意，而是让对方感到被听见。感到被听见的人，争论会少一些，解释会多一些。之后才简短地说明你这一边，并问对方什么会有帮助。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A change or a clear reason第 3 步 · 要么改，要么说清理由

Learn先学

After listening there are two honest answers, and one reply can use both. If the concern is fair, change the plan: keep a printed backup during exam week. If you cannot change it, give a clear reason and say what you will do to reduce the harm. Do not promise what you cannot deliver, and do not go quiet. A clear “no, because” is easier to accept than silence.

听完之后，诚实的回答有两种，一次回答也可以两种都用。如果顾虑合理，就改计划：考试周保留一份纸质备份。如果无法改，就说清楚理由，并说你会做什么来减少伤害。不要许下做不到的承诺，也不要沉默。一句清楚的“不行，因为……”比沉默更容易被接受。

Heard : you worry the app fails in exam week Check : is that right? Change : we keep a printed backup that week Reason : the old system ends in June, so we cannot keep both

Takeaways要点回顾

• Resistance usually means loss, unclear or no say.
• Listen and say it back before you explain.
• Fair concern: change the plan. Otherwise: give a clear reason.
• Do not label, promise too much or go quiet.

• 抵触通常意味着失去、不清楚或没发言权。
• 先听并复述，再解释。
• 顾虑合理就改计划，否则说清理由。
• 不要贴标签、不要承诺过多、也不要沉默。
