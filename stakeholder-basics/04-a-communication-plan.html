A communication plan沟通计划

Decide who hears what, how often, in what way and from whom, so nobody is left guessing · ~10 min · reading and practice定下谁听什么、多久一次、用什么方式、由谁来说，让没有人只能靠猜 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One line per audience第 1 步 · 每类对象一行

Learn先学

A communication plan is a short table. For each audience write: what they need to hear, how often, how (email, short meeting, notice board) and who sends it. Use the box from the last lesson to set the rhythm: once or twice a week for close management, before big decisions for keep satisfied, weekly or whenever there is news for keep informed, and monthly for watch.

沟通计划就是一张简短的表。为每类对象写：他们需要听到什么、多久一次、用什么方式（电邮、短会、布告栏）、由谁发出。用上一课的格子来定节奏：密切管理的每周一到两次，保持满意的在大决定之前，随时告知的每周一次或有消息就发，留意观察的每月一次。

Step 2 · Channels grow quickly第 2 步 · 渠道增长很快

Learn先学

Every pair of people who need to talk is a channel. The count is n × (n − 1) ÷ 2, because “A talks to B” is the same channel as “B talks to A”, so each pair is counted once. A team of 6 has 6 × 5 ÷ 2 = 15 channels. Add 2 people and 8 × 7 ÷ 2 = 28: 13 more, from just two extra people. More people means more places where a message can get lost, so a plan matters more as a project grows.

每一对需要沟通的人就是一条渠道。数量是 n × (n − 1) ÷ 2，因为“甲找乙”和“乙找甲”是同一条渠道，每一对只算一次。6 人的团队有 6 × 5 ÷ 2 = 15 条渠道。再加 2 个人，8 × 7 ÷ 2 = 28：多了 13 条，而只多了两个人。人越多，信息就越多地方可能丢失，所以项目越大，计划越重要。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Fit the message to the person第 3 步 · 让信息适合对方

Learn先学

The same news lands differently for different people. The principal wants the effect on cost and dates in two lines. A student wants to know what changes on Monday. Lead with what the reader cares about, keep one message to one idea, and say what you want them to do, if anything. Then check it arrived: ask one person, not the whole group.

同一条消息，对不同的人效果不同。校长要的是两行字讲清对费用和日期的影响；学生想知道星期一有什么变化。先写读者在乎的，一条消息只讲一个意思，需要对方做什么就明说。然后确认消息到了：问一个人就够，不必问全体。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A plan lists audience, message, rhythm, way and owner.
• Channels are n × (n − 1) ÷ 2: 6 people make 15, 8 make 28.
• Lead with what the reader cares about.
• Check that it arrived with one person.

• 计划写明对象、信息、节奏、方式和负责人。
• 渠道数是 n × (n − 1) ÷ 2：6 人 15 条，8 人 28 条。
• 先写读者在乎的内容。
• 用一个人确认消息到了。
