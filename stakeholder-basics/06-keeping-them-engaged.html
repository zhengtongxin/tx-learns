Working with them · Lesson 3与他们合作 · 第 3 课

Keeping them engaged让他们持续参与

Move people from where they are to where the project needs them, and review the map as things change · ~10 min · reading and practice把人从现在的位置带到项目需要的位置，并随着情况变化复查这张图 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Where they are, where you need them第 1 步 · 现在在哪里，需要在哪里

Learn先学

Describe each stakeholder’s engagement in five levels: unaware, resistant, neutral, supportive and leading (actively speaking up for the project; most people only need to reach supportive). Write today’s level and the level the project needs. A teacher who is resistant and needs to be supportive is a gap of two levels. A gap tells you where to spend effort; a person who is already where you need them can be left alone.

用五个层次描述每位干系人的参与程度：不知情、抵触、中立、支持和带头（主动为项目发声；多数人只需要做到支持）。写下今天的层次和项目需要的层次。一位抵触、但需要变成支持的老师，差了两级。差距告诉你该把力气花在哪里；已经在你需要的位置上的人，不必多打扰。

Step 2 · Show progress and close the loop第 2 步 · 展示进展，回应反馈

Learn先学

People stay engaged when they can see something happening. Share small, real progress: “the booking page now works for Year 1.” When someone gives feedback, close the loop: tell them what you did with it, even when the answer is no. People stop giving feedback when it seems to vanish.

人们看到事情在推进，才会持续参与。分享小而真实的进展：“预约页面现在已经对一年级可用。”有人给了反馈，要回应到底：告诉他们你怎么处理了，即使答案是不行。反馈像是消失了，人们就不再给了。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Review the map第 3 步 · 复查这张图

Learn先学

Stakeholders change as the project does. Someone new joins, a quiet group becomes loud, a supporter loses interest. Look at your list at a fixed rhythm, such as ten minutes at the end of each month. Ask who is new, whose score changed and who has moved a level. Update the plan only where something moved.

随着项目推进，干系人也会变化：有新的人加入，原本安静的群体变得吵，支持者失去兴趣。按固定节奏看你的名单，比如每个月底花十分钟。问：谁是新来的？谁的分数变了？谁换了层次？只在有变动的地方更新计划。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Compare each person’s level now with the level needed.
• Spend effort where the gap is.
• Share real progress and close the loop on feedback.
• Review the list on a rhythm and update only what moved.

• 把每个人现在的层次和需要的层次比较。
• 把力气花在有差距的地方。
• 分享真实的进展，并回应反馈。
• 按节奏复查名单，只更新有变动的地方。
