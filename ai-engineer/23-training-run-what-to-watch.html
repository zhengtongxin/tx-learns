Step 1 · Set up the run第 1 步 · 设置训练

Learn先学

Use the supervised fine-tuning trainer from the TRL library, with the 4-bit base, the LoRA config, a training set and a validation set. The knobs:

用 TRL 库的监督微调训练器，交给它 4 位底座、LoRA 配置、训练集和验证集。旋钮有：

  • 1 to 3 epochs (passes over the data);
  • a small learning rate (how big each update is), about 1e-4 for LoRA;
  • small batches plus accumulation if the GPU is small;
  • checkpoints (saved copies of the add-on) pushed to the hub, so a disconnect loses nothing.
  • 1 到 3 个 epoch（把数据过几遍）；
  • 小学习率（每次更新迈多大），LoRA 约 1e-4；
  • GPU 小就用小批量加累积；
  • 把检查点（附加件的存档）推送到 hub，断线也不会丢。
from trl import SFTConfig, SFTTrainer # model and lora come from the last lesson; train_set and val_set are the "train" and "validation" parts # of your dataset, one prompt and one price per row args = SFTConfig( output_dir="price-run-01", num_train_epochs=1, per_device_train_batch_size=4, gradient_accumulation_steps=8, # acts like a batch of 32 learning_rate=1e-4, lr_scheduler_type="cosine", warmup_ratio=0.03, optim="paged_adamw_8bit", eval_strategy="steps", eval_steps=200, save_steps=200, push_to_hub=True, # a checkpoint goes online every 200 steps report_to="wandb", run_name="price-run-01", ) trainer = SFTTrainer(model=model, args=args, peft_config=lora, train_dataset=train_set, eval_dataset=val_set) trainer.train()

Step 2 · Two curves: training and validation第 2 步 · 两条曲线：训练与验证

Learn先学

Training loss is how wrong the model is on examples it learns from; validation loss is how wrong it is on examples it never trains on. Training loss should fall. If validation loss climbs while training loss keeps falling, the model is memorising the practice sheet: stop early.

训练损失是模型在学习用的例子上错得多厉害；验证损失是在从不训练的例子上错得多厉害。训练损失应该下降。如果验证损失上升而训练损失还在降，模型就是在背练习卷：提前停。

step train loss validation loss 200 1.10 1.12 400 0.92 0.95 600 0.81 0.93 # validation slowing 800 0.70 0.96 # validation now rising: memorising

Step 3 · Warning signs and the right checkpoint第 3 步 · 警告信号与选对检查点

Learn先学

Log to a dashboard such as Weights & Biases and read a few real generations: a chart can look fine while answers are nonsense. Time per step times the hourly price is the bill. After the run, pick the checkpoint by test error on the same evaluate, not by loss alone.

把指标记到 Weights & Biases 之类的仪表盘，并读几条真实的生成结果：图可能很好看，答案却是胡说。每步耗时乘以每小时价格就是账单。训练后，用同一个 evaluate 在测试集上比较误差来选检查点，不要只看损失。

signal first thing to try loss shoots up, then garbage numbers ... lower the learning rate out of memory, or memory at the cap ... smaller batch, shorter cutoff validation rising, training falling ... stop, keep the best checkpoint hours x hourly price over budget ...... stop and shrink the run

What I got wrong我搞错的地方I trained too many epochs because training loss kept dropping, and forgot to push checkpoints, so a disconnect lost the run.我因为训练损失一直在降就训练了太多个 epoch，还忘了推送检查点，一断线整次训练就白费了。
Common errors常见报错CUDA out of memory: smaller batch or 4-bit. Loss jumps to a huge number then nan: cut the learning rate by ten and restart.CUDA out of memory：减小批量或用 4-bit。损失飙到很大然后变成 nan：把学习率降到十分之一后重新开始。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• 1 to 3 epochs, a small learning rate, small batches with accumulation.
• Validation loss rising while training loss falls means memorising: stop and keep the best checkpoint.
• Spikes or garbage numbers: lower the learning rate. Memory at the cap: smaller batch.
• Push checkpoints, watch cost, and pick by test error, not loss.

• 1 到 3 个 epoch、小学习率、小批量加累积。
• 训练损失在降、验证损失在升就是在死记：停下并保留最好的检查点。
• 飙高或乱码数字：降低学习率。内存顶满：减小批量。
• 推送检查点，盯着成本，用测试误差而不是损失来选。
