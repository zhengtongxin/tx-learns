Step 1 · Why not train everything第 1 步 · 为什么不全部训练

Learn先学

A model is billions of numbers, called weights. Each one is stored in 16 bits (a bit is one 0-or-1 slot, and 8 bits make a byte), so 2 bytes per weight: 3 billion weights is about 6 GB. Full fine-tuning updates all of them, and for every one you need space for the number itself plus the extra bookkeeping that training keeps. That is several times the size of the model, far beyond a free GPU. We need to train less, and store less.

模型就是几十亿个数字，叫权重。每个权重用 16 位存（一位就是一个 0 或 1 的格子，8 位是一个字节），也就是每个 2 字节：30 亿个权重大约 6 GB。完整微调会更新全部权重，每个数字除了本身，还要为训练的额外记录留空间，总共是模型体积的好几倍，远超免费 GPU。所以我们要训练得少一点，存得少一点。

rough memory for a 3-billion-weight model store the weights, 16 bits each ......... about 6 GB full fine-tuning also keeps extra copies . several times that the free notebook GPU ................... 16 GB at most (often less usable)

Step 2 · LoRA: freeze the book, add sticky notes第 2 步 · LoRA：冻住课本，贴便利贴

Learn先学

LoRA (low-rank adaptation) freezes the original weights and trains only a small add-on beside some layers. Think of a printed textbook: you do not reprint it, you stick notes in the margin. The add-on is two thin matrices (a matrix is just a grid of numbers, and r, the rank, says how thin the two grids are), and at run time their product is added to the original weights. The base model is never changed, and the add-on is a small file you can share.

LoRA（低秩适配）冻住原来的权重，只在部分层旁边训练一个小小的附加件。就像一本印好的课本：你不会重印，而是在页边贴便利贴。附加件是两个很“瘦”的矩阵（矩阵就是一格格数字，r 叫秩，决定这两张表有多“瘦”），运行时把它们的乘积加到原权重上。基础模型从不被改动，附加件是一个可以分享的小文件。

one layer is 4096 x 4096 numbers ....... 16.8 million weights (frozen) LoRA with r = 8: two thin matrices 4096 x 8 + 8 x 4096 ................ 65,536 weights (trained) that is about 0.4 percent of the layer

Step 3 · The four LoRA settings第 3 步 · LoRA 的四个设置

Learn先学

You will meet four settings. r is the thickness, usually 8 to 32. alpha scales how loudly the add-on speaks; a common choice is alpha equal to r, or twice r. target modules says which layers get an add-on, and the attention layers (the parts that decide which words to look at) are the usual start. dropout randomly switches off a little of the add-on during training to avoid memorising.

你会遇到四个设置。r 是厚度，通常 8 到 32。alpha 决定附加件“说话的音量”，常见选择是 alpha 等于 r，或 r 的两倍。target modules 指定哪些层加附加件，一般从注意力层（决定该看哪些词的部分）开始。dropout 在训练时随机关掉一小部分附加件，避免死记硬背。

from peft import LoraConfig lora = LoraConfig( r=16, # thickness of the add-on lora_alpha=32, # volume knob, here twice r target_modules=["q_proj", "k_proj", "v_proj", "o_proj"], # attention layers lora_dropout=0.05, # switch off a little while training task_type="CAUSAL_LM", )

Step 4 · Quantisation: pack the frozen part smaller第 4 步 · 量化：把冻住的部分压得更小

Learn先学

LoRA cuts what we train. Quantisation cuts how much memory the frozen part uses, by storing each weight in 4 bits instead of 16. It is like vacuum-packing clothes for a suitcase: same clothes, much less space, slightly less crisp. A 3 to 8 billion weight model then fits on one small GPU. The usual recipe is the NF4 4-bit type with bfloat16 for the maths; put LoRA on top and you have QLoRA.

LoRA 减少的是要训练的部分。量化减少的是冻住那部分占的内存：每个权重用 4 位而不是 16 位来存。就像把衣服抽真空装进行李箱：衣服还是那些，占地小很多，只是没那么挺。这样一个 30 到 80 亿权重的模型就能放进一块小 GPU。常用配方是 NF4 的 4 位类型，计算用 bfloat16；在上面加 LoRA 就是 QLoRA。

import torch from transformers import AutoModelForCausalLM, BitsAndBytesConfig pack = BitsAndBytesConfig( load_in_4bit=True, bnb_4bit_quant_type="nf4", bnb_4bit_compute_dtype=torch.bfloat16, bnb_4bit_use_double_quant=True, # also compress the small scaling numbers quantisation keeps ) model = AutoModelForCausalLM.from_pretrained( "your-small-open-model", quantization_config=pack, device_map="auto") print(model.get_memory_footprint() / 1e9, "GB")

Step 5 · Pick a base, shape the data第 5 步 · 选底座模型，整理数据

Learn先学

Pick a size your GPU can hold: 3 to 8 billion weights is fine on a cheap one. Choose a family with a good tokenizer (the splitter that cuts text into tokens, the small pieces a model reads) and a licence you can live with, and check how it splits numbers, because families that read each digit on its own make price guessing easier. Then shape each example as a prompt plus a short completion such as "Price is $123". Cut long descriptions at a token limit, and compute the loss on the answer only, so the model learns to produce the price instead of repeating the description.

选一个你的 GPU 装得下的大小，30 到 80 亿权重在便宜的 GPU 上就可以。选分词器（把文字切成 token 这种小片段的工具）好、许可证能接受的模型家族，并看看它怎么切数字：把每个数字单独读的家族，让猜价格更容易。然后把每个例子整理成“提示 + 一个短的回答”，比如 “Price is $123”。长描述按 token 上限截断，并且只在答案部分计算损失，这样模型学的是给出价格，而不是复述描述。

example = { "prompt": "Used phone, 128 GB, cracked corner, battery 82 percent. Price is $", "completion": "145", } # train on the completion only, so the loss is about the price

LoRA, quantisation, or neither?

是 LoRA、量化，还是都不是？

What I got wrong我搞错的地方I mixed the two ideas up. LoRA shrinks what is trained. Quantisation shrinks how much memory the frozen part uses. They are independent, and that is why they combine so well.我把这两个概念混在一起了。LoRA 缩小的是要训练的部分，量化缩小的是冻结部分占的内存。它们互相独立，所以才能配合得这么好。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Full fine-tuning updates billions of weights: too much for a small GPU.
• LoRA freezes the base and trains two thin matrices beside some layers.
• Four settings: r, alpha, target modules, dropout.
• Quantisation stores the frozen weights in 4 bits instead of 16.
• LoRA on a 4-bit base is QLoRA; the two ideas are independent.
• Pick a base your GPU can hold, with a sensible tokenizer and licence.
• Train on a prompt plus a short answer, and compute the loss on the answer only.

• 完整微调要更新几十亿个权重：小 GPU 吃不消。
• LoRA 冻住底座，在部分层旁边训练两个很瘦的矩阵。
• 四个设置：r、alpha、目标层、dropout。
• 量化把冻住的权重从 16 位压到 4 位来存。
• 在 4 位底座上做 LoRA 就是 QLoRA；两个概念互相独立。
• 选 GPU 装得下、分词器和许可证都合适的底座。
• 用“提示 + 短答案”训练，只对答案部分算损失。
