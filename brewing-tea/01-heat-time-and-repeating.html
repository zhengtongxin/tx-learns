A good cup · Lesson 1一杯好茶 · 第 1 课

Heat, time and repeating水温、时间与复刻

Set water heat and steeping time on purpose, and measure so you can make the same good cup again · ~6 min · reading and practice有意识地设定水温和浸泡时间，并量好茶叶，好让你再泡出同样的好茶 · 约 6 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Match the water to the leaves第 1 步 · 让水温配合茶叶

Learn先学

Different teas need different water temperatures. Hotter water pulls out more bitterness, and delicate leaves give it up fastest. So a delicate tea wants water that has cooled a little after boiling, while a sturdy tea can take it hot.

不同的茶需要不同的水温。水越烫，萃取出的苦味越多，而娇嫩的茶叶最先放出苦味。所以娇嫩的茶要用烧开后稍微放凉的水，结实的茶则可以用烫水。

A simple habit: let the kettle rest for a minute before pouring on a delicate tea.

一个简单的习惯：给娇嫩的茶倒水前，让水壶先放一分钟。

Step 2 · Steeping time sets the strength第 2 步 · 浸泡时间决定浓淡

Learn先学

Steeping time is how long the leaves sit in the water. A short steep gives a lighter cup. A long steep gives a stronger cup, and a more bitter one.

浸泡时间是茶叶泡在水里的时长。时间短，茶汤清淡；时间长，茶汤更浓，也更苦。

So if a cup is too bitter, shorten the steep next time. If it is too weak, lengthen it a little.

所以茶太苦，下次缩短浸泡时间；太淡，就稍微延长一点。

Sort each fix.

给每个调整分类。

Step 3 · Make the cup repeatable第 3 步 · 让这一杯能复刻

Learn先学

A good cup you cannot repeat is luck. Two habits fix that: use fresh water each time, and measure the leaves instead of guessing. Then write down what you did.

复刻不了的好茶只是运气。两个习惯能解决：每次都用新鲜的水，并且量好茶叶，不要凭感觉。然后把做法写下来。

Tea: (name it) Leaves: (how much, measured) Water: fresh, how hot Steep: how many minutes Taste: too bitter / too weak / just right

What I got wrong: I changed the heat and the time in the same cup, so I could not tell which one fixed it. Change one thing at a time.

我答错的地方：我在同一杯里同时改了水温和时间，结果分不清是哪一个起了作用。一次只改一样。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Hotter water pulls out more bitterness, so delicate leaves want cooler water.
• A short steep is lighter, a long steep is stronger and more bitter.
• Fresh water and measured leaves make a cup easier to repeat.
• Change one thing at a time and write it down.

• 水越烫，苦味萃取得越多，所以娇嫩的茶要用较凉的水。
• 泡得短则清淡，泡得长则更浓更苦。
• 新鲜的水和量好的茶叶，让一杯茶更容易复刻。
• 一次只改一样，并写下来。
