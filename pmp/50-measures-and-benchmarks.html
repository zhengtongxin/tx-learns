Measures and benchmarks度量与对标

Pick a few measures tied to goals, tell leading from lagging, compare with a target and a benchmark, and spot a measure that misleads · ~9 min · reading and practice选少数与目标挂钩的度量，分清领先与滞后指标，与目标值和对标比较，并识别会误导的度量 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Measures that point at a goal第 1 步 · 指向目标的度量

Learn先学

A measure (often called a KPI, a key performance indicator) is a number tied to a goal, such as the share of deliverables handed over on time. A measure that drives no decision is only decoration. Keep a few, each with an owner and a reason.

度量（常称 KPI，关键绩效指标）是与某个目标挂钩的数字，例如按时交付的成果所占比例。不推动任何决定的度量只是装饰。保留少数几个，每个都要有负责人和理由。

Some measures look back and some look ahead. A lagging indicator reports what already happened, such as last month’s late deliveries. A leading indicator warns you early, such as the number of tasks that started late this week. A trend that keeps rising or falling, such as overtime climbing week after week, is often a leading signal. Use leading ones to act in time and lagging ones to confirm the result.

有的度量回头看，有的往前看。滞后指标报告已经发生的事，例如上个月的延误交付。领先指标提早预警，例如本周晚开工的任务数。持续上升或下降的趋势，例如加班时数一周比一周多，常常是领先信号。用领先指标及时行动，用滞后指标确认结果。

Kind Meaning Example Lagging what already happened late deliveries last month Leading an early warning tasks that started late this week (种类：滞后 = 已发生；领先 = 提前预警)

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Targets and benchmarks第 2 步 · 目标值与对标

Learn先学

A target is the value you aim for. A threshold is the value that makes you act. A benchmark is a reference to compare with, such as similar past projects or your own earlier results. Example: 34 of 40 deliverables on time is 34 ÷ 40 = 85%. With a target of 90% you are 90% − 85% = 5 percentage points short. If similar past projects averaged 80%, you are 5 percentage points better than the benchmark yet still below target.

目标值是你要达到的数值。阈值是触发行动的数值。对标是用来比较的参照，例如类似的过往项目或你自己以前的结果。例子：40 个成果按时交付 34 个，即 34 ÷ 40 = 85%。目标是 90%，90% − 85% = 差 5 个百分点。若类似的过往项目平均 80%，你比对标好 5 个百分点，但仍低于目标。

Step 3 · When a measure misleads第 3 步 · 度量误导的时候

Learn先学

People adjust to what is measured. If testers are praised for a falling defect count, they may log fewer defects instead of finding fewer. Check that a measure still reflects the goal, pair it with a second measure that would expose gaming (making the number look better without making the work better), and drop it when it no longer helps a decision.

人们会向被度量的东西靠拢。如果因为缺陷数下降而表扬测试人员，他们可能只是少记录缺陷，而不是缺陷真的变少。要核对度量是否仍然反映目标，搭配第二个能暴露作假（让数字好看却没让工作变好）的度量，当它不再帮助决策时就放弃它。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A measure is a number tied to a goal and a decision; keep few.
• Leading indicators warn early; lagging ones confirm what happened.
• A target is what you aim for, a threshold makes you act, a benchmark is a reference.
• 34 of 40 on time is 85%: 5 below a 90% target.
• Check that a measure still reflects the goal; pair it with a second one.

• 领先指标提早预警，滞后指标确认已发生的事。
