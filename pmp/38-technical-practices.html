Name the working habits that keep a product easy to change, and say how a team handles technical debt · ~9 min · reading and practice能说出让产品保持易于修改的工作习惯，以及团队怎样处理技术债务 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Habits that keep change cheap第 1 步 · 让变更保持低成本的习惯

Learn先学

Agile welcomes change. That only works if the product stays easy to change. A set of team habits makes this possible, and the exam expects you to recognise them by name.

敏捷欢迎变化。但前提是产品一直容易改。有一套团队习惯让这件事成为可能，考试要求你能叫出它们的名字。

Pair programming: two people work at one screen. One writes, the other reviews as it happens. Mistakes are caught early and knowledge spreads, so no single person is the only one who understands a part.

结对编程：两个人共用一块屏幕，一个写，另一个随时检查。错误发现得早，知识也传开了，不会出现某一部分只有一个人懂的情况。

Test-driven development: write a small automated test first, watch it fail, write just enough to make it pass, then tidy up.

测试驱动开发：先写一个小的自动化测试，看着它失败；再写刚好够让它通过的内容；然后整理。

Refactoring: improving the inside of the product without changing what it does, so the next change is easier.

重构：在不改变产品功能的前提下改进内部结构，让下一次修改更容易。

Continuous integration: everyone merges their work many times a day and automated tests run each time, so a problem shows up within minutes, not at the end.

持续集成：每个人每天多次合并自己的工作，每次都自动跑测试，所以问题几分钟内就暴露出来，而不是拖到最后。

Two more: collective ownership (anyone on the team may improve any part) and sustainable pace (a speed the team can keep up for months, without regular overtime).

还有两个：集体所有（团队里任何人都可以改进任何部分）和可持续的节奏（团队能保持几个月的速度，不靠经常加班）。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Technical debt and spikes第 2 步 · 技术债务与探针

Learn先学

Technical debt is the cost of shortcuts. A quick fix today makes every later change slower, like interest on a loan.

技术债务是走捷径留下的代价。今天图快的修补，会让以后每一次修改都变慢，就像贷款的利息。

It does not get paid off in secret, and the team does not stop all feature work to clear it. The team makes it visible as items in the backlog, explains the cost to the product owner, and the product owner orders it against features.

技术债务不是悄悄还掉的，团队也不会停下所有功能开发去清理它。团队把它作为待办列表里的条目摆到明面上，向产品负责人说明代价，由产品负责人把它和功能一起排序。

A clear Definition of Done (the checklist an item must meet before it counts as finished) stops new debt from building up.

清晰的完成的定义（一个条目算作完成前必须满足的检查清单）能防止新的债务累积。

A spike is a short, time-boxed piece of research to answer one question, such as whether a library can handle the load. Use one when the team cannot estimate a story because too much is unknown.

探针是一段有时间限制的短期研究，只回答一个问题，比如某个库能不能扛住负载。当未知太多、团队没法估算某个故事时，就用它。

Step 3 · Your part as the lead第 3 步 · 你作为带队人的角色

Learn先学

You do not pick the team's technical practices. The team does. Your part is to give them the room: time for testing and tidying inside each sprint, and protection from pressure to skip them.

团队用什么技术实践，不由你来选，而是团队自己定。你的职责是给他们空间：在每个冲刺里留出测试和整理的时间，并顶住“跳过这些”的压力。

The idea underneath all of it: quality is built in as the work is done, not inspected in at the end.

这一切背后的理念是：质量是在工作过程中内建的，而不是到最后靠检查补上的。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Pairing, test-first, refactoring and continuous integration keep change cheap.
• Technical debt is made visible in the backlog; the product owner orders it.
• A spike is time-boxed research to make a story estimable.
• The team chooses its practices; you protect the time for them.
• Quality is built in, not inspected in at the end.

• 结对、先写测试、重构和持续集成，让变更保持低成本。
• 技术债务要放进待办列表摆到明面上，由产品负责人排序。
• 探针是有时间限制的研究，让故事变得可以估算。
• 实践由团队选择；你负责保护做这些事的时间。
• 质量是内建的，不是最后检查出来的。
