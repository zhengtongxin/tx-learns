Hybrid and tailoring混合与裁剪

Tailor an approach to the project and combine predictive and agile parts without losing control · ~10 min · reading and practice根据项目裁剪方法，把预测型和敏捷部分结合起来，又不失去控制 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Tailor, do not copy第 1 步 · 裁剪，不要照搬

Learn先学

Tailoring means adjusting the approach to fit the project: keep what adds value, drop what does not, and say why. It is not abandoning discipline. The factors that guide it are the project’s size and risk, how regulated it is, how experienced and spread out the team is, how available the customer is, and what the organisation expects.

裁剪是让方法适合项目：保留有价值的，去掉没价值的，并说明理由。它不是放弃纪律。指导裁剪的因素有：项目的规模和风险、监管程度、团队的经验和分布、客户能否随时参与，以及组织的期望。

A good tailoring answer starts from the situation, not from a favourite method.

好的裁剪答案从情境出发，而不是从自己偏爱的方法出发。

Regulator needs a signed design before building lean predictive Customer can review every two weeks lean agile Requirements likely to change after launch lean agile Fixed price with a fixed list of deliverables lean predictive Fixed hardware, changing software on top hybrid

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Three hybrid patterns第 2 步 · 三种混合模式

Learn先学

Hybrid is not “a bit of everything”. Three patterns are common. A predictive frame with agile inside: fixed phases and gates, with the work in each phase done in sprints. An agile core with predictive support: sprints for the product, with procurement or compliance planned up front. Different parts, different ways: the hardware is planned, the software is iterative.

混合不是“什么都来一点”。常见的有三种。预测型框架，里面用敏捷：阶段和关口是固定的，每个阶段的工作用冲刺完成。敏捷为核心，预测型提供支撑：产品用冲刺做，采购或合规提前规划。不同部分用不同方式：硬件按计划做，软件迭代做。

The leader’s job is to connect the two worlds: agree the integration points and report in a way both sides can read.

负责人的工作是把两边接起来：约定整合点，并用双方都能读懂的方式汇报。

Step 3 · Reporting on a hybrid project第 3 步 · 汇报混合项目

Learn先学

Do not force story points into a Gantt chart, or force a fixed plan onto the sprints. Show each part in its own language, then tie them to the shared milestones: what is delivered, what is forecast, and what blocks it.

不要把故事点硬塞进甘特图，也不要把固定计划硬套在冲刺上。每一部分用它自己的语言展示，再把它们和共同的里程碑对上：交付了什么，预测会怎样，什么在阻碍。

When a method is not working, inspect and adapt it, for example in the retrospective. Dropping it silently, or insisting on it unchanged, are both wrong answers.

当某个方法行不通时，去检查并调整它，比如在回顾会上。悄悄丢掉它，或者原封不动地硬撑，都是错误答案。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Tailoring means fitting the approach to the project’s factors and saying why.
• Hybrid has patterns: a predictive frame with agile inside, an agile core with predictive support, or different parts done differently.
• Agree integration points and report each part in its own language, tied to shared milestones.
• When a method fails, inspect and adapt it; do not silently drop it or insist on it unchanged.
• “Always agile” and “never agile” are both wrong rules.

• 裁剪就是让方法适合项目的各项因素，并说明理由。
• 混合有固定的模式：预测型框架里用敏捷、敏捷为核心加预测型支撑，或不同部分用不同方式。
• 约定整合点，各部分用自己的语言汇报，并对应共同的里程碑。
• 方法行不通时，检查并调整；不要悄悄丢掉，也不要原封不动硬撑。
• “永远敏捷”和“绝不敏捷”都是错误的规矩。
