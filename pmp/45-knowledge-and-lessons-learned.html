Knowledge and lessons learned知识与经验教训

Tell explicit from tacit knowledge, share each the right way, and keep lessons learned as the project runs · ~9 min · reading and practice分清显性与隐性知识，用对办法分享，并在项目进行中持续记录经验教训 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Two kinds of knowledge第 1 步 · 两种知识

Learn先学

Explicit knowledge can be written down: a procedure, a template, a checklist. Tacit knowledge lives in people's heads and hands: how to calm an angry customer, which supplier is slow in December, why a step is done in that order. Tacit knowledge is the kind that walks out of the door when someone leaves.

显性知识可以写下来：流程、模板、检查清单。隐性知识存在于人的头脑和手上：怎样安抚发怒的客户、哪家供应商十二月会拖、为什么某一步要按那个顺序做。人一走，带走的就是隐性知识。

So the two need different tools. Explicit knowledge is shared by writing it down and keeping it where people can find it. Tacit knowledge is shared through people: working side by side, mentoring (an experienced person guides a newer one), job shadowing (watching someone work) and small groups where people swap stories.

所以两者要用不同的办法。显性知识靠写下来、放在大家找得到的地方来分享。隐性知识靠人来传：并肩工作、导师带教（有经验的人指导新人）、跟岗观摩（看别人怎么做）、以及让大家交流经验的小圈子。

Step 2 · Keep lessons as you go第 2 步 · 边做边记经验教训

Learn先学

A lessons learned register is a running list of what went well, what went badly and what to do differently. Add to it during the project, not only at the end: by then people have forgotten the details, and the next phase could have used the lesson months earlier.

经验教训登记册是一份持续更新的清单：什么做得好、什么做得不好、下次该怎么改。要在项目进行中就记，而不是只在最后记：到那时细节已经忘了，而下一阶段本可以早几个月用上这条教训。

A good entry names the situation, the cause and what to do next time, without blaming a person. Short regular moments help: a few minutes at the end of each phase or sprint review (a sprint is a short, fixed block of work).

好的一条要写明情况、原因和下次的做法，不指责个人。定期留出短短几分钟就够：每个阶段结束时，或每次冲刺评审后（冲刺是一段短而固定的工作时间）。

Step 3 · Handing on what you know第 3 步 · 把所知传下去

Learn先学

When a key person is about to leave, do not wait for the exit date. Plan the transfer: have them document the explicit parts, then pair them with a successor (the person who will take over their work) so the tacit parts pass on by working together. At the end of the project, file the lessons where future teams will find them, such as the organisation's records of past projects.

关键人员要离开时，别等到离职那天。要提前安排交接：让他把显性的部分写成文档，再让他与接任者（将接手他工作的人）结对，隐性的部分在一起工作中传过去。项目结束时，把经验教训归档到未来团队找得到的地方，比如组织保存的以往项目记录。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Explicit knowledge is written down; tacit knowledge lives in people.
• Share tacit knowledge by working together: pairing, mentoring, shadowing.
• Record lessons learned during the project, not only at the end.
• Plan a handover well before a key person leaves.
• File lessons where future teams will find them.

• 显性知识可以写下来；隐性知识存在于人身上。
• 隐性知识靠一起工作来传：结对、带教、跟岗。
• 经验教训要在项目进行中记录，不是只在最后。
• 关键人员离开前很早就要安排交接。
• 把经验教训归档到未来团队找得到的地方。
