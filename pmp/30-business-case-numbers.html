Step 1 · Payback and return第 1 步 · 回收期与回报率

Learn先学

A business case says why a project is worth its cost. The exam does not ask you to build one. It asks you to read the numbers in it and pick the better project.

商业论证说明一个项目为什么值得投入。考试不要求你写商业论证，而是要你读懂里面的数字，选出更好的项目。

Payback period is how long it takes to earn back what you spent. Shorter is better. Its weakness: it ignores everything that happens after that point.

投资回收期是把投入赚回来需要多久。越短越好。它的弱点是：回本之后发生的事，它一概不看。

Return on investment (ROI) is the gain divided by the cost. Higher is better. A clinic spends S$120,000 on a booking system that saves S$40,000 a year. Payback is 3 years. Over 5 years it saves S$200,000, so ROI is (200,000 − 120,000) ÷ 120,000 = about 67%.

投资回报率（ROI）是收益除以成本。越高越好。一家诊所花 12 万新元上预约系统，每年省 4 万新元，回收期是 3 年。5 年共省 20 万新元，ROI 就是（200,000 − 120,000）÷ 120,000 ≈ 67%。

Cost S$120,000 Saving a year S$ 40,000 Payback 120,000 / 40,000 = 3 years ROI in 5 years (200,000 - 120,000) / 120,000 = 67%

Step 2 · Present value第 2 步 · 现值

Learn先学

Money you get later is worth less than money today, because today's money could be earning interest. Present value turns a future amount into what it is worth now.

以后拿到的钱不如今天的钱值钱，因为今天的钱可以拿去生息。现值就是把未来的一笔钱折算成现在值多少。

At a 10% rate, S$110,000 received a year from now is worth S$100,000 today: 110,000 ÷ 1.10.

按 10% 的利率，一年后收到的 11 万新元，今天值 10 万新元：110,000 ÷ 1.10。

Net present value (NPV) adds up the present value of everything a project brings in and subtracts what it costs. Positive means worth doing, and higher is better.

净现值（NPV）是把项目带来的全部收入折成现值相加，再减去成本。为正就值得做，越高越好。

Two things the exam checks. NPV has the cost in it already, so never subtract the cost again. And it has the timing in it already, so a longer project with a higher NPV still wins.

考试常考两点。NPV 已经扣过成本，不要再减一次。NPV 也已经考虑了时间，所以周期更长但 NPV 更高的项目照样胜出。

Step 3 · Rate of return and other terms第 3 步 · 收益率与其他术语

Learn先学

Internal rate of return (IRR) is the yearly return a project earns, as a percentage. Higher is better, and it must beat the rate the company asks for.

内部收益率（IRR）是项目每年赚到的回报，用百分比表示。越高越好，而且要高于公司要求的收益率。

Benefit-cost ratio is benefits divided by costs. Above 1 means the benefits are larger. Higher is better.

效益成本比是效益除以成本。大于 1 表示效益更大。越高越好。

Opportunity cost is the value of the option you gave up. Choose B (NPV S$60,000) over A (NPV S$45,000) and the opportunity cost is S$45,000.

机会成本是你放弃的那个选项的价值。选了 B（NPV 6 万新元）而放弃 A（NPV 4.5 万新元），机会成本就是 4.5 万新元。

Sunk cost is money already spent. It cannot be recovered, so it should not sway the decision to continue. Only future costs and benefits count.

沉没成本是已经花掉的钱。它收不回来，所以不该影响要不要继续的决定。只看今后的成本和效益。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Payback period: shorter is better, and it ignores what comes after.
• NPV, IRR, ROI and benefit-cost ratio: higher is better.
• NPV already includes the cost and the timing.
• Opportunity cost is the value of the option you did not choose.
• Sunk cost is spent; decide on what is still ahead.

• 回收期：越短越好，但它不看回本之后的事。
• NPV、IRR、ROI 和效益成本比：越高越好。
• NPV 已经包含成本和时间因素。
• 机会成本是没选的那个选项的价值。
• 沉没成本已经花掉；决定时只看今后。
