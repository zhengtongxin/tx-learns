Balancing resources平衡资源

Spot over-allocation, tell levelling from smoothing, and put the trade-offs to the sponsor when there is no float · ~9 min · reading and practice发现超负荷分配，分清资源平衡与资源平滑，没有浮动时间时把取舍交给发起人 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · When one person is needed twice第 1 步 · 一个人同时被需要两次

Learn先学

A schedule can look fine on paper and still fail because one person is booked on two tasks at the same time. That is over-allocation. Suppose Sam must do task A (3 days) and task B (3 days), and the plan runs them side by side: Sam would need 6 days of work in 3 days.

进度表在纸面上看着没问题，却可能因为同一个人在同一时间被排了两项任务而失败，这叫超负荷分配。假设 Sam 要做任务 A（3 天）和任务 B（3 天），而计划让两者并行：Sam 要在 3 天里干完 6 天的活。

There are two standard fixes. Resource levelling changes the schedule to fit the person: run A then B, so Sam takes 3 + 3 = 6 days and the end date may move later. Resource smoothing keeps the end date and only shifts tasks within their float (the spare time a task can slip without delaying the end). A task can move no further than its float, so a 3-day clash fits inside 4 days of float but not inside 2. “Team and resources” introduced both ideas; here you choose between them.

有两种标准办法。资源平衡（levelling）让进度迁就人：先 A 后 B，Sam 用 3 + 3 = 6 天，结束日期可能推后。资源平滑（smoothing）保持结束日期不变，只在任务的浮动时间（任务可以推迟而不影响结束的富余时间）内挪动任务。任务最多只能挪动它的浮动时间那么远：3 天的冲突放得进 4 天浮动，放不进 2 天。“团队与资源”已讲过这两个概念，这里要学的是如何在两者之间选择。

Step 2 · Calendars and availability第 2 步 · 日历与可用性

Learn先学

A resource calendar shows when each person (or machine) is actually available: holidays, leave, part-time hours, other projects. Plan from the calendar, not from the idea that everyone is there every day, and check it again when the schedule changes.

资源日历显示每个人（或每台机器）实际什么时候可用：假期、休假、兼职时间、其他项目。要根据日历来规划，而不是假设每个人每天都在，进度改变时再核对一次。

Step 3 · Choosing the fix第 3 步 · 选择办法

Learn先学

If the end date is fixed and a task has float, try smoothing first: it usually costs nothing extra. If there is no float, you must ask: can the date move (levelling), can you add a person or pay for overtime (cost goes up), or can some work (scope) be cut? Put the options to the sponsor (the person who backs and funds the project) with the effect of each, rather than quietly overloading someone.

如果结束日期固定、任务又有浮动时间，先试平滑：它通常不用额外花钱。如果没有浮动时间，就得问：日期能不能推（平衡）、能不能加人或付加班费（成本上升）、部分工作（范围）能不能砍？把各个选项及其影响拿给发起人（支持并出资的人）定，而不是悄悄让某个人超负荷。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Over-allocation means one person is booked for more work than the time allows.
• Levelling fits the schedule to the person; the end date may move.
• Smoothing shifts tasks within their float; the end date stays.
• Plan from the resource calendar, not from full availability.
• With no float, put the options and their effects to the sponsor.

• 超负荷分配就是一个人被排了超出时间的工作量。
• 资源平衡让进度迁就人；结束日期可能推后。
• 资源平滑在浮动时间内挪动任务；结束日期不变。
• 根据资源日历来规划，而不是假设人人全时可用。
• 没有浮动时间时，把选项及其影响拿给发起人。
