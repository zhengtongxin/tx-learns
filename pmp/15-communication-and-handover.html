Communication and handover沟通与交接

Make messages land, write the right report for the right reader, and hand a project over cleanly · ~10 min · reading and practice能让信息被听懂，为不同读者写对报告，并完成一次清晰的交接 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Making a message land第 1 步 · 让信息被听懂

Learn先学

The basic model: a sender encodes a message, sends it through a channel, and a receiver decodes it. Noise, such as jargon, accents or a bad connection, can distort it on the way.

基本模型：发送者把想法编码成信息，通过渠道发出，接收者解码。行话、口音、信号差等噪音可能在途中扭曲信息。

The sender is responsible for making the message clear and complete, and for confirming it was understood, for example by asking the receiver to say it back. The receiver is responsible for listening and asking when unsure.

发送者负责让信息清楚完整，并负责确认对方听懂了，比如请对方复述。接收者负责认真听，不确定时提问。

Face to face is the richest channel: words, tone, expression, instant questions. Then video, phone, and writing, with email poorest for a sensitive or complex topic. Use rich channels for hard topics and writing when you need a record.

面对面是最丰富的渠道：有语言、语气、表情，还能当场提问。其次是视频、电话，再到文字，复杂或敏感话题用邮件最差。难题用丰富渠道，需要留记录时用文字。

sender -> encode -> channel -> decode -> receiver ^ | +------- feedback: 'Let me say it back' ---+

Step 2 · Reports for the reader第 2 步 · 为读者写报告

Learn先学

Three reports are easy to mix up. A status report says where things stand now. A progress report says what was achieved over a period. A forecast report says what is expected next, such as the likely finish date or cost.

三种报告容易混。状态报告说现在处在什么位置。进展报告说一段时间内完成了什么。预测报告说接下来预计会怎样，比如可能的完工日期或成本。

Then tailor to the audience. An executive has little time and wants the decision. Give an executive summary: the impact, the options, and your recommendation. Keep detail in an attachment for those who want it. The team needs detail; the sponsor needs impact.

然后按读者定制。管理层时间少，要的是决策。给一份执行摘要：影响、选项、你的建议，细节放附件。团队要细节，发起人要影响。

The communications management plan records who needs what information, how often, in which form and from whom. Build it from the stakeholder analysis and update it when the audience changes.

沟通管理计划记录谁需要什么信息、多久一次、什么形式、由谁提供。它来自相关方分析，受众变了就更新。

Impact: Go-live may slip by two sprints Options: A) add two testers B) cut the export feature Recommendation: B, cheapest and keeps the date for core features Decision needed: by Friday

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Handing over第 3 步 · 交接

Learn先学

If you leave the project, or it moves to an operations team, do a structured handover, not a quick chat or a folder of files. The goal: the successor can carry on without chasing you.

如果你要离开项目，或项目移交给运营团队，要做结构化交接，而不是随口聊聊或丢一个文件夹。目标是接手的人不必再追着你问。

Cover the things that live only in your head: open issues and risks, stakeholder relationships (who matters, what they care about, any tension), commitments already made, deferred decisions and why, plus where the documents are and who has which access.

要覆盖那些只存在于你脑子里的东西：未解决的问题和风险、相关方关系（谁重要、关心什么、有什么紧张点）、已经做出的承诺、被推迟的决定及原因，以及文件位置和各人的权限。

Do it in person if you can, record it in writing, and let the successor ask questions and confirm. That reuses step one: the sender confirms understanding.

可能的话当面讲，同时书面记录，让接手的人提问并确认。这正是第一步的用法：发送者要确认对方理解了。

[ ] open issues and risks, with owners [ ] stakeholders: who, what they want, sensitivities [ ] commitments and promised dates [ ] decisions put off, and why [ ] where files live; who has access [ ] a meeting where the successor asks questions

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• The sender makes the message clear and confirms it was understood.
• Face to face is the richest channel; use it for hard topics.
• Status is now, progress is what got done, forecast is what comes next.
• Give executives impact, options and a recommendation.
• A handover covers open issues, relationships, commitments and deferred decisions.

• 发送者负责让信息清楚，并确认对方听懂。
• 面对面最丰富；难题用它。
• 状态是现在，进展是已完成，预测是接下来。
• 给管理层：影响、选项、建议。
• 交接要覆盖未决问题、关系、承诺和推迟的决定。
