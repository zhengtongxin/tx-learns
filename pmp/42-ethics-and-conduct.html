Ethics and conduct职业操守

Handle conflicts of interest, gifts, mistakes and pressure to bend the truth with honesty and in the right order · ~10 min · reading and practice用诚实的态度、按正确的顺序处理利益冲突、礼物、错误和被要求隐瞒真相的压力 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Four values to hold第 1 步 · 要坚持的四个价值

Learn先学

Every project manager works under a code of professional conduct, and your organisation's own code applies those same rules to its work. You do not need to memorise its wording. Four values explain nearly every ethics question.

每位项目经理都在职业操守准则之下工作，而你所在组织自己的准则，是把这些规则用到它的工作中。你不必背它的原文，四个价值就能解释几乎所有的伦理题。

Responsibility: own your decisions and your mistakes. Respect: treat people and their cultures with dignity, and keep confidential information confidential. Fairness: decide on merit, not favour, and say when you have a conflict of interest (a personal gain or tie that could sway your judgement). Honesty: report the real status, even when it is bad, and bring a recovery plan (the steps to get back on track). A sponsor is the person who backs and pays for the project.

责任：对自己的决定和错误负责。尊重：有尊严地对待他人及其文化，并为机密信息保密。公平：凭实际情况而不是人情做决定，有利益冲突（可能左右你判断的个人好处或关系）就要说明。诚实：如实汇报现状，哪怕现状不好，并带上追赶计划（回到正轨的步骤）。发起人是支持并出资的人。

Exam questions are short scenarios. When one contains an ethical problem, the answer is almost never to ignore it and never to cover it up. It is to be honest, to disclose, and to use the proper channel.

考试题是简短的情境。当情境里出现伦理问题时，答案几乎从来不是装作没看见，更不是掩盖。而是诚实、披露，并走正规的渠道。

Pressure to bend the truth -> report the real status, with a recovery plan (steps back to on track) A gift that could sway you -> decline it, or disclose it as the policy says Your own conflict of interest -> disclose it and step back from the decision Your own mistake -> admit it, fix it, tell the people affected

Step 2 · Conflicts, gifts and secrets第 2 步 · 利益冲突、礼物和机密

Learn先学

A conflict of interest is not a crime by itself. Hiding it is the problem. If a bidder (a firm offering to do the work) is your relative, you say so and leave the choice to others.

利益冲突本身并不是错，隐瞒才是问题。如果投标方（提出承接工作的公司）是你的亲戚，你要说明，并把选择交给别人。

Gifts: follow the organisation's policy. A small thank-you card is usually fine. Anything that could look like a reward for a decision is declined or disclosed. Confidential information: use it only for the project and share it only with people who are allowed to see it. Never use it for your own benefit.

礼物：遵守组织的政策。一张小小的感谢卡通常没问题；任何看起来像是对某个决定的回报的东西，都要拒收或披露。机密信息：只用于项目，只告诉有权知道的人，绝不用来谋取私利。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Finding a problem, in order第 3 步 · 发现问题时的顺序

Learn先学

Suppose you learn that someone else has broken a rule. Do not jump to blame, and do not stay silent. Go in order: 1. Check the facts (is it really a breach?). 2. Raise it with the person involved or your manager, as your organisation's code says. 3. If it is not resolved, use the official reporting channel (usually an ethics office, a hotline, or a named person in the code of conduct). Throughout, keep a record.

假如你得知别人违反了规则，不要急着指责，也不要沉默。按顺序来：1. 先核实事实（真的是违规吗？）。2. 按组织的准则向当事人或你的上级提出。3. 如果仍未解决，走正式的举报渠道（通常是道德办公室、热线，或准则里指定的人）。全程做好记录。

If the mistake is yours, the same honesty applies: tell the sponsor and anyone affected, correct it, and say how you will stop it happening again.

如果错误出在你自己身上，同样要诚实：告诉发起人和受影响的人，改正它，并说明如何避免再次发生。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Four values: responsibility, respect, fairness, honesty.
• A conflict of interest is declared, not hidden; step back from the decision.
• Decline or disclose a gift that could sway a decision.
• Confidential information is for the project only.
• Find a problem: check the facts, raise it, then use the official channel.
• Own your own mistakes early and tell those affected.

• 四个价值：责任、尊重、公平、诚实。
• 利益冲突要说明，而不是隐瞒；并回避相关决定。
• 可能左右决定的礼物，拒收或披露。
• 机密信息只用于项目。
• 发现问题：先核实，再提出，必要时走正式渠道。
• 尽早承担自己的错误，并告诉受影响的人。
