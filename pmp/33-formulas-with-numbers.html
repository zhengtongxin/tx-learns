Formulas with numbers公式与算例

Work the earned value, forecast, estimate and channel formulas on real numbers, and say what each result means · ~12 min · worked examples能用真实数字算出挣值、预测、估算和沟通渠道公式，并说出每个结果的含义 · 约 12 分钟 · 算例练习

Step 1 · Where are we now第 1 步 · 我们现在在哪

Learn先学

Formulas are a small part of the exam, and easy marks if you have worked each one once. This lesson uses one project all the way through.

公式在考试里占比不大，但只要每个都亲手算过一次，就是送分题。这一课从头到尾用同一个项目。

A warehouse system has a total budget of S$200,000. That total is the budget at completion (BAC). Today the plan said half the work would be done, so planned value (PV) is S$100,000. Only 40% is really done, so earned value (EV) is 40% × 200,000 = S$80,000. The team has spent S$100,000: the actual cost (AC).

一个仓储系统的总预算是 20 万新元，这就是完工预算（BAC）。按计划今天应完成一半，所以计划价值（PV）是 10 万新元。实际只完成了 40%，所以挣值（EV）是 40% × 200,000 = 8 万新元。团队已经花了 10 万新元，这是实际成本（AC）。

Every formula here starts with EV. Subtract to get a variance, divide to get an index. Negative, or below 1, is bad.

这里的每个公式都以 EV 开头。相减得偏差，相除得指数。负数，或小于 1，就是不好。

BAC 200,000 PV 100,000 EV 80,000 AC 100,000 Cost variance CV = EV - AC = 80,000 - 100,000 = -20,000 over budget Schedule variance SV = EV - PV = 80,000 - 100,000 = -20,000 behind Cost index CPI = EV / AC = 80,000 / 100,000 = 0.80 Schedule index SPI = EV / PV = 80,000 / 100,000 = 0.80

Step 2 · Where will we end第 2 步 · 我们会落在哪

Learn先学

The estimate at completion (EAC) is the new forecast of total cost. Which formula you use depends on what the question says about the future.

完工估算（EAC）是对总成本的新预测。用哪个公式，取决于题目对后续情况怎么说。

If the current cost performance will continue: EAC = BAC ÷ CPI = 200,000 ÷ 0.80 = S$250,000. If the overrun was a one-off and the rest goes to plan: EAC = AC + (BAC − EV) = 100,000 + 120,000 = S$220,000.

如果目前的成本绩效会持续：EAC = BAC ÷ CPI = 200,000 ÷ 0.80 = 25 万新元。如果超支只是一次性的，剩下的按计划进行：EAC = AC + (BAC − EV) = 100,000 + 120,000 = 22 万新元。

From EAC come two more. Estimate to complete (ETC) = EAC − AC: the money still to spend. Variance at completion (VAC) = BAC − EAC: how far over or under you will finish.

有了 EAC 还能再得两个数。完工尚需估算（ETC）= EAC − AC：还要花多少钱。完工偏差（VAC）= BAC − EAC：最终会超支或节约多少。

The to-complete performance index (TCPI) is how efficient the remaining work must be to still finish on the original budget: work left ÷ money left. Above 1 means you must do better than planned from here on.

完工尚需绩效指数（TCPI）是说：要想仍按原预算完工，剩余工作必须达到多高的效率。算法是剩余工作 ÷ 剩余资金。大于 1 表示从现在起必须比计划做得更好。

EAC = BAC / CPI = 200,000 / 0.80 = 250,000 ETC = EAC - AC = 250,000 - 100,000 = 150,000 VAC = BAC - EAC = 200,000 - 250,000 = -50,000 TCPI = (BAC - EV) / (BAC - AC) = 120,000 / 100,000 = 1.20

Step 3 · Estimates, float and channels第 3 步 · 估算、浮动时间与渠道

Learn先学

Three-point estimate. Ask for an optimistic (O), most likely (M) and pessimistic (P) figure. The weighted form counts the most likely four times: (O + 4M + P) ÷ 6. The spread, called the standard deviation, is (P − O) ÷ 6.

三点估算。分别问出乐观（O）、最可能（M）、悲观（P）三个数。加权算法把最可能值算四次：(O + 4M + P) ÷ 6。离散程度叫标准差，算法是 (P − O) ÷ 6。

Float is how long a task can slip without delaying the project: late start − early start. Tasks on the critical path, the longest chain through the schedule, have zero float.

浮动时间是一项任务可以推迟多久而不拖延项目：最晚开始 − 最早开始。关键路径（进度计划中最长的一条任务链）上的任务，浮动时间为零。

Communication channels count the possible one-to-one links in a group of n people: n × (n − 1) ÷ 2. Count yourself. The number grows fast, which is why bigger teams need a clearer communication plan.

沟通渠道是 n 个人之间可能的一对一联系数：n × (n − 1) ÷ 2。别忘了把自己算进去。这个数增长很快，所以团队越大，越需要清楚的沟通计划。

Three-point O 4, M 6, P 14 days (4 + 4x6 + 14) / 6 = 42 / 6 = 7 days spread: (14 - 4) / 6 = 1.67 days Float late start on the 12th, early start on the 9th -> 3 days Channels 6 people: 6 x 5 / 2 = 15 8 people: 8 x 7 / 2 = 28
What I got wrong我踩过的坑

I multiplied BAC by CPI to forecast the total and got a number smaller than the budget for a project that was overspending. The check that saved me: a bad index must make the forecast worse.

我曾经把 BAC 乘以 CPI 来预测总成本，结果一个正在超支的项目算出来比预算还少。后来靠一条检查救了我：指数不好，预测一定更糟。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Every earned value formula starts with EV; negative or below 1 is bad.
• EAC = BAC ÷ CPI when performance continues; AC + (BAC − EV) when the overrun was a one-off.
• TCPI above 1 means the rest must go better than planned.
• Weighted three-point: (O + 4M + P) ÷ 6.
• Channels: n × (n − 1) ÷ 2, and read whether the question wants the total or the increase.

• 所有挣值公式都以 EV 开头；负数或小于 1 就是不好。
• 绩效持续时 EAC = BAC ÷ CPI；超支是一次性时用 AC + (BAC − EV)。
• TCPI 大于 1 表示剩余工作必须比计划做得更好。
• 加权三点估算：(O + 4M + P) ÷ 6。
• 沟通渠道：n × (n − 1) ÷ 2，并看清题目问的是总数还是增加量。
