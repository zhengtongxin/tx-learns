How the exam thinks · Lesson 3考试怎么考 · 第 3 课

Choosing an approach选择方法

Match a project to a predictive, agile or hybrid way of working and say why · ~10 min · reading and practice根据项目特点选预测型、敏捷或混合方式，并说出理由 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three ways of working第 1 步 · 三种工作方式

Learn先学

Predictive plans most of the work up front, then executes and controls against the plan. It fits stable requirements and a known solution, such as building a bridge.

预测型先把大部分工作规划好，再按计划执行和控制。适合需求稳定、方案明确的项目，比如建桥。

Agile works in short cycles, delivers something usable each time and adapts to feedback. It fits unclear or changing requirements, such as a new app.

敏捷以短周期工作，每次交付可用的成果，并根据反馈调整。适合需求不明确或会变的项目，比如新应用。

Hybrid mixes both: for example a fixed plan for the hardware and short cycles for the software on top of it.

混合两者结合：比如硬件按固定计划做，在它之上的软件用短周期开发。

Requirements stable + solution known -> predictive Requirements unclear / changing -> agile Some parts fixed, some uncertain -> hybrid

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Pick from the situation第 2 步 · 从情境里选

Learn先学

The question rarely names the approach. Look for clues: requirements stable or changing, customer available for feedback, regulation that needs documented sign-off, and how costly a late change is.

题目很少直接点明方法。找线索：需求是稳定还是多变、客户能不能随时反馈、有没有法规要求书面签字，以及后期改动代价高不高。

Cost of change is a good clue: when a late change is cheap, agile fits; when it is very expensive, such as pouring concrete, plan first.

后期改动的代价是个好线索：改动便宜时适合敏捷；改动极贵（比如浇筑混凝土）时要先规划。

Do not assume agile is always better. The best approach is the one that fits the work, and an organisation may also require some governance in any approach.

别以为敏捷总是更好。最好的方法是适合这项工作的那个，而且无论哪种方法，组织都可能要求一定的治理。

Step 3 · The trap in agile questions第 3 步 · 敏捷题的陷阱

Learn先学

In agile, change is expected. There is no separate change-management step: new or changed needs go into the product backlog and the product owner orders them. Do not reach for a formal change request there.

敏捷里变化是正常的，没有单独的变更管理步骤：新的或变化的需求进入产品待办列表，由产品负责人排序。别在这里去找正式的变更申请。

In predictive work the opposite holds: a change goes through change control (see “Change control”). Check which approach the scenario is in before you pick a change answer.

预测型正相反：变更要走变更控制（见“变更控制”）。选变更类答案之前，先确认情境用的是哪种方法。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Predictive plans first; agile adapts in short cycles; hybrid mixes them.
• Stable and known points to predictive; unclear and changing to agile.
• Agile is not always the right answer.
• Agile changes go to the product backlog, not a change request.
• Predictive changes go through change control.

• 预测型先规划，敏捷以短周期适应，混合是两者结合。
• 稳定且明确倾向预测型，不明确且多变倾向敏捷。
• 敏捷不一定总是正确答案。
• 敏捷的变化进产品待办列表，不是变更申请。
• 预测型的变更走变更控制。
