Agile and people drill敏捷与人员练习

Answer nine situations by using the agile route for agile work and by involving the right people at the right size · ~12 min · practice能在九个情境里为敏捷工作选对敏捷的做法，并在合适的范围内找对人 · 约 12 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · Agile work, agile tools第 1 步 · 敏捷的工作用敏捷的工具

Learn先学

First spot the approach. Words such as sprint, backlog, product owner, demo and retrospective mean agile. Then drop any option that uses a predictive tool: a change control board, a formal impact meeting, a phase timeline.

先看出是哪种方法。出现冲刺、待办列表、产品负责人、演示、回顾会这些词，就是敏捷。然后排除用预测型工具的选项：变更控制委员会、正式的影响评估会、阶段时间表。

In agile, new requests go to the product owner and the backlog, progress is shown as working product, and the team owns its estimates and its way of working.

在敏捷里，新请求交给产品负责人并进入待办列表，进展靠可用的产品来展示，估算和工作方式由团队自己负责。

Step 2 · The right people, the right size第 2 步 · 找对人，范围要对

Learn先学

Match the response to the size of the problem. One person struggling: a private one-to-one talk. A whole-team problem: the whole team works it out. Using a group fix for one person, or a private chat for a team problem, is the trap.

回应的范围要和问题的大小对上。一个人遇到困难：私下一对一谈。整个团队的问题：整个团队一起解决。用集体办法处理个人问题，或者用私下谈话处理团队问题，都是陷阱。

Before you design a fix, involve the people it affects. On an agile team especially, the team looks at its own process first.

设计解决办法之前，先让受影响的人参与进来。尤其在敏捷团队里，团队要先自己审视自己的流程。

Step 3 · Two-way before one-way第 3 步 · 先双向，后单向

Learn先学

Reports, emails, newsletters and training push information one way. When a person is unconvinced, silent or resisting, choose the option where you talk with them and ask.

报告、邮件、简报和培训都是单向推送信息。当对方不认同、沉默或抵触时，要选与他们对话并提问的选项。

Trust on a team comes from how people are treated day to day: open communication and confidence in what they can do. A programme laid on top does not create it.

团队里的信任来自日常怎么对待人：开放的沟通，以及对他们能力的信心。外加一个项目是造不出信任的。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Agile words in the story: drop options that use predictive tools.
• New agile requests go to the product owner and the backlog.
• One person: one-to-one. Whole team: whole team.
• The team examines its own process before anyone imposes a fix.
• Talk and ask before you send, train or escalate.

• 情境里出现敏捷用词：排除使用预测型工具的选项。
• 敏捷里的新请求交给产品负责人，进入待办列表。
• 一个人的问题：一对一。全队的问题：全队一起。
• 在任何人强加办法之前，团队先自己审视流程。
• 先对话、先问，再考虑发文件、培训或上报。
