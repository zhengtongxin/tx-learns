Value and benefits价值与效益

Tell outputs, outcomes and benefits apart, and act when a project drifts from its business case · ~10 min · reading and practice分清产出、成果和效益，并在项目偏离商业论证时知道该怎么做 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Why the project exists第 1 步 · 项目为什么存在

Learn先学

A business case explains why the organisation should fund the project: the problem, the options, the cost and the expected benefits. The sponsor approves it, and every later decision is checked against it.

商业论证说明组织为什么要出钱做这个项目：问题、备选方案、成本和预期效益。发起人批准它，之后的每个决定都要对照它。

Benefits realisation is making sure those benefits actually show up. This often happens after the project ends, so someone must own it, usually the business, with the project manager supporting.

效益实现是确保这些效益真的出现。这往往发生在项目结束之后，所以必须有人负责，通常是业务方，项目经理从旁支持。

Keep three words apart. An output is what the project delivers. An outcome is the change that results from using it. A benefit is the value the organisation gains from that change.

三个词要分开。产出是项目交付的东西。成果是使用它之后产生的变化。效益是组织从这个变化中得到的价值。

Output a new online booking system goes live Outcome customers book without phoning the clinic Benefit front-desk calls drop by half, saving staff hours A project can finish its output on time and still miss the benefit if nobody uses it.

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Delivering value earlier第 2 步 · 更早交付价值

Learn先学

Value does not have to wait for the end. If stakeholders say one feature matters most, deliver it first. Reprioritise the backlog or plan so high-value work comes earlier.

价值不必等到最后。如果相关方说某个功能最重要，就先交付它。重新排列待办列表或计划，让高价值的工作靠前。

Before you reprioritise, validate the impact with the stakeholders. Reordering changes what people get and when, so confirm what it means for them first. Then update the plan.

重新排序之前，先和相关方确认影响。调整顺序会改变大家什么时候拿到什么，所以先确认对他们意味着什么，再更新计划。

Step 3 · When the plan drifts第 3 步 · 计划偏离时

Learn先学

Sometimes the approach you were told to use will not meet the approved business case, for example it costs too much or arrives too late. Do not just follow it, and do not quietly change it.

有时上级要求的做法无法满足已批准的商业论证，比如太贵或太晚。不要照做了事，也不要悄悄改掉。

Compare the options against the target, then bring the analysis to the sponsor before locking anything in. The sponsor owns the business case and makes the call.

把各个方案和目标做比较，然后在定下来之前，把分析交给发起人。商业论证归发起人负责，由他决定。

Value engineering means getting the same function at a lower cost, for example a cheaper material that does the same job. It is not cutting scope. It is also different from Monte Carlo, which simulates risk.

价值工程是用更低的成本实现相同的功能，比如用更便宜但功能相同的材料。它不是砍范围，也不同于模拟风险的蒙特卡洛。

Directed approach will not meet the business case 1. compare options against the target 2. bring the analysis to the sponsor 3. decide together, then update the plan Value engineering: same function, lower cost.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• The business case is why the project exists, and the sponsor owns it.
• Output is delivered, outcome is the change, benefit is the value gained.
• Validate impact with stakeholders before reprioritising.
• If the directed approach misses the business case, analyse options and go to the sponsor.
• Value engineering keeps the function and lowers the cost.

• 商业论证说明项目为何存在，由发起人负责。
• 产出是交付的东西，成果是变化，效益是得到的价值。
• 重新排序之前，先和相关方确认影响。
• 指定的做法不符合商业论证时，先分析方案再找发起人。
• 价值工程保持功能不变，降低成本。
