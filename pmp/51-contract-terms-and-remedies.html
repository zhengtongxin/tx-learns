Contract terms and remedies合同条款与补救

Step 1 · Late work and agreed damages第 1 步 · 延误与约定赔偿

Learn先学

A contract can say in advance what lateness costs. Liquidated damages are a daily amount, agreed up front, that the supplier pays for each day a deliverable is late. Often there is a cap, a ceiling on the total. Example: 6 days late at S$500 a day is 6 × 500 = S$3,000, but if the cap is S$2,000 the supplier pays S$2,000.

合同可以事先写明延误的代价。约定违约金是事先商定的每日金额，交付物每延误一天，供应方就付一天的约定金额。通常还有上限，即总额的封顶。例子：晚 6 天、每天 S$500，是 6 × 500 = S$3,000；但上限为 S$2,000 时，供应方只付 S$2,000。

As project manager you do not decide the legal position. You record the dates, tell the contract owner (the person in your organisation who is accountable for the contract) early and keep the evidence. Whether you are the client or the supplier, the rules on lateness and notice are the same. This lesson is general education, not legal advice.

作为项目经理，你不负责判定法律立场。你要记录日期、尽早告知合同负责人（你所在组织中对该合同负责的人），并保存证据。无论你是客户还是供应方，关于延误和通知的规则都一样。本课是一般性学习内容，不是法律意见。

Step 2 · Events nobody controls第 2 步 · 没人能控制的事件

Learn先学

A force majeure clause covers events that neither side could prevent, such as a flood that closes the site. Usually it gives relief from blame and extra time, not extra money. The party affected must tell the other side promptly, in writing, and show how the event hit the work.

不可抗力条款涵盖双方都无法阻止的事件，例如洪水封了工地。通常它免去责任并给予额外时间，而不是额外的钱。受影响的一方必须及时通知对方，并以书面形式说明事件如何影响了工作。

Step 3 · Ending a contract and disputes第 3 步 · 终止合同与争议

Learn先学

A contract can end for cause (one side broke the terms, which is called a breach) or for convenience (no fault, one side simply wants out, and the side that wants out usually pays for the work already done). Disputes usually climb a ladder: talk first, then mediation (a neutral helper), then arbitration (a neutral person or panel that makes a binding decision) or court. Keep records and pass it up to the contract owner instead of improvising.

合同可以因违约而终止（一方违反了条款，称为违约），也可以因便利而终止（没有过错，只是一方想退出，通常要为已完成的工作付款）。争议通常逐级上升：先协商，再调解（中立的帮手），然后仲裁（由中立的个人或小组作出有约束力的裁决）或诉讼。要保存记录并上报合同负责人，而不是自己随意处理。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Liquidated damages are a daily late amount agreed up front, often with a cap.
• 6 days at S$500 is S$3,000, but a S$2,000 cap means S$2,000.
• Force majeure gives time, not usually money, and needs prompt notice with evidence.
• Ending for cause follows a breach; for convenience follows no fault and usually pays for work done.
• Keep records and escalate to the contract owner; this is general education, not legal advice.

• 约定违约金是事先商定的每日延误金额，常带上限。
• 晚 6 天每天 S$500 是 S$3,000，但上限 S$2,000 时只付 S$2,000。
• 不可抗力通常给时间而不是钱，并需要及时通知和证据。
• 因违约终止源于违反条款；因便利终止没有过错，通常要为已完成的工作付款。
• 保存记录并上报合同负责人；本课是一般性学习内容，不是法律意见。
