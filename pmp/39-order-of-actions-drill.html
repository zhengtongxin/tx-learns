Order of actions drill行动顺序练习

Choose the step that comes first or next in nine situations, and say why the other steps are too early or too late · ~12 min · practice能在九个情境里选出最先或下一步该做的事，并说出其他步骤为什么太早或太晚 · 约 12 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · Check before you change第 1 步 · 先核实，再改动

Learn先学

Most wrong answers on order are good actions taken too soon. When the impact or the cause is still unknown, the first step is to find out. Then update the plan. Then tell people.

顺序题里的错误答案，大多是做得太早的正确动作。影响或原因还不清楚时，第一步是弄清楚；然后更新计划；再通知相关的人。

Formal change control exists to protect an approved baseline. Before any baseline is approved there is nothing for it to protect yet, so you assess the request and fold it into the draft plan.

正式的变更控制是为了保护已批准的基准。基准还没批准时，它还没有可保护的对象，所以你要评估请求，并把它纳入计划草案。

Step 2 · Act once you know第 2 步 · 弄清之后就行动

Learn先学

The opposite mistake is just as common: the facts are clear and the answer still only records, defers or passes the problem up. When a response is already planned, or the fix is inside your authority, do it.

相反的错误同样常见：事实已经清楚，答案却只是记录、推迟或往上推。应对措施已经计划好，或者解决办法就在你的权限内，那就去做。

Step 3 · Start and finish in order第 3 步 · 开头和收尾的顺序

Learn先学

At the start: understand why before planning how. Read the charter and the business case first.

启动时：先弄懂为什么，再规划怎么做。先读章程和商业论证。

At the end: acceptance, then lessons learned and open obligations while the team is still there, then hand over and archive, then release the team.

收尾时：先验收；趁团队还在，做经验教训总结并了结未完成的义务；然后移交和归档；最后解散团队。

1 Confirm the work is complete and formally accepted 2 Capture lessons learned, settle contracts and open items 3 Hand over to operations, archive the records 4 Release the team

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Unknown impact or cause: find out first, then update the plan, then tell people.
• No approved baseline yet means no formal change control yet.
• A planned response exists: carry it out.
• Inside your authority: act, do not escalate.
• Closing: acceptance, lessons and open obligations, handover and archive, release.

• 影响或原因不明：先弄清，再更新计划，再通知相关的人。
• 基准还没批准，就还没有正式的变更控制。
• 已有计划好的应对措施：执行它。
• 在你的权限内：行动，不要上报。
• 收尾：验收，经验教训与未完成的义务，移交与归档，解散。
