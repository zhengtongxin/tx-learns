Emotional intelligence and influence情商与影响力

Name the four parts of emotional intelligence, and use influence rather than orders when you lead without authority · ~9 min · reading and practice说出情商的四个部分，并在没有职权时用影响力而不是命令来带领 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Reading yourself and others第 1 步 · 读懂自己和他人

Learn先学

Emotional intelligence is noticing feelings, yours and other people's, and using that to act well. It has four parts: self-awareness (knowing what you feel and why), self-control (not letting a strong feeling decide what you do), empathy (understanding how another person sees the situation) and social skill (working with people to get things done).

情商是察觉感受（自己的和别人的），并据此恰当地行动。它有四个部分：自我觉察（知道自己感受到什么、为什么）、自我控制（不让强烈的情绪决定你的行为）、共情（理解别人怎样看待这件事）和社交技巧（与人合作把事情办成）。

A project manager (the person who leads the project day to day) needs it because most project problems are people problems: a stakeholder (someone with a stake in the result) who feels ignored, a team member who is quietly stressed, a meeting that went badly.

项目经理（日常带领项目的人）需要它，因为大多数项目问题其实是人的问题：感觉被忽视的相关方（与结果有利害关系的人）、默默承受压力的团队成员、一场开得很糟的会。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Influence without orders第 2 步 · 没有命令权的影响力

Learn先学

Often you must get things done through people who do not report to you. Influence is how: build trust by doing what you say, explain the reason behind a request, listen first, and show what is in it for them. Authority (the right to give orders) helps, but people follow a leader they trust much more willingly than one who only has a title.

你常常要通过不向你汇报的人把事情办成。办法就是影响力：说到做到来建立信任，解释请求背后的理由，先倾听，并让对方看到对他们有什么好处。职权（下命令的权利）有帮助，但人们更愿意追随自己信任的领导，而不只是追随头衔。

Step 3 · Putting it to work第 3 步 · 把它用起来

Learn先学

When a person reacts strongly, pause before you answer, ask what is behind it, and say back what you heard. Choose the moment and the place: raise a sensitive point in private, not in front of the whole team. If a strong feeling is your own, name it to yourself first, then decide what to do.

有人反应强烈时，先停一下再回答，问问背后是什么，并复述你听到的。要选时机和场合：敏感的事私下说，不要当着全队的面。如果强烈的情绪是你自己的，先在心里把它说出来，再决定怎么做。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Emotional intelligence: self-awareness, self-control, empathy and social skill.
• Most project problems are people problems.
• Without authority, lead by influence: trust, reasons, listening and agreement.
• Pause before answering a strong reaction, and raise sensitive points in private.
• Escalate (take it to someone with more authority) only after influence has failed.

• 情商：自我觉察、自我控制、共情、社交技巧。
• 大多数项目问题是人的问题。
• 没有职权时靠影响力来带领：信任、理由、倾听和达成一致。
• 对强烈反应先停一下再回答，敏感的事私下谈。
• 影响力用尽之后才上报（交给更有权力的人处理）。
