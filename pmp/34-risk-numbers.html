Risk numbers风险的数字

Put a money value on a risk, compare two options with a decision tree, and size a reserve · ~10 min · reading and practice能给风险算出金额、用决策树比较两个方案，并估出储备金的大小 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Expected monetary value第 1 步 · 预期货币价值

Learn先学

Rating a risk high, medium or low ranks it. To budget for it you need a number. Expected monetary value (EMV) gives one: probability × impact.

把风险评成高、中、低，只能排序。要为它做预算，就需要一个数字。预期货币价值（EMV）就是这个数：概率 × 影响。

A threat has a negative impact, an opportunity a positive one. Add the EMVs of all the listed risks and you have a reasoned size for the contingency reserve, the money set aside for identified risks.

威胁的影响是负的，机会的影响是正的。把所有已列出风险的 EMV 加起来，就得到一个有依据的应急储备数额，也就是为已识别风险预留的钱。

EMV is an average over many projects. On this one project the risk either happens or it does not, so the reserve covers the mix of risks, not each one in full.

EMV 是很多项目平均下来的结果。在这一个项目上，风险要么发生要么不发生，所以储备覆盖的是整体风险组合，而不是每个风险的全额。

Late permit 20% x -50,000 = -10,000 Supplier price rise 10% x -30,000 = -3,000 Early bulk discount 30% x +20,000 = +6,000 ------- Total EMV -7,000 Contingency reserve 7,000

Step 2 · Decision trees第 2 步 · 决策树

Learn先学

A decision tree compares options when each one has uncertain outcomes. For each option, add its known cost to the EMV of what might follow. Then pick the option with the best total.

当每个方案的结果都不确定时，用决策树来比较。对每个方案，把确定的成本加上后续可能结果的 EMV，然后选总数最好的那个。

A team must choose between building a reporting tool and buying one. Building costs S$100,000, with a 40% chance of a delay that adds S$50,000. Buying costs S$115,000, with a 10% chance of S$20,000 in integration work.

团队要在自建和购买报表工具之间选择。自建要 10 万新元，有 40% 的概率延期，多花 5 万新元。购买要 11.5 万新元，有 10% 的概率需要 2 万新元的集成工作。

Buying looks dearer on the price tag and is cheaper once risk is counted.

只看标价，购买更贵；把风险算进去，购买反而更便宜。

Build 100,000 + (40% x 50,000) = 100,000 + 20,000 = 120,000 Buy 115,000 + (10% x 20,000) = 115,000 + 2,000 = 117,000 Lower expected cost: Buy

Step 3 · Other number tools第 3 步 · 其他定量工具

Learn先学

Two more names appear in questions. You need to know what each is for, not how to run it.

题目里还会出现两个名字。你只需要知道各自用来做什么，不用会操作。

Sensitivity analysis asks which single risk or assumption moves the result most. It is drawn as a tornado diagram: bars sorted with the widest on top, so the risk to watch first is the top bar.

敏感性分析问的是：哪一个风险或假设对结果影响最大。它画出来是龙卷风图：柱子按宽度排序，最宽的在最上面，所以最该先盯的风险就是最上面那根。

Simulation, often called Monte Carlo, runs the plan thousands of times with different values and reports a chance, such as an 80% chance of finishing by June. It tells you how likely an outcome is. It does not lower any cost.

模拟（常叫蒙特卡洛）用不同的取值把计划跑上几千遍，然后给出一个概率，比如 6 月前完工的可能性是 80%。它告诉你某个结果有多大可能，并不会降低任何成本。

All of these are quantitative, so they come after the quick high, medium, low ranking, and only for the risks that deserve the effort.

这些都属于定量分析，所以要排在快速的高、中、低排序之后，而且只用于值得花这份功夫的风险。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• EMV = probability × impact; threats negative, opportunities positive.
• The sum of EMVs is a reasoned contingency reserve.
• Decision tree: known cost plus EMV for each option, then compare.
• Tornado diagram: the top bar is the risk that matters most.
• Simulation gives a probability of an outcome; it changes no cost.

• EMV = 概率 × 影响；威胁为负，机会为正。
• 各风险 EMV 之和，是有依据的应急储备。
• 决策树：每个方案的确定成本加 EMV，再比较。
• 龙卷风图：最上面那根柱子是最要紧的风险。
• 模拟给出某个结果的概率，不会改变成本。
