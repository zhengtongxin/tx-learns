Organisational change组织变革

Use ADKAR to see where people are stuck and what to do for each stage · ~10 min · reading and practice用 ADKAR 看出人们卡在哪一步，以及每一步该怎么做 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Why change is hard第 1 步 · 变革为什么难

Learn先学

A project delivers something new, but people have to change how they work to use it. Delivery alone does not give the benefit. Adoption does.

项目交付新东西，但人们得改变工作方式才能用上它。光交付并不带来效益，被采用才会。

People resist for good reasons: fear of losing a role, extra effort, doubt that it will work or no say in the decision. Treat resistance as information. It tells you what the plan has missed.

人们抗拒往往有合理原因：担心失去岗位、多出工作量、怀疑是否有效，或者没有发言权。把抗拒当作信息，它告诉你计划漏了什么。

A visible sponsor matters most. They explain why, back the change and clear obstacles. Without one, the project manager cannot carry it alone.

一位看得见的发起人最重要。他解释原因、支持变革、扫清障碍。没有发起人，项目经理一个人扛不动。

Step 2 · The ADKAR stages第 2 步 · ADKAR 五个阶段

Learn先学

ADKAR is a model of change for one person at a time. The five stages come in order, and a person cannot skip one: Awareness of why change is needed, Desire to take part, Knowledge of how to change, Ability to do it in practice, Reinforcement to make it stick.

ADKAR 是针对个人的变革模型。五个阶段按顺序进行，不能跳过：认知（为什么要变）、意愿（愿意参与）、知识（知道怎么变）、能力（能在实践中做到）、巩固（让它持续下去）。

The first gap you find is the one to fix. Training will not help someone who does not yet see why the change is needed.

找到的第一个缺口就是要先补的。如果一个人还不明白为什么要变，培训帮不上忙。

A Awareness why is change needed? -> communicate D Desire will I take part? -> sponsor, involve K Knowledge how do I change? -> training A Ability can I do it? -> practice, coaching R Reinforcement will it stick? -> recognition, follow-up

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · What the project manager does第 3 步 · 项目经理做什么

Learn先学

Match your action to the missing stage. Early on, communicate the reason and the benefit so that awareness grows. Involve people in design to build desire.

让行动对应缺失的阶段。早期要沟通原因和好处，让认知增长；让人们参与设计，培养意愿。

Then provide training and hands-on support so knowledge becomes ability. After go-live, reinforce with follow-up, recognition and fixing what still hurts.

然后提供培训和现场支持，让知识变成能力。上线后用跟进、认可和修复遗留问题来巩固。

Do this with the sponsor, not around them. Plan it from the start, as part of the project, not as an afterthought.

要和发起人一起做，而不是绕开他。从一开始就计划，作为项目的一部分，而不是事后补做。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Adoption, not delivery, produces the benefit.
• Resistance is information, not misbehaviour.
• ADKAR: awareness, desire, knowledge, ability, reinforcement, in order.
• Fix the first missing stage, not a later one.
• Plan communication and training from the start, with the sponsor.

• 带来效益的是被采用，而不是交付。
• 抗拒是信息，不是捣乱。
• ADKAR：认知、意愿、知识、能力、巩固，按顺序。
• 先补第一个缺失的阶段，而不是后面的。
• 从一开始就与发起人一起计划沟通和培训。
