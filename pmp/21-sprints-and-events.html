Sprints and events冲刺与会议

Name the four sprint events, what each is for and what to do when a sprint is in trouble · ~10 min · reading and practice说出冲刺的四个会议各自的用途，以及冲刺出问题时怎么做 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A sprint is a timebox第 1 步 · 冲刺是个定长的时间盒

Learn先学

A timebox is a fixed length of time that is not stretched. A sprint is usually one to four weeks. Each has a sprint goal: one sentence on why this sprint matters. When work will not fit, the scope changes, not the end date.

时间盒是长度固定、不会拉长的一段时间。冲刺通常是一到四周。每个冲刺有一个冲刺目标：一句话说明这个冲刺为什么重要。工作装不下时，改的是范围，不是结束日期。

Four events fill the rhythm of every sprint.

每个冲刺的节奏由四个会议构成。

Sprint planning what can we finish? set the sprint goal start Daily stand-up 15 minutes, the team syncs and raises blockers every day Sprint review show the working increment, collect feedback end Retrospective how did we work? choose 1 or 2 improvements after the review

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Review the product, retrospect the process第 2 步 · 评审产品，回顾过程

Learn先学

The sprint review inspects the product. The team shows the working increment to stakeholders (people with a stake in the product), and the feedback goes to the product owner, who updates the product backlog.

冲刺评审检查的是产品。团队向相关方（对产品有利害关系的人）展示可用的增量，反馈交给产品负责人，由他更新产品待办列表。

The retrospective inspects the way of working. Only the team takes part, and it is blameless: the question is what to change, never who to blame. At least one agreed improvement goes into the next sprint’s plan.

回顾会检查的是工作方式。只有团队成员参加，而且不追责：问的是要改什么，不是怪谁。至少有一项约定的改进要放进下一个冲刺的计划。

Step 3 · When a sprint is in trouble第 3 步 · 冲刺出问题的时候

Learn先学

If the sprint goal looks out of reach, the team and the product owner renegotiate the scope. They do not extend the sprint, and nobody adds extra work. Unfinished items go back to the product backlog and are discussed in the retrospective.

如果冲刺目标看起来够不着，团队和产品负责人重新商定范围。不延长冲刺，也不再塞工作。没做完的条目回到产品待办列表，并在回顾会上讨论。

Only the product owner can cancel a sprint, and only when its goal no longer makes sense, for example when the business direction changed.

只有产品负责人能取消冲刺，而且只在目标已经没意义时，比如业务方向变了。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A sprint is a fixed timebox; scope flexes, the end date does not.
• Planning chooses the work; the stand-up syncs the team each day.
• The review inspects the product with stakeholders; the retrospective inspects the process within the team.
• A sprint in trouble gets renegotiated scope, not extra days or extra work.
• Only the product owner can cancel a sprint.

• 冲刺是固定的时间盒；范围可调，结束日期不动。
• 计划会选工作；站会每天同步团队。
• 评审与相关方一起检查产品；回顾会在团队内部检查过程。
• 冲刺出问题，重新商定范围，不加天数、不加工作。
• 只有产品负责人能取消冲刺。
