The exam shape考试的样子

Say how the exam is split and what a question usually asks of you · ~8 min · reading and practice说清考试怎么分配内容，以及题目通常要你做什么 · 约 8 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three areas, new weights第 1 步 · 三个领域，新占比

Learn先学

The exam covers three areas: People, Process and Business Environment. People is how you lead and support the team. Process is how the work is planned and run. Business Environment links the project to the organisation and its goals.

考试分三个领域：人员、过程和商业环境。人员是怎样带领和支持团队，过程是怎样计划和执行工作，商业环境是把项目与组织及其目标联系起来。

From July 2026 the split is People 33%, Process 41%, Business Environment 26%. Before July 2026 it was 42%, 50% and 8%. Older practice questions and notes may lean on the old split, so do not read too much into how often a topic appeared there.

从 2026 年 7 月起，比例是人员 33%、过程 41%、商业环境 26%。2026 年 7 月之前是 42%、50%、8%。旧的练习题和笔记可能按旧比例出题，所以别把旧题里某个主题出现的频率看得太重。

The big change: Business Environment grows from a small slice to about a quarter of the exam. Value, compliance and organisational change now deserve real study time.

最大的变化：商业环境从一小块变成约四分之一。价值、合规和组织变革现在值得认真花时间学。

Before July 2026 From July 2026 People 42% -> 33% Process 50% -> 41% Business Env. 8% -> 26%

Step 2 · Judgement, not recall第 2 步 · 考判断，不考背诵

Learn先学

Most questions put you in a short situation and ask what to do. Several options may be reasonable. You pick the best one for that situation, using agile and predictive practice together.

大多数题目给你一个简短情境，问你该怎么做。几个选项可能都说得通，你要选对这个情境最好的那个，同时运用敏捷和预测型两种做法。

Some questions are multiple-select: two or three answers are right and you must choose all of them. Read the instruction line each time. This is a common weak spot, so practise it on purpose.

有些题是多选题：两三个答案正确，要全部选对。每次都读清题目里的选择要求。这是常见的薄弱点，要专门练。

Formulas and terms still matter. They are the tools you use to judge the situation, so learn them, then practise using them in scenarios (see “Scenario questions”).

公式和术语仍然重要。它们是你判断情境的工具，先学会，再在情境题里练习运用（见“情境题”）。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Study from your mistakes第 3 步 · 从错题里学

Learn先学

Keep a wrong-question book. Log every question you got wrong or guessed, even if you guessed right. A lucky guess hides a gap.

建一本错题本。每道做错或蒙的题都记下来，蒙对也算。运气好会掩盖知识漏洞。

Mark whether you missed it once or twice. Redo only those, with questions and options shuffled so memory cannot help. Group them by concept, not by source, so you see the theme behind the misses.

标注是错了一次还是两次。只重做这些题，并且打乱题目和选项顺序，免得靠记忆。按概念而不是按来源分类，才能看出错题背后的主题。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Three areas: People, Process, Business Environment.
• From July 2026: 33 / 41 / 26. Before: 42 / 50 / 8.
• Questions test judgement in a situation, not recall alone.
• Multiple-select questions need all the right answers.
• Log wrong and guessed questions, then redo them shuffled.

• 三个领域：人员、过程、商业环境。
• 2026 年 7 月起：33 / 41 / 26。之前：42 / 50 / 8。
• 题目考的是情境里的判断，不只是记忆。
• 多选题要选全所有正确答案。
• 记录做错和蒙的题，打乱后重做。
