People · Lesson 3人员 · 第 3 课

Stakeholders相关方

Sort stakeholders on a power and interest grid and choose how to engage each one · ~10 min · reading and practice把相关方放进权力/利益方格，并为每个人选对沟通方式 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Find and sort第 1 步 · 识别与分类

Learn先学

A stakeholder is anyone who can affect the project or be affected by it. That includes the sponsor, the team, customers, suppliers, regulators and the people who will live with the result. Identify them early and keep checking, because new ones appear as the project moves.

相关方是任何能影响项目或被项目影响的人，包括发起人、团队、客户、供应商、监管机构，以及将来要使用成果的人。要尽早识别，并且持续更新，因为项目推进中会冒出新的相关方。

The power and interest grid sorts them by two questions: how much power do they have over the project, and how much do they care? The answer tells you how much effort each one deserves.

权力/利益方格用两个问题给他们分类：他们对项目有多大权力？有多关心项目？答案决定你该在每个人身上花多少力气。

low interest high interest high power | keep satisfied | manage closely | low power | monitor | keep informed | Example: a regulator reads nothing but can stop the launch -> high power, low interest -> keep satisfied

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Engagement levels第 2 步 · 参与程度

Learn先学

An engagement assessment matrix compares where each stakeholder is today with where you need them. The usual levels run from unaware, to resistant, neutral, supportive, and leading.

相关方参与度评估矩阵把每个人目前的位置与你需要他们达到的位置作比较。常见层级从不知情、抵制、中立、支持，到引领。

Mark current (C) and desired (D). Where C and D are the same, keep it that way. Where they differ, that gap is your engagement plan.

标出现状（C）和期望（D）。两者相同就保持；不同，这个差距就是你的参与计划。

unaware resistant neutral supportive leading Sponsor C D Finance C D User lead C D

Step 3 · Engage by what they need第 3 步 · 按需求去沟通

Learn先学

There is no single best way to communicate. Ask each important stakeholder what they need to know, how often, and in what form, then write it into the stakeholder engagement plan. A busy executive may want one line a week; a team lead may want a daily conversation.

沟通没有唯一的最佳方式。要问每位重要相关方：需要知道什么、多久一次、用什么形式，再写进相关方参与计划。忙碌的高管可能每周只要一句话，团队负责人也许每天都要聊。

On the exam, when a stakeholder is unhappy or silent, the first move is usually to talk to them and understand the concern. Escalating, ignoring or reporting around them comes later, if at all. Update the register and the plan afterwards.

考试中，相关方不满或不吭声时，第一步通常是先和他们谈，弄清顾虑。上报、无视或绕开他们，要等到之后，甚至用不上。事后更新相关方登记册和参与计划。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Stakeholders are anyone affected by, or able to affect, the project; keep identifying them.
• Power and interest set the effort: manage closely, keep satisfied, keep informed, monitor.
• The engagement matrix compares current with wanted levels; the gap is the plan.
• Ask each person what they need, then write it into the engagement plan.
• With an unhappy stakeholder, understand the concern first.

• 相关方是能影响项目或被项目影响的人，要持续识别。
• 权力与利益决定投入：重点管理、令其满意、随时告知、持续观察。
• 参与度矩阵比较现状与期望，差距就是计划。
• 先问对方需要什么，再写进参与计划。
• 相关方不满时，先弄清他的顾虑。
