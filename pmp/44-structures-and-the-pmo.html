Structures and the PMO组织结构与项目管理办公室

Step 1 · How the organisation is set up第 1 步 · 组织是怎么搭起来的

Learn先学

How much say a project manager has depends on the organisation's structure. There are three basic kinds. In a functional organisation, people are grouped by specialty (engineering, finance, sales) under a functional manager, the head of that department. The project manager has little authority, and team members still report to their department head.

项目经理有多大话语权，取决于组织的结构。基本有三种。职能型组织按专业把人分组（工程、财务、销售），由职能经理（该部门的负责人）管理。项目经理的权力很小，团队成员仍然向部门负责人汇报。

In a projectized organisation, people are grouped around projects and work for the project manager, who has high authority. In a matrix organisation, people report to both a functional manager and a project manager. A weak matrix leans towards the functional manager, a strong matrix towards the project manager, and a balanced one shares power.

在项目型组织里，人围绕项目分组，为项目经理工作，项目经理权力大。在矩阵型组织里，人同时向职能经理和项目经理汇报。弱矩阵偏向职能经理，强矩阵偏向项目经理，平衡矩阵则双方分权。

Structure Project manager's authority Who controls the people Functional low department heads Weak matrix low to medium department heads Balanced medium shared Strong matrix medium to high project manager Projectized high project manager

Step 2 · The project management office第 2 步 · 项目管理办公室

Learn先学

A project management office (PMO) is a group inside the organisation that supports project work. It keeps shared templates, tools and good practice, trains project managers, and often keeps the records of past projects.

项目管理办公室（PMO）是组织内部支持项目工作的一个团队。它保管共用的模板、工具和好做法，培训项目经理，通常还保存以往项目的记录。

PMOs differ in how much they control. A supportive PMO offers templates and advice. A controlling PMO requires teams to follow its standards and checks that they do. A directive PMO runs the projects itself and assigns the project managers.

不同的 PMO，管得多少不一样。支持型PMO 提供模板和建议。控制型PMO 要求团队遵守它的标准，并检查是否做到。指令型PMO 直接管理项目，并指派项目经理。

Step 3 · Using your authority and what exists第 3 步 · 用好你的权力和现有的资源

Learn先学

Know where you stand before you ask for help. In a functional or weak matrix setting, if you need a person who works for a department head, negotiate with that functional manager first, with the facts. If you cannot agree, take it to the sponsor (the person who backs and funds the project), not around the manager.

先弄清自己的位置，再去求助。在职能型或弱矩阵的环境里，如果你需要一位属于部门负责人的人手，先带着事实去和这位职能经理谈。谈不拢，就找发起人（支持并出资的人），而不是绕过经理。

Before you create something new, look at what the organisation already has: templates, policies, and records and lessons from earlier projects (often called organisational process assets). Reusing them saves time and keeps your work in line with the rules.

新做东西之前，先看看组织已经有什么：模板、政策，以及以往项目的记录和经验教训（常称组织过程资产）。重用它们既省时间，也让你的工作符合规定。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Functional, matrix and projectized differ in who controls the people.
• A weak matrix leans to the department head, a strong one to the project manager.
• Negotiate with the functional manager first; go to the sponsor if you cannot agree.
• A PMO can support, control or direct.
• Reuse existing templates and past records before starting from scratch.

• 职能型、矩阵型和项目型的区别在于谁掌控人员。
• 弱矩阵偏向部门负责人，强矩阵偏向项目经理。
• 先和职能经理协商；谈不拢再找发起人。
• PMO 可以是支持型、控制型或指令型。
• 从头开始之前，先重用现有的模板和以往记录。
