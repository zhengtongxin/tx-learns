Agile roles敏捷角色

Say who owns what in an agile team and whose job it is to remove a blocker · ~10 min · reading and practice说清敏捷团队里谁负责什么，以及谁来扫除障碍 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three roles第 1 步 · 三个角色

Learn先学

A Scrum-style team (Scrum is a common agile method that works in short fixed cycles called sprints) has three roles. The product owner decides what is worth building and in what order, and speaks for the business. The development team decides how to build it and does the work. The Scrum master helps the team follow the method and removes what blocks it.

类似 Scrum 的团队（Scrum 是一种常见的敏捷方法，按固定的短周期即冲刺来工作）有三个角色。产品负责人决定什么值得做、先做什么，代表业务方。开发团队决定怎么做，并完成工作。Scrum Master帮助团队遵循方法，并扫除阻碍。

The team is self-organising: nobody assigns tasks to its members. They pick the work themselves and share the responsibility. The finished, working piece of product it delivers each sprint is called the increment.

团队是自组织的：没人给成员分派任务，他们自己选择工作、共同负责。每个冲刺交付的、已完成可用的产品部分称为增量。

Product owner what and why - owns the product backlog, accepts the work Team how - owns the sprint backlog, builds the increment Scrum master the process - coaches, protects the team, removes impediments

Sort each one.

Step 2 · Two backlogs第 2 步 · 两个待办列表

Learn先学

The product backlog is the ordered list of everything wanted in the product. The product owner owns it and can reorder it at any time.

产品待办列表是产品所需全部内容的有序清单，由产品负责人负责，可随时调整顺序。

The sprint backlog is the set of items the team picked for this sprint, plus its plan to finish them. The team owns it. A manager or product owner does not add work to it mid-sprint; new requests go to the product backlog and wait for planning.

冲刺待办列表是团队为本次冲刺选出的条目，加上完成它们的计划，归团队所有。经理或产品负责人不能在冲刺中途往里塞活儿；新需求进产品待办列表，等下次计划会议再说。

Step 3 · Servant leadership第 3 步 · 服务型领导

Learn先学

A servant leader serves the team: removes obstacles, shields it from outside pressure, and helps people grow. They do not hand out tasks or demand status updates.

服务型领导为团队服务：扫除障碍，挡住外部压力，帮助成员成长。他们不派活，也不催着要进度。

An impediment is anything that slows the team, such as a missing test environment or a slow decision. The team raises it, for example in the daily stand-up (a short daily team meeting), and the Scrum master or project manager works to clear it. If the team can solve it alone, let them.

障碍是拖慢团队的任何事，比如缺少测试环境或决策太慢。团队在每日站会（团队每天开的简短会议）等场合提出，Scrum Master 或项目经理负责去清除。团队自己能解决的，就让他们自己解决。

Takeaways要点回顾

• The product owner decides what and in what order; the team decides how.
• The product backlog belongs to the product owner; the sprint backlog belongs to the team.
• Agile teams organise themselves; nobody assigns their tasks.
• A servant leader removes impediments and protects the team.
• New requests in a sprint go to the product backlog, not the sprint backlog.

• 产品负责人决定做什么和先后顺序，团队决定怎么做。
• 产品待办列表归产品负责人，冲刺待办列表归团队。
• 敏捷团队自组织，没人给他们分派任务。
• 服务型领导扫除障碍、保护团队。
• 冲刺中的新请求进产品待办列表，不进冲刺待办列表。
