Motivation and team stages激励与团队阶段

Name the stage a team is in, lead it the way that stage needs, and pick what really motivates people · ~10 min · reading and practice能说出团队处在哪个阶段、用适合这个阶段的方式带队，并选对真正能激励人的做法 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Five team stages第 1 步 · 团队的五个阶段

Learn先学

A new team does not work well on day one. It moves through five stages, and the leader changes style at each one.

新团队不会第一天就配合得好。它会经过五个阶段，领导方式也要跟着变。

Forming: people are polite and unsure, so you direct: explain the goal and the roles. Storming: people disagree about how to work, so you coach and help them settle it. Norming: they agree on ways of working, so you support. Performing: they run smoothly, so you delegate. Adjourning: the work ends and the team is released.

形成期：大家客气、还不清楚状况，你要指导：讲清目标和分工。震荡期：大家对怎么做事有分歧，你要辅导，帮他们把分歧谈开。规范期：大家就做事方式达成一致，你来支持。成熟期：团队运转顺畅，你可以授权。解散期：工作结束，团队解散。

Storming is normal, not a failure. And the stages can go backwards: when a member joins or leaves, expect the team to slip back toward forming for a while.

震荡是正常的，不是失败。阶段还会倒退：有人加入或离开时，团队往往会暂时退回形成期。

Forming polite, unsure -> direct Storming arguing about how -> coach Norming agreeing on the rules -> support Performing working on their own -> delegate Adjourning work done -> release, thank

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · What motivates people第 2 步 · 什么能激励人

Learn先学

Money and rules stop people being unhappy. They do not make people care. Three well-known ideas say this in different ways.

钱和制度只能让人不抱怨，并不能让人投入。三个常见的理论从不同角度讲了这件事。

Needs come in levels. People first need pay, safety and a sense of belonging. Only then do respect and growth motivate them.

需求有层次。人先要有收入、安全感和归属感，之后尊重和成长才会起激励作用。

Two kinds of factors. Hygiene factors (pay, working conditions, job security) prevent dissatisfaction. Motivators (achievement, recognition, responsibility, growth) create real motivation. Fixing hygiene alone gives you a team that is not unhappy, and not motivated either.

两类因素。保健因素（薪酬、工作条件、岗位稳定）只能防止不满。激励因素（成就、认可、责任、成长）才带来真正的动力。只解决保健因素，得到的是一个不抱怨、但也没干劲的团队。

Two views of people. A manager who thinks people avoid work controls them closely. A manager who thinks people want to do good work gives them trust and room. The exam favours the second view.

对人的两种看法。认为人天生逃避工作的经理会紧盯下属；认为人愿意把事做好的经理会给信任和空间。考试偏向后一种。

So in a question, choose recognition during the project, a chance to learn, or more responsibility. Ask the person what they value: a reward only works if they want it.

所以做题时，选项目进行中的认可、学习机会或更多责任。还要问本人看重什么：对方想要的奖励才有效。

Step 3 · Teams in different places第 3 步 · 分布在各地的团队

Learn先学

A team spread across cities or time zones loses the small talk that builds trust. You have to create it on purpose.

分布在不同城市或时区的团队，少了建立信任的日常闲聊。你得有意识地把它补回来。

Agree the basics together and write them into the team charter (the team's own working rules): shared hours when everyone is online, which tool is used for what, and how fast to reply.

一起约定基本规则，写进团队章程（团队自己定的工作规则）：大家都在线的共同时段、哪种工具做哪件事、多久内回复。

Prefer video for anything sensitive, share meeting times fairly so the same people are not always up late, and ask about cultural habits instead of assuming.

敏感的事尽量用视频沟通；会议时间轮流照顾各地，别总让同一批人熬夜；文化习惯要问，不要想当然。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Forming, storming, norming, performing, adjourning: direct, coach, support, delegate, release.
• Storming is normal, and a new member can send the team back a stage.
• Hygiene factors prevent complaints; motivators create drive.
• Recognise people during the project and ask what they value.
• Spread-out teams agree hours, tools and reply rules together.

• 形成、震荡、规范、成熟、解散：指导、辅导、支持、授权、解散。
• 震荡是正常的，新成员可能让团队退回前一阶段。
• 保健因素防止不满，激励因素带来动力。
• 在项目进行中就认可成员，并问清他们看重什么。
• 分布各地的团队要一起约定时段、工具和回复规则。
