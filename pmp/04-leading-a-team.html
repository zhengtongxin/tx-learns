Leading the team带领团队

Choose a leadership response that fits where the team is · ~10 min · reading and practice按团队所处的阶段选择合适的带领方式 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Stages of a team第 1 步 · 团队的阶段

Learn先学

Teams usually move through five stages, often called Tuckman's model: forming (polite, unsure), storming (disagreement over roles and methods), norming (agreed ways of working), performing (productive, self-driven) and adjourning (the team breaks up).

团队通常经历五个阶段，常称塔克曼模型：形成（客气但不确定）、震荡（在角色和方法上有分歧）、规范（形成共识的工作方式）、成熟（高效、自驱）和解散（团队结束）。

Your job changes with the stage. In forming, give direction. In storming, help the team work through the disagreement. In performing, step back and remove obstacles.

你的角色随阶段而变。形成期给方向；震荡期帮团队化解分歧；成熟期退后一步，扫清障碍。

Storming is normal, not a failure. Do not treat it as a reason to replace people.

震荡是正常现象，不是失败，不要把它当成换人的理由。

Forming -> direct, clarify goals and roles Storming -> facilitate, help resolve conflict Norming -> support, reinforce agreed norms Performing -> delegate, remove obstacles Adjourning -> recognise, capture lessons

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Servant leadership第 2 步 · 服务型领导

Learn先学

A servant leader serves the team: clears blockers, protects focus, coaches and shares decisions. This is the style agile expects, and it works well in predictive teams too.

服务型领导是为团队服务：清除阻碍、保护专注、辅导、共同决策。这是敏捷所期待的风格，在预测型团队里同样有效。

The default exam answer to a team problem is to involve the team: ask them, let them decide how to do the work, and take the obstacle on yourself. Telling people what to do suits an emergency, not the normal case.

对团队问题，考试里的默认答案是让团队参与：问他们、让他们决定怎么做，你来处理阻碍。直接下命令适合紧急情况，而不是常态。

Step 3 · Build and support第 3 步 · 培养与支持

Learn先学

Build the team: agree team rules (a team charter), set clear goals, recognise good work and develop skills. Recognition should match what people value, and be tied to behaviour you want repeated.

建设团队：共同制定团队规则（团队章程）、设定清晰目标、认可好的工作并培养技能。认可要符合人们看重的东西，并与你希望重复的行为挂钩。

Also watch for pressure on individuals: ask how people are coping, share the workload fairly and take their ideas seriously. A team that feels heard performs better.

还要留意个人承受的压力：问问大家状态如何、公平分配工作量、认真对待他们的想法。觉得自己被倾听的团队表现更好。

Keep people informed, give honest feedback early and in private, and keep the team in the loop on decisions that affect them.

让大家知情，尽早私下给出诚恳反馈，并让团队了解影响他们的决定。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Forming, storming, norming, performing, adjourning.
• Storming is normal; help the team work through it.
• Servant leaders remove blockers and share decisions.
• Default answer to a team problem: involve the team.
• A team charter sets shared roles and rules.

• 形成、震荡、规范、成熟、解散。
• 震荡是正常的，帮团队走过去。
• 服务型领导清除阻碍、共同决策。
• 团队问题的默认答案：让团队参与。
