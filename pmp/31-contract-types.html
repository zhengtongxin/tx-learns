Contract types合同类型

Pick the contract type that fits how clear the work is, and say who carries the cost risk · ~10 min · reading and practice能根据工作的明确程度选对合同类型，并说出成本风险由谁承担 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Who carries the risk第 1 步 · 风险由谁承担

Learn先学

Every contract type answers one question: if the work costs more than expected, who pays? Start there and the rest follows.

每种合同类型都在回答同一个问题：如果实际成本超出预期，谁来付？从这里入手，其余就都顺了。

Fixed price: one agreed price for a defined result. The seller carries the cost risk. Use it when the scope is clear and unlikely to change.

固定总价：为明确的成果约定一个价格。成本风险由卖方承担。适合范围清楚、不太会变的工作。

Cost-reimbursable: the buyer pays the seller's real costs plus a fee. The buyer carries the cost risk. Use it when the scope is unclear, as in research.

成本补偿：买方支付卖方的实际成本，再加一笔费用。成本风险由买方承担。适合范围不清楚的工作，比如研究类。

Time and materials: a rate per hour or per unit, with no fixed total. Use it for short work or extra hands when you cannot define the scope quickly. Add a not-to-exceed cap so the bill cannot grow without limit.

工料合同：按小时或按单位计价，不定总价。适合短期工作，或一时说不清范围、需要临时增加人手的情况。加上最高限额，账单才不会无限增长。

Fixed price seller pays scope clear Cost-reimbursable buyer pays scope unclear Time and materials buyer pays short or urgent, capped

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Fees and incentives第 2 步 · 费用与激励

Learn先学

Each family has variants. The name tells you how the seller earns the extra.

每一类合同下面还有变体。从名字就能看出卖方怎么拿到额外的钱。

Fixed price with incentive fee: the seller earns a bonus for beating a target, such as early delivery. Fixed price with price adjustment: for contracts lasting years, the price can move with inflation or exchange rates.

总价加激励费：卖方达到更好的目标（比如提前交付）就拿奖金。总价加经济价格调整：用于持续多年的合同，价格可以随通胀或汇率调整。

Cost plus fixed fee: costs plus a fee that does not change. Cost plus incentive fee: buyer and seller share savings or overruns by an agreed split. Cost plus award fee: the buyer decides the fee by its own judgement of performance.

成本加固定费：成本之外再付一笔不变的费用。成本加激励费：节约或超支的部分由买卖双方按约定比例分担。成本加奖励费：费用多少由买方根据对绩效的主观评价决定。

An incentive fee lines the seller's interest up with yours. That is why it appears as the answer when a question says the buyer wants the seller to care about cost or schedule.

激励费让卖方的利益和你的一致。所以当题目说买方希望卖方在意成本或进度时，答案往往是带激励费的合同。

Step 3 · Agile work and disputes第 3 步 · 敏捷工作与争议

Learn先学

Agile work changes often, so a single fixed scope fits badly. A common pattern is a master agreement for the fixed terms (rates, warranties, how to end the contract), with the changing work described in a lighter attachment and paid per delivered increment.

敏捷工作经常变，把范围一次写死并不合适。常见做法是用一份主协议写固定条款（费率、质保、如何终止合同），把会变的工作放在较轻的附件里，按交付的增量付款。

When buyer and seller disagree, the order matters. Read the contract first, then negotiate directly. If that fails, use mediation or arbitration (a neutral third party). Court comes last.

买卖双方有分歧时，顺序很重要。先看合同怎么写，再直接谈判。谈不拢，再用调解或仲裁（中立的第三方）。诉讼放在最后。

Changes to a contract follow the contract's own change procedure and are agreed in writing. A verbal promise from a seller's engineer changes nothing.

合同变更要按合同自己规定的变更程序走，并以书面形式确认。卖方工程师口头答应的事不算数。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Fixed price: seller carries cost risk, scope must be clear.
• Cost-reimbursable: buyer carries cost risk, used when scope is unclear.
• Time and materials: short or urgent work, always with a cap.
• An incentive fee makes the seller care about your cost or schedule.
• Disputes: contract, then negotiation, then a neutral third party, court last.

• 固定总价：卖方承担成本风险，范围必须清楚。
• 成本补偿：买方承担成本风险，用于范围不清楚时。
• 工料合同：短期或紧急工作，一定要设上限。
• 激励费让卖方在意你的成本或进度。
• 争议：先看合同，再谈判，然后找中立第三方，诉讼最后。
