Change control变更控制

Handle a change request in the right order, and know when agile skips the board · ~9 min · reading and practice能按正确顺序处理变更请求，并知道敏捷项目何时不走变更委员会 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The change flow第 1 步 · 变更流程

Learn先学

Once a baseline is approved, you do not simply act on a request to change it. A change goes through change control, so every change is judged and recorded.

基准批准之后，别人一提出变更，你不能直接动手。变更要经过变更控制，每个变更都要被评估和记录。

Anyone can raise a change request: a stakeholder, a team member, the customer. The key order for you as project manager: assess the impact first on scope, schedule, cost, quality and risk, then submit the request with that analysis. The change control board (CCB) approves or rejects it. Only then do you implement and update the plans.

任何人都可以提出变更请求：相关方、团队成员、客户。项目经理的关键顺序是：先评估影响（范围、进度、成本、质量、风险），再带着分析提交请求。由变更控制委员会（CCB）批准或驳回。之后才执行并更新计划。

Even a mandatory change, such as a new regulation, goes through change control. It will almost surely be approved, but it still has to be assessed and recorded.

即使是强制性变更，比如新法规，也要走变更控制。它几乎肯定会被批准，但仍要评估和记录。

1. Change is requested (anyone) 2. Assess the impact on scope, time, cost, quality, risk 3. Submit the change request with the analysis 4. Change control board decides 5. If approved: implement, update plans and baselines 6. Tell the stakeholders

Step 2 · Change, defect or corrective action第 2 步 · 变更、缺陷与纠正措施

Learn先学

Not every request is the same kind. Name it, because the route differs.

请求并不都一样。先判断类型，因为处理路径不同。

Change request: a customer or stakeholder wants something different from the baseline. Defect repair: the work does not meet the requirement, so you fix it back to spec. Corrective action: work has slipped from the plan, and you act to bring performance back in line with the plan.

变更请求：客户或相关方想要和基准不同的东西。缺陷修复：成果不符合要求，要改回规格。纠正措施：执行偏离了计划，你采取行动让绩效回到计划上。

Corrective actions and defect repairs still get documented, and any that would move a baseline need a change request too. A customer's wish is the plain case that always needs the full flow.

纠正措施和缺陷修复同样要记录，会改动基准的还要提交变更请求。客户新提的想法则是最典型、必须走完整流程的情况。

Wants something new or different -> change request Does not meet the agreed spec -> defect repair Behind plan, pull it back -> corrective action

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Change in agile第 3 步 · 敏捷中的变更

Learn先学

An agile project expects change. It does not run a formal change board for every item. The product owner owns the product backlog, adds new items, re-orders them and removes them. The team then takes the top items into the next iteration.

敏捷项目预期会有变化，不会为每个事项开正式的变更委员会。产品负责人拥有产品待办列表，可以新增、重排和删除条目，团队再把靠前的条目拿进下一次迭代。

So a new request in agile goes to the product owner, who decides its priority. Inside a running iteration the team owns the sprint backlog, and the scope of the iteration is not changed without the team, so new items normally wait for the next one.

所以敏捷里的新需求交给产品负责人，由他决定优先级。迭代进行中，迭代待办列表属于团队，没有团队同意不改迭代范围，新条目通常等下一次迭代。

Careful: this does not mean no control. The backlog is the control, and change is welcome, but it is still decided by value and capacity.

注意：这不等于没有控制。待办列表本身就是控制手段，欢迎变化，但仍按价值和产能来决定。

Product backlog -> product owner (add, re-order, remove) Sprint backlog -> the team (what it commits to this sprint) New request -> product owner, then next sprint

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Assess the impact first, then submit; the board decides; then you implement.
• Anyone can raise a change; mandatory changes still go through control.
• Wants something new is a change; breaks the spec is a defect; behind plan is a corrective action.
• Agile handles change through the backlog and the product owner, not a formal board.
• The sprint backlog belongs to the team; the product backlog belongs to the product owner.

• 先评估影响，再提交；委员会决定；然后执行。
• 任何人都能提变更；强制性变更也要走控制。
• 要新东西是变更；不符规格是缺陷；落后计划是纠正措施。
• 敏捷通过待办列表和产品负责人处理变更，而不是正式委员会。
• 迭代待办列表属于团队，产品待办列表属于产品负责人。
