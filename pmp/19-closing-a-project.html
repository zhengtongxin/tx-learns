Closing a project项目收尾

Close a project properly: accept, hand over, learn, release and archive · ~10 min · reading and practice规范地收尾：验收、移交、总结、释放资源并归档 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Accept and hand over第 1 步 · 验收与移交

Learn先学

Closing starts by validating the work against the objectives and success criteria agreed at the start. Then the customer or sponsor gives formal acceptance in writing. Without sign-off, the project is not truly done.

收尾首先要对照一开始约定的目标和成功标准验证工作成果。然后客户或发起人以书面形式正式验收。没有签字，项目就不算真正完成。

Next comes transition to operations: the people who will run the result get it, along with knowledge transfer such as training, manuals and support contacts. A structured handover avoids gaps on day one of operations.

接着是移交运营：由将要使用成果的人接手，并进行知识转移，比如培训、手册和支持联系人。结构化的移交可以避免运营第一天出现空档。

Check the acceptance criteria in the plan, not your own opinion of the work. If the customer finds a gap, fix it through the agreed process before you close.

验收要对照计划里的验收标准，而不是你自己对工作的看法。如果客户发现缺口，先按约定流程处理，再收尾。

Acceptance criteria decide whether one deliverable is accepted. Success criteria decide whether the project achieved its objectives and benefits. A deliverable can be accepted while the project still misses its success criteria, so check both before you close.

验收标准决定某个交付物是否被接受。成功标准决定项目是否达成了目标和效益。交付物可以被接受，而项目仍然没达到成功标准，所以收尾前两者都要核对。

1. validate against objectives and success criteria 2. formal acceptance and sign-off 3. transition to operations, knowledge transfer 4. close contracts, release resources 5. review lessons learned (captured all along), archive documents

Step 2 · Learn and tidy up第 2 步 · 总结与清理

Learn先学

Lessons learned are captured throughout the project, not only at the end. By the end people forget details, so record them as they happen and review them at closing.

经验教训要贯穿整个项目收集，而不只是在结尾。到结尾时人们已忘了细节，所以随时记录，收尾时再回顾。

Close the contracts with suppliers: confirm all work is delivered, payments are settled and any claims (requests for extra payment or compensation) are resolved. Release resources: return people to their managers and hand back equipment and spaces.

关闭与供应商的合同：确认所有工作已交付、款项已结清、索赔（要求额外付款或赔偿）已解决。释放资源：让人员回到原部门，归还设备和场地。

Archive documentation so the organisation can find it later: plans, decisions, the final report and lessons.

归档文档，让组织以后能找到：计划、决定、最终报告和经验教训。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Closing early第 3 步 · 提前终止

Learn先学

A project can be cancelled early because the business case (the reasons and expected benefits that justified the project) no longer holds or funding stops. It still needs closing, not just walking away.

项目可能因商业论证（立项时的理由与预期收益）不再成立或资金中止而提前终止。它仍然需要收尾，而不是直接走人。

Record what was done and its state, settle contracts, release resources, capture lessons and archive. Tell the stakeholders why it ended. Documenting the reason helps the organisation decide better next time.

记录已完成的工作及其状态，结清合同，释放资源，收集经验教训并归档。向相关方说明终止原因。记录原因能帮组织下次做出更好的决定。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Validate against objectives, then get formal acceptance.
• Hand over with knowledge transfer so operations can run it.
• Capture lessons learned throughout, not only at the end.
• Close contracts, release resources, archive documents.
• A cancelled project still gets closed properly.

• 对照目标验证，然后取得正式验收。
• 通过知识转移完成移交，让运营能接手。
• 经验教训要贯穿全程收集，而不是只在结尾。
• 关闭合同、释放资源、归档文档。
• 被取消的项目也要规范收尾。
