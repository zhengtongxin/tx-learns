Managing a supplier管理供应方

Monitor a supplier against the contract, pay milestones only when work is accepted, and handle changes and claims in writing · ~9 min · reading and practice对照合同监督供应方，只在工作验收后支付里程碑款项，并以书面方式处理变更与索赔 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Watching the supplier第 1 步 · 盯住供应方

Learn先学

Once a supplier is chosen, the work is to monitor delivery against the contract. Compare what is delivered with what was agreed, on a schedule you set in advance: reviews, inspections and, where the contract allows, an audit (a formal check) of how the supplier works. Keep a written record of each check, because the record is what you rely on if a dispute follows.

选定供应方之后，工作就是对照合同监督交付。把交付的东西与约定的东西比较，按事先定好的节奏进行：评审、检查，以及在合同允许时对供应方的工作方式做审计（正式检查）。每次检查都要有书面记录，因为一旦发生争议，你依靠的就是这些记录。

Deliverables are what the supplier hands over; acceptance criteria are the written tests that say when something counts as done. Example: a weekly call, a monthly review of deliverables against the acceptance criteria, and a log of every difference found.

交付物是供应方交出的东西；验收标准是写明何时算完成的书面检验。例子：每周一次电话会议，每月对照验收标准评审交付物，并记录发现的每一处差异。

Step 2 · Paying for what is accepted第 2 步 · 为验收的东西付款

Learn先学

Contracts often pay in milestones: parts of the price released when agreed work is accepted. Say the price is S$50,000, split 30%, 40% and 30%. The payments are 50,000 × 30 ÷ 100 = S$15,000, then S$20,000, then S$15,000, which add back to S$50,000. If milestone 2 is delivered with defects, you do not pay S$20,000 until it is fixed and accepted, and you tell the supplier in writing why.

合同常按里程碑付款：约定的工作被验收后，释放价款的一部分。假设价款是 S$50,000，按 30%、40%、30% 分成。付款分别是 50,000 × 30 ÷ 100 = S$15,000，然后 S$20,000，再 S$15,000，加起来仍是 S$50,000。如果里程碑 2 交付时有缺陷，在修好并验收之前你不付这 S$20,000，并且要书面告诉供应方原因。

Step 3 · When something goes wrong第 3 步 · 出了问题怎么办

Learn先学

Raise a difference early and in writing, so it can be fixed while it is small. Changes to the contract go through change control, never by a hallway promise. Change control means a written request, a check of cost, time and risk, then a recorded decision. A claim is a formal request or dispute about the contract, such as the supplier asking for more money or you asking for a remedy. Log every claim and handle it by the process the contract sets out, with the contract owner (the person responsible for the contract) involved; a remedy is what the contract gives you when something goes wrong, such as a fix or a price reduction. This lesson is general education, not legal advice.

有差异要尽早、以书面方式提出，这样在问题还小时就能解决。对合同的更改要走变更控制，绝不能靠走廊里的口头承诺。变更控制就是：书面提出请求，检查成本、时间和风险，然后记录决定。索赔是关于合同的正式请求或争议，例如供应方要求加钱，或你要求补救。每一项索赔都要登记，并按合同规定的流程处理，让合同负责人（对合同负责的人）参与；补救是出问题时合同赋予你的办法，例如返工或降价。本课是一般性学习内容，不是法律意见。

按发生的时间给每个动作分类。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Monitor delivery against the contract on a schedule you set in advance, and keep written records.
• S$50,000 at 30/40/30 pays S$15,000, S$20,000, S$15,000.
• Pay a milestone only after the work is accepted; tell the supplier in writing why if not.
• Contract changes go through change control, never a hallway promise.
• Log every claim and handle it through the contract owner; this is general education, not legal advice.

• 按事先定好的节奏对照合同监督交付，并保留书面记录。
• S$50,000 按 30/40/30 付款：S$15,000、S$20,000、S$15,000。
• 工作验收后才支付里程碑款项；不付时书面告诉供应方原因。
• 合同变更要走变更控制，绝不靠走廊里的口头承诺。
• 每项索赔都要登记并通过合同负责人处理；本课是一般性学习内容，不是法律意见。
