Conflict and negotiation冲突与谈判

Pick a way to handle a conflict and a way to negotiate for the situation in front of you · ~10 min · reading and practice针对眼前的情境选择处理冲突和谈判的方式 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Five ways to handle conflict第 1 步 · 五种处理冲突的方式

Learn先学

Conflict is normal on a project. The usual causes are limited resources, priorities, schedules and differing opinions on how to do the work. Handle it early and keep it about the problem, not the people.

项目里有冲突很正常。常见原因是资源有限、优先级、进度安排以及对做法的不同意见。要早处理，对事不对人。

Five approaches: collaborate / problem-solve (face the problem and find a solution both sides accept), compromise (each gives something up), smooth / accommodate (stress agreement, play down differences), force / direct (one view wins) and withdraw / avoid (postpone or step away).

五种方式：合作/解决问题（正视问题，找到双方都接受的方案）、妥协（各让一步）、缓和/迁就（强调共识、淡化分歧）、强制/命令（一方意见获胜）和回避/撤退（推迟或走开）。

Collaborating usually gives the best lasting result because it deals with the real cause. It is the usual best answer when time allows and the issue matters.

合作通常带来最持久的结果，因为它处理了真正的原因。时间允许、问题重要时，它通常是最佳答案。

Collaborate both needs met, real cause solved Compromise both give something, partial Smooth keep peace, problem remains Force quick, one side loses Withdraw delay, problem remains

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Go direct first第 2 步 · 先直接沟通

Learn先学

When two people are in conflict, the project manager's first move is usually to talk to them directly and help them work it out, not to take it to their manager. Escalate only if direct efforts fail or the issue is beyond your authority.

两人发生冲突时，项目经理通常首先应直接找他们谈，帮他们解决，而不是把事情交给他们的经理。只有直接努力无效或超出你的权限时才上报。

Listen to each side separately first, find the real need behind the position, then bring them together on shared goals. Keep it private and respectful.

先分别听每一方，找出立场背后的真实需求，再在共同目标上把他们聚到一起。保持私下和尊重。

Step 3 · Negotiating第 3 步 · 谈判

Learn先学

Negotiation works best when you prepare. Know your goal, your walk-away point and what the other side needs. Aim for outcomes both sides can live with.

谈判要事先准备。清楚自己的目标、底线，以及对方需要什么。目标是双方都能接受的结果。

Focus on interests, not fixed positions: ask why they want something. Separate the person from the problem, stay calm and record what is agreed.

关注利益而不是死守立场：问他们为什么想要这个。把人和问题分开，保持冷静，并记录达成的约定。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Five approaches: collaborate, compromise, smooth, force, withdraw.
• Collaborating is usually best when time allows and it matters.
• Talk to the people directly before escalating.
• Look for the interest behind the position.
• Prepare goal and walk-away point before negotiating.

• 五种方式：合作、妥协、缓和、强制、回避。
• 时间允许且问题重要时，合作通常最好。
• 上报之前先直接找当事人谈。
• 找立场背后的利益。
• 谈判前准备好目标和底线。
