Business and process drill商业与流程练习

Answer nine situations about value, compliance, risk, change, quality and contracts by following the right process in the right order · ~12 min · practice能在九个情境里，按正确的流程和顺序处理价值、合规、风险、变更、质量和合同 · 约 12 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · Value and rules first第 1 步 · 先看价值与规则

Learn先学

Before doing any work, check value. A project that is on plan but whose benefits have gone should be reported to the sponsor (the person who owns the funding and the result) and the business case (the reason the project is worth doing). Outside changes such as a new regulation (compliance means following the rules that apply, such as laws) are assessed first and then decided, never ignored or just added.

在做事之前，先看价值。按计划进行但效益已经消失的项目，要告诉发起人（对资金和成果负责的人），并回到商业论证（说明项目为什么值得做的依据）那里。新法规这类外部变化（合规就是遵守适用的规定，比如法律），要先评估再决定，不能无视，也不能随手加进去。

And a delivered product only creates value when people use it: find out what blocks them and adjust the support.

交付的产品只有被人用起来才产生价值：弄清什么阻碍了他们，并调整支持。

Step 2 · Risk, change and issue第 2 步 · 风险、变更与问题

Learn先学

Keep three things apart:

  • A risk has not happened yet. When it does, use the response plan you already agreed and update the risk register (the list of each risk, its owner and its response).
  • A change request gets an impact assessment, then the change control board (the group that approves or rejects changes) decides. The agreed scope, the baseline, is frozen: a change to it only happens through change control, and every change is compared with it. This is how a predictive project works, planning its scope up front.
  • An issue is already happening: log it, give it an owner and a date, and escalate (pass it up to someone with the authority to decide) when needed.

把三件事分清：

  • 风险还没有发生。一旦发生，就用事先商定好的应对计划，并更新风险登记册（记录每个风险、负责人和应对办法的清单）。
  • 变更请求要先评估影响，再由变更控制委员会（批准或驳回变更的小组）决定。已商定的范围，也就是基准，是冻结的：只能通过变更控制来改，每个变更都要和它比较。这是预测型项目的做法，一开始就规划好范围。
  • 问题已经在发生：登记、指定负责人和日期，必要时上报（交给有权决定的人）。

Step 3 · Prevent and follow the contract第 3 步 · 预防，并遵守合同

Learn先学

Quality is built by fixing the cause, not by adding more inspection. When a seller (the supplier you contract with) is late, read the contract and follow its procedure in writing before you take a stronger step. Capture lessons learned (what went well and what to do differently, written down) while there is still work left to improve.

质量靠消除原因来建立，而不是靠多加检查。供应商（与你签合同的一方）延迟时，先查阅合同，书面按其中的程序处理，再考虑更强硬的措施。趁还有工作可以改进时就总结经验教训（做得好的和以后要改的，写下来）。

In each case the better answer is the one that acts at the right moment, with the right record.

每种情况里，更好的答案都是在恰当的时机、留下恰当的记录去行动的那个。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Check value before doing the work: take lost benefits to the sponsor.
• Assess a change or outside rule first, then decide through the process.
• Risk not yet, issue now: use the plan, or give an owner and a date.
• Fix the cause for quality, read the contract for sellers.
• Capture lessons while they can still help.

• 先看价值再做事：效益没了要告诉发起人。
• 变更或外部规则先评估，再通过流程决定。
• 风险是还没发生，问题是正在发生：用计划，或指定负责人和日期。
• 质量要消除原因，供应商问题先看合同。
• 趁经验还有用就总结。
