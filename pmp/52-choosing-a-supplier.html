Choosing a supplier选择供应方

Compare make with buy by whole cost, ask every supplier the same way, and score offers with weights agreed in advance · ~9 min · reading and practice按总成本比较自制与外购，用同样方式询问每家供应方，并用事先商定的权重给报价打分 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Make or buy第 1 步 · 自己做还是外购

Learn先学

A make-or-buy decision compares doing the work yourself with buying it from a supplier. Compare the whole cost, not the headline price. Say making it costs S$40,000. Buying costs S$35,000 plus S$2,000 of your own time to oversee the supplier, so S$35,000 + S$2,000 = S$37,000. Buying is S$40,000 − S$37,000 = S$3,000 cheaper. Cost is not the only test: skills, control and risk count too.

自制或外购决策是把自己做与向供应方购买进行比较。要比较总成本，而不是标价。假设自己做要 S$40,000。外购是 S$35,000，再加上你自己监督供应方的 S$2,000，即 S$35,000 + S$2,000 = S$37,000。外购便宜 S$40,000 − S$37,000 = S$3,000。成本不是唯一标准：技能、控制权和风险也要考虑。

Step 2 · Asking for offers第 2 步 · 征集报价

Learn先学

To compare suppliers fairly, send each the same written description of the need and the same questions. An invitation to bid asks mainly for a price on a clear need. A request for proposal asks how the supplier would solve a problem, so you compare approaches as well as prices. Share each question and its answer with every supplier, so nobody has an advantage.

要公平地比较供应方，就向每家发送同样的书面需求说明和同样的问题。招标邀请主要针对明确的需求索要价格。建议书征集则询问供应方会如何解决问题，所以你既比较方法也比较价格。每个问题及其答复都要告知所有供应方，这样没有人占便宜。

Step 3 · Scoring the offers第 3 步 · 给报价打分

Learn先学

Agree the criteria and weights before you open any offer. Say price 40%, quality 35% and schedule 25%; 40% means multiply by 0.40 (40 ÷ 100). Supplier A scores 90, 70 and 80, so 0.40 × 90 + 0.35 × 70 + 0.25 × 80 = 36 + 24.5 + 20 = 80.5. Supplier B scores 70, 90 and 80, so 28 + 31.5 + 20 = 79.5. A wins by 1 point even though B has the best quality, because the weights say price matters most. Then you pick the top score and negotiate the terms with that supplier.

在打开任何报价之前，先商定标准和权重。假设价格 40%、质量 35%、进度 25%；40% 就是乘以 0.40（40 ÷ 100）。供应方 A 得分 90、70、80，所以 0.40 × 90 + 0.35 × 70 + 0.25 × 80 = 36 + 24.5 + 20 = 80.5。供应方 B 得分 70、90、80，所以 28 + 31.5 + 20 = 79.5。A 以 1 分取胜，尽管 B 的质量最好，因为权重表明价格最重要。选出得分最高的供应方后，再与它谈判具体条款。

Sort each step into before or after the offers arrive.

把每一步分到“收到报价之前”或“收到报价之后”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Compare make with buy by whole cost: price plus your own oversight.
• Buying at 35,000 plus 2,000 beats making at 40,000 by 3,000, before skills and risk.
• Send every supplier the same need and share every answer with all.
• Agree criteria and weights before opening any offer.
• Weighted score = sum of each score times its weight.

• 按总成本比较自制与外购：价格加上你自己的监督成本。
• 外购 35,000 加 2,000，比自制 40,000 便宜 3,000，这还没算技能和风险。
• 向每家供应方发送同样的需求，并把每个答复告诉所有人。
• 在打开任何报价之前商定标准和权重。
• 加权得分 = 每项得分乘以各自的权重，再把结果相加。
