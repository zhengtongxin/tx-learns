Quality in depth质量详解

Tell assurance from control, count the cost of quality and find a root cause · ~10 min · reading and practice分清质量保证与质量控制，计算质量成本，并找出根本原因 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Assurance and control第 1 步 · 保证与控制

Learn先学

Quality assurance looks at the process: are we following the agreed way of working, and is that way good enough? An audit is a typical tool. Quality control looks at the product: does this deliverable meet the requirement? Inspection and testing are typical tools.

质量保证看的是过程：我们有没有按约定的方式工作，这种方式够不够好？审计是典型工具。质量控制看的是产品：这个交付物满足要求吗？检查和测试是典型工具。

A quick test: assurance asks “are we building it right?”, control asks “did we build it to the requirement?”.

一个简单的办法：保证问“我们做的方式对吗”，控制问“做出来的东西符合要求吗”。

Step 2 · The cost of quality第 2 步 · 质量成本

Learn先学

Quality costs money both ways. Costs of conformance are what you spend to get it right: prevention (training, good design) and appraisal (inspection, testing). Costs of non-conformance are the price of getting it wrong: internal failure (rework found before delivery) and external failure (defects found by the customer, warranty, lost trust).

质量两头都要花钱。符合成本是为做对而花的：预防（培训、好的设计）和评估（检查、测试）。不符合成本是做错的代价：内部失败（交付前发现的返工）和外部失败（客户发现的缺陷、保修、失去信任）。

The later a defect is found, the more it costs to fix, so external failure is the dearest and prevention the cheapest.

Sort each cost.

缺陷发现得越晚，修复越贵，所以外部失败最贵，预防最便宜。

给每项成本分类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Find the root cause第 3 步 · 找出根本原因

Learn先学

Fixing a symptom brings the defect back. A root cause analysis keeps asking why until you reach something you can change. The five whys is the simplest form, and a fishbone diagram groups possible causes by category such as people, method and material.

只修症状，缺陷还会回来。根本原因分析不断追问为什么，直到找到一个你能改变的原因。五个为什么是最简单的形式，鱼骨图则把可能的原因按人、方法、材料等类别归拢。

Defect: 12 forms were submitted with no phone number Why 1? The phone field accepted empty input Why 2? Nobody marked it required Why 3? The requirement said "contact details" only Why 4? The requirement was never reviewed with users Root cause: no user review step for requirements Fix: add a review step, not only fix the field

Takeaways要点回顾

• Assurance checks the process; control checks the product.
• Conformance costs are prevention and appraisal; non-conformance costs are internal and external failure.
• Prevention is generally the cheapest, external failure the dearest.
• Keep asking why until you find a cause you can change.

• 保证检查过程；控制检查产品。
• 符合成本是预防和评估；不符合成本是内部和外部失败。
• 预防通常最便宜，外部失败最贵。
• 不断问为什么，直到找到能改变的原因。
