Starting a project启动项目

Tell the charter, business case and project plan apart, and know what must exist before detailed planning · ~10 min · reading and practice分清项目章程、商业论证和项目计划，并知道详细规划之前必须有什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The charter authorises the project第 1 步 · 章程批准项目

Learn先学

The project charter formally authorises the project and gives the project manager authority to use resources. It names the project manager, the sponsor, the objectives and the high-level scope, schedule and budget. The sponsor, or someone above the project manager, issues it.

项目章程正式批准项目，并授权项目经理动用资源。它写明项目经理、发起人、目标，以及高层级的范围、进度和预算。章程由发起人或项目经理之上的人签发。

Without a charter there is no formal project yet, so detailed planning waits. If a question says work has started with no charter, the next step is to get one.

没有章程，就还没有正式的项目，所以详细规划要等。如果题目说工作已经开始却没有章程，下一步就是去拿到一份。

Project: online booking for the clinic Sponsor: Dr Tan Project manager: Mei Ling Objective: patients book without phoning High-level scope: web booking, reminders Budget ceiling: S$80,000 Target: end of Q3 Signed by sponsor: yes

Step 2 · Business case and plan are different第 2 步 · 商业论证与计划不同

Learn先学

The business case explains why the investment is worthwhile: the need, the options and the expected benefits. It comes before the charter. The project management plan explains how the work will be done, and it comes after the charter.

商业论证说明为什么这笔投资值得：需求、备选方案和预期效益。它在章程之前。项目管理计划说明工作怎么做，它在章程之后。

Match each statement to its document.

把每句话对应到它的文件。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Log early, then plan第 3 步 · 先记录，再规划

Learn先学

At the start, also identify stakeholders (everyone affected by the project or able to affect it) and keep an assumption log and a list of constraints. An assumption is something you take as true without proof, so it carries risk and must be checked later. A constraint is a limit you must work within, such as a fixed budget or a hard date.

启动时还要识别相关方（受项目影响或能影响项目的所有人），建立假设日志和约束清单。假设是你没有证据却当作真的事，所以带有风险，之后必须核实。约束是你必须在其中工作的限制，比如固定预算或死线。

Takeaways要点回顾

• The charter authorises the project and the project manager.
• The business case says why; the plan says how.
• No charter means no formal project: get one before detailed planning.
• Log assumptions and constraints at the start and check the assumptions.

• 章程批准项目，并授权项目经理。
• 商业论证说为什么；计划说怎么做。
• 没有章程就没有正式项目：详细规划之前先拿到。
• 启动时记录假设和约束，并核实假设。
