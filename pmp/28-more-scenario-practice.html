More scenario practice更多情境练习

Nine more mixed exam-style scenarios, with the lesson to revisit for each miss · ~15 min · practice再来九道混合的考试风格情境题，答错时知道该回看哪一课 · 约 15 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · People questions第 1 步 · 人员类题目

Learn先学

Understand the person’s real situation first, lead experts by serving them, and bring a late stakeholder in rather than around. Missed one? Revisit “Leading the team”, “Stakeholders” or “Team and resources”.

先弄清当事人的真实情况，用服务来领导专家，把后来的相关方请进来而不是绕开。答错了？回看“带领团队”“相关方”或“团队与资源”。

Step 2 · Process questions第 2 步 · 过程类题目

Learn先学

Assess the impact before reacting, read the index against 1, and fix the cause of repeated defects. Missed one? Revisit “Schedule and dependencies”, “Cost and earned value” or “Quality in depth”.

先评估影响再反应，把指数和 1 比较，并修正反复缺陷的原因。答错了？回看“进度与依赖”“成本与挣值”或“质量详解”。

Step 3 · Agile, benefits and closing第 3 步 · 敏捷、收益与收尾

Learn先学

Protect the sprint scope, check that the result is used and not just delivered, and finish closing before the team leaves. Missed one? Revisit “Sprints and events”, “Value and benefits” or “Closing a project”.

保护冲刺范围，检查成果是否被使用而不仅是交付，并在团队离开前完成收尾。答错了？回看“冲刺与会议”“价值与效益”或“项目收尾”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Talk with the people involved before acting.
• Assess the impact of a delay before choosing a response.
• Repeated defects need a root cause, not more inspection.
• Delivery is not benefit: plan for adoption.
• Every miss points to a lesson to revisit.

• 行动之前先和当事人沟通。
• 选择应对之前先评估延误的影响。
• 反复出现的缺陷要找根本原因，而不是多检查。
• 交付不等于收益：要为使用落地做计划。
• 每一次答错都指向一课可以回看。
