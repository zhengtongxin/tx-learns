Practice · Lesson 2练习 · 第 2 课

More scenario practice更多情境练习

Nine more mixed exam-style scenarios, with the lesson to revisit for each miss · ~15 min · practice再来九道混合的考试风格情境题，答错时知道该回看哪一课 · 约 15 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · People questions第 1 步 · 人员类题目

Learn先学

Understand the person’s real situation first, lead experts by serving them, and bring a late stakeholder in rather than around. Missed one? Revisit “Leading the team”, “Stakeholders” or “Team and resources”. Two terms the questions use: the sponsor is the person who funds and backs the project, and the stakeholder register is the list of people affected by it, with what each needs.

先弄清当事人的真实情况，用服务来领导专家，把后来的相关方请进来而不是绕开。答错了？回看“带领团队”“相关方”或“团队与资源”。题目里有两个词：发起人是出资并支持项目的人，相关方登记册是列出受项目影响的人及其需求的清单。

Step 2 · Process questions第 2 步 · 过程类题目

Learn先学

Assess the impact before reacting, read the performance index against 1, and fix the cause of repeated defects. Missed one? Revisit “Schedule and dependencies”, “Cost and earned value” or “Quality in depth”.

先评估影响再反应，把绩效指数和 1 比较，并修正反复缺陷的原因。答错了？回看“进度与依赖”“成本与挣值”或“质量详解”。

Three amounts: EV (earned value) is the value of the work actually done, PV (planned value) is the value planned to be done by now, and AC (actual cost) is the money actually spent.

三个数：EV（挣值）是实际完成工作的价值，PV（计划价值）是到目前为止计划完成的价值，AC（实际成本）是实际花掉的钱。

Two indexes: SPI = EV ÷ PV, and below 1 means behind schedule. CPI = EV ÷ AC, and below 1 means over budget. At exactly 1 you are on plan.

两个指数：SPI = EV ÷ PV，小于 1 表示进度落后。CPI = EV ÷ AC，小于 1 表示成本超支。正好等于 1 就是符合计划。

Shortening a schedule: the critical path is the longest chain of dependent tasks, so a slip on it moves the finish date. Fast tracking overlaps tasks, and crashing adds resources.

缩短进度：关键路径是相互依赖的任务中最长的一条，所以它上面的延误会推迟完工日期。快速跟进是让任务重叠，赶工是加资源。

Step 3 · Agile, benefits and closing第 3 步 · 敏捷、收益与收尾

Learn先学

Protect the sprint scope (a sprint is a short fixed period, often two weeks, in which the team builds a small working piece; the product owner decides the order of the backlog, the list of wanted work), check that the result is used and not just delivered, and finish closing before the team leaves. Missed one? Revisit “Sprints and events”, “Value and benefits” or “Closing a project”.

保护冲刺范围（冲刺是团队在一段固定的短时间内，通常两周，做出一小块可用成果；产品负责人决定待办列表，也就是想要的工作清单的先后顺序），检查成果是否被使用而不仅是交付，并在团队离开前完成收尾。答错了？回看“冲刺与会议”“价值与效益”或“项目收尾”。

Step 4 · Find your pattern第 4 步 · 找出你的规律

Learn先学

After the nine questions, tally your result for each step as right out of three, and turn it into a percentage (right ÷ 3 × 100). The step with the lowest percentage is the kind of question you misread most, so revisit its lessons first. Compare steps by percentage, not by feel: a step where you missed two of three is weaker than one where you missed one of three.

做完九道题后，把每一步的结果记成“三题中对几题”，再换算成百分比（答对 ÷ 3 × 100）。百分比最低的那一步，就是你最常读错的题型，先回看它对应的课。比较各步要看百分比，不要凭感觉：三题错两题的那一步，比三题错一题的更弱。

Step 1 People 2 of 3 right = 67% Step 2 Process 1 of 3 right = 33% <- revisit first Step 3 Agile 2 of 3 right = 67% Total 5 of 9 right = 56%

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Talk with the people involved before acting.
• Assess the impact of a delay before choosing a response.
• Repeated defects need a root cause, not more inspection.
• Delivery is not benefit: plan for adoption.
• Every miss points to a lesson to revisit; compare steps by percentage and start with the lowest.

• 行动之前先和当事人沟通。
• 选择应对之前先评估延误的影响。
• 反复出现的缺陷要找根本原因，而不是多检查。
• 交付不等于收益：要为使用落地做计划。
• 每一次答错都指向一课可以回看；按百分比比较各步，先回看最低的。
