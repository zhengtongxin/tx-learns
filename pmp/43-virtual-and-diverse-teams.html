Virtual and diverse teams远程与多元团队

Lead a team spread across places and backgrounds: agree how you work, include everyone and build trust · ~10 min · reading and practice带领分处各地、背景各异的团队：约定工作方式、让每个人参与，并建立信任 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Teams spread across places第 1 步 · 分处各地的团队

Learn先学

A virtual team is a team whose members work in different places and often different time zones, and meet through tools such as video calls and chat. It brings a wider pool of people. It also brings risks: less casual contact, misunderstandings in writing, delays across time zones (the local clock differs by hours, so a call that suits one office can be night for another), and people who feel left out.

虚拟团队是成员分处不同地点、往往还在不同时区，靠视频通话和聊天等工具见面的团队。它能让你用到更广的人才，但也带来风险：随意的交流变少、文字容易产生误会、跨时区造成延迟（各地时钟相差数小时，对一处合适的通话时间，对另一处可能是深夜），还有人会觉得被冷落。

The first move is a team working agreement: a short set of rules the team writes together. It covers core hours when everyone is reachable, how fast people reply, which tool is used for what, and how decisions are made. Because the team wrote it, they keep to it.

第一步是订一份团队工作约定：团队一起写的一套简短规则，包括大家都能联系上的核心时段、回复的速度、什么事用什么工具，以及如何做决定。因为是团队自己写的，大家才会遵守。

Core hours one shared hour for all three sites (a site is an office) Replies within one working day; urgent means a call Tools chat for quick questions, video for sensitive topics Meeting times rotate, so no site always meets late at night Decisions written down in the shared notes

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Teams with many backgrounds第 2 步 · 背景多元的团队

Learn先学

A diverse team has people of different cultures, languages, ages, experience and working styles. More viewpoints usually mean better ideas and fewer things the team misses. The project manager's job is to make sure everyone can contribute.

多元团队里有不同文化、语言、年龄、经验和工作风格的人。观点更多，通常意味着想法更好、被忽略的东西更少。项目经理的任务，是确保每个人都能参与贡献。

Speak plainly and avoid slang and jokes that people from other places may not understand. Check understanding instead of assuming it, for example by asking, "What will you do next?" Do not read silence as agreement: some people find it hard to speak up in a group, or come from a culture where disagreeing with a senior person is not done. Ask in other ways too, such as one-to-one or in writing. Respect local holidays and customs when you plan.

说话要平实，避免其他地方的人可能听不懂的俚语和玩笑。要确认对方是否听懂，而不是想当然，比如问一句：“你接下来打算怎么做？”不要把沉默当作同意：有人在群体里不容易开口，也有人所在的文化里不习惯反驳上级。可以换别的方式去问，比如一对一或书面。做计划时也要尊重当地的节假日和习俗。

Step 3 · Trust at a distance第 3 步 · 隔着距离建立信任

Learn先学

Trust grows from reliability: do what you said, reply when you said you would, and recognise people's work in front of others. Start with a kick-off (a first meeting to start the project) where people get to know each other, and keep a few minutes of small talk before the agenda in regular meetings.

信任来自可靠：说到做到，答应何时回复就何时回复，并在大家面前肯定别人的工作。可以先开一次让大家互相认识的启动会（项目开始时的第一次会议），平时的会议里也在议程开始前留几分钟闲聊。

When a message in chat starts to cause friction, move to a richer channel. A channel is a way of talking; a richer one, such as a call or video, carries more tone. Tone (the feeling behind the words) is easy to misread in writing. Then review the working agreement from time to time and change it as the team learns.

当聊天里的一条消息开始引起摩擦，就换到信息更丰富的渠道。渠道就是沟通的方式；更丰富的渠道，比如通话或视频，能传达更多语气。文字里的语气（话语背后的感觉）很容易被误读。之后定期回顾工作约定，并随团队的成长而调整。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A virtual team needs a working agreement: core hours, reply times, tools and decisions.
• Share inconvenient meeting times fairly, and write decisions down.
• Diversity brings more viewpoints; plain words and checking understanding help.
• Silence is not agreement; ask in other ways too.
• Trust grows from reliability and recognition.
• Use a richer channel when tone is at risk.

• 虚拟团队需要工作约定：核心时段、回复时间、工具和决策方式。
• 不方便的会议时间要公平分担，决定要写下来。
• 多元带来更多观点；平实的措辞和确认理解会有帮助。
• 沉默不等于同意；也要用别的方式去问。
• 信任来自可靠和认可。
• 语气容易被误读时，换更丰富的渠道。
