Outside changes外部环境变化

Respond in the right order when the world outside the project shifts, and use AI and sustainability thinking responsibly · ~10 min · reading and practice当项目之外的环境发生变化时，能按正确的顺序应对，并负责任地运用 AI 和可持续性思维 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Watch, assess, then adjust第 1 步 · 先观察、评估，再调整

Learn先学

A project lives inside a business, and the business lives inside a market. A change out there can make a good plan pointless: a new law, a competitor's launch, a supplier closing, a budget cut.

项目存在于企业之中，企业存在于市场之中。外面的一个变化就可能让好好的计划失去意义：新法规、对手发布新品、供应商倒闭、预算削减。

A simple list keeps you looking in every direction: political and legal, economic, social, technological, environmental, and competitors.

用一张简单的清单提醒自己看全各个方向：政治与法律、经济、社会、技术、环境，以及竞争对手。

When something shifts, the order is always the same. Assess what it does to the business case, the scope and the schedule. Discuss it with the sponsor or the product owner. Adjust through the proper route: change control on a predictive project, a reordered backlog on an agile one.

一旦有变化，顺序总是一样的。先评估它对商业论证、范围和进度的影响；再和发起人或产品负责人讨论；然后通过正规途径调整：预测型项目走变更控制，敏捷项目重排待办列表。

Two answers are nearly always wrong: carrying on as if nothing happened, and changing the scope yourself without anyone agreeing.

有两种答案几乎总是错的：装作什么都没发生、照常推进；以及没经任何人同意就自己改范围。

1 Assess what does it do to value, scope, time, cost? 2 Discuss sponsor or product owner, with the facts 3 Adjust change control, or reprioritise the backlog 4 Tell the team and the stakeholders it affects

Step 2 · Using AI on a project第 2 步 · 在项目中使用 AI

Learn先学

AI tools can draft a status report, summarise meeting notes, suggest risks from past projects or forecast a finish date. They speed up the routine work.

AI 工具可以起草状态报告、总结会议记录、根据以往项目提示风险，或预测完工日期。它们让例行工作更快。

Three rules hold whatever the tool. You stay accountable: an AI suggestion is input, and the decision is yours. Check before you use: the output can be wrong or biased, so verify it against real data. Protect the data: never put confidential or personal information into a tool your organisation has not approved.

不管用什么工具，三条规则都不变。责任在你：AI 的建议只是参考，决定由你来做。先核实再用：输出可能有错或带偏见，要对照真实数据核查。保护数据：不要把机密或个人信息放进组织没有批准的工具。

Be open about it. If a forecast or a report came from an AI tool, say so, and follow your organisation's policy on where AI may be used.

要公开透明。预测或报告如果出自 AI 工具，就说明这一点，并遵守组织关于 AI 使用范围的政策。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Sustainability第 3 步 · 可持续性

Learn先学

Sustainability means judging a project by its long-term effect on people and the environment as well as on profit. It is now part of how value is measured, not an extra.

可持续性是指评判项目时，除了利润，还要看它对人和环境的长期影响。它现在是衡量价值的一部分，而不是额外的东西。

In practice it shows up as ordinary project work: a requirement to cut energy use, a supplier chosen partly for its labour record, a design that can be repaired instead of thrown away.

落到实处，它就是普通的项目工作：一条降低能耗的需求、选供应商时把用工记录也纳入考虑、一种能维修而不是用完就扔的设计。

When a cheaper option harms people or the environment, do not decide alone and do not hide it. Lay out the trade-off, check it against the organisation's policies, and take it to the sponsor.

当更便宜的方案会损害人或环境时，不要自己决定，也不要隐瞒。把利弊摆出来，对照组织的政策，再提交给发起人。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Scan outside: legal, economic, social, technical, environmental, competitors.
• When something shifts: assess, discuss with the sponsor or product owner, then adjust through the proper route.
• With AI you stay accountable: check the output and protect the data.
• Sustainability is part of value: people and environment as well as profit.
• A saving never overrides a policy; raise the trade-off.

• 向外看：法律、经济、社会、技术、环境、竞争对手。
• 有变化时：评估，与发起人或产品负责人讨论，再通过正规途径调整。
• 用 AI 时责任仍在你：核实输出，保护数据。
• 可持续性是价值的一部分：除了利润，还有人和环境。
• 省钱不能凌驾于政策之上；把利弊提出来。
