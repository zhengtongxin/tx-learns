Backlog and user stories待办列表与用户故事

Write a need as a user story, say when it is done and order the backlog · ~10 min · reading and practice把需求写成用户故事，说清什么算完成，并给待办列表排序 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Write the need as a story第 1 步 · 把需求写成故事

Learn先学

A user story is one sentence that states a need from the user’s side: who wants what, and why. The “why” matters most. It carries the value, so the team can find a cheaper way to deliver the same value.

用户故事是从用户角度写的一句话：谁想要什么，为什么。“为什么”最重要，它承载价值，团队才能找到更省的办法来交付同样的价值。

Keep a story small enough to finish in one sprint. A story that is too big is called an epic and gets split before the team takes it on.

故事要小到一个冲刺能做完。太大的故事叫史诗，团队接手之前要先拆开。

As a gym member, I want to cancel a class booking on my phone, so that I do not pay the late fee. Acceptance criteria: - Cancelling more than 2 hours ahead frees the slot and refunds the credit. - Cancelling later shows the fee before I confirm.

Step 2 · Say when it is done第 2 步 · 说清什么算完成

Learn先学

Acceptance criteria belong to one story: the specific checks the product owner uses to accept it. The definition of done belongs to the whole team: the standard every item must meet, such as coded, tested, reviewed and documented.

验收标准属于某一个故事：产品负责人用来验收它的具体检查。完成的定义属于整个团队：每个条目都必须达到的标准，比如已编码、已测试、已评审、已写文档。

An item is done only when it meets both and works. “Ninety per cent done” counts as not done: it goes back to the backlog and earns no credit this sprint.

一个条目只有两者都满足并且能用，才算完成。“做了九成”等于没完成：它回到待办列表，本次冲刺不计功劳。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Order the backlog第 3 步 · 给待办列表排序

Learn先学

The product owner orders by value, risk and dependency, not by who asks loudest. The top items are small and clear; lower ones can stay rough. Refinement is the steady work of splitting, clarifying and sizing the items coming next.

产品负责人按价值、风险和依赖来排序，而不是谁喊得响就先做谁。排在前面的条目小而清楚，靠后的可以先粗一点。梳理是持续进行的工作：把接下来要做的条目拆分、澄清、估大小。

A minimum viable product (MVP) is the smallest version that real users can use and learn from. It is not a bad version, and it is not a demo: it solves one real need end to end.

最小可行产品（MVP）是真实用户能用、也能让你从中学到东西的最小版本。它不是劣质版，也不是演示品：它把一个真实需求从头到尾解决。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A user story names who wants what and why; the why carries the value.
• Acceptance criteria belong to one story; the definition of done belongs to the team.
• Done means done: nearly done counts as not done.
• The product owner orders the backlog by value, risk and dependency.
• Split items that do not fit a sprint; an MVP is the smallest version real users can use.

• 用户故事写明谁想要什么、为什么；“为什么”承载价值。
• 验收标准属于单个故事；完成的定义属于整个团队。
• 完成就是完成：差不多完成算没完成。
• 产品负责人按价值、风险和依赖给待办列表排序。
• 装不进冲刺的条目要拆分；MVP 是真实用户能用的最小版本。
