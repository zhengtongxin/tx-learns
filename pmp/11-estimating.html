Estimating估算

Choose the right estimating method for a situation and work out a three-point estimate · ~12 min · reading and practice根据情形选对估算方法，并算出三点估算 · 约 12 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three base methods第 1 步 · 三种基本方法

Learn先学

Analogous estimating uses a similar past project as the starting point. It is quick and rough, best early on when details are few.

类比估算以相似的过往项目为起点。快但粗略，适合早期细节不多的时候。

Parametric estimating multiplies a measured unit rate by a quantity: 120 square metres at $50 per square metre is $6,000. It is only as good as the data behind the rate.

参数估算用已测得的单位比率乘以数量：120 平方米、每平方米 50 元，就是 6,000 元。它的可靠程度取决于比率背后的数据。

Bottom-up estimating adds up estimates of the smallest pieces from the WBS. It is the most accurate and the slowest.

自下而上估算把 WBS 最小单元的估算加总。最准确，也最耗时。

Step 2 · Three-point estimates第 2 步 · 三点估算

Learn先学

Uncertainty shrinks if you ask for three numbers: optimistic (O), most likely (M) and pessimistic (P). The weighted (PERT) average gives the likely case four times the weight; the plain triangular average weights all three equally.

如果让人给出三个数字，不确定性就能降下来：乐观（O）、最可能（M）、悲观（P）。加权（PERT）平均给最可能值四倍的权重；简单三角分布平均则三者权重相同。

The spread (P - O) / 6 is the standard deviation: how wide the doubt is. A wide spread means a risky activity.

(P - O) / 6 是标准差，表示不确定有多宽。范围越宽，活动风险越大。

PERT E = (O + 4M + P) / 6 triangular E = (O + M + P) / 3 spread SD = (P - O) / 6 O 4 M 6 P 14 days PERT = (4 + 24 + 14) / 6 = 7 days SD = (14 - 4) / 6 = 1.67

Step 3 · Monte Carlo and value engineering第 3 步 · 蒙特卡洛与价值工程

Learn先学

Monte Carlo analysis runs the schedule or cost model thousands of times, each time drawing random values from each activity's range. The result is a spread of outcomes, such as 'an 80% chance of finishing by 14 June'. It shows how likely a result is.

蒙特卡洛分析把进度或成本模型跑成千上万次，每次从各活动的范围里随机取值。结果是一系列可能的结果，比如“6 月 14 日前完成的概率是 80%”。它告诉你某个结果有多大可能。

Value engineering is a different idea: keep the same function and look for a cheaper way to deliver it, such as a lighter material that does the same job. It is about reducing cost, not predicting outcomes.

价值工程是另一回事：功能不变，寻找更便宜的实现方式，比如用更轻的材料完成同样的作用。它是降低成本，而不是预测结果。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Analogous is fast and rough; parametric is rate times quantity; bottom-up is slow and accurate.
• PERT estimate is (O + 4M + P) / 6; the spread is (P - O) / 6.
• Read which formula the question names: PERT or the simple average.
• Monte Carlo simulates thousands of outcomes to give probabilities.
• Value engineering keeps the function and lowers the cost.

• 类比快而粗，参数是比率乘数量，自下而上慢但准。
• PERT 估算是 (O + 4M + P) / 6，范围是 (P - O) / 6。
• 看清题目指定的是 PERT 还是简单平均。
• 蒙特卡洛通过成千上万次模拟给出概率。
• 价值工程保持功能、降低成本。
