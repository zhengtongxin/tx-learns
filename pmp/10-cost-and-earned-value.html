Cost and earned value成本与挣值

Use six numbers to say whether a project is on budget and on schedule · ~10 min · reading and practice用六个数字判断项目有没有超支、有没有延期 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three inputs第 1 步 · 三个输入

Learn先学

Earned value compares three amounts at one date. PV (planned value) is the work you planned to be done by now, in money. EV (earned value) is the work actually done, valued at the original budget. AC (actual cost) is what you really spent.

挣值在同一个时间点比较三个金额。PV（计划价值）是到现在为止计划完成的工作折成的钱。EV（挣值）是实际完成的工作，按原预算折成的钱。AC（实际成本）是你真正花掉的钱。

BAC is the total budget. If the budget is $200,000 and the work is 40% done, EV is $80,000, whatever you spent.

BAC 是总预算。预算 20 万、工作完成了 40%，EV 就是 8 万，不管你花了多少。

Step 2 · Variances and indexes第 2 步 · 偏差与指数

Learn先学

Subtract to get variances in money, divide to get indexes as ratios. For both, a positive or above-1 result is good news.

相减得到以金额表示的偏差，相除得到以比值表示的指数。两种结果里，为正或大于 1 都是好消息。

Cost uses AC, schedule uses PV. A memory aid: the cost formulas end in AC, the schedule formulas end in PV.

成本用 AC，进度用 PV。记忆法：成本公式都以 AC 结尾，进度公式都以 PV 结尾。

CV = EV - AC cost variance (negative = over budget) SV = EV - PV schedule variance (negative = behind) CPI = EV / AC cost index (below 1 = over budget) SPI = EV / PV schedule index (below 1 = behind) Example: EV 80,000 AC 100,000 PV 100,000 CV = -20,000 SV = -20,000 CPI = 0.8 SPI = 0.8

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Forecasting the end第 3 步 · 预测终点

Learn先学

EAC (estimate at completion) is the likely final cost. The usual exam default assumes the current cost performance continues: EAC = BAC / CPI. With a $200,000 budget and CPI 0.8, EAC is $250,000.

EAC（完工估算）是预计的最终成本。考试里默认的做法是假设现在的成本表现会持续：EAC = BAC / CPI。预算 20 万、CPI 为 0.8，EAC 就是 25 万。

Read the wording. If the overrun was a one-off that will not repeat, use EAC = AC + (BAC - EV). ETC is what is still to spend: EAC - AC. VAC is BAC - EAC, the expected over or underrun.

要读清题目的措辞。如果超支是一次性的、不会再发生，就用 EAC = AC + (BAC - EV)。ETC 是还要花的钱：EAC - AC。VAC 是 BAC - EAC，即预计的超支或节余。

TCPI asks how efficient you must be from now on to finish on budget: (BAC - EV) / (BAC - AC). Above 1 means you must do better than so far.

TCPI 问的是：从现在起要多高效才能在预算内完工，公式是 (BAC - EV) / (BAC - AC)。大于 1 表示要比之前做得更好。

EAC = BAC / CPI trend continues (default) EAC = AC + (BAC - EV) one-off problem ETC = EAC - AC still to spend VAC = BAC - EAC expected variance at the end TCPI = (BAC - EV) / (BAC - AC) efficiency needed from now on BAC 200,000 EV 80,000 AC 100,000 CPI 0.8 EAC = 250,000 ETC = 150,000 VAC = -50,000 TCPI = 120,000 / 100,000 = 1.2

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• PV is planned work, EV is done work, AC is money spent, all in money.
• Variances subtract from EV; indexes divide EV by AC or PV.
• Cost formulas end in AC, schedule formulas end in PV.
• Below 0 or below 1 is bad; above is good.
• EAC = BAC / CPI unless the question says the problem will not repeat.

• PV 是计划的工作，EV 是完成的工作，AC 是花掉的钱，都折成钱。
• 偏差用 EV 相减，指数用 EV 除以 AC 或 PV。
• 成本公式以 AC 结尾，进度公式以 PV 结尾。
• 小于 0 或小于 1 不好，大于则好。
• 除非题目说问题不会重复，否则 EAC = BAC / CPI。
