Mixed scenario drill综合情境练习

Practise nine mixed exam-style scenarios and name the lesson to revisit for each miss · ~15 min · practice练习九道混合的考试风格情境题，答错时知道该回看哪一课 · 约 15 分钟 · 练习

Step 1 · People questions第 1 步 · 人员类题目

Learn先学

Read what the question really asks, then find the answer that talks to the people first. Escalation and replacing people come last. Missed one? Revisit “Conflict and negotiation”, “Stakeholders” or “Leading the team”.

先读清题目到底问什么，再找最先去和当事人沟通的那个答案。上报和换人放在最后。答错了？回看“冲突与谈判”“相关方”或“带领团队”。

Step 2 · Process questions第 2 步 · 过程类题目

Learn先学

Ask: has it happened, who decides, and what do I know? Assess first, then follow the agreed process. Missed one? Revisit “Managing risk”, “Cost and earned value” or “Change control”.

问自己：事情发生了吗？由谁决定？我已知道什么？先评估，再按既定流程办。答错了？回看“风险管理”“成本与挣值”或“变更控制”。

Step 3 · Agile, compliance and closing第 3 步 · 敏捷、合规与收尾

Learn先学

Teams inspect their own process in the retrospective; compliance goes through assessment and the proper process; every project, even a cancelled one, is closed. Missed one? Revisit “Sprints and events”, “Compliance and governance” or “Closing a project”.

团队在回顾会上检视自己的过程；合规要经过评估和正规流程；每个项目，哪怕被取消，也要收尾。答错了？回看“冲刺与会议”“合规与治理”或“项目收尾”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Find what the question really asks before you look at the options.
• Assess before acting, and talk with the people involved first.
• Might happen is a risk; has happened is an issue.
• Follow the agreed process: change control, retrospective, closing.
• Every miss points to a lesson to revisit.

• 先找出题目真正问什么，再看选项。
• 先评估再行动，先和当事人沟通。
• 可能发生是风险，已经发生是问题。
• 遵循既定流程：变更控制、回顾会、收尾。
• 每一次答错都指向一课可以回看。
