Reading charts看懂图表

Read the charts that show up in questions and say what each one is telling you to do · ~11 min · reading and practice能看懂题目里出现的图表，并说出每张图在提示你做什么 · 约 11 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Quality charts第 1 步 · 质量图表

Learn先学

Some questions now show a chart and ask what you would do. Each chart answers one question, so learn the question.

现在有些题会给一张图，问你该怎么做。每种图只回答一个问题，记住这个问题就行。

Control chart: is the process stable? Results are plotted over time around a middle line, between an upper and a lower control limit. The process is out of control when a point falls outside a limit, or when seven points in a row sit on the same side of the middle line. Either way you look for the cause.

控制图：过程稳定吗？结果按时间画在中心线周围，上下各有一条控制界限。只要有一个点落在界限之外，或连续七个点落在中心线同一侧，过程就算失控。两种情况都要去找原因。

Control limits come from the process itself. Specification limits come from the customer and say what is acceptable. A process can be stable and still fail the customer.

控制界限来自过程本身。规格界限来自客户，说明什么才算合格。过程可以很稳定，却仍然不合客户的要求。

Pareto chart: which causes matter most? Bars are sorted from the biggest cause down. Fix the first few and most of the problem goes.

帕累托图：哪些原因最要紧？柱子从最大的原因往下排。解决前几个，大部分问题就没了。

Histogram: how often does each value occur? Scatter diagram: do two things move together? A pattern there suggests a link, and does not prove one causes the other.

直方图：每个数值出现得有多频繁？散点图：两件事是不是一起变化？有规律只说明可能相关，不能证明谁导致了谁。

Upper limit 52 --------------------------- Middle line 50 . . * * * * * * * . . . . Lower limit 48 --------------------------- Seven results in a row above 50, all inside the limits: still out of control. Find out why.

Step 2 · Delivery charts第 2 步 · 交付图表

Learn先学

Burndown chart: how much work is left? The line should fall toward zero by the end of the sprint or release.

燃尽图：还剩多少工作？这条线应该在冲刺或发布结束时降到零。

Burnup chart: how much is done, against the total? It has two lines, work done and total scope. When the scope line jumps up, work was added. A burndown chart hides that; a burnup chart shows it.

燃起图：相对总量，完成了多少？它有两条线：已完成的工作和总范围。范围线突然上跳，说明加了工作。燃尽图看不出这一点，燃起图能看出来。

Cumulative flow diagram: where is work piling up? Each band is a state such as To do, In progress, In test, Done. A band that keeps getting wider means items enter that state faster than they leave. That state is the bottleneck.

累积流图：工作堆在了哪里？每条色带代表一个状态，比如待办、进行中、测试中、已完成。某条色带越来越宽，说明进入这个状态的条目比离开的快。这个状态就是瓶颈。

Step 3 · Which chart answers it第 3 步 · 哪张图回答哪个问题

Learn先学

For any chart question: read the title and the axes first, find the trend or the odd point, and only then choose an action. The action is nearly always find out why with the team, not punish or ignore.

遇到图表题：先读标题和坐标轴，找出趋势或异常点，然后再选行动。行动几乎总是和团队一起弄清原因，而不是处罚或置之不理。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

What I got wrong我踩过的坑

I read a control chart as fine whenever every point was inside the limits. A run of seven on one side is also a signal.

我以前觉得只要所有点都在界限内，控制图就没问题。其实同一侧连续七个点也是信号。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Control chart: a point outside a limit or seven in a row on one side means find the cause.
• Control limits come from the process; specification limits come from the customer.
• Pareto ranks causes; scatter shows a link, not a cause.
• Burnup shows added scope; burndown does not.
• A widening band on a cumulative flow diagram is the bottleneck.

• 控制图：有点出界，或同一侧连续七点，都要找原因。
• 控制界限来自过程；规格界限来自客户。
• 帕累托图给原因排序；散点图显示相关，不证明因果。
• 燃起图能看出范围增加；燃尽图看不出。
• 累积流图上变宽的色带就是瓶颈。
