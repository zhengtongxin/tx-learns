Scenario questions情境题

Read a situation, decide what to do first or next, and defend the choice · ~10 min · reading and practice读懂情境，判断该先做什么或下一步做什么，并说出理由 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Read the last line first第 1 步 · 先读最后一句

Learn先学

Scenario questions end with the real ask: what should you do first, next, or best. Read that line before the story, then read the story looking for what matters to it.

情境题的最后一句才是真正的问题：首先该做什么、下一步做什么，还是最好的做法。先读这一句，再读情境，带着问题找关键信息。

The wording changes the answer. First asks what must happen before anything else. Next asks what follows the step already taken. Watch also for except and not.

措辞会改变答案。首先问的是在其他事之前必须做什么，下一步问的是已做步骤之后接着做什么。还要留意“除外”和“不”。

Step 2 · Assess before you act第 2 步 · 先评估，再行动

Learn先学

A dependable pattern: find out what is going on before you change anything. Gather facts, analyse the impact, and use the plans and processes you already have. Jumping to escalate, accept or cancel is usually premature.

一个可靠的规律：先弄清情况，再动手改变。收集事实、分析影响、用好现有的计划和流程。直接上报、直接接受或直接取消，通常为时过早。

Go to the right person. Talk directly to the person involved before you go to their manager. Ask the team before deciding for them. Follow change control even when a change is required by someone senior.

找对人。先直接找当事人谈，再考虑找他的上级。决定之前先问团队。即使是高层要求的变更，也要走变更控制。

These are reasoning habits, not magic words. If the situation is an emergency or the facts are already clear, act on them.

这些是思考习惯，不是万能口诀。如果是紧急情况，或事实已经很清楚，就按事实行动。

1 Clarify what exactly happened? 2 Assess impact on scope, time, cost, risk 3 Check what the plan or process says 4 Involve the person or team concerned 5 Act then record and communicate

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Eliminate with reasons第 3 步 · 带着理由排除

Learn先学

Usually two options can be removed quickly: one that acts without assessing, one that hands the problem to someone else. Then compare the last two against the exact question.

通常有两个选项可以很快排除：一个是不评估就行动，一个是把问题推给别人。剩下两个，再对照题目的原话比较。

For each option you drop, say why in one phrase. If you cannot, you are guessing, and a guess goes in your wrong-question book even when it is right.

每排除一个选项，用一句话说明理由。说不出来就是在蒙，蒙对的题也要记进错题本。

For multiple-select, judge each option on its own, true or false, rather than ranking them.

多选题要把每个选项单独判断对错，而不是排名次。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• The last line is the real question: first, next or best.
• Assess and gather facts before you change anything.
• Talk to the person directly and ask the team before escalating.
• Changes go through change control, whoever asks.
• Give a reason for each option you drop; a lucky guess is still a gap.

• 最后一句才是真正的问题：首先、下一步还是最好。
• 改变任何东西之前先评估、收集事实。
• 先直接找当事人、先问团队，再考虑上报。
• 无论谁提出，变更都要走变更控制。
• 每个排除的选项都要说出理由，蒙对也算漏洞。
