Scenario questions情境题

Work through any situation question with one short routine, spot the trap answers, and handle choose-two questions · ~12 min · reading and practice能用一套简短的步骤解任何情境题、认出陷阱选项，并应对“选两项”的题目 · 约 12 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Read the last line first第 1 步 · 先读最后一句

Learn先学

Scenario questions end with the real ask: what should you do first, next, or best. Read that line before the story, then read the story looking for what matters to it.

情境题的最后一句才是真正的问题：首先该做什么、下一步做什么，还是最好的做法。先读这一句，再带着问题读情境。

The wording changes the answer. First asks what must happen before anything else. Next asks what follows the steps the story says are already done, so do not pick a step that is behind you. Watch also for except and not.

措辞会改变答案。首先问的是在其他事之前必须做什么。下一步问的是情境里已完成的步骤之后接着做什么，所以别选已经做过的步骤。还要留意“除外”和“不”。

Step 2 · Three quick checks第 2 步 · 三个快速检查

Learn先学

Before looking at the options, ask three things about the story. They take seconds and remove most wrong answers. They are parts 2 to 4 of the full routine in the box below.

看选项之前，先就情境问三件事。只要几秒钟，就能排除大部分错误答案。它们就是下面方框里完整步骤的第 2 到第 4 项。

Agile or predictive? Agile work is delivered in short cycles called sprints; predictive work is planned in full up front. Sprint, backlog (the ordered list of work), product owner and demo mean agile: new work goes to the product owner and the backlog. Baseline (the approved plan), charter (the document that authorises the project) and change request mean predictive: changes go through change control. In a hybrid, use the rule of the part the question is about: a new request for the agile part goes to the backlog.

敏捷还是预测型？敏捷是按叫做“冲刺”的短周期交付；预测型是事先把全部计划做好。出现冲刺、待办列表（排好序的工作清单）、产品负责人、演示，就是敏捷：新工作交给产品负责人，进入待办列表。出现基准（已批准的计划）、章程（授权项目的文件）、变更请求，就是预测型：变更走变更控制。混合型就用题目所问那一部分的规则：针对敏捷部分的新请求，就进入待办列表。

Do I know the facts yet? If the impact, the cause or what a stakeholder needs is unknown, the answer is to analyse, check or ask. If it is already known, the answer is to act.

事实清楚了吗？如果影响、原因或相关方的需要还不清楚，答案就是分析、核实或询问。如果已经清楚，答案就是行动。

Who is it about? One person: talk to them privately. The whole team: the team works it out together. A stakeholder: go to them directly and ask what they need.

事关谁？一个人：私下找他谈。整个团队：团队一起解决。相关方：直接找他们，问清需要什么。

1 Read the last line first, next or best? 2 Spot the approach agile or predictive? 3 Facts known? no: find out yes: act 4 Who is it about? one person, the team, a stakeholder 5 Cross out the traps then choose

Sort each one: are the facts known, or must you find out first?

给每一项分类：事实已经清楚，还是要先弄清？

Step 3 · Trap answers第 3 步 · 陷阱选项

Learn先学

Wrong options are written to sound sensible. Most belong to four families. Learn to name the family and you can cross them out quickly.

错误选项都写得听起来很合理。它们大多属于四类。学会叫出类别，就能很快把它们划掉。

Paperwork only: log it, update the register, file a note. It records the problem and changes nothing. One-way: a report, an emailed document, a newsletter or a training session when a conversation is needed. Too early: escalating to the sponsor, HR or a board before you have tried what is within your authority. Punishing or delaying: penalties, freezes, restrictions, waiting until later.

只做文书：记下来、更新登记册、存一份备忘。它记录了问题，却什么都没改变。单向沟通：该对话的时候，却发报告、用邮件发文件、发简报或安排培训。太早上报：权限内能做的还没试，就上报发起人、人力资源或委员会。处罚或拖延：罚款、冻结、限制、以后再说。

The strong answer is usually active, done with the people affected, within your authority, and in the right order.

好的答案通常是主动的、和受影响的人一起做的、在你权限之内的，而且顺序正确。

These are habits of reasoning, not magic words. In an emergency, or when something is clearly beyond your authority, escalating at once is right.

这些是思考习惯，不是万能口诀。遇到紧急情况，或事情明显超出你的权限，立即上报就是对的。

A vendor has delivered late twice. Sort each option by its trap family.

一家供应商已经两次延迟交付。把每个选项归入它所属的陷阱类别。

Step 4 · Choose two第 4 步 · 选两项

Learn先学

Some questions ask for two answers, and you score only if both are right. Do not rank the options. Judge each one alone: would I do this, yes or no?

有些题要选两个答案，两个都对才得分。不要给选项排名次。把每个选项单独判断：这件事我会不会做，会还是不会？

The two right answers usually fit together as one sensible response, such as understanding the situation and involving the people affected. If your two picks pull in opposite directions, one of them is wrong.

两个正确答案通常能合成一个合理的做法，比如弄清情况并且让受影响的人参与。如果你选的两项方向相反，其中一个就是错的。

A project has some stable work packages and some that need frequent feedback. Two of these five are right. Choose two, leave three.

一个项目里，有些工作包很稳定，有些需要频繁反馈。下面五项里有两项是对的。选两项，留三项。

What I got wrong我踩过的坑

Most of my misses came from good actions in the wrong order: I replanned or escalated before I had checked the impact.

我做错的题，大多是动作本身没错、顺序错了：还没核实影响，就去重排计划或上报。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• The last line is the real question: first, next or best.
• Check the approach, whether the facts are known, and who it is about.
• Facts unknown: find out. Facts known: act.
• Cross out paperwork-only, one-way, too-early and punishing options.
• Choose two: judge each option alone, and both must be right.

• 最后一句才是真正的问题：首先、下一步还是最好。
• 检查用的是哪种方法、事实是否清楚、事关谁。
• 事实不清：先弄清。事实清楚：行动。
• 划掉只做文书、单向沟通、太早上报和处罚类的选项。
• 选两项：每个选项单独判断，两个都对才得分。
