Flow and metrics流动与度量

Use velocity to forecast, read burndown and burnup charts and spot a bottleneck on a board · ~10 min · reading and practice用速度做预测，读懂燃尽图和燃起图，并在看板上找出瓶颈 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Points and velocity第 1 步 · 点数与速度

Learn先学

Story points measure the size of an item relative to others: how much effort, complexity and uncertainty compared with a story the team already knows. They are not hours. Velocity is the points of fully done items in a sprint. Unfinished work counts for nothing.

故事点衡量一个条目相对于其他条目的大小：与团队已熟悉的故事相比，工作量、复杂度和不确定性有多大。它不是小时数。速度是一个冲刺里完全做完的条目的点数总和，没做完的不算。

To forecast, divide the points left by the average recent velocity. Round up: a half sprint still takes a sprint.

做预测时，用剩余点数除以最近几个冲刺的平均速度，并向上取整：半个冲刺也得占一个冲刺。

Velocity: sprint 1 = 18, sprint 2 = 22, sprint 3 = 20 Average velocity: (18 + 22 + 20) / 3 = 20 points per sprint Points left in the backlog: 70 Forecast: 70 / 20 = 3.5, so plan 4 more sprints

Step 2 · Burndown and burnup第 2 步 · 燃尽图与燃起图

Learn先学

A burndown chart plots the work remaining against time, falling toward zero. A burnup chart plots the work done rising toward a second line, the total scope.

燃尽图画出剩余工作随时间下降、趋向零。燃起图画出已完成工作向上爬升，去追另一条线，即总范围。

When scope changes, the burnup shows it: the scope line steps up. A burndown just flattens or rises, and you cannot tell from the line whether the team slowed down or the scope grew. A flat burndown is a question, not a verdict: ask what is blocking the work or what was added.

范围变化时，燃起图看得出来：范围线向上跳一格。燃尽图只会变平或上升，光看线分不清是团队变慢了还是范围变大了。燃尽图变平是个疑问，不是结论：要问是什么挡住了工作，或者加了什么进来。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Flow on a board第 3 步 · 看板上的流动

Learn先学

A Kanban board shows work as cards in columns. A work-in-progress limit (WIP limit) caps how many cards a column may hold, so the team finishes before it starts. Lead time runs from the request to done; cycle time runs from work started to done.

看板把工作画成列里的卡片。在制品上限（WIP 上限）限制一列最多放几张卡，让团队先做完再开新的。前置时间从提出需求算到完成；周期时间从开始做算到完成。

A bottleneck is the column where cards pile up. Do not feed it more: help clear it.

瓶颈是卡片堆积的那一列。不要再往里塞，要去帮忙清空它。

To do (no limit) | Doing (limit 3) | Review (limit 2) | Done 6 cards | 2 cards | 2 cards | 9 cards Review is full at 2 of 2: nobody pulls a card into it. Whoever finishes a card helps to review one.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Story points are relative size; velocity is the points of done items per sprint.
• Forecast sprints by dividing points left by average velocity and rounding up.
• A burnup keeps scope visible; a burndown can hide scope growth behind a flat line.
• A WIP limit makes the team finish before starting; a bottleneck is where cards pile up.
• Velocity is a planning aid for one team, not a score to compare teams.

• 故事点是相对大小；速度是每个冲刺完成条目的点数。
• 用剩余点数除以平均速度并向上取整，预测冲刺数。
• 燃起图让范围可见；燃尽图的平线可能掩盖范围增长。
• WIP 上限让团队先做完再开始；瓶颈是卡片堆积的地方。
• 速度是单个团队的计划工具，不是用来比较团队的分数。
