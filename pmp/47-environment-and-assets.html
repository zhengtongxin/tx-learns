Environment and assets环境与资产

Tell the conditions you work within from the assets you can reuse, and use both when you plan · ~8 min · reading and practice分清你要在其中工作的条件和可以重用的资产，并在规划时两者都用上 · 约 8 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Two kinds of surroundings第 1 步 · 两种周边条件

Learn先学

Every project sits inside an organisation and a wider world. Two groups of things shape it. Enterprise environmental factors are the conditions you work within and usually cannot change: the organisation's culture and structure, laws and regulations, market conditions, the people and software the organisation already has, and how much risk people will accept.

每个项目都处在一个组织和更大的环境之中。有两类东西塑造着它。事业环境因素是你要在其中工作、而且通常改变不了的条件：组织的文化与结构、法律法规、市场状况、组织已有的人员和软件，以及大家愿意承担多大风险。

Organisational process assets are what the organisation has built up and you can use and add to: policies and procedures, templates, standard forms, records of past projects and the lessons learned from them (what went well and what to do differently, written down at the end of a project). Think of the first as the weather and the second as the toolbox.

组织过程资产是组织积累下来、你可以使用并补充的东西：政策和流程、模板、标准表格、以往项目的记录以及从中得来的经验教训（项目收尾时写下的：什么做得好、下次该怎么改）。可以把前者想成天气，后者想成工具箱。

Environmental factor weather you work within it Process asset toolbox you use it and add to it

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Why it matters when you plan第 2 步 · 为什么规划时要考虑它们

Learn先学

Before you plan, look at both. The environment tells you what limits apply, for example a rule that every supplier must be approved, or a culture where decisions are made slowly. The assets tell you what you can reuse, such as an approved plan template or the risk list from a similar project (the risks someone wrote down last time, and how they were handled). Starting from them saves time and keeps you inside the organisation's rules.

规划之前，两样都要看。环境告诉你有哪些限制，比如每个供应商都必须经过批准，或者决策很慢的文化。资产告诉你有什么可以重用，比如已批准的计划模板，或类似项目的风险清单（上次有人记下的风险，以及当时怎么处理的）。从它们出发，既省时间，也不会违反组织的规定。

Step 3 · Adding to the toolbox第 3 步 · 给工具箱添砖加瓦

Learn先学

You do not only take from the toolbox. When the project closes, add what you learned: the final plan, the lessons learned and any template that worked well, so the next team starts further ahead. These assets are kept in the organisation's shared library or records. The weather is usually out of your hands, but you can plan around it and warn the sponsor (the person who backs and funds the project) when it threatens the project.

你不只是从工具箱里拿东西。项目收尾时，要把学到的补进去：最终的计划、经验教训，以及用得好的模板，让下一个团队起点更高。这些资产存放在组织的共享库或记录里。天气通常不在你手里，但可以围绕它来规划，并在它威胁到项目时提醒发起人（支持并出资的人）。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Environmental factors are the conditions you work within and usually cannot change.
• Process assets are what you can reuse and add to.
• Look at both before you plan.
• Add what worked to the organisation's records when you close.
• Warn the sponsor when the environment threatens the project.

• 环境因素是你要在其中工作、通常改变不了的条件。
• 过程资产是你可以重用并补充的东西。
• 规划之前两样都要看。
• 收尾时把好用的东西补进组织的记录。
• 环境威胁到项目时，提醒发起人。
