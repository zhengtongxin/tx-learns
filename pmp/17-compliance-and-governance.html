Compliance and governance合规与治理

Handle rules, gates and outside pressures without skipping the project's own controls · ~10 min · reading and practice在遵守规则、通过关口和应对外部压力时，不绕过项目自己的管控 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Rules and who enforces them第 1 步 · 规则与执行者

Learn先学

Compliance means following the rules that apply to the project. External rules are laws and regulations, such as data protection or safety law. Internal rules are the organisation's policies and standards.

合规是遵守适用于项目的规则。外部规则是法律法规，比如数据保护法或安全法规。内部规则是组织的政策和标准。

Governance is how decisions are made and checked. A steering committee, a review board or a project management office sets the rules and approves key moves.

治理是决策如何作出、如何被检查。指导委员会、评审委员会或项目管理办公室制定规则并批准关键步骤。

A gate is a checkpoint where the project must show it meets criteria before moving on. An audit is an independent check that the work follows the rules. Prepare for both from the start, not the week before.

关口是检查点，项目要证明达到标准才能继续。审计是独立检查工作是否遵守规则。两者都要从一开始就准备，而不是临到前一周。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · A mandatory change第 2 步 · 强制性变更

Learn先学

A new regulation arrives and the project must comply. Because it is mandatory, it is tempting to just do it. Still assess first: what does it change in scope, cost, schedule and risk?

新法规出台，项目必须遵守。因为是强制的，很容易直接去做。但仍要先评估：它对范围、成本、进度和风险有什么影响？

Then run it through change control. Mandatory means the change will almost surely be approved, not that you skip the process. The record and the approval protect the project.

然后走变更控制。强制的意思是变更会被批准，而不是可以跳过流程。记录和批准是对项目的保护。

1. analyse the impact (scope, cost, time, risk) 2. submit a change request 3. change control board decides 4. update the plan and tell stakeholders Mandatory changes still follow steps 1 to 4.

Step 3 · The wider environment第 3 步 · 更大的环境

Learn先学

Projects sit inside an external environment: market conditions, regulation, competitors, technology and society. These change, so watch them through the project, not only at the start.

项目处在外部环境之中：市场、法规、竞争对手、技术和社会。这些会变化，所以要在整个项目期间关注，而不只是开始时。

Sustainability means considering the long-term environmental, social and economic effects of what you deliver. Include it in decisions and in risk and stakeholder thinking.

可持续性是考虑所交付成果在环境、社会和经济上的长期影响。把它纳入决策，以及风险和相关方的考虑中。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Compliance covers external laws and internal policies.
• Governance sets who decides; gates and audits check the work.
• A mandatory change still gets an impact assessment and change control.
• Watch market and regulation through the whole project.
• Sustainability is part of long-term value.

• 合规既包括外部法律，也包括内部政策。
• 治理决定谁来决策，关口和审计检查工作。
• 强制性变更仍要先做影响评估并走变更控制。
• 整个项目期间都要关注市场和法规。
• 可持续性是长期价值的一部分。
