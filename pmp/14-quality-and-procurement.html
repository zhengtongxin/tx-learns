Quality and procurement质量与采购

Tell assurance from control, and choose a contract type by who carries the risk · ~10 min · reading and practice能分清质量保证与质量控制，并按谁承担风险来选合同类型 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Building quality in第 1 步 · 把质量做进去

Learn先学

Quality assurance looks at the process: are we working in a way that should give good results? Audits belong here. Quality control looks at the deliverables: does this output meet the requirement? Testing and inspection belong here.

质量保证看过程：我们的做法能否带来好结果？审计属于这一类。质量控制看可交付成果：这个产出是否满足要求？测试和检查属于这一类。

The principle is prevention over inspection. Preventing a defect is cheaper than finding it, and finding it is cheaper than fixing it at the customer's site.

原则是预防胜于检查。预防缺陷比发现缺陷便宜，发现又比在客户那里返工便宜。

Cost of quality adds two groups. Cost of conformance is what you spend to get it right: training, planning, reviews and testing. Cost of nonconformance is what failure costs: rework, scrap, warranty claims and lost reputation.

质量成本分两组。符合性成本是为把事情做对花的钱：培训、规划、评审和测试。不符合性成本是失败的代价：返工、报废、保修索赔和声誉损失。

Are we following the right process? (audit) -> quality assurance Does this output meet the spec? (inspect) -> quality control Training, reviews, testing -> cost of conformance Rework, scrap, warranty -> cost of nonconformance

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Make or buy, and contract types第 2 步 · 自制或外购，合同类型

Learn先学

Procurement means getting work or goods from outside. First a make-or-buy decision: do it in-house, or buy it? You weigh cost, skills, capacity, control and risk.

采购就是从外部获取工作或物品。先做自制或外购决策：自己做还是买？要权衡成本、技能、产能、控制和风险。

The contract type decides who carries the risk. Fixed-price: the seller carries cost risk, so it suits clear scope. Time-and-materials: the buyer pays for hours and parts, so the buyer carries more risk, and it suits unclear scope or quick help. Cost-reimbursable: the buyer pays the actual costs plus a fee, so the buyer carries most risk, and it suits uncertain work.

合同类型决定谁承担风险。固定价格：卖方承担成本风险，适合范围清楚的工作。工时与材料：买方按工时和材料付费，买方承担较多风险，适合范围不明或要快速补人。成本补偿：买方付实际成本加费用，买方承担最多风险，适合不确定的工作。

Rule of thumb: the clearer the scope, the more a fixed price makes sense. The more unknown, the more the buyer must share the risk.

经验法则：范围越清楚，越适合固定价格；未知越多，买方越要分担风险。

Fixed-price seller carries most risk clear scope Time-and-materials shared, buyer leaning unclear scope, short help Cost-reimbursable buyer carries most risk uncertain, research-like work

Step 3 · Documents and bids第 3 步 · 文件与投标

Learn先学

Do not mix these two. The statement of work (SOW) describes what the seller must deliver. The procurement management plan describes how you will run procurement: how sellers are chosen, which contract types, how performance is tracked and how contracts close.

别把这两份混淆。工作说明书（SOW）描述卖方必须交付什么。采购管理计划描述你如何管理采购：怎么选卖方、用什么合同类型、如何跟踪绩效、怎么收尾合同。

Sellers answer with bids or proposals, and you compare them on the criteria you set before you looked at any offer. The lowest price alone is not the rule: it also has to meet the requirement and be credible.

卖方用投标或建议书回应，你按事先定好的标准比较。不只看最低价，还要满足要求并且可信。

Whatever the contract says, you still manage it: watch deliverables, handle changes through the contract's change process, and close it formally.

不管合同怎么写，你都要管理它：盯交付、通过合同里的变更程序处理变更，并正式收尾。

Statement of work WHAT the seller must deliver Procurement mgmt plan HOW we will buy and manage sellers

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Assurance checks the process; control checks the output.
• Prevention is cheaper than inspection, which is cheaper than rework.
• Cost of quality: conformance (get it right) versus nonconformance (failure).
• Fixed-price puts risk on the seller; cost-reimbursable puts it on the buyer.
• The SOW says what to deliver; the procurement management plan says how you will buy.

• 保证查过程，控制查产出。
• 预防比检查便宜，检查比返工便宜。
• 质量成本：符合性（做对）对不符合性（失败）。
• 固定价格把风险放在卖方，成本补偿把风险放在买方。
• SOW 说交付什么，采购管理计划说你怎么采购。
