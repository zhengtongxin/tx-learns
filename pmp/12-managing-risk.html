Managing risk风险管理

Find, rank and respond to risks, and say which reserve pays for what · ~10 min · reading and practice能识别风险、排出优先级、选对应对方式，并说清哪笔储备金付哪类费用 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Find and rank第 1 步 · 识别与排序

Learn先学

A risk is an uncertain event that could help or hurt the project. It has not happened yet. If it has happened, it is an issue, and you deal with it now.

风险是一个不确定的事件，可能对项目有利或有害，而且还没发生。已经发生的就是问题，要马上处理。

Everyone can identify risks: the team, the sponsor, suppliers, customers. The project manager does not do it alone. Each risk goes in the risk register with an owner, so nothing floats around unowned.

人人都可以识别风险：团队、发起人、供应商、客户。项目经理不是一个人在做。每个风险都记入风险登记册并指定负责人，不让风险无人认领。

Then rank them. Qualitative analysis is quick and uses words: probability and impact rated high, medium or low. Quantitative analysis is slower and uses numbers, such as money or time at stake. You do the qualitative pass first, and go numeric only on the few risks that deserve it.

然后排序。定性分析快，用文字：可能性和影响分成高、中、低。定量分析慢，用数字，比如涉及的金额或时间。先做定性，只对少数重要风险再做定量。

Risk: Supplier may deliver the servers late Probability: Medium Impact: High Owner: Procurement lead Response: Mitigate: order early, name a backup supplier Status: Open

Step 2 · Pick a response第 2 步 · 选择应对

Learn先学

Threats and opportunities each have their own set of responses. The exam checks that you do not mix them up.

威胁和机会各有一套应对策略。考试常考你有没有混用。

Threats: avoid (remove the cause, such as dropping a risky feature), mitigate (cut the probability or the impact), transfer (shift the impact to someone else, such as insurance or a fixed-price contract), accept (do nothing now, maybe keep a reserve), escalate (it is outside your authority, so a higher level owns it).

威胁：规避（消除原因，比如删掉有风险的功能）、减轻（降低可能性或影响）、转移（把影响交给别人，比如保险或固定价格合同）、接受（暂时不动，可留储备）、上报（超出你的权限，由更高层负责）。

Opportunities: exploit (make sure it happens), enhance (raise its probability or benefit), share (give part of it to a partner who can capture it better), accept, escalate.

机会：开拓（确保它发生）、增强（提高可能性或收益）、分享（让更擅长的伙伴分一部分）、接受、上报。

Transfer moves the impact, not the risk itself. You still manage the relationship. And skipping a check, such as a sprint demo, is not transfer: you simply accepted the risk.

转移转走的是影响，不是风险本身，你仍要管好这层关系。而跳过某个检查，比如冲刺演示，不叫转移：你只是接受了这个风险。

THREAT OPPORTUNITY avoid remove the cause exploit make it certain mitigate make it smaller enhance make it bigger transfer someone else pays share partner takes a part accept live with it accept take it if it comes escalate not yours to own escalate not yours to own

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Reserves and leftovers第 3 步 · 储备与遗留

Learn先学

Responses can leave something behind. A secondary risk is a new risk created by your response (a backup supplier may itself be unreliable). A residual risk is what remains after the response (insurance has a limit). Plan for both.

应对之后可能还留下东西。次生风险是应对措施本身带来的新风险（备用供应商也可能不可靠）。残余风险是应对之后仍然剩下的风险（保险有赔付上限）。两种都要计划。

Money for risk comes in two kinds. Contingency reserve is for identified risks, such as the late servers above, and the project manager can use it within the plan. Management reserve is for unidentified surprises and sits outside the baseline: you need management approval to use it.

风险的钱分两种。应急储备用于已识别的风险，比如上面服务器晚到，项目经理可按计划使用。管理储备用于未识别的意外，在基准之外，使用要经管理层批准。

Exam rule of thumb: a known risk draws on contingency; a surprise nobody listed draws on management reserve.

考试里的判断：已知风险用应急储备，没人列过的意外用管理储备。

Risk was in the register -> contingency reserve (project manager uses it) Risk was never identified -> management reserve (ask management first)

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A risk has not happened yet; if it has, it is an issue.
• Everyone identifies risks; they go in the register with an owner.
• Qualitative first with words, quantitative only for the risks that matter.
• Threats: avoid, mitigate, transfer, accept, escalate. Opportunities: exploit, enhance, share, accept, escalate.
• Contingency covers identified risks; management reserve covers unknown ones and needs approval.

• 风险还没发生；发生了就是问题。
• 人人都能识别风险；记入登记册并指定负责人。
• 先定性用文字，只对重要风险再做定量。
• 威胁：规避、减轻、转移、接受、上报。机会：开拓、增强、分享、接受、上报。
• 应急储备覆盖已识别风险；管理储备覆盖未知风险，使用需批准。
