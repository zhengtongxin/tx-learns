Iterative and incremental迭代与增量

Tell iterative from incremental delivery, pick the one a situation calls for, and plan when little is known yet · ~9 min · reading and practice能分清迭代与增量交付、按情境选对方式，并在信息还很少的时候做计划 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Two different ideas第 1 步 · 两个不同的概念

Learn先学

People use the two words as if they meant the same thing. They do not, and questions are built on the difference.

很多人把这两个词当成一回事。其实不是，考题正是围绕这个区别出的。

Iterative means you repeat to improve. You build a rough version of the whole thing, get feedback, and refine it. The goal is learning. Nothing may be usable until late.

迭代是反复做、越做越好。先做出整体的粗略版本，拿到反馈，再打磨。目的是学习。可能到很晚才有能用的东西。

Incremental means you deliver in finished pieces. Each piece is complete and usable, and you add the next one to it. The goal is early value: users get something useful sooner.

增量是一块一块地交付成品。每一块都是完整可用的，再往上加下一块。目的是尽早产生价值。

Agile does both: short cycles that each deliver a usable piece and feed back into the next. Predictive does neither: one plan, and usually one delivery at the end. It suits work whose scope is fixed and well understood, where one plan is enough. (Delivering in planned phases borrows the incremental idea; mixing approaches like that is called hybrid.)

敏捷两者兼有：每个短周期都交付一块可用的东西，并把反馈带入下一轮。预测型两者都没有：一个计划，通常最后一次性交付。它适合范围固定、已被充分了解的工作，一份计划就够用。（按计划分阶段交付，借用了增量的做法；这样把不同方法混用，就叫混合型。）

Iterative sketch -> clickable mock-up -> refined app same whole product, better each round Incremental login -> transfers -> bill payments one finished feature after another

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Planning with little information第 2 步 · 信息不足时怎么计划

Learn先学

Early in a project you often cannot say exactly what the later work will be. Guessing in detail gives a plan that looks precise and is wrong.

项目早期，你往往说不清后面的工作具体是什么。硬要猜出细节，只会得到一份看似精确、实则错误的计划。

Progressive elaboration means adding detail to the plan as you learn more. The plan is meant to get sharper over time.

渐进明细是指随着了解加深，逐步给计划补充细节。计划本来就应该越来越清晰。

Rolling wave planning is how you do it: plan the near-term work in detail and leave the later work as large blocks. In practice there are three tiers: detailed, outlined and coarse (shown below). As each wave of work comes closer, break its block down.

滚动式规划是具体做法：近期工作做详细计划，远期工作先保留为大块。实际上分三层：详细、概要和粗略（见下方示例）。等每一波工作临近，再把那一块拆细。

The middle tier of a rolling wave plan, the next quarter, is outlined: work packages (small, clearly defined pieces of work) with rough timing, not tasks. Waiting until every estimate is known only delays the work, so you start with what you know. Early on you know little, and detail you plan now would only be guessed and then redone, so with limited data the right plan is flexible and staged. In the exam, a detailed timeline or a full task-by-task estimate is the trap.

滚动式规划的中间一层（下一个季度）只做概要：有粗略时间的工作包（小而界定清楚的工作块），而不是具体任务。等所有估算都有了再动手只会拖延工作，所以先用已知的信息开始。早期了解有限，现在写的细节只是猜测、之后还要重做，所以数据有限时，合适的计划是灵活、分阶段的。在考试里，详细的时间表或逐项任务估算都是陷阱。

Next 4 weeks tasks, owners, days (detailed) Next quarter work packages, rough weeks (outlined) After that phases and milestones only (coarse) work package = a small, clearly defined piece of work milestone = a marker for a key point, such as "design approved"
What I got wrong我踩过的坑

I read iterative as frequent delivery. It is not: iterative refines one product and may deliver only at the end. Incremental is the word for delivering often.

我曾把迭代理解成频繁交付。其实不是：迭代是打磨同一个产品，可能到最后才交付。频繁交付对应的词是增量。

Step 3 · Planning waves with numbers第 3 步 · 用数字看滚动规划

Learn先学

A 12-week project plans the next 4 weeks in detail and keeps the other eight weeks as blocks (the same idea with the middle tier left out, to keep the numbers simple). One third of the timeline (4 ÷ 12) is detailed. When the first four weeks are done, the team breaks the next 4 weeks down. By then 4 + 4 = 8 of the 12 weeks, two thirds of the timeline, have been planned in detail. The plan gets sharper because the team knows more, not because it guessed harder.

一个 12 周的项目，把接下来的 4 周做详细计划，其余八周先保留为大块（同样的思路，省掉中间一层，让数字更简单）。时间线的三分之一（4 ÷ 12）是详细的。前四周做完后，团队再把接下来的 4 周拆细。到那时，12 周里已有 4 + 4 = 8 周，也就是三分之二的时间线做过详细计划。计划变得更清晰，是因为团队知道得更多，而不是猜得更用力。

Now weeks 1-4 detailed | weeks 5-12 one coarse block After 4 weeks 5-8 detailed | weeks 9-12 one coarse block Detailed so far: 4 of 12, then 8 of 12

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Iterative: repeat to refine, to learn.
• Incremental: deliver finished pieces, for early value.
• Agile does both; predictive does neither.
• Progressive elaboration: detail grows as you learn.
• Rolling wave: near work detailed, far work coarse.

• 迭代：反复打磨，为了学习。
• 增量：分块交付成品，为了尽早产生价值。
• 敏捷两者兼有；预测型两者都没有。
• 渐进明细：细节随着了解加深而增加。
• 滚动式规划：近期详细，远期粗略。
