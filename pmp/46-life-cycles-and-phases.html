Life cycles and phases生命周期与阶段

Say what phases and gates are, tell the life cycle shapes apart, and keep the project and product life cycles separate · ~9 min · reading and practice说出阶段和关卡是什么，分清生命周期的几种形状，并把项目与产品生命周期分开 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Phases and gates第 1 步 · 阶段与关卡

Learn先学

A project life cycle is the series of phases a project passes through from start to close, for example start, plan, build, test and hand over. Phases give the work a shape: each one ends with something finished that you can check.

项目的生命周期是项目从开始到结束经历的一系列阶段，例如启动、规划、建造、测试和交接。阶段让工作有了形状：每个阶段都以一件可以检查的完成成果收尾。

At the end of a phase there is often a phase gate: a review where the sponsor (the person who backs and funds the project) and key stakeholders (people with a stake in the result) look at what was produced and decide whether to go on, change direction or stop. Stopping a project that no longer makes sense is a good result, not a failure.

阶段结束时常有一道阶段关卡：发起人（支持并出资的人）和主要相关方（与结果有利害关系的人）审视产出，决定继续、调整方向还是停止。停掉一个已经不合理的项目是好结果，不是失败。

Step 2 · Different shapes of life cycle第 2 步 · 不同形状的生命周期

Learn先学

In a predictive life cycle the phases run one after another and the scope (what the project will deliver) is fixed early. In an iterative one, the team repeats short cycles that refine the whole product each round; in an incremental one, each cycle adds a finished piece that stays. An agile life cycle is short cycles with frequent feedback, and a hybrid one mixes predictive and agile. The right shape depends on how stable the requirements are and how costly a change is.

预测型生命周期里，阶段依次进行，范围（项目要交付什么）早早定死。迭代型里，团队重复短周期，每一轮都改进整个产品；增量型里，每个周期加上一块完成的部分并保留下来。敏捷生命周期是带频繁反馈的短周期，混合型则预测型和敏捷型兼有。选哪种，取决于需求有多稳定、改动有多昂贵。

Also keep two lives apart. The project life cycle ends when the project closes. The product life cycle (the product's whole time in use, from idea to retirement) goes on after it.

还要分清两个“生命”。项目生命周期在项目收尾时结束。产品生命周期（产品从构想到退役的全部使用时间）在那之后还会继续。

Step 3 · Using the life cycle第 3 步 · 用好生命周期

Learn先学

Do not skip a gate because the schedule is tight: the gate is where problems are caught while they are still cheap. If a gate review shows the business reason has gone, raise it with the sponsor rather than carrying on. And when the PMP exam asks which life cycle fits, read for the clue: fixed, well understood requirements point to predictive; unclear ones with a need for early feedback point to agile or hybrid.

不要因为进度紧就跳过关卡：关卡是在问题还便宜的时候把它抓出来的地方。如果关卡评审发现立项的理由已不存在，要向发起人提出，而不是继续往下做。PMP 考试问该用哪种生命周期时，抓线索：需求固定、理解透彻，指向预测型；需求不清、需要早期反馈，指向敏捷或混合型。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• A life cycle is the series of phases from start to close.
• A phase gate is a review that decides to go on, change direction or stop.
• Predictive, iterative, agile and hybrid suit different levels of certainty.
• The project life cycle ends at close; the product's goes on.
• Never skip a gate to save time.

• 生命周期是从开始到结束的一系列阶段。
• 阶段关卡是决定继续、调整或停止的评审。
• 预测型、迭代、敏捷和混合型适合不同的确定程度。
• 项目生命周期在收尾时结束；产品的还会继续。
• 不要为了省时间跳过关卡。
