Scope and requirements范围与需求

Break scope into a WBS, gather requirements, rank them and handle change without scope creep · ~10 min · reading and practice把范围分解成 WBS、收集并排序需求，并在不蔓延范围的前提下处理变更 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · The WBS第 1 步 · 工作分解结构

Learn先学

The work breakdown structure (WBS) splits the total scope into smaller and smaller deliverables, until each piece is small enough to estimate and assign. It covers all the work and only the work in scope: if it is not in the WBS, it is not in the project.

工作分解结构（WBS）把总范围逐层拆成越来越小的可交付成果，直到每块小到可以估算和分配。它覆盖全部范围内的工作，也只包含这些工作：不在 WBS 里，就不在项目里。

The smallest piece is a work package. Decomposition is the technique; the WBS is the output of it, along with the WBS dictionary that describes each piece. For the exam, remember: a WBS and a network diagram are outputs or inputs, never techniques.

最小的一块叫工作包。分解是技术，WBS 是它的输出，还配有描述每块内容的 WBS 词典。考试里要分清：WBS 和网络图是输出或输入，不是技术。

Technique (what you do) Output (what you get) decomposition -> WBS, WBS dictionary sequencing activities -> network diagram WBS = a thing you have, not a thing you do

Step 2 · Gathering and ranking requirements第 2 步 · 收集与排序需求

Learn先学

Requirements are what stakeholders need the result to do. Gather them by interviews, workshops, surveys, observation and prototypes, then record them and trace each to a need so nothing is built without a reason.

需求是相关方要求成果具备的能力。通过访谈、研讨会、问卷、观察和原型来收集，记录下来，并把每条追溯到一个需要，这样就不会无缘无故地做东西。

Two ways to rank them are often confused. MoSCoW sorts by priority for this release: Must, Should, Could, Won't (this time). Kano sorts by how customers feel: basic expectations (missing them annoys), performance (more is better) and delighters (unexpected pleasure).

有两种排序方法常被混淆。MoSCoW按本次发布的优先级分：必须有、应该有、可以有、这次不做。Kano按客户感受分：基本型（没有会不满）、期望型（越多越好）和魅力型（意外惊喜）。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Creep versus change第 3 步 · 蔓延与变更

Learn先学

Scope creep is scope growing without control: extra features slipped in, no review, no change in time or cost. Gold plating is the team adding extras nobody asked for. Both are bad.

范围蔓延是范围不受控制地扩大：悄悄加功能，没有评审，时间和成本也没跟着调。镀金是团队自己加了没人要的额外内容。两者都不好。

A change is not bad in itself. A request that goes through change control, with its impact assessed and approved, is fine. A new request first goes to be assessed, not simply refused and not simply accepted. The agile version is simpler: the new item joins the product backlog.

变更本身不是坏事。走了变更控制、评估过影响并获批准的请求没问题。收到新请求，先评估，不是直接拒绝，也不是直接接受。敏捷的做法更简单：把新条目放进产品待办列表。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• The WBS covers all and only the work in scope; decomposition builds it.
• The WBS and the network diagram are outputs or inputs, not techniques.
• MoSCoW ranks priority for a release; Kano ranks how customers feel.
• Scope creep is growth without control; gold plating is extras nobody asked for.
• A new request is assessed and sent through change control, not quietly added.

• WBS 覆盖全部且仅限范围内的工作，由分解得出。
• WBS 和网络图是输出或输入，不是技术。
• MoSCoW 排发布优先级，Kano 看客户感受。
• 范围蔓延是失控的扩张，镀金是没人要的额外内容。
• 新请求要评估并走变更控制，不能悄悄加。
