Issues and improvement问题与持续改进

Deal with an issue or a blocker at the right level, and turn what the team learns into small steady improvements · ~10 min · reading and practice能在合适的层级处理问题和障碍，并把团队学到的东西变成小而持续的改进 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Issues need action第 1 步 · 问题要靠行动解决

Learn先学

A risk might happen. An issue has happened and is hurting the project now. An impediment, or blocker, is anything stopping the team from getting work done.

风险是可能发生的事。问题是已经发生、正在影响项目的事。障碍（也叫阻碍）是任何让团队没法推进工作的东西。

Each issue goes in the issue log with an owner and a date. But writing it down is the record, not the fix. In a question, an option that only logs the issue is rarely the best answer when another option acts on it.

每个问题都记入问题日志，写明负责人和日期。但记下来只是留痕，不是解决。做题时，如果另一个选项是采取行动，只“记入日志”的选项很少是最佳答案。

When a risk you planned for happens, do not start again. Carry out the response already written in the risk register, then check that it worked.

如果发生的是你已经做过计划的风险，不要从头来过。按风险登记册里写好的应对措施执行，再确认它是否有效。

Issue: Test server has been down since Monday Impact: Two testers blocked, release at risk Owner: Infrastructure lead Action: Restore from backup, add monitoring Due: Thursday Status: In progress

Step 2 · Solve it or raise it第 2 步 · 自己解决还是上报

Learn先学

Removing blockers is part of the job. If it is within your authority, handle it with the team: reallocate people, talk to the other team's lead, change the order of work.

清除障碍是你的本职工作。只要在你的权限内，就和团队一起解决：调配人手、找对方团队的负责人谈、调整工作顺序。

Escalate only when the decision is not yours to make: it needs money or people you do not control, it breaks an agreed limit, or two senior stakeholders want opposite things and talking has failed.

只有当决定权不在你手里时才上报：需要你无权调动的资金或人员、突破了已约定的界限，或者两位高层相关方的要求相反而且沟通无效。

When you do escalate, follow the path agreed in the project's governance, and bring the facts, the impact and your recommended options. Handing over a bare problem is passing it on, not managing it.

真的要上报时，按项目治理中约定的路径走，并带上事实、影响和你建议的方案。只把问题丢上去，是推卸，不是管理。

On an agile team, blockers are raised at the daily meeting and removed that day where possible. Nobody waits for the retrospective to mention that they are stuck.

在敏捷团队里，障碍在每日站会上提出，尽量当天清除。没有人会等到回顾会才说自己被卡住了。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Small steady improvement第 3 步 · 小步持续改进

Learn先学

Continual improvement means making the way you work a little better, again and again, instead of saving every lesson for the end.

持续改进是指一次又一次地把工作方式改好一点，而不是把所有经验都留到最后。

The usual loop has four parts: plan a small change, do it, check whether it helped, then act: keep it if it worked, drop it if not. Trying a change on a small scale first is called a pilot.

常用的循环有四步：计划一个小改动，执行，检查它有没有用，然后处理：有用就保留，没用就放弃。先小范围试一个改动，叫试点。

On agile teams the retrospective is where this happens. The team picks one or two improvements, gives each an owner, and checks them at the next one. A long list with no owners changes nothing.

在敏捷团队里，这件事发生在回顾会上。团队挑一两项改进，各定一个负责人，下次回顾时检查。一长串没人负责的清单，什么也改变不了。

The team owns its process. Your part is to make it safe to speak up and to help the team run the change, not to hand them a fix you designed alone.

流程是团队自己的。你的职责是让大家敢说话，并帮团队把改动落地，而不是把你一个人设计好的方案塞给他们。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• An issue has happened; log it with an owner, then act on it.
• A planned risk that happens: carry out the planned response.
• Handle what is within your authority; escalate only what is not.
• Escalate along the agreed path, with facts and options.
• Improve in small steps: plan, do, check, act, with an owner for each change.

• 问题是已经发生的事；记下来、定负责人，然后采取行动。
• 计划过的风险发生了：执行计划好的应对。
• 权限内的自己处理；超出权限的才上报。
• 按约定路径上报，带上事实和方案。
• 小步改进：计划、执行、检查、处理，每项改动都有负责人。
