这个系列只做一件事：Kaya Travel Cover 的理赔台试点，让 Aisha 和理赔员更快清空队列，而每一笔赔付仍由老板 Goh 先生签字。它从这里开始，因为在 Mei Ling 看清真实的工作之前，没人能做出“理赔用 AI”。今天她就坐到桌边，弄清这份工作到底是什么。

The story故事

Kaya Travel Cover is a small, family-run travel insurer in a three-storey shophouse on Tanjong Pagar's five-foot way. It has never used AI. Mei Ling is posted there as its forward-deployed engineer: she works at their desks, not from a far-off office. The owner says, “Use AI on claims.” That is all the brief she has.

Kaya Travel Cover 是新加坡 Tanjong Pagar 五脚基上一栋三层店屋里的家族式小型旅游保险公司，从没用过 AI。Mei Ling 被派来做它的前线部署工程师：她就坐在他们的办公桌旁，而不是远远地在另一间办公室。老板只说了一句：“理赔这块用 AI 吧。”这就是她拿到的全部需求。

Step 1 · The first move is not code第 1 步 · 第一步不是写代码

Learn先学

A forward-deployed engineer (FDE) is an engineer who works inside the customer's business, turns a messy request into a working AI system, and stays until people actually use it. The problem it solves: “use AI on claims” is not something you can build.

So Mei Ling starts by sitting next to Aisha, the senior claims handler, for an afternoon. Think of a new chef first watching the kitchen at lunch rush before changing the menu.

前线部署工程师（FDE）是在客户的业务内部工作的工程师：把含糊的需求变成能运转的 AI 系统，并一直陪到大家真的用起来。它要解决的问题是：“理赔用 AI”这句话没法直接动手做。

所以 Mei Ling 先在资深理赔员 Aisha 身边坐一个下午。就像新来的厨师，先在午市最忙的时候看一遍后厨，再去动菜单。

What Mei Ling saw Aisha do, one claim at a time: 1. read the customer's story 2. check the policy: is this covered? 3. decide: approve, decline, or refer (pass it to a senior handler) 4. stamp it, and move to the next queue number

Which of these is the right first move on Monday morning? Decide for each.

周一早上，下面哪些是对的第一步？逐个判断。

Step 2 · Write the job in one paragraph第 2 步 · 用一段话写下这份工作

Learn先学

After watching, Mei Ling writes the job down in one paragraph. It must say four things: what goes in, what comes out, who signs it off, and what a mistake costs. If any of the four is missing, nobody knows what “working” means yet.

看完之后，Mei Ling 用一段话把这份工作写下来。里面必须有四样东西：什么进来、什么出去、谁签字确认、出错的代价是什么。少了任何一样，就没人知道“能用”到底指什么。

In: a claim (customer's story, dates, receipts) and the policy it falls under. Out: approve, decline or refer, a payout amount, and a short reason. Signed off by: a claims handler for any large payout or any referral. A mistake costs: a wrong payout is money gone; a wrong decline is an angry customer.

Step 3 · Three skills in one job第 3 步 · 一份工作里的三种本事

Learn先学

An FDE mixes three skills. Engineering builds the thing. Consulting finds the real problem behind the request. Trust makes people willing to use it. Most early value comes from the second and third, not clever code: a customer who says “use AI on claims” usually means “stop my team drowning in the same twenty kinds of claim.”

前线部署工程师把三种本事混在一起用。工程负责把东西做出来；咨询负责找出需求背后真正的问题；信任让大家愿意去用它。早期的大部分价值来自后两样，而不是花哨的代码：客户说“理赔用 AI”，通常意思是“别让我的团队再被同样那二十种理赔淹没”。

Which skill is Mei Ling using in each moment?

下面每个时刻，Mei Ling 用的是哪种本事？

Step 4 · Agree what “good” means第 4 步 · 先谈好什么叫“好”

Learn先学

Here is the hard part. Everyone at Kaya wants a “good” helper, but they mean different things. Mr Goh, head of claims, counts accuracy. Aisha counts fairness. Kumar, the one-person IT team, counts risk. If Mei Ling builds before they agree, she can win one definition and lose the other two.

难的地方在这里。Kaya 的每个人都想要一个“好”的帮手，但他们说的“好”不一样。理赔主管 Mr Goh 看准确率；Aisha 看公平；一人 IT 团队 Kumar 看风险。如果还没谈拢就开工，Mei Ling 也许赢了一种定义，却输了另外两种。

Step 5 · The road ahead第 5 步 · 前面的路

Learn先学

This track follows one path, in this order: a pilot, pick a model, give it a harness (the code around the model that lets it use tools and take turns), measure it, add memory, make it safe to pause, improve it, secure it, and hand it over. Each step fixes a problem the one before it exposes. Click through them.

这条学习线只走一条路，顺序是：做试点、选模型、给它一个 harness（包在模型外面、让它能用工具和轮流行动的代码）、衡量、加记忆、让它能安全暂停、改进、加固、交付。每一步都在解决前一步暴露出来的问题。点开看看。

What I got wrong我当时想错了I thought the job was mostly coding. It is not. The hard part is agreeing what “good” means with the customer before building: Mr Goh counts accuracy, Aisha counts fairness, Kumar counts risk. Code comes after that brief.我原以为这份工作主要是写代码，其实不是。难的是在动手之前先和客户谈好“好”是什么意思：Mr Goh 看准确率，Aisha 看公平，Kumar 看风险。代码要排在这份说明之后。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

What changed这一课之后Now the pilot has a written job: what goes in, what comes out, who signs off and what a mistake costs, with Mr Goh and Aisha each agreeing what “good” means. The next problem is turning that brief into a plan a coding agent can build from.现在试点有了一份写下来的工作说明：进什么、出什么、谁签字、出错的代价是什么，Goh 先生和 Aisha 也各自说定了“好”是什么意思。下一个问题是把这份说明变成编程智能体能照着建的计划。

Takeaways要点回顾

• An FDE builds inside the customer's world and stays until people use it.
• The first move is to watch the real process, not to write code.
• Write the job in one paragraph: in, out, who signs off, what a mistake costs.
• The job mixes engineering, consulting and trust. Early value comes from better questions and a clear brief.
• Agree what “good” means with each owner before building.
• The path: pilot, model, harness, measure, memory, safe pause, improve, secure, hand over.

• 前线部署工程师在客户的世界里做东西，并陪到大家真正用起来。
• 第一步是观察真实的流程，而不是写代码。
• 用一段话写下工作：进、出、谁签字、出错的代价。
• 这份工作混合了工程、咨询和信任；早期价值来自更好的提问和清晰的说明。
• 动手之前，先和每个相关的人谈好“好”是什么意思。
• 路线：试点、模型、harness、衡量、记忆、安全暂停、改进、加固、交付。
