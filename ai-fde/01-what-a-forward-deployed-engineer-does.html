The forward-deployed engineer前线部署工程师

The job is not mostly code: it is sitting at the customer's desk, agreeing what good means, and staying until people use the thing · ~15 min · concept这份工作大部分不是写代码，而是坐在客户的桌边、先谈好什么叫“好”、并一直陪到大家真的用起来 · 约 15 分钟 · 概念课

Step 1 · The first move is not code第 1 步 · 第一步不是写代码

Learn先学

A forward-deployed engineer (FDE) works inside the customer's business, turns a vague request like “use AI on claims” into a working system, and stays until people use it. You cannot build a vague request, so the first move is to watch the real job.

前线部署工程师（FDE）在客户的业务内部工作：把“理赔用 AI”这样含糊的需求变成能运转的系统，并一直陪到大家真的用起来。含糊的需求没法直接做，所以第一步是看清真实的工作。

What a claims handler does, one claim at a time: 1. read the customer's story 2. check the policy: is this covered? 3. decide: approve, decline, or refer (pass it to a senior handler) 4. stamp it, and move to the next queue number

Which of these is the right first move on Monday morning? Decide for each.

周一早上，下面哪些是对的第一步？逐个判断。

Step 2 · Write the job in one paragraph第 2 步 · 用一段话写下这份工作

Learn先学

Say four things: what goes in, what comes out, who signs it off, and what a mistake costs. If one is missing, nobody knows what “working” means.

写清四样：什么进来、什么出去、谁签字确认、出错的代价。少一样，就没人知道“能用”指什么。

In: a claim (customer's story, dates, receipts) and the policy it falls under. Out: approve, decline or refer, a payout amount, and a short reason. Signed off by: a claims handler for any large payout or any referral. A mistake costs: a wrong payout is money gone; a wrong decline is an angry customer.

Step 3 · Agree what “good” means第 3 步 · 先谈好什么叫“好”

Learn先学

Each owner means something different by “good”: the claims head counts accuracy, the handler counts fairness, IT counts risk. Settle all of them before building, or you win one definition and lose the rest. Early value comes from this brief, not clever code.

每位相关的人说的“好”都不同：理赔主管看准确率，理赔员看公平，IT 看风险。开工前把它们全部谈拢，否则赢了一种定义，却输了其余的。早期价值来自这份说明，而不是花哨的代码。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• An FDE builds inside the customer's world and stays until people use it.
• Watch the real process before writing code.
• Write the job in one paragraph: in, out, who signs off, what a mistake costs.
• Agree what “good” means with each owner before building.

• 前线部署工程师在客户的世界里做东西，并陪到大家真正用起来。
• 先观察真实的流程，再写代码。
• 用一段话写下工作：进、出、谁签字、出错的代价。
• 动手之前，先和每个相关的人谈好“好”是什么意思。
