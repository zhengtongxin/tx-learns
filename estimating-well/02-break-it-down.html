Making an estimate · Lesson 2做出估算 · 第 2 课

Break it down拆开来估

Split a task into small pieces and add them up, because small pieces are easier to guess and the sum shows what you forgot · ~10 min · reading and practice把任务拆成小块再相加，因为小块更好估，加起来还能看出你漏了什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Small pieces are easier to guess第 1 步 · 小块更好估

Learn先学

It is hard to guess how long “write the report” takes, because the task is a fog. It is easier to guess a piece you can picture: “list the main points” or “read it through once”. Break the task until each piece feels like something you have done before.

“写报告”要多久很难猜，因为这个任务是一团雾。能想象出来的小块就好估多了：“列出要点”或“通读一遍”。把任务拆到每一块都像是你以前做过的事。

Step 2 · Add the pieces up第 2 步 · 把各块加起来

Learn先学

Outline 1 hour, draft 3 hours, review 1 hour, format 1 hour. Together that is 6 hours. If your first gut feeling was “about 4 hours”, the sum shows it was low. The gap is not a failure; it is exactly what the exercise is for.

列提纲 1 小时，写初稿 3 小时，检查 1 小时，排版 1 小时。合起来是 6 小时。如果你的第一直觉是“大概 4 小时”，总和就说明它偏低了。这个差距不是失败，正是这个练习的用处。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Hunt for forgotten pieces第 3 步 · 找出被忘掉的块

Learn先学

The pieces we forget are usually the boring ones: waiting for someone’s reply, collecting the numbers, fixing what the review found. Ask “what comes before this and what comes after?” and add them. A list that only has the fun parts is too short.

被我们忘掉的往往是枯燥的块：等别人回复、收集数据、改掉检查时发现的问题。问问自己“这之前和之后还有什么？”然后加进去。只有好玩部分的清单，一定太短了。

Write the monthly report outline 1 h collect numbers (forgotten!) 1 h draft 3 h review 1 h format 1 h Total: 7 hours (gut feeling was 4)

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Small pieces are easier to guess.
• Add them up and compare with your gut.
• Look for the boring pieces you forgot.

• 小块更好估。
• 加起来，和直觉比一比。
• 找出你忘掉的枯燥的块。
