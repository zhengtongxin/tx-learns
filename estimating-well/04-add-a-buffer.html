Living with it · Lesson 1带着估算做事 · 第 1 课

Add a buffer加上缓冲

Add honest room for things that go wrong, sized to how much you do not know, and say that you did · ~10 min · reading and practice为意外留出诚实的余地，大小取决于你不知道多少，并且说明你这样做了 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Things go wrong a little each time第 1 步 · 事情总会出一点小岔子

Learn先学

Even a well-made estimate misses things: a slow day, a sick colleague, a file that will not open. These are not failures of planning; they are normal. A buffer is room for the ordinary surprises that you cannot name in advance.

再用心的估算也会漏掉一些事：慢的一天、同事生病、打不开的文件。这些不是计划的失败，而是常态。缓冲就是为那些事先说不出名字的普通意外留的余地。

Step 2 · Size it by how little you know第 2 步 · 按你知道得多少来定大小

Learn先学

A task you have done many times needs a small buffer, maybe 10 percent. A task that is new needs a big one, maybe half again as much. If your estimate is 6 hours and you add 20 percent, that is 6 times 1.2, which is 7.2 hours. Say “about 7 hours”.

做过很多次的任务只需要小缓冲，比如 10%。全新的任务需要大缓冲，也许要再加一半。如果你的估算是 6 小时，再加 20%，就是 6 乘以 1.2，等于 7.2 小时。说“大约 7 小时”。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Do not hide it第 3 步 · 别把它藏起来

Learn先学

If every piece gets a quiet extra hour, nobody can see where the room is, and the work grows to fill it. Keep one buffer at the end, and name it: “six hours of work plus one hour of room.” Then you can spend it on purpose, and tell people honestly when it is used up.

如果每一块都悄悄多加一小时，没人看得出余地在哪，工作也会膨胀着把它填满。在最后留一份缓冲并给它起名字：“六小时的工作，加一小时的余地。”这样你可以有意识地花掉它，用完了也能如实告诉别人。

Estimate: 6 hours of work Buffer: 20% = 1.2 hours (new kind of task) Promise: about 7 hours Rule: one named buffer at the end, bigger when I know less, never hidden inside the pieces

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ordinary surprises always arrive.
• Bigger buffer when you know less.
• One named buffer, not hidden padding.

• 普通的意外总会来。
• 知道得越少，缓冲越大。
• 一份有名字的缓冲，而不是暗藏的水分。
