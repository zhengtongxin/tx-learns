Step 1 · The first number is a starting point第 1 步 · 第一个数字只是起点

Learn先学

An estimate is made when you know the least. As you work you learn, so the number should change. Sticking to it because you said it first is pride, not planning. A good estimator treats the first number as the first draft.

估算是在你知道得最少的时候做出的。做的过程中你在学习，数字也应该变。因为第一个说出口就死守它，那是面子，不是计划。好的估算者把第一个数字当作初稿。

Step 2 · Use your real pace第 2 步 · 用你的真实速度

Learn先学

Suppose you have 10 tasks and after 2 days, 4 are done. That is 2 tasks a day. Six are left, so 6 divided by 2 is 3 more days. This forecast uses what actually happened, not what you hoped. Do the sum again at each checkpoint.

假设你有 10 项任务，2 天后做完了 4 项。这就是每天 2 项。还剩 6 项，6 除以 2 等于还要 3 天。这个预测用的是实际发生的，不是你希望的。每个检查点都重算一遍。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Say it early第 3 步 · 早点说

Learn先学

When the new forecast is later than promised, say so at once. Early news gives people time to choose: drop a piece, add help or move the date. The same news at the last minute leaves only bad options. Bring the new number, the reason and a suggestion.

新的预测比承诺的晚，就立刻说。早说，人们还有时间选择：砍掉一块、增加人手或挪日期。同样的消息到最后一刻才说，就只剩糟糕的选项。带上新的数字、原因和一个建议。

Plan: 10 tasks After 2 days: 4 done -> pace 2 per day Left: 6 tasks -> 6 / 2 = 3 more days Message: "At this pace we finish on Friday, not Thursday. Option: drop task 10, or add help."
