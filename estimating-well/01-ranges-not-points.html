Making an estimate · Lesson 1做出估算 · 第 1 课

Ranges, not points给范围，不给单点

Say an estimate as a range with a likely value, because a single number hides how unsure you are · ~10 min · reading and practice把估算说成带“最可能值”的范围，因为一个单独的数字会掩盖你有多不确定 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One number hides the doubt第 1 步 · 一个数字藏起了疑虑

Learn先学

When someone asks “how long will it take?” and you say “three hours”, they hear a promise. But you were never sure. A range keeps the doubt visible: “two hours if it goes well, three most likely, six if something breaks.” The person asking can then plan around what matters to them.

有人问“要多久？”你说“三小时”，对方听到的是一个承诺。但你其实并不确定。范围能让不确定性看得见：“顺利两小时，最可能三小时，出了岔子要六小时。”提问的人就能按自己在意的事去安排。

Step 2 · The worst case is further away第 2 步 · 最坏情况离得更远

Learn先学

Look at the three numbers. Best is one hour under likely; worst is three hours over. This is normal: things can only go so fast, but they can go wrong without limit. If your range looks balanced, check that you have really thought about what could go wrong.

看这三个数字：最好比最可能少一小时，最坏却多三小时。这很正常：事情快也快不到哪去，但出岔子可以没有上限。如果你的范围看起来很对称，就检查一下，自己是不是真的想过哪里会出问题。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Choose what to commit to第 3 步 · 选出要承诺的数字

Learn先学

A range is not a way to avoid answering. After you give it, say which number you will commit to, and why. If a missed deadline would be costly, commit near the worst case. If it hardly matters, commit near the likely one. The choice is yours to make openly.

给出范围并不是回避回答。给完之后，说明你承诺哪个数字，为什么。如果错过期限代价很大，就承诺在接近最坏的数字上；如果无所谓，就承诺在最可能的数字上。这个选择应当公开地做出。

Task: write the monthly report Best case: 2 hours Likely: 3 hours Worst case: 6 hours I commit to: 5 hours, because the report goes to my manager at noon.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Give a range, not a single number.
• The worst case is usually further away.
• Say which number you commit to, and why.

• 给范围，不给单个数字。
• 最坏情况通常离得更远。
• 说明你承诺哪个数字，为什么。
