Checking your estimate核对你的估算

Test an estimate with a second method and a size check before you share it · ~9 min · reading and practice在分享估算之前，用第二种方法和量级核对来检验它 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Estimate a second way第 1 步 · 换一种方法再估一次

Learn先学

Once you have an estimate, test it with a second, different method. If two quite different routes land near each other, you can trust the estimate more. If they are far apart, one of them is missing something, and that is worth finding before you commit.

有了估算之后，用第二种不同的方法再估一次。如果两条很不一样的路径得出的数字相近，你就更可以相信这个估算；如果相差很远，说明其中一个漏了什么，在承诺之前值得先找出来。

Step 2 · A worked check第 2 步 · 一个核对的例子

Learn先学

Say you must answer 40 emails. Bottom-up: each takes about 3 minutes, so 40 × 3 = 120 minutes, which is 2 hours. Top-down: last month a batch of 50 similar emails took 2.5 hours, which is 150 minutes, or 3 minutes each, so 40 would take about 2 hours too. Two methods, one answer: you can say 2 hours with more confidence.

假设你要回 40 封邮件。自下而上：每封约 3 分钟，40 × 3 = 120 分钟，也就是 2 小时。自上而下：上个月一批 50 封类似的邮件用了 2.5 小时，也就是 150 分钟，每封 3 分钟，所以 40 封也约 2 小时。两种方法，一个答案：你可以更有把握地说 2 小时。

Step 3 · Check the size第 3 步 · 核对数量级

Learn先学

Also ask a plain question: does this number make sense in size? A sum that comes out ten times too big or too small usually means a slipped unit or a misplaced zero, such as minutes mixed up with hours. If the answer surprises you, check the steps before you share it, and say how you checked.

再问一个朴素的问题：这个数字在量级上说得通吗？结果大了或小了十倍，通常是单位弄错或小数点错位，比如把分钟和小时混了。如果答案让你吃惊，先检查各步，再把它讲出去，并说明你是怎么核对的。

Sort these habits as good or weak: tap a habit, then tap its group.

把这些做法分为好或弱：先点一个做法，再点它所属的组。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Test an estimate with a second, different method.
• Two methods that agree raise your confidence; a big gap means something is missing.
• In the example, 40 × 3 = 120 minutes, which is 2 hours, and the top-down check also gives about 2 hours.
• Ask whether the size makes sense and check for slipped units.
• Say how you checked when you share the estimate.

• 用第二种不同的方法检验估算。
• 两种方法吻合会提高信心；差距很大说明漏了什么。
• 例子里 40 × 3 = 120 分钟，也就是 2 小时，自上而下的核对也得到约 2 小时。
• 问一问量级是否说得通，并检查有没有单位弄错。
• 分享估算时说明你是怎么核对的。
