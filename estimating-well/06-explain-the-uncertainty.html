Explain the uncertainty把不确定性讲清楚

Share an estimate so other people can use it: the likely value, the range, what it depends on and what would change it · ~10 min · reading and practice把估算讲得让别人能用：最可能值、范围、它依赖什么、什么会让它变 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Likely value first, then range第 1 步 · 先说最可能值，再说范围

Learn先学

Start with the number people can use, then give the range: “Most likely 6 hours, between 5 and 9.” If you start with the range, listeners pick whichever end suits them. If you give only the number, they treat it as a promise. Both together tell the truth.

先说大家能用的数字，再给范围：“最可能 6 小时，在 5 到 9 小时之间。”如果先说范围，听的人会挑对自己有利的一端。如果只说一个数字，他们会当成承诺。两个一起说才是实话。

Step 2 · Name the assumptions第 2 步 · 说出假设

Learn先学

Every estimate stands on assumptions: “if the data arrives by Tuesday”, “if one person reviews it”, “if the tool works”. Say them out loud. Then, when one fails, nobody is surprised that the estimate moved, and you are not blamed for a number built on something untrue.

每个估算都建立在假设之上：“如果周二前数据到位”“如果有一个人审阅”“如果工具能用”。把它们说出来。这样当某个假设落空，没人会对估算变化感到意外，你也不会因为一个建立在失实前提上的数字被责怪。

Sort each one: good practice or weak practice?

给每一项分类：是好做法，还是薄弱的做法？

Step 3 · Say what would change it第 3 步 · 说出什么会让它改变

Learn先学

Finish with the triggers: “If the data is late, add a day. If we skip the review, take away an hour but accept more risk.” This turns your estimate into a tool others can adjust, and it shows you have thought about it, which builds trust.

最后说明触发条件：“如果数据晚到，加一天。如果跳过审阅，减一小时，但风险更高。”这让你的估算变成别人也能调整的工具，也显示你认真想过，这会建立信任。

Estimate for the monthly report Most likely: 6 hours (range 5 to 9) Assumes: data arrives by Tuesday; one person reviews Changes if: data is late (+1 day); no review (-1 hour, more risk) Commitment: Thursday noon

Takeaways要点回顾

• Likely value first, then the range.
• Name the assumptions out loud.
• Say what would change the estimate.

• 先说最可能值，再说范围。
• 把假设说出来。
• 说出什么会改变估算。
