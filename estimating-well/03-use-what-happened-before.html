Making an estimate · Lesson 3做出估算 · 第 3 课

Use what happened before参考过去的经验

Base an estimate on how long similar work really took, because memory flatters and records do not · ~10 min · reading and practice让估算建立在类似工作真实花了多久之上，因为记忆会美化，记录不会 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Memory flatters第 1 步 · 记忆会美化

Learn先学

We remember the good days. A task you finished in an afternoon feels typical, even if two similar ones took a whole day. When you estimate from feeling, you tend to estimate from your best day. Real records are plainer and more honest.

我们记得的是顺利的日子。一个下午做完的任务让人觉得很典型，哪怕有两件类似的花了整整一天。凭感觉估时，往往是按你最好的一天来估。真实的记录更平实，也更诚实。

Step 2 · Compare with similar jobs第 2 步 · 和类似的活比较

Learn先学

Find two or three jobs like this one. Say the last three similar reports took 5, 7 and 6 hours. The average is 6 hours (18 divided by 3). That is a better starting point than a fresh guess, and the spread from 5 to 7 gives you a natural range.

找两三件和这次类似的活。比如最近三份类似的报告分别用了 5、7、6 小时，平均是 6 小时（18 除以 3）。这比重新猜一个数字是更好的起点，5 到 7 的跨度还直接给了你一个范围。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Keep a tiny log第 3 步 · 记一份很小的记录

Learn先学

After a task, write one line: what you estimated and what it really took. After a few weeks, you will see your own pattern: maybe you are always 30 percent low, or always low on tasks with other people. That pattern is worth more than any rule of thumb.

做完一件事，写一行：你估了多少，实际用了多少。几周之后你会看到自己的规律：也许你总是少估三成，也许涉及别人的任务总是估少了。这个规律比任何经验法则都有价值。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Memory flatters; records do not.
• Average similar past jobs.
• Log estimate against actual.

• 记忆会美化，记录不会。
• 平均类似的过往工作。
• 记录估的和实际的。
