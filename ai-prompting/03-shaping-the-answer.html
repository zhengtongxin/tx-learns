Asking clearly · Lesson 3把话说清楚 · 第 3 课

Shaping the answer塑造答案的样子

Ask for the format, length, tone and level you need so the answer is ready to use · ~10 min · reading and practice指定你需要的格式、长度、语气和难度，让答案拿来就能用 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Say the format第 1 步 · 说明格式

Learn先学

Think about where the answer will go, then ask for that shape: a table for a spreadsheet, a numbered list for steps, three short paragraphs for an email. Asking up front saves a round of reformatting.

先想答案要用在哪里，再要那种样子：要贴进表格就要表格，要步骤就要编号清单，要写邮件就要三个短段落。事先说好，能省掉一轮重新排版。

Give a length too, in words, sentences or bullets. “Short” means different things to different people.

也给个长度，用字数、句数或要点数。“简短”对不同的人意思不一样。

Same content, asked two ways: "List the pros and cons of renting vs buying." -> paragraphs of prose "Give a two-column table: pros and cons of renting vs buying, max 4 rows (no more than four lines in the table)." -> a table you can paste into a sheet

Step 2 · Set the tone and level第 2 步 · 设定语气和水平

Learn先学

Tone and reader level are easy to set and make a large difference. Name the reader (“a first-time customer”, “my manager”, “a 12-year-old”) and the tone (friendly, formal, direct).

语气和读者水平很容易设定，影响却很大。写明读者（“第一次来的客人”“我的经理”“12 岁的孩子”）和语气（亲切、正式、直接）。

Match each complaint about an answer to the fix.

把每条对答案的抱怨配上对应的改法。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Say what you want instead第 3 步 · 说想要什么

Learn先学

“Do not be boring” and “avoid jargon” (special technical words) tell the assistant what to skip, but not what to do. Often it works better to say the thing you want: “use plain words a teenager would know”.

“别无聊”“避开术语”（专业的技术词）只告诉助手要跳过什么，没说要做什么。往往直接说你想要的更有效：“用青少年也懂的大白话”。

A short “avoid” list is still useful for hard limits, such as “do not promise a delivery date”.

对硬性限制，一份简短的“避免”清单仍然有用，比如“不要承诺送达日期”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask for the shape where the answer will be used.
• Give a length in words, sentences or bullets.
• Name the reader and the tone.
• Say what you want; keep “avoid” for hard limits.

• 按答案要去的地方来要求形状。
• 用字数、句数或要点数给出长度。
• 写明读者和语气。
• 说你想要什么；“避免”留给硬性限制。
