Standing instructions长期指令

Write a few short standing rules for how an assistant works with you, test that they are followed, and remove clashes · ~9 min · reading and practice写几条简短的长期规则说明助手怎样配合你，测试它们是否被遵守，并去掉冲突 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Instructions that stay第 1 步 · 长期指令

Learn先学

Some things you ask for every time: a friendly tone, short answers, plain words. Instead of repeating them, put them in standing instructions: a few lines you paste at the start of a chat, or save in a setting if your assistant has one. They describe how you want it to work with you, not one task.

有些要求你每次都会提：语气友好、回答简短、用词平实。与其每次重复，不如写成长期指令：几行话，在对话开头粘贴，或者在助手有相关设置时保存下来。它们说明你希望助手怎样配合你，而不是某一个任务。

Step 2 · Keeping them short第 2 步 · 保持简短

Learn先学

Fewer rules are followed better. Aim for 3 to 5 clear rules, each one sentence, for example “Answer in plain words”, “Keep answers under 120 words unless I ask for more”, “Say when you are not sure”. Test them on 5 requests. If the assistant follows the rules in 4 of 5 answers, that is 4 ÷ 5 = 80%. Rewrite the rule it missed, instead of adding a second rule beside it.

规则越少，遵守得越好。目标是 3 到 5 条清楚的规则，每条一句话，例如“用平实的话回答”“除非我要求更多，回答不超过 120 字”“不确定时要说出来”。用 5 个请求测试。如果助手在 5 个回答中有 4 个遵守了规则，就是 4 ÷ 5 = 80%。把没做到的那条重写，而不是在旁边再加一条。

Step 3 · Keeping them honest第 3 步 · 让它们保持准确

Learn先学

Rules can clash. “Be very brief” and “explain every step” pull in opposite directions, so the assistant will pick one. Read your rules together and remove clashes. Review them now and then, because your needs change. A standing instruction never replaces checking: the assistant can still be wrong.

规则之间可能冲突。“要非常简短”和“解释每一步”方向相反，助手只能选一个。把你的规则放在一起读一遍，去掉冲突。也要时常回头检查，因为你的需求会变。长期指令也不能代替核对：助手仍然可能出错。

Sort each line as a good standing instruction or a weak one.

把每一行分为“好的长期指令”或“弱的长期指令”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Standing instructions say how you want the assistant to work every time, not one task.
• Keep 3 to 5 clear rules, one sentence each.
• Test them: 4 of 5 answers following the rules is 80%.
• Remove rules that clash, and review them now and then.
• They never replace checking the answer.

• 长期指令说明你希望助手每次怎样配合，而不是某一个任务。
• 保留 3 到 5 条清楚的规则，每条一句话。
• 测试：5 个回答中有 4 个遵守规则，就是 80%。
• 去掉冲突的规则，并时常回头检查。
• 它们不能代替核对答案。
