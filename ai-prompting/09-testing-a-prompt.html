Testing a prompt测试提示词

Test a prompt on several examples against a written standard, compare two prompts fairly, and keep notes on what changed · ~9 min · reading and practice按事先写好的标准用多个例子测试提示词，公平地比较两个提示词，并记下改动的内容 · 约 9 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Why test a prompt第 1 步 · 为什么要测试提示词

Learn先学

A prompt that worked once may not work next time. To know whether a prompt is good, test it: run it on several real examples and judge each answer against a clear standard you wrote down first, such as “correct, under 100 words, no invented facts”. One lucky answer proves little; a pattern across examples tells you something.

只成功过一次的提示词，下次未必还行。要知道一个提示词好不好，就要测试：用几个真实的例子运行它，并按你事先写好的明确标准逐个判断答案，例如“正确、少于 100 字、没有编造事实”。一次碰巧的好答案说明不了什么；多个例子上的规律才能说明问题。

Step 2 · Comparing two prompts第 2 步 · 比较两个提示词

Learn先学

Change one thing at a time. Say prompt A and prompt B differ only in that B includes a sample question and answer inside the prompt. Run both on the same 10 questions. If A gives 6 good answers and B gives 9, then A scores 6 ÷ 10 = 60% and B scores 9 ÷ 10 = 90%, a gap of 30 percentage points. That favours B. With only 10 questions a small gap, say 1 answer, could be luck, so a gap of 1 or 2 is not enough to decide: run more questions or repeat the test first.

一次只改一处。假设提示词 A 和 B 的区别只是 B 带了一个示例。让两者回答同样的 10 个问题。如果 A 得到 6 个好答案，B 得到 9 个，那么 A 得 6 ÷ 10 = 60%，B 得 9 ÷ 10 = 90%，差了 30 个百分点，这支持 B。只有 10 个问题时，差一两个答案可能只是运气，所以差 1 或 2 个还不足以下结论，应先多测几个问题或重复测试。

Step 3 · Keeping what you learn第 3 步 · 记下学到的东西

Learn先学

Save each prompt as a numbered copy (v1, v2). Write down the version, what you changed and the score, for example “B: added one example, 9 of 10”. Test again when the task, the examples or the assistant changes, because a prompt that scored well once can slip. Include a few hard or odd examples in your list of test questions, not only easy ones.

把每个提示词存成带编号的副本（v1、v2）。写下版本、改了什么以及得分，例如“B：加了一个示例，10 个中 9 个”。当任务、示例或助手发生变化时再测试，因为曾经得分很高的提示词也可能退步。测试集里要包含几个难的或古怪的例子，而不只是容易的。

Sort each habit as a good test or a weak test.

把每个做法分为“好的测试”或“弱的测试”。

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Test a prompt on several real examples against a standard written first.
• One good answer can be luck; a pattern across examples means more.
• 6 of 10 versus 9 of 10 is 60% versus 90%, a 30-point gap.
• A gap of one or two answers on ten is not enough to decide.
• Change one thing at a time and note the version and the score.

• 用多个真实例子，按事先写好的标准测试提示词。
• 一个好答案可能是运气；多个例子上的规律更有说服力。
• 10 个中 6 个对 9 个，是 60% 对 90%，差 30 个点。
• 十个里差一两个答案，还不足以下结论。
• 一次只改一处，并记下版本和得分。
