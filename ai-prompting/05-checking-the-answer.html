Checking the answer核对答案

Know why fluent answers can be wrong, which claims to check, and how to reduce made-up facts · ~10 min · reading and practice知道为什么流畅的答案也可能出错、该核对哪些说法，以及怎样减少编造的事实 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Fluent is not true第 1 步 · 流畅不等于真实

Learn先学

An assistant writes the most likely next words. That makes its sentences smooth, whether or not the content is right. It can state a wrong date, a number, a quote or a web link with the same calm confidence as a right one. Such invented details are often called hallucinations.

助手写的是最可能出现的下一个词，所以句子读起来很顺，不管内容对不对。它说一个错的日期、数字、引文或网址，和说对的一样平静自信。这类编造出来的细节常被叫作幻觉。

So the smoothness of an answer tells you nothing about its accuracy.

所以，答案读起来是否顺畅，完全说明不了它准不准。

Step 2 · Check what matters第 2 步 · 核对要紧的部分

Learn先学

You cannot check everything, so check by risk. Names, numbers, dates, quotes, links, laws and anything you will send to others deserve a check against a source you trust. A list of brainstorm ideas or a friendly tone needs far less.

你不可能核对一切，所以按风险来核对。人名、数字、日期、引文、链接、法规，以及你要发给别人的内容，都值得对照可信来源核实。头脑风暴的点子、友好的语气，则不需要那么多。

If the assistant names a source, open it yourself: a study title or a link can be invented.

如果助手点出了出处，要自己去打开看：论文标题或链接都可能是编的。

Sort each claim.

给每条说法分类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Give it the text to use第 3 步 · 把要用的文字给它

Learn先学

A good way to cut made-up facts is to supply the source text yourself and tell the assistant to answer only from it, and to say so when the text does not contain the answer.

减少编造事实的好办法，是自己提供原文，并要求助手只根据它作答，原文里没有答案时就直说。

Better still, ask it to quote the line it used, so that you can check in seconds.

更好的是让它引出所用的那一句，你几秒钟就能核对。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Fluent does not mean true.
• Check by risk: names, numbers, dates, quotes, links, rules.
• A cited source can be invented; open it yourself.
• Supply the text and ask for answers only from it.

• 流畅不等于真实。
• 按风险核对：人名、数字、日期、引文、链接、规定。
• 引用的出处可能是编的，自己打开看。
• 自己提供原文，要求只根据它作答。
