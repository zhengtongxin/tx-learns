Asking clearly · Lesson 2把话说清楚 · 第 2 课

Context and examples背景与示例

Give the assistant the background it cannot guess, and show one example of what you want · ~10 min · reading and practice把助手猜不到的背景告诉它，再给一个你想要的样子的示例 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Give the background第 1 步 · 把背景交代清楚

Learn先学

The assistant starts every chat knowing nothing about you. Tell it who the answer is for, what you are trying to achieve and any facts that change the answer, such as a budget or a deadline.

每次对话开始，助手对你一无所知。告诉它答案是给谁的、你想达成什么，以及会改变答案的事实，比如预算或截止日期。

More is not always better. Include what would change the answer (its content or its tone), and leave out the rest.

不是越多越好。写进会改变答案的内容（内容或语气都算），其余的不写。

A customer asks for a refund. Decide what belongs in the prompt.

一位客户要求退款。决定哪些内容该写进提示词。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 2 · Show one example第 2 步 · 给一个示例

Learn先学

Describing a style in words is slow and easy to misread. Showing one or two examples of the input and the output you want is faster, because the assistant copies the pattern. This is often called few-shot prompting: “few” means a few examples.

用文字描述风格既慢又容易被误读。给一两个“输入和想要的输出”的示例更快，因为助手会照着样子来。这常被称为少样本提示，意思就是给少量几个示例。

Pick examples that look like the real cases, and say that the new input should follow the same pattern, for example: "Follow the same pattern as the examples above."

选和真实情况相似的示例，并说明新的输入要照同样的样子处理，比如：“照上面示例的样子来处理。”

Paste the box below into an AI assistant and replace <your message here> with a message of your own; the assistant should write the label (its short answer, such as Order) after the last arrow. In the box, an arrow marks where the answer goes.

把下面的框粘贴到 AI 助手里，把 <your message here> 换成你自己的一条消息；助手应在最后一个箭头后写出标签（它的简短答案，比如“订单”）。框里的箭头标出答案该放的位置。

Label each message as Order, Complaint or Other. Message: "Where is my parcel?" -> Order Message: "The soup was cold." -> Complaint Message: "<your message here>" ->

Step 3 · Mark what is what第 3 步 · 标清楚哪是哪

Learn先学

When you paste an email or an article into a prompt, put your instructions first and wrap the pasted text in clear marks, such as “Email:” and a line of dashes. Otherwise the assistant may treat part of the pasted text as an instruction.

当你把邮件或文章粘进提示词时，先写指令，再用清楚的标记把粘贴的文字围起来，比如“邮件：”加一行横线。否则助手可能把粘贴文字里的某句话当成指令。

It also keeps your prompt easy to reuse: swap the pasted text and run it again.

这也让你的提示词便于复用：换掉粘贴的文字，再跑一次。

Summarise the email below in two sentences for my manager. Email: ---------- Hi, the supplier moved delivery to Friday. Please confirm. ----------

Step 4 · Test your examples on a small batch第 4 步 · 用一小批数据测试你的示例

Learn先学

Do not assume examples helped. Test them on ten sample messages (a small batch). Each message gets a label: the short answer the assistant gives for it, such as Order or Complaint. Use messages that are not the ones in your examples.

1. Write ten messages yourself, or copy ten real ones with private details removed. Then write the correct label for each one. This is your answer key.
2. In a new chat (it has none of your earlier examples, so the test is fair), state the three labels, paste the ten messages as a numbered list without examples and ask for one label per number.
3. In another new chat, do the same with your examples included (for example the two from the Step 2 box). Keep the label list and the ten messages exactly the same: only the examples differ.
4. Count the labels that match your key in each chat. The gap tells you whether the examples earned their place.

Ten is a small batch, so a gap of one or two messages is a hint, not proof. A gap as large as the one below is a stronger hint, but run a few more batches before relying on it. The numbers below are made up for the sums.

别想当然地认为示例有用，要测试它。准备十条样本消息（一小批），每条消息得到一个标签：助手给它的简短答案，比如“订单”或“投诉”。用的消息不要是示例里的那几条。

1. 自己写十条消息，或者拿十条真实的并去掉隐私细节，再自己写下每条的正确标签，这就是你的标准答案。
2. 在一个新对话里（里面没有你之前的示例，测试才公平），先说明三个标签，再把十条消息编号贴进去，不带示例，要求每个编号给一个标签。
3. 在另一个新对话里，带上示例再做一遍（比如第 2 步框里的那两个）。标签列表和十条消息要完全一样，只有示例不同。
4. 数一数每个对话里和标准答案一致的标签。差距告诉你示例值不值得保留。

十条是很小的一批，所以差一两条只是线索，不是证明；像下面这样大的差距，线索就强一些，但依赖它之前最好再多测几批。下面的数字是为了算账编的。

Same 10 messages, labelled Order, Complaint or Other. Without examples: 5 right out of 10 = 50% With two examples: 8 right out of 10 = 80% Gap: 80% - 50% = 30 percentage points (the difference of the two percentages, not 30% of anything)

Takeaways要点回顾

• The assistant knows nothing about you until you say it.
• Include the facts that change the answer; leave out the rest.
• One or two examples teach a pattern faster than a description.
• Mark pasted text so it is not mistaken for instructions.
• Test examples on a small batch against your own answer key, and count the gap, not just the feel.

• 你不说，助手对你一无所知。
• 写进会改变答案的事实，其余不写。
• 一两个示例比描述更快地教会它一个样子。
• 把粘贴的文字标出来，避免被当成指令。
• 用小批数据对照你自己的标准答案测试示例，数出差距，而不是凭感觉。
