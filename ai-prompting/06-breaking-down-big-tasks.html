Breaking down big tasks拆解大任务

Split a large job into steps, ask for step by step thinking when it matters, and use roles with care · ~10 min · reading and practice把大任务拆成步骤，需要时要求一步步推理，并谨慎使用角色设定 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One job at a time第 1 步 · 一次一件事

Learn先学

A huge prompt that asks for research, a plan, a draft and a polish at once often does each part badly. Split it into a chain, like the box below: list what must be decided, weigh the options, pick, draft, check against your limits, then tidy it up. You can fix a weak step before it spoils the rest.

一个巨大的提示词，要求同时做调研、计划、起草和润色，往往每一样都做不好。把它拆成一串，像下面的框那样：先列出要决定的事，比较选项，做选择，起草，对照你的限制检查，最后整理。弱的那一步可以在毁掉后面之前先修好。

Send the lines one at a time in the same chat: send line 1, read the reply, then send line 2, pasting in only what you need from the reply, such as only the options for the one decision you are working on. Replace “(my choices)” in line 3 with your own picks from line 2’s options.

一行一行地发，都在同一个对话里：先发第 1 行，读完回复，再发第 2 行，只把需要的内容从回复里贴过去，比如只贴你正在处理的那一项决定的选项。第 3 行里的“(my choices)”要换成你从第 2 行选项里做的选择。

1. List the five things we must decide for a 3-day trip to Penang. 2. For each, give two options with one pro and one con. 3. I pick: (my choices). Draft a day-by-day plan. 4. Check the plan against my budget of SGD 800. 5. Turn it into a one-page checklist.

Step 2 · Ask for the working第 2 步 · 要求写出推理

Learn先学

For maths, logic and planning, ask the assistant to work step by step and to show its reasoning (the working: the steps it took) before the final answer. Writing out the steps often catches slips and lets you see where it went wrong.

对于数学、逻辑和计划类问题，要求助手一步步推理，并在给出最终答案之前展示推理过程（推理，也就是它走过的步骤）。把步骤写出来，往往能避免疏漏，也让你看出错在哪里。

Still check the steps. A neat-looking chain of reasoning can contain a wrong step.

但仍然要检查每一步。一串看起来整齐的推理里，也可能藏着错的一步。

Step 3 · Roles, with care第 3 步 · 谨慎使用角色

Learn先学

“Act as a careful editor” sets a viewpoint and a level (how simple or how expert the answer is), and often improves the style of the answer. It does not add knowledge the assistant lacks, and it does not make the content more accurate.

“扮演一位仔细的编辑”设定了视角和水平（回答是浅显还是专业），常能改善答案的风格。它不会增加助手缺的知识，也不会让内容更准确。

Sort these uses of a role by one test: does it improve the viewpoint or style, or does it claim to add facts?

用一个标准给这些角色设定的用法分类：它是改善视角或风格，还是声称能增加事实？

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 4 · Catch a slip in the working第 4 步 · 从推理里抓出错误

Learn先学

Showing the working pays off when you read it. Say you ask whether a Penang trip fits a budget of SGD 800 (Singapore dollars) and the assistant replies as below. The assistant says the total is 780. Add the four lines yourself: what do you get? A confident "within budget" can still be wrong. Without the steps you would only see the verdict. A good follow-up is: "Check each line and add the total again, showing the sum."

这些步骤只有你自己去读，才能帮上忙。假设你问槟城之行是否在 800 新元（新加坡元）之内，助手如下回答。助手说总数是 780。自己把四行加一遍，你得到多少？一句自信的“在预算内”也可能是错的。没有这些步骤，你只会看到结论。好的追问是：“逐行检查，并把总数重新加一遍，写出算式。”

Hotel: 2 nights x SGD 180 = SGD 360 Food: 3 days x SGD 60 = SGD 180 Transport: SGD 120 Activities: SGD 150 Total: SGD 780, within the SGD 800 budget.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Split big jobs into a chain of small steps.
• Paste each step’s reply into the next.
• Ask for step by step working, then check the steps.
• A role sets viewpoint and style, not facts.

• 把大任务拆成一串小步骤。
• 把每一步的回复贴进下一步。
• 要求一步步推理，然后检查每一步。
• 角色设定视角和风格，不增加事实。
