Breaking down big tasks拆解大任务

Split a large job into steps, ask for step by step thinking when it matters, and use roles with care · ~10 min · reading and practice把大任务拆成步骤，需要时要求一步步推理，并谨慎使用角色设定 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · One job at a time第 1 步 · 一次一件事

Learn先学

A huge prompt that asks for research, a plan, a draft and a polish at once often does each part badly. Split it into a chain: outline first, check the outline, then draft one section at a time, then edit. You can fix a weak step before it spoils the rest.

一个巨大的提示词，要求同时做调研、计划、起草和润色，往往每一样都做不好。把它拆成一串：先出大纲，检查大纲，再一节一节起草，最后修改。弱的那一步可以在毁掉后面之前先修好。

Each step can be a follow-up in the same chat, and paste the reply from one step into the next.

每一步都可以是同一个对话里的追问，把上一步的回复贴进下一步。

1. List the five things we must decide for a 3-day trip to Penang. 2. For each, give two options with one pro and one con. 3. I pick: (my choices). Draft a day-by-day plan. 4. Check the plan against my budget of SGD 800. 5. Turn it into a one-page checklist.

Step 2 · Ask for the working第 2 步 · 要求写出推理

Learn先学

For maths, logic and planning, ask the assistant to work step by step and to show its reasoning before the final answer. Writing out the steps often catches slips and lets you see where it went wrong.

对于数学、逻辑和计划类问题，要求助手一步步推理，并在给出最终答案之前展示推理过程。把步骤写出来，往往能避免疏漏，也让你看出错在哪里。

Still check the steps. A neat-looking chain of reasoning can contain a wrong step.

但仍然要检查每一步。一串看起来整齐的推理里，也可能藏着错的一步。

Step 3 · Roles, with care第 3 步 · 谨慎使用角色

Learn先学

“Act as a careful editor” sets a viewpoint and a level, and often improves the style of the answer. It does not add knowledge the assistant lacks, and it does not make the content more accurate.

“扮演一位仔细的编辑”设定了视角和水平，常能改善答案的风格。它不会增加助手缺的知识，也不会让内容更准确。

Sort these uses of a role.

给这些角色设定的用法分类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Split big jobs into a chain of small steps.
• Paste each step’s reply into the next.
• Ask for step by step working, then check the steps.
• A role sets viewpoint and style, not facts.

• 把大任务拆成一串小步骤。
• 把每一步的回复贴进下一步。
• 要求一步步推理，然后检查每一步。
• 角色设定视角和风格，不增加事实。
