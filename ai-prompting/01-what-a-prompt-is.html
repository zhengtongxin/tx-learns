What a prompt is什么是提示词

See why a vague request gets a vague answer, and the four parts of a clear one · ~10 min · reading and practice看清为什么含糊的请求只会得到含糊的答案，以及清晰请求的四个部分 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A request plus what it needs第 1 步 · 请求，加上它需要的信息

Learn先学

A prompt is whatever you type to an AI assistant. The assistant writes a likely, helpful reply from those words alone. It cannot see your office, your reader or your plans, so anything you leave out, it fills in with a safe, generic guess.

提示词就是你输入给 AI 助手的内容。助手只凭这些字，写出一个可能有用的回答。它看不到你的办公室、你的读者或你的打算，所以你没写的部分，它会用安全而笼统的猜测补上。

That is why a short, vague request gets a short, vague answer. The fix is not magic words. It is saying what you would tell a new colleague who has never met you.

这就是为什么简短含糊的请求，只会得到简短含糊的答案。解决办法不是什么咒语，而是把你会对一位刚来、从没见过你的同事说的话说出来。

Vague: Write about exercise. Clear: Write a 100-word note for office workers in Singapore. Friendly tone. Topic: stretching at the desk. Give three tips as a short list.

Step 2 · Task, context, format, limits第 2 步 · 任务、背景、格式、限制

Learn先学

A clear prompt has up to four parts. The task is what you want done. The context is who it is for and what the assistant should know. The format is the shape of the answer. The limits are length and anything to avoid.

清晰的提示词最多有四部分。任务是要做什么；背景是写给谁、助手该知道什么；格式是答案的样子；限制是长度和要避开的东西。

You do not need all four every time. Add the part that the first answer was missing.

不用每次都写全四部分。第一次的答案缺哪一部分，就补哪一部分。

Sort each line of a prompt into its part.

把提示词里的每一句归到对应的部分。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · The first answer is a draft第 3 步 · 第一个答案只是草稿

Learn先学

Ask the same question twice and the wording will differ. That is normal: the assistant picks likely words, not stored sentences. So treat the first answer as a draft, not a verdict.

同一个问题问两次，措辞会不同。这很正常：助手选的是可能的用词，而不是存好的句子。所以把第一个答案当作草稿，不是定论。

If it is close, say what to change. If it is far off, the prompt was probably missing a part.

如果接近了，就说要改什么；如果差得远，多半是提示词缺了某一部分。

Takeaways要点回顾

• A prompt is a request plus what the assistant needs to do it well.
• The four parts: task, context, format, limits.
• A vague prompt gets a generic answer.
• The first answer is a draft, not a verdict.

• 提示词是一条请求，加上助手做好它所需要的信息。
• 四个部分：任务、背景、格式、限制。
• 含糊的提示词只得到笼统的答案。
• 第一个答案是草稿，不是定论。
