Improving by follow-up用追问改进

Steer a draft with small follow-ups, let the assistant ask you questions, and know when to start fresh · ~10 min · reading and practice用小幅追问调整草稿，让助手反过来问你，并知道何时重开一个对话 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Change one thing at a time第 1 步 · 一次只改一件事

Learn先学

A follow-up keeps everything the assistant already knows from the chat, so it is cheaper than starting over. Make it specific, and change one thing: “Shorter, and keep point two.” If you change five things at once, you cannot tell which change helped.

追问会保留助手在这次对话里已经知道的一切，比重来省事。要具体，而且只改一件事：“更短一点，保留第二点。”一次改五件事，你就分不清是哪个改动起了作用。

Say what to keep as well as what to change, or a good part can disappear.

既要说改什么，也要说保留什么，否则好的部分可能跟着消失。

You: Write a 3-sentence invitation to the team lunch. AI: (a formal draft) You: Warmer, like a friend. Keep the date and place. AI: (a warmer draft) You: Add that vegetarian options are available. AI: (final)

Step 2 · Let it ask you第 2 步 · 让它问你

Learn先学

When your goal is fuzzy, flip the roles: “Before you answer, ask me up to three questions that would help you do this well.” The assistant then asks for the background it lacks, and you answer once instead of guessing what to write.

当你的目标还模糊时，把角色反过来：“回答之前，先问我最多三个有助于你做好这件事的问题。”助手就会问出它缺的背景，你一次回答，不必猜该写什么。

This works well for plans, letters and decisions, where the right answer depends on details only you know.

这对计划、信件和决定特别有用，因为正确答案取决于只有你知道的细节。

Step 3 · Know when to start fresh第 3 步 · 知道何时重开

Learn先学

Long chats drift. The assistant rereads the whole chat before every reply, so in a very long chat early details can get lost, and old instructions can fight new ones. If the answers get worse, or you change topic, open a new chat and paste a short summary of what matters.

长对话会跑偏。助手每次回复前都会重读整个对话，所以对话很长时，早先的细节可能被忽略，旧指令还可能和新指令打架。如果答案变差了，或者你换了话题，就开一个新对话，把要紧的内容用一小段话贴进去。

Decide which situation each one is.

判断下面每种情况属于哪一类。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Takeaways要点回顾

• Follow up with one specific change at a time.
• Say what to keep as well as what to change.
• When the goal is fuzzy, let the assistant ask you questions.
• When a chat drifts, start fresh with a short summary.

• 每次追问只做一个具体改动。
• 既说改什么，也说留什么。
• 目标模糊时，让助手向你提问。
• 对话跑偏了，就带着简短摘要重开。
