Getting it right · Lesson 2把数算对 · 第 2 课

Estimate first先估算

Make a quick rough estimate before the exact sum, so a slip shows up straight away · ~10 min · reading and practice在精确计算之前先快速粗估，这样有失误能马上看出来 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Round to easy numbers第 1 步 · 取成容易算的数

Learn先学

To estimate 49 × 21, round to numbers that are easy to multiply: 50 × 20 = 1,000. That took seconds. The exact answer is 49 × 20 + 49 = 980 + 49 = 1,029, because 21 is 20 + 1, close to the estimate. The estimate is not meant to be right; it is meant to be near enough to show the size.

估算 49 × 21，先取成容易乘的数：50 × 20 = 1,000，几秒钟就算完。精确答案是 49 × 20 + 49 = 980 + 49 = 1,029（因为 21 = 20 + 1），和估算很接近。估算不求准确，只求足够接近，能显示出答案的量级。

Step 2 · Expect a range第 2 步 · 预期一个范围

Learn先学

Split a bill of 387 between 6 people. Round 387 to 400: 400 ÷ 6 is about 67. Rounding 387 down to 360 gives 60 and up to 420 gives 70, so you expect something between 60 and 70. The exact answer, 387 ÷ 6 = 64.5, fits. If your calculator said 6.45 or 645, you would know at once that a decimal point slipped.

387 的账单，6 个人平摊。把 387 取成 400：400 ÷ 6 约为 67。387 往下取成 360 得 60，往上取成 420 得 70，所以你预期答案在 60 到 70 之间。精确答案 387 ÷ 6 = 64.5 符合。如果计算器给出 6.45 或 645，你马上就知道小数点错位了。

Step 3 · Compare, then trust or recheck第 3 步 · 对比，再决定相信还是重算

Learn先学

When the exact answer is close to the estimate, you can trust it. When it is far off, say ten times too big, stop and look: a wrong key, a wrong unit, a wrong operation. The estimate costs a few seconds and saves the embarrassment of sending out a wrong figure.

精确答案和估算接近，就可以相信它。差得很远，比如大了十倍，就停下来查：按错了键、单位错了、运算选错了。估算只花几秒钟，却能避免把错误数字发出去的尴尬。

49 × 21 estimate: 50 × 20 = 1,000 exact: 49 × 20 + 49 = 1,029 (close) 387 / 6 estimate: 400 / 6 = about 67 exact: 64.5 (close) calculator shows 645? -> a slip, check again

Takeaways要点回顾

• Round to easy numbers and estimate.
• Expect a range before you calculate.
• If the answer is far off, recheck.

• 取成容易的数来估算。
• 计算之前先预期一个范围。
• 答案差得远，就重查。
