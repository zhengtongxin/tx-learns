Working with quantities · Lesson 3处理数量 · 第 3 课

Ratios and scaling比例与缩放

Share a total by a ratio, scale a recipe up or down and compare prices by the unit price · ~10 min · reading and practice按比例分配总量，放大或缩小配方，并用单价比较价格 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Split by a ratio第 1 步 · 按比例分配

Learn先学

A ratio of 2 to 3, written 2 : 3, means 2 parts juice for 3 parts water, 5 parts in all. For 500 ml, one part is 500 ÷ 5 = 100 ml. So the juice is 2 × 100 = 200 ml and the water is 3 × 100 = 300 ml. Together: 200 + 300 = 500. The check is that the pieces add to the total.

2 比 3（写作 2 : 3）的比例，意思是 2 份果汁配 3 份水，一共 5 份。总量 500 毫升，则一份是 500 ÷ 5 = 100 毫升。果汁是 2 × 100 = 200 毫升，水是 3 × 100 = 300 毫升。合起来 200 + 300 = 500。检查方法：各部分相加要等于总量。

Step 2 · Scale by finding one第 2 步 · 先求“一份”再缩放

Learn先学

A recipe for 4 people uses 200 g of rice. For 10 people, first find the amount for one: 200 ÷ 4 = 50 g. Then multiply: 50 × 10 = 500 g. Going through “one” works for any size, up or down, with no need to work out a fraction such as 10 ÷ 4 = 2.5.

一份够 4 个人的配方用 200 克米。要做 10 个人的，先求一个人的量：200 ÷ 4 = 50 克，再乘：50 × 10 = 500 克。通过“一份”来算，放大缩小都适用，也不用去算 4 分之 10 这样的除法（10 ÷ 4 = 2.5）。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Compare with the unit price第 3 步 · 用单价比较

Learn先学

A 500 g pack costs $3.00 and a 750 g pack costs $4.20. Which is cheaper? Work out the unit price, the price for one unit, here 100 g. 500 g is 5 groups of 100 g, and 750 g is 7.5 groups. First: 3.00 ÷ 5 = 0.60. Second: 4.20 ÷ 7.5 = 0.56 (check: 0.56 × 7.5 = 4.20). The bigger pack is cheaper per 100 g. A price only compares fairly when both use the same unit.

500 克一包卖 $3.00，750 克一包卖 $4.20，哪个便宜？算出单价，也就是一个单位的价格，这里是每 100 克。500 克是 5 组 100 克，750 克是 7.5 组。第一包：3.00 ÷ 5 = 0.60。第二包：4.20 ÷ 7.5 = 0.56（检查：0.56 × 7.5 = 4.20）。大包每 100 克更便宜。只有两者用同一单位，价格才能公平地比较。

Juice : water = 2 : 3, total 500 ml one part = 500 / 5 = 100 ml juice = 200 ml, water = 300 ml Rice: 200 g for 4 people for 1 = 200 / 4 = 50 g for 10 = 50 × 10 = 500 g 500 g for 3.00 -> 0.60 per 100 g 750 g for 4.20 -> 0.56 per 100 g

Takeaways要点回顾

• Split a total by finding one part first.
• Scale by going through “one”.
• Compare prices by the unit price.

• 分配总量：先求一份。
• 缩放：通过“一份”。
• 比较价格：看单价。
