Units and conversions单位与换算

Keep the unit with every number and convert with one multiplication or division, so sums stay in the same unit · ~10 min · reading and practice每个数字都带着单位，换算只用一次乘法或除法，这样计算始终用同一种单位 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A number needs its unit第 1 步 · 数字要带单位

Learn先学

A bare 5 could be 5 metres or 5 kilometres, and the difference is a thousand times. Write the unit every time, also in your working. Sums only make sense between the same units: you cannot add 2 hours and 30 minutes until both are in hours or both in minutes.

单独一个 5，可能是 5 米，也可能是 5 公里，相差一千倍。每次都把单位写上，计算过程里也要写。只有相同的单位才能相加：2 小时和 30 分钟，要先都换成小时，或都换成分钟，才能加。

Step 2 · Convert with one factor第 2 步 · 用一个系数换算

Learn先学

The basic facts: 1 km = 1,000 m, 1 kg = 1,000 g and 1 hour = 60 minutes. To go to a smaller unit, multiply: 2.5 km is 2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 m. To go to a bigger unit, divide: 90 minutes is 90 ÷ 60 = 1.5 hours. If you are not sure which way, ask: should the number get bigger or smaller? Smaller units give bigger numbers.

基本关系：1 公里 = 1,000 米，1 千克 = 1,000 克，1 小时 = 60 分钟。换成更小的单位，就乘：2.5 公里是 2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 米。换成更大的单位，就除：90 分钟是 90 ÷ 60 = 1.5 小时。不确定方向时，问自己：数字该变大还是变小？单位越小，数字越大。

Step 3 · Check the size第 3 步 · 检查大小

Learn先学

After converting, look at the result. 750 g becoming 0.75 kg feels right: a bit less than a kilo. If you got 7.5 kg or 0.075 kg, the decimal point slipped. A quick look at the size catches most conversion slips before they reach anyone else.

换算完看一眼结果。750 克变成 0.75 千克是对的：比一千克少一点。如果得到 7.5 千克或 0.075 千克，就是小数点错位了。快速看一眼大小，多数换算错误在传出去之前就能发现。

2.5 km = 2.5 × 1,000 = 2,500 m 90 min = 90 / 60 = 1.5 h 750 g = 750 / 1,000 = 0.75 kg 1 h 30 min + 45 min = 90 min + 45 min = 135 min = 2 h 15 min

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write the unit with every number.
• Convert with one factor: smaller unit, multiply; bigger unit, divide.
• Look at the size of the result.

• 每个数字都写上单位。
• 用一个系数换算：换小单位就乘，换大单位就除。
• 看一眼结果的大小。
