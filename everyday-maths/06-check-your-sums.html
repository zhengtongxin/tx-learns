Getting it right · Lesson 3把数算对 · 第 3 课

Check your sums检查你的计算

Check a result by undoing it, by doing it another way and by checking the units · ~10 min · reading and practice用反向运算、换一种算法和检查单位来验证结果 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Undo it第 1 步 · 反过来算

Learn先学

Division is undone by multiplication. If 387 ÷ 6 = 64.5, check it by multiplying back: 64.5 × 6 = 387, which is where you started. Subtraction is undone by addition, and a square root by squaring (the square root of 36 is 6, and 6 × 6 = 36). If you do not land back on the start, one of the two sums is wrong. If you rounded on the way, expect a small gap, no bigger than the rounding.

除法可以用乘法还原。如果 387 ÷ 6 = 64.5，就反过来乘：64.5 × 6 = 387，回到起点。减法用加法还原，开方用平方还原（36 的平方根是 6，6 × 6 = 36）。如果没有回到起点，说明两道算式中有一道错了。如果中途取过整，会有一点小差距，不会超过取整带来的误差。

Step 2 · Do it another way第 2 步 · 换一种方法算

Learn先学

Split 18 as 20 − 2, so 18 × 25 is 20 × 25 − 2 × 25. That is 500 − 50 = 450. Doing it directly gives 18 × 25 = 450 too, so the two routes agree. Two different routes that agree are much stronger evidence than the same sum repeated twice, where you may repeat the same slip.

把 18 拆成 20 − 2，于是 18 × 25 = 20 × 25 − 2 × 25，即 500 − 50 = 450。直接算 18 × 25 也是 450，两条路线一致。两条不同的路线得出一致的结果，比把同一道算式重复两遍更有力，因为重复很可能犯同样的错。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Check unit and size第 3 步 · 检查单位和大小

Learn先学

Ask three things at the end. Is the unit what the question asked for? Is the size sensible? Does it match the estimate? A 15% tip on 40 is 0.15 × 40 = 6, and 6 is plausible for a meal of 40. It also matches a quick estimate: 10% of 40 is 4 and 5% is 2, so about 6. If you had 60 or 0.6, one look would tell you something is off.

最后问三件事：单位是不是题目要的？大小合不合理？和估算相符吗？40 的餐费，15% 小费是 0.15 × 40 = 6，对 40 的一餐来说是合理的。也和快速估算相符：40 的 10% 是 4，5% 是 2，约 6。如果算出 60 或 0.6，一眼就能看出有问题。

387 / 6 = 64.5 undo: 64.5 × 6 = 387 ok 18 × 25 direct: 450 another way: 20 × 25 - 2 × 25 = 500 - 50 = 450 ok 15% tip on 40 = 0.15 × 40 = 6 plausible

Takeaways要点回顾

• Undo the sum with the opposite operation.
• Do it a second, different way.
• Check the unit, the size and the estimate.

• 用相反的运算还原。
• 换一种不同的方法再算。
• 检查单位、大小和估算。
