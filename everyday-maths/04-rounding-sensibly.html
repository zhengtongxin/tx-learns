Rounding sensibly合理取整

Round only at the end, to the number of digits the question needs, and say that it is rounded · ~10 min · reading and practice只在最后取整，取到问题需要的位数，并说明这是取整后的数 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Round last第 1 步 · 最后才取整

Learn先学

Three items cost 1.34 each. Exactly, that is 1.34 × 3 = 4.02. If you round each item to 1.3 first, you get 1.3 × 3 = 3.9, which is off by 0.12. Rounding errors can add up, most of all when they all go the same way. Keep the full numbers while you work and round once, on the answer.

三件商品每件 1.34。精确地算，是 1.34 × 3 = 4.02。如果先把每件取整成 1.3，就得到 1.3 × 3 = 3.9，差了 0.12。取整的误差会累加，尤其当它们都朝同一个方向时。计算过程中保留完整的数，只对最后的答案取整一次。

Step 2 · Round to what is needed第 2 步 · 取整到需要的位数

Learn先学

Ask who will use the number and for what. A price to pay needs two decimals. A rough size for a plan may only need the nearest whole number. To round, look at the next digit: 5 or more, round up; 4 or less, round down. One decimal place means one digit after the point. So 7.46 to one decimal place is 7.5, and 4.02 is 4.0.

想想谁会用这个数，用来做什么。要付的价格需要两位小数。计划里的大致尺寸，取到整数可能就够了。取整时看下一位：5 或更大就进，4 或更小就舍。取一位小数，就是小数点后只留一位。所以 7.46 取一位小数是 7.5，4.02 是 4.0。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Say it is rounded第 3 步 · 说明这是取整后的

Learn先学

A rounded number looks exact but is not. Say “about 4” or “roughly 4.0”. If someone then multiplies your rounded figure, they should know it already carries an error. When exactness matters, as with money, round only the final amount, to the nearest cent.

取整后的数看起来精确，其实不是。说“约 4”或“大约 4.0”。如果别人拿你取整后的数再去乘，他应该知道这个数已经带着误差。需要精确时，比如钱，只对最后的金额取整，取到分。

1.34 × 3 = 4.02 exact 1.3 × 3 = 3.9 rounded first: off by 0.12 4.02 to one decimal -> 4.0 7.46 to one decimal -> 7.5 (next digit 6, round up)

Takeaways要点回顾

• Round only at the end.
• Round to the number of digits the question needs.
• Say “about” for rounded numbers.

• 只在最后取整。
• 取整到问题需要的位数。
• 取整后的数说“约”。
