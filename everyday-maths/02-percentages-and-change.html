Percentages and change百分数与变化

Find a percent of an amount and a percent change, and always know which number is the starting point · ~10 min · reading and practice求一个量的百分之几和变化的百分比，并始终清楚哪个数是起点 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A percent of an amount第 1 步 · 一个量的百分之几

Learn先学

Percent means “out of 100”. To find 20% of 80, turn 20% into 0.20 and multiply: 0.20 × 80 = 16. For 10% just move the decimal point one place: 10% of 80 is 8. Then 20% is double that, 16. Easy ones like 10%, 50% and 25% are worth doing in your head.

百分数的意思是“每一百份中的几份”。求 80 的 20%，把 20% 写成 0.20 再乘：0.20 × 80 = 16。10% 只要把小数点左移一位：80 的 10% 是 8，20% 就是它的两倍，16。10%、50%、25% 这类简单的，值得练到心算。

Step 2 · Change as a percent of the start第 2 步 · 变化是起点的百分之几

Learn先学

A price goes from 50 to 60. The change is 10. As a percent of what? Of the starting value: 10 ÷ 50 = 0.20, so it rose 20%. Dividing by the new value (60) gives a different number and is the usual slip. The rule: change divided by where you started.

价格从 50 涨到 60，变化是 10。是多少的百分之几？是起点的百分之几：10 ÷ 50 = 0.20，所以涨了 20%。如果除以新值 60，就会得出另一个数，这是常见的失误。规则是：变化量除以起点。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Up and down do not cancel第 3 步 · 上涨和下跌不会抵消

Learn先学

Take 10% off a price of 200: it becomes 180. Now add 10% to that: 10% of 180 is 18, so you reach 198, not 200. The second change works on a different base. Whenever two percent changes follow each other, work them one after the other.

一件 200 的商品打九折，变成 180。再加价 10%：180 的 10% 是 18，得到 198，而不是 200。第二次变化的基数不同。凡是连着两次百分比变化，就一次一次地算。

20% of 80 = 0.20 x 80 = 16 50 -> 60: change = 10 10 / 50 = 0.20 = 20% rise 200, 10% off: 200 - 20 = 180 then 10% more: 180 + 18 = 198 (not 200)

Takeaways要点回顾

• Percent of an amount: turn it into a decimal and multiply.
• Change as a percent: divide by the starting value.
• Two changes in a row: work them one after the other.

• 求百分之几：变成小数再乘。
• 变化的百分比：除以起点。
• 连着两次变化：一次一次地算。
