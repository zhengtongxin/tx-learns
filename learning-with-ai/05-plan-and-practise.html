Making it stick · Lesson 2让它记得住 · 第 2 课

Plan and practise做计划，多练习

Ask an AI to build a small study plan and practice tasks, then adjust it to the time you really have · ~10 min · reading and practice让 AI 帮你做一份小的学习计划和练习任务，再按你真实有的时间来调整 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Goal, deadline, time第 1 步 · 目标、期限、时间

Learn先学

A plan needs three facts: what you want to be able to do, by when, and how many hours a week you truly have. Tell the AI all three, plus what you already know. Be honest about the hours: a plan for ten hours a week is useless if you have three.

一份计划需要三个事实：你想做到什么、到什么时候、每周真正有几个小时。把这三点和你已经懂的内容都告诉 AI。时间要说实话：如果你一周只有三小时，一份十小时的计划毫无用处。

Step 2 · Short sessions第 2 步 · 短的学习时段

Learn先学

Ask for a plan of 25 to 40 minute sessions, each with one aim and one practice task, such as “explain it aloud” or “solve three problems”. Plans that list only topics to read feel like progress but do not build skill. Practice tasks do.

请它做成每段 25 到 40 分钟的计划，每段一个目标、一个练习任务，比如“大声讲一遍”或“做三道题”。只列出要读的主题的计划，让人觉得有进展，却不会长出能力。练习任务才会。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Review and adjust第 3 步 · 回顾并调整

Learn先学

Once a week, tell the AI what you did and what was hard, and ask it to adjust the next week. You are in charge of the plan. If it adds too much, cut it. A smaller plan you finish beats a perfect one you drop after three days.

每周一次，告诉 AI 你做了什么、什么难，请它调整下周。计划由你做主。它加得太多，就删。一份你完成的小计划，胜过一份三天就放弃的完美计划。

Goal: explain loan interest to a friend. Deadline: 3 weeks. Time: 3 hours a week. Please make 4 sessions of 40 minutes a week (160 minutes, which fits in 3 hours). Each session: one aim and one practice task. I will report back every Sunday in this same chat (a new chat starts blank, so I will paste this plan in again).

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Give goal, deadline, hours.
• Short sessions with practice.
• Review weekly.

• 给出目标、期限、时间。
• 短时段加练习。
• 每周回顾。
