When to put it away什么时候该放下它

Know when to learn without AI, so you build skills that stay with you and remain honest about what you can do · ~10 min · reading and practice知道什么时候不靠 AI 学，才能建立真正留在你身上的能力，并诚实面对自己会什么 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Help or doing it for you第 1 步 · 帮你学，还是替你做

Learn先学

Ask yourself: after this, can I do it alone? An AI that explains, quizzes and corrects you is helping. An AI that writes your answer or solves your problem is doing the work, and the learning goes with it. Both are fine sometimes; just know which one you chose.

问问自己：做完这一步，我自己能做吗？替你讲解、出题、纠错的 AI，是在帮你。直接写出你的答案或解掉你的题的 AI，是在替你做，学习也随之溜走。两种有时都可以，只要你清楚自己选了哪一种。

Step 2 · Try first, then ask第 2 步 · 先试，再问

Learn先学

Spend five or ten minutes on the problem yourself before asking. Write down what you tried. Then ask for a hint, not the answer, and try again. The struggle is uncomfortable and also the part that builds the skill. Ask for the full solution last.

提问之前，自己先花五到十分钟做题，写下你试过什么。然后只要提示，不要答案，再试一次。挣扎令人不舒服，也正是建立能力的部分。完整解答留到最后再要。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Test with no AI第 3 步 · 不靠 AI 测试自己

Learn先学

Every week, close the chat and try something from memory: explain a topic aloud, solve a problem on paper, write a paragraph. What you can do alone is what you actually know. If the result is shaky, that is useful news, not failure: go back and practise that part.

每周关掉对话，凭记忆试一件事：大声讲一个主题、在纸上解一道题、写一段话。你独自能做到的，才是你真正知道的。如果结果不稳，那是有用的信息，不是失败：回头练那一部分。

This week I did alone, no AI: - explained ______ aloud for 2 minutes - solved ______ on paper What was shaky: ______ Next: practise that part, then test again.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Test yourself with no AI.
