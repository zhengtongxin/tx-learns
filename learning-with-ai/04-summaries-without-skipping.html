Summaries without skipping摘要，但不省力

Use an AI to summarise a text for you in a way that still makes you do the thinking · ~10 min · reading and practice让 AI 帮你做摘要，但仍由你来动脑 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Read first第 1 步 · 先读

Learn先学

If you ask for a summary before reading, you skip the part that teaches you. Skim the text yourself, or read a section, then paste that passage into the chat and ask the AI for a summary. You will notice what it left out, because you know what was there. A summary is most useful after you have met the text once.

没读就让它做摘要，你就跳过了真正让你学到东西的部分。自己先浏览，或者读一节，再把这段原文贴进对话，让 AI 做摘要。因为你知道原文里有什么，就能发现它漏掉了什么。读过一遍原文之后，摘要最有用。

Step 2 · Your summary first第 2 步 · 先写你自己的摘要

Learn先学

Write three sentences from memory: the main point and two supporting ideas. Then ask the AI for its summary and compare. What did you miss? What did it add that is not in the text? The comparison shows both your gaps and its slips.

凭记忆写三句话：主要观点和两个支撑点。然后让 AI 给出它的摘要，两相对照。你漏了什么？它又加了什么原文没有的东西？对照既显出你的漏洞，也显出它的失误。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A map, not the land第 3 步 · 地图，不是实地

Learn先学

A summary is a map: good for finding your way, but the details live in the text. For anything you must get right, go back to the original passage. Ask the AI to point to the part of the text each claim comes from, then check that the quoted words really are on that page; a pointer can be made up as easily as a summary.

摘要是一张地图：帮你找路，但细节在原文里。凡是必须做对的内容，都要回到原文。让 AI 指出每个说法出自原文哪一部分，再亲自去核对引用的原话是否真的在那一页上；指向原文的位置，也可能和摘要一样是编的。

1 I read the section first. 2 My summary (3 sentences): ______ 3 AI summary: pasted below. 4 What did I miss? What did it add? 5 Ask: which part of the text supports each claim? Check it.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Read first, summarise after.
• Write yours, then compare.
• Use summaries as maps.

• 先读，后摘要。
• 先写你的，再对照。
• 把摘要当地图。
