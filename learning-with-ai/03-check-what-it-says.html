Asking well · Lesson 3问得好 · 第 3 课

Check what it says核对它说的话

Treat an AI answer as a first draft to check, because it can sound sure and still be wrong · ~10 min · reading and practice把 AI 的回答当作需要核对的初稿，因为它可能说得很肯定却仍然是错的 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Sounding sure is not being right第 1 步 · 听起来肯定，不等于正确

Learn先学

An AI writes fluent, confident text whether it is right or not. Names, dates, numbers, quotes and sources are where mistakes hide, and it may invent a source that does not exist, because it predicts the words that most likely come next, the way a phone suggests the next word, and does not look facts up. The smoother the answer sounds, the more it helps to check. Slow down most when the answer is something you will rely on.

AI 无论对错都会写出流畅、自信的文字。人名、日期、数字、引文和出处正是错误藏身的地方，它甚至可能编出一个不存在的出处，因为它是在预测接下来最可能出现的词，就像手机输入法猜你的下一个词，并不会去查证事实。答案听起来越顺，越值得核对。凡是你要依赖的内容，越要放慢。

Step 2 · A second source第 2 步 · 找第二个来源

Learn先学

Check one important claim somewhere else: a textbook, an official page, or a person who knows. Two independent sources that agree are much stronger than one. If you cannot find any second source for a specific fact, treat it as unconfirmed and do not repeat it as true.

重要的说法，去别处核对一次：教科书、官方网页，或者懂行的人。两个独立来源一致，比一个可靠得多。如果某个具体事实找不到第二个来源，就当它“未证实”，不要当成真的转述。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Ask for reasons and doubts第 3 步 · 要它给出理由和疑点

Learn先学

Ask: “How sure are you? What would make this wrong? Show the steps.” Neat steps can look like proof but can be wrong too, so check the key step against a second source. A good answer names its limits. Be careful when it simply agrees with everything you say: pushing back with a claim you know is wrong (say, a wrong date for an event you know well) and seeing whether it simply gives in is a quick test, because an AI tends to agree with the person it is talking to.

问：“你有多确定？什么情况下这会是错的？把步骤写出来。”整齐的步骤看着像证明，其实也可能出错，所以关键的一步要用第二个来源核对。好的回答会说出自己的局限。要留意它对你说的一切都点头同意：故意拿一个错误的说法反驳，看它会不会随声附和，是个快速的测试，因为 AI 往往倾向于附和和它对话的人。

Claim: ______ Where could it be wrong? (name, date, number, source) Second source: ______ Agree? yes / no / not found If not found: unconfirmed, do not repeat as fact.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Check specifics: names, dates, numbers, sources.
• Find a second source.
• Ask how sure it is.

• 核对具体信息：人名、日期、数字、出处。
• 找第二个来源。
• 问它有多确定。
