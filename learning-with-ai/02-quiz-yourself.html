Asking well · Lesson 2问得好 · 第 2 课

Quiz yourself让它出题考你

Turn an AI from an answer machine into a coach that asks you questions, so you practise recalling instead of just reading · ~10 min · reading and practice把 AI 从“答案机器”变成会向你提问的教练，让你练习回忆，而不只是阅读 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Questions before answers第 1 步 · 先要问题，后要答案

Learn先学

Reading an explanation feels like learning, but pulling it from memory is what makes it stay. Tell the AI: “Ask me five questions on this topic, one at a time, and wait for my answer.” The waiting part matters: the assistant then stops after each question until you reply, instead of giving the answer straight away. If yours keeps going, remind it to wait for your answer.

读讲解让人觉得学会了，但从记忆里把它拉出来，才会让它留下。对 AI 说：“就这个主题问我五个问题，一次一个，等我回答。”“等我回答”很关键：这样助手每问一题就会停下，等你回复，而不是马上给出答案。如果它不停，就提醒它等你回答。

Step 2 · Answer first, then check第 2 步 · 先答，再核对

Learn先学

Write your answer, even a guess, before you read the AI’s answer. Then ask the AI to mark it: what was right, what was missing, what was wrong. A guess that turns out wrong teaches more than an answer you only recognised. Keep a short note of the wrong ones.

先写下你的答案，哪怕是猜的，再去看 AI 的答案。然后让 AI 批改：哪里对、缺了什么、哪里错。猜错的那一次，比单纯“认得出来”的答案教你更多。把答错的简短记下来。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Aim at your gaps第 3 步 · 对准你的漏洞

Learn先学

After a round, ask: “Which ideas did I get wrong? Ask me two new questions on each.” Come back the next day and ask again; some assistants keep old chats, but a brand-new chat starts blank, so paste your list of weak ideas into it. The weak spots should shrink each round. If the AI keeps asking only easy questions, tell it to make them harder.

一轮结束后问：“我哪些概念答错了？每个再出两道新题。”第二天回来再问一遍；有些助手会保留旧对话，但全新的对话是空白的，所以把你的薄弱概念清单贴进去。薄弱点应该一轮比一轮少。如果 AI 只出简单题，就让它出难一点的。

Ask me 5 questions on: how loan interest works. One at a time. Wait for my answer. After each, tell me: right, partly right, or wrong, and why. At the end list my weak ideas and ask 2 new questions on each.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask for questions first.
• Answer before you check.
• Practise on your gaps.

• 先要问题。
• 先答再核对。
• 练习用在漏洞上。
