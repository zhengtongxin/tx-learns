Asking well · Lesson 1问得好 · 第 1 课

Ask at your level按你的水平提问

Tell an AI who you are and what you already know, so its explanation fits you instead of the average reader · ~10 min · reading and practice告诉 AI 你是谁、已经懂什么，让它的讲解适合你，而不是适合“平均读者” · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Say who you are第 1 步 · 说明你是谁

Learn先学

An AI cannot see your face, so it cannot tell if you are a beginner. Start with three short lines: what you are learning, what you already know, and where you are stuck. “I know what a percentage is, but I do not understand how loan interest adds up.” The answer then starts where you are.

AI 看不到你的脸，也就无从知道你是不是新手。先写三行短话：你在学什么、已经懂什么、卡在哪里。“我知道百分比是什么，但不明白贷款利息是怎么累加的。”这样答案就从你所在的地方开始。

Step 2 · One idea, with an example第 2 步 · 一次一个概念，配一个例子

Learn先学

A long list of questions gets a long, shallow answer. Ask about one idea and request one concrete example from daily life. If the example does not click, say so and ask for another. You are allowed to ask the same thing three ways.

一连串问题只会得到又长又浅的回答。每次问一个概念，并要一个来自日常生活的具体例子。例子没听懂，就直接说，再要一个。同一件事换三种问法，完全可以。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Ask for a different angle第 3 步 · 要一个不同的角度

Learn先学

When an explanation does not land, do not just ask for “simpler”. Name what to change: shorter, with a picture in words, using an analogy (a comparison to something you already know), or step by step. Then say back what you understood and ask whether you got it right. Saying it back is where the learning happens.

讲解没听懂时，别只说“再简单点”。说明要改什么：更短、用文字画个图、用类比（拿你已经熟悉的东西来比），或者一步一步。然后把你的理解说回去，问对不对。把它说出来，正是学习发生的地方。

I am learning: how a loan interest works. I already know: percentages. I am stuck on: why the interest is not just a flat fee. Please explain in 5 sentences with one everyday example. Then I will say it back and you tell me what I missed.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Say what you know and where you are stuck.
• One idea and one example at a time.
• Say it back to test yourself.

• 说明你懂什么、卡在哪。
• 一次一个概念、一个例子。
• 说回去，检验自己。
