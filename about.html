Who writes it谁在写

I am TX. I am learning AI myself, and I learn best by explaining. Every lesson here starts from my own notes and is written for someone who is new to the topic.我是 TX。我自己也在学 AI，而讲给别人听是我学得最好的方式。这里每一课都从我自己的笔记开始，写给刚接触这个主题的人。

02

How lessons are made课是怎么做出来的

I write the notes. A team of AI agents drafts each lesson from them, reads it the way its intended reader would, checks the facts and the code, and tests the page before it goes live. Nothing is published without passing those checks.笔记由我来写。一组 AI 智能体根据笔记起草每一课，以这一课目标读者的眼光读一遍，核对事实和代码，并在上线前测试页面。没通过这些检查的内容不会发布。

  1. My notes我的笔记
  2. Agents draft智能体起草
  3. Read as the reader以读者视角读
  4. Facts and code checked核对事实和代码
  5. Page tested, then live测试页面后上线

03

What the site records本站记录什么

Your progress and language choice stay in your own browser. The site keeps two counts per lesson.你的学习进度和语言选择只保存在你自己的浏览器里。本站对每一课只保留两个计数。

  • Clear or not清楚与否The yes or no answers to "Was this lesson clear?"“这一课讲清楚了吗？”的“清楚 / 不太清楚”回答。
  • Read to the end读完次数How often the lesson was read to the end.这一课被读完的次数。
  • Never从不No cookies, no visitor tracking, and it never asks for your name or email.不使用 Cookie，不追踪访客，也从不索取你的姓名或邮箱。
