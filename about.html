About this site关于本站

A practitioner's learning notes on AI, project management and IT service management, turned into interactive lessons and maintained by automated agents.一位实践者在 AI、项目管理和 IT 服务管理方面的学习笔记，整理成可交互的课，由自动化智能体持续维护。

01

Purpose宗旨

TX Learns turns learning notes into structured, interactive lessons for people new to a field. The AI tracks are built from TX's notes and general knowledge, and TX learns best by explaining; the project management and IT service management exam tracks are written from general knowledge in our own words, and the study coach lets you do the same with your own material.TX Learns 把学习笔记整理成结构清晰、可交互的课，面向刚进入某个领域的读者。AI 路线依据 TX 的笔记和通用知识整理，TX 通过讲解来学得最好；项目管理和 IT 服务管理的备考路线则用我们自己的话，依据通用知识写成，学习助手让你也能用自己的资料做同样的事。

02

Production产出

Notes are the single source. Automated agents draft each lesson, review it from the target reader's point of view, verify the facts and the code, and test the page. A lesson goes live only after every check passes.笔记是唯一的内容来源。自动化智能体负责起草每一课，以目标读者的视角审阅，核对事实与代码，并测试页面。只有全部检查通过，课才会上线。

03

Privacy隐私

Your progress, the day you finished each lesson (used for the review list on the study page) and your language choice stay in your browser. The site keeps two anonymous counts per lesson.学习进度、每课的完成日期（用于学习教练页的复习清单）和语言选择只保存在你的浏览器里。本站对每一课只保留两个匿名计数。

  • Clarity清晰度Yes or no answers to “Was this lesson clear?”对“这一课讲清楚了吗？”的“清楚 / 不太清楚”回答。
  • Completion完成度How often the lesson is read to the end.这一课被读到结尾的次数。
  • Not collected不收集No cookies, no visitor tracking, no name or email.不使用 Cookie，不追踪访客，不索取姓名或邮箱。
04

Corrections勘误

Lessons can be wrong or out of date. Report any issue on GitHub; it reaches TX directly.课可能有误，也可能已经过时。请在 GitHub 提交 issue，TX 会直接收到。
