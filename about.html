01

Purpose宗旨

TX Learns turns first-hand AI notes into structured, interactive lessons for people new to the field. Every lesson starts from notes by TX, who learns best by explaining.TX Learns 把第一手的 AI 笔记整理成结构清晰、可交互的课，面向刚进入这个领域的读者。每一课都源自 TX 的笔记，TX 通过讲解来学得最好。

02

How lessons are produced课是如何产出的

Notes are the single source. Automated agents draft each lesson, review it from the target reader's point of view, verify the facts and the code, and test the page. A lesson goes live only after every check passes.笔记是唯一的内容来源。自动化智能体负责起草每一课，以目标读者的视角审阅，核对事实与代码，并测试页面。只有全部检查通过，课才会上线。

  1. Notes笔记
  2. Draft起草
  3. Reader review读者审阅
  4. Fact and code check事实与代码核对
  5. Test and publish测试并发布

03

Privacy隐私

Your progress and language choice stay in your browser. The site keeps two anonymous counts per lesson.学习进度和语言选择只保存在你的浏览器里。本站对每一课只保留两个匿名计数。

  • Clarity清晰度Yes or no answers to “Was this lesson clear?”对“这一课讲清楚了吗？”的“清楚 / 不太清楚”回答。
  • Completion完成度How often the lesson is read to the end.这一课被读到结尾的次数。
  • Not collected不收集No cookies, no visitor tracking, no name or email.不使用 Cookie，不追踪访客，不索取姓名或邮箱。
