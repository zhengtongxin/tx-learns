About this site关于本站

One learner's notes on AI, rebuilt as lessons you can click through.一个学习者的 AI 笔记，重写成可以点着学的课。

Who writes it谁在写

I am TX. I am learning AI myself, and I learn best by explaining. Every lesson here starts from my own notes and is written for someone who is new to the topic.我是 TX。我自己也在学 AI，而讲给别人听是我学得最好的方式。这里每一课都从我自己的笔记开始，写给刚接触这个主题的人。

How lessons are made课是怎么做出来的

I write the notes. A team of AI agents drafts each lesson from them, reads it as a newcomer, checks the facts and the code, and tests the page before it goes live. Nothing is published without passing those checks.笔记由我来写。一组 AI 智能体根据笔记起草每一课，以新手的眼光读一遍，核对事实和代码，并在上线前测试页面。没通过这些检查的内容不会发布。

What the site records本站记录什么

Your progress and language choice stay in your own browser. The site counts page views and the answers to "Was this lesson clear?" without cookies and without knowing who you are. It never asks for your name or email.你的学习进度和语言选择只保存在你自己的浏览器里。本站会统计页面浏览量和“这一课讲清楚了吗？”的回答，不使用 Cookie，也不知道你是谁。本站从不索取你的姓名或邮箱。

Found a mistake?发现错误？

Lessons can be wrong or out of date. If you spot something, please open an issue on GitHub. It reaches me directly.课里可能有错，也可能已经过时。如果你发现问题，请在 GitHub 上提一个 issue，我会直接收到。
