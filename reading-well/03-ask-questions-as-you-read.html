Ask questions as you read边读边提问

Keep a few questions in mind while you read, and stop when something does not make sense instead of pushing on · ~10 min · reading and practice读的时候心里带着几个问题，遇到不通的地方就停下来，而不是硬着头皮往下读 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Three questions第 1 步 · 三个问题

Learn先学

Three questions work on almost any text: What is the main point? What is the evidence for it? What does it mean for me? Asking them while you read turns you from someone who receives words into someone who tests them.

三个问题几乎适用于任何文章：主要观点是什么？证据是什么？这对我意味着什么？边读边问，你就从被动接收文字的人，变成检验文字的人。

Step 2 · Notice when you drift第 2 步 · 察觉走神

Learn先学

Everyone sometimes reaches the end of a page and remembers nothing. When that happens, you stopped understanding a few lines earlier and kept moving your eyes. A simple check after each paragraph helps: can I say what that paragraph said in one sentence? If not, read it again.

每个人都会有读到页尾却什么都没记住的时候。那是因为几行之前你就不再理解了，眼睛却还在动。每读完一段做个简单检查：我能用一句话说出这段讲了什么吗？不能，就再读一遍。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Mark it and move on第 3 步 · 标出来再继续

Learn先学

When something is confusing, put a small mark in the margin or on a note, such as a question mark, and keep reading. Sometimes the writing is simply unclear, and that is not your fault. Many confusing points are explained a few paragraphs later. If it is still unclear at the end, you now have a precise question to look up or to ask someone.

遇到不懂的地方，在页边或便签上做个小标记，比如问号，然后继续读。有时是文字本身写得不清楚，那不是你的错。很多不懂的点在几段之后就有解释。如果读完还是不清楚，你就有了一个明确的问题，可以去查或去问别人。

Main point? Late reports have two causes that can be fixed. Evidence? Two months of timing data in section 2. For me? I should send my figures by Tuesday. Confusing: "handover lag" (an invented example term, marked with ?) After reading: section 3 explains it, it means waiting for sign-off.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Ask for the main point, the evidence and what it means for you.
• Check each paragraph in one sentence.
• Mark what is unclear and keep going.

• 问主要观点、证据和对你的意义。
• 每段用一句话检查。
• 标出不清楚的地方继续读。
