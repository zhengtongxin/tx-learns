Keeping what you read · Lesson 2记住读过的内容 · 第 2 课

Say it back复述出来

Summarise what you read from memory, aloud or on paper, to find out what stayed and what did not · ~10 min · reading and practice凭记忆把读到的内容复述出来，说出来或写下来，看看哪些留下了、哪些没有 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Memory first第 1 步 · 先凭记忆

Learn先学

Rereading feels easy because the text is right in front of you, but it does not show what you know. Retelling the text from memory does. After reading, spend two minutes saying or writing what the text said without looking. This is hard on purpose, and the effort makes the memory stronger.

重读感觉轻松，是因为文章就在眼前，但它并不能显示你到底记住了什么。凭记忆复述才能。读完后，花两分钟不看原文，把文章的内容说出来或写下来。这样做是故意让它变难，而这份吃力会让记忆更牢。

Step 2 · Find the holes第 2 步 · 找出漏洞

Learn先学

Compare your retelling with the text. The things you left out, or got wrong, are your holes. Those are exactly the parts to read again. You now spend your time on what you do not know, instead of on what you already do.

把你的复述和原文对照。漏掉的或说错的地方就是你的漏洞，也正是需要再读的部分。这样你的时间就花在不会的内容上，而不是已经会的内容上。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A short shape第 3 步 · 一个简短的结构

Learn先学

A good summary can follow a simple shape: the main point, two or three reasons or steps, and what to do about it. Three or four sentences are enough. If you cannot keep it that short, you do not yet see what matters most, and trying to cut it down is a good way to find out.

好的总结可以用一个简单的结构：主要观点、两三条理由或步骤、以及该怎么做。三四句话就够了。如果你压缩不到这么短，说明你还没看清最重要的是什么，而试着删减正是找出重点的好办法。

Point: our reports are late because of two things. Reasons: data arrives late; sign-off takes too long. Action: ask for data by Wednesday and name one person to sign off. Holes found: I forgot the numbers in section 2. Reread that part.

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Retell before you look.
• Reread only the holes.
• Keep the summary to three or four sentences.

• 先复述，再对照。
• 只重读漏洞。
• 总结保持三四句话。
