Preview before you read先预览再读

Spend a minute on titles, headings and the first and last lines, so the details have somewhere to land when you read them · ~10 min · reading and practice花一分钟看标题、小标题和首尾几行，这样读细节时，它们才有地方可放 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · A one-minute look第 1 步 · 一分钟看一眼

Learn先学

Before reading closely, look at the title, the headings, any bold words, the first paragraph and the last paragraph. This takes about a minute. You now know roughly where the text is going, and that is what makes the details easier to follow and remember.

细读之前，先看标题、小标题、加粗的词、第一段和最后一段。这大约只要一分钟。你就大致知道文章往哪走，细节也就更容易跟上和记住。

Step 2 · Headings are a map第 2 步 · 小标题是地图

Learn先学

Headings show how the writer has organised the ideas. Turn each heading into a question: “Why does the cost go up?” Then read the section looking for the answer. If a section’s heading is not relevant to your goal, you can skip it with a clear conscience.

小标题显示了作者如何组织想法。把每个小标题变成一个问题：“为什么成本会上升？”然后带着这个问题去读这一节。如果某一节的标题和你的目标无关，就可以放心跳过。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Guess first第 3 步 · 先猜一猜

Learn先学

After the preview, write one sentence guessing what the main point will be. When you finish, compare. A right guess shows you followed it; a wrong guess shows exactly where your understanding changed, which is when you learn the most.

预览之后，写一句话猜主要观点是什么。读完再对照。猜对了说明你跟上了；猜错了则清楚地显示出你的理解在哪里发生了变化，那恰恰是学得最多的时候。

Title: Why our reports are late Headings: Where the time goes / Two causes / What we will change First paragraph: late by an average of two days. Last paragraph: two fixes proposed. My guess: the main point is that two causes can be fixed.

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Preview for about a minute first.
• Turn headings into questions.
• Write a guess, then compare.

• 先预览约一分钟。
• 把小标题变成问题。
• 写下猜测，再对照。
