Keeping what you read · Lesson 3记住读过的内容 · 第 3 课

Connect and revisit联系与回顾

Link new ideas to what you already know and come back to them after a gap, so the reading is still there when you need it · ~10 min · reading and practice把新想法和你已知的联系起来，并隔一段时间再回头看，这样需要时读过的内容还在 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Hook it to what you know第 1 步 · 挂到你已知的东西上

Learn先学

New ideas stick when they attach to something you already know. After reading, ask: what does this remind me of? Where would I use it? Write one line connecting them, such as “late reports are like a queue at a counter: the slowest step sets the speed”. A connection gives the idea a place to live.

新想法如果能挂在你已有的知识上，就更容易记住。读完问自己：这让我想起什么？我会在哪里用它？写一行把两者联系起来，比如“报告迟交就像柜台排队：最慢的那一步决定速度”。联系给想法找到了落脚的地方。

Step 2 · Come back after a gap第 2 步 · 隔一段时间再回头

Learn先学

One reading, however good, fades. Look at your notes again after a day, then after a few days, then after a couple of weeks. Each time, try to recall before you look. The gaps are what make the memory last, and each review takes only a few minutes.

再好的一次阅读也会淡去。一天后再看一遍笔记，几天后再看，再过几周再看。每次都先尝试回忆，再看笔记。正是这些间隔让记忆持久，而每次回顾只要几分钟。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · A small reading log第 3 步 · 一个小小的阅读记录

Learn先学

Keep a short list: what you read, the date, one line on the main point and one line on how you will use it. It takes a minute per text. Months later it is a map of what you have learned, and it tells you what to revisit.

记一个简短的清单：读了什么、日期、一行主要观点、一行打算怎么用。每篇只要一分钟。几个月后，它就是你学过什么的地图，也告诉你该回顾什么。

Date: 3 March Text: report on late deliveries Point: two fixable causes, data and sign-off Use: ask for data by Wednesday Link: like a queue, the slowest step sets the speed Revisit: after 1 day, then 3 days, then 2 weeks

More practice再练一练

Now make it yours现在把它变成你的

Takeaways要点回顾

• Link each new idea to something you know.
• Come back after a gap and recall first.
• Keep a short log of what you read.

• 把每个新想法联系到已知的事。
• 隔一段时间回来，先回忆。
• 记一个简短的阅读记录。
