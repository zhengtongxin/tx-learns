Reading with purpose · Lesson 1带着目的阅读 · 第 1 课

Know why you read知道为什么读

Decide what you want from a text before you start, so you can read for that and stop when you have it · ~10 min · reading and practice开始前先想清你要从文章里得到什么，这样就能围绕它阅读，拿到了就停 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Name the goal第 1 步 · 说出目标

Learn先学

Before you read, finish this sentence: “I am reading this to find out ...”. It might be “how to set up the tool”, “whether the report supports a decision” or “what the main argument is”. A reader without a goal treats every line as equally important and gets tired fast. A goal tells you which lines matter.

读之前，先把这句话补完：“我读它是为了弄清……”。可以是“怎么装好这个工具”“这份报告是否支持某个决定”或“主要论点是什么”。没有目标的读者会把每一行都当成同样重要，很快就累了。有了目标，你就知道哪些行重要。

Step 2 · Match the way you read第 2 步 · 让读法匹配目标

Learn先学

Different goals need different reading. To find one fact, scan for it and leave the rest. To decide whether a text is worth your time, read the start and the end. To truly understand an idea, read slowly and stop to think. Choosing the way on purpose beats reading everything at one speed.

不同的目标需要不同的读法。找一个事实，就扫着找，其余不管。判断一篇值不值得读，就看开头和结尾。要真正弄懂一个想法，就慢读，停下来想。有意识地选读法，比所有内容一个速度读完要好。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Stop when you are done第 3 步 · 够了就停

Learn先学

Finishing is not the goal; getting what you came for is. If you wanted the setup steps and you have them, you may close the page. If a text turns out not to answer your question, putting it down early is a good decision, not a failure.

读完并不是目的，得到你要的才是。如果你要的是安装步骤，而你已经拿到了，就可以关掉页面。如果一篇文章回答不了你的问题，早点放下是好决定，不是失败。

Text: the team handbook, chapter on leave. I am reading this to find out: how many days notice I need. How I will read: scan the headings for "notice", read that part slowly. Done when: I can say the number and where it came from.

Takeaways要点回顾

• Write the goal before you read.
• Choose a way of reading that fits it.
• Stop once you have what you need.

• 读之前先写目标。
• 选择适合它的读法。
• 拿到需要的就停。
