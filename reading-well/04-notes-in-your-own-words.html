Notes in your own words用自己的话做笔记

Write notes that explain an idea in your own words instead of copying sentences, because putting it into words is what builds understanding · ~10 min · reading and practice用自己的话写笔记来解释想法，而不是抄句子，因为把它说出来才能真正理解 · 约 10 分钟 · 阅读加练习

Step 1 · Copying is not noting第 1 步 · 抄写不是做笔记

Learn先学

It feels productive to highlight half a page or copy whole sentences. But the eyes and the hand can do that without the mind. The notes look full, and little has been understood. The test of a good note is whether you could explain it to a friend without looking at the text.

把半页都划上重点，或者整句整句地抄，会让人觉得很有成效。但眼睛和手可以在大脑不参与的情况下完成这些。笔记看着很满，却没怎么理解。好笔记的检验标准是：不看原文，你能不能向朋友讲清楚。

Step 2 · Say it your way第 2 步 · 用自己的话说

Learn先学

After a section, close the text and write the idea in your own words, as if for a friend who has not read it. Where you get stuck, you have found what you did not yet understand. Open the text, fix that part, and write again. The effort of finding the words is the learning.

读完一节，合上文章，用自己的话把想法写出来，像是写给没读过的朋友。卡住的地方，就是你还没弄懂的地方。再打开文章，补上那一块，重新写。找词的这份努力，就是学习本身。

Sort each one.

给每一项分类。

Step 3 · Keep it short第 3 步 · 保持简短

Learn先学

A note is a reminder, not a copy. A line or two per idea is usually enough, plus one example if it helps. If your notes are as long as the text, you will not reread them, and a note you do not reread does not help.

笔记是提醒，不是副本。每个想法一两行通常就够，必要时加一个例子。如果你的笔记和原文一样长，你就不会再读它，而不会再读的笔记没有用处。

Idea: a late report usually has two causes. In my words: the data arrives late, and sign-off takes too long. Example: sales figures came on Thursday, sign-off took until Monday. Can I explain it without the text? yes

Takeaways要点回顾

• Do not copy; put it in your own words.
• Where you get stuck, you have found a gap.
• Keep notes short enough to reread.

• 不要抄，要用自己的话。
• 卡住的地方就是缺口。
• 笔记短到愿意重读。
